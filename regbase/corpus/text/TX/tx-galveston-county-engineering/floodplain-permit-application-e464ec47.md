You must have all required documents and required information before the application can be submitted.
Prior to starting the permit application process, use the GIS map below to determine information that is required within the application e.g., is the property in unincorporated Galveston County, the property's flood zone, distance from mean high tide and if the property is within a concrete regulated area.
In addition to the information collected from the GIS Map, the following documents may be required. The Owner Acknowledgment is required for ALL applications. Based on the type of permit applied for, you may be prompted to upload one or more of the documents linked below before your application can be submitted.
Please allow 5 to 7 business days for an initial response. Large scale development may take up to 10 business days.
Beach Front Construction Certificate and Dune Protection Permit applications, for properties on the Bolivar Peninsula and within 1,000 ft. of mean high tide, are submitted to the General Land Office (GLO) for further review after the County completes their review. The GLO has 10 business days to respond with questions and/or comments (30 business days for large scale development). If additional information is requested their response time starts over once they receive the information requested.

## Installation Of Culverts
When placing a culvert on a County Road or ROW, a Driveway access permit is required. Please, contact the Road & Bridge Dept. @ 281-534-4125, their office is located at 5115 Hwy 3, Dickinson, Texas 77539 if you need assistance. If the proposed development is on a Hwy and you need driveway access, you will need to Contact TxDOT for a driveway access permit. The driveway access permit will need to be submitted with the floodplain permit application.

## Unincorporated Utility Providers
Issuance of a Galveston County Floodplain Permit does not guarantee utility availability. It is the responsibility of the landowner, not Galveston County, to determine utility availability to their property. A floodplain permit is not contingent on availability of all utilities. Some permit applications may require approvals from the utility providers or the Galveston County Health District. Those contacts can be found below:
