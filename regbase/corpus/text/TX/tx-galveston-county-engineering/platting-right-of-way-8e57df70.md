## Galveston County Platting & Right-of-Way Services
Administer subdivision regulations.
Issue permits for use of Right-of-Way.
Work with Galveston County Road & Bridge Department. to determine if unimproved right-of-way can be opened or extended.
Aid in the sale of excess county property.
Issue encroachment agreements to satisfy title issues or to comply with county regulations.
