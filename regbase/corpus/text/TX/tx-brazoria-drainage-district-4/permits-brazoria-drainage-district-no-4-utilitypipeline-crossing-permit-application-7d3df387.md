Brazoria Drainage District No. 4 controls certain activities within its jurisdictional authority. The District grants permits and requires on-site availability of such permits. Permits granted for activity within the District shall list the specific activities for which the permit was issued and the date of expiration. Activities shall be limited to those shown on the issued permit. The applicant is responsible for renewing permits prior to the expiration date and will be considered in non-compliance during the period that the permit is expired.
Types of Permits:
General Construction Permit Application – Any work associated with the construction of a project which has received drainage plan approval from the District.
Sand Pit Permit Application – The District’s Sand Pit Policy , effective December 1, 2021, requires that the operator of a sand pit obtain a permit prior to beginning any construction or mining related activity.
Utility/Pipeline Crossing Permit Application – The District has minimum requirements for utility/pipeline crossings of a District facility that is within Brazoria Drainage District No. 4’s jurisdiction.
