Class I and Class III new permit, renewal and major amendment applicants are required to provide plain language summaries for permit applications declared administratively complete on or after May 1, 2022, in accordance with 30 TAC Chapter 39 Subchapter H. These documents are available in English and may be required in alternative languages.
An electronic copy of the administratively and technically complete applications and draft permit(s) are available for permit applications declared administratively complete on or after June 1, 2024.
