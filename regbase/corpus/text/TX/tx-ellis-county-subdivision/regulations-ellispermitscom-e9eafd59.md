## MULTI-FAMILY & COMMERCIAL REGULATIONS
Prior to construction, all commercial & most multi-family developments require review and approval from Development, Engineering, and Fire Marshal’s Office . Plat requirements must be met prior to beginning the development application process. In addition, all of these project types fall under the 2018 International Fire Code and the adopted Ellis County Fire Code . Please contact the water provider to ensure the available water has the capacity to meet fire suppression requirements, as well as other utility provider requirements.
Please email Gabby Simon for further information or to begin the commercial development application process. Depending on the project, you may be advised to schedule a pre-application meeting.
