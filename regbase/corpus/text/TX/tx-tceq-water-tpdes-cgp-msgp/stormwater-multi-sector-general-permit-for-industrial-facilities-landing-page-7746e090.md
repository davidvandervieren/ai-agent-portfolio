## HOT Issued 2026 Renewal of the Multi-Sector General Permit (MSGP), TXR050000
The new 2026 Multi-Sector General Permit (MSGP) for industrial facilities has been issued with an effective date of August 14, 2026. The renewal period for existing permittees begins on August 14, 2026, at 8:00 AM CDT, after which existing permittees will have a 90-day grace period to renew their authorizations by submitting a renewal Notice of Intent (NOI) or No Exposure Certification (NEC), as applicable, using TCEQ’s online e-permitting system.
The renewal period is only available from August 14 - November 12, 2026. Existing permittees will not be allowed to submit a renewal application before August 14, 2026.
To continue your authorization under the MSGP, you must submit a renewal Notice of Intent (INOI-R) or No Exposure Certification (INEC-R), as applicable, using TCEQ’s online e-permitting system STEERS/ePermits by the deadline of November 12, 2026. The application fee is $100. 2026 Multi-Sector General Permit for Industrial Facilities Below are the PDF documents for the 2026 MSGP, Fact Sheet, and Executive Director’s Response to Comments:

NOTE: If you no longer need your MSGP authorization, you will need to submit a Notice of Termination (NOT) online before Sept. 1, 2026, to avoid the $200 annual water quality fee.

## Electronic Application Requirements
Effective September 1, 2017, applicants must submit the NOI, NEC, NOT, and NOC forms using the online e-permitting system available through the TCEQ website or request and obtain an electronic reporting waiver. Electronic reporting waivers are not transferrable and expire on the same date as the authorization to discharge.
Paper Forms: If you do not have access to a computer, you must call the TCEQ Stormwater Processing Center at 512-239-3700, to obtain the application forms.
