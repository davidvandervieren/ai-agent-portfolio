Attachments and Supplemental Materials 
The following are required for an application to be considered complete for review: 
1. Completed application including signed and notarized signature, pages 1 through 4; 
2. Completed and signed Permission to Enter and Cross document, page 5; 
3. A map showing the vicinity of the proposed site, such as a county map; 
4. A scale drawing of the proposed project area that indicates: 
a) The location of the operation, identified by prominent landmarks and showing the limits of the 
proposed project (show distances in miles, feet, etc.); 
b) The name of the waterway; 
c) Graphic scale of the drawing; 
d) The direction of north by arrow; 
e) The direction of flow in streams and the direction of ebb and flow in tidal waters; 
f) The mean high and low waterlines in tidal areas and the ordinary high and low waterlines in a 
lake or stream; 
g) The property lines and names of adjacent property owners; 
h) If the waterway separates two or more counties, show the county lines and the names of the 
counties; 
i) The location of associated activities such as access routes, staging areas, sorting and washing 
sites, settling ponds, etc.; 
j) The average depth in feet and/or inches of the water at 200-feet intervals; 
k) Location of stockpiles, temporary placement, etc., if applicable. 
5. At least four color photographs of the site adequately illustrating where the operation will be conducted: 
a) One from each side of the river at the work area, showing the banks, river, and vegetation; 
b) One from the downstream and upstream ends of the project area, showing river and banks from 
close to the elevation of the water. 
6. Sediment Impact Assessment per Item 26; 
7. Draft Public Notice per Item 32; 
8. List of landowners per Item 33; 
9. Documentation of mailing of Public Notice per Item 35; 
10. Documentation of publications of Public Notice per Item 36.

Sand and Gravel Permit Application Instructions 
Sand and Gravel Permit Application Instructions
T
his is a complete form set for applying for a Sand and Gravel Permit, including a checklist of the Attachments 
and Supplemental Materials required for an application to be considered administratively complete. An 
application that is not administratively complete will be returned unprocessed. 
A. Fees
T
he application processing fees for a Sand and Gravel Permit are as follows: 
Individual Permit if the materials are to be sold (revenue permit): $1,200 [31 TAC § 69.114(a)(1)], 
Individual Permit if the materials will not be sold: $500 [31 TAC § 69.114(a)(2)], 
General Permit: $250 [31 TAC § 69.119]. 
P
rojects proposing to disturb or remove 1,000 cubic yards or more of material must be processed as an 
Individual Permit. Projects under 1,000 cubic yards may be processed as an Individual Permit, depending upon 
project details, at TPWD discretion. 
F
ees are required for renewal applications. Application processing fees are nonrefundable, even if your 
application is denied. 
P
lease make your check or money order payable to Texas Parks and Wildlife Department. 
B. Section I: Applicant Information (line numbers 1 – 8)
1. If Applicant is an individual, provide the Applicant’s full legal name. Provide the entity or company’s
name if it is a d/b/a, partnership, or corporation. If the Applicant is a governmental entity, use the
agency name, and include the parent political subdivision. For example, if the Applicant is a municipal
water department, give the name of the city as well as the department. SSN, Date of Birth, and Driver’s
License information are required for applicants that are individuals or sole proprietorships in order to
complete mandatory background checks [Texas Family Code, Section 231.302 and Federal Statute 42
U.S.C. §666.]
2. P
rovide the Applicant’s physical mailing address. If you use a P.O. Box, it will not be possible for the
Department to use a commercial overnight express service.
3. P
rovide the Applicant’s contact information, including email, business telephone numbers, and any
applicable cellular and telefax numbers.
4. P
rovide the Applicant’s Comptroller’s ID number or Federal Employers Identification Number (FEIN).
This is necessary to track payments from revenue permits.
5. I
f the Applicant has a current Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) permit for the same site
and is renewing that permit, fill in that permit number.
6
-8. If an agent, consultant, or attorney is acting on behalf of an Applicant, fill in his or her name and contact
information. TPWD will generally contact the agent before contacting the Applicant.

C. Section II: Location of Proposed Activities (line numbers 9 – 14) 

9. Provide the name of the river, creek, bayou or other water body where the disturbance or removal will 
take place. 

10. Provide the name of the county or counties where the disturbance or removal will take place. 

11. Provide the name of the nearest city or town to the project location that has a post office. If the city or 
town is in a different county, please state that fact. 

12. Provide the name of the nearest upstream public road crossing (bridge or low-water) from the project 
location and state how far away it is from the proposed location. 

13. Provide the name of the nearest downstream public road crossing (bridge or low-water) from the project 
location and state how far away it is from the proposed location. 

14. Provide the latitude and longitude of the project or a point within the project. If there are several project 
areas, provide a latitude and longitude for each. 

D. Section III: Description of Proposed Activity (line numbers 15 – 29) 

15. Provide a narrative description of the proposed activity including a description of the methods and 
equipment to be used. Attach additional sheets as necessary to provide a full description. Design 
drawings, engineering plans, or restoration plans should be attached, and may be required, depending 
on the project. 

16. How much sedimentary material (in cubic yards) will be disturbed or removed within the streambed 
during each month of the year? If the project is a one-time activity, please indicate that fact and provide 
the maximum volume of material proposed to be disturbed or removed. 

17. How much sedimentary material (in cubic yards) will be disturbed or removed within the streambed 
during each year? 

18. Describe what will be done with all disturbed and removed sedimentary material? [e.g. removed from 
the 100-year floodplain and stabilized with native vegetation, contoured and stabilized within the 
channel, etc.] 

19. How much of the surface area of the waterbody bottom (in square feet) will be disturbed? Include 
access areas and equipment activity areas within the channel. 

20. List the desired start date of permitted disturbance or removal. This date is considered tentative; 
jurisdictional activity is not authorized until a permit is received from TPWD. 

21. How long is the operation proposed to take once it begins? This will be considered in setting the 
expiration date of the permit. The maximum length of a General Permit is one year; the maximum 
length of an Individual Permit is three years. 

22. List any State or Federal listed threatened or endangered species known to be or anticipated to be in 
the project vicinity and whether any might be affected by the project. 

23. List proposed measures that will be taken to avoid or minimize harm to aquatic and riparian habitat 
within the project area during, and after, the proposed project.

E. Section IV: Public Notice and Requirements (line numbers 30 – 36) 

Public notification of a permit application and a subsequent public comment hearing are required. Notice is 
required: 1) in the newspaper of greatest circulation in the county, 2) in the newspaper from the community 
closest to the project, if any, 3) by certified mail to alongshore landowners within one mile upstream and 
downstream of the project, and 4) in the Texas Register. The Public Notice must be approved by TPWD before 
being published or mailed. Coordinate approval with the TPWD Sand and Gravel Program. TPWD will publish 
notice in the Texas Register after receiving documentation that the newspaper and mailed notices have been 
completed. Notice requirements are found in Parks and Wildlife Code §86.003 and 31 Texas Administrative 
Code §69.105. Public comment hearing procedures are governed by 31 TAC § 69.106. 

30. I dentify the newspaper with greatest circulation in the county that will be used for the public notice and 
provide contact information. 

31. I dentify the newspaper, if any, published for the community closest to the proposed project and provide 
contact information. 

32. Prepare and submit a draft public notice using the available template (see below). 

33. Provide the landowner and address list to be used for mailing the public notice to alongshore property 
owners as required by Parks and Wildlife Code chapter 86, §86.003(c)(2). If a parcel of property has 
multiple owners, list all of them. Provide a map, such as from the appraisal district, showing the land 
tracts and ownership for the required mile upstream and downstream from all project areas. 

34. Please be sure to request a U.S. Postal Service Certified Mail Receipt of Mailing for each address and 
provide to TPWD as required by §86.003(c)(2) and Item #35 of the application form. 

35. Following approval of the Public Notice, the applicant must have the notice published in three 
consecutive issues of the newspaper in Item #30 above and in one issue of the newspaper in Item #31 
above, and provide to TPWD copies of publisher’s affidavits or other acceptable documentation of 
publication for all newspaper notices. 

36. If this is a renewal of an existing permit, are there any proposed changes to your permit? If so, please 
describe them.
