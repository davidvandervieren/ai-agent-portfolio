ARTICLE 4.04: OIL AND GAS DRILLING - City of Denver City, TX
It shall be unlawful for any person, firm, or corporation to begin drilling, to begin workovers, or to begin recompletions on producing or injection wells within the corporate limits of the city except as permitted by this article.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(a)
)
Any person, firm, or corporation purposefully in the production of oil and gas must comply with all current rules and regulations of the state railroad commission pertaining to operations in populated areas. Nothing herein shall be construed as inconsistent with such rules and regulations.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(b)
)
The well locations within corporate limits must be fenced and locked while unsupervised. On all production, storage, or transportation equipment, power sources must be locked, and all belts and pulleys on electrical or gas pumping and transportation equipment must be covered by approved guards.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(c)
)
Oil and gas producers on location within the corporate limits can undertake trips in and out of the well bore until 10:00 p.m. Work can start after 6:00 a.m. Work can continue after 10:00 p.m. if company personnel deem it necessary. The location must be locked when work has shut down for the day, or the drilling rig or pulling unit rigged down and moved off the location. All drilling or pulling units must have a H2S monitor and standby safety equipment on location until all work has been completed and the drilling rig or pulling unit has been rigged down.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(d)
)
The injection wellheads shall have a 2,000 psi or greater working pressure. The tubing head shall have studded or flanged side outlets and have at least one flanged valve installed. The injection tree shall have a minimum of 3,000 psi working pressure. The tree connections shall be flanged, studded or clamped and designed according to ANSI or API 6A specifications. The injection tree shall include two master valves and be equipped with an emergency shut-down device (see drawings attached to Ordinance 91-1).
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(e)
)
Addition of new permanent storage facilities (except for replacement of existing facilities) for hydrocarbons or any produced fluids are not permitted within the corporate limits of the city.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(f)
)
On any drilling location, a retainer wall must be built to keep drilling fluids or contaminated fluids on the location. The retainer wall will be designed to reasonably prevent any such fluids from washing off the location.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(g)
)
Oil and gas producers are encouraged to use produced water for injection rather than fresh water inside the corporate limits; this conserves the limited fresh water supply in this area.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(h)
)
X-ray inspection of any welded connection shall be undertaken on all welded pressure piping installed above grade after the adoption hereof.
(
Ordinance 91-1
adopted
3/25/91
;
1998 Code, art. 4.400(i)
)
On permanently plugged wells, all pipe and abandoned equipment shall be removed from the location and the location shall be returned as close as is reasonably possible to its original state within ninety (90) days of abandonment. All abandoned flow lines must be removed or cut off thirty-six (36) inches below ground level, flushed with treated fresh water and plugged at each end.
