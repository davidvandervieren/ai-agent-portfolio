# Form H-9, Certificate of Compliance, Statewide Rule 36 (Hydrogen Sulfide), Online Filing Now Available
The Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC) is encouraging oil and gas operators to file the Form H-9, Certificate of Compliance, Statewide Rule 36 (Hydrogen Sulfide) , online using the RRC Online System at https://webapps.rrc.texas.gov/security/login.do . To view the Certificate of Compliance Statewide Rule 36 User Guide , visit the RRC website at http://webapps.rrc.texas.gov/H9/resources/docs/H-9_User_Guide.pdf .
The Form H-9 is required to certify that an operator has complied or will comply with 16 Texas Administrative Code §3.36 (Statewide Rule 36).
To view the full notice, visit the RRC website.
