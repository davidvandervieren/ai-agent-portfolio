Austin, Texas July 2019 
RAILROAD COMMISSION OF TEXAS 
Oil and Gas Division 
NOTICE TO OIL AND GAS OPERATORS 
Form H-9, Certificate of Compliance, Statewide Rule 36 (Hydrogen Sulfide), 
Online Filing Available 
As part of the Texas Oilfield Relief Initiative, the Railroad Commission of Texas (RRC) is encouraging 
oil and gas operators to file the Form H-9, Certificate of Compliance, Statewide Rule 36 (Hydrogen 
Sulfide), online using the RRC Online System at https://webapps.rrc.texas.gov/security/login.do. To 
view the Certificate of Compliance Statewide Rule 36 User Guide, visit the RRC website at 
http://webapps.rrc.texas.gov/H9/resources/docs/H-9_User_Guide.pdf. 
The Form H-9 is required to certify that an operator has complied or will comply with 16 Texas 
Administrative Code §3.36 (Statewide Rule 36). 
Existing users with accounts in the RRC Online System do not need to designate a new security 
administrator. The organization’s security administrator needs only to assign rights for filing online 
through their administration accounts to grant their users access. If your company needs to set up 
security administrator rights to file online using the RRC Online System, view the instructions on RRC 
website at https://www.rrc.texas.gov/forms/online-filing-at-rrc/. 
While the RRC will continue to accept hard copy filings of the form, operators are highly encouraged 
to file online to save staff time and resources. The form is available for download from the RRC 
website at https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/oil-and-gas-forms/. 
If you have questions regarding this notice, contact the Field Operations department of the Oil and 
Gas Division at OG_FOPS@rrc.texas.gov or 512-463-6838 for assistance. 
Please Forward to the Appropriate Section of Your Company
