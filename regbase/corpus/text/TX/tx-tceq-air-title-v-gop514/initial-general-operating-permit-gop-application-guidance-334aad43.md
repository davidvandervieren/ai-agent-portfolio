Background and Definitions 
• Authorization to Operate (ATO) - Authorizes an owner/operator to operate under a 
general operating permit. 
• General Operating Permit (GOP) - Refers to the documents that contain all permit 
requirements for the industry type. 
• Permit - The documents required to be maintained at a location specified in the 
application. These documents include: the ATO grant letter, a copy of the 
application, and the GOP for which the ATO is being granted. 
A GOP is available for qualifying sites that are similar in terms of operations, emission units, and 
applicable requirements for the following industries: Oil and Gas (GOPs 511 – 514), Municipal 
Solid Waste Landfill (GOP 517) and Air Curtain Incinerator (GOP 518). 
Sites classified as a major source are required to obtain a Federal Operating Permit (FOP). If 
the site meets the qualification criteria of the GOP, then an ATO under the GOP for which the 
site qualifies can be submitted via an initial issuance application. Currently, the major source 
thresholds for a site can be determined by referring to 30 TAC Chapter 122, Subchapter A, 
§122.10 (General Definitions) and the Texas State Implementation Plan website 
www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/sip. 
Additionally, sites at which municipal solid waste (MSW) landfills are operated require a FOP. If 
the qualification criteria are met, an initial issuance application for the applicable GOP can be 
submitted. An initial issuance application needs to be submitted when: 
• S ubject to 40 CFR Part 60 Subpart WWW (Standards of Performance for Municipal 
Solid Waste Landfills) and the design capacity is greater than 2.5 million mega 
grams and 2.5 million cubic meters; 
• Subject to 40 CFR Part 60 Subpart XXX (Standards of Performance for Municipal 
Solid Waste Landfills That Commenced Construction, Reconstruction, or 
Modification After July 1, 2014); 
• S ubject to 40 CFR Part 63 Subpart AAAA (National Emission Standards for 
Hazardous Pollutants: Municipal Solid Waste Landfills); or 
• S ubject to 30 TAC CH113, Subchapter D, Division 1 (Municipal Solid Waste 
Landfills). 
Any air curtain incinerator (ACI) is required to obtain an FOP, regardless of whether the ACI is a 
major or minor source of air pollutants and regardless of whether the ACI is temporary or 
permanent at a site. If the ACI meets the qualification criteria of the ACI GOP, the owner or 
operator can request an ATO under a GOP.

Required Forms and Documents 
• Cover letter - may be used to identify the applicant’s special concerns or requests 
that are not covered by standard application forms 
• Area map 
• Plot plan 
• Process description 
• Process flow diagram 
• Form OP-1 (Site Information Summary) 
• Form OP-CRO1 (Certification By Responsible Official) 
• Form OP-ACPS (Application Compliance Plan and Schedule) - used to indicate the 
compliance status of the site or application area with the applicable regulations. 
• Form OP-REQ1 (Area-Wide Applicability Determination) - ATO applicants only need 
to answer the “starred” (diamond) questions as per form instructions 
• Form OP-PBRSUP (Instructions Permits By Rule Supplemental Table) - used to 
provide supplemental information for all the Permits by Rule (PBRs) under which the 
site or application area is operating 
Potentially Required Forms 
The following forms or documents are needed depending on the site’s characteristics: 
• The Core Data Form - For a new site/area without a Regulated Entity Number (RN) 
or Customer Number (CN), or at a site/area where the Core Data requires changes. 
• Form OP-DEL (Delegation of Responsible Official Authority) - For delegation of 
authority by the Responsible Official to a Duly Authorized Representative(s). The 
form is actually an optional form. A General Operating Permit does not have to have 
a Duly Authorized Representative(s). 
• Form OP-SUM (Individual Unit Summary) - For individual unit summaries and needs 
to be submitted if units with unit specific applicable requirements (UA forms) are 
included in the application. Note that there are no longer any “Grandfathered” units. 
• Unit Attribute forms (UA forms) - Need to be submitted for units that have specific 
applicable requirements. Most likely, oil and gas sites and municipal solid waste 
landfill sites can be covered under relevant UA forms for GOPs. Some of the unit 
attribute forms may not need to be completed as per form instructions (e.g. crude oil 
condensate storage tanks each less than 210,000 gallons capacity in an attainment 
county). Some unit attribute forms may need to be completed to show non-
applicability (e.g. storage tanks; flares for 40 CFR 60 Subpart A, 40 CFR 63 Subpart 
A, and 30 TAC Chapter 111). 
• Form OP-MON (Monitoring Requirements) - If additional monitoring, periodic 
monitoring or compliance assurance monitoring is required to assure compliance 
with applicable requirements. 
• Form OP-REQ3 (Applicable Requirements Summary) - Complete this form to 
establish provisional terms and conditions.
