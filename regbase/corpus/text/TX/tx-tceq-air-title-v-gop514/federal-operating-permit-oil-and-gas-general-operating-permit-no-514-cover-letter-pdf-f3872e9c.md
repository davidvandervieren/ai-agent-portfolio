Federal Operating Permit 
Oil and Gas General Operating Permit No. 514 
This general operating permit may be used for oil and gas sites located in all Texas counties 
except: Aransas, Bexar, Brazoria, Calhoun, Chambers, Collin, Dallas, Denton, El Paso, Ellis, 
Fort Bend, Galveston, Gregg, Hardin, Harris, Jefferson, Johnson, Kaufman, Liberty, Matagorda, 
Montgomery, Nueces, Orange, Parker, Rockwall, San Patricio, Tarrant, Travis, Victoria, Waller, 
and Wise. 
This general operating permit is issued in accordance with and subject to the Texas Clean Air 
Act (TCAA), Chapter 382 of the Texas Health and Safety Code, and Title 30 Texas 
Administrative Code (30 TAC) Chapter 122 (Federal Operating Permits Program). Owners or 
operators of sites, subject to the Federal Operating Permits Program, that qualify for this 
general operating permit may submit an application to the Texas Commission on Environmental 
Quality (TCEQ, commission) for an authorization to operate under this general operating permit. 
This general operating permit does not relieve the permit holder from the responsibility of 
obtaining preconstruction authorization for new or modified facilities in accordance with 30 TAC 
Chapter 116 (Control of Air Pollution by Permits for New Construction or Modification). 
The permit holder shall comply with all applicable terms and conditions codified in this general 
operating permit. Additionally, the permit holder shall comply with any other applicable rules, 
regulations, and/or orders of the commission or of the United States Environmental Protection 
Agency. Any noncompliance with either the permit terms and conditions or the provisional terms 
and conditions, if any, constitutes a violation of the federal Clean Air Act and the TCAA and may 
be grounds for enforcement action. It shall not be a defense in an enforcement action that it 
would have been necessary to halt or reduce the permitted activity in order to comply with the 
permit terms and conditions of this general operating permit. 
The permit holder need not comply with terms and conditions codified in this general operating 
permit that have been replaced by provisional terms and conditions before the granting of a new 
authorization to operate under this general operating permit. A copy of this general operating 
permit, the permit application, and the authorization to operate shall be maintained at the 
location specified in the authorization to operate. 
Permit No. 514 
Issuance Date: October 15, 2025
