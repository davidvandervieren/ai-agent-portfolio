# Planning & Zoning Commission

<!-- heading-path: Planning & Zoning Commission > About -->
## About
The Roosevelt City Planning & Zoning Commission consists of 7 voting members. They are appointed by the Mayor with advice and consent of the City Council from among qualified electors of the city in a manor providing balanced geographic, professional, neighborhood and community interest representation. (Ordinance 2011-366 1 (part))
Roosevelt City Planning and Zoning Department is designed and enacted for the purpose of promoting the health, safety, values, convenience, order, prosperity and welfare of the present and future inhabitants of Roosevelt City. This includes, among other things:
The lessening of congestion in the streets or roads
Securing safety from fire and other dangers
Providing adequate light and air
Classification of land uses and distribution of land development and utilization
Protection of the tax base
Securing economy in governmental expenditures
Fostering industries
Protection of urban development. (Ordinance 2011-366 1 (part)) (Ordinance 79-101 1 - 2 (formerly 17.04.020))

<!-- heading-path: Planning & Zoning Commission > Types of Planning and Zoning Requests -->
## Types of Planning and Zoning Requests
The following issues will be decided by the Planning and Zoning Commission after an application has been filled out by the citizen and approved by the Planning and Zoning Division Manager:
Lot line adjustment/Property boundary adjustment
Master site plan
Conditional Use Permit
Zoning map amendment (rezone)
Application for reimbursement of public improvements
Hearing office appeal
Hearing Officer Variance request
Infrastructure reimbursement agreement
Manufactured home park
Ordinance/general plan amendment
Planned Residential Unit Development (PRUD)
Subdivision preliminary plat
Subdivision, minor
Subdivision vacation/amendment
Time Extension
Subdivision final plat

<!-- heading-path: Planning & Zoning Commission > Meeting With the Planning & Zoning Board -->
## Meeting With the Planning & Zoning Board
An application to meet with the Planning and Zoning Board needs to be filed with the City 14 days prior to the requested meeting date.

<!-- heading-path: Planning & Zoning Commission > Membership Details -->
## Membership Details
The members of the Planning & Zoning Commission shall select from their own members a chairperson and vice chairperson shall adopt rules and regulations for their organization and for the transaction of business and the conduct of their proceedings. Nothing shall prohibit the chairperson or vice chairperson from serving consecutive terms. It is the responsibility of the chairman to call the meeting to order and conduct the business of the meeting and ensure accurate minutes are kept and approved.
