SOURCE INFORMATION 
General Description 
Kinder Morgan Altamont LLC (Kinder Morgan) operates a natural gas compressor station located near 
Altamont, Utah. The station's primary function is to compress and dehydrate field gas. 

Process Description 
Natural gas from the Altamont field enters the site through pipelines. Incoming field gas is sent through a 
slug catcher where free liquids are separated. The free liquids are directed to a gunbarrel tank where the 
free liquids are separated into water and condensate. The separated condensate is directed to two 
condensate tanks and is stored to be removed via truck load-out. The separated water remains in the 
gunbarrel tank and is removed through truck load-out. Overhead vapor from the gunbarrel and condensate 
tanks is sent to a tank flare. 

Overhead gas from the slug catcher is directed to a natural gas manifold connected to three natural 
gas-fired compressor engines. Natural gas for the compressor engines is provided by the Altamont Main 
Gas Plant. Any liquid removed throughout the compression stages is recycled back to the inlet slug 
catcher. Compressed gas is directed to a sulfur scrubbing process. After treatment, the gas is discharged to 
the triethylene glycol (TEG) unit, equipped with a flash tank and still vent condenser, for the removal of 
entrained water. Gas from the dehydration unit flash tank is routed to the facility flare, while still vent 
condenser vapors are routed to an enclosed combustor.

PUBLIC NOTICE STATEMENT 

The NOI for the above-referenced project has been evaluated and has been found to be consistent with the 
requirements of UAC R307. Air pollution producing sources and/or their air control facilities may not be

intent to approve will be published in the Uintah Basin Standard on November 19, 2025. During the

practicable to the location of the source. Any comments received during the public comment period and 
the hearing will be evaluated. The proposed conditions of the AO may be changed as a result of the 
comments received.
