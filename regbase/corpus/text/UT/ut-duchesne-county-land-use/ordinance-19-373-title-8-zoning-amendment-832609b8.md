SECTION 3. Section 8-9-9 of the Duchesne County Code is amended to read: 79 
80 
8-9-9: KEEPING OF LIVESTOCK: 81 
82 
For properties located in the unincorporated portion of the County, the keeping of 83 
livestock is permitted, providing the parcel consists of at least one acre (43,560 square 84 
feet). Adequate fences and corrals shall be required to keep animals off adjacent lands 85 
unless located within a designated open range area. Barns, corrals, pens, sheds and other 86 
structures sheltering animals shall be located a minimum of three feet (3') from a side or 87 
rear property line and thirty feet (30') from the a front property line or a side property 88 
line abutting a street. 89

Section 8-9-17: CANNABIS PRODUCTION ESTABLISHMENTS AND MEDICAL 94 
CANNABIS PHARMACIES: 95 
96 
A. A person may not operate a Cannabis Production Establishment in the County 97 
without a license issued by the assigned State of Utah agency. 98 
99 
B. A person may not operate a Cannabis Production Establishment in the County 100 
that is less than 1,000 feet of a Community Location or within 600 feet of an area 101 
zoned primarily for residential use, as measured from the nearest entrance to the 102 
Cannabis Production Establishment by following the shortest route of ordinary 103 
pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the Community Location or area 104 
zoned primarily for residential use, unless the County Commissioners recommend 105 
in writing that the State waive the community location proximity limit. 106 
107 
C. The county may not deny or revoke a land use permit or business license to 108 
operate a Cannabis Production Establishment on the sole basis that the applicant or 109 
Cannabis Production Establishment violates federal law regarding the legal status 110 
of cannabis. 111 
112 
D. A person may not locate a Medical Cannabis Pharmacy in or within 600 feet of 113 
an area zoned primarily for residential use. 114

SECTION 5. Section 8-13-5-1 of the Duchesne County Code is amended to read: 118 
119 
8-13-5-1: PRODUCED WATER DISPOSAL FACILITIES: 120 
121 
A. Fencing: The site must be fenced to sustain safety, and prevent access by livestock, 122 
wildlife and unauthorized personnel; 123 
124 
B. Compliance With State: Mandatory compliance with state division of oil, gas and 125 
mining (DOGM) general rules, including submittal of the DOGM application and DOGM 126 
approval to the county prior to the receipt of water; 127 
128 
C. Odor Control: Mandatory odor control; 129 
130 
D. Distance Requirements: Must be located a minimum of two (2) miles from any state or 131 
federal highway, city, town, or primary residential use (as determined by the county tax 132 
rolls) and at least one mile from the property line of a parcel containing a primary 133 
residential use, unless written consent to locate closer is obtained from the owner of the 134 
property containing the primary residential use; 135 
136 
E. Number Permitted: Produced water disposal ponds shall be limited by number on each 137 
conditional use permit to the number of ponds that would be under construction within 138 
eighteen (18) months and completed within three (3) years of permit approval, and cannot 139 
be enlarged or modified until the issue is re-presented to the planning commission for a 140 
new conditional use permit and the enlargement or modification is approved; 141 
142 
F. Bond Required: Mandatory bonding or other assurance shall be furnished to the 143 
county, prior to the receipt of water, in the amount of one hundred twenty five percent 144 
(125%) of a contractor's estimate to remove buildings and tanks, reclaim roads and 145 
driving surfaces, remove fencing and revegetate the site to a standard agreed to by the 146 
property owner and the county. The contractor's estimate and bond amount shall be 147 
recalculated every five (5) years. This is in addition to the state division of oil, gas and 148 
mining general rules (R649-9-9), which require bonding to treat or remove waste from 149 
the site and secure the site to prevent future contamination; 150 
151 
F. G. Section 404 Permit Required: Prior to receipt of water, the applicant must provide 152 
the county with evidence of compliance with the section 404 permit process administered 153 
by the U.S. army corps of engineers and the migratory bird treaty act administered by the 154 
U.S. fish and wildlife service; 155 
156 
G. H. Notice Requirements: Notwithstanding the notification requirements of section 8-157 
13-3 of this chapter, mailed notice of the public hearing shall be given to all property 158 
owners within two (2) miles of the parcel proposed for the use; 159 
160 
H. I. On Site Operator: An operator shall be on site at all times for monitoring of the site 161 
during receiving operations. The facility shall be secured against entry when an operator 162 
is not present. 163

SECTION 8. Section 8-16-1 of the Duchesne County Code is amended to read: 301 
302 
8-16-1: ZONING ADMINISTRATOR DECISIONS: 303 
304 
A. Appeal to Board of Adjustment Planning Commission: Decisions of the zoning 305 
administrator in applying the provisions of this title may be appealed to the board of 306 
adjustment Planning Commission by filing an application and all necessary materials 307 
within ten (10) days of the date of the zoning administrator's decision. The only persons 308 
with standing to appeal are the applicant, a board or officer of the county or any person 309 
adversely affected by the decision of the zoning administrator. 310 
311 
B. Notice, Hearing, Decision: Following receipt by the board of adjustment Planning 312 
Commission of an appeal of a decision by the zoning administrator, the board of 313 
adjustment Planning Commission shall give notice and schedule a public hearing. The 314 
appellant shall present all theories of relief to the board of adjustment Planning 315 
Commission, as failure to raise such theories at this stage of appeal precludes 316 
presentation of such theories to district court at later stages. Following the hearing and 317 
the submission of all necessary and relevant information, the board of adjustment 318 
Planning Commission shall render a decision on the appeal. 319
