## 14.04.010 - Uniform Building Code.

code to the Uniform Building Code shall mean the said edition adopted by the Utah

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.04.065 - Wild Land Urban Interface Code. -->
## 14.04.065 - Wild Land Urban Interface Code.
A.
Uintah County adopts the 2006 Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code, published by the
International Code Council, with the following amendments:
1.
Chapter 2 Definitions.
Approved. Acceptable to the building official, fire official, or authority having
jurisdiction.
2.
Chapter 4.
Section 402.2 Individual Structures.
402.2.1 Access. Individual structures hereafter constructed or relocated into or within
the wildland urban interface areas shall be provided with approved fire apparatus
access roads and driveways in accordance with section 403.2.
3.
Section 402.2.2 Water Supply.
Exception:
a. A structure that is no more than 1,000 square feet, with the required defensible
space and without reasonable access to a municipal, or similar, water system.
b. A structure that originally did not exceed 1,000 square feet, when adding an addition
of 25% or less to the total square footage.
4.
Section 403.2 Driveways.
Exceptions:
a. New structures and additions to existing structures on existing approved driveways.
b. Approved driveways through easements less than required driveway width.
5.
Section 403.3 Fire apparatus access roads.
When required, fire apparatus access roads shall be approved roads with a minimum
width of 20 feet (6096 mm) and a clear height of 13 feet 6 inches (4115 mm); shall
be designed to accommodate the loads and turning radii for fire apparatus; and have
a gradient negotiable by the specific fire apparatus normally used at that location
within the jurisdiction. Dead-end roads in excess of 150 feet (45 720 mm) in length
shall be provided with turnarounds as approved by the code official. An all-weather
road surface shall be any surface material acceptable to the code official that would
normally allow the passage of emergency service vehicles to protect structures and
wildlands within the jurisdiction.
Exceptions:
a. Fire apparatus access roads on federal and state land.
b. Fire apparatus access roads through easements less than the required road width.
c. Existing fire apparatus roads.
B.
Authority of Uintah Fire Suppression Special Service District Director. The Uintah
fire suppression special service district director, or his or her designee, is authorized
to administer and enforce this chapter and all ordinances of Uintah County pertaining
to designated wild land urban interface areas. The Uintah fire suppression special
service district director, or his or her designee, shall have the power to render
interpretations of this code and to adopt and enforce rules and supplemental regulations
to clarify the application of its provisions. Such interpretations, rules and regulations
shall be in conformance with the intent and purpose of that code.
C.
Applicable Area. The Wild Land Urban Interface Code of Uintah County is applicable
in all unincorporated, private land designated wild land urban interface areas in
Uintah County, which area shall be depicted in a map entitled "Wild Land Urban Interface
Area" which is adopted hereto and made a part hereof. All other areas of Uintah County
are exempt from the requirements of the Wild Land Urban Interface Code.
D.
Revised Sections. The following sections are revised as indicated:
1.
Section 101, "General" shall include an additional paragraph, to wit; subsection 101.6,
"Conflicts" which shall include the following language:
Where conflicts between this Code and the International Fire Code exist, the International
Fire Code shall apply.
2.
Section 404, "Water Supply" shall include an additional paragraph, to wit; subsection
404.11 "No available water supply", which shall include the following language:
In areas where there is no available water supply, fire sprinklers, as approved by
the code official may be considered as an alternative. As a minimum, fire sprinklers
shall be required in the primary structure, any attached type "U" structures, and
in all exterior eaves, porches, patios, roof extensions, etc., in excess of two feet,
measured horizontally from the exterior wall.
3.
Section 602, "Automatic Fire Sprinkler Systems" shall omit the word "Deleted" and
shall include the following paragraph, to wit:
All structures deemed to be high hazard or extreme hazard may be reduced by one hazard
level when fire sprinklers are installed as approved by the code official. As a minimum
fire sprinklers shall be required in the primary structure, any attached type "U"
structures, and in all exterior eaves, porches, patios, roof extensions, etc.
E.
Place Where Filed. Three copies of the Wild Land Urban Interface Code shall be filed
for use and examination by the public in the administrative offices of the Uintah
Fire Suppression Special Service District and a copy for information purposes shall
be available in the Uintah County Planning and Zoning Office.
F.
Repealer. This section repeals all ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict
herewith.
(Ord. 5-19-2008 O-1 § 3))
(Ord. No. 09-21-2015 O1, § 3, 10-19-2015)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter 14.12 - FLOOD HAZARD AREAS -->
## Chapter 14.12 - FLOOD HAZARD AREAS

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.12.010 - Actions of board of county commissioners. -->
## 14.12.010 - Actions of board of county commissioners.
The board of county commissioners hereby:
A.
Assures the Federal Insurance Administration that it will enact as necessary, and
maintain in force for those areas having flood hazards, adequate land use and control
measures with effective enforcement provisions consistent with the criteria set forth
in Section 1910 of the National Flood Insurance Program Regulations; and
B.
Vests the zoning administrator of the county with the responsibility, authority and
means to:
1.
Delineate or assist the administrator, at his request, in delineating the limits of
the areas having special flood hazards on available local maps of sufficient scale
to identify the location of building sites,
2.
Provide such information as the administrator may request concerning present use and
occupancy of the flood plain,
3.
Cooperate with federal, state and local agencies and private firms which undertake
to study, survey, map and identify the flood plain and cooperate with neighboring
communities with respect to management of adjoining flood plain areas in order to
prevent aggravation of existing hazards,
4.
Submit on the anniversary date of the community's initial eligibility an annual report
to the administrator on the progress made during the past year within the community
in the development and implementation of flood plain management measures;
C.
Appoints the zoning administrator of the county to maintain for public inspection
and to furnish upon request a record of elevations (in relation to mean sea level)
of the lowest floor (including basement) of all new or substantially improved structures
located in the special flood hazard areas. If the lowest floor is below grade on one
or more sides, the elevation of the floor immediately above must also be recorded;
D.
Agrees to take such other official action as may be reasonably necessary to carry
out the objectives of the program.
(Ord. 5-12-75)

## Chapter 14.16 - BUILDING REQUIREMENTS

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.010 - Purpose -->
## 14.16.010 - Purpose
The purpose of this chapter is to provide the community with a clear understanding
of building permits within the unincorporated portions of Uintah County. This chapter
will explain building inspection requirements, expiration of building permits, enforcement
and penalties when they are in noncompliance with county codes and ordinances. This
chapter will help people understand what their rights and responsibilities are when
a building permit is issued to them.
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.020 - Definitions -->
## 14.16.020 - Definitions
"Approved Inspection"- A required inspection or other inspection that is approved
by the Building Official. Inspections that require corrections shall not be considered
an approved inspection.
"Noncompliant building or structure"- Any building or structure that does not comply
with the provisions of this chapter and/or the building codes adopted by Uintah County
Code Chapter 14.04 .
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.030 - Permanent living quarters. -->
## 14.16.030 - Permanent living quarters.
All dwellings on private lots must be permanent structures and comply with the building
codes adopted in Chapter 14.04 in the Uintah County Code. All dwellings must have a perimeter foundation that is
constructed to International Residential Code (IRC) minimum standards as out lined
in Chapter 4 of the IRC, Manufactured homes must comply with Section 17.08.010 "Manufactured Home" of the Uintah County Code. No more than one dwelling may occupy
any one lot except as permitted by Uintah County Code. Nothing with the Uintah County
Code shall be construed to allow a camping trailer, licensed vehicle or trailer, tent,
recreational vehicle, accessory structure, or items as a permanent dwelling. No parcel
can be used for temporary living quarters, unless permitted for such use by the zoning
of the parcel or as specified under a conditional use permit for a campground or a
recreational vehicle park, or as provided for in Chapter 8.16 of the Uintah County Code.
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.040 - Issuing a building permit. -->
## 14.16.040 - Issuing a building permit.
A Uintah County Building Permit shall be issued by the Building Official subject to
the building codes adopted in Chapter 14.04 in the Uintah County Code. A building permit must be obtained prior to the start
of construction. For manufactured or modular homes a building permit must be obtained
prior to moving the unit onto a lot. A mobile home, manufactured home, or modular
home may not be kept or stored on any lot unless a building permit has been issued
for such structure. Approved building plans shall be stamped by the Building Official.
The building permit along with the approved plans must be kept at the property for
which the permit was issued, in a location that they can be accessed by the county.
Items that are not shown or are overlooked on the approved building plans do not justify
building a structure, or any part thereof, that does not comply with the building
codes adopted in Chapter 14.04 in the Uintah County Code. It is the responsibility of the property owner and those
that are hired by the property owner to know and understand the applicable building
codes.
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

## 14.16.050 - Permits contrary to law.

(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.060 - Expiration of a building permit. -->
## 14.16.060 - Expiration of a building permit.
Every building permit issued shall become invalid unless the work authorized by such
permit is commenced within one hundred eighty (180) days after its issuance, or if
the work authorized by such permit is abandoned for a period of one hundred eighty
(180) days. The work shall be considered abandoned unless there is one or more approved
inspection(s) during each one hundred eighty-day period. The Building Official is
authorized to grant, in writing, not more than three extensions of time, for periods
of not more than one hundred eighty (180) days each. The extension shall be requested
in writing and justifiable cause shall be demonstrated.
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009)

<!-- heading-path: Title 14 - BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION > 14.16.070 - Enforcement and penalties. -->
## 14.16.070 - Enforcement and penalties.
Nothing herein shall prevent the county or any other public official or private citizen
from taking such other lawful action as is necessary to prevent or correct any violation
of this title or of the Utah State Code. The following are available to enforce compliance
with the provisions of this chapter:
A.
Criminal action. Any person who willfully violates or fails to comply with any provisions
of this chapter or any provisions of the codes adopted in Chapter 14.04 , or who, having obtained a permit hereunder, shall willfully fail to continue to
comply with the conditions as set forth herein, shall be guilty of a class "B" misdemeanor.
A separate violation is deemed to have occurred with respect to each building or structure
not in compliance with this chapter. Further, each day such violation continues constitutes
a separate offense.
B.
Recording a Certificate of Noncompliant Building or Structure. The Building Official
may cause to be recorded, with the Uintah County Recorder, a certificate of noncompliant
building or structure any time after a Uintah County Code Violation (red tag), listing
the items in violation, has been left in a conspicuous location at the building site.
C.
Within 10 days after a certificate of noncompliant building or structure has been
recorded a letter of notice and order will be served upon the owner of record of the
property in violation. The notice and order may be served upon the owner of record
in one of the following ways: 1) Certified mail or 2) Delivered directly to the owner
of record by an employee of Uintah County. When the notice and order is delivered
by a Uintah County employee, said employee must certify in writing the time and date
of delivery. The notice and order must include: an address and tax id number for the
affected parcel, a list of the violations, what actions must be taken to correct the
violations, a date, of not less than fifteen (15) days from the date of the notice,
for the owner to appeal the notice and order, any fees that are associated with the
violation, notice that a certificate of noncompliant building or structure has been
recorded on the property, and the name and contact information of the Building Official
or designee.
If the property owner appeals the notice and order within the time limit stated in
the notice and order the matter will be taken before the Uintah County Board of Commissioners.
The Uintah County Commission shall have no authority relative to interpretation of
the administrative provisions of the adopted building codes nor shall the Commission
be empowered to waive requirements of the building codes. The owner of record shall
be notified of the time and date of the County Commission Meeting a minimum of three
days prior to the date of the meeting. When the violations have been corrected or
the building has been demolished so that it no longer exists on the property, the
Building Official shall cause to be recorded a new certificate with the County Recorder
certifying that the building has been demolished or all stipulated violations have
been corrected, and that the building is found to be compliant.
(Ord. No. 04-06-2009, O1, § 3, 4-13-2009; Ord. No. 11-14-2022, O2, § 3, 11-28-2022)
