CERTIFICATE OF POSTING 
STATE OF UTAH) SS: 
COUNTY OF EMERY) 

I, Brandy Wagner, do hereby certify that I am the duly appointed, qualified and acting City Recorder of 
Huntington City, State of Utah, and do further certify that the foregoing is a summary copy of 
Ordinance No. 2-2026. 

AN ORDINANCE REPEALING AND REPLACING TITLE 9 OF THE HUNTINGTON CITY CODE; ZONING AND 
SUBDIVISION REGULATIONS 
WHEREAS, the public interest will best be served by maintaining a clear, organized, and updated 
Huntington City Code Book; and 

WHEREAS, Title 9 of the Huntington City Code establishes the zoning and subdivision regulations for 
Huntington City; and 

WHEREAS, the Huntington City Planning Commission has reviewed the proposed revisions, held a duly 
noticed public hearing, and recommended adoption of the revised Title 9 to the Huntington City 
Council; and 

WHEREAS, the Huntington City Council has determined that revisions to Title 9 are necessary to clarify 
regulations, update provisions, and ensure consistency with current planning practices and applicable 
law; 

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the City Council of Huntington City, Utah, as follows: 

Title 9 of the Huntington City Code, consisting of Chapters 9-1 through 9-17, is hereby repealed in its 
entirety and replaced with a newly adopted Title 9 entitled 

“Zoning and Subdivision Regulations,” as set forth in Exhibit A and incorporated by reference. A 
summary of changes is attached as Exhibit B. 

THE FULL ORDINANCE CAN BE VIEWED ON THE PUBLIC NOTICE WEBSITE 
OR AT HUNTINGTON CITY HALL. 

PASSED and posted by unanimous vote of the Huntington City Council at the regularly scheduled 
meeting of the Council on the 18th day of March, 2026. 

I certify that after its passage I caused to be posted a summary of the ordinance in the following 
locations: 

1. Huntington City Hall 2. Utah Public Notice Website 3. www.huntingtonut.com 

Dated this 19th day of March 2026 

SEAL: 

Brandy Wagner, Huntington City Recorder
