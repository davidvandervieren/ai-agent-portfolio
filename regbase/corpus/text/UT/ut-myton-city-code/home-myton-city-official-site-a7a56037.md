# Welcome to Myton City Myton City… A Great Place to Live!
In the Myton City, we are defined less by boundaries on a map than by the sense of shared values our residents hold dear. Small town values, guided growth, preservation of historical, cultural, and natural heritage are just a few of the core principles that makes the Myton City a wonderful place to call home.
