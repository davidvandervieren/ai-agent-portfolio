This Code shall be known as the "Kersey Municipal Code" and it shall be sufficient
to refer to said Code as such in any prosecution for the violation of any provision
thereof or in any proceeding at law or equity. It shall be sufficient to designate
any ordinance adding to, amending, correcting or repealing all or any part or portion
thereof as an addition or amendment to, or as a correction or repeal of, the "Kersey
Municipal Code." Further, reference may be had to the chapters, divisions, articles,
sections and subsections of the "Kersey Municipal Code" and such reference shall apply
to that numbered chapter, division, article, section or subsection as it appears in
the Code.
(Code 1994, § 1-3; Ord. No. 195, § 1, 1993)
Secondary codes may be adopted by reference, as provided by state law.
(Code 1994, § 1-4; Ord. No. 195, § 1, 1993)
All existing ordinances and portions of ordinances of a general and permanent nature
which are inconsistent with any ordinance included in the adoption of this Code, to
the extent of such inconsistency, are hereby repealed as of the effective date of
the ordinance adopting this Code, except as hereinafter provided.
(Code 1994, § 1-5; Ord. No. 195, § 1, 1993)
(a)
The repeal of ordinances and parts of ordinances of a permanent and general nature
by section 1-5 shall not affect any offense committed or act done, any penalty or forfeiture incurred
or any contract, right or obligation established prior to the time said ordinances
and parts of ordinances are repealed.
(b)
When an ordinance that repealed another shall itself be repealed, the previous ordinance
shall not be revived without express words to that effect.
(c)
An ordinance which is in conflict in its entirety with a subsequent ordinance shall
be deemed to be repealed in its entirety, even if no specific repealer is stated.
If an ordinance is only partially in conflict with a subsequent ordinance, only the
portion of the previous ordinance in conflict with the subsequent ordinance shall
be deemed to have been repealed, even if a specific repealer is not stated.

The following words, terms and phrases, whenever used in the ordinances of the town
and/or any codification of the same, shall be construed as defined in this section,
unless a different meaning is intended from the context or unless a different meaning
is specifically defined and more particularly directed to the use of such words or
phrases:
Board of trustees. The term "board of trustees" means the board of trustees of the Town of Kersey.
Campground. The term "campground" means an area used for temporary placement and occupancy of
recreational vehicles or camping tents.
Camping. The term "camping" means a recreational activity involving the spending of up to
seven consecutive nights in a tent, primitive structure, travel trailer or recreational
vehicle at a campsite.
Code. The term "Code" or "this Code" means the Kersey Municipal Code of the Town of Kersey,
Colorado, as designated in section 1-1 .
County. The term "county" means the County of Weld, Colorado.
C.R.S. The abbreviation "C.R.S." means Colorado Revised Statutes, including all amendments
thereto.
Law. The term "law" denotes applicable federal law, the constitution and statutes of the
state, the ordinances and resolutions of the town and, when appropriate, any and all
rules and regulations which may be promulgated thereunder.
May. The term "may" is permissive.
Misdemeanor. The term "misdemeanor" means and is to be construed as meaning a violation of this
Code and is not intended to mean a crime or criminal conduct.
Month. The term "month" means a calendar month.
Must; shall. The terms "must" and "shall" are both mandatory.
Oath. The term "oath" shall be construed to include an affirmation or declaration in all
cases in which, by law, an affirmation may be substituted for an oath, and in such
cases the words swear and sworn shall be equivalent to the words affirm and affirmed.
Or, and. The term "or" may be read "and," and the term "and" may be read "or," if the sense
requires it.
Ordinance. The term "ordinance" means a law of the town; provided that a temporary or special
law, administrative action, order or directive may be in the form of a resolution.
Owner. The term "owner," applied to a building or land, includes any part owner, joint owner,
tenant in common, joint tenant or tenant by the entirety, of the whole or a part of
such building or land.
Person. The term "person" means natural person, joint venture, joint stock company, partnership,
association, club, company, corporation, business, trust or organization and any other
group acting as a unit, or the manager, lessee, agent, servant, officer or employee
of any of them.
Personal property. The term "personal property" includes money, goods, chattels, things in action and
evidences of debt.
Proceeding; following. The terms "preceding" and "following" mean next before and next after, respectively.
Property. The term "property" includes real and personal property.
Real property. The term "real property" includes lands, tenements and hereditaments.
Sidewalk. The term "sidewalk" means that portion of a street between the curbline and the adjacent
property line intended for the use of pedestrians.
State. The term "state" means the State of Colorado.
Street. The term "street" includes all streets, highways, avenues, lanes, alleys, courts,
places, squares, curbs or other public ways in the town which have been or may hereafter
be dedicated and open to public use, or such other public property so designated in
any law of this state.
Tenant; occupant. The terms "tenant" and "occupant," applied to a building or land, includes any person
who occupies all or a part of such building or land, whether alone or with others.
Town. The term "town" means the Town of Kersey, Colorado, or the area within the territorial
limits of the Town of Kersey, and such territory outside of the town over which the
town has jurisdiction or control by virtue of any constitutional or statutory provision.
Written; in writing. The terms "written" and "in writing" include printed, typewritten, mimeographed or
multigraphed, or otherwise reproduced in permanent visible form.
Year. The term "year" means a calendar year.

(Code 1994, § 1-75; Ord. No. 195, § 1, 1993)
Whenever in this Code any act or omission is made unlawful, it is also unlawful to
cause, allow, permit, aid, abet or suffer such unlawful act or omission. Concealing
or in any manner aiding in the concealing of any unlawful act or omission is similarly
unlawful.
(Code 1994, § 1-76; Ord. No. 195, § 1, 1993)
(a)
(1)
In any case where a fine or penalty is unpaid or the alleged violation (ticket) has
not been successfully protested/appealed within 35 days of issuance, the municipal
court clerk shall immediately declare it to be delinquent and the amount due shall
then be the amount established for delinquent assessments by judicial order, by this
Code, or by other ordinance of the board of trustees
(2)
The defendant shall have 30 days in which to pay the delinquent penalty assessment
amount or appear before the municipal court that month to protest the alleged violation.
In cases where a person makes payment after the 30-day time limit, the amount paid
will be considered as a partial payment until paid in full.
(b)
In any case where a delinquent violation notice assessment is unpaid or the alleged
charge has not been successfully protested or appealed as described above, the clerk
of the municipal court shall pursue collection of the amount due from the defendant.
(c)
The clerk of the municipal court shall adopt procedures for the collection of fines/penalties
which may include the engaging of collection services. The defendant shall additionally
pay any associated collection costs, fees, including reasonable attorney fees, and/or
commissions for these collection services.
