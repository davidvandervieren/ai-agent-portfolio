(a)
General. The following restrictions shall apply to all persons or properties in or out of
the town using water supplied by the town through a water tap:
(1)
Between March 1 and October 31 of each year, watering or irrigation of landscaped
areas, vegetable gardens, perennial flower gardens, trees and shrubs with hose-end
sprinklers or automatic irrigation systems shall be prohibited between the hours of
10:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. each day.
(2)
There shall be no water use restrictions between November 1 of each year and the last
day of February the following year.
(b)
Other sections unaffected. All other water restrictions or limitations as imposed by prior ordinances shall
remain unaffected by this section.
(c)
Penalties. The minimum penalty for violation of this section shall be as follows:
(1)
The first offense shall be a minimum fine of $100.00.
(2)
The second offense shall be a minimum fine of $200.00.
(3)
In addition to any penalty imposed by the court pursuant to this section and section 13-351 , a flow restrictor may be placed upon the property or water service may be terminated
as may be determined by the board of trustees.
(Code 1994, § 13-71; Ord. No. 172, § 1, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05,
§ 1; Ord. No. 2005-O06, § 1; Ord. No. 2005-O13, §§ 1—3; Ord. No. 2010-O02, § 1)
The Kersey Mobile Home Park shall be subject to the regulations imposed upon the mobile
home park by the Central Weld County Water District and is not subject to the requirements
of this article.
(Code 1994, § 13-72; Ord. No. 172, § 2, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1, 2003; Ord. No.
2003-O05, § 1, 2003)
Owners of property who use well water for sprinkling and who are sprinkling on days
inconsistent with this article shall place a sign in a conspicuous place on their
property advising authorities that well water is being used. Placing a sign in accordance
with this section fraudulently and when municipal water is in fact being used for
the sprinkling of lawns, gardens or trees shall be a violation of this article.
(Code 1994, § 13-73; Ord. No. 172, § 3, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1, 2003; Ord. No.
2003-O05, § 1)
The sale of water to non-tapholders shall only occur when such non-tapholder has obtained
a permit to purchase water from the town manager. The town manager shall issue such
a permit only when the town manager is satisfied that the sale of water will be properly
metered or that some other method of measuring the water taken is established. Water
sold to a non-tapholder shall be sold at the rate as may be set forth in the town's
adopted fee schedule.
(Code 1994, § 13-74; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05, § 1)
The town clerk or other person designated by the board of trustees may grant an exception
to alter the restrictions set forth in this article for individuals or businesses
who may own lots, common areas or grounds with both odd- and even-numbered addresses
and which are served by a single sprinkler system. The town clerk or other authority
designated by the board of trustees may also grant a permit excepting such applicant
from the requirements of this article for persons who have newly seeded or sodded
lawns. Such permits shall last for a period of two months or less and sprinkling pursuant
to such permit shall be allowed on each day of the month and at any time during the
24-hour period. Any person obtaining such a permit shall display the permit in a door
window or window closest to the door wherein the address of the premises is located.
(Code 1994, § 13-75; Ord. No. 172, § 4, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05,
§ 1)
The board of trustees may, by resolution, further limit the hours or days of sprinkling
or irrigation, or may entirely prohibit the use of water for lawn or landscape irrigation
purposes in case of failure of the water system or any portion thereof or for reason
of other shortage of water supply and, in the event such a resolution is adopted by
the board of trustees, any watering in violation of such a resolution shall be a violation
of this article.
(Code 1994, § 13-76; Ord. No. 172, § 5, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05,
§ 1)
It is unlawful for any person to use municipal water for sprinkling during any fire
within the town or while the fire protection district is using municipal water for
firefighting purposes.
(Code 1994, § 13-77; Ord. No. 172, § 7, 1986; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05,
§ 1)
(a)
Use of water to wash a motor vehicle, motor bike, boat, trailer, airplane or other
vehicle is prohibited except on designated watering days and designated watering times
established in section 13-342 . Such washing shall be done only with a hand-held bucket or hand-held hose equipped
with a positive shut-off nozzle for quick rinses. When hand washing of vehicles is
performed, residents are encouraged, when appropriate, to wash their vehicles on landscaped
areas. Vehicle washing may be done at any time on the immediate premises of a commercial
carwash or commercial service station. Washing of vehicles is exempt from this article
when washing is necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the public as in the
washing of garbage trucks and vehicles used to transport food and perishables.
(b)
Use of water to fill, refill or add to any indoor or outdoor swimming pools, wading
pools, Jacuzzi-type pools or hot tubs is prohibited, except on designated watering
days and designated watering times established in section 13-342 .
(c)
Use of water to wash the exterior of residential and commercial structures is prohibited,
except on designated watering days and designated watering times established in section 13-342 .
(Code 1994, § 13-78; Ord. No. 2002-07, § 1; Ord. No. 2002-10, §§ 1, 5; Ord. No. 2003-O01,
§ 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05, § 1)
The following uses of water are defined as nonessential and are prohibited:
(1)
Washing down any sidewalks, walkways, driveways, parking lots or other hard-surfaced
areas, except for commercial areas, when necessary to maintain public health, safety
and welfare.
(2)
Flushing street gutters or permitting water to run or accumulate in any gutter or
street. This subsection shall not apply to the use of water for cleaning of roof gutters
and downspouts, when such cleaning is done with a positive shut-off nozzle.
(3)
Failure to control leaks within a reasonable time after having been given notice directing
repair of such leaks.
(Code 1994, § 13-79; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05, § 1)
(a)
It shall be unlawful for any person to violate any provisions of this article.
(b)
Unless otherwise stated, any person convicted of violating any provision of this article
or who shall fail to comply with any of the provisions of this article shall, upon
conviction thereof, be punished in accordance with section 1-95 .
(Code 1994, § 13-80; Ord. No. 2003-O01, § 1; Ord. No. 2003-O05, § 4)
There is hereby imposed upon all customers including residential, commercial, and
industrial customers of the town water utility, rates as defined and adopted in the
adopted fee schedule approved contemporaneously with that year's budget to include
water rates based on the cumulative monthly use per each individual water meter.
(Ord. No. 2015-O04, § 2, 5-12-2015; Ord. No. 2017-0016, § 2, 11-14-2017)
Customers of the town water utility shall in each calendar year be eligible to use
water based on the following criteria:
(1)
Residential water customers of the town who were customers on January 1, 2015, are
presumed to have provided to the town one unit of C-BT water unless the town records
reflect a greater amount or the user provides clear and convincing evidence showing
a greater amount of raw water has been dedicated to the town.
(2)
Any residential customer, who uses water obtained through the town shall be subject
to the rates established in that year's current fee schedule based on the number of
cumulative gallons used per monthly cyclic reading as recorded by the town.
(3)
The water rates established in the fee schedule shall be determined by the town manager
who receives information and advice from multiple sources including, but not limited
to, the town engineer, Northern Water Conservancy District, Central Weld Water, Weld
County, and the State of Colorado. These rates, along with other fees, are subject
to approval by the town board and shall be included in the current adopted fee schedule.
The cash in lieu price for C-BT shall also be determined in this same manner and included
in the current adopted fee schedule.
(Ord. No. 2015-O04, § 3, 5-12-2015; Ord. No. 2017-0016, § 3, 11-14-2017)
All commercial, industrial and nonresidential users shall in each calendar year be
eligible to use water based on the following standards:
(1)
Commercial, industrial and nonresidential water customers of the town who were customers
on January 1, 2015, are presumed to have provided to the town one unit of C-BT water
unless the town records reflect a greater amount or user provides clear and convincing
evidence showing a greater amount of raw water has been transferred to the town.
(2)
Any commercial, industrial, or any other nonresidential customers who use more than
the historical average of .60 percent allotment per unit of C-BT dedicated to the
town, shall dedicate an additional unit or additional units of C-BT raw water to the
town. The Town shall provide commercial, industrial or nonresidential customers written
notice of the need to provide additional units of C-BT water. If the additional unit
of C-BT is not provided to the town within 60 days after notice is provided to the
customer, water service may be terminated. The 60 day termination may be extended
by action of the board of trustees or the requirement may be waived upon showing that
the use was unusual and that use in excess of the allowed amount is unlikely to occur
again. The requirement to provide an additional unit of C-BT may be met through a
cash in lieu payment to the town per the town's currently adopted fee schedule. All
agreements with commercial users made prior to adoption of this article shall remain
in effect but subject to future regulatory changes as approved by the town board.
(3)
Any commercial/industrial user, who uses water obtained through the town shall be
subject to the rates established in that year's current adopted fee schedule based
on the number of cumulative gallons used per monthly cyclic reading as recorded by
the town.
(4)
The water rates established in the adopted fee schedule shall be determined by the
town manager who receives information and advice from multiple sources including,
but not limited to the town engineer, Northern Water Conservancy District, Central
Weld Water, Weld County, and the State of Colorado. These rates, along with other
fees, are subject to approval by the town board and shall be included in the current
adopted fee schedule. The cash in lieu price for C-BT shall also be determined in
this same manner and included in the current adopted fee schedule.
(Ord. No. 2015-O04, § 4, 5-12-2015; Ord. No. 2017-0016, § 4, 11-14-2017)
Nothing in this article shall be construed to modify any other existing adopted fee
schedule or ordinance. The rates imposed by this article shall be charged in addition
to all fees, rates, late fees, or impact fees imposed by any other ordinance or resolution
of the town. This article shall not affect any other ordinance or resolution authorizing
termination of service or any ordinance or statute regarding collection of water service
fees and the fees herein set forth may be collected as any other delinquent water
fees and may be collected by certification to the county treasurer for collection
as taxes pursuant to state law. The rate structure herein set forth may be modified
by the board of trustees in the town's adopted fee schedule. All C-BT units of water
dedicated to the town shall become owned by and property of the town.
(Ord. No. 2015-O04, § 5, 5-12-2015; Ord. No. 2017-0016, § 5, 11-14-2017)
The raw water tiered structure set forth in the adopted fee schedule is based upon
several factors including the historical average allotment provided by the Northern
Colorado Water Conservancy District, the average cumulative consumptive water use
specific to the town, and information provided to the town indicating water availability.
The board of trustees may by resolution modify the fee or the gallons herein set forth
for the year in which such lower or higher allotment is provided by the Northern Colorado
Water Conservancy District.
(Ord. No. 2015-O04, § 6, 5-12-2015; Ord. No. 2017-0016, § 6, 11-14-2017)
