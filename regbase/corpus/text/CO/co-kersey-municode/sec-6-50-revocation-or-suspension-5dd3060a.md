The town board finds that certain areas within the town are zoned to allow for adult
enterprise. The following shall apply to adult enterprise:
(1)
Regulation of adult enterprise protects and preserves the health, safety and welfare
of the patrons of such enterprise, as well as the citizenry.
(2)
Regulation of adult enterprise furthers substantial governmental interests and is
necessary because, in the absence of such regulation, significant criminal activity
has historically and regularly occurred. This history of criminal activity has included
prostitution, narcotics and liquor law violations, violent crimes against persons
and property crimes.
(3)
Adult enterprise are frequently used for unlawful and unhealthful sexual activities,
including prostitution and sexual liaisons of a casual nature.
(4)
The concern over sexually transmitted diseases, including AIDS, is a legitimate health
concern of the town which demands reasonable regulation of adult enterprise in order
to protect the health and well-being of the citizens.
(5)
Adult enterprise have a deleterious effect on both neighboring enterprise and surrounding
residential areas, causing an increase in crime and a decrease in property values.
(6)
It is recognized that adult enterprise have serious objectionable characteristics,
particularly when they are located in close proximity to each other, thereby contributing
to urban blight and downgrading the quality of life in the adjacent area.
(7)
Restricted hours of operation will further prevent the adverse secondary effects of
adult enterprise.
(8)
The town board desires to minimize and control these adverse effects and thereby protect
the health, safety and welfare of the citizens; preserve the quality of life; preserve
the property values and character of surrounding neighborhoods; deter the spread of
urban blight; and protect the citizens from increased crime.

The intent of this section is to regulate adult enterprise to:
(1)
Promote the health, safety, morals and general welfare of the citizens of Kersey;
(2)
Establish reasonable and uniform regulations to prevent any deleterious location and
concentration of adult enterprise within the town; and
(3)
Reducing or eliminating the adverse secondary effects from such adult enterprise.
(4)
The provisions of this section have neither the intent nor effect of imposing a limitation
or restriction on the content of any constitutionally protected communicative materials,
including sexually oriented materials.
(5)
Neither is it the intent nor effect of this section to condone or legitimize the distribution
of obscene material.

The following provisions shall apply to an adult arcade, adult bookstore, adult novelty
store, adult video store, adult business, adult cabaret, adult entertainment establishment,
adult motel, and adult motion picture theater (collectively hereafter referred to
as "such adult enterprises"):
(1)
Age restriction. No one under 18 years of age shall be admitted to such adult enterprises, nor shall
any employee, agent or independent contractor working on the premises of such adult
enterprises be under 18 years of age.
(2)
Hours and days of operation. Such adult enterprises shall be permitted to be open only between the hours of 7:00
a.m. and 12:00 midnight, Monday through Saturday.
(3)
Buffers. Such adult enterprises shall be adequately buffered through the use of façade treatment,
landscaping, and/or fencing to minimize adverse impacts on commercial or residential
uses, parks, churches, community centers, recreational centers, schools, or childcare
facilities which are in proximity to, or within sight distance of, such adult enterprises.
(4)
Lighting and signs. All outside lighting and signs of such adult enterprises shall be arranged, shielded,
and restricted so as to prevent adverse impacts to, and any nuisance for, adjacent
streets, commercial or residential uses, parks, churches, community centers, recreational
centers, schools, or childcare facilities.
(5)
Location restrictions. No such adult enterprises shall be operated or maintained within 1,000 feet of the
following uses measured in a straight line, without regard to intervening structures
or objects, from the closest property line of the use to the property line of such
adult enterprise:
a.
Schools;
b.
Property zoned for residential use;
c.
Community centers;
d.
Recreational centers;
e.
Churches;
f.
Parks;
g.
Any such adult enterprise in existence.
(6)
Site limits. Not more than one such adult enterprise shall be operated or maintained in the same
building, structure, or portion thereof.
