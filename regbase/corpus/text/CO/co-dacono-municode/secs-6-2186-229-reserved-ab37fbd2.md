Editor's note— Ord. No. 960 , §1, adopted April 8, 2024, repealed art. 8, §§ 6-210—6-24 and enacted a new art.
8 as set out herein. Former art. 8 pertained to outdoor pushcarts, mobile vending/food
carts and kiosks and derived from Ord. 486 §1, adopted 1997.
For the purposes of this Article, the following terms shall have the meanings as indicated,
unless the context otherwise requires:
City Clerk means the Dacono City Clerk or the Clerk's designee.
Mobile food truck means a retail food establishment that is not intended to be permanent and is a motorized
wheeled vehicle, or a trailer that is licensed for use on public roadways, designed
and equipped to serve food and beverages, operating in either a static or transitory
location and serving the public at times pursuant to the provisions of this Article.
Mobile food vendor means any person who sells food and/or beverages from a mobile food truck or pushcart.
Pushcart means a retail food establishment that is not a motorized vehicle, limited to serving
commissary prepared or prepacked food or that is commercially designed and approved
to handle food preparation and service.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a mobile food truck or pushcart within
the City limits without first having obtained a food truck license pursuant to this
Article.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
(a)
When issuing a food truck license, the City Clerk may include any conditions necessary
to protect public health, safety, or welfare, or that of adjacent property owners.
(b)
In addition, all mobile food truck licenses shall be subject to the following conditions:
(1)
Only food, nonalcoholic beverages, and branded merchandise specific to the vendor
may be sold.
(2)
Compliance with all local ordinances, including those related to lighting, noise,
and signage.
(3)
Provision of adequate parking for customers, if applicable.
(4)
Removal of grease or other waste in appropriate containers.
(5)
Reporting and paying all current applicable taxes.
(6)
Provision of an insurance policy, a copy of which shall be furnished with the vendor's
food truck license application.
(7)
Food truck licenses are not transferrable or assignable.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
Mobile food trucks and pushcarts, once licensed by the City, are permitted to operate
in the following areas within the City:
(a)
Nonresidential areas (private property).
(1)
A mobile food vendor shall obtain written consent from any owner of private property
on which the mobile food truck or pushcart intends to operate.
(2)
A mobile food truck or pushcart shall comply with all building setback requirements
in the underlying zone district.
(3)
A mobile food truck or pushcart shall not park in any ADA accessible parking spaces.
(4)
A mobile food truck or pushcart must be parked and operated on an improved surface,
as defined in Section 16-280 of this Code, outside of the public right-of-way. In the case of an active construction
site where an improved surface may be unavailable or unbuilt, the food truck or pushcart
shall be parked on a material that minimizes the tracking of dirt onto the street
and that has been approved by the City.
(5)
A mobile food truck or pushcart that occupies parking spaces must ensure that the
parking spaces that remain unobstructed accommodate the off-street parking requirements
for the principal use of the property pursuant to Section 16-280 of this Code.
(b)
City-owned property (excluding right-of-way).
(1)
A mobile food truck or pushcart shall not be operated on any-City owned property unless
authorized pursuant to a license agreement signed by the City Clerk and mobile food
vendor. Separate fees for use of City property may apply as set by resolution.
(c)
Public right-of-way.
(1)
A mobile food truck or pushcart shall not occupy any portion of the public right-of-way,
including any public on-street parking spaces, while in operation unless the street
has been closed for a special event.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
(a)
A mobile food vendor must keep the sidewalks, roadways, and other spaces adjacent
to their location clean and free of refuse of any kind generated from the operation
of their business. Any trash container maintained by a mobile food vendor shall be
removed by the operator at the close of business and in accordance with any and all
State regulations and any applicable provisions of the Code.
(b)
While not in use, a maximum of one (1) mobile food truck or one (1) pushcart may be
stored outside on a property used for a one-family dwelling so long as it is parked
on an improved surface, as defined in Section 8-20 of this Code, and not on the public right-of-way.
(c)
Additional structures, canopies, tables, or chairs associated with operation of a
mobile food truck or pushcart are prohibited unless otherwise expressly permitted
by the food truck license issued by the City.
(d)
Signage shall be permanently affixed to the vehicle. During hours of operation, a
mobile food vendor may place one (1) menu or sandwich board not to exceed six (6)
square feet on site.
(e)
Prior to operation of a mobile food truck or pushcart within a multi-tenant shopping
center, the property owner shall provide written notice to the tenants of such shopping
center.
(f)
Mobile food trucks or pushcart operators shall not obstruct pedestrian or bicycle
access/passage and must not obstruct parking lot circulation or impede traffic flow.
(g)
Mobile food trucks and pushcarts, including any structures, canopies, table or chairs
allowed pursuant to subsection (c) of this section, must be removed from any site
at the end of each business day unless otherwise expressly permitted by the food truck
license issued by the City.
(h)
A mobile food vendor shall obey all parking and traffic laws.
(i)
No vehicle queuing shall be permitted at a mobile food cart or pushcart. Further,
no such food truck or pushcart shall operated as a drive-thru service.
(j)
No odor from the food truck or pushcart shall be detectable on neighboring properties.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
Every person seeking a food truck license shall apply to the City and shall provide
the following information:
(a)
The name and residence of the applicant. If the applicant is not a natural person,
then all documents related to the creation and maintenance of the entity such as articles
of incorporation and any similar relevant documents shall be included.
(b)
A valid driver's license.
(c)
A description of the food and beverage items proposed for sale.
(d)
City and State sales tax numbers or certificate of sales tax exemption.
(e)
Current Weld County mobile food unit retail food establishment license.
(f)
A sanitation management plan, demonstrating that trash and debris will be appropriately
managed such that there is no accumulation on site.
(g)
Proof of a valid insurance policy that provides minimum liability coverage of five
hundred thousand dollars ($500,000.00) per food truck or fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00)
per pushcart. The City shall be named as an additional insured if the food truck or
pushcart will operate on City-owed property.
(h)
Any such additional information deemed necessary by City Clerk in determining whether
to issue the license and under what conditions.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
(a)
No application will be processed until the City Clerk determines that it is complete.
Upon receipt of a complete application, the City may forward the application to any
pertinent internal and external agencies for review and comment.
(b)
The City Clerk shall be responsible for granting or denying a food truck license.
(c)
The food truck license shall be valid for up to one (1) year until December 31 of
each calendar year.
(d)
The annual food truck license fees under this Article shall be set by resolution of
the City Council.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
(a)
Upon seven (7) days' written notice to a licensee stating the contemplated action
and in general the grounds therefor, and after a reasonable opportunity to be heard,
the City Clerk may suspend or revoke a food truck license if any of the following
have occurred:
(1)
Failure to remit any sales and use tax when due.
(2)
Failure to operate the food truck or pushcart in compliance with the terms and conditions
of the license or this Chapter.
(3)
Failure to comply with any other law, rule or regulation applicable to the operation
of the food truck or pushcart.
(4)
Operation of the food truck or pushcart in a manner that endangers public health,
safety or welfare.
(5)
There are insufficient parking spaces to accommodate the customers of the food truck
or pushcart.
(b)
The City Clerk may refer any such decision to a hearing officer for a determination
under this Section.
( Ord. No. 960 , §1, 2024)
