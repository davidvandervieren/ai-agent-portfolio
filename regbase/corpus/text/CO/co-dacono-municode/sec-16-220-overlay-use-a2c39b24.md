This district is to be used as an overlay to any other zone where geological and flood
prone areas exist, as illustrated by an overlay map.
(Ord. 232 §1, 1980)
Permitted uses are: Parks and open space, and any use set forth in the existing zone,
provided that the hazard has been mitigated to the satisfaction of the Planning Commission
and the City Council, and by Special Use Permit only when that legislation is enacted.
(Ord. 232 §2, 1980)
