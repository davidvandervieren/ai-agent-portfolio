As used in this Article:
Officer means the Municipal Judge, the City Manager, the City Attorney, the Police Chief,
the City Clerk, the Director of Finance and such other persons designated as officers
of the City by the Home Rule Charter or by the ordinances of the City.
(Prior code 2.08.010; Ord. 349 §1, 1988; Ord. 995 §3, 2025)
The appointment or removal of any officer of the City shall be by a vote of a majority
of the entire City Council.
(Prior code 2.08.010; Ord. 349 §1, 1988)
Except as otherwise specifically required by the state statutes, each officer of the
City shall serve at the pleasure of the City Council.
(Prior code 2.08.020; Ord. 349 §1, 1988)
(a)
Each officer of the City shall have the powers and shall perform the duties as provided
for in the state statutes or as provided by ordinance, resolution or motion of the
City Council.
(b)
The Clerk shall be authorized to designate a clerk pro tem, who shall perform the
duties of the City Clerk during the Clerk's absence or inability to act. Such designation
shall be in writing and shall continue in effect until written designation of an alternate
clerk pro tem is made by the Clerk, or until the designation is revoked by the Clerk
or the City Council.
(Prior code 2.08.030; Ord. 349 §1, 1988; Ord. No. 829 , § 1, 6-26-2017)
The compensation of each officer of the City shall be established by resolution of
the City Council.
(Prior code 2.08.040; Ord. 349 §1, 1988)
