As used in this Chapter, the following terms shall have the following meanings, unless
the context clearly indicates otherwise. Terms used but not defined herein shall have
their common meanings, unless the context clearly indicated otherwise.
Acceptable water rights shall mean water rights acceptable to the City pursuant to the applicable criteria
set forth in this Chapter 13 and deliverable to a location acceptable to the City.
Accessory dwelling unit shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-600 (a).
Apartments shall mean multi-family dwelling units wherein said units are rented and are not
individually owned.
Building shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-28 of this Code.
CBT Unit refers to a contractual interest in a right to receive water under an allotment contract
in the Colorado-Big Thompson Project from the Northern Colorado Water Conservancy
District, which interests are allocated based on units. The City shall give a credit
of six-tenths (0.6) acre feet per CBT Unit for all purposes in this Chapter.
City Manager shall mean the City Manager or his or her designee.
City water service or water service shall mean the provision of potable and non-potable water provided through the City
water system under the terms of this Chapter.
City water system shall mean all water and water rights, waterworks, water mains, water lines, equipment
and appurtenances thereto, and machinery and supplies used by the City to supply potable
water and non-potable water.
Condominiums shall mean multi-family dwelling units wherein said units may be individually owned
or intended to be individually owned, but the lands on which said units are located
are not individually owned.
Conveyance of acceptable water rights shall mean the transfer of legal title of acceptable water rights to the City pursuant
to the terms of this Chapter.
Dedicate or dedication shall mean a conveyance by the owner of an interest in land or water rights to the
City for some public use, which interest has been accepted by the City for such use
on behalf of the public, including without limitation the dedication of water rights
for a particular development within the City. The water rights dedication requirements
set forth in this Chapter apply to either or both potable and non-potable water supplies.
Development shall mean any change in the intensity of use of land, such as an increase in the
number of dwelling units in a structure or on a tract of land, a material increase
in the intensity and impacts of a development, the installation of landscaping within
a public right-of-way when installed in connection with the development of adjacent
property, and any other man-made change to improved or unimproved real estate which
requires additional water resources.
Dwelling, one-family shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-34 of this Code.
Dwelling, two-family shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-35 of this Code.
Dwelling, multiple-family shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-36 of this Code.
Dwelling unit shall have the meaning set forth in Section 16-37 of this Code.
Fees in lieu of water dedication shall mean the payment of fees to the City for the purpose of obtaining water rights
for all beneficial uses and any associated infrastructure, as deemed appropriate by
the City. Fees in lieu of water dedication are separate and distinct fees from water
tap fees.
Irrigation shall mean the use of water for application to plants, grass, and all other vegetation
on lawns, gardens, pastures, parks, greenbelts, golf courses, open space, or any other
type of irrigated land use.
Meter or water meter shall mean a device installed on the City water system that measures the volume of
water used by a water user.
Non-potable irrigation system shall mean any system of ditches, piping, sprinklers, or other infrastructure used
for the delivery of non-potable water for irrigation purposes and that is separate
from the potable water system. Non-potable irrigation system does not refer to a gray
water system or any other system designed or intended to implement the reuse of water
provided via the potable water system.
Non-potable water shall mean any water which does not conform to drinking water quality standards.
Person shall mean any individual person, corporation, developer, subdivider, property owner,
or any other entity.
Potable water shall mean any water which conforms to drinking water quality standards.
Record owner or owner shall mean the person, corporation or other entity holding title to property. However,
if some other person, corporation or other entity provides proof satisfactory to the
City that such other person, corporation or other entity is serving as the bona fide
management agent of the record owner pursuant to a written agreement with the record
owner, the City may grant written permission to such other person, corporation or
other entity to perform some or all of the responsibilities of the record owner provided
for in this Chapter.
Service line shall mean the water delivery pipeline running from the water meter to the property
served.
Subdivider or developer shall have the meaning set forth in Section 17-31 of this Code.
Subdivision shall have the meaning set forth in Section 17-32 of this Code.
Sufficient priority shall mean that a water right has a date as of which it is entitled to use water
which is sufficiently senior in relation to other water rights deriving their supply
from the same source such that it may reasonably be expected to provide a dependable
water supply for the requirements of this Chapter. Factors to be considered by the
City in making this determination shall include, but are not limited to, the appropriation
date and adjudication date of the water right, the decreed use(s), the historical
use of the water right, the physical flow available, and the administrative practices
of the Division of Water Resources.
Supplemental irrigation water shall mean additional potable water which will be required for irrigation at times
when water is not available through a non-potable irrigation system.
Supply line shall mean the water delivery pipeline running from the City water main to the point
of connection with the water meter for the property.
Tap or water tap shall mean the point of connection of a supply line to the City water system.
Water tap application shall mean an application seeking the right to tap and connect to the City water
main for the purpose of taking and using water from the City water system.
Water right shall mean a decreed right to use the waters of the State in accordance with its
priority by reason of the appropriation of the same, including, but not limited to,
appropriations for direct flow, groundwater, and storage water rights. Water right
shall also be used in the context of this Chapter to include allotment contracts with
the Northern Colorado Water Conservancy District and its Municipal Subdistrict.
Water user shall mean any person lawfully connected to the City water system and eligible to
receive water service through the City water system under the terms of this Chapter,
regardless of whether said water user is actually receiving and using water.
Windy Gap Unit refers to a contractual interest in a right to receive water under an allotment contract
in the Windy Gap Project from the Municipal Subdistrict of the Northern Colorado Water
Conservancy District, which interests are allocated based on units.

No change of ownership or occupancy of any property shall affect the application of
this Chapter or any of its provisions, and the failure of any successor in interest
of any record owner to learn that the successor acquired property against which a
lien exists pursuant to this Chapter shall not affect the successor's liability for
payment in full. No such successor, nor any occupant of property, nor any user of
the City water system shall have the right to make any claim of any kind whatsoever
against the City for refusing to furnish water service until payment of all amounts

(e)
No person shall obtain water from the City's water system nor shall any water be furnished
therefrom to any property, building, dwelling, or unit except by, through, and under,

(f)
Except as the City Council may otherwise authorize by resolution or written agreement,
no person residing or situated with the City's corporate boundaries shall use or obtain
water for usual and ordinary water purposes, except: (1) by, through, and from the
City water system; (2) through other water systems specifically approved by the City
in writing; or (3) by an existing well or ditch in use prior to November 28, 2022.
(g)
The City will have dominion and control of all water supplied through its water system,
subject to reasonable use thereof by its customers in compliance with applicable water
service agreements, leases or licenses, the Code, and the City's rules and regulations.
Such dominion and control will continue without interruption as to all wastewater,
return flows, runoff, sewage or tail water attributable to or originating in water
supplied through City-owned or -controlled water rights or facilities. The City shall
have the exclusive right to recapture such return flows or claim credit therefrom
for reuse, successive use, exchange, replacement, augmentation, substitute supply
or any other lawful purposes, and the City's dominion and control over water shall
continue to attach to all such return flows, regardless of form, even after they return
to the ground water aquifers or the surface stream systems. All return flows from
water supplied through City-owned or -controlled water rights or facilities remain
the property of the City.
(h)
The City may provide water service to property outside City boundaries pursuant to
a service agreement and on such terms and conditions as the City Council determines
to be in the best interests of the City.
(i)
As a condition of water service from the City, the owner of property served shall
be deemed to have irrevocably consented to the use by the City as a part of its water
system, of all water subject to the owner's control in the Denver, Arapahoe, Laramie-Fox
Hills and Dakota aquifers underlying the property, which use includes without limitation,
the exclusive right to take, appropriate, produce, use, reuse, and otherwise dispose
of said water. The owner shall further be deemed to have irrevocably consented to
being served with such groundwater through the City's system, wherein such water may
be commingled with water underlying the property of others for the purpose of being
furnished by the City. The owner shall further be deemed to have irrevocably consented
to the City's adjudication of said water in the City's name in water court.

(a)
A water tap application shall be made by the record owner of the property to be connected
and served upon forms provided by the City. At a minimum, the water tap application
must designate the location of the property to be served and state the purpose for
which water may be required. The City may require evidence of the record owner's title
to the property. The completed water tap application shall be accompanied by payment
of the then-current water tap fee established pursuant to resolution, this Code or
City ordinance or in accordance with Section 13-6.
(b)
No water tap application shall be approved until all applicable fees have been paid,
the water dedication requirements in Article 3 of this Chapter have been satisfied,
the City has determined the water tap requested is of a sufficient size for the use
to be served and any other applicable conditions of any City ordinance, resolution,
rule, regulation and contract have been met.
(c)
No water tap application shall be approved until the applicant has provided the City
with evidence satisfactory to the City that arrangements for sewer service to the
property, or other arrangements for sewage disposal satisfactory to the City, have
been made.
(d)
In addition to any other conditions applicable to the granting of a building permit,
no building permit shall be granted for the construction of any building for which
water service is required by the City until a water tap application has been approved.
(e)
If a water tap application has been approved, but the water tap has not been installed
and has not been certified to the Central Weld County Water District within ninety
(90) days of that approval, the City may require the record owner to pay the difference
between the fees paid at the time of the approval of the water tap application and
any increased water tap fees charged by Central Weld County Water District at the
time of certification.
(f)
The water tap and meter requirements shall be determined based on the dwelling, building,
or unit type, as follows:
(1)
A separate water tap and meter shall be required for (i) each one-family dwelling,
including mobile homes; (ii) each dwelling unit within a two-family dwelling; and
(iii) each single-unit commercial building. For accessory dwelling units authorized
pursuant to Chapter 16 , Article 26 of this Code, water tap and meter requirements shall be made in accordance with the
City's municipal utility requirements, as determined by the Community Development
Director based on the overall water consumption of the principal residence, water
pressure, and ability to serve the ADU from the existing tap.
(2)
As determined by the Community Development Director, all multiple-unit commercial
buildings whether or not units are individually owned, may have either: (i) one (1)
water tap and a master meter; or (ii) separate water taps and meters for each commercial
unit.
(3)
As determined by the Community Development Director, all multiple-family dwellings
in which the dwelling units are individually owned or intended to be individually
owned, including, but not limited to, condominium complexes and townhomes, may have
either: (i) one (1) water tap and a master meter; or (ii) separate water taps and
meters for each dwelling unit.
(4)
All dwelling units in a multiple-family dwelling with the same address and not intended
to be individually owned, including without limitation apartments, apartment houses,
and apartment hotels, shall be served by one (1) water tap and a master meter.
(5)
If, at any time after issuance of a water tap in accordance with subsection (f)(4)
of this Section 13-4, a multiple-family dwelling is legally converted to individually-owned
dwelling units, including without limitation to condominiums, the property owner shall
notify the City. The City will determine the tap and meter requirements in accordance
with subsection (f)(3) or (f)(6) of this Section 13-4, and may require the property
owner(s) to apply for new water taps if necessary.
(6)
If the City determines, in its sole discretion, that a particular type of building
does not conform to the definitions herein, the City may determine the particular
tap and meter requirements for any such building.
(g)
Water service shall be provided from a water tap only to the dwelling, unit, or building
described in the water tap application.
(h)
No water taps shall be granted to serve any property outside the City's service area,
nor shall any water be furnished from the City water system to any person or property
outside said service area, except by a special written agreement with the City and
under such conditions as the City Council may deem proper.
(i)
The installation of taps shall be in accordance with this Code and the ordinances,
resolutions, rules and regulations of the City, including, but not limited to, Section
13-5.
(j)
The standard size of a residential water tap is three-quarters (¾) inch. The standard
size of a residential water meter is five-eighths (⅝) inch. However, the City will
determine the required tap and meter sizes for any dwelling, building, or unit type,
in its sole discretion.
(k)
An application for an increase in the size of any previously installed water tap and/or
meter shall be treated as an application for a new water tap to the extent of the
increase. The City may require additional water dedication to the City to the extent
of the increase, in an amount to be determined in the sole discretion of the City.
(l)
All water meters shall be installed by the City or its authorized agent. Water meters,
valves, service lines and all other materials used in the meter installation shall
meet the specifications and standards approved by the City. The cost of all materials
necessary to install a water meter shall be borne by the property owner. All installation
shall occur under the supervision of the public works manager and shall be inspected
and accepted by the City prior to water service becoming available.
(m)
The City will not issue both a master meter and separate sub-meters. The City may
require that a sub-meter be installed to measure water used for irrigation purposes.
Additional metering requirements not addressed in this Section 13-4 may be established
in the sole discretion of the Public Works Director or the City Manager.
(n)
Each water meter shall be tested by the City or the manufacturer and shall be found
to be correct and properly adjusted prior to installation. If the test is conducted
by the manufacturer, certification of the test and results will be furnished to the
City prior to installation of the water meter.
(o)
The City's authorized employees and agents shall have immediate and unhampered access
to all water meters at all times.

this Chapter.
(3)
Delinquency fees: The delinquency fee and other fees that may be applied by the City to delinquent
payment of any amounts due and owing under this Chapter.

(a)
If any charge imposed pursuant to this Chapter is not paid by the date due, the City
may avail itself of any or any combination of the following remedies, in addition
to any other available remedies:
(1)
The City may foreclose the lien imposed by this Chapter in accordance with law.
(2)
The City may maintain an action in any court of competent jurisdiction for the amount
of the charge due and any interest, costs and attorney's fees as allowed by law.
(3)
The City may certify the amount of the charge due to the County Clerk and Treasurer,
together with all delinquency fees, including the delinquency certification fee, to
become an assessment upon the property served and to be collected and paid over to
the City in the same manner as taxes.
(4)
The City may discontinue water service in the following manner:
a.
All City water bills shall contain a notice regarding service termination for nonpayment,
which notice shall advise that, once a bill becomes delinquent, the City will send
a notice of termination of service that shall give a date by which payment must be
received or service may be disconnected, which date shall be no sooner than ten (10)
days after the date the disconnect notice is mailed. The notice on the water bill
shall further advise that, if the occupant or record owner does not believe reasonable
cause exists for the proposed termination of service, a written request for a conference
with the City Manager may be filed prior to suspension. At such conference, the sole
issue to be determined shall be whether the delinquency in fact exists and the amount
of the delinquency.
b.
If a discontinuation notice is sent pursuant to this Section, a late fee shall be
charged to the record owner.
(b)
If payment of any charge imposed pursuant to this Chapter is made by a dishonored
payment, a bad payment fee shall be imposed in addition to such charge, and such charge
shall be considered delinquent in the same manner as if no payment had been made.
(c)
The payment of any charge imposed pursuant to this Chapter shall be considered delinquent
if not received in the offices of the City before the close of business, pursuant
to office hours as established from time to time, on the last day specified for payment.
A delinquency fee shall be charged for any charges not timely paid in accordance with
this Chapter.
(d)
If water service is discontinued as provided in this Section, water service shall
not be reinstated to the property until either:
(1)
Notice of the continuing lien established by this Chapter has been filed with the
County Clerk and Recorder, with unpaid liens to be certified to the County Treasurer
pursuant to subsection (3) of this Section, to be collected and paid over to the City
in the same manner as taxes; or
(2)
The record owner has paid in full all charges imposed pursuant to this Chapter and
due the City, and has paid in full all amounts due the City pursuant to any other
fee, assessment or other charge which has been imposed by the City pursuant to this
Code and which has been made a lien against the property.
(e)
Prior to disconnection for nonpayment, City staff is authorized to enter into payment
plans with water customers who have past-due balances, with terms and conditions to
be approved by the City's Director of Finance, provided such payment plans provide
a steady decrease in the amount of the past-due balance and current water charges
are paid in full each month.
(f)

but not limited to providing a fire watch to properties with fire suppression systems.

(a)
Legislative intent. It is the intent of this Article 4 to protect the City's water distribution system
from contamination or pollution by backflow from a property owner's internal distribution
system or private water system, and to provide for the maintenance of a continuing
program of cross connection control, which will systematically prevent the contamination
or pollution of the City's water distribution system.
(b)
Authority. In addition to the City's home rule powers, the authority to implement and maintain
controls and regulations on cross connections is contained in the following legislation
and regulations:
(1)
Clean Water Act 33, United States Code (U.S.C.) § 1251 et seq. (1 987 ).
(2)
Safe Drinking Water Act; Title XIV - Safety of Public Drinking Water Systems of the
Public Health Service Act, codified as 42 U.S.C. § 300f et seq. (1974), Public Law
104-182 Safe Drinking Water Act Amendments of 1 996 .
(3)
Colorado Department of Health Law Title 25-1-114, 25-1-114.1, Colorado Revised Statute
(C.R.S).
(4)
Colorado Primary Drinking Water Regulations Section 14.1 (Hazardous Cross-Connections).
(5)
Colorado Cross-Connection Control Manual, 5th Edition, published in 2006 by the Colorado
Department of Public Health and Environment, Water Quality Control Division, 4300
Cherry Creek Drive South, Denver, Colorado, 80246-1530.
(c)
Responsibility.
(1)
The Public Works Director is hereby delegated the responsibility for implementing
a backflow prevention and cross connection control (BPCCC) program in accordance with
this Chapter and for enforcement thereof. If a backflow prevention device is required
for the protection of the City water system, the Public Works Director or designee
shall give notice in writing to the property owner to install an approved backflow
prevention method or assembly. The property owner shall install an approved device
or devices at the owner's expense.
(2)
No provision of this Chapter exempts the property owner from complying with the BPCCC
program requirements set forth by the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment
or provisions for internal water distribution systems as set forth in the most current
International Plumbing Code, adopted by reference in Chapter 18 , Article 3 of the Dacono Municipal Code.
(3)
The property owner is solely responsible for preventing pollutants and contaminants
in water distributing pipes located within their property from entering the municipal
potable water supply.
a.
The property owner's responsibility starts at the point of delivery and includes all
of the connections to, and the operation of any domestic, irrigation, or fire sprinkler
systems.
b.
The property owner is solely responsible for the purchase, installment and maintenance
of any required backflow prevention device, and for ensuring that all initial and
annual testing is completed per the requirements of this Chapter.
c.
The property owner must keep accurate records of all tests, inspections and repairs
for at least three (3) years. The property owner is required to provide the Public
Works Department with copies of these records upon request.
(d)
Definitions. When not clearly otherwise indicated by the context, the following words and phrases
when used in this Article shall have the following meanings:
Annual due date means the annual date as established by the City when required tests must be completed
and evidence of testing received by City's Public Works Department. In those years
when the annual due date does not fall on a regular business day, the due date for
that year will be the next regular business day.
Approved means accepted by City's Public Works Department as meeting the applicable specifications
set forth in this Section, or as suitable for the proposed use.
Approved backflow prevention device means devices detailed in Subsection 13-30 (k) of this Section.
Backflow prevention assembly means any mechanical assembly installed at a water service line or at a plumbing
fixture to prevent a backflow contamination event, provided that the mechanical assembly
is appropriate for the identified contamination at the cross connection and is an
in-line field-tested assembly.
Backflow prevention method means any method and/or non-testable device installed at a water service line or
at a plumbing fixture to prevent a backflow contamination event, provided that the
method or non-testable device is appropriate for the identified contamination at the
cross connection.
Certified cross-connection control technician means a person who possesses a valid backflow prevention assembly tester certification
from one (1) of the following approved organizations: American Society of Sanitary
Engineering (ASSE) or the American Backflow Prevention Association (ABPA).
Containment means the control of cross-connections within a property owner's plumbing system
by the installation of approved backflow prevention assemblies or methods on the property
owner's service line after the meter and before any branches.
Controlled means having a properly installed, maintained, and tested or inspected backflow prevention
assembly or backflow prevention method that prevents backflow through a cross connection.
Cross-connection means any connection that could allow any water, fluid, or gas such that the water
quality could present an unacceptable health and/or safety risk to the public, to
flow from any pipe, plumbing fixture or a customer's water system into a public water
system's distribution system or any other part of the public water system through
backflow.
Isolation means the control of cross-connections within the plumbing system of a property owner
by isolating individual cross-connections at or near the point of potential contamination
or pollution with approved backflow prevention assemblies or methods.
Responsible party means the person billed for water service provided by the City, or the owner of the
property served by City water if the owner is different than the person billed. In
the event that the property owner and the person billed for City water service are
different, the owner shall be solely responsible for all obligations and duties of
this Section.
Uncontrolled means not having a properly installed and maintained and tested or inspected backflow
prevention assembly or backflow prevention method, or the backflow prevention assembly
or backflow prevention method does not prevent backflow through a cross connection.

(2)
Service of water to any premises may be discontinued by the City if uncontrolled cross-connections
exist on the premises, if any defect is found in an installed backflow prevention
assembly, or if a backflow prevention assembly has been removed or bypassed or does
not adequately protect the public health. Service will be discontinued within ten
(10) days of notification of a failed test or notification of an inadequate backflow
prevention method and service shall not be restored until such conditions or defects
are corrected to the satisfaction of the City's Public Works Department.
(k)
Approved types of backflow prevention assemblies. Any backflow prevention assembly shall be of a model and size approved by City's
Public Works Department. Examples of preferred backflow devices can be found in the
City of Dacono BPCCC program. The devices listed in the BPCCC program shall serve
as guidance; however, final approval shall be made by the Public Works Department.
