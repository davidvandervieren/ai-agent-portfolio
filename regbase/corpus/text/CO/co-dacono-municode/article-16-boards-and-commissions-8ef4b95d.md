(a)
The chairperson of each board and commission established by the Charter or ordinances
of the City shall be appointed by the Mayor from among the full membership of the
board or commission. Each board and commission shall elect a vice-chairperson from
among its members and create and fill such other offices as it may determine, and
may adopt such written rules of procedure as it finds necessary and expedient to fulfillment
of the duties assigned to it.
(b)
The vice-chairperson shall perform the duties of the chairperson in the chairperson's
absence or due to incapacity of the chairperson. During a vacancy in the office of
the chairperson, the vice-chairperson shall perform such duties as are imposed on
the chairperson until such time as the Mayor appoints a new chairperson.
( Ord. No. 829 , § 2, 6-26-2017; Ord. 898 §1, 2021)
