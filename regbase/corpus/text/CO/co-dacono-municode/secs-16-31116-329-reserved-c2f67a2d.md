The height limitations of this Chapter shall not apply to church spires, belfries,
cupolas, penthouses or domes not used for human occupancy, chimneys, ventilators,
skylights, water tanks, silos, parapet walls, cornices without windows, antennas,
towers and refining structures or necessary mechanical appurtenances usually carried
above the roof level or to the public building or structures located more than one
(1) foot horizontally from the property line for each foot of building height.
(Prior code 17.36.010)
