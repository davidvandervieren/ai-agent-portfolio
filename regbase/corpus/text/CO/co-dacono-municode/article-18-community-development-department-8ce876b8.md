The Community Development Department is hereby established, consisting of the Community
Development Director and any personnel subordinate to the Community Development Director.
( Ord. 956, §1, 2024)
(a)
The Community Development Director shall be responsible for the proper administration
of the Community Development Department and shall be responsible for the performance
of the following functions and duties:
(1)
Land use, planning and development related functions; administration and enforcement
of the City's zoning and subdivision ordinances, comprehensive plan and building codes;
community relations; City events; and such other duties as provided by ordinance,
resolution or motion of the City Council.
(b)
The Community Development Director shall be appointed and may be removed by City Council
as provided in Section 7-6(b) of the City Charter.
( Ord. 956 §1, 2024)
