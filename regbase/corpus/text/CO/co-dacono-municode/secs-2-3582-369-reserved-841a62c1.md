For the purposes of this Article the following terms, phrases, words, and their derivatives
shall have the meanings given in this Section:
Article XXVIII shall mean Article XXVIII of the Colorado Constitution, entitled Campaign and Political
Finance, as amended from time to time.
Candidate shall mean any person who seeks nomination or election to the office of City Council
at any election. A person is a candidate if the person has filed a nominating petition
with the City Clerk for the office of City Council. Candidate shall also mean any
elected official who is the subject of recall proceedings pursuant to the City Charter.
City campaign finance matter shall mean any campaign finance matter exclusively related to a City campaign, including:
matters involving a candidate for City office; a City ballot issue or ballot question;
and contributions or expenditures made by any person, committee or group to support
or oppose any candidate for municipal office, municipal ballot issue or ballot question.
City Clerk shall mean the City Clerk of the City of Dacono or that person's designee.
FCPA shall mean the Fair Campaign Practices Act, Section 1-45-101, et seq., C.R.S. as
amended from time to time.
Secretary shall mean the Secretary of State or such person's designee.
(Ord. 895 §2, 2020; Ord. No. 966 , §1, 2024)
Article XXVIII and the FCPA shall apply to and govern all City elections except to
the extent that a direct and irreconcilable conflict with the provisions of this Article
exists, in which case the provisions of this Article shall prevail.
(Ord. 895 §2, 2020)
(a)
The reports required by Section 1-45-108, C.R.S. shall be filed with the City Clerk
on:
1.
The twenty-first (21st) day prior to the City election;
2.
The Friday before the City election; and
3.
Thirty-five (35) days after the City election.
(b)
If the reporting day is on a weekend or a legal holiday, the report shall be filed
by the close of the next business day.
(c)
Any candidate or committee that has not accepted any contributions, made any expenditures,
or entered into any obligations during a reporting period shall file a report with
the City Clerk on the days specified in Subsection A above, certifying that the committee
has not accepted any contributions, made any expenditures, or entered into any obligations
during the relevant reporting period.
( Ord. No. 966 , §1, 2024)

(a)
The maximum amount of aggregate contributions that any one (1) person or political
committee may make to a candidate committee is four hundred dollars ($400.00) per
election.
(b)
The maximum amount of aggregate contributions that any one (1) small donor committee
may make to a candidate committee is one thousand five hundred dollars ($1,500.00)
per election.
(c)
No candidate committee shall accept contributions that have an aggregate amount or
value in excess of the amounts listed in Subsections A or B. Any contribution or portion
thereof that exceeds the limits in Subsections (a) or (b) shall be returned to the
contributor within seventy-two (72) hours of receipt.
(d)
The limits imposed in this Section shall not apply to contribution of a candidate's
personal funds to the candidate's own campaign.

The City Clerk shall:
(a)
Develop a filing and indexing system consistent with the purposes of the FCPA and
this Chapter.
(b)
Keep a copy of any report or statement required to be filed by the FCPA and this Chapter
for a period of one (1) year from the date of filing. In the case of candidates who
were elected, each such candidate's reports and filings shall be kept until one (1)
year after the candidate leaves office.
(c)
Make reports and statements required to be filed under the FCPA and this Chapter publicly
available on the City's website or for in-person public inspection. The City Clerk
may charge applicable copying and research and retrieval fees when such reports are
requested through an open records request.
(d)
Upon request by the Secretary or in response to any order issued by a court of competent
jurisdiction, transmit records and statements filed under the FCPA and this Chapter
to the Secretary or as otherwise directed by court order.
(e)
Notify any candidate who has failed, or whose candidate committee has failed, to fully
comply with the provisions of this Chapter. The City Clerk's notification obligations
shall be deemed fulfilled if the City Clerk sends written notice to the candidate's
designated e-mail address or mailing address.
(f)
Process complaints of apparent or possible violations of Article XXVIII, the FCPA
or this Chapter in accordance with Sections 2-355 and 2-356 of this Code.
(g)
The City Clerk shall not be required to verify the accuracy of any information contained
in reports filed pursuant to the FCPA and this Chapter.
( Ord. No. 966 , §1, 2024)
(a)
Any person who believes a violation of Article XXVIII, the FCPA, or this Chapter has
occurred related to a City campaign finance matter may file a written complaint with
the City Clerk.
(b)
Complaints must be filed no later than ninety (90) calendar days after the complainant
knew or should have known by the exercise of reasonable diligence of the alleged violation.
(c)
A written complaint shall be filed on a form provided by the City Clerk, which shall
include the following information:
(1)
The name, address, e-mail address, telephone number and signature of the complainant
(if the complainant is represented by counsel, include the counsel's name, address,
e-mail address, telephone number and signature).
(2)
The name and, if known, the telephone number and address of the person alleged to
have committed a violation (the "respondent").
(3)
The particulars of the alleged violation and any available documentation or evidence
supporting the allegation.
(d)
If an incomplete complaint is received, the date on which the originally filed complaint
was received is considered the filed date if a complete copy is received within seven
(7) business days of notification from the City Clerk that the complaint was incomplete.
(e)
Initial review.
(1)
The City Clerk will review the complaint to determine:
a.
Whether the complaint was timely filed; and
b.
Whether the complainant has specifically identified a violation of Article XXVIII,
the FCPA or this Chapter.
(2)
Within ten (10) business days of receiving the complaint, the City Clerk must take
one (1) or more of the following actions:
i.
If the City Clerk determines that the complaint was not timely filed or has not specifically
identified a violation of Article XXVIII, the FCPA or this Chapter, the City Clerk
will dismiss the complaint and notify the complainant and respondent of the reasons
for dismissal. The City Clerk's dismissal is a final decision, and subject to review
under Rule 106, C.R.C.P.
ii.
If the City Clerk determines that the complaint alleges a curable violation, the City
Clerk will notify the respondent and provide an opportunity to cure as described in
Subsection (f).
iii.
If the City Clerk determines that the complaint may not be curable or that additional
factual findings or legal interpretation is required in connection with the complaint,
the City Clerk will take the actions set forth in Subsection (g).
iv.
If the City Clerk determines in writing that the complaint presents an actual or potential
conflict for the Clerk, the Clerk shall refer the complaint to the Secretary in accordance
with Section 1-45-111.7, C.R.S When so referred, the Secretary is authorized to use
the provisions of the FCPA to process, investigate, and resolve the complaint; except
that Section 2-355 (b) shall apply when determining whether the complaint was timely filed. When a complaint
is referred to the Secretary, the City shall cooperate with the Secretary in processing
and investigating the complaint and hereby disclaims any interest in the fines collected
in connection with the referred complaint.
(f)
Curing violations.
(1)
If the City Clerk determines that the complaint alleges a curable violation, such
as a failure to file or otherwise disclose required information, the City Clerk will
notify the respondent by email or by mail of the curable deficiencies.
(2)
Respondent shall have fourteen (14) business days from the date the notice is mailed
to cure any deficiencies specified in the notice.
(3)
The City Clerk may ask the respondent to provide additional information and may grant
an extension of time to cure.
(4)
After the period for cure, the City Clerk will determine whether the respondent cured
the violation.
i.
If the Clerk determines that respondent cured the violation, the Clerk shall dismiss
the complaint and notify complainant and respondent of such dismissal.
ii.
If the Clerk determines that respondent failed to cure the violation, the Clerk shall
notify respondent of such determination, together with the fine or other penalty imposed.
iii.
The respondent shall have fourteen (14) business days from the date of the Clerk's
notice of determination to either pay the fine and accept any penalty imposed or contest
the violation by submitting a request for a hearing to the Clerk.
(g)
Upon receipt of a request for a hearing under Subsection f.4.iii., or upon the Clerk's
determination that the complaint falls under e.2.iii, the City Clerk shall notify
the City Manager of the complaint, and the City Manager, in consultation with the
City Clerk and the City Attorney, shall refer the complaint to an independent hearing
officer to hear and determine such complaint.
(h)
A hearing shall be held within thirty (30) days of the referral of the complaint to
the hearing officer, unless an enlargement of time is granted. Upon written motion,
the hearing officer may grant the respondent a continuance upon a showing of good
cause.
(i)
Notice of the hearing and any applicable rules governing the hearing process shall
be sent to the complainant and to the respondent, who shall also receive a copy of
the entire complaint received by the City Clerk within five (5) business days of the
date of the referral to the hearing office.
(j)
Upon the request of either party, the hearing officer may issue an administrative
subpoena requiring the attendance of a witness or party in relation to an alleged
campaign finance violation, which shall be served on the party to whom it is directed
by the requesting party pursuant to the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure. It shall
be unlawful for a witness or party to fail to comply with such subpoena, and any person
convicted of a violation hereof shall be punished in accordance with Section 1-70 of this Code.
(k)
At the hearing, the complainant and the respondent shall be allowed to be heard. The
complainant shall have the burden of proof by proving the alleged violation by a preponderance
of the evidence.
(l)
Following the hearing, the hearing officer shall issue a decision within thirty (30)
business days. The decision may be issued orally at the conclusion of the hearing
or may be issued in writing, at the discretion of the hearing officer.
(m)
If the hearing officer determines that a violation has occurred, the hearing officer's
decision shall include any appropriate order, sanction or relief authorized hereunder
and may include, without limitation, sanctions as follows:
(1)
Impose the civil penalties set forth in subsection (n), except the hearing officer
may increase or reduce the penalties upon a finding of good cause or excusable neglect
based on evidence presented at the hearing.
(2)
Order disclosure of the source and amount of any undisclosed contributions or expenditures.
(3)
Order the return to the donor of any contribution made which was the subject of the
violation.
(n)
The civil fines for violations of Article XXVIII, the FCPA, or this Chapter shall
be in the amounts adopted by the Colorado Secretary of State for violations of campaign
finance laws.
(o)
If the civil fine is not paid when due or upon entry of a civil judgement by the hearing
officer, the City may commence procedures to collect the fine, including commencement
of a civil action to collect the fine. A person found to be in violation of this Article
shall be responsible for all costs of collection, including reasonable attorney fees.
(p)
All decisions by the hearing officer shall be final subject only to judicial review
pursuant to Rule 106 of the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure.
