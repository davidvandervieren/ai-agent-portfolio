On the interpretation and application of the provisions of this Chapter, the following
shall govern:
(1)
The provisions contained in this Chapter shall be regarded as minimum requirements
for the protection of the public health, safety and welfare.
(2)
Whenever a provision of this Chapter and any provision in any other law of the City
cover the same subject matter, whichever is the most restrictive or imposes the higher
standard or requirement shall govern.
(Prior code 16.40.010)
