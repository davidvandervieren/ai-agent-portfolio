The Human Resources Department is hereby established, consisting of the Human Resources
Director and any personnel subordinate to the Human Resources Director.
(Ord. 1011 §1, 2026)
(a)
The Human Resources Director shall administer all activities of the Human Resources
Department.
(b)
The Human Resources Director shall be appointed and may be removed by City Council
as provided in Section 7-6(b) of the City Charter.
(Ord. 1011 §1, 2026)
