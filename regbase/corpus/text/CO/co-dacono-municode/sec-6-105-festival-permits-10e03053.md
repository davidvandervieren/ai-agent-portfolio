(a)
Permit required. A licensee authorized by state statute to apply for a festival permit must also obtain
a local festival permit from the City before holding a festival in the City; except
that a limited winery licensee or winery licensee need not obtain a local permit to
participate in or hold a festival.
(b)
Number of festivals. A festival permit granted hereunder allows a licensee to hold up to nine (9) festivals
during a twelve-month period. To hold any festival after the initial festival described
in the application, the applicant shall file a supplemental application as provided
in subsection (d) of this Section.
(c)
Application. An applicant for a local festival permit shall:
(1)
Specify the location and date of the festival.
(2)
Establish the physical boundaries of the festival permit area in a manner to assure
that alcohol beverages will be kept within the physical boundaries of the festival
permit area.
(3)
File a complete festival permit application, including the local fee at least thirty
(30) days before the festival is to be held. The local fee will be fixed by City Council.
(d)
Supplemental applications. To hold additional festivals after the initial festival described in the application,
the permittee shall submit a supplemental application containing the information and
fee described in subsection (c) at least thirty (30) days prior to any additional
festival being held.
(e)
Denial of a festival permit. The Authority may deny an application or a supplemental application for the following
reasons:
(1)
The application is incomplete, was not timely filed, or otherwise fails to comply
with the requirements of applicable law.
(2)
The applicant has a documented history of liquor violations.
(3)
The issuance of the permit would be injurious to the public welfare because of the
nature of the festival or its location.
(f)
Conditions. The Secretary or Authority, as applicable, may impose reasonable conditions on the
festival permit, including but not limited to the experience or training of the organizers
and servers, adequate security, and adequate signage and demarcation of premises boundaries.
(g)
Event permit. Except when part of a City-sponsored event, any applicant seeking a festival permit
for alcohol service may also be required to obtain an event permit as provided in Chapter 11 , Article 3 of this Code.
