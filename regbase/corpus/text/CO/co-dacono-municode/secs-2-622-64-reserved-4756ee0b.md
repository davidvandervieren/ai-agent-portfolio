The Administrative Services Department is hereby established, consisting of the Administrative
Services Director and any personnel subordinate to the Administrative Services Director.
(Ord. 995 §1, 2025)
(a)
The Administrative Services Director shall be responsible for the proper administration
of the Administrative Services Department and shall be responsible for the performance
of the following functions and duties:
(1)
Office of the City Clerk;
(2)
Municipal Court services;
(3)
Administrative services, including customer service, Information Technology (IT) support,
office management;
(4)
Community relations; and
(5)
Events.
(b)
The Administrative Services Director shall be appointed and may be removed by City
Council as provided in Section 7-6(b) of the City Charter.
(Ord. 995 §1, 2025)
