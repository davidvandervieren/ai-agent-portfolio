As used in this Article:
Abandoned personal property means personal property which comes into the custody of the City and which is unclaimed
by the owner of such property, except for abandoned motor vehicles, for which disposition
shall be governed by Section 42-44-1601 et seq., C.R.S., contraband articles, for
which disposition shall be governed by Section 16-13-501 et seq., C.R.S., and any
other personal property for which a method of disposition is specified by the state
statutes as from time to time amended.
City Manager means the City Manager or the City Manager's designee.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991; Ord. 995 §11, 2025)
The City Manager shall be the custodian of abandoned personal property.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991; Ord. 995 , §12, 2025)
The City Manager shall make reasonable efforts to identify and locate the owner of
abandoned personal property and shall return the property to the owner upon the presentation
of satisfactory proof of ownership.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991; Ord. 995 §13, 2025)
If no owner claims ownership of and provides satisfactory proof of ownership of any
abandoned personal property within sixty (60) days from the date when the property
comes into the custody of the City, then the property shall escheat to and become
the property of the City, and no person shall thereafter have any claim or right to
the property or any proceeds therefrom.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991)
The City Manager shall cause to be maintained a record of all abandoned personal property,
including the date on which the property came into the custody of the City, a description
of the property, including any serial numbers, and the date and means of disposition
of the property.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991; Ord. 995 §14, 2025)
The City Manager may from time to time cause any abandoned personal property which
becomes the property of the City as provided in this Article to be destroyed or to
be sold after advertisement to the highest bidder, and the proceeds of the sale deposited
to the general fund of the City. The City Manager, at his or her discretion, may elect
not to destroy or sell the property and to vest title to the property in the City.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991; Ord. 995 §15, 2025)
This Article shall not apply to personal property held by the Police Department as
evidence in any court case or held by the Police Department for investigation. The
Police Department shall retain custody of such property until written notice is received
from the person responsible for the court case or the investigation that the property
is no longer needed for such purposes, and upon such receipt, the procedures set forth
in this Article shall apply.
(Ord. 388 §1, 1991)
