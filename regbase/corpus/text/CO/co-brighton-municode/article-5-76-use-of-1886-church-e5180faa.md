Except as hereinafter provided, no person, organization or association shall use the
1886 Church located at 147 South Main Street, City of Brighton, Colorado, without
first having obtained authorization therefor from the City.
(Ord. 926 §1(part), 1977)
Application for such use shall be made in such manner as is approved by the City Manager.
(Ord. 926 §1(part), 1977)
The schedule of fees to be charged for use of the 1886 Church shall be reviewed at
such times as the City Council determines to be necessary and shall be set by resolution
duly adopted by the City Council.
(Ord. 1247, 1986)
This Article shall not be construed to require any payment or deposit of fees with
the City by the Adams County Historical Society for use of the 1886 Church.
(Ord. 926 §1(part), 1977)
