DEFINITIONS. As used in this Charter, the following words and phrases shall have the following
meanings, except as otherwise specifically provided or indicated by the context:
The authorized amount of monies set aside for expenditure during a specified time
for a specified purpose.
A board or commission established by or pursuant to this Charter.
A person seeking nomination, appointment, or election as an elected official.
City of Brighton, Colorado, a municipal corporation.
The City Clerk of the City of Brighton or such office designation as may be used in
place of the office of City Clerk.
The Constitution of the State of Colorado as it exists now or may hereafter be amended.
The City Council of the City of Brighton, Colorado, including the Mayor.
Each member of the City Council except the Mayor.
The Mayor and each Council Member.
An existing condition which, in the opinion of the City Council, requires immediate
action for the preservation of the public property, health, welfare, peace, or safety.
Each compensated person in the service of the City who is designated as an employee
in the personnel rules and regulations of the City.
The statewide election held in even-numbered years pursuant to State Statutes.
The power of the registered electors of the City of Brighton to propose to City Council,
in accordance with the provisions of this Charter, any ordinance for adoption by City
Council which, if not adopted by Council, shall be submitted to a vote of the registered
electors of the City for acceptance or rejection.
The City Manager of the City of Brighton appointed pursuant to this Charter.
The Mayor of the City of Brighton.
One or more newspapers of general circulation in the City as selected by the Council
which meets the requirements for a legal newspaper as established in the State Statutes.
(Amended 11-8-11 election)
A permanent regulation adopted by legislative action of the City Council pursuant
to the procedures set forth in this Charter.
(A)
Publication in a newspaper;
(B)
Publication on a publicly accessible web page; or
(C)
In the event of an emergency or when no such newspaper or webpage is available, posting.
Anything published by such posting because of an emergency shall subsequently be published
in a newspaper. (Amended 11-8-11 election)
The power of the registered electors of the City of Brighton to request that City
Council reconsider any ordinance adopted by the Council and, if Council fails to repeal
such ordinance, to require that Council submit the referred ordinance to a vote of
the registered electors of the City for approval or rejection, all in accordance with
the provisions of this Charter.
An elector who has registered in compliance with the provisions for registration to
vote as set forth in the State Statutes.
A City election held pursuant to Section 3.2 of this Charter.
An expression of an administrative or a ministerial act of Council without any required
form or procedure, as distinguished from legislative acts embodied in City ordinances.
A City election held at a time other than a regular municipal election pursuant to
Section 3.3 of this Charter.
The Statutes of the State of Colorado as they now exist or as they may hereafter be
amended, changed, repealed, or otherwise modified.
The City Treasurer of Brighton or such office designation as may be used in place
of the office of Treasurer.
