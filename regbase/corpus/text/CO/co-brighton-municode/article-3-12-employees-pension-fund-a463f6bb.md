ARTICLE 3-12 - Employees' Pension Fund
Sec. 3-12-10. - Created.
There is created the City of Brighton employees' pension fund, which fund is to be
accounted for in the fiduciary funds of the City.
(Ord. 864 §1, 1976; Ord. 1714 §2, 2001)
