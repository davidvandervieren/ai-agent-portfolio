ARTICLE 2-56 - Officers' Salaries
Sec. 2-56-10. - Salaries determined by annual budget.
The salaries of all officers and employees of the City shall be as provided annually
in the annual budget.
(Ord. 829 §1, 1975; Ord. 1599 §2, 1999)
