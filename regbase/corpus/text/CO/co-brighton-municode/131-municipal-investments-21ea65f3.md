The Council may, by ordinance or resolution, initiate and adopt guidelines for municipal
investments as long as those guidelines comply with the following conditions:
(A)
Such guidelines are subject to any applicable limitations in the Colorado Constitution,
including any requirements for voter approval; and
(B)
Such guidelines are determined by Council to be in the best interest of the City.
