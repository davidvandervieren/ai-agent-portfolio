Subject to Applicable Law, regulations, rules, resolutions and ordinances of the Town
and the provisions of this Franchise, Grantee may perform all excavation, construction
and related work in the Rights-of-Way for any facility needed for the maintenance
or extension of Grantee's Cable System. Applicable Law related to rights-of-way construction
and maintenance is governed by applicable provisions of the Windsor Municipal Code,
includes without limitation, Chapter 11 , as amended and the Windsor Construction Standards and Specifications Manual.
(Ord. 2020-1619 §1)
