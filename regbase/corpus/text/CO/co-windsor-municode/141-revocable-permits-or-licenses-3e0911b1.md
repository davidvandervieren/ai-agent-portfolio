Except as otherwise provided in this Charter, the Town may grant a permit or license
at any time for the temporary use or occupation of any street, alley, other public
way, or Town-owned place. Any such permit or license shall be revocable by the Town
at any time and without cause, whether or not such right to revoke is expressly reserved
in the permit or license.
