A.
A franchise may be granted or renewed for a period of twenty-five (25) years or less.
B.
Any franchise, and any renewal of or amendment to a franchise, shall be granted by
the Town Board by ordinance. Any ordinance granting, renewing or amending a franchise
shall be subject to a referendum, notwithstanding any emergency declaration in the
ordinance, if one is filed in accordance with the procedures and requirements set
forth in this Charter. If such an election is ordered, the grantee of such franchise
shall deposit the cost of the election with the Town Clerk in an amount determined
by the Town Clerk.
