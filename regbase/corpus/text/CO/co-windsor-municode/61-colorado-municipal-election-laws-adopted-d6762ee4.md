Town elections shall be governed by the State Statutes contained in the Colorado Municipal
Election Code, except as otherwise provided by this Charter or by ordinance. All regular
and special elections shall be nonpartisan.
A regular municipal election shall be held in April of each even-numbered year on
the Tuesday succeeding the first Monday of April.
A special election shall be called by a resolution adopted at least sixty (60) days
prior to the election. The resolution calling a special election shall set forth the
purpose of the election.
A.
The Town Clerk shall have charge of all activities and duties required pursuant to
this Charter relating to the conduct of Town elections. In any case where election
procedure is in doubt, the Town Clerk shall prescribe the procedure to be followed.
B.
Reserved. (Amended by voters on April 3, 2012; amended by voters on February 18, 2025 )
