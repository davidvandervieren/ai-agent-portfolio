The Electric Motor Vehicle (EV) Charging Station requirements are intended to streamline
the process for permitting electric motor vehicle charging systems, as required by
HB24-1173 and pursuant to the International Building Code requirements adopted by
the Town and set forth in Chapter 18 .
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Accessory Use EV Charging Project means a proposed development of Direct Current Fast Charging ("DCFC") Charging Stations
and Equipment that are incidental and subordinate to the Principal Use on the Subject
Property.
Electric Vehicle (EV) means a motor vehicle which relies partially or entirely on electrical energy to
power its movement, requiring periodic electrical current charging of its battery.
EV Charging Equipment means all equipment necessary to provide electrical current charging for EVs, except
for EV Charging Stations. This may include power conversion equipment, electrical
distribution equipment such as transformers, switchgear boxes, and distribution lines,
and other supportive equipment.
EV Charging Port means a power supply device that provides electrical current charging for EVs. One
(1) EV Charging Port provides power for one (1) vehicle.
(1)
Direct Current Fast Charging (DCFC). High-speed charging that provides about fifty
(50) to three hundred fifty plus (350+) kW of power per hour and uses a four-hundred-eighty-volt
(480V), three-phase outlet.
(2)
Level 2 Charging. Mid-speed charging that provides about seven (7) to nineteen (19)
kW of power per hour and uses a two-hundred-forty-volt (240V) outlet.
(3)
Level 1 Charging. Slow charging that provides about one (1) to two (2) kW of power
per hour and uses a one-hundred-twenty-volt (120V) outlet.
EV Charging Project means a proposed development of Principal or Accessory Use EV Charging Stations and
Equipment, which may include other supporting site improvements like landscaping,
lighting, or weather protection.
EV Charging Station means equipment that includes one (1) or more EV Charging Ports to provide charging
for EVs, located adjacent to dedicated space(s) for the vehicle(s) while they charge.
One (1) EV Charging Station may provide power for one (1) or more vehicles.
Site Lighting means the area on a horizontal surface in which a light fixture provides direct or
high intensity illumination for an EV Charging Station.
Principal Use EV Charging Project means any proposed development of DCFC EV Charging Stations and Equipment that serves
as the principal function and land use on the Subject Property.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
A permit shall be required for EV charging development that meets the definition of
an "EV Charging Project" (and associated definitions for "Principal Use" and "Accessory
Use" EV Charging Projects).
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Applications for EV Charging Projects shall use the applicable permitting processes
from the zoning district where projects are proposed.
(1)
Major Site Plan application for EV charging stations as a new principal use. Site
Plan shall include location of charging stations and equipment, and shall also show
setbacks, dimensions to property lines, structures, and landscaping.
(2)
EV Plot Plan for accessory use to an existing site, to be included with a building
permit application for. Plot Plan shall include location of charging stations and
equipment, and shall also show setbacks, dimensions to property lines, structures,
and landscaping.
(3)
EV Plot Plan for EV Charging Stations as an accessory use in a new site plan. Plot
Plan shall include location of charging stations and equipment, and shall also show
setbacks, dimensions to property lines, structures, and landscaping.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
No site improvements to existing land uses on the same Subject Property with a proposed
Accessory Use EV Charging project shall be required, nor will there be any requirements
to otherwise conform with land use regulations, unless the existing land uses propose
simultaneous changes to their use, structures, or site that would otherwise require
such conformance.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Development standards for similar uses as contained in Chapter 15 of this Code.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Principal Use EV Charging Projects and Accessory Use EV Charging projects shall be
permitted as a use by right in all zoning districts, including multi-family projects,
with the exception of Single Family Detached (SF-1), Single Family Attached (SF-2)
and Estate Residential (ER). In the Residential Mixed Use One and Residential Mixed
Use Two (RMU-1 and RMU-2) zones, EV Charging Stations are permitted as a use by right
for commercial and multifamily uses as outlined in those zone districts. DCFC EV Charging
Stations are not permitted in areas of residential uses found in the RMU-1 and RMU-2
zones.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Any parking space served by an EV Charging Port or any parking space used to site
EV Charging Stations or Equipment must be counted toward applicable parking minimums
as at least one (1) standard automobile parking space.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Any van-accessible parking space that is designated to accommodate a person in a wheelchair,
is served by an EV Charging Port, and is not designated as parking reserved for a
person with a disability under C.R.S. 42-4-1208 must be counted as at least two (2)
standard automobile parking spaces towards applicable parking minimums.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
The design of parking spaces and parking access for all EV Charging Projects shall
comply with the U.S. Access Board Design Recommendations for Accessible Electric Vehicle
Charging Stations or any applicable accessibility regulations issued by the federal
Department of Justice or Department of Transportation, or in state statute.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
For Accessory Use EV Charging Projects, EV Charging Stations and Equipment, and any
required screening material, shall be permitted within designated setbacks of the
Subject Property, as long as there are no encroachments into existing easements or
right-of-way.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Subject to EV plan approval, the appearance, materials, and dimensions of EV Charging
Stations and Equipment shall be determined by current EV Charging manufacturing standards
and utility design standards. Digital displays shall comply with the lighting standards
found in this Chapter. There shall be no audio associated with EV Charging equipment,
charging stations, or displays.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Screening for Accessory Use EV Charging Projects shall not be required except in the
following circumstances:
(1)
Screening may be required for EV Charging Equipment located along the perimeter of
a Subject Property and shall comply with screening requirements set forth in Chapter 15 , Articles 7 and 11 of this Code.
a.
Screening may only be required along the length of the EV Charging Equipment and parallel
to the property line between the EV Charging Equipment and the adjacent property.
b.
Screening dimensions shall be determined based upon the size and length of the EV
Charging Equipment.
c.
Screening materials shall be the same materials and be compatible with the principal
use, walls, fencing or landscaping materials or a combination of all, so long as there
are no encroachments into any right-of-way or easements.
d.
Any applicant may request an exception to screening requirements by demonstrating
in their application that site-specific conditions negate the need for visual separation,
which will be administratively reviewed.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Charging Stations proposed as part of an EV Charging Project must meet the site lighting
requirements, as defined in this Code. Lighting may be provided by existing on-site
lighting and/or by new lighting fixtures integrated into EV Charging Stations or Equipment
or by new separate light fixtures.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Existing standards for parking pavement markings and striping established within this
Code apply to EV Charging Projects.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
EV Charging Stations and EV Charging Equipment shall comply with the requirements
set forth in this Code.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
When existing landscape plants on a Subject Property are removed during the installment
of an Accessory Use EV Charging Project, they shall be replaced with similar plants
in close proximity to the charging station project so as to maintain the property's
compliance with landscaping standards for the property's existing uses, and in compliance
with this Code.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
Signage may be provided as part of an EV Charging Project to provide information about
charging types, voltages, fees, wayfinding, or other related information. Any signage
provided is subject to the applicable standards in this Code.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
If weather canopies are included as part of an EV Charging Project, they are subject
to the applicable siting, dimensional, setback, appearance, materials, and other standards
in this Code.
(Ord. 2025-1731 §1)
