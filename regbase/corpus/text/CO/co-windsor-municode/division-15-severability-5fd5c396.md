If any Section, subsection, Subsection, term or provision of this Franchise is determined
to be illegal, invalid or unconstitutional by any court or agency of competent jurisdiction,
such determination shall have no effect on the validity of any other Section, subsection,
Subsection, term or provision of this Franchise, all of which will remain in full
force and effect for the term of the Franchise.
(Ord. 2020-1619 §1)
