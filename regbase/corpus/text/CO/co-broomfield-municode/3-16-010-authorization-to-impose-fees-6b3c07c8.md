There is hereby imposed, pursuant to section 29-11-101, C.R.S., et seq., upon all
telephone exchange access facilities within the city an emergency telephone charge
in the amount of $0.17 per subscriber line per month for purposes of defraying the
cost of the E-911 emergency telephone service.
(Ord. 583 §1, 1984)
Telephone service suppliers providing telephone service in the city are hereby authorized
to collect the emergency telephone charge imposed by this chapter in accordance with
section 29-11-101, C.R.S., et seq.
(Ord. 583 §1, 1984)
