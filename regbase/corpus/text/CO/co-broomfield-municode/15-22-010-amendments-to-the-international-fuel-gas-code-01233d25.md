The following amendments are hereby made to the 2024 International Fuel Gas Code:
(A)
Chapter 1 is hereby deleted. Refer to chapter 15-03 , B.M.C. for administrative requirements.
(B)
Section 202 GENERAL DEFINITIONS, of the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code is amended
by defining "CERTIFIED PIPE WELDER" to read as follows:
CERTIFIED PIPE WELDER. A person who specializes in the welding of pipes and holds
a valid certificate of competency from a recognized testing laboratory based on the
requirements of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessels Code, Section IX.
(C)
Section 404 PIPING SYSTEM INSTALLATION, is amended by adding a new subsection 404.17,
Certified pipe welder, to read as follows:
404.17 Certified pipe welder. All welded joints in the piping system shall be welded
by a certified pipe welder as defined in Section 202.
(D)
Subsection 406.4.1 is amended to read as follows:
406.4.1 Test pressure. The test pressure to be used shall be no less than 10 psi (68.9
KPA) gauge pressure. For welding pipe and piping carrying gas at pressures in excess
of 14 inches, the test pressure shall not be less than 60 psi (413.4 KPA) gauge pressure.
(Ord. 1858 §1, 2008; Ord. 1926 §18, 2011; Ord. 1994 §15, 2014; Ord. 2042 §14, 2017; Ord. No. 2115 , 2-25-20; Ord. No. 2205 , 3-7-23; Ord. No. 2295 , § 9, 2-24-26)
