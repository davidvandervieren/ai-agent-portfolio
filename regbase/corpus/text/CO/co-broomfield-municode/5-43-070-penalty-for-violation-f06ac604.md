(A)
Applicant means the person applying for a secure transportation service license.
(B)
Authority means the Broomfield Board of Health.
(C)
Based means the location where the client is picked up for the service.
(D)
Catastrophe means a manmade or natural disaster or event.
(E)
Director means the director of public health or their designee.
(F)
License means the authorization issued by the director to operate a secure transportation
service in the city.
(G)
Patient means an individual experiencing a behavioral health crisis who is eligible for urgent
secure transportation services.
(H)
Secure transportation or secure transportation service shall have the same meaning as in Article 3.5 of Title 25, C.R.S.
(I)
To operate in the city and County of Broomfield means the providing of secure transportation service within the boundaries of the
city.
( Ord. No. 2208 , § 1, 4-11-23)
(A)
Secure transportation license required. No secure transportation service shall operate within the city unless a license has
been issued by the county in which the secure transportation service is based, unless
exempted by statute or regulation.
(B)
Secure transportation permit required. No secure transportation service vehicle shall operate within the city unless a permit
has been issued by the county in which the secure transportation service is based,
and the permit is displayed as directed by the director, unless exempted by statute
or regulation.
(C)
Exceptions to licensing and permitting requirements. The provisions of the licensing and permit subsections as set forth above shall not
apply to the following:
(1)
The exceptional emergency use of a private or public owned vehicle, including search-and-rescue
unit vehicles not ordinarily used in the formal act of transporting patients.
(2)
A vehicle rendering services as a secure transportation vehicle in the case of a major
catastrophe or emergency, when secure transportation vehicles with permits based in
the localities of catastrophe or emergency are insufficient to render the services
required.
(3)
Secure transportation services based outside this state, which are transporting a
patient in Colorado.
(4)
Secure transportation services and vehicles operating within the city that are licensed
and permitted under a reciprocity agreement with the city.
(D)
Insurance. No secure transportation service shall operate within the city unless it is covered
by insurance as set forth in state regulation.
(1)
Every insurance policy required shall contain a provision for continuing liability
thereunder to the full amount thereof, notwithstanding any recovery thereon, that
the liability of the insured shall not be affected by the insolvency or bankruptcy
of the insured, and that until a policy is revoked, the insurance company will not
be relieved from liability on account of nonpayment of premiums, failure to renew
license at the end of the year, or any act or omissions of the named insured. At any
time said insurance is required to be renewed, proof of renewal shall be provided
to the director.
(2)
A certificate of insurance, with the city identified as the certificate holder, shall
indicate the vehicles covered by the policy, type of insurance (vehicle and professional
liability, etc.) policy number or numbers, policy effective date, policy expiration
date, amount of coverage, and contain a provision that thirty days' prior written
notice of any cancellation or termination or revocation of said insurance policy shall
be given to the director.
(3)
Any changes in the status of vehicles listed on the certificate of insurance during
the licensing cycle shall be noted on a new certificate of insurance and forwarded
to the director within thirty days of the changes.
(4)
Notification of any changes in insurance shall be made in writing within thirty days
of such changes to the director by the licensee, to be followed with a certificate
of insurance as outlined in previous paragraphs. The director may require additional
proof of insurance at any time as needed in order to promote health, safety, and welfare
of residents of the city.
(E)
Secure transportation equipment. Each secure transportation vehicle shall be equipped as established by state regulations.
(F)
Secure transportation staffing. Each secure transportation service shall comply with Manager, Administrator, and
Staffing requirements as established by the state regulations. All updated certifications,
and all certifications for newly hired personnel, shall be sent to the director within
thirty days of completion or hire.
(G)
Secure transportation vehicle inspections. Through an intergovernmental agreement with participating cities and counties or
as otherwise designated by the city, an inspector will be hired to inspect each secure
transportation vehicle to be issued a permit under a valid license in the city once
a year or more often if required. Such inspection shall determine that each secure
transportation vehicle is being properly maintained and contains the equipment specified
in these regulations. Maintenance records shall be made immediately available upon
the director's or inspector's request. Prior to the inspection, the vehicle will obtain
an annual motor vehicle condition inspection as determined by the director. Such inspections
shall be in addition to other safety or motor vehicle inspections required to be made
under Colorado law and shall not excuse compliance with any requirements of any other
applicable Colorado law.
(H)
General regulations.
(1)
The city shall be authorized to promulgate and enforce such rules and regulations
as deemed necessary to provide for quality secure transportation services and ensure
compliance with state law and any resolution adopted by city council, which regulate
the operation and licensing of secure transportation services in the city.
(2)
All secure transportation services operating in the city must comply with all applicable
local, state, and federal statutes and regulations.
(3)
All secure transportation services shall notify the director in writing within fourteen
business days of any changes of the manager or administrator of record.
( Ord. No. 2208 , § 1, 4-11-23)
(A)
Application for secure transportation license. An application for a secure transportation license shall be submitted to the director
on a form as provided by the city, and shall be accompanied by:
(1)
A certificate of insurance as set forth and required in these regulations or proof
of self-insurance as required by the director; and
(2)
A personnel list to include the name of each employee, the employee's hire date, driver's
license number and expiration date, and readable copies of all training and certifications
required by state regulations.
(B)
Issuance of secure transportation service license and vehicle permits. Upon receipt of an application for a license to provide secure transportation service,
the director shall review the application and applicant's records. The director may
issue the applicant a license to operate a secure transportation service and issue
a permit for each secure transportation vehicle inspected, providing that the secure
transportation service staff, vehicle(s), equipment, and location comply with the
requirements of this chapter and all state statutes and regulations.
(C)
Vehicle permit fees. The applicant shall pay a vehicle permit fee for each secure transportation vehicle
permit to be issued or renewed as determined by the Authority.
(D)
License and permit duration.
(1)
Secure transportation service licenses shall be valid for a period of three years
following the date of issue.
(2)
Secure transportation vehicle permits shall be valid for a period of one year following
the date of issue, except that for a vehicle brought into service during a licensing
period and whose secure transportation service license expires in less than one year,
the permit shall expire at the same time the secure transportation service license
expires.
(E)
Secure transportation license and vehicle permit renewal. Unless revoked by the authority, a licensee may renew any such license or permit.
Applications for renewal shall be filed not less than thirty days before the date
the license or permit expires, except that the director, for good cause, may waive
the time requirement set forth in this subsection. Applicants who file for renewal
at least thirty days prior to the expiration of the applicant's license may continue
to operate until the director has taken final action to approve or deny the licensee's
renewal application. If a renewal application is not received at least thirty days
prior to expiration and the applicant's license expires, the applicant must cease
operation.
(F)
Transfer of license or permit. No license or permit shall be sold, assigned, or otherwise transferred.
(G)
Change of ownership. Change of ownership shall require a new application and license, with payment of
the same license fee as is required for an original application. Any sale or exchange
of stock in excess of 25% of the total outstanding stock of a corporation to anyone
other than an existing stockholder at the time of the original issuance of license
shall be deemed a change of ownership for the purpose of these regulations. Any change
of ownership or any transfer of stock ownership of 10% or more shall be reported in
writing to the director within thirty days of such change or transfer.
( Ord. No. 2208 , § 1, 4-11-23)
(A)
The authority may on its own motion or on complaint, after investigation and public
hearing at which the licensee shall be afforded an opportunity to be heard, suspend
or revoke any license or permit issued by the director pursuant to this chapter. After
such hearing, the authority may suspend any license or permit issued pursuant to these
regulations, for any portion of or for the remainder of its life. At the end of such
period, the person whose license or permit was suspended may apply for a new license
or permit as in the case of an original application. Suspension or revocation may
result from violation of:
(1)
Any provision of these regulations;
(2)
Any applicable law or regulation of the state or any applicable ordinance or regulation
of the city and evidence of such violation may be considered by the authority; or
(3)
Any rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to these regulations.
(B)
All hearings before the authority shall be public and every vote and official act
of the authority shall be public. The authority has the power to administer oaths
and issue subpoenas to require the presence of persons and the production of papers,
books, and records necessary to the determination of any hearing, which the authority
is authorized to conduct. The municipal court of the city shall enforce the subpoenas
of the authority and, upon good cause shown, shall enter its orders compelling witnesses
to attend and testify and produce books, records, or other evidence, and shall impose
penalties of punishment for contempt in case of failure to comply with such orders.
(C)
Written notice of temporary suspension or revocation as well as any required notice
of such hearing shall be given by certified mail to the licensee or permit holder
at the address contained in such license or permit application.
(D)
The director, pending any prosecution, investigation, or public hearing, may temporarily
suspend any license. Nothing in this section shall prevent the summary suspension
of such license for a period of not more than thirty days after such temporary suspension.
The licensee shall receive written notice of such temporary suspension, and a hearing
shall be held no later than ten days after such temporary suspension. If any license
is suspended or revoked, no part of the fees paid therefor shall be returned to the
licensee.
(E)
The director has the authority to suspend the permit for any individual secure transportation
vehicle if inspection reveals that the vehicle is not in compliance with the secure
transportation regulations. suspension of a permit for the operation of any individual
vehicle shall be made in writing at any time upon inspection by the individual appointed
by the director to perform such inspection. The individual appointed by the director
to perform such inspection may make written notification of suspension for the operation
of any individual vehicle upon inspection. Also, written notice shall be given by
the director to correct the deficiency within a reasonable amount of time before the
hearing to revoke the permit. The director shall have a hearing on the revocation
of such permit, and such hearing shall be conducted within ten days of the temporary
suspension. A suspension or revocation inspection must be conducted prior to resuming
operation on a reinstatement permit. The reinstatement permit finding may be made
by the director prior to the revocation hearing, upon a reinspection and finding by
the director that the secure transportation vehicle fully complies with the provisions
of these regulations. Upon issuance of a reinstatement permit, no hearing is necessary.
(F)
Upon revocation or suspension of a license issued under this chapter, all vehicle
permits issued to said company will be automatically revoked and all permits must
be returned to the director within forty-eight hours.
(G)
It shall be the duty of the director to notify local law enforcement authorities,
fire departments, and hospitals of revocation or suspension.
(H)
The following practices shall be unlawful and may be grounds for a suspension or revocation
of license after an investigation is completed:
(1)
Administering unnecessary treatment or supplies to a patient for the purpose of increasing
the patient's bill;
(2)
Conduct which constitutes a significant threat to the health or safety of the individuals
receiving secure transportation services.
( Ord. No. 2208 , § 1, 4-11-23)
(A)
In any legal action against a licensee in which it is alleged that plaintiff's injury,
illness, or incapacity was aggravated by, or that the plaintiff was otherwise injured
by the negligence of the licensee, no negligence shall be presumed because of such
allegations.
(B)
If a judgment is entered against such licensee, the licensee shall within thirty days
file a copy of such findings and order of the court with the director. The director
shall take note of such judgment for purposes of investigation and appropriate action
if there appears any violation of these regulations or of any state law.
( Ord. No. 2208 , § 1, 4-11-23)
The director shall develop a written complaint and investigation process to address
complaints against secure transportation services operating within the city, and to
investigate allegations of unlicensed secure transportation services or unpermitted
vehicles operating within the city.
