Except where a different penalty is specifically prescribed by an ordinance of the
city, any person found in violation of any provision of this title shall be subject
to a fine of not more than $500.00 per violation.
(Ord. 1393 §8, 1999; Ord. No. 2201 , § 5, 1-24-23)
