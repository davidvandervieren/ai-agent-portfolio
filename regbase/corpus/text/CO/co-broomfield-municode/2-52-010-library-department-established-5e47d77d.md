A library department and library fund are hereby established within the general governmental
funds of the city. The library fund will provide for the operations of the library
department as established under the provisions of section 8.8 of the Charter. Said library department shall be responsible for providing a full
range of public library services to the citizens of the city and, under inter-library
cooperative agreements, to the public at large. Said services shall be provided free
or under reasonable charges approved by the city council.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 2025 §1, 2016)
There shall be and is created a library board consisting of seven regular members
and one youth member. A member shall not receive a salary nor other compensation for
services as a board member, but necessary traveling and subsistence expenses actually
incurred may be paid from the library fund.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 665 §1, 1985; Ord. 762 §1, 1988; Ord. 770 §1, 1988; Ord.
951 §1, 1993; Ord. 1104 §1, 1995; Ord. 1302 §5, 1998; Ord. 1386 §2, 1998; Ord. 1413
§1, 1999; Ord. 1882 §6, 2007; Ord. 2025 §2, 2016; Ord. No. 2064, § 1, 2-13-18; Ord. No. 2128 , § 5, 6-23-2020)
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 26, 2-13-2024)
The powers of the library board shall be advisory only and the board shall have the
following duties:
(A)
To adopt rules and regulations for the conduct of meetings of the library board and
for the orderly operation of the city library.
(B)
To prepare and submit to council a master plan for the development and maintenance
of a modern library within the city and from time to time revise and amend the plan.
(C)
To review the annual library budget request prepared by the library director prior
to its submittal to the city manager and city council and make written recommendations
regarding approval or modification of the same.
(D)
To review and recommend to the city manager for acceptance or denial such gifts of
money or property or endowments as may be granted for library purposes and to take
steps as the library board may deem feasible to encourage grants or gifts in support
of the city library.
(E)
To recommend rules, regulations, terms, and conditions under which residents and nonresidents
of the city may use the books and other materials of the public library for enactment
by city council.
(F)
To review the qualifications of the final candidates for library director selected
by the city manager, interview the candidates when practical, and report on an advisory
basis to the city manager the board's preference.
(G)
To do all other acts necessary to assist in the orderly and efficient management of
the library as directed by council.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 2025 §3, 2016)
Effective the 1979 budget year, beginning January 1, 1979, all assets and property
of the library fund and library board shall be transferred to a library fund within
the general governmental funds of the city, with the exception of gifts and endowments
as provided for in section 2-52-030 , and all obligations and liabilities shall be assumed by the city library fund. Other
revenue sources include a mill levy amount set by the city council, fees, and other
revenues as budgeted.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 746 §1, 1987; Ord. 2025 §4, 2016; ; Ord. No. 2074 , § 1, 2018)
The library director and the library board shall provide an annual report to the city
council showing the number of books, periodicals, or other materials on hand, the
number loaned and borrowed, technology resources offered, the number of public programs,
and other such statistics and information and such suggestions as they deem of public
interest. A copy of this report shall be filed with the state librarian.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 2025 §5, 2016)
The library board shall meet monthly on the first Thursday of the month or at such
other time as shall be agreed upon. An annual meeting to organize and elect a chairperson,
vice chairperson, and secretary shall be held following the city council annual appointments
of Board and Commission members. The board may hold special meetings as the chairperson
of the board requests. A record of the minutes of each meeting shall be kept and given
to the cityclerk for public inspection and distribution to the city council.
(Ord. 330 §1, 1978; Ord. 1413 §2, 1999; Ord. 2025 §6, 2016)
The library department may collect fees for damaged and lost materials up to the catalog
price of the item. The library shall not collect fines or fees for overdue items.
(Ord. 897 §1, 1991; Ord. 1196 §18, 1996; Ord. 1460 §1, 2000; Ord. 1568 §17, 2001;
Ord. 1665 §1, 2002; Ord. 1707 §1, 2003; Ord. 2025 §8, 2016; Ord. 2074, § 2, 2018)
( Ord. No. 2166 , § 1, 11-9-21; Ord. No. 2298 , § 1, 4-14-2026)
