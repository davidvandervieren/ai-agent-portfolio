(a)
The City and County shall maintain a Personnel Merit System.
(b)
The Personnel Merit System shall include all city and county employees excluding the
City and County Manager, deputy and assistant City and County manager(s), department
heads, elective officers, appointees of Council, temporary employees, appointed members
of boards and commissions, and persons employed to make or conduct a special inquiry,
investigation, examination or installation or audit.
(c)
The Personnel Merit System shall provide any included, non-probationary employee who
has been subject to disciplinary action with a right to an appeal.
(Amendment passed 11/5/24 )
