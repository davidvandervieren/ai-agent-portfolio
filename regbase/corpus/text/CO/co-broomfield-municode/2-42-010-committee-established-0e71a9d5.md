There is hereby established an open space and trails advisory committee, which will
advise the city council on amendments to the open space element of the master plan.
The open space and trails advisory committee is charged with the responsibility of
making recommendations to the city council regarding the following:
(A)
The acquisition of open space parcels;
(B)
The location, type, and timing of trail segments and crossings;
(C)
Upon request of the department, assist and provide feedback on the Open Space, Parks,
Recreation, and Trails Action Plan, maintenance/management plans, or other special
projects;
(D)
Upon the request of the department, provide comments on the public land dedication,
open space, trails, and wildlife related to projects going through the development
review process; and
(E)
Upon the request of the department, provide feedback on department recommendations
for open space, trails, and wildlife projects in the CIP plan.
(Ord. 1018 §1, 1993; Ord. 1111 §1, 1995; Ord. 1414 §1, 1999)
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 21, 2-13-2024)
The membership of the open space and trails advisory committee will consist of seven
regular members at large and one youth member.
(Ord. 1018 §1, 1993; Ord. 1102 §1, 1995; Ord. 1302 §9, 1998; Ord. 1414 §1, 1999; Ord.
1699 §1, 2002; Ord. 1882 §4, 2007; Ord. No. 2064, § 1, 2-13-2018; Ord. No. 2128 , § 1, 6-23-2020)
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 22, 2-13-2024)
(A)
The members of the open space and trails advisory committee will meet at the call
of the city manager.
(B)
The open space and trails advisory committee will meet at the call of the chair or
as often as required in the bylaws. The open space and trails advisory committee will
hold a regular meeting at least twice annually. The open space and trails advisory
committee shall hold a joint meeting with the parks, recreation, and senior services
advisory committee, at least once annually, for purposes of coordination. The city
manager is directed to provide the open space and trails advisory committee with clerical
assistance and with a location at a city building to hold its meetings. All meetings
of the open space and trails advisory committee are subject to the provisions of part
4 of the Colorado Sunshine Act of 1972, as amended.
(Ord. 1018 §1, 1993; Ord. 1414 §1, 1999; Ord. No. 2064, § 1, 2-13-2018; Ord. No. 2128 , § 2, 6-23-2020)
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 23, 2-13-2024)
The open space and trails advisory committee may hold public hearings, if directed
by city council, on the acquisition of parcels for open space and trails. Notice of
all such public hearings will conform to the provisions of chapter 17-52 , B.M.C., insofar as may be practicable.
(Ord. 1018 §1, 1993; Ord. 1414 §1, 1999)
