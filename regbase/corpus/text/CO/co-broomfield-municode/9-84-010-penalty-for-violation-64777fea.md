Whenever in any section of this title, or any section of a rule or regulation promulgated
under this title the doing of any act is required, prohibited, or declared to be unlawful,
and no definite fine or penalty is provided for a violation thereof, any person who
is convicted of a violation of any such section shall, for each offense, be punished
as provided in chapter 1-12 , B.M.C.
(Ord. 15 §100, 1963)
