(A)
The Noxious Weed Advisory Board, as established by section 2-44-010 of the code, as it may be amended, shall cause to be prepared a noxious weed management
plan which may include approved and recommended measures and practices for the management,
control, elimination and disposal of local and state noxious weeds. Such management
plan, including amendments, shall be approved by the city council and shall govern
the management, control, elimination and disposal of local and state noxious weeds
by landowners within the City and County of Broomfield.
(B)
The noxious weed management plan shall be reviewed at regular intervals by the Noxious
Weed Advisory Board but at least once every three years as required by the Colorado
Noxious Weed Act.
(Ord. No. 2174 , § 2, 2-22-22)
Any weeds classified as Type A, B or C noxious weeds by the State of Colorado or weeds
classified by the City and County of Broomfield as noxious weeds found growing in
any lot or tract of land in the city are a public nuisance and shall be removed, contained
or managed as deemed appropriate by the city. Failure to comply with the city's notices
or orders issued pursuant to this chapter is a violation of this code.
(Ord. No. 2174 , § 2, 2-22-22)
