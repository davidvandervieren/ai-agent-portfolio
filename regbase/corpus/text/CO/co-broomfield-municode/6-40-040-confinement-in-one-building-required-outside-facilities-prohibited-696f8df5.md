It is unlawful for any person to maintain or to operate any pet shop within the city
without first having obtained a license as required by this chapter.
(Ord. 233 Art. IX §1, 1975)
Sections 6-36-030 through 6-36-060 , B.M.C. and section 6-36-080 through and including section 6-36-110 , B.M.C. shall apply in their entirety to pet shops.
(Ord. 233 Art. IX §2, 1975)
The annual license fee for pet shops shall be $50.00.
(Ord. 233 Art. IX §3, 1975)
Pet shops shall be confined in a building that is totally enclosed, and all animals
shall be confined exclusively to that building. The pet shop may not have outside
runs or pens, and none of the animals owned by the pet shop are to be harbored or
housed outside of the building at any time for any reason.
(Ord. 233 Art. IX §4, 1975)
It is unlawful for any pet shop to sell or to offer for sale any sick or injured animals.
(Ord. 233 Art. IX §5, 1975)
It is unlawful for any pet shop to sell or to offer for sale any unweaned animal,
or any animal that is so young or weak that its sale would be injurious to the animal.
(Ord. 233 Art. VI §6, 1975)
