The city council finds that since the boundaries of the city's five wards were last
changed, there has been rapid growth in population in the various wards.
(Ord. 978 §1, 1993; Ord. 1239 §1, 1997)
The purpose of this chapter is to change the boundaries of the various wards in compliance
with section 4.2 of the Charter.
(Ord. 978 §1, 1993; Ord. 1239 §1, 1997)
The location and boundaries of the five wards established by this chapter are shown
upon the "Municipal Election Map of The City and County of Broomfield" attached to
Ordinance No. 2134 and incorporated into this chapter by reference. The map, together
with everything shown thereon and all amendments thereto, shall be made a part of
this chapter as if fully set forth and described herein. Each change in the official
map shall be subject to the amendment procedures required for ordinances. The official
map shall be signed by the mayor or mayor pro tem and attested by the city clerk.
A copy of the official map is on file with the city clerk and is available for public
inspection in the office of the city clerk during regular city business hours.
(Ord. 978 §1, 1993; Ord. 1239 §1, 1997; Ord. 1531 §1, 2001; Ord. 1723 §1, 2003; Ord.
1919 §1, 2009; Ord. 2008 §1, 2015; Ord. No. 2134 , § 1, 8-25-2020)
No write-in vote for any municipal office shall be counted unless an affidavit of
intent has been filed with the city clerk by the person whose name is written in prior
to 5:00 p.m. sixty-four days before the day of the election indicating that such person
desires the office and is qualified to assume the duties of that office if elected.
(Ord. 886 §1, 1990; Ord. 1013 §1, 1993)
( Ord. No. 2204 , § 1, 5-23-2023)
The mayor and all councilmembers will be elected using a ranked voting method, as
defined in Title 1 of the C.R.S., beginning with the November 2, 2027 municipal election
and for all elections moving forward.
( Ord. No. 2155 , § 2, 7-13-2021; Ord. No. 2254 , § 1, 2-11-2025)
The purpose of this chapter is to ensure compliance with the Colorado Fair Campaign
Practices Act (C.R.S. § 1-45-101 et. seq.) with respect to municipal elections.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019)
This chapter applies to municipal elections.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019)
The city clerk shall adopt rules and procedures to review compliance with the Colorado
Fair Campaign Practices Act (C.R.S. § 1-45-101 et. seq.), including deadlines and
procedures for addressing violations and adjudicating complaints.
The city clerk has authority to determine whether a violation has occurred.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019)
The city clerk may hire hearing officers to review and adjudicate municipal campaign
finance complaints. Hearing officers are authorized to perform all duties with respect
to municipal campaign finance matters as set forth in the city clerk's procedures
or as delegated by the city clerk.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019)
If any candidate or committee fails to comply with the Colorado Fair Campaign Practices
Act, this chapter or rules promulgated in accordance with this chapter, the city clerk
may issue an order to show cause or cure.
Nothing in this section limits the authority of the city clerk to take any action
without first issuing an order.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019; Ord. No. 2171 , § 1, 5-10-2022)
(A)
If the city clerk finds that a violation of the Colorado Fair Campaign Practices Act,
or this chapter or rules promulgated in accordance with this chapter has occurred,
the city clerk may assess civil penalties in accordance with Colorado Department of
State (Secretary of State) published guidelines.
(B)
After issuance of an order to show cause or cure, a candidate or committee shall have
the opportunity for a hearing before the city clerk or hearing officer. Such hearing
shall be conducted in accordance with procedures adopted by the city clerk.
(Ord. No. 2094 , § 1, 8-20-2019; Ord. No. 2171 , § 1, 5-10-2022)
(A)
For any particular election cycle, no person shall make contributions, including contributions
in-kind, to a candidate and/or their candidate committees that, in the aggregate,
exceed the following amounts: Councilmember $1,250.00; Mayor $2,500.00.
(B)
No candidate committee shall accept any contributions, including contributions in-kind,
from any person if that person's contributions on behalf of the candidate have an
aggregate amount or value in excess of the amounts listed in (A). Any contribution
or portion thereof exceeds the limits in (A) shall be returned to the contributor
within seventy-two hours of receipt.
(C)
The limitations imposed by subsections (A) and (B) of this section shall not apply
to contribution of a candidate's personal funds to the candidate's own campaign, or
to any loan which is personally guaranteed by the candidate or is secured by property
owned by the candidate.
(D)
Each election-cycle limit on contributions described in subsection (A) of this section
shall be adjusted by an amount based upon the percentage change over a two-year period
in the United States bureau of labor statistics consumer price index for Denver-Boulder-Greeley,
all items, all consumers, or its successor index, rounded to the nearest lowest $5.00.
The first adjustment shall be done in the second quarter of 2024 and then every two
years thereafter. The clerk and recorder shall calculate such an adjustment in each
limit and specify the limits in rules promulgated in accordance with section 4-08-030 .
( Ord. No. 2171 , § 1, 5-10-2022)
(A)
In non-election years candidates, candidate committees and all other committees permitted
in accordance with state campaign finance laws and regulations shall file in accordance
with the schedule set forth by the Colorado Fair Campaign Practices Act; and
(B)
In election years candidates, candidate committees, independent expenditure committees,
issue committees and political committees shall file the following reports:
(1)
Monthly reports for each month for the complete months of July, August and September
which shall be filed no later than the fifth day of the next month; the July report
shall also include any contributions and expenditures prior to the month of July during
that election cycle; and
(2)
Pre-election reports for the reporting period from October 1 through October 15 due
on October 18 and for the reporting period from October 16 through October 31 due
on the November 1; and
(3)
A post-election report for contributions and expenditures for the reporting period
of November 1 through thirty days after the election, due thirty-five days after the
election.
(C)
If any due date in subsection (B) falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in
which Broomfield offices are closed, the report shall be due on the following business
day.
( Ord. No. 2171 , § 1, 5-10-2022; Ord. No. 2303 , § 1, 4-14-2026)
