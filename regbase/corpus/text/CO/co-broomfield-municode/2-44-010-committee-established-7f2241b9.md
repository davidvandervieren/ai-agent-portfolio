There is hereby established a parks, recreation and senior services advisory committee,
which will advise the city council on amendments to the parks and recreation portions
of the master plan and serve as the Broomfield Noxious Weed Advisory Board. The parks,
recreation and senior services advisory committee is charged with the responsibility
of making recommendations to the city council regarding the following:
(A)
Review and provide feedback on the parks, recreation, and senior services long range
plan for the development of programs and services.
(B)
In consultation with the appropriate City and County of Broomfield staff, identifying
and prioritizing potential land acquisitions.
(C)
Review annual parks, recreation, and senior services CIP plan and review recommendations
for new or upgraded departmental assets.
(D)
Upon the request of the department, participate, assist, and provide feedback on the
Open Space, Parks, Recreation and Trails Master Plan or other special projects.
(E)
Review and provide feedback on issues relating to the Broadlands Golf Course or any
cemetery in the City and County of Broomfield.
(F)
Possess and exercise the duties and authority of a local noxious weed advisory board
as provided by the Colorado Noxious Weed Act, as it may be amended from time to time.
(G)
Prepare a noxious weed management plan, pursuant to Chapter 8-45 .
(Ord. 1414 §2, 1999)
( Ord. No. 2163 , § 1, 10-12-21; Ord. No. 2174 , § 1, 2-22-22)
The membership of the parks, recreation and senior services advisory committee will
consist of seven regular members at large and one youth member.
(Ord. 1414 §2, 1999; Ord. 1760 §1, 2004; Ord. 1882 §5, 2007; Ord. No. 2064, § 1, 2-13-2018 ; Ord. No. 2128 , § 3, 6-23-2020)
( Ord. No. 2163 , § 1, 10-12-2021; Ord. No. 2211 , § 24, 2-13-2024)
The members of the parks, recreation and senior services advisory committee will meet
at the call of the city manager or designee, and as often as required in the bylaws.
The committee will hold a regular meeting at least twice annually. The committee shall
hold a joint meeting with the open space and trails advisory committee for purposes
of coordination at least once annually. All meetings of the parks, recreation and
senior services advisory committee are subject to the provisions of part 4 of the
Colorado Sunshine Act of 1972, as amended.
(Ord. 1414 §2, 1999; Ord. No. 2064, § 1, 2-13-2018; Ord. No. 2128 , § 4, 6-23-2020)
( Ord. No. 2163 , § 1, 10-12-2021; Ord. No. 2211 , § 25, 2-13-2024)
The parks, recreation, and senior services advisory committee may hold public hearings,
if directed by city council, on the acquisition of parcels for parks and recreation
purposes. Notice of all such public hearings will conform to the provisions of chapter 17-52 , B.M.C., insofar as may be practicable.
(Ord. 1414 §2, 1999)
( Ord. No. 2163 , § 1, 10-12-21)
