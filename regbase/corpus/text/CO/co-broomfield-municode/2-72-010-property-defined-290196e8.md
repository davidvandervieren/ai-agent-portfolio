For purposes of this chapter, Broomfield's property includes any personal property,
real property, and other property interests owned or held by the City and County of
Broomfield. Property includes any and all leasehold interests, mineral interests,
minerals, water rights, and water.
(Ord. 2039 §1, 2017)
No person shall take, appropriate, transfer, lease, or dispose of Broomfield's property
without the consent of Broomfield.
(Ord. 2039 §1, 2017)
The penalty for violation of this chapter shall be as prescribed in chapter 1-12 , B.M.C.
(Ord. 2039 §1, 2017)
