Editor's note— Ord. No. 2211 , § 1, adopted February 13, 2024, amended the title of ch. 2-61 to read as herein
set out. The former ch. 2-61 title pertained to boards and commission appointments.
This chapter applies to any permanent boards, committees, and commissions created
by city council unless specifically addressed elsewhere in the Broomfield Municipal
Code or as established by resolution approved by city council, in which case the more
specific provisions shall apply to that board, committee, or commission.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 2, 2-13-2024)
(A)
All board, committee, and commission appointments shall be by a majority vote of city
council.
(B)
Non-elected individuals appointed by city council to serve on any city board, committee,
or commission shall serve and be appointed solely to one board, committee, or commission
at any time. This limitation may be waived by a supermajority of eight affirmative
votes of the city council.
(C)
All appointees shall be residents of the city. If an appointee ceases to reside in
the city, their membership shall immediately terminate.
(D)
All appointees may continue to serve following the expiration of their terms until
a successor is duly qualified and appointed by city council.
(E)
Each appointed member is expected to attend all regularly scheduled meetings. Members
who have missed an excessive number of regularly scheduled meetings or who have three
or more consecutive unexcused absences may be removed by city council. Unexcused absences
are those that were not communicated to the board chair or to the city staff member
assigned to the board prior to the regularly scheduled meeting.
(F)
All appointees shall serve without compensation and shall not hold any other municipal
office.
(G)
Any member appointed may be removed by city council under the provisions of section 11.2 of the Charter.
( Ord. No. 2163 , § 2, 10-12-21; Ord. No. 2211 , § 3, 2-13-2024)
(A)
All regular appointments are for four-year, overlapping terms.
(B)
No regular member may serve more than two consecutive four-year terms.
(1)
Any regular member who has served at least twenty-four months of a term shall be considered
to have served a four-year term.
(2)
A person who has served two consecutive four-year terms may still serve an unlimited
number of consecutive alternate member terms.
(3)
The term limits in this section may be waived by a supermajority of eight affirmative
votes of the city council.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 4, 2-13-2024)
(A)
City council may appoint two alternate members to each board, committee, or commission,
who may serve with all of the powers and duties of an absent regular member.
(B)
All alternate member appointments shall be for two-year terms.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 5, 2-13-2024)
(A)
A person appointed to a youth position in any city board, committee, or commission
must be at least fourteen, but less than eighteen, years old at the time of the appointment.
A person appointed to any youth position in any city board, committee, or commission
may serve for the entire term of the appointment, even after turning eighteen years
old.
(B)
All regular youth appointments are for two-year terms.
(C)
City council may appoint one alternate youth member to any city board, committee,
or commission where a youth member is appointed. An alternate youth member may only
serve in place of an absent youth member. When an alternate youth member is serving,
the alternate youth member shall have all of the powers and duties of the absent youth
member.
(D)
No person younger than eighteen years old may be appointed to any quasi-judicial board,
committee, or commission.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 6, 2-13-2024)
(A)
Every board, committee, and commission shall have a chairperson and vice chairperson,
who shall be elected annually by majority vote of the board, committee, or commission
at the first meeting following the city council annual appointments of board and commission
members. The officers shall assume their duties upon election, and shall serve one-year
terms. Officers must be members of the board, committee, or commission and may succeed
themselves in office. No two offices may be held by the same person.
(B)
No member appointed to a youth or alternate position may serve as an officer of any
board, committee, or commission, except when elected the temporary chairperson for
a meeting where the chairperson and vice chairperson are absent.
(C)
Every board, committee, and commission shall develop bylaws and regulations as necessary
to carry out its stated function.
(D)
Every board, committee, and commission shall conduct its meetings according to Robert's
Rules of Order Newly Revised.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 7, 2-13-2024)
A quorum of any board, committee, or commission shall be a simple majority of the
number of regular member and regular youth member appointments to that board, committee,
or commission, as authorized by this code. The act of a majority of the members present
at a meeting shall be the act of the board, committee, or commission. A board, committee,
or commission may increase its quorum or voting threshold in its rules of procedure.
( Ord. No. 2211 , § 8, 2-13-2024)
