There is hereby created a stormwater activity enterprise denominated the City of Broomfield
Colorado Stormwater Enterprise ("Stormwater Enterprise"). The city council shall be
the governing body of the Stormwater Enterprise.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 2, 10-22-2024)
The Stormwater Enterprise is authorized to issue or reissue bonds, notes, or other
obligations payable from the revenues derived or to be derived from the function,
service, benefits or facility or from any other available funds of the Stormwater
Enterprise.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 2, 10-22-2024)
The Stormwater Enterprise is authorized to conduct any stormwater activity under C.R.S.
§ 37-45.1-101 et seq, as it may be amended, and any other applicable law.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 2, 10-22-2024)
The enterprise shall be a government-owned business. The enterprise shall not receive
more than 10% of its annual revenue in grants from all Colorado state and local governments
combined.
( Ord. No. 2255 , §§ 2, 4, 10-22-2024)
The Stormwater Enterprise shall finance various stormwater activities. The revenues
from the stormwater activities shall be deposited by the Enterprise into a fund designated
as the City and County of Broomfield Colorado Stormwater Activity Enterprise Fund.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 2, 10-22-2024)
The Stormwater Enterprise shall not levy a tax which is subject to section 20(4) of
Article X of the Colorado Constitution or any other general taxes.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 2, 10-22-2024)
