Editor's note— Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, adopted August 12, 2025, amended the title of ch. 17-18 to read as herein set
out. The former ch. 17-18 title pertained to R-5 High-Density Residential District.
The demand for multiple-unit housing should be accommodated primarily through this
district and the R-7 district. The location of this zone should occur in areas where
moderate density development can assist in providing convenience to its occupants
without negatively affecting the surrounding neighborhood.
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §1, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
The following uses are permitted by right in the R-5 district:
(A)
Multiple-unit dwellings;
(B)
Churches mosques, synagogues, temples, and other religious facilities;
(C)
Public and private schools for elementary, junior, and senior high school education;
(D)
Public and private recreational areas;
(E)
Aboveground or underground distribution and transmission lines for public utilities
when said lines primarily serve the city;
(F)
Clubhouses and associated community spaces when associated with a residential community;
(G)
Home occupations as defined in section 17-32-020
(H)
Accessory buildings and uses.; and
(I)
Group Living Homes consistent with the standards established within Chapter 17-33 of the Broomfield Municipal Code.
(Ord. 744 §1, 1987)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
The following uses are permitted by special review in the R-5 district:
(A)
Other public and private recreational facilities not permitted by right;
(B)
Colleges and universities;
(C)
Mobile home communities, subject to the provisions of chapter 17-42 , B.M.C.;
(D)
Nursing homes, and senior living facilities;
(E)
Medical and dental clinics;
(F)
Offices;
(G)
Public utility substations and major transmission lines when said lines primarily
serve areas other than Broomfield. Substations shall not contain major office, repair,
or storage space;
(H)
Water and sewer facilities and stations including storage; and
(I)
Child day care facilities;
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §3, 1973; Ord. 257 Art. 1 §3, 1975; Ord. 968 §9, 1993)
( Ord. No. 2123 , § 9, 5-12-20; Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
Maximum density in the R-5 district shall not exceed forty units per gross acre.
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §4, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)

Nonresidential | 20,000 square feet

(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §5, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
Maximum building height in the R-5 district shall not exceed four stories or fifty
feet. including rooftop access structures Accessory buildings shall be a maximum of
twenty-five feet. Maximum height for buildings adjacent to R-1 zoned residential properties
shall be limited to a maximum height of thirty-five feet within thirty feet of the
shared property line.
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §6, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
In the R-5 district, a minimum of 15% of the site shall be developed and maintained
as private open area for use by the occupants of the project.
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §7, 1973; Ord. 1364 §3, 1998)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
Use of land in the R-5 district shall also conform to the parking, signing, and other
provisions of this title. An overall site plan shall be provided with all building
permits to ensure consistency with the standards established in this chapter. Engineering
reports may be requested as needed.
(Ord. 149 Art. 11 §8, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 2, 8-12-2025)
