No door-to-door solicitation for the purpose of selling goods, wares, merchandise,
or services, or anything of value for present or future delivery or performance, shall
be permitted within the city before 9:00 a.m. or after 9:00 p.m.
(Ord. 292 §11, 1977)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 2, 6-11-19)
(A)
If a tenant provides evidence of adverse financial or health related hardship because
of the COVID-19 emergency, it shall be unlawful for any landlord or property management
entity to assess a monetary sum, fee, or any other penalty against a tenant or mobile
home owner as a result of failure to timely pay any portion of rent. Any fee or penalty
assessed on or after the date set forth in subsection (D) below shall apply only to
rent due on or after that date.
(B)
Evidence of hardship could include a lay off notice, a letter or other statement from
the tenant's employer attesting that the tenant's employment and associated compensation
has been suspended, reduced, or terminated as a result of the COVID-19 emergency;
documentation of the tenant's receipt of unemployment benefits; or documentation showing
that the tenant or a member of the tenant's household who contributes to the monthly
rent payment has been diagnosed with, or has been hospitalized or received medical
care for, COVID-19; or other evidence of financial or health related hardship related
to the COVID-19 emergency.
(C)
Nothing in this ordinance shall be construed as relieving an individual from their
obligation to make rent payments.
(D)
This ordinance shall remain in effect until August 31, 2021 or when the Governor rescinds
the declaration of a disaster emergency related to COVID -19 in the state of Colorado,
whichever occurs first.
( Ord. No. 2135 , § 2, 8-11-20; Ord. No. 2152 , § 2, 3-30-21)
Any person who fails to comply with this chapter, upon conviction, shall be punishable
as provided in chapter 1-12 , B.M.C.
( Ord. No. 2135 , § 2, 8-11-20; Ord. No. 2152 , § 2, 3-30-21)
