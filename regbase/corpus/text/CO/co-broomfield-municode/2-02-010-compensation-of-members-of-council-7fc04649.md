(A)
Effective November 14, 2017, for those council members elected at the general municipal
election held on November 7, 2017, and for any council member thereafter duly elected
or chosen prior to November 2, 2021, each such council member shall receive the sum
$800.00 per month or any part thereof.
(B)
Commencing November 14, 2017, any council member whose term of office commenced after
the November 7, 2017 general municipal election but prior to November 2, 2021 shall
receive an additional $100.00 per month or any part thereof while serving as mayor
pro tem.
(C)
For those council members elected at the general municipal election held on November
2, 2021, and for any council member thereafter duly elected or chosen, each such council
member shall receive the sum of $1,200.00 per month or any part thereof.
(D)
Any council member who is duly elected or chosen to serve as mayor pro tem on or after
the November 2, 2021 general municipal election shall receive an additional $300.00
per month or any part thereof while serving as mayor pro tem.
(Ord. 605 §1, 1985; Ord. 1157 §1, 1995; Ord. 1535 §1, 2001; Ord. 2024 §1, 2016)
( Ord. No. 2165 , § 1, 10-26-21)
(A)
Effective November 14, 2017, for the mayor elected at the general municipal election
held on November 7, 2017 and for any mayor thereafter duly elected prior to November
2, 2021, such mayor shall receive the sum of $1,100.00 per month or any part thereof.
(B)
For the mayor elected at the general municipal election held on November 2, 2021 and
for any mayor thereafter duly elected, such mayor shall receive the sum of $1,750.00
per month or any part thereof.
(Ord. 605 §1, 1985; Ord. 1157 §2, 1995; Ord. 1535 §2, 2001; Ord. 2024 §2, 2016)
( Ord. No. 2165 , § 2, 10-26-21)
(A)
The compensation for the council and mayor as set forth in this section shall be reviewed
by council at least once in 2022 and then at least once every two years thereafter
during even number years with respect to any such terms and, if applicable, modified
by ordinance.
(B)
Starting in 2023, the amount of compensation for the council and mayor shall automatically
increase every two years on July 1st by an amount equal to the average percentage
increase in wages provided in the previous year to those city employees covered by
the personnel merit system. Any increase made pursuant to this subsection (B) will
take effect at the beginning of a member's new term of office and would not take effect
during a member's term of office.
(Ord. 605 §1, 1985)
( Ord. No. 2165 , § 3, 10-26-21)
Editor's note— Ord. No. 2165 , § 3, adopted October 26, 2021, amended the title of § 2-02-030 to read as herein
set out. The former § 2-02-030 title pertained to when effective.
(A)
Pursuant to section 4.8 of the Charter, the council hereby orders that the mayor and councilmembers be paid
their actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties of
office upon submitting evidence of such expenses to the finance department.
(B)
Such submission to the finance department shall be on a form provided by the city
manager to the mayor and councilmembers. The mayor and councilmembers shall include
either a receipt or other evidence of expenditure with each form submitted to the
finance department. For each expense submitted to the finance department, the mayor
or councilmember shall include on the form a concise written statement of the purpose
of the expense. The mayor or councilmembers may request payment by the city in advance
for anticipated expenses but shall refund any unspent moneys to the city and account
for any expenditures by a concise written statement on the form.
(C)
When either the mayor or any councilmember attends or participates in a conference,
seminar, training session, or meeting related to city business and the expense therefor
is reimbursed by the city, the mayor or any such councilmember will submit a written
or oral report to the city council regarding the matters or issues discussed at the
conference, seminar, training session, or meeting. If two or more members of the city
council attend or participate in the same conference, seminar, training session, or
meeting, no such written or oral report is required to be submitted to the city council.
(Ord. 605 §1, 1985; Ord. 1052 §1, 1994)
For the mayor and council members elected at the general municipal election held on
November 7, 2023, and for any mayor and any council member thereafter duty elected,
such elected officials shall be eligible for medical, dental and vision insurance
benefits as available to benefited city employees at the same contribution rate and
eligibility period as such employees.
( Ord. No. 2206 , § 1, 2-28-23)
