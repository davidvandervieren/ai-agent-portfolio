Editor's note— Ord. No. 2210 , § 1, adopted May 9, 2023, amended the title of ch. 5-28 to read as herein set out.
The former ch. 5-28 title pertained to alcoholic liquor.
This chapter is known and may be cited as the "Broomfield Alcohol Beverages Code."
Reference to the Broomfield alcohol beverages code and the applicable section or sections
thereof shall be sufficient when citing the provisions of this chapter in any legal
document, including, but not limited to, summons, subpoena, pleading, summons and
complaint, and memorandum.
(Ord. 884 §1, 1990; Ord. 1013 §1, 1993)
( Ord. No. 2210 , § 2, 5-9-2023)
The city council declares that the purpose of this chapter is to provide for the regulation,
control, and licensing of the sale at retail of alcohol beverages within the city,
and that such matters are in the interest of and are those in which the city has responsibility
for the public health, safety, and welfare.
(Ord. 884 §1, 1990)
( Ord. No. 2210 , § 3, 5-9-2023)
(A)
As used in this chapter, unless the context clearly requires otherwise, the following
words and terms shall have the meanings set forth in this section:
(1)
Applicant means and includes:
a.
If an individual, that person making an application for a license under this chapter;
b.
If a partnership, all the partners of the partnership which is making application
for a license under this chapter; or
c.
If a corporation, the president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, the directors,
manager, and each stockholder owning more than 10% of the stock of the corporation
making an application under this chapter.

Attach or attached means the connection of any licensed premises to a communal outdoor dining area or
adjoining common consumption area.
(3)
Authority or licensing authority means the local licensing authority as set forth in the code.

(5)

Common consumption area shall have the same meaning as in the Colorado Liquor Code.
(7)
Communal outdoor dining area shall have the same meaning as in the Colorado Liquor Code.
(8)
Entertainment district shall have the same meaning as in the Colorado Liquor Code.
(9)
Investigator means the liquor license investigator, as set forth in section 5-28-080 , B.M.C.

administer the transactions and acts of servants of the establishments governed by
this chapter.

as applicable, in Title 44, Articles 3 through 5, C.R.S., and in chapter 1-04 of this
code.

The city clerk shall post public notice on the city's website not less than thirty

non-liquor licensed establishment within the common consumption area shall post notice

Prior to certifying the promotional association, the authority shall consider:
(1)
The reasonable requirements of the neighborhood, the desires of the adult inhabitants

(2)
The sufficiency of the proposed plans to ensure that the proposed common consumption

(3)
The sufficiency of the proposed plans to prevent the consumption of alcohol beverages

(G)
Once certified by the authority, the promotional association may operate a common
consumption area consistent with the terms of the application submitted and any conditions
of approval established by the authority. Certification shall be valid for twelve
months.
(H)
A common consumption area must be a single contiguous area and may not overlap with
another common consumption area.
(I)
A promotional association shall apply for recertification not less than sixty days
prior to the expiration of its current certification, on a form provided by the city
clerk.
(1)
For a promotional association's first application for recertification, the city clerk
shall set the application for a public hearing with the authority. At least one member
of the board of directors of the promotional association shall appear at the public
hearing.
(2)
For a promotional association's second and subsequent application for recertification,
The city clerk may approve any application if it is substantially similar to the promotional
association's current operation and no potential cause for denial exists. The city
clerk, in its sole discretion, may refer any application for recertification to the
authority for consideration, however the city clerk shall refer the application to
the authority if any condition to deny an application appears to be true.
(J)
An application for certification or recertification of a promotional association may
be denied after a public hearing if:
(1)
Any member of the promotional association, or the promotional association itself,
has violated any term of this chapter, any law or regulation involving the sale of
alcohol beverages, or any condition placed upon its license within the twelve months
preceding the application;
(2)
Any member of the promotional association, or the promotional association itself,
has had a liquor license suspended or revoked within the twelve months preceding the
application;
(3)
The common consumption area is not compatible with the reasonable requirements of
the neighborhood and the needs of the adult residents of the neighborhood, or the
area does not maintain support of the business community within the neighborhood;
(4)
The information and evidence available to the authority shows that the common consumption
area will adversely impact the health, welfare, or public safety of the neighborhood;
(5)
The information and evidence available to the authority fails to establish that the
procedures and security plans will prevent the consumption of alcohol beverages by
underage persons;
(6)
The common consumption area does not have sufficient barriers to prohibit vehicular
traffic and limit pedestrian access; or
(7)
The common consumption area or promotional association or the application fails to
comply with any state or local law or regulation.
(K)
In addition to any other penalty prescribed by the Broomfield Municipal Code, the
authority may, or its own motion or on complaint, and after a hearing at which the
alleged violator is afforded an opportunity to be heard, decertify a promotional association;
temporarily or permanently close a common consumption area; temporarily suspend a
license or certification; or detach a business from a common consumption area for
any of the following reasons:
(1)
Any violation of state or local law or regulation regarding the sale or permitted
consumption of alcohol beverages;
(2)
Any fact or condition existing that would result in the denial of certification or
recertification of a promotional association; or
(3)
Any violation of a condition placed on the operation of a common consumption area.

The city clerk may approve any application to reduce the area of a common consumption
area or to detach a licensee from a common consumption area, however the city clerk
may, in its sole discretion, refer the matter to the authority.
(2)
Upon receipt of a completed application to attach to or expand a common consumption
area, the city clerk shall schedule a public hearing with the authority not less than
thirty days after receipt of the completed application.
(3)
Public notice for a hearing on an application to attach to or expand a common consumption
area shall be consistent with Colorado Liquor Code requirements for new license applications.
Proof of posting notice shall be sent to the city clerk to be added to the application.
(4)
Prior to authorizing the attachment of a new licensee to a common consumption area
or authorizing the expansion of a common consumption area, the authority shall consider
the following:
(a)
The reasonable requirements of the neighborhood, the desires of the adult inhabitants
of the neighborhood, and the position of the business community in the neighborhood;
(b)
The sufficiency of the updated plans to ensure that the proposed common consumption
area will not adversely impact the health, welfare, or public safety of the neighborhood;
(c)
The sufficiency of the updated plans to prevent the consumption of alcohol beverages
by underage persons.
(C)
All applications made under this section shall be accompanied by the respective fee
and the promotional association shall provide updates to all documents included in
its application to reflect the changes requested in the application. No application
shall be considered complete without all changed documents from the promotional association.
(D)
An applicant applying for an initial liquor license or a transfer of ownership may
contemporaneously apply to attach to a common consumption area. The authority may
choose to hear both applications at the same public hearing.
