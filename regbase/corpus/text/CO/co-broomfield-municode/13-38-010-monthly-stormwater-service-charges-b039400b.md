(A)
Residential stormwater service charges .
(1)
Flat monthly charge shall be charged as set forth below:

Customer Type | Flat Monthly Charge
Single-Family Home | $12.00
Multi-Family: Townhome I Duplex | $9.60
Multi-Family: Apartment | $7.20

(B)
Nonresidential stormwater service charges . All nonresidential customers shall be charged monthly based on the amount of impervious
square feet on the customer's property. The monthly charge will be equal to $2.88
per 1,000 impervious square feet.
(C)
Rates effective until changed . All monthly stormwater service charges and nonresidential stormwater service charges
established by this chapter 13-38 shall be in accordance with the rates and schedule set forth herein and shall remain
in effect until said charges are amended or changed by the city council.
( Ord. No. 2255 , § 3, 10-22-2024; Ord. No. 2286 , § 6, 10-28-2025)
Editor's note— Ord. No. 2286 , § 7, adopted Oct. 28, 2025, provides for an effective date of Jan. 1, 2026.
