Editor's note— Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, adopted August 12, 2025, amended the title of ch. 17-16 to read as herein set
out. The former ch. 17-16 title pertained to R-3 Medium-Density Residential District.
It is intended that the R-3 district be applied in those instances where residential
uses such as small lot single-unit residential, townhouses, duplexes, triplexes or
low-density multiple-unit developments are desired.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §1, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)
The following uses are permitted by right in the R-3 district:
(A)
One-unit dwellings attached and detached;
(B)
Multiple-unit dwellings; including but not limited to duplexes, triplexes and quadplexes;
(C)
Churches mosques, synagogues, temples, and other religious facilities;
(D)
Public and private schools for elementary, junior, and senior high school education;
(E)
Public and private recreational areas;
(F)
Aboveground and underground distribution and transmission lines for public utilities
when said lines primarily serve the city;
(G)
Home occupations as defined in section 17-32-020 ;
(H)
Accessory buildings and uses; including accessory dwelling units consistent with the
standards established within section 17-32-160 ;
(I)
Clubhouses and associated community spaces when associated with a residential community;
and
(J)
Group Living Homes consistent with the standards established within Chapter 17-33 of the Broomfield Municipal Code.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §2, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)
The following uses are permitted by special review in the R-3 district:
(A)
Child day care facility;
(B)
Other public and private recreational facilities not permitted by right;
(C)
Mobile home communities subject to the provisions of chapter 17-42 ;
(D)
Public utility substations and major transmission lines when said lines primarily
serve areas other than Broomfield. Substations shall not contain major office, repair,
or storage space;
(E)
Water and sewer facilities and stations including storage.
(F)
Clubhouses and community spaces when associated with a principal residential use.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §3, 1973; Ord. 257 Art. 1 §§2(part), 3(part), 1975; Ord. 968 §8,
1993)
( Ord. No. 2123 , § 8, 5-12-20; Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)
Maximum density in the R-3 district shall not exceed twenty units per gross acre.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §4, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)

Maximum building height in the R-3 district shall not exceed forty-five feet including
rooftop access structures. Accessory buildings shall be a maximum of twenty-five feet.
Maximum height for buildings adjacent to R-1 zoned residential properties shall be
limited to a maximum height of thirty-five feet within thirty feet of the shared property
line.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §6, 1973)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)
In the R-3 district, developments with four or more dwellings on one property shall
require a minimum of 25% of the site s maintained as private open area for use by
the occupants of the project.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §7, 1973; Ord. 1364 §2, 1998)
(Ord. No. 2275 , § 1, 8-12-2025)
Use of land in the R-3 district shall also conform to the parking, signing, and other
provisions of this title. An overall site plan shall be provided with all building
permits to ensure consistency with the standards established in this chapter. Engineering
reports may be requested as needed.
(Ord. 149 Art. 10 §8, 1973)
