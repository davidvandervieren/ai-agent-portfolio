As provided for in Article XX, section 10 of the Constitution of the State of Colorado,
on and after November 15, 2001, all territory in the municipal boundaries of the city
of Broomfield shall be detached from the counties of Adams, Boulder, Jefferson, and
Weld and shall be consolidated into a single county and municipal corporation with
the name "The City and County of Broomfield." Any reference in this Charter to "City"
or to the "City of Broomfield" shall mean The City and County of Broomfield. (Amendment
passed 11/6/01).
