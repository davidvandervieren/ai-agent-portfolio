(A)
It is unlawful for any owner, keeper, or controller of any animal to refuse or fail
to remove promptly excrement deposited by said animal upon any common thoroughfare,
street, sidewalk, play area, park, other public property, or any private property
when permission of the owner or tenant of said property has not been obtained; and
(B)
It is unlawful for any animal owner, keeper or controller to fail to remove promptly
all fecal wastes from private premises as necessary to prevent diseases, hazards,
and odors.
(Ord. 993 §10, 1993)
