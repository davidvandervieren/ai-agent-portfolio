(A)
It shall be unlawful for any solicitor, as defined in section 5-20-020 of this chapter, to engage in business within the city without first obtaining a
license as provided in this chapter; except that the provisions of this chapter shall
not apply to established delivery routes, mobile food vending and food trucks, or
persons calling by appointment.
(B)
The following types of organizations and individuals selling goods or services on
their behalf are not required to obtain a license under this chapter but shall otherwise
comply with section 5-20-140 of this chapter:
(1)
State and local governmental departments, agencies and subdivisions, including public
schools;
(2)
State-accredited private schools and academies; and
(3)
Charitable, civic, patriotic, religious, educational, recreational, fraternal or cultural
organizations which are tax exempt pursuant to Section 501(c) of the United States
Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.
(Ord. 35 §1, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
(A)
Solicitor means any individual who sells goods or services or takes orders for the sale or
future delivery of goods or services at any residence in the city
(B)
Solicit means to sell goods or services or take orders for the sale or future delivery of
goods or services.
(C)
Residence means any home, apartment, condominium unit or other building or part thereof, occupied
in whole or in part, as the dwelling place of one or more persons.
(Ord. 35 §2, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
Applicants for a license or renewal of a license under this chapter shall file an
application with the city clerk on forms provided by the city with the following information:
(A)
Name, date of birth, personal identifying information, and valid identification;
(B)
Address, both personal and business;
(C)
A brief description of the nature of the business and the goods or services to be
sold or delivered;
(D)
If a vehicle is to be used, a description of the same, together with the license number
or other means of identification;
(E)
A statement as to whether or not the applicant has been convicted of any crime, misdemeanor,
or violation of any municipal ordinance, the nature of the offense, and the punishment
or penalty assessed therefore;
(F)
Credentials from the person, firm, or corporation for which the applicant proposes
to do business, authorizing the applicant to act as a representative;
(G)
Such other reasonable information as to the identity or character of the person having
the management or supervision of the applicant's business, or the method or plan of
doing such business, as the city clerk may deem proper to fulfill the purpose of this
chapter in the protection of the public good.
(Ord. 35 §3, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
The application fee for each solicitor license or license renewal shall be set by
the city clerk, and must be paid at the time of application or renewal. Any business
associated with the applicant must obtain a license as required under section 3-04-080,
BMC and comply with all state and local laws and regulations.
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
(A)
Upon receipt of the completed application required in section 5-20-030 , the application shall be referred to the chief of police, who shall cause an investigation
to be made of the application and the applicant's criminal background.
(B)
The chief of police, or their designee, shall provide a recommendation for approval
or denial to the clerk.
(C)
Each of the following are grounds for denial:
(1)
Applicant has provided false information or misrepresented a material fact in connection
with the application;
(2)
Applicant has failed to comply with the requirements of this chapter;
(3)
Applicant has been convicted of a felony or any crime involving moral turpitude, such
as theft, fraud, assault, menacing, harassment, battery, sexual offenses, or any crime
of a similar nature;
(4)
Applicant has been convicted of any crime while engaged in solicitation within the
City;
(5)
Within the past five years, applicant has been denied a Broomfield solicitor's license
or has had their license suspended or revoked.
(D)
Unless the city clerk finds grounds for denial, the clerk shall issue a license. The
license is effective from the date of issuance and expires one year after the date
of issuance.
(E)
If the city clerk denies the application, the clerk shall notify the applicant in
writing, stating the reasons for the denial, and shall mail such notice to the applicant
at the address provided on the application.
(Ord. 35 §4, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
The city clerk shall issue a badge for each individual licensed under this chapter.
The badge shall state "Licensed Solicitor" and contain the signature and seal of the
city clerk. The badge shall show the name and address of the licensee, the date of
expiration, and the license number. Every solicitor shall conspicuously display their
badge at all times when engaged in the business for which they are licensed.
(Ord. 35 §5, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)

Every solicitor, shall be required to make an individual application and secure a
license, which shall be issued in the individual's name. No license shall be transferable
or used by any other person.
(Ord. 35 §7, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
Authority as used in this section means the local licensing authority.
(A)
Licenses issued under the provisions of this chapter may be suspended or revoked by
the authority, after notice and hearing, for any of the following causes:
(1)
Fraud, misrepresentation, or false statement contained in the application for license;
(2)
Fraud, misrepresentation, or false statement made in the course of carrying on the
business;
(3)
Any violation of this chapter;
(4)
Conviction of any crime involving moral turpitude; or
(5)
Conducting business in an unlawful manner, or in such manner as to constitute a breach
of the peace, or to constitute a menace to the health, safety, or general welfare
of the public.
(B)
Notice of the hearing for suspension or revocation of a license shall be given in
writing, setting forth specifically the grounds of complaint and the time and place
of hearing. Such notice shall be mailed to the licensee at their last known address
at least five days prior to the date set for hearing.
(Ord. 35 §8, 1963)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
Any person whose application for a solicitor's license has been denied by the city
clerk shall have the right to appeal to the authority. The applicant may request that
the authority review the denial by submitting a written request, setting forth fully
the grounds for appeal, to the city clerk no later than ten days after issuance of
the notice of denial. The authority shall set a time and place for a hearing on such
appeal, and notice of the hearing shall be given to the applicant in the same manner
as is provided in section 5-20-080 , B.M.C. for notice of hearing on suspension or revocation. The decision and order
of the authority on such appeal shall be final and conclusive.
(Ord. 35 §9, 1963; Ord. 1770 §1, 2004)
(Ord. No. 2087 , § 1, 6-11-19)
