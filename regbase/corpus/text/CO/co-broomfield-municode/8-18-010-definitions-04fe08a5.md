(A)
Burn permit. A burn permit required by the North Metro Fire Rescue District or the Colorado Department
of Public Health and Environment as administered by the Broomfield Public Health and
Environment Department for the open burning of:
1.
Yard waste, tree trimmings, or other landscape materials by a land and homeowner;
2.
Agricultural clearing of irrigation ditches or otherwise preparing land for crop cultivation;
or
3.
Bonfires.
(B)
Fire ban/fire restriction . A declaration by the State or the chief of police in conjunction with the North
Metro Fire Rescue District restricting outdoor burning and the use of open flame.
Fire ban and fire restriction levels include:
(1)
Level 1 - Fire restriction . .....Restrictions required due to severe, dry conditions.
The following activities are prohibited during Level 1 Fire Restrictions:
a.
Building, maintaining, attending, or using any fire or recreational campfire or open
flame on public or private property, except as modified below.
b.
Launching or releasing model rockets and using tethered or non-tethered sky lanterns
or similar devices.
c.
The use of fire and flame for commercial purposes outdoors (e.g., welding, blasting,
operating a blow torch, etc.) without first obtaining the required permit.
d.
Use of any fireworks or non-fireworks (e.g., toy caps, sparklers, highway flares,
etc.) defined in B.M.C. Chapter 9-76 . Public firework displays with a permit as required by B.M.C. 9-76-040 are allowed.
The following activities are allowed during Level 1 Fire Restrictions:
a.
Persons with a burn permit from the fire chief that specifically authorizes the otherwise
prohibited use of open flame or open burning.
b.
Any outdoor fires or open flame contained within a liquid fuel or gas stove, and fireplaces
within buildings.
c.
Use of portable or fixed outdoor fireplaces.
d.
Outdoor cooking using charcoal, pellet, or other solid fuel, propane, natural gas,
or electric grills or smokers at private one- and two-family dwellings.
(2)
Level 2 - Fire Ban: Extreme Conditions . No Open Burning Allowed.
The following activities are prohibited during a Level 2 Fire Ban:
a.
All open burning and use of open flame.
b.
Any outside fire or the use of any appliance that utilizes a solid fuel (e.g., wood,
wood chips, charcoal, wood pellets) or liquid fuel (e.g., tiki torches) outside a
structure that cannot be immediately turned off and extinguished by a switch, valve,
knob, or dial.
c.
The launching or release of model rockets and the use of tethered or non-tethered
sky lanterns or similar devices.
d.
Smoking outdoors near combustible materials.
(C)
Open burn/open burning. The intentional outdoor burning of materials wherein products of combustion are emitted
directly into the ambient air without passing through a stack or chimney from an enclosed
chamber.
(D)
Outdoor cooking. the use of liquid- or gas-fueled grills, stoves, and cooktops (e.g., fueled by natural
gas or propane), charcoal grills, barbeque grills, pellet and electric smokers, etc.
for the preparation of food.
(E)
Person . Any individual, association, organization, partnership, firm, corporation, business,
or other entity recognized by law.
(F)
Portable and fixed outdoor fireplaces means wood-burning fire pits and natural gas fireplaces, portable outdoor fireplaces,
and chimineas.
(G)
Recreational fire. An outdoor fire where the total fuel area is three feet or less in diameter and two
feet or less in height for pleasure, religious, ceremonial, cooking, warmth, or similar
purposes.
(H)
Trash/rubbish includes, but is not limited to, household garbage, tires, chemicals, plastics, construction
materials, or any other discarded waste material.
( Ord. No. 2183 , § 2, 6-28-2022)
(A)
Open burning in violation of a Level 1 Fire Restriction, as defined in this chapter,
is prohibited when a Level 1 Fire Restriction is enacted.
(B)
Open burning in violation of a Level 2 Fire Ban, as defined in this chapter, is prohibited
when a Level 2 Fire Ban is enacted.
(C)
Open burning that requires a burn permit is prohibited without obtaining a burn permit,
as defined in this chapter.
(D)
Open burning of trash or rubbish is prohibited.
(E)
A recreational fire, as defined in this chapter, is permitted:
(1)
When not otherwise prohibited by a Level 1 Fire Restriction or Level 2 Fire Ban;
(2)
Only if it is attended at all times by an adult eighteen years or older;
(3)
If positioned away from structures or combustible materials; and
(4)
A fire extinguisher device or other mitigation source must be available nearby.
( Ord. No. 2183 , § 2, 6-28-2022)
Any person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter shall be punished as
described in B.M.C. Chapter 1-12 .
( Ord. No. 2183 , § 2, 6-28-2022)
