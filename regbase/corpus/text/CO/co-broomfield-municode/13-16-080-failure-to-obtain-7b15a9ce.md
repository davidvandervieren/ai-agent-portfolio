A temporary water permit is required whenever a temporary service connection is made
to the city's water system. Applicants requesting a temporary water permit shall be
required to provide a calibrated meter and certified backflow prevention device, except
as provided in section 13-16-010 (D) and (E) below. Special purposes for which temporary permits may be issued include:
(A)
Temporary sales office;
(B)
Construction water;
(C)
Temporary irrigation;
(D)
Special or short-term events that require the use of water for a limited period of
time, not to exceed two weeks or exceed 10,000 gallons. If an applicant for a special
or short-term event does not own an approved meter and backflow prevention device,
the city may rent the applicant a meter and backflow prevention device subject to
the payment of the applicable deposits and permit fees in section 13-16-040 of this chapter.
(E)
City-owned meters and backflow prevention devices will be provided on city-owned construction
projects and/or city maintenance activities that require temporary water permits.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
Applications for temporary permits may be made to the city and shall be accompanied
by the deposit and permit fee. Applications must contain the following information:
(A)
Proposed dates of water usage.
(B)
The size of the water meter to be used.
(C)
A description of the purposes for which the water is to be used.
(D)
An estimate of the monthly or total water use.
(E)
The legal description and address of the property on which the connection will be
located, if applicable.
(F)
The number and location of the fire hydrant to be used, if applicable.
(G)
The name, address, telephone number, and email address of the applicant, including
contact information for on-site personnel or field representative.
(H)
Other information required by the city to assist in administering this chapter.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
Only one-inch and two-inch meters are allowed for a temporary permit. The permittee
shall be required to furnish his or her own meter. The meter must have a current annual
inspection and accuracy report and an approved reduced pressure principle (RP) backflow
device with current backflow device test and maintenance report, or a certified air
gap.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 1567 §1, 2001; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
(A)
The following deposit and permit fee shall be paid:

Meter Size | Type of Use | Deposit | Permit Fee
1" (furnished by permittee) | Temporary landscaping, deep root tree watering, and similar irrigation activities | $ 1,500.00 | $ 50.00
2" (furnished by city) | City-owned construction projects and/or city maintenance activities | 2,500.00 | None
2" (furnished by city) | Short-term and special events (less than two weeks and under 10,000 gallons) | 2,500.00 | 50.00
2" (furnished by permittee) | Dust control, compaction, temporary irrigation, etc. | 1,500.00 | 50.00

(B)
The deposit shall guarantee payment of water use charges and payment for damage to
city equipment and distribution system infrastructure.
(C)
High-volume water use applicants (500,000 gallons per month or more) shall be required
to pay an additional deposit equivalent to two months of documented or estimated water
usage, or provide an alternative form of payment, such as a credit card for auto-pay
purposes.
(D)
All permittees shall pay the water use charges as provided by section 13-12-010 , B.M.C.
(E)
If a new water tap installation is required to supply the temporary water permit,
the applicant shall be required to pay the applicable water tap and inspection fees.
(F)
Temporary water permits for single-family residential construction will be assessed
a one-time charge of $25.00, which will be added at the time of the building permit
issuance. The use of a pre-manufactured meter yoke jumper or idler will be required.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 919 §5, 1991; Ord. 950 §5, 1993; Ord. 1379 §4, 1999; Ord.
1567 §2, 2001; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
(A)
A temporary permit shall be issued by the city manager or his or her designee if the
following conditions exist:
(1)
The applicant demonstrates compliance with all applicable zoning and building codes,
city standards and specifications, this chapter, and other applicable laws.
(2)
An approved reduced pressure principle (RP) backflow device is required for all temporary
permit applications. The term approved backflow device assembly means a device that
has been manufactured in full conformance with AWWA C511-97 and has met all laboratory
and field conformance specifications outlined in the University of Southern California,
Foundation of Cross Connection Control and Hydraulic Research (FCCC & HR) 69-2 or
most current issue, or a certified air gap.
(3)
Current meter accuracy test certificate and backflow device test and maintenance report
are required.
(4)
All fees and deposits have been paid.
(5)
The applicant has demonstrated a need for temporary water service, except that temporary
water service for oil and gas operations requires city council approval.
(B)
Permittees shall follow all city instructions regarding operation of the temporary
connection.
(C)
When not in use, equipment shall be safely and securely stored.
(D)
Permittees shall report the meter reading by the 28th day of each month to the utility
billing office and upon request by the city. Failure to report monthly meter readings
shall result in a $50.00 fee per meter, per month, regardless of usage.
(E)
The temporary water permit shall be available for inspection on the property by the
city at all times.
(F)
The permittee shall be responsible for, and shall pay the costs for, all line extensions
and other construction which is required for temporary water service.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 1567 §3, 2001; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
A temporary water permit for a temporary sales office shall expire one year after
it has been issued. All other temporary permits shall expire ninety calendar days
after they have been issued. The permittee shall present all equipment used (meter
and backflow preventer) upon expiration of the temporary permit for verification of
operability and final meter reading. The permittee may reapply for an additional temporary
water permit by following the same procedures as required to apply for a new temporary
water permit. All outstanding balances shall be paid in full prior to the issuance
of any subsequent temporary water permit.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
The city may revoke a temporary water permit for any one or more of the following
reasons:
(A)
Failure to comply with the provisions of this code;
(B)
Failure to pay applicable fees or, charges, or usage fees;
(C)
Misuse of city equipment and distribution system infrastructure; and
(D)
Use of the permit for other than the purposes stated in the application.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
In addition to any other penalties provided by this code, failure to obtain the temporary
permit required by section 13-16-010 , B.M.C. will result in charges being assessed to the violator for the permit and
the approximate amount of water used. The user shall be subject to the following fees
for failing to obtain the temporary permit: The fee for the first violation shall
be $250.00; the fee for the second violation shall be $500.00; and the fee for the
third violation shall be $750.00. Any user who has more than three violations shall
be prohibited from obtaining a temporary water service connection under this chapter.
(Ord. 892 §1, 1991; Ord. 1196 §18, 1996; Ord. 1567 §4, 2001; Ord. 1568 §17, 2001;
Ord. 1821 §1, 2005; Ord. 2044 §1, 2017)
