The Town Manager may grant permits for amplified music upon application of a property
owner under the following conditions:
(1)
The amplified music shall not be allowed for more than four hours in total.
(2)
The amplified music shall not be allowed between 10:00 p.m. and 10:00 a.m.
(3)
For a special use: maximum one permit per property per year is allowed.
(Prior code, § 6.6.1; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
