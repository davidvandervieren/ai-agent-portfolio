This Article shall apply to all open fires or open burnings when the restrictions
authorized by this Article are in effect.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this Article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this Section:
Department of Fire Prevention for the Town means the Poudre Fire Authority.
Fire Code Official for the Town means the Chief of the Poudre Fire Authority or his or her designee.
Fireworks means fireworks, as defined in Section 7-6-10 of this Chapter.
Open fire or open burning means any outdoor fire, including but not limited to campfires, warming fires, the
lighting of any fused explosives and fireworks of any kind or brand, the lighting
of model rockets and the burning of fence lines or rows, grasslands, fields, farm
lands, range lands, wild lands, trash and debris.
Stage 1 fire restrictions means the following activities are prohibited:
(1)
Open burning of any kind without a permit from the Poudre Fire Authority.
(2)
Use and sale of fireworks.
(3)
Open discarding of cigarettes and smoking materials.
(4)
Welding or the operation of torches with open flame in outdoor areas, including public
parks, greenbelts, open spaces and trail systems, without a permit.
(5)
Operating or using any internal combustion engine (e.g., chainsaw) without a spark-arresting
device properly installed, maintained and in effective working order.
(6)
Operating any pest control equipment which relies upon the ignition of flammable gases
or liquids.
Allowable activities include:
(1)
Fires within liquid-fueled or gas-fueled appliances, including fire pits, grills and
camp stoves; fireplaces within buildings; charcoal grill fires within developed residential
or commercial areas; and fires within wood-burning stoves.
(2)
Permitted fires by persons with a permit specifically authorizing the prohibited act
or omission, including permitted professional fireworks displays.
(3)
Fire Department training fires.
(4)
Tiki torches; small recreational fires at developed picnic or campground sites contained
in permanent fire pits or fire grates having a total fuel area of three feet (914
mm) or less in diameter and two feet (610 mm) or less in height and which are supervised
by a responsible person at least 21 years of age.
(5)
Burning of explosive wastes by the manufacturer of explosives in areas zoned for industrial
use, when the burning is supervised by a fire protection district.
(6)
Open fires or open burning by any federal, state or local officer or member of an
organized fire protection district or department in the performance of official fire
suppression functions.
Stage 2 fire restrictions means the following activities are prohibited:
(1)
All Stage 1 fire prohibitions.
(2)
Open burning of any kind.
(3)
Use and sale of fireworks.
(4)
Use of or fires in chimineas, other portable fireplaces or patio fire pits (which
are not liquid-fueled or gas-fueled).
(5)
Campfires at developed campgrounds or picnic areas.
Allowable activities include:
(1)
Fires within liquid-fueled or gas-fueled appliances, including fire pits, grills and
camp stoves.
(2)
Charcoal grill fires within developed residential or commercial areas.
(3)
Use of all fireplaces and wood-burning stoves within buildings.
(4)
Fire Department training fires.
(5)
Burning of explosive wastes by the manufacturer of explosives in areas zoned for industrial
use, when the burning is supervised by a fire protection district.
(6)
Open fires or open burning by any federal, state or local officer or member of an
organized fire protection district or department in the performance of official fire
suppression functions.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
It is unlawful for any person to start, build, maintain, attend or use an open fire
or conduct open burning in violation of the prohibitions of Stage 1 fire restrictions
in the Town, including public, private, state and federal lands, while such restrictions
are in effect.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
It is unlawful for any person to start, build maintain, attend or use an open fire
or conduct open burning in violation of the prohibitions of Stage 2 fire restrictions
in the Town, including public, private, state and federal lands, while such restrictions
are in effect.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
The restrictions imposed by this Article are authorized and shall take effect immediately
upon the issuance of an administrative order executed by the Town Manager and shall
remain in effect until rescinded by similar order. All such orders shall be issued
only after consultation with the Fire Code Official for the Town, when practicable,
taking into account weather, fire and local conditions, suppression resources and
fire restrictions in neighboring communities. The authority granted by this Article
is ongoing, and administrative orders may be issued from time to time as conditions
warrant.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
The provisions of this Article shall be enforced by the Police Department and the
Department of Fire Prevention.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
A violation of any provision of this Article shall be punishable by a fine of not
more than $500.00, plus court and administrative costs, as applicable. Each violation
shall be deemed a separate offense for purposes of assessing a fine.
(Ord. No. 7, § 4, 2012, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
