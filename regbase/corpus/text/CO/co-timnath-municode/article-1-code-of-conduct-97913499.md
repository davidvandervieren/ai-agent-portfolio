(a)
The holding of public office is a public trust created by the confidence which the
electorate reposes in the integrity of public officers. Officers have sworn an oath
to uphold the Constitution of the United States, the Constitution and statutes of
the State and the ordinances of the Town. It is the intent of the Town that its officers
and employees adhere to high levels of ethical and behavioral conduct so that the
public will have confidence that persons in positions of public responsibility are
acting for the benefit of the public.
(b)
The purpose of this Article is to provide clear conduct guidelines and necessary enforcement
procedures and to fully implement the provisions of the Charter.
(Prior code, § 12.1; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015; Ord. No. 6, § 1, 2021, 6-8-2021)
The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this Article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this Section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Council member means a member of the Town Council of the Town of Timnath, Colorado.
Employee means any person performing labor or services for the benefit of the Town in which
the Town may command when, where and how much labor or services shall be performed.
For the purpose of this Article, an individual primarily free from control and direction
in the performance of the service, both under his or her contract for the performance
of service and in fact, and who is customarily engaged in an independent trade, occupation,
profession or business related to the service performed, is not an employee . In addition and for the purpose of this Article, employee does not include any member of Town Council or any person appointed by the Town Council
to serve on any Town board, commission, committee, agency or authority.
Malfeasance means an act or failure to act committed by an officer in the course of his or her
official duties which the officer had no legal right to do (or not do); it includes
when an officer acts without any authority whatsoever or exceeds, ignores or abuses
his or her powers.
Misconduct means any willful and unlawful behavior by an officer in relation to the duties of
his or her office.
Officer means any person or officer holding a position by election or appointment to fill
a vacancy in an elected position in the service of the Town; i.e., members of the
Town Council, including the Mayor.
Town means the incorporated home rule Town of Timnath, Colorado.
Town Council means the Town Council of the Town of Timnath, Colorado, comprising of the Council
members and Mayor.
(Prior code, § 12.2; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
The requirements of this Article shall be in addition to the applicable requirements
of the laws of the Town and of the State, including without limitation Section 24-18-101,
C.R.S., et seq., Section 31-4-404, C.R.S, as amended, and Article XXIX of the Colorado
Constitution (the "Statutory Requirements"). The more stringent, applicable standards
of conduct shall prevail in the event of any conflict among locally adopted standards
and Statutory Requirements. This Article shall not create any private rights of action.
(Prior code, § 12.3; Ord. No. 11, § 1 , 2015, 11-10-2015; Ord. No. 6, § 2, 2021, 6-28-2021)
(a)
An officer shall not:
(1)
Commit malfeasance or misconduct in office. Upon a finding that an officer has committed
malfeasance or misconduct in office, he or she shall be subject to removal from office
and/or other appropriate discipline in accordance with the provisions set forth in
this Article;
(2)
Accept gifts of $50.00 or more which would tend to improperly influence a reasonable
person in a public position to depart from the faithful and impartial discharge of
the officer's public duties;
(3)
Use or authorize the use of his or her title, the name "Town of Timnath", or the Town's
logo in a manner that suggests endorsement, approval, favoritism, or bias by the Town
or an officer regarding goods or services, unless the use is authorized by a majority
vote of the Town Council.
(b)
In conducting the Town's business, an officer shall:
(1)
Strive at all times to serve the best interests of the Town regardless of his or her
personal interests;
(2)
Refrain from personally attacking other persons whether in writing or speech, including
use of inflammatory or defaming language against any individual;
(3)
Attend all meetings, unless given an excused absence prior to the meeting (three consecutive,
unexcused absences, or five unexcused absences in any year (calculated from the date
of the officer's election or appointment), shall result in automatic removal;
(4)
Refrain from disciplining or directing Town staff; communication with Town staff shall
be coordinated through the Town Manager or the Town Attorney, as applicable.
(Prior code, § 12.4; Ord. No. 11, § 1 , 2015, 11-10-2015; Ord. No. 6, § 3, 2021, 6-28-2021)
An officer, upon full disclosure of facts to the Town Manager, may directly request
an advisory opinion from the Town Attorney regarding the application of this Article
to the officer.
(Prior code, § 12.5; Ord. No. 11, § 1 , 2015, 11-10-2015)
(a)
If an alleged violation of the Code of Conduct is referred to the Town Council in
accordance with this Article, the Town Council shall have the responsibility and authority
for the enforcement of this Article.
(b)
The Town Council shall promptly review and consider the recommendations made by the
Mayor, the Mayor Pro Tem or a majority of the Town Council regarding the alleged violation.
(c)
In exercising its authority hereunder, the Town Council shall have the power to:
(1)
Make a determination that any complaint is founded or unfounded;
(2)
Dismiss frivolous complaints without conducting a public hearing (complaints dismissed
as frivolous shall be maintained confidential);
(3)
Hold a hearing to make a determination whether any complaint is founded or unfounded;
or
(4)
Initiate any suit and prosecute any criminal or civil action on behalf of the Town
wherein it believes, in its sole and exclusive discretion, that such action is appropriate.
(d)
The following procedures shall apply if the Town Council determines that a hearing
on the allegations should be held:
(1)
The hearing shall be conducted in a judicial or quasi-judicial forum. The allegations
shall be presented on behalf of the Town by a designated agent, such as the Town Attorney,
selected by the Town Council.
(2)
The officer against whom the allegations are raised shall receive a notice at least
ten days before the hearing, stating the substance of the allegation and notice of
all hearings pertaining thereto.
(3)
The Town shall have the burden of proof, which shall be a preponderance of the evidence.
(4)
In addition to any rules of procedure adopted by the Town Council, the parties shall
have the following rights:
a.
Present testimony.
b.
Produce evidence.
c.
Cross-examine witnesses.
d.
Be represented by legal counsel.
e.
Raise any statutory privilege.
(5)
The Town Council shall announce its decision and any penalty within 15 days of the
completion of the hearing. Any penalty shall be stayed until expiration of the reconsideration
period, if applicable.
(6)
If the Town Council votes to remove the officer, the officer may ask for reconsideration
upon filing a written request with the Town Manager's office within 48 hours of the
Town Council's announcement of the penalty. Reconsideration may be considered only
in the event new evidence, not known or available to the officer at the time of the
hearing, is set forth in the request for reconsideration.
(e)
The action taken by the Town Council on any complaint shall be the final action of
the Town on the matter.
(f)
The various provisions of this Section are cumulative and not exclusive, and shall
not be construed to limit any administrative, civil or criminal action or proceeding
which may be instituted by the Town pursuant to state statutes.
(Prior code, § 12.6; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
(a)
After a hearing held pursuant to Subsection 12-1-60 (d) above, if the Town Council, by a majority vote of the members of Town Council
entitled to vote thereon, determines that a violation of the Code of Conduct has occurred,
the Town Council, by majority vote of the members entitled to vote, may impose the
following penalties:
(1)
If the violation was unintentional or an oversight, the Town Council may issue a verbal
admonition, which shall be part of the record of proceedings.
(2)
If the conduct was negligent, the Town Council may issue a verbal admonition or an
official written reprimand, which shall be part of the record of proceedings.
(3)
If the conduct was intentional, the Town Council may, in its sole discretion:
a.
Issue a verbal admonition, which shall be part of the record of proceedings;
b.
Issue an official written reprimand, which shall be part of the record of proceedings;
c.
Publically censure the officer; and/or
d.
Remove the officer from his or her office.
(b)
The penalties provided for in this Section shall not foreclose the application of
any other cause of action or right of action arising under this Code, the Charter
or other applicable law.
(Prior code, § 12.7; Ord. No. 11, ; s; 1 , 2015, 11-10-2015)
