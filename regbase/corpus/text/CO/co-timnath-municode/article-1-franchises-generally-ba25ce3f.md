(a)
All entities, including utility companies ("Company") requesting the use of public
rights-of-way pursuant to a franchise agreement or any amendment thereto or renewal
thereof ("Agreement"), shall be responsible for all reasonable costs incurred by the
Town in negotiating the Agreement, reviewing any plans, reports, designs or other
documents deemed necessary by the Town or pursuing any remedy or enforcement of the
Agreement by the Town, which include, but are not limited to, legal, engineering and
inspection fees, and publication and court costs.
(b)
Reimbursement by the Company shall be made to the Town within ten days of receipt
of a written statement from the Town.
(c)
This Section may be superseded by a written agreement between the Town and the Company
which specifically refers to this Section.
(Prior code, § 5.1; Ord. No. 11, § 1, 2015, 11-10-2015)
