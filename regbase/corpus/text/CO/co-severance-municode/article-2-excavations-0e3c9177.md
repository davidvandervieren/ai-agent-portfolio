The owner, occupant, lessee or person in possession or control of any premises or
property shall maintain the sidewalks adjoining such premises or property in good
repair and in a safe, unobstructed condition, free of snow, weeds and debris.
(Ord. 2007-07 §1)
Editor's note— Ord. No. 2022-24 § 4, adopted Oct. 25, 2022, repealed § 11-1-20, which pertained to snow and ice removal
from sidewalks and derived from Ord. 2004-07; Ord. 2007-07.
No planting of shrubs, trees, flowers or the erection or construction of any solid
fence, structure or the placement of any other solid material which is over thirty-six
(36) inches above the level of the roadway and which obstructs the view of traffic
shall be allowed within thirty (30) feet of any street corner as measured along the
property lines from the corner.
(Prior code 5-8; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
Campers, camper trailers, motor homes, trucks, trailers or other large vehicles shall
be parked at least twenty-five (25) feet from any street corner so as not to obstruct
the view of traffic at any intersection.
(Prior code 5-9; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
(a)
Prior to issuance of a certificate of occupancy on any new structure or addition,
all sidewalks, curbs and gutters shall be inspected by the Town and any required repairs
shall be made by the builder.
(b)
All maintenance of sidewalks, curbs and gutters shall be the responsibility of the
owner of the adjacent property. Upon written notice from the Town, a property owner
shall have ninety (90) days in which the complete specified repairs, after which time
such repairs may be completed by the Town ad the cost assessed against the property
owner and the property.
(c)
Nothing in this Article shall require or authorize property owners to maintain any
roads. However, all property owners shall maintain, repair and replace as necessary
any and all curb, gutter, tree lawns and sidewalks adjacent to their properties.
(Ord. 2004-07 §§3—5; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
Unless acting under a duly authorized written contract with the Town, it is unlawful
for any person, other than a duly authorized employee of the Town in the course of
his or her employment, to make, cause or permit to be made any excavation or opening
in or under the surface or pavement of any street, alley, sidewalk or other public
place in the Town limits without first having obtained and having in force a permit
therefor.
(Prior code 5-1)
Any person desiring to procure a permit required by Section 11-2-10 above shall file with the Town Clerk, at least twenty-four (24) hours before the
time for beginning the proposed work, a written application upon a form prepared and
provided by the Town.
(Prior code 5-2)
No permit to excavate in any street or alley shall be issued unless the applicant
therefor shall have first deposited with the Town Clerk a performance or cash bond
in an amount sufficient to cover the entire expense of replacing the surface and subsurface
material in the event it is not replaced or replaced improperly by the applicant.
Nothing in this Article shall relieve the applicant from any duty to replace, repair
or repave any excavation to its original condition or as close thereto as possible.
(Prior code 5-3)
As set forth in the current fee schedule, a fee shall be charged for a permit issued
under the provisions of this Article for any excavation of twenty-five (25) square
feet or less. For excavations larger than twenty-five (25) square feet, an additional
permit fee as set forth in the current fee schedule shall be charged for each twenty-five
(25) additional square feet or fraction thereof.
(Prior code 5-4)
After completion to the satisfaction of the Town of the work covered by the permit
issued under this Article, such permittee shall be entitled to a return of such bond
or deposit.
(Prior code 5-1; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
Any person making or causing to be made any excavation or opening in any street or
alley or within five (5) feet of the public right-of-way shall keep the excavation
barricaded at all times. In addition, between sunset and sunrise on every night that
the same remains open or danger exists therefrom, he or she shall keep such excavation
or opening properly lighted so as to warn persons of such excavation. Failure to provide
such barriers shall be unlawful.
(Prior code 5-6; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
Parks in the Town shall be closed to public use between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and
6:00 a.m. on Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays of each week. Parks in the
Town shall be closed to public use between the hours of 11:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. on
Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays of each week.
(Ord. 6-95 §1)
Any person violating this Article shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and shall
be punished in accordance with the provisions of Section 1-4-20 of this Code.
(Ord. 6-95 §2; Ord. 2007-07 §1)
