(Ord. 85-1 §5)
(a)
Authority. The Town has the authority to adopt this Article pursuant to the Town's
general police powers, the authority granted in the Charter, Section 6 of Article
XX of the Constitution of the State of Colorado, and C.R.S. § 29-20-104.5. Said fees
shall be in addition to all other development fees levied and assessed by the Town.
(b)
Intent. The provisions of this Article are intended to impose certain fees to be collected
at the time of permit issuance for development or construction for the purpose of
funding the provision of additional capital improvements as the Town's population
increases. The imposition of these fees is intended to regulate the use and development
of land by ensuring that new growth and development in the Town bears a proportionate
share of the costs of capital expenditures necessary to provide water, sewer, park,
library, police, fire, general government, and transportation capital improvements.
Funds from said fees shall not be used to remedy existing deficiencies, but only to
provide new capital improvements which are necessitated by new development. The amount
of revenue generated by said fees shall not exceed the cost of providing the capital
improvements for which they are imposed, and the same shall be expended solely to
provide the specified capital improvements related to each fee assessed.

(a)
Establishment of service area. There is one (1) service area for the development impact
fees. The service area includes all of the territory located within the Town's municipal
corporate boundaries and/or the respective service areas of each of the Town's utility
enterprises, as each may be amended from time to time, and is known as the "service
area."
(b)
Application. Except as otherwise provided herein, this Article shall apply to all
development within the service area, excluding the development of any Town-owned facility.
(c)
Applicability of fees. The development impact fees adopted and imposed pursuant to
this Article shall apply only to development for which a complete application for
a development permit is submitted to the Town on or after March 1, 2024 (the effective
date of the ordinance enacting this Article), unless the development is exempted,

(d)
Town Manager authority. The Town Manager or such person's designee is authorized to
make determinations regarding the application, administration, and enforcement of

(e)
Exemptions. Development impact fees shall not be owed under any of the following conditions:
(1)
Previous payment of same amount of impact fee. Development for which an impact fee
has already or previously been paid in an amount that equals or exceeds the impact
fee required under this Article and the permit(s) which triggered the collection of
the impact fees have not expired or been voided.
(2)
Development permit denial. The Town denies the development permit application.
(3)
Replacing existing residential unit with new residential unit. Reconstruction, expansion,
alteration, or replacement of a previously existing residential unit that does not
create any additional residential unit.
(4)
Building after fire or catastrophe. Rebuilding the same amount of floor space of a
structure that was destroyed by a fire or other catastrophe.
(5)
Accessory structure. Construction of unoccupied accessory structure related to a residential
or nonresidential building.
(6)
No building permit required. Development for which a building permit is not required.
(7)
Pending complete applications. Any development permit for which the developer submitted
a complete application before March 1, 2024 (the effective date of the ordinance enacting
this Article).
(8)
Town-approved plans recognized as "site specific development plans". Developments
having already received an approved site-specific development plan as a vested property
right before March 1, 2024 (the effective date of the ordinance enacting this Article).
(9)
New impact generating development that creates no greater demand than previous development.
New impact generating development that an applicant or developer can demonstrate will
create no greater demand over and above that produced by the existing use or development.
By way of illustration, an applicant or developer may demonstrate that the approval(s)
that trigger the collection of development impact fees involve modifications to existing
development that do not add new dwelling units or increase floor area, or increase
the impact of existing development on existing or future capital facilities, or change
the land-use type of the existing development to a different category of development
for which a higher development impact fee would have been due.
(10)
Replacement. Replacement of a building with a comparable new building which would
not be assessed a higher fee under this Article than the pre-existing building would
be assessed at the time of the replacement.
Any claim for an exemption shall be made no later than the time when the applicant
or developer applies for the first building permit. Any claim for exemption not made
at or before that time shall be waived.

( Ord. No. 2024-01 , §4)
Impact fees shall be imposed on all new development in the service area, and shall
be collected according to the following provisions:
(1)
Residential uses.
a.
No building permit shall be issued for any dwelling unit prior to the payment of the
development impact fees for residential uses as set forth in Appendix 4A, Development
Impact Fee Schedule, at the end of this Article.
b.
For purposes of this subsection (a), any remodeling activity which results in the
creation of an additional dwelling unit shall be subject to the payment of the fee
as specified in this subsection (a).
(2)
Nonresidential uses. No building permit shall be issued for any structure to be used
primarily for nonresidential use prior to the payment of the development impact fee
for nonresidential uses, per one thousand (1,000) square feet of gross floor area,
as set forth in Appendix 4A, Development Impact Fee Schedule, at the end of this Article.
(3)
Change in use or expansion of use. For a change in the type of building use or land
use, or an increase in square footage, or the expansion, redevelopment, or modification
of an existing development, the impact fees shall be assessed on the net increase
in the development impact fees for the new use as compared to the previous use and
shall include any impact fees established subsequent to such prior use or prior payment
of impact fees. When a change in use results in a higher fee for the proposed use,
the difference between the two (2) fees shall be payable to the Town prior to the
issuance of a building permit for the area of the changed use. However, at no time
shall a change in use result in a refund or credit of fees by the Town.
(4)
Timing of collection. The development impact fees shall be paid prior to the time
a building permit is issued, and no building permit shall be issued for (i) any dwelling
unit or (ii) any structure to be used primarily for nonresidential use, prior to the
payment of the applicable development impact fees. Except as indicated otherwise herein,
the development impact fees shall be assessed in accordance with the rates in effect
at the time of submittal of a complete application for a building permit.
(5)
Option to pursue special fee determination. Where a development has unique characteristics
such that the actual burdens and costs associated with providing necessary public
services to that development will differ substantially from that associated with other
developments, then an applicant may provide the Town with an alternative impact fee
analysis. Based on a projection of the actual burdens and costs that will be associated
with the development, the alternative impact fee analysis may propose a unique fee
for the development. If, on the basis of generally recognized principles of impact
analysis, it is determined by the Town that the data, demand information, methodologies
and assumptions used by the applicant to calculate the impact fees in the independent
fee calculation study more accurately measures the proposed impact-generating development's
impact on the appropriate capital facilities, the fees determined in the independent
fee calculation study shall be deemed by the Town to be the fees due and owing for
the proposed development. The fee adjustment shall be set forth in a development agreement
executed prior to payment of the adjusted fees and the issuance of the first building
permit. If the alternative impact fee analysis fails to satisfy these requirements,
the fees applied shall be the fees established in Appendix 4A, Development Impact
Fee Schedule. The Town shall review the alternative impact fee analysis and shall
make a determination as to the development impact fees to be charged within thirty
(30) days of receipt of an application for an alternative fee analysis. Such decision
shall be appealable pursuant to subsection 4-4-100 (d) below.
(6)
Deferral of fees. A development agreement may provide for the deferral of payment
of the development impact fees for a residential development beyond the issuance of
a building permit, provided that such fee must be paid no later than issuance of the
temporary or final certificate of occupancy for that dwelling unit. The development
agreement shall provide for the value of any deferred development impact fees to be
supported by appropriate security, including a surety bond, letter of credit, or cash
bond.
(7)
Town Manager's authority. When a use is not listed in the development impact fee schedule,
the Town Manager or such person's designee shall have the authority to interpret this
Article and assess fees accordingly: whether a proposed use is residential or nonresidential;
what type of nonresidential use a proposed use is most similar to; the apportionment
of multiple uses within a development; and, for a mixed-use development, how much

(a)
Commencing on January 1, 2025, and on January 1 of each year thereafter, the impact
fees levied and assessed pursuant to this Article shall be adjusted to reflect the
effects of inflation on the construction costs for capital improvements and facilities
using the Engineering News Record (ENR) Construction Cost—20 Cities National Average
Index. For each such adjustment, the development impact fee shall be multiplied by
a fraction, the numerator of which is the ENR Construction Cost Index for the most
recent month for which figures are available, and the denominator of which is the
ENR Construction Cost Index over the preceding year prior to the period reflected
in the numerator.
(b)
Adjusted impact fees reflecting such inflationary adjustment shall be established
annually by the Town Manager or designee to be effective on January 1 of each year.
(c)
The Development Impact Fee Schedule in Appendix 4A, as adjusted in accordance with
this Section, shall be published by the Town Manager or designee on an annual basis.

(a)
The Town shall offset the reasonable costs of any capital improvements constructed,
or real property dedicated, by or on behalf of any property owner or developer of
real property from whom an impact fee is due and payable under this Article for that
category of capital improvements and against the fees due for capital improvements
in that category, subject to the provisions of this Section.
(b)
To be eligible for a credit, the subject development must be located within the service
area of the eligible capital improvement facility.
(c)
No offset or credit shall be given for capital improvements or dedications not approved
by the Town as part of the developer's subdivision improvements agreement or by a
separate written agreement approved by the Town Council.
(d)
No offset or credit shall be granted for capital improvements otherwise required by
any applicable Town Code or ordinance.
(e)
Credits shall not be available for any infrastructure provided by a developer if the
cost of such infrastructure will be repaid to the developer by the Town through another
agreement or mechanism. To the extent that the developer will be paid or reimbursed
by the Town for any contribution, payment, construction, or dedication from any Town
funding source including an agreement to reimburse the developer with future collected
development impact fees pursuant to this Section, any credits claimed by the developer
shall be deducted from any amounts to be paid or reimbursed by the Town or reduced
by the amount of such payment or reimbursement.
(f)
Valuation of credits.
(1)
Credit for land dedication shall be valued at the fair market value of the land established
by a professional appraiser acceptable to the Town in an appraisal paid for by the
developer or by agreement with the Town.
(2)
Credit for construction of capital improvements shall be valued by the Town based
upon the completed engineering drawings, specifications, and construction cost estimates
submitted by the developer to the Town. The Town shall determine the amount of the
credit due based upon the information submitted, or, if determined that the information
is inaccurate or unreliable, then on alternative construction costs acceptable to
the Town.
(3)
A credit or offset shall not exceed the actual costs the applicant incurred in providing
the eligible capital facility.
(g)
If an offset or credit has not been exhausted within ten (10) years of the date of
issuance of the first building permit for which a fee was due and payable under the
provisions of this Article, or within such other period as may be designated in writing
by the Town, such offset or credit shall lapse.
(h)
Any offset or credit claimed under the provisions of this Section shall be applied
in the following manner:
(1)
In the case of residential developments, the offset or credit shall be prorated among
all residential structures to which the offset or credit is to be applied, and shall
be applied at the time of filing and acceptance of the application for the building
permit for each such structure.
(2)
In the case of nonresidential development, the offset or credit shall be applied to
the fees as they become due for each building permit for such development until the
offset or credit has been exhausted.
(i)
A developer or property owner claiming entitlement to an offset or credit shall apply
for the same prior to or at the time of application for the issuance of any development
permit for the development in question, which application shall be submitted to Town
Manager utilizing a form and information as may be required by the Town Manager or
designee. Upon receipt of such application, the Town Manager shall determine, in writing,
the maximum value of the offset or credit that may be applied against fees due and
payable from the applicant. The Town Manager's decision on the application for offset
or credit may be appealed to the Town Council as provided in Section 4-4-100 (d).
(j)
Nothing in this Section obligates the Town to authorize any offset or credit or to
enter into any credit or other agreement that may be permitted by this Section.

Town by a factor of ten percent (10%) or more. In such event, the current owner of
the subject real property shall, upon request as set forth in subsection (b) of this
Section, be entitled to a refund for the difference between the amounts of the impact
fee charged for and attributable to such construction cost and the amount the impact
fee would have been calculated to be if the actual construction cost had been included
in the nexus study. The refund contemplated by this paragraph shall relate only to
the costs specific to the construction of the applicable capital improvement and shall
not include any related design, administrative, or other costs not directly incurred
for construction of the capital improvement that are included in the impact fee.
(b)
Procedure for refund. Refunds shall be administered by the Town Manager or such person's
designee according to the following process:
(1)
Application submission. A refund application shall be submitted within one (1) year
following the end of the tenth year from the date the impact fee was paid or the first
building permit for the development was issued, whichever is earlier. The refund application
shall include the following information:
a.
A copy of the dated receipt issued for payment of the fee;
b.
A copy of the building permit; and
c.
Evidence that the applicant is the successor-in-interest to the fee payer (if relevant).
Such evidence shall consist of a sworn statement that the applicant is the current
owner of the property for which the fee is paid and a certified copy of the current
deed to the property.
(2)
Determination of completeness. Within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the refund application,
the Town Manager or such person's designee shall determine if it is complete. If it
is determined the application is not complete, a written statement specifying the
deficiencies shall be forwarded by mail to the person submitting the application.
The Town Manager or such person's designee shall take no further action on the refund
application until it is deemed complete.
(3)
Decision on refund application. When it is determined the refund application is complete,
it shall be reviewed within thirty (30) days and shall be approved if it is determined
the applicant is entitled to a full or partial refund of the impact fee(s) paid, as
permitted under this Section. At the time of payment of the refund, the applicant
shall sign a sworn statement acknowledging the facts stated in the application remain
true and correct as of the date of payment.
(4)
Earned interest. A refund of an impact fee shall include any interest actually earned
by the Town on the refunded portion of the fee from the date of collection to the
date of refund.
(5)
No refund if project demolished, destroyed, altered, reconstructed, or reconfigured.
After an impact fee has been paid pursuant to this Article, no refund of any part
of such fee shall be made if the development for which the fee was paid is later demolished,
destroyed, or is altered, reconstructed or reconfigured so as to reduce the size of
the development or the number of units in the development.

(a)
Requirements to construct improvements; other obligations. Nothing in this Article
shall restrict the Town from requiring an applicant for a development permit to construct
reasonable capital improvements designed and intended to serve the needs of the applicant's
project, whether or not such capital improvements are of a type for which credits
or offsets are available under Section 4-4-080. The impact fees charged pursuant to
this Article shall be in addition to any other fees, charges, tolls or requirements
applicable to development, including, by way of example and not limitation, public
land dedication, fair contributions for public school sites, tap fees and building
permit fees.
(b)
Administrative costs. The Town shall be entitled to retain not more than two percent
(2%) of the development impact fees collected as payment for the expenses of collecting
the fees and administering this Article. In the case of refunds of impact fees under
Section 4-4-090, the Town shall be entitled to retain not more than an additional
two percent (2%) of the impact fee payment made as payment for the expenses of processing
the refund request.
(c)
Mistake and misrepresentation in payment of fee. If a development impact fee has been
calculated and paid based on a mistake or misrepresentation, it shall be recalculated.
Any amounts overpaid by a developer shall be refunded by the Town within thirty (30)
days after the Town's acceptance of the recalculated amount, with interest at the
rate of five percent (5%) per annum since the date of such overpayment. Any amounts
underpaid by the developer shall be paid to the Town within thirty (30) days after
the Town's acceptance of the recalculated amount, with interest at the rate of five
percent (5%) per annum since the date of such underpayment. In the case of an underpayment
to the Town, the Town shall not issue any additional development permits or approvals
for the project for which the development impact fee was previously paid until such
underpayment is corrected, and if amounts owed to the Town are not paid within such
30-day period, the Town may also repeal any permits issued in reliance on the previous
payment of such development impact fee and refund such fee to the then-current owner
of the land.
(d)
Appeal of decision of Town Manager or designee.
(1)
Appeal. An appeal may be filed of any determination or decision made by the Town Manager
or such person's designee under this Article regarding: (i) the applicability of an
exemption, as provided in Section 4-4-03 0(e) of this Article; (ii) the availability
and allocation of a credit pursuant to Section 4-4-080 of this Article; (iii) the
calculation and amount of the development impact fees for the development; or (iv)
the amount of any refund. Such appeal shall be made to the Town Council by filing
with the Town Clerk or designee within thirty (30) days of the determination or decision
for which the appeal is being filed: (i) a written notice of appeal on a form provided
by the Town Clerk or designee; and (ii) a written explanation of why the applicant
feels the determination or decision is in error.
(2)
Town Council review. The Town Council shall complete its review at a public meeting
and render its decision to uphold or deny the Town Manager's decision or determination
within thirty (30) days of the filing of the request for an appeal. The Town Council's
review shall be limited to consideration only of the record presented to or conducted
before the Town Manager. The standard of review for such an appeal shall be limited
to an "abuse of discretion." Under this standard, the Town Council may only overturn
the Town Manager's decision if it finds that the Town Manager abused its exercise
of discretion. The appellant shall bear the burden of persuasion in such appeal. The
decision of the Town Council shall be final.
