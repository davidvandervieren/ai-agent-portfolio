All elections shall be held and conducted in accordance with the Colorado Municipal
Election Code of 1965, Article 10 of Title 31, C.R.S., as amended, except as otherwise
provided in the Town Charter or by ordinance. The Town may by ordinance or resolution
determine to follow all or part of the provisions of the Uniform Election Code of
1992, Articles 1 to 13 of Title 1, C.R.S., in lieu of the Colorado Municipal Election
Code of 1965, for any election.

intent is filed with the Town Clerk by the person intending to be a write-in candidate.

the office for which such person desires to be a candidate and a statement that the
person is qualified to assume the duties of that office if elected. Such affidavit
shall be filed with the Town Clerk prior to sixty-four (64) days before the date of
election.

(a)
The Town Manager is authorized to dispose of any personal property owned by the Town,
whenever it is determined at the Town Manager's discretion that:
(1)
The item or group of items:
a.
Is a motor vehicle or piece of equipment; or
b.
Has a fair market value of less than fifty thousand dollars ($50,000);
(2)
The item or group of items are surplus property; and
(3)
Sound property management principles and financial considerations indicate that the
interests of the Town would best be served by disposing of the item or group of items.
(b)
Personal property other than motor vehicles or equipment with a value equal to or
greater than fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) may be declared surplus and disposed
of only with the approval of the Town Council.
(c)
The Town Manager shall determine the fair market value of the surplus property for
disposal and may dispose of any such property by any means which is reasonably calculated
to yield the best net result to the Town, including but not limited to the methods
of sale described below. Such sale may be public or private and with or without notice
and minimum waiting period.
(1)
Trade-in. Property declared as surplus may be offered as a trade-in for credit toward
the acquisition of new property.
(2)
Return to manufacturer. Surplus property may, when possible, be returned to the manufacturer
for buy-back credit.
(3)
Sealed written bids. Sealed bids may be solicited for the sale of surplus property,
with the award going to the highest responsive, responsible bidder. All notices shall
be published in a commercially reasonable manner, using any appropriate mediums of
printed or electronic commerce which, in the opinion of the Town Manager, are reasonably
likely to ensure an optimum bidding process. The Town Manager shall reserve the right
to reject any and all bids received for any item.
(4)
Auction. Surplus property may be sold by public auction or through an electronic auction
site.
(5)
Negotiated sale. If no bids are received or if a determination is made that the market
value of the property exceeds the offer of the highest responsible bidder, all bids
may be rejected, and the Town Manager may negotiate a sale, provided that the negotiated
sale price meets or exceeds the market value.
(6)
Donation. Surplus property may be donated to any other public agency or charitable
organization exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. "Public
agency" means the state or any agency or subdivision thereof, any city, county, special
district, or school district.
(e)
The Town Manager may give away surplus property to the public at no charge on a first-come,
first-served basis for property that it would otherwise pay to dispose of. A Town
employee may take property under this provision only after it has been available to
the public for a reasonable period of time.
(f)
The Town Manager may dispose of surplus property that has no monetary value or that
has a monetary value insufficient to cover the costs of a sale or repair by disposing
of the property as waste. Property that would otherwise be disposed of as waste may
be made available to the public at no charge or at a minimal charge. If after attempting
to dispose of the property by such nontraditional methods, or determining that such
method of disposal is impractical given the valueless condition of the property, the
Town Manager may authorize the Town Clerk to direct the salvaging of any recyclable
material and the disposition of the remainder as refuse through appropriate methods
of waste disposal.
(g)
All personal property sold or otherwise disposed of by the Town shall be sold as-is
without any warranty of any kind. Goods sold through an auction or other third-party
seller shall be provided along with a bill of sale or receipt disclaiming all warranties.
The Town Manager is authorized to execute and deliver any applicable title documents.
(h)
The Town Manager shall keep a record of all property sold under this Section and that
record shall generally describe the property sold or exchanged, to whom it was sold
or with whom exchanged and the amount of money or other consideration received for
each sale or exchange
(i)
"Surplus property" as used in this Section is used generically to describe any personal
property, vehicle, equipment or material belonging to or owned by the Town that is
no longer needed or usable by the Town for the conduct of Town business, is scheduled
for replacement in an adopted budget, has become obsolete or worn out, or which has
no practicalsalvage or recyclable value.

Definitions.

º Personal illness or family emergency;
º Pre-approved vacation or travel;

º No prior notice is given;
º The reason is not deemed valid by the Chair;

that a subsequent unexcused absence may result in removal.

the Town Council.

3.
The member shall be given an opportunity to respond in writing or in person before
the Council makes a final decision.

(1)
Attendance expectations. All members of Town boards, commissions, and advisory bodies
are expected to regularly attend scheduled meetings and fully participate in their
respective roles' responsibilities.
(2)
Definitions.
a.
Excused absence: An absence is considered excused when a member notifies the Chair or designated staff
liaison at least 24 hours in advance of the meeting and provides a valid reason, such
as:
º
Personal illness or family emergency;
º
Pre-approved vacation or travel;
º
Work or school-related conflict;
º
Other extenuating circumstances approved by the Chair.
b.
Unexcused Absence: An absence is considered unexcused if:
º
No prior notice is given;
º
The reason is not deemed valid by the Chair;
º
The member is habitually late or leaves before a meeting quorum is established without
good cause;
(e)
Removal for attendance. A board member shall be subject to removal from their position
if any of the following occur within a calendar year:
(1)
The member accrues three (3) unexcused absences from regular meetings:
(2)
The member accrues four (4) total absences (excused or unexcused), unless extenuating
circumstances exist as determined by the Town Council;
(3)
The member fails to attend any required training or orientation sessions without prior
approval.
(f)
Notice requirement. After a member's second unexcused absence, the Mayor shall provide
the member with written notice outlining the attendance policy (ordinance! and warning
that a subsequent unexcused absence may result in removal.
(g)
Removal process.
(1)
Any member meeting the criteria outlined above may be removed by a majority vote of
the Town Council.
(2)
Prior to removal, the member shall be provided with written notice at least seven
(7) days in advance of the proposed action, stating the reasons for removal.
(3)
The member shall be given an opportunity to respond in writing or in person before
the Council makes a final decision.
(h)
Discretion of the Council. Nothing in this section shall limit the Town Council's
authority to remove members for other reasons, including neglect of duty, malfeasance,
conduct inconsistent with Town values or responsibilities, or for any reason deemed
in the public interest.

The members of the Citizen Advisory Board shall annually elect a Chair and Vice Chair,

The Citizen Advisory Board shall hold meetings from time to time as necessary, unless
a meeting is canceled at the discretion of the Town Manager or designee, and adopt

constitute a quorum.
