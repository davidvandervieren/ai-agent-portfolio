This Code constitutes a republication of the general and permanent ordinances of the
Town of Platteville, Colorado.
Source materials used in the preparation of the republication were the Town Code,
as updated through November, 2024, and subsequent ordinances. The source of each new
section is included in the history note appearing in parentheses at the end thereof.
The absence of such a note indicates that the section is part of the 2001 Code. By
use of the comparative tables appearing in the back of this republication, the reader
can locate any section of the Code, as updated.
