Pursuant to Article XIV, Section 14-9 , Section 5 of the Weld County Home Rule Charter, written, sealed bids and a ten-day
period for consideration and investigation of the bids are required for purchases
in excess of an amount set annually by the Board of County Commissioners by ordinance.
Through repeal and reenactment of this Appendix, the amount set for the year 2026
is $50,000.00; however, the amount is $150,000.00 for all contracts for a disaster
emergency event in the case where a declaration of disaster is made by the Board of
County Commissioners.
