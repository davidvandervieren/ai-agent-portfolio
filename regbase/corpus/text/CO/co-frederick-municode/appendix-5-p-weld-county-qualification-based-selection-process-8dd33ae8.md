Advertise Request for Qualifications (RFQ)
↓
Review and Evaluate RFQs (use Appendix 5-Q : Request for Qualification Scoring Form)
↓
Short List (3-5 Consulting Firms)
↓
Is QBS Required by State or Federal Requirements?

YES: Quality Based Selection | NO: Best Value
1. Request QBS Request for Proposals (RFPs) | 1. Request Best Value Request for Proposals (RFPs)
2. Hold Pre-Proposal Mandatory Meeting | 2. Hold Pre-Proposal Mandatory Meeting
3. Review RFPs/Schedule Interviews (Use Appendix 5-S : QBS Scoring Form to score RFPs and interviews) | 3. Review RFPs (Use Appendix 5-R : Best Value Scoring Form to score RFPs and interviews)
4. Conduct Interviews (all selected firms) | 4. Interview Low Bidder (optional) to confirm low bid meets specifications?
5. Select Highest Ranked Consultant | YES: Award Contract (contract awarded to lowest bid that meets all specifications) | NO: Interview next low bid until low bid that meets specifications is identified
6. Finalize Final Scope of Work and Negotiate Fees. Was negotiation successful? | 
YES: Award Contract | NO: Go to next highest ranked firm. Repeat Step 6 until negotiations with next highest
ranked firm are successful. | 

(Weld County Code Ordinance 2013-12 )
