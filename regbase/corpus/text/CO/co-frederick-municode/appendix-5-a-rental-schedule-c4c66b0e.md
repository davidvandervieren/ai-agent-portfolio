MEETING ROOMS
The following rental schedule applies to the Events Center in Administration, the
Training Room at the Training Center, and the large conference room at the Southwest
Weld Administration Building for meetings by any Colorado non-profit corporation that
has a demonstrated partnership with the County, or an agreement with the County to
provide services.
$50.00 per hour
$100.00 Refundable Cleaning Deposit
Any damage to the facility will be charged on a cost-recovery basis determined by
the County.
A County employee responsible for the building must be present during events after
business hours.
If the event is not sponsored by a County member (must be present), the organization
will reimburse the County for an additional employee to be present at a rate of $25.00
per hour.
Any County labor provided beyond routine building access service will be charged on
a cost-recovery basis determined by the County.
Recognized Weld County government groups shall not be charged the above fees for use
for County business.
DOWNTOWN PARKING

Centennial Underground Garage | Per space | $12.50 per month
Surface Lots | Per space | $15.00 per month (non-County employee)

No charge for employees parking in surface lots, employees working shifts 5:00 p.m.
to 8:00 a.m. or elected officials.
(Weld County Code Ordinance 2001-8; Weld County Code Ordinance 2002-1; Weld County
Code Ordinance 2007-7; Weld County Code Ordinance 2004-10; Weld County Code Ordinance 2013-16 ; Weld County Code Ordinance 2016-13 )
