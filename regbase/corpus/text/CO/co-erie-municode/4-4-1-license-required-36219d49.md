Every person engaged or engaging in the business of purchasing or selling old iron,
rags, bottles, bones or junk of any kind or nature whatsoever, within the town, shall
first obtain from the town clerk, a license to carry on said business.
(Ord. 155, 4-6-1953)
For a license to carry on said business of buying or selling old iron, bottles, rags,
bones or junk of any kind and nature whatsoever, there shall be paid to the town the
sum as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code, payable in advance.
(Ord. 155, 4-6-1953; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001)
Every such person as defined in section 4-4-1 of this chapter shall be required to surround and enclose said junkyard with a solid
fence of board, sheet metal or other construction approved by the town council. Such
fence or enclosure shall be of the height of eight feet but not to exceed ten feet
and shall be tight against the ground. It shall be painted and kept painted and in
good condition and repair. Licensee shall keep such yard in a clean and healthy condition
and shall exterminate all rats, mice, rodents, insects or other harmful or disease
bearing objects. Failure so to do, upon notice by the town council to such licensee,
shall be grounds for revocation of license and such other penalties as are provided
in this chapter.
(Ord. 155, 4-6-1953)
Whoever shall violate the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor
and, upon conviction, shall be punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.
(Ord. 155, 4-6-1953; amd. 2004 Code)
