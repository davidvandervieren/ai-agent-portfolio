The purpose of this chapter is to provide for the regulation and licensing of all
businesses operating in the town.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018)
As used in this chapter, the following terms shall have the following meanings:
Engage or engaging in business: To perform services or to sell, lease, rent, deliver or install tangible personal
property for storage, use or consumption in the town, including without limitation:
A.
Directly, indirectly, or by a subsidiary, maintaining a building, store, office, salesroom,
warehouse, or other place of business in the town;
B.
Sending any employees, agents, or salespersons into the town to solicit business or
to install, repair, service, or assist in the use of products, or for demonstration
or other purposes;
C.
Maintaining any employees, agents, or commissioned salespersons on duty at a location
in the town;
D.
Owning, leasing, renting, or otherwise exercising control over real property in the
town; or
E.
Making more than one delivery into the town within one year.
General business license: A license for a wholesale, retail, service, trade, manufacturing or other business
that is not a home occupation.
Home occupation license: A license for a home occupation.
Mobile vending: Selling or attempting to sell any services, goods, wares or merchandise, including
without limitation food or beverage, from a mobile truck, mobile trailer or mobile
cart at any location in the town.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018; Ord. No. 009-2023 , 3-28-2023)
It is unlawful for any person to engage in business in the town without first having
received a general business license or home occupation license from the town.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018)
A.
An application for a general business license or home occupation license shall be
submitted to the town on forms provided by the town, accompanied by the fee established
by resolution of the town council.
B.
A separate application shall be required for each separate business location, unless
the places of business are contiguous to each other, communicate directly and openly
with each other, and are operated as one unit.
C.
Each application shall be accompanied by evidence that the business will be operated
in full compliance with all applicable law, including without limitation zoning. No
license shall be granted to any business not in compliance with applicable law. Following
a determination that the application is complete and the business complies with applicable
law, the town shall issue the business license.
D.
Each business license shall expire on December 31 of the year in which it is issued.
Any renewal application shall be filed prior to December 1 to ensure that the renewal
is processed prior to expiration of the current business license.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018; Ord. 23-2021 , § 1, 8-10-2021; Ord. 06-2022 , § 1, 4-26-2022; Ord. No. 009-2023 , 3-28-2023; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
Every licensed business shall provide written notification to the town of any changes
to the following: business name, ownership structure, location, tax identification
number, or nature of the business. Notification shall occur within 30 days of any
such change.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018)
A.
Violation: It is unlawful to violate any provision of this chapter.
B.
Penalty: A violation of this chapter is punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code. Each day of violation shall constitute a separate offense.
In addition to any other available penalties, the town may enjoin a person from engaging
in business in the town without a license required by this chapter.
(Ord. 28-2018, 10-9-2018)
A.
Exemptions: This chapter shall not apply to the following entities:
1.
Nonprofit corporations or businesses;
2.
Businesses without a physical presence in the town that have a state standard retail
license and make retail sales within the town; and
3.
Businesses with only an incidental physical presence in the town that have a state
standard retail license and make retail sales within the town.
B.
Waivers: Business license fees shall be waived for the following:
1.
A business certified through the Erie Green Business Program, for the term of the
business's participation in the program.
2.
A mobile vending business.
3.
A business that is participating as a vendor in a special event and does not have
a physical presence in the town.
( Ord. No. 23-2022 , § 1, 10-25-2022; Ord. No. 009-2023 , 3-28-2023)
The license for a particular business location shall be posted at all times in a conspicuous
place at that location. If the business is not operated, conducted or carried on at
a fixed location, then the licensee shall carry the license upon their person when
operating, conducting or carrying on any such business.
( Ord. No. 009-2023 , 3-28-2023)
A business license shall not be transferable from person to person or place to place.
( Ord. No. 009-2023 , 3-28-2023)
