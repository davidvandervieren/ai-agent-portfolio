It shall be unlawful for any person not having a regularly established place of business
or operation within the corporate limits of the town to directly or indirectly pass,
distribute or circulate any printed or written handbills, posters, dodgers or other
advertising matter without first having obtained a license so to do from the town
clerk.
(Ord. 113, 8-1-1943; amd. 2004 Code)
Every person applying for such license shall file with the clerk his permanent address
and, if a corporation, shall give the state where it is incorporated and, if a firm,
association or partnership, the names and addresses of its members and shall pay for
such license charge or fee as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code, which license
shall give the person obtaining the same the privilege of distributing his advertising
matter for the period designated in such license; provided, however, that no charitable
or nonprofit organization or enterprise shall be required to pay any license fee but
shall be required to give to the clerk the name and address of its officers or promoters.
(Ord. 113, 8-1-1943; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001; 2004 Code)
Any person violating any of the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed guilty
of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.
(Ord. 113, 8-1-1943; amd. 2004 Code)
