For purposes of this chapter, the following terms shall have the following meanings:
Local event means an event authorized by the town's parks and recreation department.
Mobile food vendor means a retail food and beverage establishment that is a wheeled vehicle, including
without limitation a mobile truck, trailer, or pushcart, that is readily movable and
designed for the service of food from the interior of the unit.
Mobile vendor means a person who sells or attempts to sell, or offers to the general public, any
services or goods, including without limitation food and beverage from a mobile vendor
vehicle, but excluding mobile vendors that are scheduled by a property owner or occupant
to deliver goods and services directly to the owner or occupant.
Mobile vendor vehicle means a motor vehicle, trailer, pushcart, wagon, or similar vehicle used to conduct
mobile vendor operations.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
It is unlawful for any person to operate as a mobile vendor in the town without first
obtaining a mobile vendor permit.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
An application for a mobile vendor permit shall be submitted to the town clerk on
forms provided by the town clerk, and shall include the following information, at
a minimum:
1.
The application fee as set forth by resolution of the town council;
2.
A scaled drawing of the dimensions of the mobile vendor vehicle, and any displays,
signage, furniture, or other appurtenances thereto;
3.
A site plan of the property or public right-of-way on which the mobile vendor intends
to operate;
4.
A written plan for waste disposal;
5.
If on private property, proof of ownership or written permission from the property
owner;
6.
If on public property, a certificate of insurance naming the town as an additional
insured in amounts no less than $1,000,000.00 per incident;
7.
Evidence of all required permits and licenses, including without limitation and as
applicable: state sales tax license; business license; building permit; and local
event permit; and for mobile food vendors, approval from the applicable health department;
and
8.
Any other information deemed necessary by the town clerk to determine compliance with
this chapter.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Criteria. The town may administratively approve an application for a mobile vendor permit if
the following criteria are satisfied:
1.
The application is complete;
2.
The applicant has demonstrated the ability to comply with this chapter and any necessary
permit conditions; and
3.
The applicant is not delinquent in the payment of any other fees or tax payments to
the town or the state.
B.
Conditional approval. The town may impose reasonable conditions on a mobile vendor permit if such conditions
would allow the application to comply with the above-referenced criteria, including
without limitation modifying the term of the permit. Such conditions shall be specified
in the mobile vendor permit.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
A.
Term. A mobile vendor permit shall be valid for one year from the date of issuance, unless
revoked pursuant to this chapter.
B.
Renewal. An application for the renewal of a mobile vendor permit shall be submitted to the
town on forms provided by the town, at least 30 days prior to the expiration of the
current permit. The renewal application shall be processed in the same manner as an
initial application, provided that any information previously submitted that has not
changed need not be resubmitted with a renewal application.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
A.
Location.
1.
A mobile vendor may operate on private property, with permission of the property owner,
or in public rights-of-way and public parking lots, with permission of the town.
2.
A mobile vendor shall not operate within 20 feet of an intersection, or in any area
so as to interrupt or interfere with the normal flow of vehicular or pedestrian traffic.
3.
A mobile vendor shall not place equipment, food, tables, chairs, other fixtures, generators,
or awnings so as to obstruct the free travel of pedestrians or vehicles in, on, near,
or above any public street, sidewalk, or other public right-of-way that is open to
such travel.
4.
Except during local events, a mobile food vendor shall not operate within 250 feet
of a brick-and-mortar food and beverage service use during open business hours without
written permission from the owner of such use. The owner of a brick-and-mortar food
and beverage service use shall not directly profit from the granting of such permission.
B.
Parking. Mobile vendor vehicles shall abide by all town parking rules and standards.
C.
Compliance with law. Each mobile vendor shall comply with all applicable laws, including without limitation
any applicable conditions placed on its mobile vendor permit and local event permit.
D.
Waste. Each mobile vendor shall provide adequate waste receptacles for its operation and
shall maintain the immediate area surrounding its mobile vending vehicle free of debris,
trash, and hazards.
E.
Display of permit and license. Each mobile vendor shall conspicuously display its mobile vendor permit and town
business license at all times while operating in the town.
F.
Indemnity. As a condition of every permit, the mobile vendor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless
the town, its officers, employees, and agents against any and all claims for damage
to persons or property arising out of or resulting from the mobile vendor's activities
in the town.
G.
Conflicts. The town occasionally authorizes local events that may impact specific properties;
mobile vendors who have been granted a permit for a designated property shall not
operate during such local events unless specifically authorized in writing by the
town or the local event permittee.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
A.
The following shall be grounds for suspension or revocation of a mobile vendor permit:
1.
A violation of any requirement of the permit, this chapter, or any other applicable
law;
2.
Operating in such a manner as to create a public nuisance or constitute a danger to
the public health, safety and welfare;
3.
Failure to pay state or local taxes that are related to the operation of the business
associated with the permit; or
4.
Fraud, misrepresentation, or a false statement of material fact contained in the original
or renewal permit application.
B.
Revocation of a permit shall be effective immediately. The town shall deliver written
notice of revocation to the permittee, by first class United States mail to the permittee's
last known address, or by personal delivery. The notice shall state the reasons for
revocation.
C.
The town reserves the right to end the mobile vendor permit program altogether and
revoke all mobile vendor permits if the town discovers that mobile vendors are negatively
impacting other businesses in the town, or posing a threat to the public health, safety,
and welfare.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
A.
An applicant who was denied a permit or a permittee whose permit was revoked may appeal
such decision pursuant to this section.
B.
To appeal, the applicant or permittee shall file a written notice of appeal with the
town clerk within seven days of the date of the decision being appealed, stating the
basis for the appeal. Failure to file a timely appeal shall be a jurisdictional bar
to the town council's authority to hear such appeal.
C.
The town council shall hear such appeal on a de novo basis at a public meeting within 30 days, after providing the applicant or permittee
an opportunity to be heard on the matter. The decision of the town council shall be
final.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
It is unlawful to violate this chapter.
B.
In addition to revocation, violations of this chapter shall be subject to the civil
penalties set forth in section 1-4-4 of this Code. Each day of violation shall constitute a separate offense.
( Ord. No. 022-2023 , § 1, 9-26-2023)
