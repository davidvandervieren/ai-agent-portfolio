# Oil & Gas
Oil and gas is a top priority in the Town of Erie. We continue to work together and establish those priorities with Town staff, residents, legal teams, experts and professionals, other municipalities, the State, and the oil and gas industry as we protect Erie’s health and safety.

Air Quality
The Town of Erie has expanded its air quality monitoring program in partnership with Boulder A.I.R., adding new real-time monitoring stations and updated tools to track pollutants near oil and gas operations and throughout the community. Residents can now access near real-time data and request individual canister samples for lab analysis. Learn more and view the data portal here.
Get Involved
The Town of Erie is committed to responsive, effective leadership by supporting an open, transparent and accountable government. As part of that commitment, the Town encourages residents to stay informed and engaged in oil and gas activity in their community. The resources below are intended to keep residents up to date and provide opportunities for public involvement.
