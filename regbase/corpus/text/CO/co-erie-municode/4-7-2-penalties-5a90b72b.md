A.
Business receiving: The receiving of sewage sludge for deposit on or in any place within or within one
mile of the town limits, which place, as a part of its business, receives sewage sludge
for a consideration, is hereby prohibited.
B.
Business sending: The sending of sewage sludge for deposit to a place within or within one mile of
the town limits, which place, as a part of its business, receives sewage sludge for
a consideration, is hereby prohibited.
(Ord. 496, 1-11-1996)
Any person in violation of this chapter may be punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code. Each occurrence of sending and/or receiving sewage sludge
shall constitute a separate violation of this chapter.
(Ord. 496, 1-11-1996; amd. 2004 Code)
