Alcohol beverages: Fermented malt beverage or malt, vinous, or spirituous liquors; except "alcohol beverages"
shall not include confectionery containing alcohol within the limits prescribed by
statute.
Fermented malt beverage: As defined in the Colorado Beer Code, as amended.
Malt liquors: As defined in the Colorado Liquor Code, as amended.
Open container: Any container other than an original closed container as sealed or closed for sale
to the public by the manufacturer or bottler of the liquor or beverage. If an original
container has been unsealed, undone, or opened in any manner, it is an opened container
for purposes of this chapter.
Possession of alcohol beverage: A person has or holds any amount of alcohol beverage anywhere on his or her person,
or a person owns or has custody of any alcohol beverage, or has any alcohol beverage
in his or her immediate presence and control.
Premises: All or any part of the physical boundaries of any establishment duly licensed for
the sale and service of alcohol beverages in the town. The term "premises" shall include
not only the building wherein the business is conducted, but also the parking lot(s)
adjacent thereto.
Spirituous liquors: Any alcohol beverage obtained by distillation, mixed with water and other substances
in solution, and includes among other things brandy, rum, whiskey, gin, and every
liquid or solid, patented or not, containing at least one-half of one percent alcohol
which is fit for use for beverage purposes. Any liquid or solid containing beer or
wine in combination with any other liquor, except as provided in the definitions of
"malt liquors" and "vinous liquors", as defined herein, shall not be construed to
be fermented malt or malt or vinous liquor but shall be construed to be "spirituous
liquor".
Vinous liquors: Wine and fortified wines which contain not less than one-half of one percent and
not more than 21 percent alcohol by volume, and shall be construed to mean alcohol
beverage obtained by the fermentation of the natural sugar contents of fruits or other
agricultural products containing sugar.
(Ord. 608, 9-24-1998; amd. 2004 Code; Ord. 07-2006, 2-7-2006; Ord. 13-2006, 3-14-2006;
Ord. 33-2012, 10-9-2012; Ord. 01-2019, 1-22-2019)
A.
Prohibitions enumerated: Except upon premises holding valid licenses to sell or serve alcohol beverages, it
is unlawful:
1.
Generally: To carry or possess any open container of alcohol beverage on any street, sidewalk,
alley or other public place in the town, or in or upon any motor vehicle so located
in the town, or on the grounds of any public or private school, college or university
in the town.
2.
Public consumption: To consume any alcohol beverage in any public place except any licensed premises
permitted under state law to sell such liquor by the drink for consumption on the
premises.
3.
Open container in vehicle: For the operator of a vehicle in any public place in the town to allow any open container
of alcohol beverage within the passenger compartment of the vehicle.
B.
Exemptions: Nothing in this section shall prohibit any of the following:
1.
Possession or consumption of alcohol beverages in public parks where authorized by
a properly issued special event permit issued pursuant to C.R.S. § 12-48-101, et seq.,
where the person possessing or consuming the alcohol beverage is 21 years of age or
older; provided that no such permit shall allow the possession or consumption of alcohol
beverages in any vehicle.
2.
Possession or consumption of fermented malt beverages in public parks where authorized
by a permit issued pursuant to section 4-8-12 of this Code, where the person possessing or consuming the fermented malt beverage
is 21 years of age or older. No permit shall allow the possession or consumption of
alcohol beverages in any public park, nor shall any permit authorize the consumption
of fermented malt beverages or alcohol beverages in any vehicle.
3.
Possession or consumption of fermented malt beverages at a block party where authorized
by a permit issued pursuant to section 7-1-5 of this Code, where the person possessing or consuming the fermented malt beverage
is 21 years of age or older. No permit shall allow the possession or consumption of
alcohol beverages in any public park, nor shall any permit authorize the consumption
of fermented malt beverages or alcohol beverages outside the area of the permitted
block party.
(Ord. 608, 9-24-1998; amd. 2004 Code; Ord. 13-2006, 3-14-2006; Ord. 23-2013, 6-11-2013; Ord. No. 003-2023 , 2-14-2023)
A.
Prohibitions enumerated: It is unlawful for any person:
1.
To sell, serve, give away, dispose of, exchange, or deliver or permit the sale, serving,
giving, or procuring of any alcohol beverage to or for any person under the age of
21 years; or
2.
To obtain or attempt to obtain any alcohol beverage by misrepresentation of age or
by any other method in any place where alcohol beverages are sold when such person
is under the age of 21 years; or
3.
To possess or consume alcohol beverages anywhere in the town including, but not limited
to, in any public or private place, or in any vehicle, when such person is under 21
years of age; or
4.
Permit any person under 21 years of age, of whom he or she may be a parent or guardian,
to violate subsection A.1., A.2. or A.3. of this section.
5.
Illegal possession or consumption of an alcohol beverage by an underage person is
a strict liability offense.
B.
Prima facie evidence: Prima facie evidence of a violation of subsection A.3. of this section shall consist
of:
1.
Evidence that the defendant was under the age of 21 years and possessed or consumed
alcohol beverages anywhere in the state; or
2.
Evidence that the defendant was under the age of 21 years and manifested any of the
characteristics commonly associated with alcohol intoxication or impairment while
present anywhere in the state.
C.
Defense: It shall be an affirmative defense that the person under the age of 21 years was
participating in a religious ceremony or practice, or is participating in a supervised
and bona fide investigation conducted by a law enforcement agency.
D.
Additional penalty: In addition to any other penalty imposed by the court, upon each conviction, or entry
of guilty or nolo contendere plea, for violation of subsection A.2. or A.3. of this
section, any driver's permit or license held by the offender shall be revoked as provided
in C.R.S. § 42-2-125(1)(m), as amended, where:
1.
The offender has failed to complete an alcohol evaluation or assessment, an alcohol
education program, or an alcohol treatment program ordered by the municipal court
in connection with such conviction; or
2.
The offender has been convicted, entered a guilty or nolo contendere plea, for violation
of subsection A.2. or A.3. of this section and has a previous conviction for any similar
local or state alcohol offense.
E.
Immune from criminal prosecution: An underage person shall be immune from criminal prosecution under this section if
he or she establishes the following:
1.
The underage person called 911 and reported in good faith that another underage person
was in need of medical assistance due to alcohol consumption;
2.
The underage person who called 911 provided his or her name to the 911 operator;
3.
The underage person was the first person to make the 911 report;
4.
The underage person who made the 911 call remained on the scene with the underage
person in need of medical assistance until assistance arrived and cooperated with
medical assistance or law enforcement personnel on scene.
(Ord. 608, 9-24-1998; amd. 2004 Code; Ord. 20-2009, 9-22-2009; Ord. 09-2015, 5-12-2015)

During any trial for a violation of this chapter, any bottle, can or any other container
with labeling indicating the contents of such bottle, can or container shall be admissible
into evidence, and the information contained on any label on such bottle, can or other
container shall be admissible into evidence and shall not constitute hearsay. A jury
or a judge, whichever is appropriate, may consider the information upon such label
to determine whether the contents of the bottle, can or other container were composed
in whole or in part of alcohol. A label which identifies the contents of any bottle,
can or other container as "beer", "ale", "malt beverage", "fermented malt beverage",
"malt liquor", "wine", "champagne", "whiskey", "gin", "vodka", "tequila", "schnapps",
"brandy", "cognac", "liquor", "cordial", or "alcohol" shall constitute prima facie
evidence that the contents of the bottle, can or other container was composed in whole
or in part of alcohol.

A.
Permitting disturbance unlawful: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
permit any disturbance, unlawful or disorderly act or conduct to be committed by any
person or group of persons upon any premises.
B.
Encouragement/participation in disturbance unlawful: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, in
any manner to encourage or participate in any disturbance, unlawful or disorderly
act or conduct upon the premises.
C.
Prohibited activities: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
permit within or upon the licensed premises, any of the following:
1.
The loitering of a visibly intoxicated person or habitual drunkard;
2.
Rowdiness, undue noise, or other disturbances or activity offensive to the senses
of the average citizens, or to the residents of the neighborhood in which the licensed
establishment is located.
D.
Consumption during allowed times only: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
permit the consumption of alcohol beverages on the licensed premises at any time when
the sale of such beverages is prohibited by law.
E.
Service to intoxicated person: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
sell, serve, give away, dispose of, exchange, or deliver, or permit the sale, serving,
giving, or procuring of, any alcohol beverage to a visibly intoxicated person or a
habitual drunkard.
F.
Certain alcohol beverages prohibited: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
have in possession or upon the licensed premises any alcohol beverage, the sale of
which is not permitted by said licensee.
G.
Absence of licensee not a defense: It shall not be a defense to prosecution under this section that the licensee was
not personally present on the premises at the time the alleged violation occurred.
An employee or agent of the licensee shall not be responsible hereunder when absent
from the premises and not on duty.
H.
Penalties: This section is declared criminal in nature. Upon conviction, the penalties set forth
at subsection 1-4-4 B. of this Code may be imposed by the municipal court.

Reporting of disorderly acts required: It shall be unlawful for any licensee, or employee or agent of said licensee, to
fail to immediately report to the Erie police department any unlawful or disorderly
act, conduct or disturbance committed on the premises.
B.
Posting of sign: Each licensee shall post and keep posted at all times visible to the public in a
conspicuous place on the premises, a sign to be provided by the town clerk's office
which shall provide:
WARNING: Town of Erie Police Department must be IMMEDIATELY notified of all disturbances
that occur in this establishment, including the grounds and parking lot.
C.
Absence of licensee not a defense: It shall not be a defense to prosecution under this section that the licensee was
not personally present on the premises at the time the alleged violation occurred.
An employee or agent of the licensee shall not be responsible hereunder when absent
from the premises and not on duty.
D.
Failure to comply: Failure to comply with the requirements of this section may be considered by the
local liquor licensing authority in any action relating to suspension, revocation
or nonrenewal of a liquor or beer license. Repeated failure to comply with the requirements
of this section shall constitute prima facie grounds for the suspension, revocation
or nonrenewal of a liquor or beer license.
E.
Penalties: This section is declared criminal in nature. Upon conviction, the penalties set forth
at subsection 1-4-4 B. of this Code may be imposed by the municipal court.
