This chapter is enacted to protect, preserve, and promote the public health, safety
and welfare by requiring the registration of home childcare establishments.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
For purposes of this chapter, home childcare establishment means a business providing care and education for children in a residential dwelling
unit, subject to the licensing requirements of the state and registration requirements
of the town.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
A.
It is unlawful for any person to operate a home childcare establishment in the town
without first registering with the town.
B.
Registration shall renewed on an annual basis.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
An application to register a home childcare establishment shall be submitted to the
town clerk each year on forms provided by the town clerk, and shall include the following
information, at a minimum:
1.
The applicant's name;
2.
The physical address and mailing address of the establishment;
3.
The applicant's phone number and email address; and
4.
A copy of the applicant's current state childcare license.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
Each home childcare establishment in the town shall maintain a current and valid state
license at all times, and shall operate in accordance with such license.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
A.
It is unlawful for any person to violate any provision of this chapter.
B.
Violations of this chapter shall be punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.
( Ord. No. 022-2024 , § 1, 6-11-2024)
