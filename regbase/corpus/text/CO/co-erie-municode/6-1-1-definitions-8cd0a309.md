As used in this title, the following definitions shall apply:
Ballistic knife: Any knife that has a blade which is forcefully projected from the handle by means
of a spring loaded device or explosive charge.
Blackjack: Any billy club, sand club, sandbag or other hand operated striking weapon consisting,
at the striking end, of an encased piece of lead or other heavy substance and, at
the handle end, a strap or springy shaft which increases the force of impact.
Bodily injury: Physical pain, illness or any impairment of physical or mental condition.
Criminal negligence: When a person, acting in gross deviation from the standard of care that a reasonable
person would exercise, fails to perceive a substantial and unjustifiable risk that
a result will occur or that a circumstance exists.
Deadly weapon: Any firearm, whether loaded or unloaded, knife, bludgeon or other weapon, device,
instrument, material or substance, whether animate or inanimate, which, in the manner
it is used or intended to be used, could produce death or serious bodily injury.
Firearm: Any handgun, automatic, revolver, pistol, rifle, shotgun or other instrument or device
capable or intended to be capable of discharging bullets, cartridges or other explosive
charges, but does not include a BB gun, pellet gun or air-soft gun.
Firefighters: The volunteers and personnel of fire districts and fire departments who are assisting
the fighting of fires, responding to hazardous materials incidents, or emergency situations.
Gas or mechanically operated gun: An air, gas, or spring operated gun or blow gun that discharges pellets, BB shots,
arrows, or darts, including, without limitation, BB guns, spring guns, and similarly
operated guns or weapons.
Handgun: A pistol, revolver or other firearm of any description, loaded or unloaded, from
which any shot, bullet or other missile can be discharged, the length of the barrel
of which, not including any revolving, detachable, or magazine breech, does not exceed
12 inches.
Intentionally or with intent: A person acts "intentionally" or "with intent" when such person's conscious objective
is to cause the specific result proscribed by the ordinance defining the offense.
It is immaterial to the issue of specific intent whether or not the result actually
occurred.
Knife: Any dagger, dirk, knife or stiletto with a blade over three and one-half inches in
length, or any other dangerous instrument capable of inflicting cutting, stabbing
or tearing wounds, but does not include a hunting or fishing knife carried for sports
use. The issue that a knife is a hunting or fishing knife must be raised as an affirmative
defense.
Knowingly or willfully: A person acts "knowingly" or "wilfully" with respect to conduct or to a circumstance
when such person is aware that his or her conduct is of such nature or that such circumstance
exists. A person acts "knowingly" or "wilfully" with respect to a result of his or
her conduct, when such person is aware that his or her conduct is practically certain
to cause the result. Neither requires knowledge of the unlawfulness of such conduct.
Litter: All rubbish, waste material, refuse, garbage, trash, fecal material, debris or other
foreign substance, solid or liquid, of every form, size, kind and description.
Nunchaku: An instrument consisting of two sticks, clubs, bars or rods used as handles, connected
by rope, cord, wire or chain, designed as a weapon used in connection with the practice
of a self-defense system.
Open container: Any container other than an original closed container as sealed or closed for sale
to the public by the manufacturer or bottler of the liquor or beverage. If an original
container has been unsealed, undone, or opened in any manner, it is an opened container
for purposes of this title.
Owner: A person or entity owning, leasing, occupying or having the right of possession or
control of any property, real or personal, located in the Town.
Premises: Any real estate and all improvements erected thereon.
Recklessly: When a person acts in conscious disregard of a substantial and unjustifiable risk
that a result will occur or that a circumstance exists.
Substances releasing toxic vapors: The following products: plastic (styrene) cements containing toluene, acetone, benzene,
aliphatic acetates (such as ethyl acetate and methylcellosolve acetate), hexane; model
cements containing acetone, toluene or naphtha toluene or naphtha of petroleum origin;
household cements containing toluene, acetone, isopropanol, methyl ethyl ketone or
methyl isobutyl ketone; fingernail polish removers containing acetone, aliphatic acetates,
benzene or alcohol; lacquer thinners containing toluene, aliphatic acetates or methyl,
ethyl or propyl alcohol; lighter fluids or cleaning fluids containing naphtha of petroleum
origin, perchlorethylene, trichloroethane or carbon tetrachloride.
Throwing stars: A disk having sharp radiating points or any disk shaped blade object which is handheld
and thrown, and which is designed as a weapon used in connection with the practice
of a self-defense system.
