A.
Authority to grant licenses or permits; term; conditions: The town council may grant such number of licenses or permits for a period of not
to exceed one year to such persons as shall comply with the requirements of this section.
B.
Application for license or permit: Any person desiring to carry on the business of temperance hall, restaurant, pool
or billiard or card room, or any room where any game of chance or skill, or any gaming
device whatsoever is carried on shall present and file with the town clerk and the
state, a written application, stating particularly the name of the applicant or applicants,
or if a firm or partnership, the name or names of the partners composing the same,
together with the name and style of the said partnership or firm, the kind and nature
of the said business proposed to be carried on and the premises or place where the
same is to be conducted, giving the location and number of rooms if more than one,
which said application shall be signed by a majority of the property owners on both
sides of the street adjoining the block where the same is to be carried on, requesting
that a permit or license be issued to the person named in such application at the
place and premises therein designated, and when such premises or proposed place of
business shall happen to be on the corner of any block, then such application aforesaid
shall be signed by a majority of the property owners half way in such block and on
each side thereof from such corner.
C.
License fee: The town council shall not entertain or consider any such application until such
applicant shall deposit with the town clerk the sum as provided in section 2-10-2
of this Code upon the receipt of which it is made the duty of the clerk to endorse
on such application that such applicant has deposited with him said sum, the same
being the license fee for which such application is made, and if there be more than
one room in which such business is to be carried on, then there is required an additional
sum as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code for each and every other room so to
be used for such business, to be deposited in addition to the sum first above mentioned;
said sum when so deposited shall be the payment of such permit or license fee for
the period of one year granted, and if for any reason the same be not granted or is
refused, then the clerk shall, on demand and tender of receipt of such applicant so
depositing the same, return to said party the sum so placed on deposit.
D.
Issuance of license or permit; form: In case the application is allowed and granted by the town council, then it shall
be the duty of the clerk to issue and deliver to such applicant a permit or license
to carry on such business, which shall be substantially in the following form:
Town of Erie, Colorado, 20___ To All Whom It May Concern:
Be It Known, That the Town Council of the Town of Erie, in the State of Colorado,
on receipt of, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, have and do hereby license
and empower to pursue and carry on the business of at and in the building and premises
known as consisting of rooms, situate on Street, in the said Town of Erie, State of
Colorado, from the day of ___, A.D. 20___, to the day of ___, A.D. 20___, both days
inclusive, under and subject to such regulations and restrictions as are now or may
hereafter be provided by the ordinances of the said Town of Erie, Colorado, in relation
thereto.
Mayor
(Seal) Attest:
Clerk
Upon the issuance of such permit or license as aforesaid, the clerk shall turn over
to the town treasurer all monies so deposited with him by the applicant therefor,
and take his receipt or voucher therefor, which he shall file in his office, subject
to the inspection of any officer of the town, or other person having an interest therein.
E.
Transfer of license or permit: The permit or license thus issued shall not be transferable or assignable to any
other person, nor shall it allow or authorize such person named to carry on such business
therein designated at any other premises, room or rooms than are stated in such permit
or license and application therefor unless permission shall have been first had and
obtained from the town council by a written application, stating such facts and signed
by such transferee or assignee, or in case of the change of rooms or the addition
of rooms to the premises which the said business is to be carried on by the original
applicant in the same manner as in the case of an original application as above set
forth, in which event the said town council shall have the power and authority to
grant permission for such transfer, assignment or change of rooms or premises as in
the first instance.
F.
License or permit to be posted; penalty: It shall be the duty of each and every person to whom the permit or license shall
have been issued or granted as aforesaid to post the same in some conspicuous place
on the wall within the premises or place of business thus carried on by him so as
to permit easy access and inspection thereof by anyone desiring to inspect the same
and especially by any police officer of the town, and any person failing so to do
or failing to show the same when so requested by any of the town officers or council
members shall be in violation of this chapter.
G.
Days and hours of operation: Each and every one of the proprietors or keepers, or any or either of them, of the
places and premises enumerated in this section, or any of the said game or games of
skill or chance, or gaming device or game whatsoever, shall promptly and punctually
close their several places of business at 11:00 p.m. of the evenings of weekdays,
and keep the same closed until 5:00 a.m. of the following day, and from 11:00 p.m.
on Saturday evening until 5:00 a.m. on the following Monday morning. The same shall
at all times be left closed during each and all of the said hours of closing, and
no person, except the proprietors or keepers, shall be permitted to go in or to remain
around the same, but all and every other person shall be expelled or put out of the
same at the time named. If it shall appear that any person not employed in and about
such place of business has been permitted to be in or around any such place during
the time herein prohibited, such fact shall be prima facie evidence in favor of the
guilt of the accused party.
H.
Certain persons prohibited:
1.
Minors and drunkards prohibited: Any person being the proprietor or keeper of any of the places or kinds of businesses
mentioned and contemplated, either with or without a license therefor, obtained in
the manner as herein provided, who shall permit any minor or habitual drunkard to
frequent or to be in or about the said premises or place of business, or to procure
or drink any spirituous, malt or vinous liquors in or about the same, or to participate
in any of the game or games carried on in the said premises or place of business,
or in any game of pool, billiards, cards, or any other game of skill or chance whatsoever,
either with or without betting, shall be in violation of this chapter.
2.
Sign requirements: Each and every proprietor or keeper of any of the places or premises herein enumerated
or contemplated within the terms or provisions of this section, or any of the game
or games, or any gaming device, whatsoever, which shall be carried on within the corporate
limits of the town shall and are hereby required to make and place in every room a
sign, with letters not less than two inches in height and of corresponding proportions,
so as to be plainly legible and seen from any part of the room in which the same is
placed, and containing the following words: "No Minor or Habitual Drunkard Allowed
Here", which sign may be printed on cardboard, or any other suitable substance, capable
of making the said sign permanent and easily read, and which must be so placed as
to attract notice, and any person, either the proprietor or keeper of any of the places
or premises, failing to comply with the requirements of this subsection, shall be
in violation of this chapter.
I.
Gambling prohibited: Nothing in this section shall be held or construed to allow or permit any game or
gaming, or any sort or species of gambling, at any place within the corporate limits
of the town in violation of any law or statute now or hereafter in force within the
state, the intention of this section being only to regulate and control all such games
of chance or skill as are permitted by the laws of the state and in conformity with
the said laws, and for no other purpose.
(1928 Code, part IV; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001; amd. Ord. 489, 7-27-1995; 2004 Code; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
License required: It shall not be lawful for any person to carry on the business of keeper, for gain
or hire, within this town, any billiard table, bagatelle table, pigeonhole table,
shuffleboard, pin alley, shooting gallery, or any table on which games are played
with balls, without a license therefor first obtained according to the provisions
of this section.
B.
License fee: For a license, for one year, to keep any table mentioned in subsection A. of this
section, the amount as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code shall be paid. No license
shall be issued for any table or game mentioned in subsection A. of this section until
the fee therefor is paid.
C.
Enforcement: It shall be the duty of the chief of police to see that the provisions of this section
are strictly enforced.
(1928 Code, part IV; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001; amd. 2004 Code)
Any person violating any provision of this chapter shall be punishable as provided
in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.

When used in this article, the following words and terms shall have the meanings ascribed
to them in this section:
Music box: A coin operated device for the reproduction of music, or any mechanical or electrical
device for which the listener is required to pay for the privilege of choosing and
listening to a certain musical selection or series of selections.
Person: Includes every natural person, company, corporation, copartnership or other business
organization.
Pinball game: A game which conforms to the following basic principles of operation: a game or amusement
device contained in a rectangular box with a glass top and is released for operation
when a coin is deposited in the machine, so that small balls are made available for
play. These balls are propelled by a plunger operated by the player, and each ball
so propelled moves and behaves according to the manner in which the plunger is manipulated
by the player and eventually drops in one of a number of holes in the surface upon
which it has been traveling; these holes are protected by greater or lesser hazards,
and the object of the game is to direct the ball so that it will drop in the more
difficult hole by skillful manipulation of the plunger. The machines exit no money,
slugs or any article of any kind, and the player receives no reward from any source
either in merchandise, prizes, money or anything whatsoever, other than the amusement
and pleasure derived from playing the game.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953)
All persons are hereby prohibited from keeping, operating or leasing, for gain or
hire, any "pinball game", "music box" or other similar amusement device, as defined
in section 4-2A-1 of this article, within the town, unless such persons shall have first obtained a
license therefor in accordance with the provisions of this article and shall comply
with the other provisions hereof. No person operating or keeping any such pinball
game, music box or other similar amusement device shall lease or rent the same from
any person who does not have a license to lease such games or devices within the town.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953; amd. 2004 Code)
A.
Operation of amusement devices: For a license for one year to keep, maintain or operate any pinball game, music box
or similar amusement device, the location operator shall pay to the town clerk the
fee as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code.
B.
Dealers in amusement devices: Any dealer or owner of pinball games, music boxes or other similar amusement devices
who shall lease or rent the same for operation within the town shall pay to the town
clerk the sum as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code as a license fee for the
privilege of renting or leasing such games or mechanisms within the town. The foregoing
license fee does not include the license fee herein provided for the operation of
each specific pinball game, music box or other similar amusement device, but covers
only the right to lease or rent pinball games, music boxes or similar amusement devices
within the town.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001)
All licenses provided for herein shall be upon a calendar year basis and shall be
due and payable on January 1 in each year, payable in advance at the town hall.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953; amd. 2004 Code)
The license for the operation of a pinball game, music box or similar amusement device
shall be conspicuously posted upon the machine or mechanism.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953)
The licenses issued under this article shall not be transferable, except as specified
herein, and shall expire on January 1 each year, and no deduction in license fee shall
be allowed for a fractional part of a year.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953)
A.
Gambling: It shall be unlawful to permit betting, wagering or gambling of any kind upon the
result of any pinball game.
B.
Prizes and awards: No slugs, coin or merchandise awards of any kind shall be given to the player of
any pinball game, music box or similar amusement device.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953)
A.
Standards for revocation: All licenses issued hereunder shall be revocable by the town council for breach of
any of the provisions of this article. In the event that a pinball machine, music
box, or other similar amusement device shall be so constructed as to permit a violation
of this article by the giving of coins, slugs, merchandise or an award of any kind
to the player, the license of the owner thereof to lease or operate machines within
the town may be revoked by the town council.
B.
Notice of revocation; hearing; fees nonrefundable: In the event that a license is revoked as aforesaid, the town council shall give
to the holder of such license notice of its intention to revoke the same and an opportunity
to be heard before the town council on the date specified in the said notice. In the
event of the revocation of any such license, the holder thereof shall be entitled
to no refund for the unexpired period of such license.
(Ord. 157, 5-4-1953; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
Any person violating any of the terms and provisions of this article shall be deemed
guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be punishable as provided
in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.

All special events, races, festivals, or similar exhibitions, parades, processions
or open air meetings conducted within the town shall be required to obtain a permit
from the town prior to commencing such activities. This requirement shall not apply
to such activities as private parties, community group functions, church activities,
etc., that are conducted solely on private property; except, that such activities
shall be conducted in compliance with all applicable ordinances of the town, applicable
regulations imposed by the Counties of Boulder and/or Weld, and applicable laws of
the state.
(Ord. 03-2017, 2-14-2017)
Permit application forms may be obtained from the town clerk or designee. All applications
must be properly completed and submitted to the town clerk or designee at least 60
days prior to the date the activity is to occur or a permit may not be issued due
to a lack of proper review and evaluation time.
(Ord. 03-2017, 2-14-2017)
A.
Fee: The fee as provided in section 2-10-2 of this Code shall be paid by the applicant
to the town at the time an application is submitted to the town clerk or designee.
Failure to pay the required fees will be grounds for the town clerk or designee to
declare the application incomplete.
B.
Deposit: Deposit may be required at the time of application submittal; deposit amount shall
be determined on a case-by-case basis considering the nature of the activity to be
conducted; except, that no deposit shall be less than $50.00.
(Ord. 03-2017, 2-14-2017)
Any person found to be in violation of this article shall, upon conviction thereof,
be punishable as provided in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.
