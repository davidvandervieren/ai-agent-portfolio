A.
Fund established: A fund is created entitled "Public Works Fund". Funds formerly collected in the drainage
fund will be deposited into said new fund. Erie Municipal flood control and drainage
revenues and expenditures, authorized by the Erie Town Council, will be transacted
through this fund.
B.
Disposition of funds: All revenues and fund balances will be used exclusively to repay the Colorado Water
Conservation Council loans. Year end fund balances shall be retained in this fund
for annual or reallocation by the town with a CWCB endorsement. In the event the year
end fund balance exceeds a one year advance payment balance, the town may wish to
allocate these funds to operation and maintenance activities.
C.
Implementation of fund: The fund shall be set up immediately, and funds formerly collected in the drainage
fund will be transferred into this public works fund. Immediately after transfer,
the drainage fund shall cease to exist.
(Ord. 429, 12-19-1991; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Fee assessed: A monthly fee as provided in section 2-10-4 of this title is hereby assessed to all
existing and future users having a water tap supplied by the town either inside or
outside the Erie Town limits and to all users inside the town limits who are furnished
water by other suppliers.
B.
Separate drainage fee: A separate drainage fee in the amount set forth in section 2-10-4 of this title shall
be charged for each dwelling unit of a multi-family building and each business of
a multi-tenant building. All fees will be deposited into the public works fund of
the Town as revenue for the purpose of defraying the expenses of that fund.
(Ord. 588, 12-11-1997; amd. Ord. 756, 8-14-2001; 2004 Code)
The town shall have the right to recover all sums due the town under the terms of
this chapter from property owners in the town and shall seek such recovery by lien(s)
against property, and/or judgment and execution thereon through civil action in any
court of competent jurisdiction. Failure to pay the fee with the monthly water bill
will subject customers to the same water bill delinquency charges mandated by section 8-1-18 of this Code.
(Ord. 429, 12-19-1991)
At a minimum, fees, fund, and this chapter will continue to exist until repayment
to CWCB has been satisfied.
(Ord. 429, 12-19-1991)
