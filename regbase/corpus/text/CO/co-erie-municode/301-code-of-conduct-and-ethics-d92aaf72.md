The Town Council shall adopt and maintain by ordinance a Code of Conduct and Ethics,
which shall address Town Council conflicts of interest and behavior. The adopted Code
of Conduct and Ethics shall specify an enforcement mechanism, including penalties,
for violations of this Charter.
