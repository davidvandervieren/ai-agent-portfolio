The accumulation of garbage, rubbish and junk encourages the infestation of rodents,
pests and vermin, compromises the public health resulting from dangerous and unsanitary
conditions, constitutes a fire hazard and threat to public safety and general welfare,
and otherwise erodes the aesthetic conditions of the community, degrading the quality
of life of the citizens of the town.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012)
As used in this chapter, the following definitions shall apply:
Building materials: Lumber, bricks, concrete or cinderblocks, plumbing materials, electrical wiring or
equipment, heating ducts or equipment, shingles, mortar, concrete or cement, sand,
gravel, dirt, rocks, nails, screws or any other materials, or the debris from such
materials, used in construction or modifying any building, structure or landscaping
improvement on any real property.
Garbage: Any animal or vegetable waste resulting from the preparation, cooking and serving
of food, or the storage or sale of produce, and includes any discarded, damaged and/or
dilapidated household items, wearing apparel of any kind.
Junk: Any manufactured goods, appliances, fixtures, furniture, machinery, salvage material,
scrap metal, scrap material, and other items in such condition of deterioration or
disrepair so as to be unusable in their existing state.
Owner: Any person leasing, owning, occupying, having the right of possession and/or control
of any property real or personal located within the town boundaries.
Property: The area of the owner's lot or tract of land, improved or vacant, and the area to
the center of the alley adjacent to the lot or tract of land, and the sidewalk, parking
area, curb, and gutter adjacent to said lot or tract.
Recreational vehicle: A self-propelled, wheeled or tracked vehicle primarily designed to be operated for
recreational purposes on land or on land and water and which is not licensed for travel
on public streets, roads or highways, including without limitation trail bikes, dirt
bikes, minibikes, tote-goats, dune buggies, go-carts, snowmobiles and all-terrain
vehicles.
Rubbish: All accumulation of waste; refuse; rejected animal, mineral or vegetable matter;
manure; ashes; wastepaper; cans; bottles; leaves; grass cuttings; shrubbery and tree
trimmings; weeds; or other waste material. Rubbish shall also mean discarded construction
materials, including, but not limited to, the following: lumber, plaster, roofing
material, gravel, bricks, sheetrock, concrete blocks and landscaping ground cover.
Town manager: The town manager or designee.
Unusable merchandise: Parts of machinery or motor vehicles, appliances that can no longer be used for their
intended purpose, remnants of wood, metal, plastic or any other material or cast off
material of any kind, whether or not same could be put to some reasonable use, or
any item of merchandise that is so dilapidated or deteriorated that it cannot be reasonably
used for its intended purpose.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012; amd. Ord. 04-2019, 2-12-2019; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Building materials: In any area zoned for residential use, the outside, uncovered storage upon any real
property of building materials is prohibited, unless there is a current, valid building
permit issued by the town for construction or modification of a structure on the real
property, and the building materials are intended for use in connection with such
construction or modification. Building materials may be stored inside an enclosed
building, or may be stacked and covered in the rear of the property, out of view from
any adjoining street, whether or not a building permit is in force.
B.
Outside storage: In any area zoned for residential use, the outside storage or accumulation of unusable
merchandise is prohibited.
C.
Accumulation of trash:
1.
In any area zoned for residential use, the outside storage or accumulation of trash,
rubbish, garbage, refuse of any kind or litter upon or around any part of the yard
or any area of the ground immediately surrounding the building or structure located
on the real property, including porches or landings of such buildings, is prohibited.
2.
In any area zoned for multifamily residential use, the common areas, parking areas
and streets shall also be kept clear of trash, garbage, rubbish and refuse, except
for common trash enclosures.
3.
In any area zoned for commercial use, the owner, proprietor, landlord or management
company shall ensure that the premises, including the parking lot and that part of
any public right-of-way adjoining the premises and outside the curb line are kept
free of trash, rubbish, garbage, discarded merchandise, discarded goods or materials
or any other kind of junk or refuse of any kind. The owner, proprietor, landlord or
management company shall provide for daily cleaning of the premises and other such
areas and take all reasonable steps to provide trash receptacles and order all employees
and tenants to use them.
D.
Motor vehicles: In any area zoned for residential use, the owner or occupant of real property shall
maintain any and all areas of said real property free and clear of inoperable, unsafe,
unlawful or abandoned motor vehicles, motor homes or recreational vehicles.
(Ord. 04-2019, 2-12-2019)

A.
Removal and disposal required: All garbage and rubbish must be removed from the places where the same shall accumulate,
and be disposed of in such a manner as provided herein. It shall be unlawful for an
owner of property to fail to cause all garbage and rubbish to be removed from said
property on a regular basis, so as not to create a public nuisance.
B.
Storage of garbage and rubbish:
1.
Storage requirements: Garbage and rubbish stored for prompt removal from the property shall be stored in
closed containers located on the property. In the event the size of an item of garbage
or rubbish exceeds the size of the container intended for storage of garbage and rubbish,
the item(s) may be stored on the property in a location so as not to create a public
nuisance. Any oversize item of garbage or rubbish stored outside of a closed container
shall be promptly removed from the property, as required in subsection A. of this
section.
2.
Containers:
a.
The container used for storage of garbage and rubbish shall be a receptacle of a solid
and durable grade of metal or plastic provided with a tightly fitting cover, or a
plastic bag of adequate weight and strength to resist tearing.
b.
The container shall be covered or sealed at all times except when depositing garbage
or rubbish.
c.
The container shall be maintained in a clean and sanitary condition so as to promote
insect, pest and rodent control, and prevent attracting wild or stray animals.
d.
Each container may be located adjacent to the street or sidewalk only for pick up
by the licensed hauler, and only for a period of 24 hours from the time of pick up.
e.
Except for purposes of pick up, no container shall be stored in a front yard adjacent
to a street.
C.
Frequency and methods of removal:
1.
All garbage and rubbish accumulated pursuant to this section shall be removed from
the property at least once every two weeks.
2.
Removal may be accomplished in the following manner:
a.
Commercial hauler licensed to operate in the town.
b.
A person may haul or dispose of his/her own garbage and/or rubbish provided the health,
safety and welfare of the public is not endangered and that no items of garbage and/or
rubbish are scattered or thrown onto any street, alley, sidewalk, public or private
property.
D.
Burning prohibited: It shall be unlawful for any person to burn any garbage and/or rubbish in any incinerator,
stove, fireplace, furnace or by any other means.
(Ord. 751, 5-23-2001)
A.
Prohibited: It is unlawful and a public nuisance for any person to deposit or store on any street,
alley, sidewalk or other public right-of-way, any debris, lumber, sand, gravel, dirt,
rocks, brick, or any other construction or landscaping materials of any type, or any
dumpster, container, or other material or substance.
B.
Permit—Dumpster: Upon proper application filed with the town clerk's office, a permit may be issued
to allow the temporary placement of a dumpster or similar container on a street, alley
or other public right-of-way as specifically designated in the permit. No such dumpster
or similar container shall be placed on any sidewalk or in such a manner as to block
traffic, pedestrians or in such a manner as to create a safety hazard.
C.
Permit—Landscape materials: Upon proper application filed with the town clerk's office, a permit may be issued
to allow the temporary placement of landscape materials, to include, but not be limited
to soil, mulch, rock, gravel, on a street, alley or other public right-of-way as specifically
designated in the permit. No such items shall be placed on any sidewalk or in such
a manner as to block traffic, pedestrians or in such a manner as to create a safety
hazard.
D.
Authorized items: A permit shall be issued only where the items authorized are necessary for landscaping,
construction, alteration or repair of adjoining property, and shall be limited to
60 days in any consecutive 12-month period, unless the town clerk grants one 60-day
extension prior to the expiration of the permit.
E.
Violations: Violations of this section are noncriminal. Upon conviction, the penalties in subsection 1-4-4 A. of this Code shall apply.
(Ord. 25-2019, 8-27-2019)
A.
Compost piles permitted: The owner of any property may maintain compost piles that are separated areas containing
plant refuse materials and soil maintained to facilitate decomposition and produce
organic material to be used as a soil conditioner.
B.
Nuisance conditions prohibited: No owner of property who maintains a compost pile shall fail to prevent a compost
pile from becoming a nuisance by putrefaction or attracting insects, rodents or other
animals. No owner of property shall permit a compost pile to become a health or safety
hazard.
C.
Violations: Violations of this section may be subject to prosecution in the municipal court and/or
abatement procedures pursuant to sections 6-12-11 and 6-12-12 of this chapter.
(Ord. 751, 5-23-2001)
A.
Summons and complaint: Upon a violation of this chapter, an officer may issue the owner a summons and complaint
to appear in the Erie Municipal Court.
B.
Violations: The provisions of this chapter are designated as noncriminal violations. Upon conviction,
penalties set forth at subsection 1-4-4 A. of this Code shall apply.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012)
The chief of police, or designee, or other such person as may be designated by the
town manager, shall ascertain and cause all public nuisances declared to be such by
this or any other provisions of this Code to be abated. If practicable, such officers
may initially request the owner or occupant for permission to enter private property
not then open to the public for purposes of inspection and abatement enforcement.
Otherwise, unless an emergency is found to exist, resulting in exigent circumstances,
such officer shall obtain a search warrant as provided for in rule 241, Colorado Municipal
Court Rules of Procedure.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Whenever the town manager determines that a nuisance exists on or in any property,
under this chapter, the town manager may provide written notice in the manner provided
in subsection C. of this section.
B.
Written notice of any nuisance, shall:
1.
Include the property address;
2.
Describe the violation(s);
3.
Include a correction order allowing ten days, or less time where warranted at the
discretion of the town manager, to make necessary repairs and improvements to abate
the nuisance;
4.
Inform the owner, occupant of the right to appeal pursuant to this chapter;
5.
Include a statement of the town's right to abate the nuisance and file a lien in accordance
with this section.
C.
Service of the written abatement notice shall be by first class mail, postage prepaid,
to the owner as reflected on the records of the county clerk's office. Besides service
by mail, the officer may, if practicable, provide a copy of the abatement notice by
hand delivery to the owner, or by posting such notice on the subject property, or
by other means reasonably calculated to provide notice.
D.
It shall be the duty of the owner or occupant of any property, in or upon which any
nuisance may be found, to abate the same within the time specified in the abatement
notice. Timely filing of an appeal from the abatement notice under this section shall
extend the time for abatement until a final ruling on the appeal is issued. In addition
to any other available sanction or relief, if such owner or occupant neglects or refuses
to comply with the requirements of such order within the time specified, he or she
commits a violation of this chapter.
E.
The town manager, or designee, shall be duly authorized to abate any nuisance specified
in this chapter, which shall include authority to engage the necessary assistance
and incur necessary expense therefor.
F.
The expense incurred by the town in abating any nuisance as provided herein may be
recovered by proper action from the owner or occupant of such property.
G.
The town manager shall certify to the town clerk the costs incurred in the abatement
of the nuisance, including administrative costs and attorney fees and five percent
for inspection and other incidental costs in connection therewith.
H.
Notice of assessment; appeal of charges:
1.
Upon receipt of the statement of charges, the town clerk shall mail to the owner of
record of the subject property a notice, stating the amount and grounds for the charges;
the deadline for payment of the assessment, and that in the event such payment is
not made, the town will assess the charges against the property; that any objection
to the proposed assessment must be made in writing and filed with the town clerk's
office within 14 days of the date of the notice of assessment. Upon the expiration
of the 14-day period, if the town clerk receives no objections, and if payment in
full has not been received, the town clerk shall certify such charges to the clerk
to the county treasurer who shall collect the assessment together with a ten percent
penalty for cost of collection, in the same manner as other taxes are collected.
2.
If the property owner files timely appeal with the town clerk, the town clerk shall
refer the matter to a hearing officer appointed by the town manager.
3.
Upon conclusion of administrative review, the hearing officer shall determine, in
writing, whether the charges are proper. The appellant shall have the burden of proof,
by a preponderance of the evidence. The hearing officer shall affirm, cancel or reduce
the charges. The written ruling of the hearing officer shall be provided to the town
clerk and the property owner.
4.
The hearing officer may reduce or cancel a proposed assessment if it is determined
that any of the following did not conform to the provisions of this section:
a.
Any required notice to correct the subject violations; or
b.
The work performed in abating the nuisance; or
c.
The computation of charges.
5.
If the hearing officer affirms the assessment, the owner shall pay the amount in full
by the reasonable deadline established by the hearing officer. In the event timely
payment is not received, the town clerk may certify the charges to the clerk to the
county treasurer as provided herein.
I.
In addition to the penal remedy and abatement procedure stated in this chapter, any
violation of this chapter is a public nuisance, for which there is no adequate remedy
at law, and therefore any court of competent jurisdiction shall, upon the town attorney's
application, enjoin the nuisance, as defined in this chapter. Nothing in this chapter
shall prevent the town attorney from seeking other appropriate legal or equitable
relief from any court of competent jurisdiction. In addition, the owner of any property
constituting a nuisance shall be liable to the town, jointly and severally with every
person creating or maintaining the nuisance, for the town's reasonable expense incurred
in abating the nuisance and the town attorney may commence and prosecute to final
judgment an action at law in any court of competent jurisdiction to collect such expenses.
As used in this section, court of competent jurisdiction includes, without limitation,
the Erie Municipal Court, which insofar as practicable, shall consider and determine
applications for legal or equitable relief according to the procedures otherwise applicable
to proceedings in Colorado District Courts.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Whenever the town manager finds that a nuisance exists under this chapter, and places
the public health, safety or welfare in immediate danger, the town manager may issue
an emergency abatement order, which shall comply with subsection 6-12-11 B. of this chapter, except that the emergency abatement order shall:
1.
Allow a time less than ten days, as is reasonable in the circumstances, to abate the
nuisance;
2.
State that, if the nuisance remains unabated at the end of the reasonable time given,
the town manager will cause abatement of the nuisance according to subsection 6-12-11 E. of this chapter; and
3.
State that, due to the emergency need for abatement, a timely appeal, according to
subsection 6-12-11 H.2. of this chapter may be heard after the abatement.
B.
Timely filing of an appeal from the emergency abatement order shall not extend the
time for abatement.
C.
If the nuisance remains unabated at the end of the reasonable time given, the town
manager shall cause abatement of the nuisance according to subsection 6-12-11 E. of this chapter.
D.
If the hearing officer, upon a timely and proper appeal, finds that the emergency
abatement order is unjustified, the town clerk shall not certify and the town shall
not recover abatement costs as a charge or lien against the property.
(Ord. 05-2012, 3-13-2012; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
Every owner, developer, tenant, homeowner's association or special district that has
assumed landscape maintenance responsibility shall keep all landscaping in a well-maintained
and healthy growing condition, which shall include the following, at a minimum:
1.
Mowing, irrigating, weeding, fertilizing, pruning, and other maintenance appropriate
for the type of plant materials in the property.
2.
Removal of trash, litter, weeds.
3.
Treating plant materials that exhibit evidence of insect, pest or disease damage.
4.
Removing dead or dying plants or shrubs.
5.
Removing dead plant materials, including trees.
6.
Replenishing natural landscape materials such as mulch, which no longer cover the
area in which they were originally deposited so as to achieve full coverage.
7.
Repairing, replacing or maintaining related structural features, including without
limitation reflecting pools, outdoor artwork, screening walls, retaining walls, fences,
benches or other street furniture elements, if readily seen from an adjoining street,
sidewalk or town-maintained trail, park, landscape or open space area.
8.
Any other action reasonably necessary to maintain landscaping.
B.
Maintenance responsibility shall include public right-of-way landscaping up to the
curb or edge of the street or roadway adjacent to said property.
