A.
Purpose and intent: Through the fence operations are those activities permitted or licensed by the town
to off airport users authorizing ground access to the Erie Municipal Airport. The
obligation to make an airport available for the use and benefit of the public does
not impose any requirement for the town to authorize ground access by aircraft from
adjacent property.
B.
Definitions:
Access area: That portion of an off airport parcel that is designated by the off airport user
of that land as the only area of such land on which aircraft may be maneuvered, stored,
repaired, operated or otherwise allowed. The access area shall not include any part
of an access taxiway or taxi lane.
Access license, commercial access license, license: Authorization from the Town of Erie for a commercial off airport user to convey aircraft
to and from the airport. A commercial access license may be one of the following:
1.
Commercial business access license.
2.
Commercial nonbusiness access license.
Owners of commercial land, held individually or in common, with a valid Town of Erie
business license are eligible for a commercial business access license. A commercial
business access license may be for multiple years.
Owners of commercial land, held individually or in common, used for the storage of
noncommercial aircraft are eligible for a commercial nonbusiness access license. A
commercial nonbusiness access license shall be for one year.
Access location: The point at which ground access from an off airport parcel to the airport is authorized
by the town.
Access permit, residential access permit, permit: Authorization from the Town of Erie for a resident of the Erie Airpark to convey
aircraft to and from the airport. A residential access permit is issued annually and
is valid for 12 months.
Access taxiway or taxi lane: An aircraft taxiway or taxi lane located on property other than the airport that
connects into a taxiway or taxi lane on the airport and that is constructed for the
purpose of allowing aircraft to be conveyed between on airport and off airport land.
Aeronautical activity: Activity that involves, makes possible, or is required for the operation of aircraft
or that contributes to or is required for the safety of such operations, as defined
by FAA rules and regulations and FAA grant assurances.
Airport: The area of land known as the Erie Municipal Airport (EIK) designed to accommodate
B-I aircraft, as referenced in the airport reference code (ARC), set aside for aeronautical
activity and which is to be used in the interest of the public for such purpose.
Commercial access license agreement: A written agreement between the town and a commercial access license holder stating
the terms and conditions of the licensee's ground access to the airport.
Erie Airpark: The residential community located adjacent to Erie Municipal Airport and consists
of residential lots within the Erie Airpark Subdivision located in Erie, Weld County,
Colorado.
FAA: The federal aviation administration.
FAA grant assurance: A provision within a federal grant agreement with which the recipient (town) of federal
airport development funding, has agreed to comply in consideration for the funding
provided.
Off airport parcel: Any tract of land not located on the airport that abuts or is adjacent to or will
abut or be adjacent to an on airport access taxiway or taxi lane.
Off airport user: Shall be one of the following:
1.
Commercial user: An off airport business, association or person desiring on airport ground access
from an off airport parcel for providing commercial or noncommercial aeronautical
activity.
2.
Residential user: A resident of the Erie Airpark desiring on airport ground access from their Erie
Airpark residence for noncommercial aeronautical activity.
Permittee or licensee: The holder of a permit or license authorizing ground access to the airport.
Through the fence access: Access permitted or licensed by the town to off airport users authorizing ground
access to the airport.
Town: The Town of Erie, Colorado.
C.
Conditions of commercial access license; fees: The amount of the license fees and the conditions of this section are as follows:
1.
Conditions of commercial access license:
a.
Application of provisions: All commercial off airport users will be governed by this section for ground access
onto the airport.
b.
Commercial access license requirements: All commercial off airport users who wish to have ground access to the airport shall
apply for a commercial access license from the town. A commercial access license is
granted to off airport users, subject to the terms and conditions of this section
and terms of the commercial access license granted by the town to an off airport user.
c.
License fees required: The commercial access license fee is required of all commercial off airport users
seeking ground access to the airport. If the licensee desires ground access for the
transient use by customers, lessees, or invitees, no additional fee is required.
d.
Payment of fee; delinquency: The commercial access license fee is due and payable on the first business day following
January 1 of each year. There will be a late fee of 12 percent per annum of the amount
of the license fee for any license fee paid after the first business day following
January 31.
e.
Transfer of license: With prior written approval from the town, this commercial access license is transferable
to a purchaser of a property to which a commercial access license has been issued.
f.
Lapse of license; renewal: A commercial off airport user that lets their commercial access license lapse is
eligible to apply for a new license in accordance with the terms and conditions as
set forth in this section; section 2-10-4 of this title; and the town's commercial
access license agreement.
2.
Commercial fees: The town will maintain a fee structure for commercial access licenses that shall
be in compliance with all FAA grant assurances. The commercial through the fence access
fee is set forth in section 2-10-4 of this title and was arrived at and based on the
current yearly rate for unimproved on airport land. Air recreational vehicles are
considered aircraft for the purposes of this section, and there will be no proration
for any license fees.
D.
Conditions of residential access permit; fees: The amount of the residential access permit fees and the conditions of this section
are as follows:
1.
Conditions of residential access permit:
a.
Application of provisions: Each residential off airport user will be governed by this section for ground access
onto the airport.
b.
Access permit required: Each resident of Erie Airpark who wishes to have ground access to the airport shall
apply for a residential access permit from the town. Permits are granted to off airport
users, subject to the terms and conditions of this section and terms of the permit
granted by the town to each off airport user.
c.
Permit fee required: The residential access permit fee is required of all Erie Airpark residents seeking
ground access to the airport. If the permittee desires ground access for the transient
use by invitees, no additional fee is required.
d.
Payment of fee; delinquency: The residential access permit fee is due and payable on the first business day following
January 1 of each year. There will be a late fee of 12 percent per annum of the amount
of the permit fee for any permit fee paid after the first business day following January
31.
e.
Transfer of permit: With prior written approval from the town, a residential access permit is transferable
to a subsequent resident at the same address within Erie Airpark to which a current
residential access permit has been issued.
f.
Lapse of permit; renewal: An individual that lets their residential access permit lapse is eligible to apply
for a new permit in accordance with the terms and conditions as set forth in this
chapter, section 2-10-4 of this title and the town's residential access permit.
2.
Residential fees: The town will maintain a fee structure for residential access permits that shall
be in compliance with all FAA grant assurances. The residential access fee is set
forth in section 2-10-4 of this title and was arrived at and based on a current yearly
on airport aircraft tie down rate. Air recreational vehicles are considered aircraft
for the purposes of this section, and there will be no proration for any permit fees.
E.
Authority to grant access: The Town of Erie retains the sole right to grant ground access to enter and exit
the airport. Authority to grant ground access to enter and exit the airport includes
the designation of access areas, access taxiways and taxi lanes, and the designation
of airport access locations. A through the fence license or permit holder does not
have a right to grant or sell access through its property allowing other parties on
adjacent land to gain access to the airport.
F.
Compliance with rules and regulations: Any off airport user receiving a commercial access license or residential access
permit must comply with all airport rules and regulations promulgated by the town
and must adhere to all requirements of existing or future FAA regulations (FARs) including
the FAA airport compliance manual order then applicable, other applicable federal
obligations, and must operate their aircraft in accordance with FAA rules and requirements
and any other applicable governmental regulations.
G.
Grant assurances: Should any provision of an access license or permit violate compliance with the rules
and regulations as stated in subsection F. of this section, the town shall have the
unilateral right to amend or terminate the access license or permit to remain in compliance
with the town's FAA grant assurances and federal obligations.
H.
Off airport business: The town will not enter into any commercial access license agreement that grants
"through the fence" access to the airport for aeronautical businesses that would compete
with an on airport aeronautical service provider. Exceptions may be granted on a case
by case basis where operating restrictions ensure safety and equitable compensation
for use of the airport and contribute to the airport's economic development and self-sustainability.
In compliance with the applicable FAA airport compliance manual order, the term of
commercial access license agreements will not extend beyond a reasonable period, from
the town's perspective, and will not depend upon nor extend to the full depreciation
of the "through the fence" operator's off airport investment. Should the commercial
access license agreement be renegotiated, the term of the new agreement should reflect
an economic rent for the depreciated off airport aeronautical facilities comparable
to what would be charged by the town for similar on airport facilities. The town will
maintain a fee structure for commercial access licenses that shall be in compliance
with all FAA grant assurances.
I.
Rights not exclusive: Nothing contained herein shall be construed to grant or authorize the granting of
an exclusive right in violation of grant assurances 22 and 23, economic nondiscrimination
and exclusive rights, forbidden by section 308 of the federal aviation act of 1958
or for aeronautical activities.
J.
Payment of fee; access authorized: So long as the airport remains a public airport, nothing contained herein shall be
deemed a denial of any right of access to any off airport user so long as a license
or permit is obtained in accordance with this section.
K.
Penalty: Any person utilizing the airport by way of through the fence access who does not
have a current commercial access license or residential access permit is in violation
of this section. Said violation constitutes a noncriminal offense, punishable as provided
in title 1 , chapter 4 of this Code.
(Ord. 03-2010, 2-23-2010)
A.
Fund created: A fund is created entitled "airport fund", to be a self-balancing set of accounts
for the purposes of acquisition and operation of airport facilities. This fund shall
comply with all accounting and budgetary laws of the town and the state.
B.
Relationship to other funds:
1.
Loans, transfers, and investments may be made to this fund by other funds for the
above stated purposes if approved by the town council. Any assistance by other funds
to the airport fund will be repaid to the other funds.
2.
The airport fund shall be self-supporting and not an encumbrance to other funds. The
airport shall be operated in a fiscal balance whereby the facilities accrue revenue
in excess of expenditures. If the airport does not accomplish this fiscal balance,
then the town council may consider closing the facility and ending its operation.
(Ord. 421, 9-12-1991; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
