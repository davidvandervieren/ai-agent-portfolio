There is hereby fixed and established a service charge in the amount of ten percent
to be collected from users of solid waste disposal sites, facilities and transfer
stations within the town for the purpose of financing solid waste management in the
town. Said service charge shall be added to all disposal fees received by any operator
operating a solid waste disposal site and facility or transfer station within the
town. "Solid waste disposal site and facility and transfer station" shall mean any
facility which is required, pursuant to applicable provisions of state law, to obtain
a certificate of designation or other approval from the Town of Erie or approval by
a county or state government prior to its operation.
(Ord. 14-2006, 5-9-2006)
It shall be the duty of the operator of any solid waste disposal site and facility
and transfer station in the town to collect said service charge at the time dumping
fees are collected for disposal, and to remit the same to the town director of finance
on or before the twentieth day of the month following the month in which said service
charge is collected. Failure to timely pay may result in imposition of a late charge
of one and one-half percent simple interest per month on the late payment amount.
In the event that the service charge collected by the operator of a transfer station
already includes the applicable town service charges on the landfill disposal fees
as required herein, no additional town service charge shall be collected for disposal
of the waste at a solid wastes disposal site and facility.
(Ord. 14-2006, 5-9-2006)
Should the solid waste site and facility or transfer station be one at which no disposal
fee is collected for the reason that the facility serves an individual user, the fee
shall be computed based upon the cost within the market for similar disposal services.
(Ord. 14-2006, 5-9-2006)
The service charge imposed by this chapter shall be used for the purposes of financing
solid waste management in the town including, but not limited to, costs of health
inspections; removal of litter and debris from roadsides; establishment, construction
and maintenance of roads providing access to solid waste sites and facilities and
transfer stations; financing the landfill management program; equalizing costs for
transfer sites in the town; capital outlay associated with a transfer site system;
insurance costs; town staff support costs; financing the requirements for compliance
with the resource conservation recovery act; costs of designation of future site locations;
support of programs that encourage recycling of wastes and proper handling of hazardous
wastes; and addressing environmental issues created by past and future solid waste
sites and facilities and other solid waste disposal sites.
(Ord. 14-2006, 5-9-2006)
All operators of solid waste disposal sites and facilities and transfer stations in
the town shall, at all times of operation, make available to the town director of
finance all books of account of said operator of solid waste disposal sites and facilities
for auditing purposes to ensure compliance by said operators with the provisions of
this chapter.
(Ord. 14-2006, 5-9-2006)
