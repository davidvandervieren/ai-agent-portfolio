This chapter is known and may be cited as the Town of Erie Land Use Fee Chapter.
(Ord. 487, 7-13-1995)
It is the purpose of this chapter to adopt uniform and accurate fees concerning certain
land uses.
(Ord. 487, 7-13-1995)
A.
Applicability: This chapter directly applies to the following land use activities:
1.
Annexations.
2.
De-annexations.
3.
Subdivision of land.
4.
Replats.
5.
Special use review.
6.
Variance/appeal.
7.
A change in zoning (zoning or rezoning).
8.
A vacation, amendment, or adjustment of streets, alleys, or other public ways.
9.
Document recording.
10.
Preapplication conferences.
11.
Site plan reviews.
12.
Plan amendment.
13.
Subdivision exemptions.
14.
Floodplain special use permits.
B.
Exemptions:
1.
This chapter does not apply to the following land use activities:
a.
Oil and gas exploration.
b.
Other types of mineral mining.
c.
Construction of buildings.
d.
Public infrastructure improvements.
e.
Legal challenges to a proposed land use activity.
2.
No fees shall be applied to land use activities which are undertaken solely by and
for the town. In the case of joint application by the town and a second applicant,
only those fees which directly pertain to the town involvement shall be waived.
(Ord. 487, 7-13-1995)
Prior to any official land use action by the town, any person desiring a land use
change or other land use activity covered by this chapter shall submit to the town
a completed application, furnished by the town, and shall pay in full the appropriate
fees as outlined in section 2-10-5 of this chapter. A separate application and applicable
fees shall be required for each land use change or processing stage being requested.
There is a separate application fee for each stage of subdivision (preapplication
conference, preliminary plat, and final plat).
(Ord. 487, 7-13-1995)
In the event certain land use activities require additional costs beyond the base
fees associated with the processing of a land use application, the additional cost
shall be billed to the applicant. The applicant shall be notified of the additional
activities and costs prior to those activities being undertaken. Additional costs
billed must be paid within 30 days of the billing date, and must be fully paid prior
to any final action being taken upon the requested land use activity, and before any
additional stage application is accepted.
(Ord. 487, 7-13-1995)
