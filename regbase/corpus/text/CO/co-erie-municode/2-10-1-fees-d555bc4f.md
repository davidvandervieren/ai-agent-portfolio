The town's current fee schedule shall be adopted by the town council by resolution.
The fee schedule shall include administrative fees, police department fees, public
works department fees, planning and development department fees, building permit fees,
parks and recreation department fees, cemetery fees and other miscellaneous fees.
(Ord. 30-2021 , §§ 1, 2, 10-12-2021; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023; Ord. No. 001-2024 , § 5, 2-13-2024)
