It shall be an affirmative defense to any provision of this chapter that the act was
committed by a peace officer in the lawful discharge of his or her duties.
(Ord. 11-2015, 5-12-2015; amd. Ord. 06-2017, 2-28-2017)
A.
It is unlawful for any person to knowingly:
1.
Carry a knife concealed on or about his or her person; or
2.
Carry a firearm concealed on or about his or her person.
B.
It shall be an affirmative defense if the defendant was:
1.
A person in his or her own dwelling or place of business or on property owned or under
his or her control at the time of the act of carrying; or
2.
A person in a private automobile or other private means of conveyance who carries
a weapon for lawful protection of such person's or another's person or property while
traveling; or
3.
A person who, at the time of carrying a concealed weapon, held a valid written permit
to carry a concealed weapon issued pursuant to C.R.S. § 18-12-105.1, as it existed
prior to its repeal, or, if the weapon involved a handgun, held a valid permit to
carry a concealed handgun or a temporary emergency permit issued pursuant to the provisions
of C.R.S. tit. 18, art. 12, pt. 2, as amended; except that it shall be an offense
under this section if the person was carrying a concealed handgun in violation of
C.R.S. § 18-12-214, as amended;
4.
A peace officer, as defined at C.R.S. § 16-2.5-101, as amended, when carrying a weapon
in conformance with the policy of the employing agency as provided at C.R.S. § 16-2.5-101(2),
as amended;
5.
A United States probation officer or United States probation pretrial services officer
while on duty and serving in the State of Colorado under the authority of rules and
regulations promulgated by the judicial conference of the United States.
(Ord. 11-2015, 5-12-2015; amd. Ord. 06-2017, 2-28-2017)
A.
It is unlawful to:
1.
Knowingly and unlawfully aim or point a firearm at or in the direction of another
person; or
2.
Knowingly or recklessly display or flourish a firearm in a manner calculated to alarm
another person; or
3.
Knowingly, recklessly or with criminal negligence discharge a firearm within the jurisdictional
limits of the town; or
4.
Recklessly or with criminal negligence discharge any gas or mechanically operated
gun on any public property within the jurisdictional limits of the town; or
5.
Recklessly or with criminal negligence discharge any gas or mechanically operated
gun on private property within the jurisdictional limits of the town;
6.
Knowingly or recklessly set a loaded gun, trap or device designed to cause an explosion
upon being tripped or approached, and leave it unattended by a competent person immediately
present;
7.
Have in one's possession a firearm while the person is under the influence of intoxicating
liquor or of a controlled substance, as defined at C.R.S. § 18-18-102(5), as amended.
Possession of a permit issued under C.R.S. § 18-12-105.1, as it existed prior to its
repeal, or possession of a permit or a temporary permit issued pursuant to the provisions
of C.R.S. tit. 18, art. 12, pt. 2, as amended, is no defense to a violation of this
section; or
8.
Knowingly aim, swing or throw a throwing star or "nunchaku" as defined in this title
at another person, or knowingly possess a throwing star or nunchaku in a public place
except for the purpose of presenting an authorized public demonstration or exhibition.
When transporting throwing stars or nunchaku for a public demonstration or exhibition,
they shall be transported in a closed, nonaccessible container.
B.
This section is declared to be a criminal violation. Upon conviction, the penalties
set forth at subsection 1-4-4 B. of this Code shall apply.
C.
Exception . It shall not be a violation of subsection A.3. hereof to discharge a firearm on
a single parcel of private property of more than five acres in size, provided that:
such property is zoned agricultural holding (AGH) under title 10 of this Code; the discharge of the firearm is for recreational purposes, such as
hunting or target shooting; the discharge of the firearm is made safely and in compliance
with all applicable law; and the bullet, cartridge or other projectile does not leave
the property.
(Ord. 11-2015, 5-12-2015; amd. Ord. 06-2017, 2-28-2017; Ord. No. 034-2024 , § 1, 11-12-2024)
A.
Any police officer, for probable cause for a violation of this chapter, may, in addition
to taking any other appropriate action, seize the weapon or item(s) used in the course
of a criminal episode. Any weapon or item(s) seized shall be secured by the police
officer in accordance with the applicable police policy.
B.
Upon motion of the prosecuting attorney after the conviction of a defendant, the court
may order the forfeiture of any firearms or other weapons which were used by the defendant
during the course of the criminal episode which gave rise to said conviction as an
element of sentencing or as a condition of probation or a deferred sentence. Firearms
forfeited under this section shall be disposed of pursuant to C.R.S. § 16-13-311,
as amended.
(Ord. 11-2015, 5-12-2015; amd. Ord. 06-2017, 2-28-2017)
No person shall carry a deadly weapon, concealed or openly, within the portion of
the Erie Police Station and Municipal Court Building, 1000 Telleen Avenue, Erie, Colorado
(the "building"), occupied by the Erie Municipal Court (the "court") nor within the
entrance and public lobby area of the building. It shall be an affirmative defense
under this section that the person carried a deadly weapon as part of the person's
duty as a peace officer designated in C.R.S. § 16-2.5-101 et seq. The town manager
or the town manager's designee shall ensure that:
A.
Signs are posted at the entrance to the public lobby area of the building and the
entrance to the court informing persons that carrying deadly weapons is prohibited
in that specific area;
B.
Security personnel and electronic weapons screening devices are permanently in place
at each public entrance to the court;
C.
Security personnel electronically screen each person who enters the court to determine
whether the person is carrying a deadly weapon of any kind; and
D.
Security personnel require each person who is carrying a deadly weapon of any kind
to leave the deadly weapon in possession of security personnel while the person is
in the court.
(Ord. 06-2017, 2-28-2017; Ord. No. 031-2023 , § 1, 11-28-2023)
A.
It shall be unlawful for any person to openly carry any deadly weapon within any town
owned building or indoor facility, or any building or indoor facility leased or occupied
by the town, or any department or agency thereof.
B.
Nothing herein shall be deemed to affect or impair in any way the authority of any
private or public property owner other than the town to prohibit the carrying of deadly
weapons into or upon other public or private property.
C.
This section shall not apply to any peace officer, as defined at C.R.S. § 16-2.5-101,
as amended, when carrying a weapon in conformance with the policy of the employing
agency as provided at C.R.S. § 16-2.5-101(2), as amended.
D.
This section is declared to be a criminal violation. Upon conviction the penalties
set forth at subsection 1-4-4 B. of this Code shall apply.
(Ord. 22-2018, 8-14-2018)
