Editor's note— Ord. 15-2020 , § 1, adopted March 10, 2020, added provisions designated as ch. 4.13, §§ 4-13-1—4-13-3.
Inasmuch as ch. 4.13 had previously been added to the Code, said provisions have been
added as ch. 4.14, §§ 4-14-1—4-14-3, as set out herein.
A.
The purposes of this chapter are to reduce waste, encourage recycling and obtain information
for the tracking and planning of waste diversion in the town.
B.
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 30-15-401(8), the town hereby consents to the applicability of
the Boulder County Commercial Waste Hauler Ordinance, as amended, within the town.
c.
This Chapter shall apply to all commercial waste haulers operating in the town. For
purposes of this chapter, the term "commercial waste hauler" shall have the same meaning
as "hauler" in the Boulder County Commercial Waste Hauler Ordinance, as amended.
( Ord. 15-2020 , § 2, 3-10-2020)
Each commercial waste hauler operating in the town shall comply with the licensing,
reporting and service requirements of the Boulder County Commercial Waste Hauler Ordinance,
as amended, and all regulations adopted thereunder.
( Ord. 15-2020 , § 2, 3-10-2020)
A.
Violation: It is a violation of this chapter for any commercial waste hauler to violate
any provision of the Boulder County Commercial Waste Hauler Ordinance, as amended.
B.
Penalty: A violation of this chapter shall be subject to the noncriminal penalty set
forth in section 1-4-4 A. of this Code. Each day of violation shall be deemed a separate offense.
( Ord. 15-2020 , § 2, 3-10-2020)
