(1)
The Town Council shall have authority to create and disband boards, commissions, and
committees and confer upon the same such powers and duties as it deems advisable by
resolution or by ordinance.
(2)
Members of Town boards, commissions, and committees shall be appointed in accordance
with a resolution or an ordinance setting forth the number of members, qualifications,
terms, appointment and removal procedures, and compensation, if any, for each board,
commission, or committee.
