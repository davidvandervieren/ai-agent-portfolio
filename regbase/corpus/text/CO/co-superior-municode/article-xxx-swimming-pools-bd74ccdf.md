Swimming pools may be located in any zone district as an accessory use, provided that
such pools are situated on a lot, tract or parcel in a manner which is not detrimental
to the health, safety and welfare of the users of the pool or the adjacent property
owners and which meet the design standards of the zone district in which the pool
is located.
(Ord. O-10 §1, 2010)
All swimming pools shall be completely enclosed by a minimum of a four-foot-high fence,
or be elevated at least four (4) feet above the ground level.
(Ord. O-10 §1, 2010)
Swimming pool areas and all accesses thereto shall be kept closed and made inaccessible
without the knowledge and consent of the owner.
(Ord. O-10 §1, 2010)
