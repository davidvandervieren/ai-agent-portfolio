The Public Works policies contained in Chapter 8 of this Code replace all previous road and bridge policies, fugitive dust control
on Weld County roads and procedures regarding survey monuments. Please refer to Chapter 8 for specific policies.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
A.
According to Section 42-4-406(6)(a), C.R.S.:
". . . local authorities, within their respective jurisdictions may, for the purpose
of road construction and maintenance, temporarily close to through traffic or to all
vehicular traffic any highway or portion thereof for a period not to exceed a specified
number of work days for project completion and shall, in conjunction with any such
road closure, establish appropriate detours or provide for an alternative routing
of the traffic affected. . . ."
In the past numerous requests have been received from the Department of Public Works
for temporary closure of County roads for periods of seventy-two (72) hours or less,
due to temporary road conditions and/or road construction. Said requests for closure
have often been placed on the Board's agenda for consideration after the roadways
have been reopened and the need for the temporary closure has been obviated. By resolution
dated January 10, 1994, the Board delegated the duty of ordering temporary closure
of County roads for periods of seventy-two (72) hours or less to the Director of Public
Works, for the following purposes:
1.
For the protection of health, safety and welfare of the traveling public from unsafe
road conditions.
2.
Maintenance of County roads and bridges.
3.
Construction of County roads and bridges.
B.
The Director of Public Works is directed by the Board to post such traffic control
devices as shall be reasonably necessary to regulate, warn and/or guide traffic on
or around those County roads and bridges which have been temporarily closed and that
said traffic control devices shall conform to the Federal Manual on Uniform Traffic
Control Devices.
C.
The Director of Public Works shall report said closures to the Board on a monthly
basis.
A.
When dry weather conditions cause prairie lands located in the County to become a
high fire danger, there is a need for a cooperative approach among citizens and private
and public entities to fight prairie fires which may occur on public and/or private
lands.
B.
Because of the extreme danger to public and/or private lives and property, the Board
of County Commissioners, by Resolution #2002-1582, approved June 17, 2002, authorized
the use of County motor graders or other heavy equipment operated by County personnel
to fight prairie fires on public or private lands in the County.
1.
Use of the equipment shall be deemed necessary by the on-scene fire commander.
2.
County personnel necessary to operate the motor graders or heavy equipment shall be
under the supervision and direction of the on-scene fire commander.
(Weld County Code Ordinance 2002-12; Weld County Code Ordinance 2007-6)
