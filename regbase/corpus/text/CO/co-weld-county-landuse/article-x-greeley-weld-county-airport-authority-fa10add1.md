A.
There is hereby created, pursuant to the provisions of the Public Airport Authority
Law, Section 14-10-101, et seq., C.R.S., an airport authority.
B.
The airport authority hereby created shall be known as the Greeley-Weld County Airport
Authority, hereinafter referred to as the Airport Authority.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
The Airport Authority shall be a body corporate and politic constituting a political
subdivision of the State, and shall have all power and authority granted to it under
the Colorado Public Airport Authority Act.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
A.
The City of Greeley and the County have contributed assets to the Airport Authority
as set forth in Exhibit A of Ordinance No. 30, 1978, on file with the Clerk to the
Board, which is incorporated herein by reference as if set forth fully herein. The
City has contributed seventy-four percent (74%) of the real property assets, and the
County has contributed twenty-six percent (26%). The City has contributed forty-seven
percent (47%) of the other assets, and the County has contributed fifty-three percent
(53%).
B.
Upon dissolution of the Airport Authority, by agreement of the County and City or
for any other reason, its liabilities and obligations to creditors shall be paid,
and the assets shown on said Exhibit A or the proceeds of their sale shall then be
distributed to the City and County in proportion to their capital contributions.
C.
After the formation of the Airport Authority, a list of contributions shall be maintained
showing what real property assets and other assets have been contributed by the City
and the County. Upon dissolution these assets shall be distributed to the City and
the County in proportion to their contributions. Property acquired by the use of federal
funds shall be returned to the United States Government if so required and, if such
is not required, it shall be divided fifty percent (50%) to the City and fifty percent
(50%) to the County.
D.
If a dissolution or termination of the Airport Authority shall occur and the City
and the County are in dispute as to any matter, the settlement of any such dispute
will be arbitrated in accordance with the then prevailing rules of the American Arbitration
Association.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
After formation of the Airport Authority, it is the intent of the County to contribute
funds or money to the Airport pursuant to the Airport Master Plan for capital investment,
but not to contribute toward the normal operations and maintenance of airport facilities.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
A.
All powers, privileges and duties vested in or imposed upon the Airport Authority
pursuant to the provisions of the Colorado Public Airport Authority Act shall be exercised
and performed by and through a Board of Commissioners (Airport Commissioners) representing
the City of Greeley and the County. Three (3) Airport Commissioners shall be appointed
by the City, two (2) to be City Councilpersons, and three (3) Airport Commissioners
shall be appointed by the County, two (2) to be County Commissioners.
B.
A seventh Airport Commissioner shall be appointed jointly by the City and the County,
the initial appointment to be made by a method agreeable to both the City and the
County. After the initial appointment, when a vacancy occurs in this position, the
Airport Commissioners, no later than one (1) month after notice of the vacancy, shall
recommend a candidate for the vacancy to the City Council and the Board of County
Commissioners. The City Council and the Board of County Commissioners must act upon
the recommendation within one (1) month of receipt and shall appoint the recommended
candidate except for good cause. Should the City Council or the Board of County Commissioners
reject the candidate recommended, the Airport Commissioners shall recommend another
candidate within one (1) month. Vacancies in the other six (6) Commissioner positions
shall be filled in the same manner as the original appointments and by the same governmental
entity (City or County) which made the original appointment.
C.
The term of each City Councilperson and County Commissioner shall be four (4) years,
or until such time as he or she is no longer a member of the City Council or Board
of County Commissioners, whichever is sooner, except that the initial terms of one
(1) of the City Councilpersons and one of the County Commissioners shall be two (2)
years. The Airport Commissioners who are not City Councilpersons or County Commissioners
shall each be appointed for four-year terms, except that, for those terms beginning
January 1, 1999, the terms shall be as follows: the City appointee shall be one (1)
year, the County appointee shall be two (2) years, and the joint appointee shall be
four (4) years. A quorum for the Airport Commissioners shall consist of four (4) Airport
Commissioners present.
(Weld County Codification Ordinance 2000-1)
