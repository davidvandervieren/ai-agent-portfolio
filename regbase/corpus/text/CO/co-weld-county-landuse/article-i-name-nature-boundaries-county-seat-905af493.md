The name of the county as it operates under this Charter shall continue to be Weld
County.
From the time that this Charter takes effect the County shall continue to be:
(1)
an agency of the State and
(2)
a body politic and corporate.
The boundaries of the County as it operates under this Charter shall be the boundaries
now or hereafter prescribed for the County by the laws of the State.
The seat of government of the County as it operates under this Charter shall continue
to be in the City of Greeley; provided nothing herein shall prevent the location of
county buildings and facilities elsewhere in the County.
