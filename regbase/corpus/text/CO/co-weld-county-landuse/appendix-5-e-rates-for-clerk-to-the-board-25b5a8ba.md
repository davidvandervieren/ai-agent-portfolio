TRANSCRIPTS FROM TAPES:
IF TRANSCRIBED IN-HOUSE: $60.00/hour for time spent on the transcription, plus $4.00 per page. (Provides two
(2) certified copies of transcript to requestor.) Minimum charge of $60.00, with a
deposit of three (3) times the clock time of the tape to be paid in advance, with
balance payable prior to receipt of transcript. Transcripts will be completed as staff
time is available. Fee nonrefundable. No deadline will be assured.
IF TRANSCRIBED BY OTHER THAN CLERK TO BOARD STAFF: Actual cost of services performed, including postage and delivery charges, with one
(1) paper copy of transcript and one (1) copy on diskette provided for County. Minimum
charge of $60.00, with a deposit of estimated costs figured at $4.00 per page and
forty-five (45) pages per hour, plus $20.00 for postage and handling.
COPIES OF TAPES/USE OF RECORDING EQUIPMENT:
$60.00 machine set-up fee plus $10.00/hour for use of equipment and $20.00/hour for
clerk's salary, pro-rated to half-hour segments, based on estimated time of hearing;
and $5.00 per tape. Estimated cost to be paid in advance. No unsupervised use of equipment
will be allowed. Copies of tapes will be completed as staff time is available. No
deadlines will be assured.
SENDING RECORDS BY FAX:
Cost of research of public records, as listed in Appendix 5-D , #6 plus long distance charges, as estimated by the Department of Information Technology.
SENDING RECORDS BY INTERNET E-MAIL:
Cost of research of public records, as listed in Appendix 5-D , #6.
(Weld County Code Ordinance 2001-8; Weld County Code Ordinance 2002-10; Weld County
Code Ordinance 2003-8; Weld County Code Ordinance 2005-15; Weld County Code Ordinance 2022-06 )
