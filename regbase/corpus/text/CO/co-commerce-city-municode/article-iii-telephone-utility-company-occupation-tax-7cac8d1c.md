(a)
There is hereby levied on and against each telephone utility company operating within
the city, a tax on the occupation and business of maintaining a telephone exchange
and lines connected therewith in the city and of supplying local exchange telephone
service to the inhabitants of the city. The annual amount of tax levied shall be six
dollars ($6.00) for each telephone account for which local exchange telephone service
is provided by a tax paying telephone utility company within the corporate limits
of the city on January first of the current year for which the tax is levied.
(b)
The levy may be collected by the telephone utility company from each customer receiving
telephone service within the city according to a formula authorized by the state public
utilities commission.
(Code 1969, § 191/2-1)
The tax provided for in this article is upon occupations and businesses in the performance
of local functions and is not a tax upon those functions related to interstate commerce.
None of the terms of this article shall be construed to mean that any telephone utility
company is or shall be issued a franchise by the city.
(Code 1969, § 191/2-6)
(a)
Except as provided in this article, the tax authorized in this article shall be in
lieu of all other payments by, or fees or taxes on, any telephone utility, subject
to the provisions of this article and in addition shall be in lieu of any free telephone
services furnished the city by any telephone utility.
(b)
The tax provided for in this article shall not be in lieu of ad valorem taxes, special
property assessments for local improvements or fees imposed for excavations in any
street, alley, sidewalk or other public place owned by the city.
(Code 1969, § 191/2-7)
Each year, on or before February first, each telephone utility company subject to
this article shall file with the finance director in such form as the finance director
may require, a statement showing the total telephone accounts for which local exchange
telephone service was provided within the corporate limits of the city on January
first of the current year. The statement shall be accompanied by the first monthly
installment payment of the tax for the current year as provided in section 20-60 .
(Code 1969, § 191/2-3)
Each annual tax payable pursuant to this article shall be due and payable in twelve
(12) equal monthly installments with the first installment being payable February
first of each year. Thereafter, each monthly installment shall be payable on the first
day of each consecutive month subject to determination effective January first of
each year of the total annual amount payable pursuant to this article in accordance
with the total number of telephone accounts for which local exchange telephone service
was provided within the corporate limits of the city on January first of the then
current year as shown on the statement required by section 20-59 .
(Code 1969, § 191/2-2)
The finance director or his duly authorized representative shall have the right at
all reasonable hours and times to examine the books and records of any telephone utility
company which is subject to the provisions of this article and to make copies of the
entries or contents thereof.
(Code 1969, § 191/2-5)
If any telephone utility company subject to the provisions of this article shall fail
to pay the taxes as provided in this article, including any installment thereof, the
full annual amount thereof shall be due and payable by the company and the same together
with interest thereon computed at the rate of one (1) percent per month on the unpaid
balance of taxes due plus expenses of collection incurred by the city including reasonable
attorney's fees, shall be a debt due and owing from the company to the city. The city
attorney upon the direction of the city council shall commence and prosecute to final
judgment and determination in any court of competent jurisdiction an action at law
to collect the debt of behalf of the city.
(Code 1969, § 191/2-4)
