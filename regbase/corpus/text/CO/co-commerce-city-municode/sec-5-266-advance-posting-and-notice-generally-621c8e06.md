The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Mover shall mean any person, firm or corporation who desires to move a house, building,
structure or other object.
Oversized structure shall mean and include every building, house or structure measured while loaded exceeding
eight (8) feet in overall width or thirteen and one-half (13½) feet in overall height
or sixty-five (65) feet in overall length.
This article shall apply only to those public streets over which the city exercises
exclusive jurisdiction and, therefore, any moving of oversized structures through
the city solely upon streets or highways that are a part of the state highway system
or the federal highway system are not subject to the requirements of this article.
In any case of a failure to comply with any requirement of this article, the city
or any person affected by the failure may, in addition to the penalties provided by
law, initiate a civil action for injunction, mandamus, abatement or any other appropriate
relief to prevent, enjoin, abate, remove or eliminate the violation or failure.
It shall be the duty of the city manager to direct and supervise the moving of any
oversized structure over, upon, along or across any public street in the city or from
one (1) site to another site within the city.
Time of the movement shall be designated by the city manager. Every permit to move
an oversized structure shall become and be void unless the removal shall be completed
and the oversized structure removed from the public right-of-way within the time specified
on the application for the permit. Provided, however, that the city manager may extend
the time when the moving of an oversized structure is rendered impractical by reasons
of inclemency of weather, strikes or other causes not within the control of the mover.
The city manager may prohibit parking for a period not in excess of twenty-four (24)
hours along a route designated for the moving of the oversized structure, provided
that the notice shall be given not less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the time
of moving and shall be given by posting signs stating "No Parking" between certain
hours. The city manager is hereby empowered to move any vehicle parked in violation
of the foregoing parking restriction and to charge the cost thereof to the motor vehicle
or its owner.
Before any oversized structure shall be moved, the mover shall give written notice
to the public utilities or agencies designated in the application not less than three
(3) days in advance of the proposed move.
No person moving any oversized structure over, upon, along or across any public street
shall fail, neglect or refuse to keep signal devices as required by the city manager
at all times at each corner of the oversized structure and at the end of any projection
thereon, while the same is located in or upon any public street.
The city manager shall have the authority to require a mover to provide an escort
at the mover's expense for the purpose of regulating traffic along the route the oversized
structure is being moved.
(a)
It shall be the duty of any person moving any oversized structure under the provisions
of this article to forthwith repair any damage done to any sidewalk, alley, street,
trees, utility poles, wires or other property and failure to do so shall constitute
a violation of this article.
(b)
Whenever the person moving any oversized structure under the provisions of this article
is not the owner of the vehicle making the move, the owner of the load as well as
the owner of the vehicle and the person operating the same shall be jointly and severally
liable and responsible for compliance with the requirements of this article.
It shall be unlawful for any person to move any oversized structure over, upon, along
or across any public street in the city or from one (1) site to another site within
the city without having first obtained a written permit to do so from the city.
Application for a permit as required by this division shall be furnished by the community
development director and application for a permit shall contain, or have attached
thereto, the following information:
(1)
Name and address of applicant;
(2)
Location of oversized structure to be moved;
(3)
Location or proposed site to which oversized structure is to be moved;
(4)
Date and time of proposed removal;
(5)
Map or description of route to be taken;
(6)
Height, width and length of oversized structures to be moved and truck or equipment
to be used for moving the oversized structures;
(7)
Proof of insurance in writing in such form as approved by the city manager with an
insurance company licensed to do business in the state with the following stipulations:
a.
Bodily injury liability including motor vehicle in the amount of:
1.
One hundred thousand dollars ($100,000.00), each person;
2.
Three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000.00), each accident.
b.
Property damage liability including motor vehicle in the amount of:
1.
Twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000.00), each occurrence;
2.
Fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00) aggregate.
Such proof of insurance in writing shall be specified for all operations of the mover
and for all of the mover's vehicles to be used in the course of his or her operations
in the city.
(8)
Permit bond if required by section 5-199.
(Code 1969, § 5-43; Ord. No. 1602, § 1, 11-21-05)
State Law reference— Motor Vehicle Financial Responsibility Act, C.R.S., tit. 42, art. 7.
The application fee for moving oversized structures within the City of Commerce City
shall be set by resolution of the city council and shall remain in effect until such
resolution is amended by action of the city council.
Every applicant before being granted a permit to move an oversized structure shall,
in the discretion of the city manager, furnish the city with a permit bond issued
by a surety company, conditioned upon the permittee's faithful performance of all
rules, regulations, conditions and requirements.
Each permit to move an oversized structure shall specify the date and time on which
the proposed removal may be accomplished and shall also specify the route to be taken
in connection with the removal. The mover shall have the permit in the vehicle during
the progress of the movement and shall show the permit, on demand, to any police officer
or any authorized employee of the city.
