Editor's note— Ord. No. 871, § 1, adopted May 15, 1989, repealed Art. IV, §§ 20-76—20-83, relative
to pari-mutuel wagering, and enacted similar new provisions in lieu thereof to read
as set out in §§ 20-76—20-94. Formerly, Art. IV derived from §§ 19-1—19-8 of the city's
1969 Code.
State Law reference— Racing, C.R.S., § 12-60-100.2 et seq.
The city council finds, determines and declares that except as hereafter provided
in this article the placing or making of a pari-mutuel wager as defined in this article
by any person in the city, or the making or placing of any pari-mutuel wager at a
simulcast facility as defined in this article on any race meet which takes place within
the city is the exercise of a taxable privilege and is subject to the imposition by
the city of an excise tax on each such wager pursuant to the terms and conditions
of this article, and such excise tax is hereby determined to be reasonable, proper,
uniform and nondiscriminatory and necessary for a just and proper distribution of
tax burdens imposed by the city.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89; Ord. No. 959, § 1, 1-20-92; Ord. No. 1002, § 1, 11-16-92)
The administration of the provisions of this article is hereby vested in the director
who shall prescribe forms and reasonable rules and regulations in conformity with
this article for the making of returns, for the ascertainment, assessment and collection
of the tax imposed under this article and for the proper administration and enforcement
hereof.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
The following words and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings
respectively ascribed to them:
City means the City of Commerce City, Colorado, as it now exists and as it may hereafter
be expanded by annexation of areas thereto.
City council means the elected legislative body of the city.
Director means the director of finance of the city or any duly authorized agent or representative
acting in his stead or on his behalf.
Licensee means any person or organization licensed for the conduct of a race meet pursuant
to authority of C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60, and which conducts a race meet in the city.
Pari-mutuel facility means a facility located in the city operated by a race track licensed pursuant to
C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60 on track premises licensed pursuant to C.R.S. tit. 12, art.
60 for the handling of wagers processed through, and as a part of, the pari-mutuel
system of an operating track licensed pursuant to the provisions of C.R.S. tit. 12,
art. 60, for the race wagered upon.
Pari-mutuel wager means a wager that is placed pursuant to a system of betting on races authorized
pursuant to C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60, whereby the winners divide the total amount bet,
after authorized deductions, in proportion to the sums they have wagered individually
at a pari-mutuel facility as herein defined.
Person means and includes an individual, a business entity, a partnership, a firm, a corporation,
or an association.
Program means a daily schedule of animal races conducted at a race meet in the city.
Race meet as used herein means any exhibition of animal racing where the pari-mutuel system
of wagering is used and which is authorized pursuant to the provisions of C.R.S. tit.
12, art. 60.
Simulcast facility means a facility located outside the city at which pari-mutuel wagers are placed
pursuant to the authority of C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60, on simulcast races as defined
below and which are received at such facility and processed through and as a part
of a pari-mutuel system of a pari-mutuel facility located in the city and licensed
pursuant to the provisions of C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60, for the race wagered upon.
Simulcast race means a live, audio-visual broadcast which is received by a simulcast facility and
which is transmitted simultaneously with the performance of a live meet at a race
track which is licensed pursuant to the provisions of C.R.S. tit. 12, art. 60, and
which is located within the city.
Taxpayer means any person who places or makes a pari-mutuel wager which is subject to the
imposition of the pari-mutuel wagering tax imposed by this article.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89; Ord. No. 959, § 2, 1-20-92)
There is hereby levied by the city, except as hereafter provided, an excise tax on
the placing or making by any person of a pari-mutuel wager in the city on any race
meet which takes place within the city and on the placing or making by any person
of any pari-mutuel wager at a simulcast facility on any race meet which takes place
within the city except for any such pari-mutuel wager placed or made at a location
outside the State of Colorado. The excise tax levy hereby imposed is one cent ($0.01)
for each ninety-nine cents ($0.99) expended by a taxpayer for any pari-mutuel wager
taxable hereunder.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89; Ord. No. 959, § 3, 1-20-92; Ord. No. 1002, § 2, 11-16-92)
Each licensee which has been granted a license pursuant to the provisions of C.R.S.
tit. 12, art. 60, to conduct a race meet and who is thereby authorized to conduct
a race meet in the city is responsible for collecting, accounting for and remitting
to the city the excise tax imposed by this article on all pari-mutuel wagers made
on its premises and at simulcast facilities on any race meet which takes place on
its premises except for any such pari-mutuel wager placed or made at a location outside
the State of Colorado. The licensee shall pay to the city, within ten (10) days after
collection, the tax collected each day pursuant to the provisions of this article.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89; Ord. No. 959, § 4, 1-20-92; Ord. No. 981, § 1, 8-3-92;
Ord. No. 1002, § 3, 11-16-92)
The tax levied pursuant to this article is imposed for revenue purposes for the support
of the government of the city and is in addition to all other taxes, excises and licenses
levied and imposed under any other ordinances of the city or any other governmental
entity.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
It shall be the duty of the licensee to keep and preserve suitable records and other
such books or accounts as may be necessary to determine the amount of tax payable
pursuant to this article. Such books and records shall be open for examination at
any reasonable time by the director.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
If any licensee hereunder fails to remit the tax required under this article at the
time prescribed, interest on such unpaid amount at the rate of twelve (12) percent
per annum shall be paid for the period from the date due to the date paid.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
As soon as practicable after a tax return is filed under this article, the director
shall examine it and shall determine the correct amount of tax. If the tax found due
shall be greater than the amount theretofore paid, a notice of deficiency shall be
mailed to the licensee.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
The licensee or any taxpayer may request a hearing on the issue of the amount or the
validity of the tax paid by application to the director, within thirty (30) days of
the mailing of the notice of deficiency in the case of the licensee and within thirty
(30) days after payment of the tax in the case of the taxpayer. The request for hearing
shall set forth the reasons for and the amount of the requested changes in the tax
levy imposed. If no timely request for a hearing is made as herein provided, the tax
levy of the city shall be deemed consented to by the licensee and the taxpayer, and
any claim against imposition of such tax levy shall be forever barred.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
For the purpose of ascertaining the correctness of a tax return or for the purpose
of determining the amount of tax due or the validity of a tax paid, the director may
conduct investigations, and upon request for a hearing made pursuant to section 20-85 , the director shall hold hearings concerning any matters covered by this article
and may examine any relevant books, papers, records or memoranda and may require the
attendance of witnesses and may take testimony and require proof for such purposes.
The director shall have power to administer oaths to such witnesses.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
Every hearing before the director under this article shall be held in the city.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
All subpoenas issued pursuant to this article may be served by any person over twenty-one
(21) years of age. The fees of witnesses for attendance at hearings shall be the same
as the fees of witnesses before the municipal court, such fees to be paid when the
witness is excused from further attendance. When the witness is subpoenaed at the
instance of the director, such fees shall be paid by the city. When a witness is subpoenaed
at the instance of any other party to any such proceeding, the director may require
that the cost of service of the subpoena and the fee of the witness be paid by the
party at whose instance the witness is summoned. In such case, the director, in the
director's discretion, may require a deposit to cover the cost of such service and
witness fees. A subpoena issued as aforesaid shall be served in the same manner as
a subpoena issued from the municipal court.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
The judge of the municipal court, upon the application of the director, may compel
the attendance of witnesses, the production of books, papers, records or memoranda
and the giving of testimony before the director in the same manner as production of
evidence may be compelled before the municipal court.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
Every decision of the director shall be in writing, and notice thereof shall be mailed
to the party who requested the hearing. Each decision shall become final upon the
expiration of thirty (30) days after notice of such decision shall have been mailed
by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the person who requested the hearing
at the address shown on the request for the hearing, unless legal proceedings are
timely begun for judicial review of such decision pursuant to Rule 106(a)(4) of the
Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
(a)
Failure of the licensee to collect and remit the tax imposed by this article to the
city shall create a debt owed by the licensee to the city.
(b)
In case of failure of the licensee to collect the taxes or any portion thereof imposed
by this article and to remit the same along with any charge or interest thereon when
due, the director may recover from the licensee in an action at law the amount of
such unpaid taxes, charges, interest and reasonable attorney's fees incurred by the
city for the collection thereof in any court of competent jurisdiction.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
The director is hereby authorized to waive, for good cause shown, any charge assessed
over and above the tax liability herein imposed, except that the director may only
waive any interest imposed in excess of six (6) percent per annum on the unpaid tax
liability.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
In addition to other remedies provided for herein, the city may initiate a civil action
for mandatory injunction, mandamus or for any other appropriate relief to require
compliance with the provisions of this article.
(Ord. No. 871, § 1, 5-15-89)
