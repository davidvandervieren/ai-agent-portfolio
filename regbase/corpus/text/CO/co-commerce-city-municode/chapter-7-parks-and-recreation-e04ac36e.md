[The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this chapter, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:]
Department means the Department of Parks, Recreation and Golf.
Motorized vehicle means and includes any of the following as the same are defined by the model traffic
code as adopted by the city:
(1)
Motorcycle;
(2)
Motor-driven cycle;
(3)
Motor home;
(4)
Motor vehicle;
(5)
Motor scooter or motor bicycle;
(6)
Motorized bicycle;
(7)
Motorized device.
Park property means any and all public recreation lands, waters or facilities owned, leased or
operated by the city, including, but not limited to, all city parks, trails and open
space, all city recreation facilities and the Buffalo Run Golf Course.
Park trees means any trees, shrubs, bushes, hedges or other woody vegetation in or on any park
property and any other area owned by the city or to which the public has free access,
other than within a street right-of-way unless such trees are maintained by the department.
The provisions of this chapter shall apply to, within and upon all park property;
provided, however, that the provisions of this chapter shall not apply to employees
or agents of the city engaged in the performance of city business.
The following activities are prohibited on all park property except where expressly
permitted by public posting or written authorization of the city:
(1)
Swimming;
(2)
Boating;
(3)
Ice skating;
(4)
Ice hockey;
(5)
Sledding;
(6)
Fishing; and
(7)
Playing or practicing golf except within the boundaries of the Buffalo Run Golf Course
in accordance with golf course rules, regulations and policies.
(a)
It is unlawful for any unauthorized person to operate any motorized vehicle or ATV
on or within any park property except as expressly provided in section 11-2100 of this Code.
(b)
It is unlawful to park any trailer or motorized vehicle or ATV on park property, except
where specifically authorized by the city, or in such a manner as to create a hazard
to natural resources or vehicular, pedestrian, equestrian or bicycle traffic or to
obstruct emergency vehicle/equipment access.
(c)
It is unlawful to wash, service or repair any vehicle within any park property without
prior authorization by the city.
(d)
Except for the vehicles of government employees engaged in official business or when
specifically authorized by the city, it is unlawful to park any motorized vehicle
or trailer, whether attended or unattended: (1) when the owner or operator is not
using the park property; (2) on park property between 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m.; or
(3) where posted, on the portion of any public street abutting any park property between
11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m. Any vehicle in violation of this subsection shall be deemed
abandoned and subject to immediate towing, impoundment, or relocation. The violation
of this subsection is a civil infraction.
(e)
Authority to designate areas.
(1)
The city manager is hereby authorized to designate any areas within park property
for the operation and parking of motorized vehicles and ATVs and to close or restrict
access to such areas.
(2)
The areas in which the operation and parking of motorized vehicles or ATVs is authorized
shall be clearly marked with signs giving notice that such operation or parking is
permitted.
(f)
Minimum fine. Any violation of subsection (a) resulting from the use of an ATV as
defined by section 11-2100 of this Code, or a motorcycle as defined by the Model Traffic Code, shall result
in a minimum fine of one thousand dollars ($1000.00).
( Ord. No. 2293 , § 2(Exh. A), 9-21-20; Ord. No. 2380, § 2(Exh. B), 3-21-22)
(a)
It is unlawful for any person to enter into or trespass upon any enclosed areas of
a park property, including, but not limited to, fenced swimming pools, tennis courts,
ball fields, city trails, the Buffalo Run Golf Course and any related buildings, whenever
such enclosed areas are closed to the public by locked gates, doors or barricades
or where any park property, including ball fields, trails or the Buffalo Run Golf
Course, is posted with a sign restricting the use of such area.
(b)
Except for government employees engaged in official business or when specifically
authorized by the city, it is unlawful for any person to be present within any park
property, including any streets, parking areas, or roadways located therein between
the hours of 11:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m.
(c)
Additional restrictions—Buffalo Run Golf Course.
(1)
It is unlawful for any person to enter or remain within the boundaries of the Buffalo
Run Golf Course when the golf course is open to the public unless upon arrival such
person registers with staff at the golf course pro shop and such person adheres to
the golf course rules, regulations and policies.
(2)
It is unlawful for any person to enter or remain within the boundaries of the Buffalo
Run Golf Course when the golf course is closed to the public.
( Ord. No. 2293 , § 2(Exh. A), 9-21-20)
It is unlawful for any person to possess any glass container within the boundaries
of any park property.
It is unlawful for any person to deposit on any park property, including within designated
waste receptacles, any rubbish, waste material, refuse, garbage, trash, debris or
other foreign substance unless such rubbish, waste material, refuse, garbage, trash,
debris or other foreign substance is incidental to such person's legitimate use of
the park property. "Legitimate use" means any permitted use or activity engaged in
within or on the park property.
It is unlawful to do or attempt any of the following within any park property:
(1)
Build, start or light any fire of any nature except in outdoor fireplace/picnic grills
provided by the city;
(2)
Burn any material in a careless manner;
(3)
Leave any fire unattended;
(4)
Fail to comply with any existing fire ban;
(5)
Fail to completely extinguish any fire; or
(6)
Dispose of in any waste receptacle any hot, burning or smoldering coals, brands, kindling
or other combustible material.
(a)
It is unlawful to possess fireworks, explosives, firearms, BB guns, pellet guns, air
guns, cross bows, long bows or slingshots while upon any park property except in areas
specifically authorized in writing by the city; provided, however, that persons possessing
a valid permit or license to carry a concealed firearm, which license or permit was
issued pursuant to applicable state or federal law, shall be allowed to possess a
firearm, only in accordance with such permit, while upon park property.
(b)
It is unlawful to discharge or use fireworks, explosives, firearms, BB guns, pellet
guns, air guns, cross bows, long bows or sling shots while upon any park property
except in areas specifically authorized in writing by the city.
(c)
It is unlawful to throw, discharge or launch any type of missile while upon any park
property except as expressly authorized by the city.
It is unlawful within any park property to hunt, trap, catch, molest, harm, kill or
chase any wild animal, or to molest the habitat thereof, except as expressly authorized
by the city.
(a)
It is unlawful to camp overnight, or to park a trailer or camper for overnight camping
purposes, on any park property without the prior authorization of the city.
(b)
It is unlawful to camp, or to park a trailer or camper, in any designated area for
a period of time in excess of the posted or permitted limits without the prior authorization
of the city.
(c)
It is unlawful for any person to erect any type of tent or other shelter or structure
on any park property without the prior authorization of the city.
It is unlawful to conduct any commercial activity, or provide any service, product
or activity for which a fee is charged, on any park property without the prior written
authorization of the city.
(a)
Except within a designated dog park, pet animals shall be restrained while upon any
park property in accordance with the requirements of chapter 4 of this Code.
(b)
An owner or person having control of any pet animal shall not permit such pet animal
to defecate on or within any park property unless such defecation is immediately removed.
(c)
It is unlawful to allow pet animals to harm, kill, chase or otherwise harass any animal
or park user on any park property.
(d)
It is unlawful to relocate or release animals, fish, birds or insects onto any park
property without the written permission of the city.
(a)
It is unlawful for any person, other than duly authorized city personnel, to plant
or transplant, or to allow the planting or transplanting of, trees or any other vegetative
material within any park property.
(b)
It is unlawful for any person, other than duly authorized city personnel, to top,
trim, prune or in any way cut any park trees without first obtaining written authorization
from the city.
(c)
It is unlawful for any person to intentionally damage park trees by any means or to
attach ropes, wire, nails, advertising posters or other contrivance to park trees
without first obtaining written authorization from the city.
(d)
It is unlawful for any person to allow any gaseous liquid or solid substance that
is harmful to park trees to come in contact with them or to set fire to, or permit
any fire to burn when such fire or the heat thereof will injure, any portion of any
park trees.
(a)
General prohibition. Except as provided in paragraph (b), it is unlawful for any person to smoke in or
on park property.
(b)
Exempt areas. The following areas shall be exempt from the prohibition contained in paragraph (a)
of this section unless specifically posted otherwise at the site:
(1)
The Buffalo Run Golf Course; and
(2)
Any areas of park property that are designed and intended for the parking of automobiles.
(Ord. No. 1851, § 1, 4-4-11)
