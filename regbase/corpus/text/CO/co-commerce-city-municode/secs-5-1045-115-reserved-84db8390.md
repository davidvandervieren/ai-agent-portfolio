The building official may accept reports from an approved person or agency that the
construction documents have been examined and conform to the requirements of the City
of Commerce City Building Code (C3BC) and may establish requirements for the person
or agency submitting such reports in accordance with section 5-103 of this Code. In addition, where such reports have been submitted, the building official
may expedite permit processing and the building/life-safety review.
(Ord. No. 2494 , § 2(Exh. A), 5-15-23)
The building official may accept reports from an approved person or agency that the
construction has been inspected and conforms to the requirements of the C3BC and may
establish requirements for the person or agency submitted such reports in accordance
with section 5-103 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 2494 , § 2(Exh. A), 5-15-23)
The building official shall have a written policy establishing the minimum acceptable
qualifications for third-party plans examiners and inspectors. The policy shall include
the format and timeframe required for submission of reports, prequalification or preapproval
requirements before conducting third-party services and any other requirements and
procedures established by the building official.
In determining third-party plans examiner or inspector qualifications, the building
official may consider such items as national certifications, state professional registrations,
state licensure, related experience, education and any other factor that would demonstrate
competency and reliability to conduct third-party services.
(Ord. No. 2494 , § 2(Exh. A), 5-15-23)
