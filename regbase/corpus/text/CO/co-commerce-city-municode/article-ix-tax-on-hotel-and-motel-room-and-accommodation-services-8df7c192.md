The purpose of this article is to impose a four (4) percent tax on the price paid
for short-term hotel and motel room and accommodations as herein defined.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
Unless the context clearly indicates otherwise, the following words and phrases as
used in this article shall have the following meaning:
(a)
Gross sales means the total amount received in money, credit, property or other consideration
valued in money for all hotel and motel rooms or accommodations.
(b)
Hotel and motel room or accommodation services means the provision of a regular sleeping room or unit, a meeting room, display room,
banquet room or other room for which a charge is made by a vendor as herein defined.
(c)
Price means the aggregate value in money or any thing or things paid or delivered or promised
to be paid or delivered for hotel or motel rooms or accommodations.
(d)
Purchase or sale means the acquisition or disposition for a price of the right to use a hotel or motel
room or accommodation.
(e)
Return means the hotel or motel room and accommodations tax reporting form used to report
the hotel and motel room and accommodations tax.
(f)
Tax means the hotel and motel room and accommodations tax imposed by this article.
(g)
Vendor means the owner or operator of a hotel, motel, lodging house, inn, guest house, hostel
or bed and breakfast that provides hotel or motel room or accommodation services as
herein defined for a consideration.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
Except for exempt transactions, on and after the first day of January, 2008, there
is levied on hotel and motel room and accommodation services as herein defined and
shall be paid and collected an excise tax of four (4) percent on the price paid by
any person or charged by any vendor for the leasing, rental or providing for a consideration
hotel or motel room or accommodation services in the City of Commerce City. This tax
shall be in addition to sales and use tax as established pursuant to article V of chapter 20 of this code.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
The following transactions shall be exempt from the hotel and motel room and accommodation
services tax imposed under this article:
(a)
All sales made directly to charitable organizations, in the conduct of their regular
religious, charitable functions and activities, provided that such sales are paid
for directly to the vendor by draft or warrant drawn on the funds of the exempt organization;
(b)
All direct sales to the United States of America, to the State of Colorado, its departments,
institutions or political subdivisions, which are acting in their governmental capacity,
and to all sales to the city or its departments, provided that such sales are paid
for directly to the vendor by draft or warrant drawn on the funds of that government
entity;
(c)
All sales to any occupant who is a resident of any hotel, motel, lodging house, inn,
guest house, hostel, bed and breakfast or any other place which provides sleeping
rooms or facilities and who enters into or has entered into an agreement and has subsequently
paid for occupancy of a room or rooms or accommodations for a period of at least thirty
(30) consecutive days. The exemption hereby provided for shall be allowed so long
as the rental is for a period of at least thirty (30) days even though payment of
the rent is made in increments for less than thirty (30) days;
(d)
All hotel and motel rooms or accommodations provided to persons that the city is prohibited
from taxing under the Constitution or laws of the United States or the State of Colorado.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
(a)
There is hereby created an account known as the "Hotel and Motel Room and Accommodation
Services Tax Account" for the deposit of funds collected pursuant to this article
and to be used according to this section.
(b)
All revenues from the hotel and motel room and accommodation services tax collected
pursuant to this article, plus all interest earned on said account, shall be placed
in the room and accommodations tax account and used by the city for economic development,
marketing and parks and recreation. No part of such revenue and accrued interest shall
be allocated for any purposes other than for the purposes herein provided.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
It shall be unlawful for any person to engage in the business of providing hotel and
motel room or accommodation services without first having obtained a Commerce City
business license, which license shall be granted and issued by the city, upon an application
form provided by the city and shall be in force and effect until revoked.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
Every vendor or provider of hotel and motel room or accommodation services shall be
liable and responsible for the payment of an amount equal to four (4) percent of all
proceeds derived from the providing of hotel and motel room or accommodation services
as defined in this article.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
The hotel and motel room and accommodation services tax shall be subject to collection
by the city and administered in the same manner as the sales and use tax as provided
in article V of chapter 20 of this code including assessment of interest and penalties for nonpayment.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
It shall be a violation of this article for any vendor to refuse to make any return
required by this article, or to make any false or fraudulent return, or any false
statement in any such return, or to fail or refuse to make payment to the director
of finance of any taxes collected or due the city pursuant to this article, or in
any manner to evade the collection and payment of any tax required by this article,
or any part thereof, or for any person or purchaser to fail or refuse to pay such
tax or evade the payment thereof, or to aid or abet another in any attempt to evade
the payment of the tax imposed by this article.
(Ord. No. 1710, § 1, 12-17-07)
