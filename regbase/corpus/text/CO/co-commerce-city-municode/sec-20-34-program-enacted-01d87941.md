The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this division, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Dwelling and dwelling unit shall mean any residence, abode, house trailer, mobile home, room, habitation or
apartment in which the applicant for a refund pursuant to this division permanently
resides. The terms shall be deemed to include, but not be limited to a single-family
or multifamily house, an apartment, condominium, rented room, house trailer, mobile
home and a nursing home residence.
Household shall mean one (1) or more persons who live under one (1) roof and who pay property
tax or rent on the dwelling unit in which the members of the household live. Any person
who pays property taxes or rent on a dwelling unit shall not be considered to be a
part of any other household.
Owned shall mean possessed of legal incidents of ownership of a dwelling or dwelling unit
within the city including contractual rights to ownership, irrespective of whether
the name of the applicant for a refund pursuant to this division appears on the recorded
deed or title to the dwelling or dwelling unit.
Senior citizen shall mean any person sixty-two (62) years of age or older as of the date application
is made for a tax refund pursuant to this division.
Tax year shall mean the calendar year immediately preceding the calendar year in which an
application for a refund pursuant to this division is made.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84; Ord. No. 1306, § 1, 10-18-99)
No person applying for benefits under this division shall make any false statement
or submit any false information either knowingly or with a careless disregard for
the truth of the statement made or information submitted.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84)
The finance director, subject to approval by the city manager, may promulgate such
rules and regulations as he deems appropriate for the administration of this division,
and the rules and regulations may, among other things, impose reasonable requirements
upon applicants for refunds pursuant to this division regarding proof of eligibility.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84)
There is hereby enacted a city tax refund program for the senior citizens of the city.
It if the purpose of this program to refund taxes to the senior citizens of this city
as provided in this division.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84)
The following conditions must exist in order for any person to be eligible for a tax
refund pursuant to this division:
(1)
Either the person applying for the refund, or his spouse if they live together, must
be sixty-two (62) years of age or older as of the date application for a tax refund
is made; and
(2)
During some part of the tax year, the applicant must have lived as a resident of the
city in a dwelling or dwelling unit located in the city, and because of his or her
ownership of all or part of the dwelling or dwelling unit or because of an enforceable
contractual obligation, the applicant must have been obligated to pay all or a part
of the taxes thereon for the tax year, and the applicant in fact must have paid all
of the taxes which he or she was legally obligated to pay thereon.
(3)
As an alternative to the requirements of subsection (2) of this section, during some
part of the tax year the applicant must have lived as a resident of the city in a
dwelling or dwelling unit located in the city, and must have been legally obligated
to pay rent with respect to the dwelling or dwelling unit and in fact must have paid
the rent.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84; Ord. No. 1105, § 1, 6-20-94; Ord. No. 1306, § 2, 10-18-99)
Every person desiring a city tax refund must file in the office of the finance director
a written application on a form to be provided by the city. The application must be
filed between January 1 and November 1 of the calendar year in which the taxes sought
to be refunded or partially refunded, as applicable, were paid. The application form
may require any and all information reasonably necessary for the administration of
this division and shall contain an affidavit by the applicant certifying to the qualifications
for the refund entitlement.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84; Ord. No. 1105, § 1, 6-20-94)
(a)
If an application has been filed in proper form showing compliance with all of the
conditions contained in this division, the city shall refund to the applicant an amount
equal to the total of the amounts refundable pursuant to subsections (b) and (c) of
this section.
(b)
For an entitled owner or owners of a dwelling or dwelling unit in the city, including
an owner of a mobile home or house trailer, a refund shall be allowed for each qualifying
month or portion thereof that the applicant verifies that property tax was paid. A
rebate of one hundred (100) percent of the tax paid shall be provided where the applicant
provides proof to the city of the amount of the tax paid. The refund shall not be
less than three dollars ($3.00) per month for each qualifying month.
(c)
For an entitled tenant of a dwelling or dwelling unit in the city, a refund shall
be allowed at the rate of two dollars and fifty cents ($2.50) for each month or portion
thereof during the tax year that the applicant qualified as a tenant of a dwelling
or dwelling unit pursuant to this division.
(d)
If more than one (1) person qualifies as an owner or as a tenant pursuant to this
division for one (1) dwelling unit, the refund provided for in this division shall
be equally divided between the qualified applicants for each dwelling unit or in the
discretion of the director of finance, paid jointly to such qualified applicants.
(Ord. No. 279, § 1, 12-3-84; Ord. No. 1105, § 4, 5-1-95; Ord. No. 1306, § 3, 10-18-99;
Ord. No. 1501, § 1, 8-18-03)
City tax refunds shall be due and payable after November 15 as determined by the director
of finance in the calendar year in which application is timely made.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84; Ord. No. 1105, § 5, 5-1-95)
The city shall make tax refunds pursuant to this division only directly to the applicant
who qualifies therefor and refunds shall not be paid to anyone other than the applicant
himself. If an applicant dies prior to receiving or cashing a refund pursuant to this
division, any check representing the refund shall be null and void and the refund
shall not become an asset of the decedent's estate. If, however, the decedent is survived
by a spouse who would be qualified as an applicant for the refund, the city will honor
an application for a refund by the surviving spouse, even though the application is
not timely made in accordance with the provisions of this division.
(Ord. No. 729, § 1, 12-3-84)
