Well Permitting
The Division of Water Resources (DWR) has several types of well permit application forms to assist with obtaining a permit to construct a new or replacement well, or to register an existing well. Additionally, for some Groundwater Basins and River Basins, special Use and Measurement Rules apply. These rules are available under Services, Flow Measurement subpages in the top navigation.

## Apply for a New Well Permit
Guides to assist you with applying for a new well permit can be found under the Important Links Section. To learn more about different application types and the process, you may review the Beginner's Guide to Well Permits. The Form Submittal, Payment Options and Fee Schedule gives additional guidance on how DWR accepts application forms and payments.
The first step is to complete the appropriate application and pay the associated fee. Visit the Applications eForms Dashboard link on this page for all well permitting forms, or use the buttons below for Residential or Non-Residential Forms. The Division of Water Resources accepts applications by email, and associated fees can be paid online. Once your emailed application has been received, you will receive an email back with instructions on paying the application fees.

## Change or Update Owner on a Well Permit
To change the name of the owner(s) or update contact information for an existing owner, submit a “Change of Owner Name/Contact Information” form to this office using the links below. A Change of Owner Name/Contact Information form can only be filed for a valid permit. A Change of Owner Name/Contact Information form is for contact information purposes only, and does not convey real property. In many cases, the title company will file the Title Companies - Change of Owner Name/Contact Information form in connection with a real estate transaction. However, if updates are needed after closing or if the title company did not submit the form, the well owner, their attorney, or agent may file the Change of Owner Name/Contact Information (for use by the well owner or the well owner’s attorney/authorized agent). Once the form is submitted, all contacts will receive an email confirmation that it was received. Please allow several weeks for the well permit contact information to be updated. A second confirmation email will be sent once the request is processed. Submitting duplicate requests causes delays.

## Permit Processing and Requests for Emergency Well Permit Approval
DWR reviews new and replacement well permit applications in the order they are received, and review of complete applications may take up to 49 days. DWR will act upon emergency requests for well permit approval for some replacement well situations, some location changes, and some changes in source/aquifer. See the document, Emergency Well Permit Procedures below for additional information.
