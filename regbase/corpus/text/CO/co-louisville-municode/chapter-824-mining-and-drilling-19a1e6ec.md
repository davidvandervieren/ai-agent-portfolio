It is unlawful for any person, company or corporation to mine, or cause to mine for
any coal or other mineral or gravel, or a combination thereof, within the city limits
of the city. It is also unlawful for any person, company or corporation to drill or
cause to drill for oil or gas, or a combination thereof, within the city limits unless
such person, company or corporation shall have been specifically approved for a special
review use under chapter 17.68 of this Code and unless all other applicable ordinances of the city are met.
(Code 1977, § 8.24.010; Ord. No. 753-1982, § 3; Ord. No. 1193-1995, § 1)
In addition to the fines and penalties provided in section 1.28.010 , the city council, the city attorney, or any owner of real estate within the city
may institute injunction, mandamus, abatement pursuant to chapter 8.01 , or any other appropriate action or proceeding to prevent, enjoin, or abate mining
or drilling as described in section 8.24.010 .
(Code 1962, § 29.31(b); Ord. No. 363-1968, § 1; Code 1977, § 8.24.020; Ord. No. 753-1982,
§ 3; Ord. No. 1707-2015, § 8, 11-20-2015)
