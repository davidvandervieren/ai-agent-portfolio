Cross reference— Uniform Fire Code, ch. 15.12.
State Law reference— Fireworks, C.R.S. § 12-28-101.
Definitions as used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:
Fireworks means any article, device or substance prepared for the primary purpose of producing
a visual or auditory sensation by combustion, explosion, or detonation. "Fireworks"
does not include:
1.
Toy caps;
2.
Sparklers, trick matches, cigarette loads, trick noise makers, toy smoke devices and
novelty auto alarms;
3.
Highway flares, railway fuses, smoke candles and other emergency signal devices.
Retailer means any person who sells, delivers, cosigns or furnishes fireworks to another person
not for resale.
(Code 1977, § 8.44.010; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
It is unlawful for any person to offer for sale, to expose for sale, sell or have
in his possession with intent to offer for sale, to sell, to use or to explode any
fireworks within the city.
(Code 1977, § 8.44.020; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
A.
The city council may grant permits for supervised public displays of fireworks to
any civic or nonprofit organization or group upon receipt of written application.
Application for permit shall be made with the city manager at least 20 days in advance
of the date of display. Each application shall establish the date of the display,
the proposed location and the designated operators to be involved with the display.
B.
No permit shall be transferable or assignable.
(Code 1977, § 8.44.030; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
The police shall seize, take and remove, at the expense of the owner, all stocks of
fireworks, or combustibles offered or exposed for sale, stored or held in violation
of this chapter.
(Code 1977, § 8.44.040; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
