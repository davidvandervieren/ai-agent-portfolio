There is created and established a water department of the city for the purpose of
the management, maintenance, care, and operation of the waterworks of the city.
(Code 1962, § 13-1-1; Ord. No. 377-1970, § 1; Code 1977, § 13.04.010)
A.
The city manager shall appoint the public works director of the city or another qualified
person to act as superintendent of the water works.
B.
The superintendent shall have the immediate control and management of all things pertaining
to the city waterworks system, and he shall perform all acts necessary for the prudent,
efficient, and economical management and protection of such waterworks, subject to
the approval of the city manager.
C.
The city council shall have the power, by motion, to prescribe such other and further
rules and regulations governing the power and duties of the superintendent and such
other rates, rules and regulations as are not contained in chapters 13.04 , 13.08 , and 13.12 .
(Code 1962, § 13-1-2; Ord. No. 377-1970, § 1; Code 1977, § 13.04.020; Ord. No. 914-1986,
§§ 1, 2)
A.
Application for the use of water shall be made to the city at the designated department.
The application must be made by the owner or agent of the owner for the property to
be benefitted and must designate the location of the property and state the purpose
for which the water is required.
B.
In the furnishing of water for any purpose other than domestic purposes, the city
shall not be responsible for the quality or quantity of the water furnished.
(Code 1962, § 13-1-5; Ord. No. 377-1970, § 1; Code 1977, § 13.04.030; Ord. No. 914-1986,
§§ 1, 2)
Whenever in his judgment he deems it necessary, the superintendent may inspect the
premises or buildings of any water consumer for the purpose of examining the condition
of all pipes, motors, meters and water fixtures, or the manner in which the water
is used. The superintendent shall be vigilant to protect and remedy all abuses, whether
from waste or other improper use of water.
(Code 1962, § 13-1-4; Ord. No. 377-1970, § 1; Code 1977, § 13.04.040)
A.
It shall be unlawful for any person to use or take water from the city waterworks
without a permit therefor; or to make any fraudulent representation for the purpose
of obtaining water; or to take or use water from the waterworks for a purpose or purposes
other than as provided for in the customer's permit; or to wilfully or unreasonably
waste water; or to violate any of the regulations set forth in this chapter or chapters 13.08 and 13.12 .
B.
Any person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter, or chapter 13.08 or 13.12 shall be guilty of a violation of such provisions, and shall be subject to the penalty
provided in section 1.28.010 .
(Code 1962, § 13-1-28; Code 1977, § 13.04.100; Ord. No. 514-1977, § 4)
In all new construction and in any repair and/or replacement of fixtures, only fixtures
and trim not exceeding the following flow rates and/or water usage shall be installed.
(Rates are based on a pressure at the fixture of 40 to 50 psi.)
A.
Water closets, maximum 3½ gallons per flush;
B.
Water closets flushometer type, maximum three gallons per flush;
C.
Shower heads, maximum flow of three gallons per minute;
D.
Sink and faucets, maximum flow of three gallons per minute;
E.
It shall also be required in all new construction and repair or replacement of fixtures,
that a pressure reducing valve shall be installed on the incoming service to such
structure. The pressure reducing valve must provide adjustment of the pressure for
the household service to within the range of 40 to 50 psi.
(Code 1977, § 13.04.110; Ord. No. 842-1984, § 1)
