For the purposes of this title, the following definitions shall apply:
Infestation. A parcel of property is infested if one square yard of area contains 25 or more pests
at any one time.
Insect pests means any of the small invertebrate animals in the phylum Arthropoda which are harmful
to plants and animals.
Pests means insect pest and animal pests, except rodents, jackrabbits and predatory animals.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.010; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1)
No property owner, agent or occupant shall allow his property to become infested by
insect pests, or to maintain any property in any manner which allows pests to freely
breed. It shall be the duty of the property owner to control insect pests and the
breeding of insect pests upon all parcels of real property within the city.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.020; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1)
A.
Any person may file a written complaint with the city concerning any infestation or
concerning a pest breeding area. All such complaints shall include the address or
location of the infested property or breeding area.
B.
Upon the filing of a complaint, or upon the action of the city pest inspector, the
property owner, agent or tenant shall receive written notice by certified mail of
such a complaint from the city, notifying such owner, agent, or tenant that appropriate
treatment must be undertaken within ten days from the date of the notice to eliminate
the infestation or breeding area. The notice shall further state that if the problem
area is not treated within the specified time period, the city shall take the necessary
actions to treat the area.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.030; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1)
Infested areas or pest breeding areas shall be treated by one or more of the methods
as shall be approved by the city. Such methods shall include mowing, controlled burns
and/or the application of approved insecticides, herbicides, vegetation killers or
baiting.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.040; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1)
In the event that an infestation or infection of pests is treated at the direction
of the city, the city shall assess the costs and expenses thereof, plus an additional
administrative fee against the lots or tracts of land which were treated. The amount
of the administrative fee shall be as established by resolution of the city council.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.050; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1; Ord. No. 1594-2011, § 20, 6-7-2011)
In the event an owner, agent or occupant, within 30 days after billing for pest treatment
by the city as provided for in section 8.40.050 , fails to pay the costs and expenses of such pest treatment, a lien shall be assessed
against the property for such pest treatment by the city clerk. The city clerk shall
certify such assessment to the treasurer of the county, who shall extend the assessment
upon the tax roll and collect the assessment in the same manner as other taxes upon
such property.
(Code 1977, § 8.40.060; Ord. No. 716-1981, § 1)
