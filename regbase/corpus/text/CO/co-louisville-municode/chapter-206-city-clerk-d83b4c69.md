The city manager shall appoint a qualified person to serve as the city clerk.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
A.
The city clerk shall perform the responsibilities provided for in the Charter, the
city's ordinances, and other applicable laws, and other duties as directed by the
city manager.
B.
The city clerk shall have custody of all the laws, ordinances, and resolutions of
the city, and shall keep regular and correct records of the proceedings of the city
council. The city clerk shall record all ordinances and resolutions passed by the
city council in books provided for such purpose, and shall publish all ordinances
and other matters requiring publication and keep proofs thereof.
C.
The city clerk shall keep on file all contracts and leases to which the city is a
party, all deeds and other instruments relating to city-owned real estate, and other
records of the council. The city clerk shall have custody of the seal of the City
and shall affix the seal to all instruments as may be required. The city clerk shall
perform such other duties as may be required by the Charter or this Code.
D.
The city clerk shall furnish to any officer of the city or to any department head
such information as such officer or department head may require in the proper conduct
of his or her office. All books and records of the city clerk shall be open to inspection
by an officer of the city at all times and such books and records shall be open to
the inspection of the public at all reasonable times during office hours.
E.
The city clerk shall furnish on demand certified copies of any record, ordinance or
other proceeding on file in the clerk's office upon payment of such fees as are established
by the city manager or the city clerk as the manager's designee. All such fees are
to be deposited in the city treasury and to become the property of the city.
F.
The city clerk may appoint one or more deputy city clerks as are necessary.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
A.
The city clerk shall have charge of all activities and duties required pursuant to
the Charter and any ordinance relating to the conduct of city elections. In any case
where election procedure is in doubt, the city clerk shall prescribe the procedure
to be followed.
B.
No person shall dictate, prevent, or interfere with the city clerk's responsibilities
as an independent and neutral election official.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
