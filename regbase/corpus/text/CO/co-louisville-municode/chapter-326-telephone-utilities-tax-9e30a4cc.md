Editor's note— Ord. No. 1500, § 1, adopted Sept. 19, 2006, repealed the former ch. 3.26, §§ 3.26.010—3.26.070,
and enacted a new ch. 3.26 as set out herein. The former ch. 3.26 pertained to similar
subject matter and derived from Code 1977, §§ 3.26.010—3.26.070; Ord. No. 624-1978,
§ 1; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2.
Cross reference— Emergency telephone charge, ch. 3.30.
State Law reference— Telephone charge, C.R.S. § 29-11-101 et seq.
The city council does hereby find, determine and declare as follows:
A.
The operations of telephone utility companies involve substantial use of the public
streets and rights-of-way, frequent excavations in the public streets and rights-of-way,
and the regular installation, maintenance and repair of many poles, lines and cables
in, under, and above the public streets and rights-of-way.
B.
The operations of telephone utility companies place a substantial burden on the city
in its efforts to provide for the public safety, and efficiently maintain and administer
the public streets and rights-of-way.
C.
Considering the nature of telephone utility companies and the burdens placed by such
telephone utility companies on the city, the classification of such utilities separately
from other businesses and occupations is reasonable and nondiscriminatory.
D.
The City Council of the City of Louisville is authorized to impose taxes on the privilege
of engaging in occupations or businesses solely for the purpose of raising revenue.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
As used in this chapter:
"Account" means a business transaction between provider and inhabitant whereby provider provides
to inhabitant basic local exchange service to a single address within the city.
"Basic local exchange service" means "basic local exchange service" authorized by a certificate of public convenience
and necessity, or otherwise, under Title 40, Article 15, Colorado Revised Statutes,
as amended or recodified from time to time.
"Base count" means the total number of accounts for which the incumbent provider provides basic
local exchange service within the corporate limits of the city on January 1 of the
current year.
"Incumbent basic local exchange service provider" or "incumbent provider" means the company or entity providing basic local exchange service in the city as
of January 1, 1979.
"Inhabitant" means any individual, corporation, partnership, joint venture, company, firm, association,
proprietorship or other entity residing or having a place of business within the city.
"New basic local exchange service provider" or "new provider" means any company or entity other than the incumbent provider who enters the business
of providing basic local exchange service.
"New provider's initial count" means the number of accounts for which a new provider provides basic local exchange
service within the city 30 days after that new provider's effective date, under this
chapter.
"Provider" means a company or entity providing basic local exchange service, through use of
its own facilities, through resale, or through any combination of the two.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
There is hereby levied on and against each telephone utility provider operating within
the city, a tax upon the occupation and business of providing basic local exchange
service within the city and upon supplying basic local exchange service at retail
to the inhabitants of the city. The annual amount of tax levied shall be equal to
$8.00 per telephone account for which basic local exchange telephone service is provided
within the corporate limits of the city on January 1 of the current year for which
the tax is levied, and upon each anniversary thereof.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
For each provider the tax levied by this chapter shall commence on January 1, 1979,
or for a new provider, shall commence 30 days from the date the provider first provides
basic local exchange service within the city. Such tax shall be due and payable in
12 equal monthly installments, with the first such installment due 30 days after such
effective date. Thereafter, each monthly installment shall be payable on the first
day of each consecutive month subject to determination of the base count effective
January 1 of each year and the total annual amount payable under this chapter.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
Within 30 days after the effective date, as provided in section 3.26.040 , each provider subject to this chapter shall file with the finance director, in such
form as the finance director may require, a statement showing the total telephone
accounts for which basic local service was provided within the corporate limits of
the city on January 1 of the current year. Such statement shall be accompanied by
the first monthly installment payment of the tax for the current year as provided
in section 3.26.040 .
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
If any provider subject to the provisions of this chapter shall fail to pay the taxes
as provided in this chapter, the full amount of such taxes shall be due and collected
from such provider, and such taxes together with an addition of ten percent of the
amount of taxes due shall be and is declared to be a debt due and owing from such
provider to the city. The city attorney, upon direction of the city council, shall
commence and prosecute to final judgment and determination in any court of competent
jurisdiction an action at law to collect the such debt in the name of the people of
the city.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
If any officer, agent or manager of a provider which is subject to the provisions
of this chapter shall fail, neglect or refuse to make or file the annual statement
of accounts provided in section 3.26.050 , the officer, agent, manager or person shall, on conviction thereof, be subject to
the provisions of section 1.28.010 .
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
The city, by its officers, agents or representatives, shall have the right at all
reasonable hours and times to examine the books and records of the provider which
are subject to the provisions of this chapter and to make copies of the entries or
contents of such records.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
The tax herein provided is upon occupations and businesses in the performance of local
functions and is not a tax upon those functions relating to interstate commerce. It
is expressly understood that none of the terms of this chapter shall be construed
to mean that any provider is issued a franchise by the city.
(Ord. No. 1500-2006, § 1, 9-19-2006)
