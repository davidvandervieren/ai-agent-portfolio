Sewer rates for all residential uses, including single-family dwelling units and multiple-family
dwellings, shall be as set forth in a city council resolution establishing such rates.
Mobile homes are considered single-family dwelling units for this section.
(Code 1977, § 13.28.010; Ord. No. 1057-1991, § 1; Ord. No. 1178-1995, § 2; Ord. No.
1318-1999, § 1, 12-21-99; Ord. No. 1594-2011, § 26, 6-7-2011)
A.
Sewer rates for all uses other than residential uses described in section 13.28.010 shall be as set forth in a city council resolution establishing such rates.
B.
In addition to the rates set forth in a city council resolution establishing such
rates, each sewer tap shall be assessed an additional fee per month for the payment
of the principal of and interest on bonded indebtedness. Such additional monthly fee
shall be as established by resolution of the city council.
(Code 1977, § 13.28.020; Ord. No. 1057-1991, § 1; Ord. No. 1178-1995, §§ 3, 4; Ord.
No. 1594-2011, § 28, 6-7-2011)
A.
All those sewer customers residing within the boundary limits of the old Louisville
sanitation district at the time of its dissolution and outside of the city limits
shall pay in addition to the rates designated in sections 13.28.010 and 13.28.020 , on a monthly basis, an amount equal to one-twelfth of the sum that would be raised
by the tax levied by the city for sewer purposes if such tax could have been levied
on the property served.
B.
Except as provided herein, all other sewer customers receiving service outside the
city limits shall pay two times the rates stated in sections 13.28.010 and 13.28.020 . The Town of Superior, Colorado shall pay for city sewer service provided pursuant
to an agreement between the city and Superior at the rates established by such agreement
and any amendments thereto.
(Ord. No. 402-1973, § 3; Code 1977, § 13.28.030; Ord. No. 1064-1992, § 1)
The city shall bill all users in accordance with the monthly sewer service charges.
Billings shall be prepared, forwarded and due as provided in section 13.12.110 .
(Code 1977, § 13.28.040; Ord. No. 956-1988, § 8)
All sewer rents and rates, including late charges and penalties, shall be a charge
and lien upon the real property to which sewer service is provided from the time such
rents are due, and shall be a perpetual charge against such property until paid. In
the event such rents are not paid when due, the city clerk may certify such delinquent
rents to the treasurer of the county, and such rents shall be collected in the same
manner as though they were a part of the taxes on such property. Alternatively, the
city shall be entitled to judgment against the consumer, or any owner or occupant
of any real property served for the costs and expenses of such proceeding, including
attorney fees in addition to the amount of delinquency.
(Code 1977, § 13.28.050; Ord. No. 956-1988, § 8)
A.
Any person who desires to permanently discontinue the use of water shall file written
notice with the city and pay all current and back charges for water used.
B.
Any person who desires to temporarily discontinue sewer use shall file written notice
to the city utility billing department and pay all current and back charges for sewer
usage. During the time a sewer service is temporarily discontinued there shall be
no minimum monthly charge for sewer usage. However, there shall continue to be charged
bond retirement and interest payment cost, as per subsection 13.28.010.B and section 13.28.020 .
C.
When a person wishes to reactivate a temporarily discontinued service they shall file
with the city's utility billing department a written notice, and pay all current and
back charges for sewer usage. Sewer service discontinuance may only be done in conjunction
with discontinuance of water service per section 13.12.130 .
(Code 1977, § 13.28.060; Ord. No. 1057-1991, § 2)
