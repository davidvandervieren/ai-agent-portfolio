Cross reference— Telephone utility tax, ch. 3.26.
There is hereby imposed, pursuant to C.R.S § 29-11-101 et seq., as amended, (the act)
an emergency telephone charge in an amount not to exceed $1.25 per month per exchange
access facility, per wireless communications access, and per interconnected voice-over-internet-protocol
service in those portions of the city for which emergency telephone service will be
provided, the proceeds from which shall be collected and administered according to
the terms of the intergovernmental agreement concerning the implementation of a "E911"
emergency telephone service between the County of Boulder, the City of Boulder, et
al., dated September 22, 1987, as amended, and the act, as amended. The city council
further authorizes the authority board as described in the intergovernmental agreement
to set such lesser charges as the authority board may from time to time determine
are appropriate in the course of the authority board's exercise of its functions under
the intergovernmental agreement.
(Code 1977, § 3.30.010; Ord. No. 995-1989, § 1; Ord. No. 1348-2000, Exh. A, 11-21-00;
Ord. No. 1662-2014, § 1, 5-20-2014; Ord. No. 1875-2024, § 1, 7-9-2024)
Service suppliers providing exchange telephone services, telecommunications service
via wireless carrier, and interconnected voice-over-internet-protocol service shall
collect the emergency telephone charge imposed by this chapter in accordance with
C.R.S. § 29-11-101 et seq., as amended.
(Code 1977, § 3.30.020; Ord. No. 922-1986, § 3; Ord. No. 1662-2014, § 1, 5-20-2014)
