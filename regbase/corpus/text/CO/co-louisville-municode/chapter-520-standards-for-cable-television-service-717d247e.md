This chapter shall be known and may be cited as "Standards for Louisville Cable Television
Service."
(Code 1977, § 5.20.010; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
For the purpose of this chapter the following terms, phrases, words and their derivations
shall have the meaning given herein.
Applicant means any person who seeks a license for any authority to construct, erect, operate
and maintain in the city any type of community antenna television system. "Applicant"
also includes any person who seeks to have an existing license assigned or transferred
to them in any manner.
Cable television system or CATV mean a system of antennas, towers, satellite earth stations, micro-wave, co-axial
cable, fiber optics, waveguides, or other conductors, converters, equipment and facilities,
designed and constructed for the purpose of producing, receiving, transmitting, amplifying
and distributing, audio, video, digital and other forms of electronic and electrical
signals to persons who subscribe to programs and services delivered by such signals.
Such definition shall not include in one or more multiple unit dwellings under common
ownership, control or management, and does not use city rights-of-way.
Licensee means any person who has been granted the authority to operate a CATV system with
the city pursuant to this chapter.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.020; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
No person shall install, operate, or maintain a cable television system within all
or any portion of the city without first obtaining a license therefor from the city
council.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.030; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
Any applicant to the city must provide a specific written proposal to the city manager
which details such matters as: system design; technical standards; number of channels;
types of specific programming being offered; construction schedules; subscriber costs;
and other matters as required by the city manager. This specific proposal must provide
for, but must not be limited to, standby power provisions, emergency override provisions,
manner of testing of the system, proposed service to schools and public buildings,
and complete financial statements of the applicant. The applicant shall supply 12
copies of the written proposal and such written proposal shall become part of any
written agreement entered into with the city.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.040; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
The city council shall, by resolution, establish fees to be paid to the city upon
the filing of a new application or of an application for transfer or assignment of
an existing license.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.050; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1; Ord. No. 1594-2011, § 14, 6-7-2011)
Upon receipt of the specific proposal from the applicant, city council shall determine
after review of the proposal, whether a nonexclusive revocable privilege and license
shall be granted to the applicant to construct, erect, operate and maintain, a CATV
system in the city. Such authority shall be granted by special ordinance passed by
the city council. In determining whether or not to grant a license, the city council
shall consider the following:
A.
Whether the proposed system is consistent with the public interest, safety and welfare;
B.
Does the applicant demonstrate financial and technical capabilities to complete the
proposed system in a timely manner;
C.
Are the plans submitted by the applicant technically adequate; and
D.
Does the application and the proposed system comply fully with the terms of this chapter?
(Code 1977, § 5.20.060; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
All special ordinances granting authority to operate a CATV system within the city
shall include the following:
A.
Requirement for construction commencement;
B.
Construction and technical standards;
C.
Operation and maintenance standards;
D.
Condition on street occupancy;
E.
Signal quality requirements;
F.
Safety requirements;
G.
City rights relative to operation;
H.
Forfeiture provisions;
I.
Duration clause;
J.
Revenues payable to the city;
K.
Security and surety provisions;
L.
Liability and indemnification; and
M.
Any other sections which the city council may deem necessary.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.070; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
A.
Upon the passage by city council of a special ordinance granting an applicant authority
to operate a CATV system within the city, but prior to commencement of any installation
by a licensee, the licensee shall execute an agreement specifically ratifying the
terms required by section 5.20.070 .
B.
The city council may upon failure of the licensee to sign the required operational
agreement within 15 days after the effective date of the special ordinance, immediately
repeal such special ordinance and licensee shall have no further rights to install
or operate a CATV system within the city.
(Code 1977, § 5.20.080; Ord. No. 676-1980, § 1)
