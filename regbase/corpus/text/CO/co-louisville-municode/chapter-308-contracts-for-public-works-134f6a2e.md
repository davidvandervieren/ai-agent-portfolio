All work done by the city in the construction of works of public improvement shall
be done by contract let to the lowest, best and responsive, responsible bidder at
determined by the city, pursuant to purchasing procedures and requirements adopted
by resolution of the city council, using the following purchasing levels:

Cost of Project | Bid Procedure
0 to $4,999.99 | Bids or quotes not required
$5,000.00 to $9,999.99 | Minimum of three telephone bids/quotes/proposals
$10,000.00 to $49,999.99 | Minimum of three written bids/quotes/proposals
$50,000.00 to $99,999.99 | Competitive bid process - approval by city manager
Over $99,999.99 | Competitive bid process - approval by city council

The city shall not be required to advertise for and receive bids for such technical,
professional, or incidental assistance as it deems wise to employ in guarding the
interests of the city against the neglect of contractors in the performance of such
work.
(Code 1977, § 3.08.010; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 3, 8-21-2012;
Ord. No. 1701-2015, § 1, 11-2-2015; Ord. No. 1890-2025 , § 1, 2-18-2025)
All local improvements, the cost of which is to be assessed against the property in
local improvement districts, shall be constructed by independent contract and all
contracts shall be let by the mayor with the approval of the city council. All such
contracts shall be let to the lowest reliable and responsible bidder after public
advertisement, once a week for three consecutive weeks, in a newspaper of general
circulation in the city; except that after such advertisement, if it is determined
by the city council that the bids are too high or that the proposed improvement could
be made by the city for less than the bid of the lowest reliable and responsible bidder,
the city may provide for doing the work by hiring labor by the day or otherwise, and
arranging for the purchase of necessary material, all under the supervision of the
city council.
(Code 1962, § 7.2; Code 1977, § 3.08.020)
Whenever it is required by law or deemed desirable by the city council that any contract
of the city for construction work, services, equipment, supplies, or other thing be
let upon a competitive bid process ($25,000 and over), the procedure for obtaining
such bids shall be as set out in sections 3.08.040 through 3.08.080 and in the purchasing procedures and requirements adopted by resolution of the city
council.
(Code 1962, § 7.3; Code 1977, § 3.08.030; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 4, 8-21-2012)
The city shall cause notice of the request for competitive bids to be published on
the city's website at LouisvilleCO.gov and the city's official bidding website at
least 14 days prior to the date set for the opening bids. Such notice may also be
published in any other publication of limited circulation or trade journal designated
by the city. The city may also mail or e-mail copies of such notice to a reasonable
number of persons or firms who could reasonably be expected to make a bid, but such
mailing or e-mailing is not required unless directed by the city council or city manager.
Wherever a larger number of publications is required by state law or this Code, as
in section 3.08.020, or wherever federal procurement requirement are applicable, such
notice requirements shall be followed. The notice to bidders shall describe the subject
of the bids and the place where the specifications, conditions, and terms of the proposed
contract may be obtained or examined, the time when and place where bids shall be
received, and the time and place of the opening of bids. The notice to bidders shall
also state that the city reserves the right to reject any and all bids.
(Code 1962, § 7.3-1; Code 1977, § 3.08.040; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 5, 8-21-2012; Ord.
No. 1890-2025 , § 2, 2-18-2025)
All bids shall be in writing, and shall show the residence of the person or the principal
place of business of the firm making the bid, together with the amount of the bid
and any other information required by the bid documents. Such bids shall be signed
by the bidder, sealed in an envelope, and filed with the city within the required
time. The bids shall also include any bid bond which may be required by the city and
stated in the notice to bidders.
(Code 1962, § 7.3-2; Code 1977, § 3.08.050; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 6, 8-21-2012)
Bids filed with the city shall not be opened until the time for opening specified
in the notice. At the time and place specified, all bids shall be opened and examined.
All bidders may be present at such time and place and may inspect the bids. The city
shall have the right to reject any and all bids submitted.
(Code 1962, § 7.3-3; Code 1977, § 3.08.060; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 7, 8-21-2012)
If no bids are received, or if the city determines that all bids are too high, the
city may enter into negotiations concerning a contract. Under no circumstances shall
the negotiated price exceed the lowest, best and responsive, responsible bid received
during the bidding process at determined by the city.
(Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 8, 8-21-2012)
Whenever any bid is accepted, the city shall award the contract to the lowest, best
and responsive, responsible bidder at determined by the city, subject to his furnishing
the necessary performance bond and complying with the other requirements which have
been determined by the city council and set forth in the proposal. Upon such acceptance
the bid bonds of other bidders shall be returned.
(Code 1962, § 7.3-4; Code 1977, § 3.08.070; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 9, 8-21-2012)
If the person whose bid was accepted by the city fails to enter into a contract, such
person's bid bond shall be forfeited to the city, and the city may then accept the
bid of the next lowest, best and responsive, responsible bidder or reject all bids
as in its judgment may be best for the interest of the city.
(Code 1962, § 7.3-5; Code 1977, § 3.08.080; Ord. No. 1621-2012, § 10, 8-21-2012)
