Cross reference— Administration and personnel, tit. 2.
A.
The city manager shall appoint the public works director of the city or another qualified
person to act as superintendent of the wastewater system.
B.
The superintendent shall have the immediate control and management of all things pertaining
to the city wastewater system, and he/she shall perform all acts necessary for the
prudent, efficient and economical management and protection of the wastewater system,
subject to the approval of the city manager.
C.
The city council shall have the power, by motion, to prescribe such other and further
rules and regulations governing the power and duties of the superintendent and such
other rates, rules and regulations not contained in chapters 13.20 , 13.24 , 13.28 and 13.32 .
(Code 1977, § 13.20.010; Ord. No. 956-1988, § 6)
Any person who violates any of the provisions of chapters 13.20 , 13.24 , 13.28 or 13.32 shall be guilty of a violation of such provisions, and be subject to the penalty
provided in section 1.28.010 , in addition to any penalties and remedies set forth in such chapters.
(Code 1977, § 13.20.020; Ord. No. 956-1988, § 6)
