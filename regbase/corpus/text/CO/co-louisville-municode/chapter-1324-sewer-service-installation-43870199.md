A.
No sewer tap shall be made upon any sewer main or any other portion of the sewer works
of the city without first securing a permit therefor. The application for a sewer
tap permit shall be made in writing upon a form furnished by the director of public
works, or the director's authorized representative, and shall state clearly the tap
size, the address or other sufficient description of the property to be served, the
address and name of the person applying for the tap, and any other information that
the director of public works may reasonably require.
B.
Each sewer tap permit issued by the city shall be appurtenant to, and may not be transferred
apart from, the property for which the permit is issued.
(Ord. No. 402-1973, § 1; Code 1977, § 13.24.010; Ord. No. 1254-1997, § 3)
A.
Unless subsection B of this section applies, no sewer tap permit shall be issued until
payment in full of the applicable sewer tap fee. In any event, no sewer service shall
be furnished to property unless a sewer tap permit is issued for that property. The
owner shall be responsible for all labor, materials, and excavation necessary for
installation of such sewer tap. The city shall perform all necessary inspections to
determine that all work is performed in accordance with the Uniform Plumbing Code.
B.
Payment of all applicable sewer tap fees may, at the option of the owner, be deferred
to a time not later than the first subdivision of the subject property if each of
the following conditions is met:
1.
At the time the property is annexed to the city it is zoned planned community zone
district (PCZD);
2.
At least 30 acres of such property is zoned commercial and/or industrial; and
3.
The requested tap is to service a preexisting use on the property at the time of annexation.
C.
If the owner elects to defer payment as provided in subsection B of this section,
the amount of the fee shall be the amount in effect at the time of payment.
(Code 1977, § 13.24.020; Ord. No. 973-1988, § 1; Ord. No. 1254-1997, § 3)
A.
The sewer tap fee shall be computed by reference to the provisions of this chapter
and shall be set forth in a table of fees established by the city manager. The city
manager shall by order establish a table of sewer tap fees no later than January 1
of each year.
B.
The sewer tap fee for each senior independent living unit, as defined in section 13.12.020 of this Code, shall be 60 percent of the existing charge for a single-family dwelling
sewer tap. The tap fee for each multifamily dwelling, each townhouse in a group of
attached "townhouses," as defined in section 17.08.560 of this Code, and each accessory dwelling unit authorized pursuant to section 17.16.035 of this Code shall be 80 percent of the existing charge for a single-family dwelling
sewer tap.
(Code 1977, § 13.24.030; Ord. No. 977-1988, § 2; Ord. No. 1082-1992, § 3; Ord. No.
1134-1993, § 2; Ord. No. 1156-1994, § 4; Ord. No. 1239-1996, § 2, 11-19-1996; Ord.
No. 1267-1997, § 1, 9-16-1997; Ord. No. 1273-1997, § 2, 12-2-1997; Ord. No. 1290-1998,
§ 2, 12-1-1998; Ord. No. 1319-1999, § 2, 12-21-99; Ord. No. 1594-2011, § 27, 6-7-2011;
Ord. No. 1633-2013, § 3, 5-21-2013; Ord. No. 1663-2014, § 1, 5-20-2014; Ord. No. 1900-2025 , § 9, 8-5-2025)
A.
Any individual, firm, or corporation desiring an extension of the city sewer system
to serve his private property shall pay the entire cost of extending the city sewer
service to his property, and shall deed to the city all sewer lines and improvements
necessary to extend the service to his property.
B.
Any individual, firm or partnership desiring an extension of the city sewer system
shall pay the cost of any oversizing which the city requires to serve any individual
property as a result of any master plan adopted by the city for sewer service within
or outside of the existing city limits.
C.
All extensions shall conform to city specifications.
D.
In the event any user is required to extend a main adjacent to land not owned or controlled
by him, the city will negotiate a repayment agreement with the user which will allow
such user to recoup as much of the cost of the extension between the boundary of his
land and the nearest properly sized city sewer main as can be agreed upon.
(Ord. No. 402-1973, § 5; Code 1977, § 13.24.050; Ord. No. 579-1978, § 19)
If the size line required to serve a subdivision or individual is smaller than the
size line the city wishes installed for sound future planning, the subdivision or
individual shall bear the cost for the required oversizing, and repayment by other
line users will follow as set out by resolution.
(Code 1977, § 13.24.060; Ord. No. 579-1978, § 19)
