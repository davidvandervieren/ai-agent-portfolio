State Law reference— Minimum standards, C.R.S. § 12-48.5-101 et seq.
As used in this chapter, the words set out in this section shall have the following
meanings unless the context indicates otherwise:
Massage means pressure upon or friction against, or stroking, rubbing, kneading or other
manipulation of the body by means, with or without apparatus, including but not limited
to vibrators, infrared heat, sun lamps, and external baths.
Massage parlor means an establishment or place of business where any person, association, firm,
or corporation engages in, conducts, provides or carries on, or permits to be engaged,
conducted, provided, or carried on any massage.
Massage therapist means any person 21 years of age or older who has graduated from a massage therapy
school, accredited by the state educational board or division charged with the responsibility
of approving private occupational schools, or from a school with comparable approval
or accreditation from another state with transcripts indicating completion of at least
500 hours of training in massage therapy. If the school is located in the state, it
must meet the qualifications established by C.R.S. § 12-59-101 et seq. or other applicable
statutes pertaining to vocational education.
Operator means any person, partnership, association, firm, or corporation who owns, operates,
or manages a massage parlor. A manager of a massage parlor must be 21 years of age
or older.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.010; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.1; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
The following classes of persons and establishments are exempted from the regulations
of this chapter:
A.
Physicians, osteopaths, physical therapists, chiropodists, podiatrists, or chiropractors
licensed to practice in this state while performing such services in the practice
of their respective professions;
B.
Registered nurses and licensed practical nurses who are licensed to practice in this
state while performing such services in their usual nursing duties;
C.
Barbers and cosmetologists duly licensed under the laws of this state in the course
of the practice of their usual and ordinary licensed vocation and profession, as defined
in C.R.S. § 12-8-101 et seq.;
D.
Massage practiced at the athletic department or training rooms of public or private
schools accredited by the state board of education or approved by the division charged
with the responsibility of approving private occupational schools;
E.
Hospitals, clinics, nursing and convalescent homes, and other similar health care
institutions dedicated to medical or nursing practices licensed under the laws of
this state;
F.
A facility operated for the purpose of massage therapy by a massage therapist;
G.
Training rooms of recognized professional or amateur athletic teams.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.020; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.3; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
It shall be unlawful for any person, partnership, association, firm, or corporation
to render or to permit to be engaged in, conducted, provided or carried on, in or
upon any premises within the city, the business of a massage parlor within the city
without first obtaining a license issued pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.030; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.1; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
A.
Any person, firm, partnership, association, or corporation seeking to obtain a massage
parlor license shall make application to the city clerk.
B.
Each application for a new license filed with the city clerk shall be accompanied
by an application fee in the amount as established by resolution of the city council.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.040; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.4; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1; Ord. No.
1594-2011, § 11, 6-7-2011)
Each application for a new license for a massage parlor must contain the following
information:
A.
The full name and present address of the operator; and, if a corporation, partnership,
firm or association, the names and residence addresses of all owners, officers, and
directors of the such massage parlor;
B.
The age of each person listed on the application;
C.
A physical description and recent photograph of each person listed on the application;
D.
A listing of all past experience including training and certification related to massage
of all persons listed;
E.
Address of the premises where massage will be conducted;
F.
Acknowledgement of all convictions or pleas of nolo contendere for any criminal offenses
(except traffic offenses) within the last five years by all persons listed in the
application;
G.
A report from a physician licensed to practice medicine in the state that the operator
and all employees performing massage have within 30 days immediately prior thereto,
been examined and found to be free of any contagious or communicable disease; and
H.
A set of fingerprints for each person listed in the application.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.050; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.5; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
A.
The city council shall conduct a public hearing on each license application received
as set forth in this section. The city council shall enter a finding when issuing
a license that each operator has met the following standards:
1.
That each individual to be associated with the operator is of good character;
2.
That each massage parlor conforms to all zoning regulations and restrictions and to
the standards of the building code, adopted in title 15 ; and, further,
3.
That other existing licenses relative to the particular locality in issue are inadequate
for the reasonable needs for the neighborhood.
B.
Upon instruction from the city council, the city clerk shall issue the proper license
to the applicant for a term of one year.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.060; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
A.
Every operator to whom or for which a license has been granted shall display such
license in a conspicuous place so that the license may be readily seen by any person
entering the premises where the massage is given.
B.
Each operator or agent or employee of such operator shall obtain an identity card
from the chief of police and shall carry such identity card at all times while in
or upon the licensed premises.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.070; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
No massage parlor shall be open for business, admit customers or prospective customers,
or allow or permit any massage or treatment of any person upon the premises between
the hours of 12:00 midnight and 8:00 a.m.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.080; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.8; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
Any person who is employed at or is an operator of a massage parlor shall immediately
report to the city police department any unlawful or disorderly act, conduct, or disturbance
committed on the premises of the massage parlor.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.090; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.9; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
No person under the age of 18 years shall be allowed within or upon the premises of
a massage parlor.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.100; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.10; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
A.
A license for a massage parlor cannot be transferred from one geographic location
to another. A change of location of the premises previously licensed under this chapter
shall require a new application for a license and the payment of the application fee.
The city council shall hold a public hearing before issuing a new license for the
new location.
B.
Upon the sale or transfer of any ownership interest in a massage parlor, the existing
license shall expire. A new application for a license shall be made by the new owner
or operator. A public hearing may be required prior to issuing a license required
by transfer or sale to determine if the applicant is of good moral character and reputation.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.110; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1)
Application for the renewal of an existing license shall be made to the city council
not less than 45 days prior to the date of expiration of the existing license. The
city council may cause a hearing on the application to be held. No such renewal hearing
shall be held until a notice of hearing has been conspicuously posted on the licensed
premises for a period of ten days and notice of the hearing has been provided the
applicant at least ten days prior to the hearing. The city council may refuse to renew
any license for good cause shown.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.120; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
The license fee for a new massage parlor license and the renewal fee for a massage
parlor license shall be as established by resolution of the city council.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.130; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2; Ord. No. 1047-1991, § 1; Ord. No.
1594-2011, § 12, 6-7-2011)
State Law reference— Limitation on local license fee, C.R.S. § 12-48.5-110.
The city council may, after determining at a public hearing that the licensee has
violated any provisions of this Code or of the state statutes, suspend a license for
a specific length of time not less than five days nor longer than 20 days, or upon
determination of repeated violations of this Code or the state statutes, revoke any
license issued under this chapter.
(Code 1977, § 5.16.140; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.14)
Any person who violates any provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a violation
hereof and shall be subject to the penalty provided in section 1.28.010 .
(Code 1977, § 5.16.150; Ord. No. 532-1977, § 1.15)
