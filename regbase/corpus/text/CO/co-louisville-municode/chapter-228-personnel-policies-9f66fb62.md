The city manager shall have authority, subject to city council appropriation, to provide
for city employees' hours of employment, working conditions, vacations, sick leave,
classification and salary schedules, group life insurance, hospitalization, medical
care, worker's compensation, and other personnel benefits.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The city council or city manager, or their designee, may investigate the official
acts of any officer, public body member, or employee of the city, and may obtain the
assistance of the city attorney or such other persons as the city council or city
manager determines necessary to conduct such investigation.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
