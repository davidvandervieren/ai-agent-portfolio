State Law reference— Land development charges, C.R.S. § 29-1-801 et seq.
There is created a capital improvement fund in the city. All capital improvement fees
collected prior to February 11, 2007, in accordance with section 3.16.020 (repealed effective February 11, 2007, by Ordinance No. 1506, Series 2006) shall
be paid into a special and segregated interest-bearing fund. The treasurer shall account
separately for all receipts and disbursements to and from the fund and shall not commingle
any part of such fund with other funds of the city. Any interest or other income earned
on money deposited in the fund shall be credited to the fund.
(Code 1977, § 3.16.030; Ord. No. 874-1985, § 2; Ord. No. 1506-2006, § 4, 12-19-2006)

State Law reference— Fund created, C.R.S. § 29-1-803.
There is hereby created a downtown parking improvement fund ("fund") in the city.
All parking improvement fees paid and collected in accordance with section 17.20.025 shall be paid into the fund. The fund shall be an interest-bearing account of the
city. The fund account may be within existing funds of the city, provided that amounts
within the fund account are accounted for separately and that the aggregate amount
of collected parking improvement fees is clearly identified. Any interest income earned
on parking improvement fees shall be credited to the fund. The treasurer shall account
separately for all receipts and disbursements to and from the fund and shall not commingle
any part of the monies in such fund with other funds of the city.
(Ord. No. 1341-2000, § 4; Ord. No. 1506-2006, § 5, 12-19-2006)

A.
Any and all disbursements from the downtown parking improvement fund ("fund") shall
be limited to the following uses determined by the city council to be reasonably necessary
to respond to and assist in defraying the costs of parking needs created by new development
within Downtown Louisville which is subject to the fee:
1.
Acquisition, construction, improvement, and provision of additional on-street and
off-street public parking spaces to serve Downtown Louisville;
2.
Grading; installation of curb, gutter, and pavement; construction of drainage improvements;
and the installation of lighting, landscaping, trash receptacles, outdoor seating,
bike racks and similar facilities, in association with the acquisition, construction,
improvement or provision of additional on- and off-street public parking space to
serve Downtown Louisville;
3.
Purchase or lease of real and personal property for the construction, improvement,
or provision of additional on-street and off-street public parking space to serve
Downtown Louisville;
B.
A portion of the parking improvement fee collected may be used to help defray the
administrative expenses of the departments of finance, parks and recreation, community
development, public works and city administration reasonably incurred in connection
with and equitably apportioned to acquisition and construction of improvements pursuant
to this section and the cost of accounting and management of the parking improvement
fee and fund, with such portion of the fee being deposited into the appropriate fund
of the city to help defray such expenses.
C.
The city manager may from time to time establish written administrative regulations
to facilitate the implementation of this section and related sections of this Code
concerning the Downtown Louisville parking improvement fee. Such regulations shall
be consistent with the purposes and provisions of this chapter and related sections
of this Code.
(Ord. No. 1341-2000, § 4; Ord. No. 1506-2006, § 5, 12-19-2006)

Any and all disbursements from the capital improvement fund shall be limited to the
following uses determined by the city council to be reasonably necessary to respond
to and assist in defraying the costs of needs created by the new construction subject
to the fee: constructing and improving major thoroughfares within the city, the purchase
and planting of trees within the city, the purchase of real and personal property
in conjunction with construction of the capital improvement, to construct or otherwise
acquire open space, parks, trails, and other recreational facilities, to construct,
enlarge, and otherwise enhance the public library system, to construct, enlarge, and
otherwise enhance the police administration facility, the repair or installation of
sidewalks and curbs, street repair, street overlay or seal coating, the construction
of drainage improvements, and the construction of other capital improvements as approved
specifically by the city council. Up to $0.55 per square foot of the capital improvement
fee collected may be used to help defray the expenses of the departments of finance,
parks and recreation, community development, public works and city administration
reasonably incurred in connection with and equitably apportioned to the construction
and acquisition of the capital improvements pursuant to this chapter and the cost
of accounting and management of the capital improvement fee and fund, with such portion
of the fee being deposited into the appropriate fund of the city to help defray such
expenses.
(Code 1977, § 3.16.060; Ord. No. 874-1985, § 2; Ord. No. 1095-1993, § 2)
