No person shall deposit or cause to be deposited any rubbish, trash, manure, ashes,
tin cans, garbage, waste matter, or debris of any kind in any ditch, canal, or waterway,
nor in any street or alley, nor upon any property within the city, whether such property
is publicly or privately owned.
(Code 1962, § 12.13(a); Code 1977, § 8.04.010; Ord. No. 519-1977, § 2)
All garbage or refuse consisting of waste, or animal or vegetable matter upon which
rats may feed, and all small dead animals, shall be placed and stored in a covered
metal or plastic container as prescribed by code enforcement officer.
(Code 1977, § 8.04.020; Ord. No. 718-1981, § 2)
It shall be unlawful for any person to allow or permit trash, brush, garbage, rubbish,
or building materials to accumulate in any building, on any property, or along any
alley or sidewalk.
(Code 1962, § 12.14; Code 1977, § 8.04.030; Ord. No. 519-1977, § 2)
