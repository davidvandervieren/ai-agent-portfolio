The city manager shall appoint a qualified person to serve as the city treasurer.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
A.
The city treasurer shall perform the responsibilities provided for in the Charter,
the city's ordinances, and other applicable laws, and other duties as directed by
the city manager.
B.
The city treasurer shall be custodian of all funds belonging to the city.
C.
The city treasurer shall keep a separate account of each fund or appropriation and
the debits and credits belonging thereto.
D.
The city treasurer shall keep a register of all checks drawn upon the city treasurer,
whether such checks are paid or not, showing the number, the date, to whom issued,
for what purpose issued, the fund on which the check is drawn, and the amount. Such
register shall be open to the inspection of all city officers at all times.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
