A.
Any sale or disposal of any lot or parcel of real estate owned by the city shall conform
to the requirements of the Charter.
B.
At least ten days' notice to the public shall be given prior to any final determination
to purchase for the city any lot or parcel of real estate. The notice may be given
on a city council meeting agenda and by publication and shall include the general
location and a brief description of the intended use of the real estate.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
