which has been surfaced with cement, stone, or other similar material.

thereof, known as the roadway, the portion used for sidewalks, and the portion between
the property line and the roadway, known as the parkway. The term also includes an
alley.

It is unlawful for any person to store or park on any public street or right-of-way
any snowplow which is designed to be mounted on a motor vehicle but which is not hooked
up or otherwise attached to a motor vehicle in a manner to be used for its intended
purpose.
(Code 1977, § 12.20.090; Ord. No. 1087-1992, § 1)
A.
The purpose of this section is to prevent dangers to persons and property, to prevent
delays, and to avoid interference with the traffic flow. Roadways that have center
medians often are designed to deal with specific traffic flow problems. Any delay
or distraction may interfere with traffic planning. Sometimes persons stand near intersections
and near traffic lights to contact drivers or passengers in cars that are passing
or that are stopped temporarily due to traffic lights.
B.
It shall be unlawful for any person to solicit employment, business, contributions,
or sales of any kind, or collect monies for the same, from the occupant of any vehicle
traveling upon any street or highway when such solicitation or collection:
1.
Causes the person performing the activity to enter onto the traveled portion of a
street or highway.
2.
Involves the person performing the activity to be located upon any median area which
separates traffic lanes for vehicular travel in opposite directions.
3.
The person performing the activity is located such that vehicles cannot move into
a legal parking area to safely conduct the transaction.
C.
It shall be unlawful for any person to solicit or attempt to solicit employment, business,
or contributions of any kind from the occupant of any vehicle on U.S. Highway 36 and
State Highway 42 including any entrance to or exit from such highway.
D.
For purposes of this section, the traveled portion of the street or highway shall
mean that portion of the road normally used by moving motor vehicle traffic.
E.
Any person who violates any provision of this section shall be subject to the penalty
provided in section 1.28.010 .

The procedure set out in sections 12.24.030 through 12.24.050 is adopted for the naming of streets and the numbering of houses within the city
limits and new additions to the city.
(Ord. No. 397-1972, § 1; Code 1977, § 12.24.010)
The purpose of this roadway-naming and house-numbering plan is to provide an orderly
and contiguous pattern throughout the city, and to enable particular roadways and
houses to be found as quickly and easily as possible.
(Ord. No. 397-1972, § 1; Code 1977, § 12.24.020)
Plats of new subdivisions shall be reviewed by the city manager or his representative
in order to avoid duplication of roadway names. House numbers in the city shall be
assigned by the building inspection department as building permits are issued, or
upon demand.
(Ord. No. 397-1972, § 1(a); Code 1977, § 12.24.030)
Roadways shall be named in accordance with the following guidelines:
A.
Roadways running east and west are streets.
B.
Roadways running north and south are avenues.
C.
Roadways that both originate and terminate on an avenue are "ways," if less than 1,000
feet in length; or "drives," if over 1,000 feet in length.
D.
Roadways with a dead end or a cul-de-sac are "courts," if less than 1,000 feet in
length; or "lanes," if over 1,000 feet in length.
E.
Curving roadways returning to their origin are "circles."
F.
Other roadways designations shall be approved by the city manager.
G.
Roadway names shall not be duplicated in the city.
H.
Roadway signs shall be readable from each direction of travel.
I.
Temporary signs are required during construction periods.
(Ord. No. 397-1972, § 1(a); Code 1977, § 12.24.040; Ord. No. 1068-1992, § 1)
Houses shall be numbered according to the following guidelines:
A.
Front footage along a street rather than a particular structure, is given a number.
Since there is no way of knowing how many structures will eventually be built on land
now vacant, numbers should be spaced from zero through 99 on each major block so that
the 50 will be in the middle of the block.
B.
Odd numbers shall be on the north and west sides of roadways, with even numbers on
the south and east sides.
C.
Numbers should not be duplicated for five blocks. (For example: first house 501; next
street first house 503; next street, first house 505; 507, etc.)
D.
House numbers shall be readable from the roadway, particularly at night.
E.
All new developments and numbering systems shall be submitted to the city manager
for approval.
(Ord. No. 397-1972, § 1(b); Code 1977, § 12.24.050)
