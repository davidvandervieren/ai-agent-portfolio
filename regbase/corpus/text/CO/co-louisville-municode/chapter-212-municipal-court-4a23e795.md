A.
The city council shall appoint, by the affirmative vote of two-thirds of the entire
council, a presiding municipal judge.
B.
The presiding municipal judge shall be appointed for a two-year term, except that
the initial appointment may be for a term which expires on the date of the next regular
election. Any vacancy in the office of presiding municipal judge shall be filled by
appointment for the remainder of the unexpired term.
C.
The presiding municipal judge shall be, at all times while serving as judge, an attorney
at law admitted to practice in Colorado.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The Louisville Municipal Court is established as a qualified municipal court of record
as defined under C.R.S. § 13-10-102. There shall be kept a verbatim record of all
proceedings and evidence presented at trials in the municipal court by either electric
device or stenographic means.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The municipal court shall have exclusive original jurisdiction to hear, try, and determine
all alleged violations of the ordinances of the city. The municipal court is further
vested with jurisdiction over matters arising under the Charter and ordinances of
the city.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The municipal judge shall have the power to issue local rules of operation or procedure
consistent with any rules of procedure adopted by the state supreme court. The municipal
judge shall have all powers incident to a court of record in relation to the attendance
of witnesses, the punishment of contempt, and to enforcing the orders of court, provided,
that no imprisonment for contempt shall exceed six months and no fine for such case
shall exceed $1,000.00.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
A.
The city council shall appoint, by the affirmative vote of two-thirds of the entire
council, such deputy municipal judges as the council deems necessary.
B.
Each deputy municipal judge shall be appointed for a two-year term, except that the
initial appointment may be for a term which expires on the date of the next regular
election. Any vacancy in the office of deputy municipal judge shall be filled by appointment
for the remainder of the unexpired term.
C.
Each deputy municipal judge shall be, at all times while serving as judge, an attorney
at law admitted to practice in Colorado.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The presiding municipal judge shall receive as full compensation for the judge's services
a yearly salary of $33,600.00 payable on a monthly basis of $2,800.00 per month, effective
March 1, 2020. Each deputy municipal judge shall receive compensation for such deputy
municipal judge's services as is set by city council resolution.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018; Ord. No. 1788-2019, § 1, 1-7-2020)
The removal of any judge during the term of office shall require the affirmative vote
of two-thirds of the entire council, any such removal shall only be for one or more
cause listed in section 9-3 of the Charter.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
A municipal judge in the judge's discretion may assess costs against any defendant
who pleads guilty or nolo contendere or who enters into a plea agreement or who, after
trial, is found guilty of an ordinance violation, such costs not to exceed $100.00
for trial to the court and $200.00 for trial by jury. The municipal judge, city manager,
or a designee, is authorized to use any lawful method of collecting fines, fees, default
judgments, personal recognizance bond forfeitures, civil penalties, or any other unpaid
amounts due from any person assessed such sums by the municipal court, including court
costs, surcharges and the reasonable costs of collection. Reasonable costs of collection
shall include, without limitation, the fees and costs of a collection agency or counsel,
but such fees and costs shall not exceed 25 percent of the unpaid amount.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
