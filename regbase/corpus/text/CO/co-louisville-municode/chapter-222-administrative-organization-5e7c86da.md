The city manager shall exercise supervision and control over all city departments,
and make recommendations to the city council concerning the establishment, consolidation
or abolition of city departments. Departments shall be defined by the city's organizational
chart included in the annual budget and amended from time to time. Department heads
shall be appointed by and serve at the pleasure of the city manager.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
There are created the following departments public works, parks and recreation, planning
and building safety, finance, human resources, information technology, police, library
and museum services, and economic development.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
Each department head shall be responsible for hiring of all the employees needed for
their department, subject to staffing level authorizations, budgetary limitations
and the supervision and control of the city manager. The department head shall coordinate
or supervise each employee in their department. Each departmental employee shall be
subject to the city's policies and procedures.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
Before permitting any member of the council or any city employee to perform any function
or duty involving the handling of city funds, the city shall obtain a fidelity bond
or insurance coverage in an amount acceptable to the council.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The positions of city manager, city attorney, city water attorney, city prosecuting
attorney, bond counsel, auditors and all other consultants for the city shall serve
at the pleasure of the city council and their appointment may be terminated at will,
subject to specific written agreements between the city and the individual. Such positions
shall be subject to an annual review by the city council at the option of the city
council. At the option of the city council, the city may enter into such written service
agreements with any consultants of the city.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
