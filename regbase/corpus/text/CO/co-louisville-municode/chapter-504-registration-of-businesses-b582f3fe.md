Editor's note— Ord. No. 1559, § 1, adopted Oct. 20, 2009 deleted the former Ch. 5.04, §§ 5.04.010—5.04-100,
and enacted a new Ch. 5.04 as set out herein. The former Ch. 5.04 pertained to registration
of businesses and derived from Ord. No. 1384-2002, § 1, 5-21-2002. The amendments
set forth in Ord. No. 1559 shall take effect on Jan. 1, 2010.
The purpose of this chapter shall be to require the registration and annual renewal
of such registration of all business activities and enterprises physically located
within the city and to provide the city with necessary information concerning the
businesses within the city, including but not limited to the nature of the business
operation, the number of employees, place of business, and emergency contacts, in
order to protect the health, welfare and safety of the city's inhabitants.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
For the purposes of this chapter the following terms, phrases, words, and their derivatives
shall have the meanings given in this section:
Business means and includes all kinds of vocations, occupations, home occupations, professions,
enterprises and establishments, any of which are conducted on any premises in this
city or anywhere else within its jurisdiction.
Premises means and includes all land, structures and places, and the equipment and appurtenances
connected or used therewith, in any business, and also any personal property which
is either affixed to, or otherwise used in connection with, any such business conducted
on such premises.
Finance director means the director of finance and administrative services of the City of Louisville,
or such person's designee.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
A.
It is unlawful for any person, whether as a principal, agent, clerk or employee, either
for himself or for any other person, corporation or otherwise, to commence, carry
on or establish any kind of business within the city without first having registered
with the city to do so, and each and every calendar year thereafter without having
paid the annual registration fee provided for in section 5.04.070 of this Code, or without complying with any and all regulations of such business
contained in this chapter and Code.
B.
No person shall be required to register with the city under this chapter if such person
maintains no fixed business premises within the city.
C.
Any person, business or enterprise transacting retail sales within the city is required
to purchase a city sales tax license and remit city sales tax in compliance with chapter 3.20 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
Every person required to register under the provision of this chapter shall submit
a form for such business registration to the finance director. The form shall:
A.
Be a written statement upon forms provided by the finance director. Such forms shall
include an affidavit signed by the registrant stating the name of the business, owner
or owners of the business, manager of the business, if any, the address and telephone
number of the business, the number of employees, and the nature of the business;
B.
Require the disclosure of all additional information necessary for compliance with
this chapter and any other information which the finance director finds to be reasonably
necessary to the fair administration of this chapter; and
C.
Be submitted within 30 days of commencement of business within the city.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
A.
Upon the receipt of the business registration form, where any provision of this Code
necessitates an inspection or further investigation, the finance director shall refer
the business registration form to the proper officer, board or commission for making
such investigation.
B.
Registration does not permit the conduct of any business if the premises to be used
for the business, or the proposed conduct of such business, does not fully comply
with the requirements of this Code.
C.
Registration does not permit the conduct of any business or performance of any act
which would constitute a violation of the zoning code of the city as set forth in title 17 , or any other provision of this Code.
D.
Registration does not permit the conduct of any business which violates any existing
state or federal statutes, or municipal ordinance.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
The finance director shall:
A.
Enforce all reasonable rules and regulations necessary to the operation of this chapter;
B.
Adopt all forms and prescribe the information to be given in such forms;
C.
Require registrants to submit all affidavits and oaths necessary to the administration
of this chapter;
D.
Submit all registrations to interested city officials for their review as to compliance
by the registrant with all city ordinances, rules and regulations which such officials
have the duty of enforcing; and
E.
Maintain a listing of all registered businesses located within the city.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
There shall be a nonrefundable annual fee to register a business. The annual fee shall
be as established by resolution of the city council. Such annual registration shall
remain in effect until December 31 of the year of registration or until such time
as the registrant no longer conducts such business within the city limits or until
such time as the ownership of such business changes, whichever first occurs. A business
registration is not transferable whether by sale, gift, operation of law or otherwise.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009; Ord. No. 1594-2011, § 2, 6-7-2011)
A separate registration application must be made for each branch, establishment or
separate place of business in which the business is carried on.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
A.
A registration may be denied, canceled, suspended or revoked for as provided for in
this section.
B.
The finance director may deny a registration if the registrant has failed to file
any reports or furnish any other information that may be required by any provisions
of this Code relating to the specific business, or the business registration form
contains a material omission or misstatement of fact.
C.
The finance director may cancel a registration if the registrant has failed to timely
pay the annual registration fee.
D.
The finance director, upon seven days written notice to a registrant, stating the
contemplated action and in general the grounds therefor, and after a reasonable opportunity
for the registrant to be heard, may suspend or revoke any business registration filed
with the city if the finance director finds that:
1.
The applicant has failed to file any reports or furnish any other information that
may be required by any provisions of this Code relating to the specific business;
2.
The applicant has violated any of the provisions of this Code pertaining to the business,
or any regulation or order lawfully made relating thereto;
3.
The business registration form contains a material omission or misstatement of fact;
4.
The business has failed to timely pay sales tax; or
5.
Any other fact or condition exists which, if it had existed or had been known to exist
at the time of the submittal of the business registration form, would have warranted
the refusal of registration.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
The carrying on of any business as provided in this chapter, without first having
registered with the city, or without complying with any and all regulations of such
business contained in this chapter, shall be deemed a separate violation of this chapter
for each and every day that such business is carried on.
(Ord. No. 1559-2009, § 1, 10-20-2009)
