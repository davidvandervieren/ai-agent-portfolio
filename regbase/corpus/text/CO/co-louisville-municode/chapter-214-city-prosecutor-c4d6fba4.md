The city council, by the affirmative vote of a majority of the entire council, shall
appoint a city prosecutor to serve at the pleasure of the council.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The council shall establish the city prosecutor's compensation.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The city prosecutor shall be, at all times while serving as city prosecutor, an attorney
at law admitted to practice in Colorado.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
The removal of the city prosecutor shall require the affirmative vote of a majority
of the entire council.
(Ord. No. 1753-2018, § 1, 2-6-2018)
