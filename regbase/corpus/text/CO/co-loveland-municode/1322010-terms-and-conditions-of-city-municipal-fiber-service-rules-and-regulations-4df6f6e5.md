A.
Residential and commercial customers of the City's municipal fiber service, both within
and without the corporate limits of the City, shall pay the rates, fees, and charges
set by the City Manager for such services. Customers shall take municipal fiber service
in accordance with terms and conditions as well as general rules and regulations as
may be set by the City Manager or his or her designee. Terms and conditions of service
and rules and regulations may differ for residential and commercial municipal fiber
services.
B.
To the extent this chapter or other applicable conditions do not govern and control,
the applicable terms and conditions of service as set forth in subscription agreements
shall govern and control the rendering of municipal fiber services and charging and
collecting rates, fees and charges for the sale of such services.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
A.
Municipal fiber service rates, fees, and charges shall apply to the provision of municipal
fiber services by the City. Municipal fiber services include, but are not limited
to, broadband internet services, voice services, streaming services, bulk fiber license
services, wholesale services, point-to-point fiber services, and other services provided
by the City through its Electric and Communications Enterprise.
B.
The City Manager is authorized to set rates, fees and charges for furnishing municipal
fiber services to municipal fiber customers and licensees, subject to the following
conditions:
1.
The City Manager shall publish and make available to the public the municipal fiber
services rates, fees, and charges sheet.
2.
The rates, fees, and charges charged by the City for municipal fiber services for
different customer classes shall be available on a uniform basis for the services
provided to each customer class.
3.
The City Manager shall post municipal fiber rates, fees, and charges for municipal
fiber services determined in accordance with this Subsection B to be charged from
time to time on the City's website. A copy of such rates, fees, and charges shall
be filed with City Clerk and shall be available for public inspection.
4.
The City Manager shall ensure that the rates, fees, and charges for municipal fiber
services shall be sufficient to pay the costs of operation and maintenance of the
municipal fiber system, to provide and maintain adequate working capital funds for
the day-to-day business of the Electric and Communications Enterprise, to provide
and maintain an adequate fund for the replacement of depreciated and obsolete property,
and for the extension, improvement, enlargement, and betterment of the municipal fiber
system.
5.
The City Manager shall ensure that the rates, fees, and charges for municipal fiber
services also shall be sufficient to pay all bonded indebtedness, pay legally required
refunds related to the municipal fiber service, cover the cost of operation, maintenance,
additions, extensions, betterments, and improvements, provide a reasonable return
on the City's investment in municipal fiber service properties and capital investments,
and reimburse the general fund for administrative services and overhead provided and
incurred by the City on behalf of the municipal fiber service, together with a sum
which, in the City's judgment, is equivalent to that which would have been obtained
from a franchise fee or utility occupation tax on the utility.
6.
Such rates, fees, and charges may include a charge for damage to or failure to return
municipal fiber equipment, such as set top boxes, telephones, optical network terminals,
modems, or other moveable personal property leased or otherwise made available to
municipal fiber subscribers.
7.
The City Manager shall further ensure that all applicable state and federal rates,
fees, charges, and taxes arising from the municipal broadband services are properly
levied.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
In provision of its municipal fiber services, the City must treat all lawful data
on the internet equally, and not discriminate by individual user, content, website,
platform, application, type of attached equipment, or method of communication and
will not intentionally block, slow down or charge money for specific lawful websites
and online content. This nondiscrimination and equal access obligation shall not be
construed to prevent the City from conducting network management activities to ensure
the City's provision of municipal fiber service and public use of the municipal fiber
system are consistent with the City's use policies and public health and welfare.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
A.
Due to the sensitive nature of municipal fiber usage and data, the City will maintain
as confidential all municipal fiber customer information and data usage and shall
not release information provided by a customer or about a customer.
B.
Except in accordance with a judicial order or as otherwise provided by law, the City
shall not divulge or make known in any way any information about individual customer
data usage, sites accessed, or other individual private customer account and usage
information. The City shall only provide customer usage information or data information
to a third party, including a federal, state, or local law enforcement agency, upon
receipt by the City of a warrant or subpoena signed by a court of law requesting the
release of such information by the City.
C.
City Staff shall not be required to produce any reports or other information regarding
individual customer internet access or data. Nothing shall prohibit the publication
of aggregate statistical reports and information regarding data usage and customer
accounts, as long as individual residential and business customer information is not
identifiable.
D.
The City may maintain as confidential and will refuse to release information provided
to the City by its customers if such information is characterized by the customers
as confidential, privileged, or a trade secret.
E.
Certain rate classes or agreements with customers may be confidential, privileged,
or a trade secret, and as such will not be released by the City without written authorization
from the customer. Any such authorization must be signed by the customer or employee
of customer with authority to request the release of such information.
F.
The City shall not release any information otherwise protected under statutory exceptions
to the Colorado Open Records Act. All information and records related to individual
customers shall not qualify as public records under the Colorado Open Records Act.
G.
Requests for billing information shall be handled in accordance with administrative
regulations to be adopted by the City.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Loveland Municipal Code, the City Manager,
or his or her designee, may execute contracts for video streaming services to be offered
to customers of the City's municipal fiber services without City Council review and
approval, regardless of the contract amount. The City Manager shall notify the City
Council of the execution of any video streaming contract executed under the authority
granted in this subsection above that entails a City expenditure of more than $500,000.00.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
The City shall own all aspects of the municipal fiber system. Customers' maintenance
duties may be defined more particularly in their terms of service or rules and regulations
adopted by the City Manager or his or her designee.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
The City Manager shall report to City Council regularly, but no less than monthly,
on any significant action taken related to municipal fiber service.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 5, 5-5-2020)
