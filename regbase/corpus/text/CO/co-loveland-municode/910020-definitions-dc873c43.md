A.
It is unlawful for any person, with intent to harass, annoy or alarm another person,
to:
1.
Strike, shove, kick or otherwise touch a person or subject him or her to physical
contact; or
2.
In a public place direct obscene language or make an obscene gesture to or at another
person; or
3.
Follow a person in or about a public place; or
4.
Initiate communication with a person, anonymously or otherwise, by telephone, computer,
computer network, or computer system in a manner intended to harass or threaten bodily
injury or property damage, or make any comment, request, suggestion or proposal by
telephone, computer, computer network or computer system which is obscene; or
5.
Make a telephone call or causes a telephone to ring repeatedly, whether or not a conversation
ensues, with no purpose of legitimate conversation; or
6.
Make repeated communications at inconvenient hours that invade the privacy of another
and interfere in the use and enjoyment of another's home or private residence or private
property; or
7.
Repeatedly insult, taunt or challenge another in a manner likely to provoke a violent
or disorderly response.
B.
As used in this section, unless the context otherwise requires, obscene means a blatantly
offensive description of ultimate sexual acts or solicitation to commit ultimate sexual
acts, whether or not said ultimate sexual acts are normal or perverted, actual or
simulated, including masturbation, cunnilingus, fellatio, anilingus or excretory functions.
C.
Any act prohibited by Subsection A.4 of this section may be deemed to have occurred
or to have been committed at the place at which the telephone call was either made
or received.
(Ord. 5253 § 2, 2007)
A.
The city council finds that sexual predators and the specified sex offenders who prey
on children or who use physical violence present an extreme threat the public safety.
Sexual predators and the specified sex offenders have a high rate of recidivism, making
the cost of sex offender victimization to society at large extremely high. Removing
such offenders from regular proximity to places where children are located and limiting
the frequency of contact is likely to reduce the risk of reoffending.
B.
This chapter is intended to serve the city's compelling interest to promote, protect,
and improve the public health, safety, and welfare by prohibiting sexual predators
and the specified sexual offenders from establishing temporary or permanent residence
near locations where children regularly congregate in concentrated numbers.
( Ord. No. 6783 , § 1, 8-19-2025)
For purposes of this Chapter, the following terms shall have the following meanings:
Permanent residence means place where a person abides, lodges, or resides for five or more consecutive
days.
Temporary residence means a place where a person abides, lodges, or resides for a period of five or more
days in the aggregate during any calendar year and which is not the person's permanent
residence, or a place where a person routinely abides, lodges, or resides for a period
of five or more consecutive or nonconsecutive days in any month and which is not the
person's permanent address.
Registered sex offender means:
1.
Any person found to be a sexually violent predator as that term is defined in C.R.S.
§ 18-3-414.5, as amended from time to time; or
2.
Any person who has been required to register under the Colorado Sex Offender Registration
Act found at C.R.S. § 16-22-101 et seq., as amended from time to time, and:
a.
Who has been convicted of a felony for an offense requiring registration where the
victim was under the age of 18 years;
b.
Who has multiple convictions for offenses requiring registration where at least one
victim was under the age of 18 years; or
c.
Whose offenses requiring registration involved multiple victims and at least one victim
was under the age of 18 years.
Youth populated area means:
1.
A private or public kindergarten, elementary, middle, junior high, or high school;
2.
A designated public or private school bus stop;
3.
A library open to the public;
4.
A playground open to the public;
5.
A youth center, defined as a facility where children up to the age of 17 come together
for programs and activities;
6.
A recreation facility open to the public, defined as an area, place, structure, or
other facility that is used either permanently or temporarily for community recreation,
even though it may be used for other purposes. "Recreation facility" includes, but
is not limited to, a gymnasium, playing court, playing field, and swimming pool;
7.
An arcade open to the public;
8.
A park or recreational trail open to the public or to all the residents of a private
community; or
9.
A licensed child-care facility or preschool.
( Ord. No. 6783 , § 1, 8-19-2025)
A.
It is unlawful for any registered sex offender to establish a permanent or temporary
residence within 1,000 feet of any youth populated area.
B.
It is unlawful to let or rent any portion of any property, place, structure, trailer
or other vehicle to a registered sex offender with the knowledge that it will be used
as a permanent or temporary residence in violation of this chapter.
C.
Any person who violates any provision of this chapter 9.10 shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and subject to the general penalty clause found
in chapter 1.12 .
( Ord. No. 6783 , § 1, 8-19-2025)
A.
A registered sex offender is not guilty of a violation of section 9.10.030.A if:
1.
The registered sex offender established the permanent or temporary residence prior
to the effective date of this chapter; provided, however, that this exception shall
not apply if the person committed an offense for which registration under the Colorado
Sex Offender Registration Act is required or was found to be a sexually violent predator
after the effective date of this chapter;
2.
The registered sex offender is placed in the residence pursuant to a State of Colorado
foster care program; or
3.
The youth populated area was opened or designated after the registered sex offender
established the permanent or temporary residence and does not replace an existing
youth populated area.
B.
A person is not guilty of a violation of section 9.10.030.B if:
1.
The person let or rented the property, room, place, structure, trailer or other living
space to the registered sex offender prior to the effective date of this chapter;
2.
The person let or rented the property, room, place, structure, trailer or other living
space to a registered sex offender pursuant to a state-licensed foster care program;
or
3.
The youth populated area was opened or designated after the person let or rented the
property, room, place, structure, trailer or other living space to the registered
sex offender.
( Ord. No. 6783 , § 1, 8-19-2025)
For purposes of determining the minimum distance separation required by this chapter,
the measurement shall be made by following a straight line from the outer property
line of the property on which the youth populated area is located to the nearest point
on the outer property line of the property on which the permanent or temporary residence
is located.
( Ord. No. 6783 , § 1, 8-19-2025)
