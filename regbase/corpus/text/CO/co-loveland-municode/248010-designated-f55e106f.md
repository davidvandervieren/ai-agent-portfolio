There is created a department of community services which shall be under the direction
of a director of community services. It shall be the director's duty to organize,
direct and manage the provision of transportation and building services, including
the following service areas: traffic operation, engineering, solid waste, street maintenance,
building inspection, code enforcement, development center operation, and current planning.
The director shall perform such other functions as may be prescribed by the city manager.
(Ord. 3975 § 9, 1994; Ord. 2072 § 1, 1982; Ord. 1707 § 1 (part), 1978)
