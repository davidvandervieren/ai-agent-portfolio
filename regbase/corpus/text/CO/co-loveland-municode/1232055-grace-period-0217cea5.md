The purpose of this chapter is to protect the health, safety and welfare of the City
by licensing persons who cut, prune or remove trees in the City. This Chapter provides
that when property owners hire a person engaged in the business of cutting, pruning,
or removing any trees above 15 feet from ground level, that person must possess a
City license and shall have adequate qualifications to perform the work.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
The City Manager, or a designee, possesses the authority to promulgate reasonable
rules and regulations concerning the business of cutting, pruning, removing any trees,
or to effectuate the intent of any Section within this Chapter. Any such rules and
regulations shall be made available to the public for inspection and shall be conspicuously
posted on the City's website.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
Certified arborist means a licensed tree contractor who meets the qualifications set forth in Subsections
A. or B. of Section 12.32.050 .
Licensed tree contractor means a professional engaged in the business of any type of tree work such as cutting,
pruning, or removing any trees above 15 feet by any means including ladders, climbing
and aerial lift equipment on public or private property within the City.
Supervise means to oversee or direct the work of another, but does not require that the individual
perform the work or be present while the work is performed.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
A.
To qualify as a "certified arborist," an applicant shall show proof of current International
Society of Arboriculture (ISA) Certified Tree Worker Climber Specialist Certification
or an equivalent exam. An employee or individual conducting any tree work such as
cutting, pruning, or removing any trees above 15 feet, must be supervised by a "certified
arborist" for compliance with the requirements of this Section 12.32.050 .
B.
The City Manager, or a designee, may approve an application to be a "certified arborist"
for persons that have demonstrated equivalent qualifications to those described in
Subsection A. above.
C.
To qualify as a "licensed tree contractor" a person must meet all of the requirements
of this chapter, and the qualifications required under Subsections A. or B. of this
Section.
D.
Nothing in this Section shall be construed to create a limitation on the ability of
a property owner to care for and maintain their own trees and shrubs. However, any
such care or maintenance of trees and shrubs shall be in conformity with the rules,
regulations, and standards of the City.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
Any former or current licensee of the City who has applied for and has been granted
a tree trimming license under Chapter 12.32 of the Loveland Municipal Code in the year 2021, or any former or current licensee
of the City who has applied for and has been granted a tree trimming license under Chapter 12.32 by December 1, 2022, who does not meet the qualification standards set forth in Section 12.32.050 , may continue to operate, subject to an annual application process as determined
by the City Clerk, without meeting the qualification standards in Section 12.32.050 until December 31, 2024.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
A.
No person shall engage in the business of cutting, pruning, or removing any trees
that require work above 15 feet from ground level, or the removal of any trees that
are greater than 15 feet, on public or private property within the City without employing
a person to supervise such work who is a licensee under this Chapter.
B.
All tree related work in the City must be performed in accordance with all requirements
adopted by the City Manager, or a designee, and all City, state and federal laws.
Any licensee not complying with the requirements of this Chapter may have their license
suspended or revoked consistent with this Chapter.
C.
All motor vehicles and other equipment used by any licensee hereunder in conducting
the licensed tree services must be conspicuously identified with the licensee's business
name and phone number or the business name and phone number of the licensee's employer.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
A.
Failure to adhere to any of the standards established by the City for engaging in
the business of cutting, pruning, or removing any trees on public or private property
within the City may result in revocation. In addition, the City Manager may deny a
tree contractor license or revoke a tree contractor license when:
1.
The applicant has failed to supply any of the information required on the application;
2.
The applicant has provided false information or misrepresented a material fact in
connection with an application;
3.
The applicant has failed to obtain required insurance;
4.
The applicant has failed to pay the required license fee;
5.
The applicant has within the past three years, from application date, violated a law
or condition in a license governing the activities permitted by the license;
6.
The applicant has previously unlawfully conducted activities that require a permit
or license without obtaining such permission in advance;
7.
The applicant had a City issued license revoked within the past three years;
8.
The applicant is not qualified by experience, training, or education to engage in
the activity authorized by the license; or
9.
The applicant has been finally convicted of an offense and would create danger to
the public health, safety, or welfare if the applicant were to engage in such offensive
conduct after the license were issued.
B.
A licensee under this Chapter is responsible for all work performed under each contract
executed pursuant to the license, whether the contractor, an employee or a subcontractor
performs the work.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
Nothing in this Chapter shall be deemed to prohibit the City Manager, or a designee,
from imposing other penalties authorized by this Code or other ordinance of the City,
including filing a complaint in the Municipal Court for a violation of this Code or
other ordinance of the City.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
An applicant for a tree contractor license shall:
A.
Apply as a qualified person consistent with Section 12.32.050 on forms furnished by the City Clerk;
B.
Pay the fee as provided in Section 12.32.110 ; and
C.
Provide evidence of insurance coverage as follows:
1.
Commercial general liability insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000.00. The City
shall be named as an additional insured; and
2.
Workers' compensation and employers' liability insurance. Applicants shall maintain
coverage as required by statute; and
3.
Auto liability insurance with minimum limits of $150,000.00 combined single limit;
4.
For all coverages, the City Clerk shall be notified of cancellation within 30 days,
ten days if due to non-payment of premium. Upon notification of cancellation, the
license is cancelled and the holder of the license shall do no further work until
such time as adequate insurance is provided.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
A.
No licensee under this Chapter shall conduct tree related work within ten feet of
a primary or secondary power line unless the licensee has contacted and obtained the
approval of the City's Power Division prior to the initiation of the tree related
work.
B.
No licensee under this Chapter shall conduct tree related work within the City's right-of-way
unless the licensee has obtained an appropriate right-of-way permit from the Public
Works Engineering Division.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
No license shall be issued thereunder until the applicant has paid the license fee
of $50.00 for original license and $25.00 for the renewal of a license. Every license
issued hereunder expires December 31 of the year in which the license was issued.
Renewal applications may be submitted at the office of the City Clerk at any time
within 30 days of the date the license will expire.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
A.
It shall be the duty of the owner of any property within the City to cut, trim, prune
or remove as necessary any trees, shrubs, or other vegetation located upon the property
of such owner in order to provide for the safe and convenient use of streets, sidewalks,
alleys or other City-owned property in accordance with the following requirements:
1.
Vegetation growth must not encroach upon the plane of a public sidewalk or street;
2.
Tree branch growth must be maintained at a height no lower than eight feet over a
public sidewalk and not less than 14 feet over the travel lanes of a street or alley;
3.
To ensure the safety of motorists on adjacent streets, sight distance triangles pursuant
to Section 18.05.02.02 must be maintained to protect visual clearances as required
by the Traffic Engineer;
4.
Branches, trees, shrubs and other vegetation must not obscure street name signs or
official traffic control devices;
5.
Branches or trees that are broken, hanging, decayed or otherwise defective in any
way that threatens public property or the safe use thereof must be removed.
B.
Nothing in this Section shall be construed as requiring the owner of a private property
to trim or remove any trees or shrubs when such trimming or removing is required to
prevent interference with City utility lines. It shall be the duty of the City to
do any cutting, trimming, pruning or removing of trees or shrubs necessary for the
safe use of City utilities.
C.
Should a property owner fail to maintain trees, shrubs or other vegetation in the
manner required by Subsection A. of this Section, the City's Urban Forester, or designee,
may require that such work be done by notice served upon the owner of such property
in accordance with Section 12.32.170. Such work must be performed within the time
period specified in such notice.
D.
If the Traffic Engineer reasonably determines a branch, tree, shrub or other vegetation,
or any other object on private property obscures an official traffic control device
or traffic or directional sign or view of traffic within a sight distance triangle,
or otherwise constitutes a hazard to drivers or pedestrians, the Traffic Engineer
may, in his or her reasonable discretion and at the City's expense, work with the
City's Urban Forester, or designee, to immediately remove such obstruction within
the public right-of-way without notice to the property owner.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
It shall be the duty of the owner of any property to remove any dead trees, dead or
hanging limbs, infected, or infested trees consistent with Chapter 16.28 of this Code. In the event a dead or dangerous tree is located upon the property
of one or more property owners, both property owners shall be jointly and severally
liable for the removal of the dead or dangerous tree. Should the City seek removal
of the dead or dangerous tree pursuant to Section 12.32.170, the City shall provide
for a good faith apportionment of the cost to so remove the dead, dangerous, infected,
or infested tree between relevant property owners.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
The City's Urban Forester, or a designee, may enforce any provision of this Chapter 12.32 or Chapter 16.28 of this Code through the issuance of a summons and complaint into Loveland Municipal
Court, the issuance of a notice of abatement, or any other lawful means not expressly
prohibited by the Loveland Municipal Code.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
In conjunction with Section 16.28.070 , whenever the condition of any tree, shrub, or other vegetation interferes with,
obstructs, or in any other way endangers the safe public use of streets, alleys, sidewalks,
or other public places consistent with Subsection 12.32.135.A of this Code, the City
shall have the authority, at its own expense, to treat, trim, remove, or otherwise
care for trees, shrubs, or other vegetation causing the interference, obstruction,
or endangerment. Conditions warranting City action under this Section would include,
but not be limited to, obstructions which affect the operation and maintenance of
utility facilities, signs, or traffic control devices, and interference within the
sight distance triangle pursuant to Section 18.05.02.02.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
In order to accomplish the purposes of this Section, the City or its authorized representative
is authorized to go upon any property in the City for the purpose of inspecting trees,
shrubs or other plants.
(Ord. No. 6597 , § 1, 12-6-2022)
The imposition of any penalty for a violation of this Chapter 12.32 or Chapter 16.28 shall not be construed as a waiver of the right of the City to collect the cost of
removal or treatment of any tree, shrub or other plant in accordance with provisions
of this Section.
