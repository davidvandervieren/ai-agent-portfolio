Note— Prior history: Prior code § 4.15. Ords. 1337 and 877.
There is created a library department which shall be under the direction of a library
director. The director shall be responsible for the administration of all affairs
necessary for the operation and maintenance of the city library. The director shall
perform such other duties as may be assigned by the city manager and shall consult
with the library board regarding the establishment of policies for the operation of
the library.
(Ord. 3975 § 13, 1994)
