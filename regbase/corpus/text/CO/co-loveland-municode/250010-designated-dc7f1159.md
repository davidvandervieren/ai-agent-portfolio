There is created a department of employee relations which shall be under the direction
of a director of employee relations. It shall be the director's duty to organize,
direct and manage the hiring, discharging, and general supervision of city employees
and shall perform such other functions as prescribed by the city manager.
(Ord. 3975 § 11, 1994; Ord. 3480 § 7, 1987; Ord. 3434 § 3, 1987; Ord. 2072 § 3, 1982;
Ord. 1707 § 1 (part), 1978)
