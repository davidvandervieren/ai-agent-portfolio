State Law reference— Deposits and investments of city funds, C.R.S. § 31-20-303.
A.
There is created and established a capital improvement fund in which all moneys from
the sale of the Fort Collins-Loveland Municipal Airport and any other improvements
which may be sold by the city shall be placed.
B.
Expenditures from the fund shall be made only for capital improvements and by order
of the city council.
C.
The moneys in the fund, except as needed, shall be kept invested by the city treasurer
in such securities as are approved by the state for fiduciary and trust funds.
(Ord. 5733 § 4, 2012; Ord. 762 § 1, 1962; prior code § 6.12)
