There is created a department of parks and recreation which shall be under the direction
of a director of parks and recreation. It shall be the director's duty to organize,
direct and manage the city's parks, recreation programs and facilities, cemeteries,
golf courses, and recreation trails system. The director shall perform such other
functions as may be prescribed by the city manager.
(Ord. 3975 § 12, 1994)
