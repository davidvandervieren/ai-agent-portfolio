A.
No write-in vote for any municipal office shall be counted unless an affidavit of
intent has been filed with the municipal clerk by the person whose name is written
in prior to 20 days before the day of the election indicating that such person desires
the office and is qualified to assume the duties of that office if elected.
B.
If the only matter before the voters is the election of persons to office and if,
at the close of business on the 19th day before the election, there are not more candidates
than offices to be filled at such election, including candidates filing affidavits
of intent, the city clerk, if instructed by resolution of the city council either
before or after such date, shall cancel the election, and city council shall, by resolution,
declare the candidates elected. Upon such declaration, the candidates shall be deemed
elected. Upon the cancellation of the election, the city clerk shall publish notice
of cancellation, if possible, and shall post such notice at each polling place and
in not less than one other public place.
(Ord. 4091 § 1, 1995; Ord. 3768 § 1, 1991; Ord. 3396 § 1, 1987)
Where a vacancy or vacancies exist in the office of council member from a ward, and
a successor or successors are to be elected at the next regular election to fill the
unexpired term or terms, the candidate from that ward receiving the highest number
of votes shall be elected to a four-year term and the candidate from that ward receiving
the next highest number of votes shall be elected to fill the unexpired term.
(Ord. 4505 § 2, 2000; Ord. 1212 § 2, 1972; Ord. 931 § 4, 1965; prior code § 3.4)
A.
The provisions of this section supplement the recall provisions of the city charter
and Title 31 of the Colorado Revised Statutes, implement certain disclosure provisions
of Title 1 of the Colorado Revised Statutes, and shall apply to all members of the
city council as the local governing body.
B.
Prior to submitting a petition for approval as to form, the recall committee shall
notify the city clerk of the committee's intent to file a petition for approval as
to form. Within three business days of receiving the notice, the city clerk shall
provide the committee with an estimate of the cost of conducting the recall election.
The total estimated cost of conducting the recall election shall be comprised of the
direct estimated cost of conducting the recall election that excludes city staff time
and the estimated cost of city staff time of conducting the recall election. No petition
shall be approved as to form unless it lists the direct estimated cost, the estimated
cost of city staff time and the total cost.
C.
No petition shall include profane or false statements.
D.
Upon submission of a petition for recall of any member of city council that includes
the estimated cost of the recall election as set forth above, the city clerk shall
disapprove the petition and, within three business days, notify the committee and
the officer who is the subject of the recall and provide the officer with a copy of
the proposed petition. The notification shall occur by regular first-class mail and,
if an email address is provided by the committee, email and shall provide the specific
reasons for disapproval.
1.
Within five days of the date of the notification, the officer may submit to the city
clerk a statement of no more than 300 words of justification for the officer's course
of office that contains no profanity or falsehoods. The electors of the municipality
shall be the sole and exclusive judges of the statement's legality, reasonableness
and sufficiency, and the statement shall not be open to review.
2.
If the officer timely submits the statement, the city clerk shall send forthwith by
regular first-class mail and, if an email address is provided by the committee, email
a copy of the statement to the committee for inclusion in the petition. If the officer
does not timely submit the statement, the city clerk shall notify the committee forthwith
in the same manner.
E.
The committee shall have three business days from date of the city clerk's notice
regarding the statement to resubmit the petition. If the committee does not timely
resubmit the petition, the petition shall be considered a new petition. If the officer
does not timely submit a statement, the statement shall not be a requirement for approval
of the petition. If the officer timely submits a statement, the petition shall not
be approved unless the statement is included in the petition and each section thereof
immediately following the committee's general statement of the grounds on which the
recall is sought. The city clerk shall have three business days to approve or disapprove
the petition resubmitted pursuant to this paragraph E.
(Ord. No. 6541 , § 1, 7-5-2022)
