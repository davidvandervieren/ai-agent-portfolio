Editor's note— Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, adopted Oct. 6, 2020, repealed the former Ch. 2.70, §§ 2.70.010—2.70.100, and
enacted a new Ch. 2.70 as set out herein. The former Ch. 2.70 was entitled "Pension
Plan," and derived from: Prior code §§ 4.33—4.33-8; Ord. 1320 § 1 (part), 1973; Ord.
1554 § 1, 1977; Ord. 1729 § 1, 1978; Ord. 1735 § 1, 1978; Ord. 2083 § 1 (part), 1983;
Ord. 3268 § 2, 1986; Ord. 3781 § 1, 1991; and Ord. 3854 §§ 1, 2, 1992.
The City establishes a defined contribution retirement plan for the employees of the
city pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-54-101 et seq. The plan will be known as the "City of
Loveland Employees' Retirement Plan." The City may offer additional retirement plans
as an employee benefit.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
The retirement plan shall not apply to or include: Sworn Police Personnel employed
by the city; or other employees of the city who are eligible for exemption from the
plan and who are exempted from the plan by resolution of the city council.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
The retirement plan shall be funded by contributions from the city and from the employees
covered by the plan. Each employee will contribute to the plan an amount equal to
three percent of his or her base salary. The city shall contribute to the plan an
amount equal to five percent of the base salary of each employee covered by the plan.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
Duties and Responsibilities:
The management of the city employees' retirement plan shall be vested in an employees'
board of retirement, which board is hereby established. The Board shall determine
plan fees or revenues sufficient to cover all plan expenses. Membership on the board
shall consist of the Chief Financial Officer or designee, Human Resources Director
or designee, two current employee plan participants elected by an employee vote, and
a fifth member who is a previous employee and active plan participant or an individual
who is a taxpaying elector of the City appointed by the Board. The Board shall by
its own rules establish staggered four year terms for its members, other than the
Chief Financial Officer and Human Resources Director.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
There is established an "employees' retirement fund." Contributions from the city
and from city employees to the retirement plan shall be paid into the fund. Management
of this fund is vested in the employees' board of retirement.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
All moneys forfeited by participants in the city employees' retirement plan due to
termination or change of status in employment prior to full vesting in the employee
shall be first applied to plan expenses and the remainder transferred to the city's
retirement plan funding obligations.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
A.
There shall be paid to the fund established in Section 2.70.050 , an additional sum equal to the percentage of base salary set forth in the following
table according to the number of years of employment with the city completed:

No. of Years Completed | Up Through Year | Percentage of Base Salary
7 | 10 | 1%
10 | 15 | 2%
15 | 20 | 3%
20 or more | Retirement | 4%

B.
Upon retirement after completion of not less than 25 years of employment with the
city, there shall be paid to each such employee a one-time payment of $100.00 times
the number of complete years of employment with the city. For the purposes of this
subsection, employment with the city means permanent, full-time employment, and any
interruptions in employment other than for vacation and sick leave shall not count
in computing the number of years.
The benefits set forth in this section shall not apply to or include fire or police
personnel employed by the city.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
There is established an alternative police retirement program as an employee benefit.
( Ord. No. 6426 , § 1, 10-6-2020)
