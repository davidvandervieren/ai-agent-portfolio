The International Energy Conservation Code, 2021 Edition (the "2021 IECC"), issued
and published by the International Code Council, 4051 West Flossmoor Road, Country
Club Hills, IL 60478-5795, is hereby adopted by reference as the energy conservation
code of the City of Loveland as if fully set forth herein, with the modifications,
if any, set forth in Section 15.48.020 below. This code is a complete code to safeguard public health, safety and welfare
by regulating and governing energy efficient building envelopes and installation of
energy efficient mechanical, light, and power systems and providing for issuance of
permits and collection of fees therefore. At least one copy of the 2021 IECC, which
has been certified by the Mayor and City Clerk, shall be on file in the office of
the City Clerk and may be inspected during regular business hours.
(Ord. No. 6590 , § 1, 11-15-2022; Ord. No. 6377 , § 1, 12-15-2020)
The International Energy Conservation Code, 2021 Edition, adopted in this chapter,
is modified as follows:
A.
Section C101.1 and R101.1 - Title - is amended to read as follows:
These regulations shall be known as the International Energy Conservation Code of
the City of Loveland, hereinafter referred to as "this code".
B.
Sections C103.1 and R103.1 - Construction documents - are amended to read as follows:
Construction documents and other supporting data shall be submitted electronically
with each application for a permit. The construction documents shall be prepared by
a registered design professional where required by the statutes of the jurisdiction
in which the project is to be constructed. Where special conditions exist, the code
official is authorized to require necessary construction documents to be prepared
by a registered design professional.
C.
Sections C104 and R104 - Fees - are deleted in their entirety.
D.
Sections C109 and R109 - Stop work orders - are deleted in their entirety.
E.
Sections C110 and R110 - Means of appeals - are deleted in their entirety.
F.
Section R401.2.1 - Prescriptive Compliance Option - is amended to read as follows:
The Prescriptive Compliance Option. Requires compliance with Sections R401 through R404.
For new single-family dwellings, two (2) family dwellings, townhomes, and R-2, R-3,
and R-4 occupancies three (3) stories or less, the Prescriptive Compliance Option
shall not be a compliance option.
G.
Section R401.2.2 - Total Building Performance Option - is amended to read as follows:
The Total Building Performance Option requires compliance with Section R405.
For new single-family dwellings, two (2) family dwellings, townhomes, and R-2, R-3,
and R-4 occupancies three (3) stories or less, the Simulated Performance Alternative
(Performance) Option shall be a performance option.
H.
Section R401.2.3 - Energy Rating Index Option - is amended to read as follows:
The Energy Rating Index (ERI) Option requires compliance with Section R406.
For new single-family dwellings, two (2) family dwellings, townhomes, and R-2, R-3,
and R-4 occupancies three (3) stories or less, the Energy Rating Index (ERI) Option
shall be an option.

It shall be unlawful for any person, firm or corporation to erect, construct, enlarge,
alter, repair, move, improve, remove, convert or demolish, equip, use, occupy or maintain
any building or structure in the City of Loveland or cause the same to be done contrary
to or in violation of any of the provisions of this code, as adopted and modified
by the City of Loveland. Any person, firm or corporation violating any of the provisions
of this code, as adopted and modified by the City of Loveland, shall be deemed guilty
of a misdemeanor and subject to penalties as set forth in Section 1.12.010 of the City of Loveland Municipal Code.
(Ord. No. 6590 , § 1, 11-15-2022; Ord. No. 6377 , § 1, 12-15-2020; Ord. 5779 § 1, 2013; Ord. 5602 § 1, 2011; Ord. 5241 § 1, 2007)
