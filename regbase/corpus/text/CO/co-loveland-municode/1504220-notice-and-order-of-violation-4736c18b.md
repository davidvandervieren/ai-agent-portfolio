A.
The purpose of Title 15 is to provide minimum standards for the construction, alteration, moving, demolition,
repairs as defined by this code, and maintenance of all buildings, structures, and
portions thereof within the City of Loveland.
B.
The minimum standards of this Title are intended to provide a reasonable level of
protection for buildings, structures, and the public and are not intended to guarantee
safety to properties, structures, or the public. It is not the purpose of this Title
to create or otherwise establish or designate any particular class or group of persons
who will or should be especially protected or benefited by the terms of this code
(as defined below) and the Adopted Construction Codes (as defined below).
C.
The purpose of this Chapter is to prescribe the administrative procedures for this
code and the Adopted Construction Codes.
( Ord. No. 6370 , § 1, 12-15-2020)
A.
The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all regulations set forth in this Title 15 , including the Adopted Construction Codes.
B.
The provisions of this code shall apply to the construction, alteration, moving, demolition,
repairs as defined by this code, and maintenance of any building or structure within
this jurisdiction, except work located primarily in a public way, public utility towers
and poles, mechanical equipment not specifically regulated in this Title, and hydraulic
flood control structures.
C.
When, in any specific case, different sections of this code specify different materials,
methods of construction, or other requirements, the most restrictive shall govern.
Where there is a conflict between a general requirement and a specific requirement,
the specific requirement shall be applicable.
D.
In the event of a conflict among different provisions of this code, or a conflict
between the provisions of this code and any other City ordinance, the provisions which
impose the highest standard shall prevail.
E.
In the event of a conflict between the provisions of this code and any state or federal
law, the provisions which impose the highest standard shall prevail.
F.
The use of the term "Adopted Construction Codes" shall refer to all of the provisions
of all of the codes adopted by reference in this Title 15 . The use of the words "this code" in this chapter and elsewhere in Title 15 where a different definition is not provided refers to the entirety of Title 15 and the Adopted Construction Codes.
( Ord. No. 6370 , § 1, 12-15-2020)
In the event that any part or provision of this Title or the Adopted Construction
Codes is held to be illegal or void, this shall not have the effect of making void
or illegal any of the other parts or provisions.

A.
General.
1.
The position of Building Official is created. The Building Official shall be the chief
enforcement officer for all building regulations contained in this Title, including
the various Adopted Construction Codes, but excluding the International Fire Code
and Chapter 7 of the International Property Maintenance Code, which are enforced by
others as described in this Title, (collectively the "Building Regulations"). The
Building Official shall be considered the "building code official" or "code official"
as referenced in the Building Regulations.
2.
The Building Official may appoint plans examiners, building inspectors, other technical
officers, inspectors and assistants as authorized by the City Manager. All such employees
shall have powers as delegated by the Building Official.
3.
The Building Official and his or her duly authorized administrators are authorized
and directed to enforce the Building Regulations. The Building Official is authorized
to enforce the Building Regulations through the issuance of summonses and complaints
into the Loveland Municipal Court. The Building Official may appoint building code
enforcement officers to enforce the Building Regulations, including enforcement through
issuance of summonses and complaints into the Loveland Municipal Court.
4.
The Building Official shall have the power to render interpretations of this Title
and to adopt and enforce rules, supplemental regulations and policies in order to
clarify the application of its provisions. Such interpretations, rules, regulations
and policies shall be in conformance with the intent and purpose of this code and
the Adopted Construction Codes.
B.
Applications and permits. The Building Official shall receive applications; review
construction documents and issue permits for the erection, and alteration, demolition
and moving of buildings and structures; inspect the premises for which such permits
have been issued; and enforce compliance with the provisions of this Title and the
Building Regulations.
C.
Inspections. The Building Official shall make the required inspections, or the Building
Official shall have the authority to accept reports of inspection by approved agencies
or individuals.
D.
Department records. The Building Official shall keep official records of applications
received, permits and certificates issued, fees collected, reports of inspections,
and notices and orders issued.

For existing buildings located in flood hazard areas for which repairs, alterations
and additions constitute substantial improvement, the Building Official shall not
grant modifications to provisions related to flood resistance unless a determination
is made that:
A.
The applicant has presented good and sufficient cause that the unique characteristics
of the size, configuration or topography of the site render compliance with the flood-resistant
construction provisions inappropriate;
B.
Failure to grant the modification would result in exceptional hardship;
C.
The granting of the modification will not result in increased flood heights, additional
threats to public safety, extraordinary public expense nor create nuisances, cause
fraud on or victimization of the public or conflict with existing laws or ordinances;

below the design flood elevation increases risks to life and property.

No permit issued - when. No permit shall be issued to any person who is delinquent on the payment of any fees

Time limit for procuring. All permits issued under this Title must be procured within 90 days after notification

A.
Purpose. The purpose of this section is to promote the public health, safety and welfare by
requiring that persons erecting, constructing, enlarging, altering, repairing, moving,
removing or converting buildings and other structures in the City are licensed for
such activities and have shown that they are qualified to perform such services by
experience or examination.
B.
Definitions. For purposes of this section, the following terms shall have the meaning indicated:
1.
Building Official shall mean the chief enforcement officer as provided under Section 15.04.050 or his or her designee, who shall serve as the licensing official for all contractor
licenses except for S-4 and S-5 licenses, for which the Fire Marshal shall be the
licensing official.
2.
Contractor means any person or entity who undertakes, within the City, to perform any work on
any building or structure or any portion thereof for which a permit is required by
this Title.
3.
License means a license issued pursuant to this section.
4.
Registration means a registration issued to an electrician or plumber pursuant to this section
who is also licensed as an electrician or a plumber by the State of Colorado.
5.
Remodel means to alter, enlarge, demolish or replace.
C.
License required.
1.
No contractor shall perform any work without having first obtained the required license
to perform such work from the chief Building Official under this section.
2.
No contractor that is not a natural person shall perform any work without having in
its employ a natural person licensed as a contractor under this chapter for the type
of work performed. The name of such person shall be registered with the Building Official.
If such person should leave the employ of the licensee, the license shall be deemed
suspended until another licensed natural person is registered with the Building Official.
Such person shall provide personal supervision at the work, job or project site adhering
to reasonable attention to the job site to ensure proper construction as determined
by the Building Official.
3.
Each licensee under this section shall be issued an identification card and shall
present such card upon request of any City representative.
4.
The following persons are exempt from the licensing requirements of this section:
a.
A person working under the direct supervision of any other person licensed in the
relevant trade under this chapter;
b.
A homeowner who builds, constructs, alters, repairs, adds to or demolishes any building
or structure or any portion thereof that constitutes the owner's residence, or a building
or structure accessory thereto, and that is intended for the owner's personal use.
This section shall not apply to the installation or repair of fire alarms and fire
sprinklers.
c.
A public utility company and its employees, when engaged in the installation, operation
and maintenance of its equipment used for the production, generation or distribution
of the utility, product or service through the facilities owned or operated by the
public utility company to the point of customer service.
D.
License applications, qualifications, and changes.
1.
An applicant may apply for a license on forms furnished by the Building Official,
and shall provide information relating to the applicant's competence, experience and
job references as required, insurance coverages as required, and pay the fees as set
forth herein.
2.
No license shall be issued to an applicant until the applicant has successfully passed
an examination to test the applicant's qualifications for the category of license
requested, as required by Section 15.04.200.H. In order to be eligible for exemption
from the examination requirement, an applicant shall demonstrate competency in the
licensing category applied for in Section 15.04.200.G satisfactory to the Building
Official.
3.
Every licensee shall report a change of name or address in writing to the Building
Official not later than 14 business days after the change.
4.
A licensee may elect to have his license become inactive by notifying the Building
Official. No one shall perform work with an inactive license. Thereafter, no further
fees shall be required until the licensee's license is reactivated.
5.
A licensee may upgrade a license to a broader classification by submitting a new application,
successfully completing the examination if required, and paying the difference between
the fees paid and the fee for the new license.
E.
Renewal. A licensee under this section shall renew his or her license every two years, unless
otherwise required by the State of Colorado, by filing a completed application for
renewal with the Building Official and paying the fees required in Section 15.04.200.I.
Approval of a renewal application designates the beginning of the renewal period.
Licenses issued by the City of Loveland which have been expired or inactive for more
than 90 days may be renewed upon providing evidence of passing the examination in
the appropriate category of licensure under Section 15.04.200.G.
F.
Insurance. Every applicant for a license or renewal license under this section shall present
to the Building Official for review a valid certificate of insurance at the time of
application. It shall be the duty of every licensee to continually maintain valid
liability insurance. The minimum required general aggregate liability shall be $1,000,000.00
with $300,000.00 fire damage and $1,000,000.00 each occurrence.
G.
Registration and classification of licenses. The following licenses or registrations are available pursuant to this section:
1.
Standard General Building Contractor (A). This license entitles the licensee to construct, remodel or repair any structure
or addition thereto for which a building permit is required. This license does not
allow work that includes electrical, plumbing, mechanical, signs, fire and burglar
alarms, fire protection, and trades outside the license holder's area of expertise.
2.
Standard Building Contractor (B). This license entitles the licensee to construct, remodel or repair commercial buildings
and single or multi-dwelling buildings not exceeding three stories in height for which
a building permit is required. This license is limited to allow work on non-residential
tenant finish as well as residential structures which contain fewer than sixteen dwelling
units. This license does not allow work that includes electrical, plumbing, mechanical,
signs, fire and burglar alarms, fire protection, and other trades outside the license
holder's area of expertise.
3.
Standard Residential Building Contractor (C). This license entitles the licensee to construct, remodel or repair any residential
building subject to the International Residential Code, for which a building permit
is required. This license does not allow work that includes electrical, plumbing,
mechanical, signs, fire and burglar alarms, fire protection, and other trades outside
the license holder's area of expertise.
4.
Class S, Specialty License. These licenses entitle the licensee to perform only the type or types of work described
below:
a.
S-1: Roofing Contractor/Subcontractor. Installation and replacement of roof coverings.
b.
S-2: Swimming Pools and Spas. Installation of swimming pools and spas.
c.
S-3: Signs. Fabrication, erection, installation, remodeling, repair and maintenance of all types
of signs.
d.
S-4: Fire Alarms. Installation of fire and burglar alarms.
e.
S-5: Fire Suppression. Installation of fire extinguishing systems.
f.
Other: The Building Official shall have the authority to grant other specialty licenses
as the Building Official determines appropriate in his or her sole discretion.
5.
Standard Master Mechanical. This license entitles the holder to install, maintain, alter, or repair mechanical
systems that are permanently installed and utilized to provide control of environmental
conditions and related process within buildings. This shall include evaporative refrigeration
systems and related appurtenances.
6.
Electrical Contractor Registration. In order to be registered as an electrical contractor as required herein, the applicant
must also be licensed as a master electrician by the State of Colorado Electrical
Board and possess an electrical contractor's license issued by the State of Colorado
Electrical Board. Registration as an electrical contractor under this chapter entitles
the registrant to plan, layout, supervise and install, add to, alter and repair wiring
apparatus and equipment for electric light, heat and power, except that such registration
does not entitle the registrant to plan or design commercial electrical installations,
which require a professional engineer. A registered professional engineer who plans
or designs electrical installation shall not be considered an electrical contractor.
7.
Plumbing Contractor Registration. In order to be registered as a plumbing contractor as required herein, the applicant
must be licensed as a master plumber by the State Examining Board of Plumbers and
possess a plumbing contractor license issued by the State of Colorado Examining Board
of Plumbers. Registration as a plumbing contractor under this section entitles the
registrant to plan, lay out, supervise, install, add to, alter and repair potable
water supplies and distribution pipes and piping, plumbing fixtures and traps, drainage
and vent pipes, and building drains, including their respective joints and connections,
devices, receptacles and appurtenances, except that such registration does not entitle
the registrant to plan or design commercial plumbing installations, which require
a professional engineer. A registered professional engineer who plans or designs plumbing
installations shall not be considered a plumbing contractor.
H.
License examination.
1.
The Building Official shall issue a license to contractors who have passed the International
Code Council (ICC) examination or an examination which is equivalent to the ICC examination
and that meet all other requirements of this Chapter.
2.
The Building Official shall have the discretion to accept the results of an examination
given in another jurisdiction which is equivalent to the ICC examination. Before a
license is issued, all other requirements of this Chapter must be met.
3.
Examinations are required for the following licenses:
a.
Standard General Building Contractor (A);
b.
Standard Building Contractor (B);
c.
Standard Residential Building Contractor (C);
d.
Standard Master Mechanical;
e.
S-1 Roofing Contractor/Subcontractor; and
f.
S-3 Signs.
I.
License fees. License fees shall be as adopted by City Council annually in the City's rates, charges
and fees.
J.
Contractor responsibilities.
1.
A licensee is responsible for all work performed pursuant to a license issued by the
City under this section, including work performed by an individual working under the
licensee.
2.
A licensee is responsible for obtaining permits prior to performing any work on a
site.
3.
Every Class (A), (B), (C) and (S) licensee holder as described in Section 15.04.200.G,
shall inform the Building Official at the time of application for a permit to be issued,
of the major subcontractors working on the project, if any, including but not limited
to, plumbing, electrical and mechanical subcontractors, and shall engage only subcontractors
who are properly licensed under this chapter.
K.
Denial of a license.
1.
The Building Official may deny a license under this chapter upon a finding of any
of the following:
a.
The applicant has failed to provide information requested on the application form;
b.
The applicant is not qualified by experience, training or education to engage in the
activity authorized by the license;
c.
The applicant's license for the same or similar work is under suspension or revocation
in this or another jurisdiction; or
d.
The applicant has been convicted of an offense relating to the requested license.
2.
If the Building Official denies a license application under this section, he or she
shall notify the applicant in writing stating the specific grounds for the denial.
The applicant may thereafter appeal the denial of the application to a hearing officer
appointed by the Construction Advisory Board and obtain a hearing as described in
Section 15.04.200.L.1 by filing a notice of appeal with the Building Official within
15 days after the Building Official's notice of denial. The notice of appeal shall
state the basis of the appeal.
L.
Revocation and suspension of license.
1.
The Building Official shall, upon the verified complaint in writing of any person,
including the Building Official, that the licensee has violated any of the acts set
forth in Paragraph 2 below, require the licensee to appear before a hearing officer
appointed by the Construction Advisory Board on the possible suspension or revocation
of the licensee's license. The licensee shall be given a copy of the complaint and

2.
The following acts may result in the suspension or revocation of a licensee's license:
a.
The licensee has engaged in any fraudulent, deceptive, or dishonest act or practice
in performance of building, contracting or the trade in which the licensee is licensed;
or
b.
The licensee has violated any applicable provision of this section, the Adopted Construction
Codes, the Loveland Municipal Code, or state regulation or law; or
c.
The licensee has failed to maintain the requirements of licensure, including insurance
or sales tax license as required by the Building Official; or
d.
The licensee has failed to provide copies of records in his or her possession related
to a matter under review; or
e.
The licensee has failed to pay fees in a timely manner; or
f.
The licensee has failed to respond to a lawful order or directive of the Building
Official or other authorized representative of the City; or
g.
The licensee has failed to possess a valid state license for the particular trade,
if such a license is required by state law;
h.
The licensee has used the license or registration to obtain permits required under
the building codes for any other person;
i.
The licensee has misrepresented a material fact when applying for a license, or fraud
in obtaining a license;
j.
The licensee has been grossly negligence in the work done by the licensee;
k.
The licensee has failed to obtain a required permit;
l.
The licensee has failed to obtain the appropriate inspections prior to proceeding
with work;
m.
The licensee has failed to give written notice to the chief Building Official of any
matter for which notice is required by this chapter; or
n.
The licensee has been convicted of an offense involving misapplication of funds or
property received from another.
3.
The Construction Advisory Board hearing officer, after review of the evidence presented,
shall have the power to suspend or revoke the license or take other disciplinary action
on the license, including the issuance of a formal letter of reprimand; or order the
licensee to pay unpaid permit fees and inspection or investigative costs incurred
by the City; or impose a probationary period during which time any further violations
would result in automatic suspension or revocation of the license.
4.
Emergency Suspension. If the Building Official finds that an emergency exists which justifies immediate
suspension or revocation of a license, he or she may enter an order for immediate
suspension of such license, pending further investigation and proceedings for suspension
or revocation as provided in this chapter. The licensee may, upon notice of such suspension,
request an immediate hearing before the Building Official.
5.
The Building Official may suspend licenses upon the written consent and approval of
the licensee.

A.
Basis of appeal. A person may appeal a decision or other action of the Building Official or his or
her duly authorized representative or of a duly authorized code enforcement officer
to the Construction Advisory Board for the following types of decisions or actions:
(a) denial of a permit or certificate of occupancy; (b) revocation or suspension of
a permit or certificate of occupancy; (c) notice of abatement; (d) issuance of a stop
work order; and (e) determination of alternative materials and methods.
B.
Deadline for appeal. An appeal under Section A above must be filed in writing with the Building Official
no later than 15 calendar days after the decision or action has been taken. The notice
of appeal shall state the basis of the appeal.
C.
Public hearing. Every appeal shall be heard at a public hearing. Notice of the public hearing shall
be given at least 15 days in advance by publication of a notice of the public hearing
in a newspaper of general circulation in the City. At the appeal hearing, members
of the public, the appellant and City staff shall be entitled to address the appeals
board.
D.
Review on appeal. The Construction Advisory Board shall review the information supplied by the appellant
to the Building Official in determining the appeal. The Construction Advisory Board
may consider the following:
1.
Whether the decision or action of the Building Official or code enforcement officer
was not justified;
2.
Whether the material or methods of construction required are not reasonable for the
particular building;
3.
Whether the alternate materials and methods of construction proposed by the appellant
are sufficient to insure public health and safety; and
4.
Whether the requirements of the applicable provisions of this Title 15 would work an undue hardship upon the appellant.
E.
Decision on appeal. The Construction Advisory Board is authorized to overturn the decision or action
of the Building Official and, when doing so, may impose such conditions on the appellant
as it reasonably determines to be justified under the circumstances.

A.
If under the fire code adopted by this Title 15 a person is denied a permit, has a permit revoked, is issued an order to correct
or abate, or issued a stop work order by the Fire Marshal or his or her duly appointed
representative, such person may appeal the Fire Marshal's action to the Loveland Fire
Rescue Authority Board by filing a written notice of appeal to the Fire Marshal not
later than 15 days after the permit has been denied or revoked or 15 days after the
issuance of an order to correct or abate or stop work order, which notice shall specifically
state the appellant's grounds for appeal.
B.
Each appeal will be heard at a public hearing. Notice of the public hearing shall
be given at least 15 days in advance by publication of a notice of the public hearing
in a newspaper of general circulation in the City. At the appeal hearing, members
of the public, the appellant and the Loveland Fire Rescue Authority staff shall be
entitled to address the appeals board. The public hearing shall be recorded.
C.
The Loveland Fire Authority Board may authorize the issuance of a denied permit or
rescind the revocation of a permit, order to correct or abate, or a stop work order,
and when doing so may designate and impose such conditions as it may reasonably determine
to be justified under the circumstances only if the board determines the following:
1.
That the denial of the permit, the revocation of the permit, order to correct or abate
or the issuance of a stop work order is not justified under the applicable provisions
of the fire code; or
2.
That the alternative design, materials, or methods of construction proposed by the
appellant are equivalent to those prescribed by the applicable fire code provisions
concerning quality, strength, effectiveness, fire resistance, durability, safety and
all other pertinent factors and adequately protect the health safety or welfare of
the occupant, intended occupants, surrounding properties and the public generally;
or
3.
That the applicable requirements of the fire code would work an undue and unique hardship
upon the appellant. An appeal based on undue hardship must also include a statement
from the appellant specifying the nature and extent of the hardship; and
4.
That the issuance of the denied permit or the rescission of the revocation of a permit,
order to correct or abate, or stop work order will not unreasonably jeopardize the
health, safety and welfare of the occupant, intended occupants, surrounding properties
and the public generally.
