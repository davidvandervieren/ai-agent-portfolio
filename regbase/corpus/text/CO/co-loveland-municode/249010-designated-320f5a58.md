There is created a department of water and power which shall be under the direction
of a director of water and power. It shall be the director's duty to organize, direct
and manage the water, wastewater, stormwater, municipal fiber, electric production,
and electric distribution systems and plants of the city and the city's warehouse
operations. The director shall be responsible for all matters relating to construction,
management, maintenance, and operation of said systems and plants and shall perform
such other functions as may be prescribed by the city manager.
( Ord. No. 6389 , § 1, 5-5-2020; Ord. 3975 § 10, 1994; Ord. 2072 § 2, 1982)
