# Town Codes

<!-- heading-path: Town Codes > Building Codes -->
## Building Codes
The Building Codes for the Town of Johnstown establish the regulations and standards for the construction, alteration, and maintenance of buildings and structures within the town. These codes are designed to ensure the safety, health, and welfare of the community. Compliance with these building codes is mandatory for all construction projects within Johnstown. By adhering to these regulations, the town ensures that buildings are safe, sustainable, and suitable for use.

<!-- heading-path: Town Codes > Construction and Design Standards -->
## Construction and Design Standards
These Standards and Specifications set the acceptable standards for the design, construction, materials, use, and maintenance of public improvements. This includes streets, parks, open spaces, parking lots, utility installations, and related structures. All work must comply with these standards and be approved by the Town of Johnstown.

<!-- heading-path: Town Codes > Johnstown Municipal Code -->
## Johnstown Municipal Code
The Municipal Code of the Town of Johnstown consists of ordinances and bylaws that govern the municipality's operations and conduct of business. These ordinances cover various aspects such as public health and safety, zoning, budgeting rules, land use issues, and more. The Town's code is built upon the laws established by the State of Colorado.
The Town Council is responsible for voting on new ordinances or amendments to existing ones. To provide transparency and accessibility, the Town uses the Municode system to present ordinances and track their history. This historical information includes the dates of adoption, effectiveness, and any subsequent changes or removals.

<!-- heading-path: Town Codes > Land Use and Development Code -->
## Land Use and Development Code
The Land Use and Development Code of the Town of Johnstown establishes the regulations and guidelines for land use, development, and zoning within the town. This code is essential for ensuring orderly growth and development. It is designed to promote the health, safety, and general welfare of Johnstown's residents. By following these regulations, the town can preserve its character, improve quality of life, and ensure sustainable growth for future generations.
