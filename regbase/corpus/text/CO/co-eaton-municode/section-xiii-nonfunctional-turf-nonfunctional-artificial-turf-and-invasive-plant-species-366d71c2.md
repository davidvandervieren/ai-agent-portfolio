For purposes of this section:
(a)
The terms nonfunctional turf, nonfunctional artificial turf, invasive plant species, multifamily
residential housing premises property, new development project and redevelopment project shall have the meanings set forth in § 37-99-102, C.R.S., as may be amended from
time to time;
(b)
The term applicable property shall mean, and shall be amended from time to time as amended by state law to mean:
(1)
A street right-of-way, parking lot, median, or transportation corridor;
(2)
Commercial, institutional and industrial property;
(3)
Common interest community property; and
(4)
Multifamily residential housing premises property with more than twelve (12) dwelling
units.
( Ord. 668 §2, 2025)
(a)
Commencing on and after January 1, 2026, no person shall install, plant or place as
part of a new development or redevelopment project, nonfunctional turf, nonfunctional
artificial turf or invasive plant species within:
(1)
A street right-of-way, parking lot, median, or transportation corridor;
(2)
Commercial, institutional and industrial property;
(3)
Common interest community property; or
(4)
Multifamily residential housing premises property with more than twelve (12) dwelling
units.
(b)
Commencing on and after January 1, 2028, the prohibition set forth in subsection (a)
above shall apply to any portion of applicable properties that include multifamily
residential housing premises property.
( Ord. 668 §2, 2025)
