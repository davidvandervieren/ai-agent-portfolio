Every Elective City Officer of the City of Greeley, Colorado, may be recalled from
office at any time after the Officer has held office for six (6) months, by the electors
of the City of Greeley, by recall petition filed with the City Clerk, with signatures
of qualified electors equal in number to twenty-five (25) per centum of the entire
vote cast at the preceding general municipal election for all candidates for the position
which the incumbent sought to be recalled occupies.
Such petitions shall contain a general statement of not more than two hundred (200)
words, of the ground on which the recall is sought, and may be on one (1) or more
sheets of paper. Each signer of such petition shall add to the signer's signature
the date of signing, place of residence and street number. The person circulating
such petitions shall subscribe to an oath on each such sheet of paper that the signatures
thereon are genuine.
The procedure hereunder to effect the recall of an elective officer shall be, as far
as applicable, by the method provided in Article XXI, and Section 4 thereof, of the
Constitution of the State of Colorado, known as "Recall from Office," with power in
the City Council to provide by ordinance such other and further procedure as it may
deem expedient, not inconsistent herewith.
[Editor's History] Note— Amended by vote of citizens on November 4, 1997.
