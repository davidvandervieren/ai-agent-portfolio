All streets within the city shall be constructed to the minimum standards established
by the Greeley Street Standards, dated December 1, 1993, in order to safeguard the
public health, safety and welfare of the citizens of the city. Copies of the Greeley
Street Standards shall be kept and maintained by the city clerk and department of
public works and shall be available for inspection at those locations during all business
hours.
(Code 1994, § 16.14.010; Ord. No. 1, 1994, § 1, 1-18-1994)
