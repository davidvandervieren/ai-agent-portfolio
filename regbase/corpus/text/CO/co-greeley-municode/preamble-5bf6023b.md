We, the people of the City of Greeley, Colorado, in order to promote the general welfare
of our community and to exercise the rights, privileges, and responsibilities of self-government
granted to us under the authority of the Constitution of the State of Colorado, do
ordain and establish this Home Rule Charter for the City of Greeley, Colorado.
