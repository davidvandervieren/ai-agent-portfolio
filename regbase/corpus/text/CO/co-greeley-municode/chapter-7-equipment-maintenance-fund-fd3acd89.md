There is created and established a special fund to be known as the Equipment Maintenance
Fund. All monies accumulated in the fund shall be used exclusively for the maintenance
and replacement of equipment assigned to the equipment pool and construction, repair
and maintenance of garage facilities. The monies may be used separately for such purposes
or jointly with other monies that may be subsequently budgeted or earmarked for such
purposes.
(Prior Code, § 2-63(d)(2); Code 1994, § 4.28.010)
