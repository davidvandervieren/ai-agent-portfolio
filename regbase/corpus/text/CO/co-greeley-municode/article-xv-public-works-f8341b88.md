Section 15-3, Powers and Duties, repealed by Ord. No. 45, 1993, § 4, adopted September

The City Council shall by ordinance provide for the functions and operations of general
municipal public works, including, but not limited to, providing all engineering,
architectural, maintenance, construction and work equipment services required to be
performed by the City, except those performed by private persons, firms, or corporations
under contract, or those functions assigned elsewhere in this Charter.
