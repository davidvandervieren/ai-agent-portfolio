All words used in this chapter shall have the same meaning as that ascribed to them
in regulations promulgated by the civil aeronautics authority.
(Prior Code, § 6-1; Code 1994, § 14.32.010)
No person shall operate any aircraft over or within the city or from the Weld County
Municipal Airport in violation of any valid current air traffic or other rule or regulation
established by the civil aeronautics authority.
(Prior Code, § 6-2; Code 1994, § 14.32.020)
