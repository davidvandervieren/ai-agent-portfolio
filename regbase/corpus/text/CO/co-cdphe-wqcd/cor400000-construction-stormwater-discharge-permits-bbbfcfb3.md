Application — Use the Colorado Environmental Online Services (CEOS) to apply for new permits, modify or terminate existing permits, and change permit contacts. A complete application signed by both the owner and operator will result in a certification within 10 days. Permit termination can only be done once final stabilization is complete. A map is required.
Modification Supplement Form - for modification to remove acreage - include this document as an attachment to your modification application in CEOS.
Transfer Application Supplement form part 2 — include this document as an attachment in CEOS to your transfer application. One form for each contact being removed.

<!-- heading-path: CEOs > How To Find An Issued Certification -->
### How To Find An Issued Certification
If you have a CEOS account, you’ll find your certification on your dashboard. Otherwise, certifications that have been issued are available in the electronic records drawer . Under search type, select CDPHERM WTR CW Search. Under CDPHERM Container, type in the certification number that begins with ‘COR4’. If you are having difficulty finding your permit, please use the user guide .

#### Information on qualifying local programs for Arvada, Durango, Golden, and Lakewood.
The following is information for permitting of small construction activities located in one of the listed cities.
