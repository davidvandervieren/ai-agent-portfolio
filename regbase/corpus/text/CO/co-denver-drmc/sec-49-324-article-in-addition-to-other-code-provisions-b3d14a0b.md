Cross reference— Driving around or through barricades prohibited, § 54-136.
The manager of transportation and infrastructure shall have and is hereby invested
with power and authority, from time to time, to adopt rules and regulations, and from
time to time, to revise, amend and repeal the same, governing and requiring the use
of lights, signs or other warning devices, and of barricades and similar protective
safeguards, to warn and protect persons and property from danger from any and all
types of construction, demolition, excavation or other work in, or resulting in the
obstruction of, any public ways or places or any part or parts thereof.
(Code 1950, § 381.1; Ord. No. 39-20, § 119, 2-3-20)
Cross reference— Rules and regulations generally, § 2-91 et seq.
Rules and regulations under this article shall apply to such of the sidewalks, parkings,
parkways, streets, alleys, bridges, viaducts, tunnels, highways or other public places
or ways, or parts thereof, and to such parks and other places owned or under the control
of the city, or parts thereof, as shall be specified or described therein, and may
prescribe the conditions and circumstances under which the use of such devices or
safeguards is required, the locations in which the same shall be placed, the kind
or nature, appearance, size, wording, if wording is required, design, construction
and maintenance of such devices and safeguards and the installation and operation
thereof.
(Code 1950, § 381.2)
It shall be unlawful for any person carrying on or causing to be carried on any type
of construction, demolition, excavation or other work in, or resulting in the obstruction
of, any such public way or place, or any part or parts thereof, to fail, omit, neglect
or refuse to comply with all of such rules and regulations as are applicable thereto.
(Code 1950, § 381.3)
The rules and regulations adopted under this article may prescribe warning devices
or protective safeguards additional to those required by articles VIII and X of this
chapter.
(Code 1950, § 381.4)
