State Law reference— Motorcycles, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1301 et seq.
Traffic laws apply to persons operating motorcycles. Every person operating a motorcycle
shall be granted all of the rights and shall be subject to all of the duties applicable
to the driver of any other vehicle except as to those provisions which by their nature
can have no application.
(Code 1950, § 517.14)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1301.
(a)
A person operating a motorcycle shall ride only upon the permanent and regular seat
attached thereto.
(b)
A person shall ride upon a motorcycle only while sitting astride the seat, facing
forward, with one (1) leg on either side of the motorcycle.
(Code 1950, §§ 517.14-1, 517.14-2)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1302.
(a)
It shall be unlawful for the operator of a motorcycle to carry any other person, nor
shall any other person ride on a motorcycle unless such motorcycle is designed to
carry more than one (1) person, in which event a passenger may ride upon the permanent
seat if designed for two (2) persons, or upon another seat firmly attached to the
motorcycle at the rear or side of the operator.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for the operator to carry any person, nor shall any person ride,
in a position that will interfere with the operation or control of the motorcycle
or the view of the operator.
(c)
Any motorcycle carrying a passenger, other than in a sidecar or enclosed cab, shall
be equipped with footrests for such passengers.
(Code 1950, §§ 517.14-1, 517.14-4, 517.14-5)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1302.
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a motorcycle while carrying packages,
bundles or other articles which prevent him from keeping both hands on the handlebars.
(Code 1950, § 517.14-3)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1302.
(a)
All motorcycles are entitled to full use of a traffic lane, and no motor vehicle shall
be driven in such a manner as to deprive any motorcycle of the full use of a traffic
lane. This subsection shall not apply to motorcycles operated two (2) abreast in a
single lane.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for the operator of a motorcycle to overtake or pass in the same
lane occupied by the vehicle being overtaken.
(c)
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a motorcycle between lanes of traffic,
or between adjacent lines or rows of vehicles.
(d)
Motorcycles shall not be operated more than two (2) abreast in a single lane.
(Code 1950, §§ 517.14-6—517.14-9)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-1303.
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate any motorcycle or motor-driven cycle
on any street in the city unless such person and any passenger thereon are wearing,
covering the eyes, goggles or eyeglasses with lenses made of safety glass or plastic.
(Code 1950, § 517.23)
State Law reference— Similar provisions, C.R.S. 1973, 42-4-231.
