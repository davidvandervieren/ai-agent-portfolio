Editor's note— Ord. No. 186-92, § 1, adopted Mar. 30, 1992, repealed Art. II of this chapter, which
pertained to stock brokers, stock exchanges and bucket shops, consisted of Divs. 1
and 2, §§ 21-11, 21-12, and 21-21—21-24, and derived from §§ 961.1—961.6 of the city's
1950 Code.
Editor's note— Ord. No. 417-92, § 1, adopted June 29, 1992, repealed former Art. III of this chapter,
relative to employment agencies, which consisted of §§ 21-36—21-39 and 21-51—21-53
and derived from §§ 962.1—962.9 of the city's 1950 Code, as amended by Ord. No. 342-85,
§ 1, adopted June 24, 1985.
Editor's note— Ord. No. 618-92, § 1, adopted Sept. 8, 1992, repealed Art. IV of this chapter, which
pertained to lenders, consisted of §§ 21-61—21-63 and 21-71—21-74, and derived from
§§ 964.1, 964.1-1, 964.2, 964.2-1, 964.3—964.5 and 964.7 of the 1950 Code.
