Editor's note— Ord. No. 423-93, § 1, adopted June 7, 1993, repealed § 46-1, which pertained to signs
near the civic center and derived from § 622.7 of the 1950 Code.
It shall be unlawful to erect or remodel any ground sign or roof sign upon or along
any boulevard or parkway, except in business or industrial districts.
(Code 1950, § 622.8)
It shall be unlawful to erect any roof sign within a distance of two hundred fifty
(250) feet of any public park measured from the inner curb line of the street which
bounds the park. It shall be unlawful to erect any projecting sign or ground sign
upon any property which fronts on any public park, except in business or industrial
districts.
(Code 1950, § 622.9)
Cross reference— Parks and recreation generally, Ch. 39 .
Editor's note— Section 1 of Ord. No. 713-85, adopted Dec. 16, 1985, repealed § 46-4, concerning signs
near freeways, as derived from Code 1950, § 508.14.
It shall be unlawful to erect or remodel any sign within a distance of fifty (50)
feet of any viaduct or any ramp thereof when any part of such sign would extend higher
than the floor level of such viaduct or ramp.
(Code 1950, § 508.15)
