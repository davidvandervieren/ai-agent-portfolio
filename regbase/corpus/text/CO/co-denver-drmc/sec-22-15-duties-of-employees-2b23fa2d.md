The employees of the fire department shall perform such duties as may be required
by their superiors, including the manager of safety, and they shall wear such uniforms,
caps, badges and other insignia as required by department rules and regulations as
approved by the manager of safety.
(Code 1950, § 221.2)
(a)
The chief of the fire department shall:
(1)
Be responsible for the discipline, good order and proper conduct of the whole fire
department, the enforcement of all laws, ordinances and regulations pertaining thereto
and for the care and condition of the houses, engines and all other property and apparatus
of the department.
(2)
Have the superintendence, control and command of all the employees of the department,
of all the firehouses, engines and all other fire apparatus, equipment and property
of the department belonging to the city;
(3)
Have control of all persons present at fires and other emergencies where the fire
department has primary control, and to that end shall exofficio have and exercise
all the powers of the chief of police thereat;
(4)
Wear the authorized badge designating his rank of fire chief and shall be responsible
for taking immediate and proper measures for the extinguishment of all fires within
the city;
(5)
Have power, if need be, to summon any and all persons present to aid in extinguishing
any fire, in removing personal property from any building on fire or in danger thereof,
and in guarding the same;
(6)
Have power to order the cutting down and removing of any structure, erection, fence,
or other thing if the chief shall deem it necessary for the purpose of checking the
progress of any fire;
(7)
Perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the manager of safety.
(b)
In case of the absence of the chief from any fire, the assistant chiefs, or in their
absence the highest ranking firefighter of the company first at the fire or other
emergency, shall, during such emergency, have powers and perform the duties of such
chief.
(Code 1950, § 221.8)

The provisions of this article shall apply to the monitoring of any fire alarm system.
(Ord. No. 650-80, § 1(238.1), 12-1-80)
The following words and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings
respectively ascribed to them:
(1)
Approved shall mean officially acceptable or satisfactory to the department as meeting the
requirements of this article and/or applicable standards adopted by the department.
(2)
Automatic dialer shall mean a unit located at the protected premises that will contact the receiver
via the telephone switched network (dial system) and transmit data identifying the
change of status at the protected premises.
(3)
Central alarm station shall mean any facility which monitors fire alarm systems installed at more than
two (2) locations.
(4)
Coded register shall mean an approved device capable of transmitting from a central alarm station
to headquarters, over an electrically supervised circuit, a signal or fire alarm intelligence
identifying a specific building or address, either by direct means or through the
use of reference cards.
(5)
Department shall mean the chief of the fire department or his/her designated representative.
(6)
Direct phone shall mean a continuous service telephone line which connects a central alarm station
with the switchboard at headquarters.
(7)
Headquarters shall mean the department fire alarm headquarters, which is presently located in
the city at 950 Josephine Street.
(8)
Operator shall mean a person who operates the equipment installed in a central alarm station
and relays any alarms to the headquarters.
(9)
Required fire alarm system shall mean a system required by the building code.
(10)
Runner shall mean a person who is sent to the location where a fire alarm system is installed
when the system is not operational, who remains on the premises for the purpose of
detecting fires until the fire alarm system is fully operational.
(Ord. No. 650-80, § 1(238.2), 12-1-80)
(a)
Class I central station. Every class I central alarm station shall:
(1)
Maintain an approved direct phone, and shall arrange for and pay all costs of equipment,
installation, line lease, repair and removal;
(2)
Maintain an approved coded register, and shall arrange for and pay all costs of, equipment,
installation, line lease, repair and removal; and
(3)
Report alarms. Any alarm or combination signal which indicates the presence of fire, smoke or water
flow at the premises of a subscriber shall be deemed by a central alarm station to
be an alarm of fire and the information shall be immediately transmitted to headquarters
in the following sequence:
a.
Voice communication over the direct phone giving the following:
1.
The headquarters box number;
2.
The name of the institution, building or premises involved;
3.
The correct address of the premises involved, in numerical form, if possible;
4.
The type of alarm, if known, including, but not limited to, waterflow, heat detector
or manual station;
5.
The location of the alarm installation on the premises, if known;
b.
Tapping out three (3) rounds of the headquarters box number assigned to the protected
premises involved on the coded register.
c.
The department may permit forms of communication of fire alarm intelligence to headquarters,
other than the voice communication and tapping prescribed in subsection (a) of this
section.
(4)
Whenever a required fire alarm system which is located on the premises of a subscriber
is not in operation for any reason, the central alarm station shall dispatch a runner
to such premises who shall remain thereon until such equipment is restored to operative
condition.
(5)
Submit to headquarters semiannually, on July first and on December thirty-first, a
complete listing of its subscribers containing such information as may be requested
by the department, for verification of records used for the transmission of alarms.
Any changes in the subscriber list shall be brought to the department's attention
within twenty-four (24) hours and confirmed in writing by the earliest first class
mail.
(6)
Maintain, at all times, at the central alarm station two (2) licensed operators, one
of whom may be an apprentice and who shall have no other duties than that of operating
the central alarm station.
(7)
Maintain the central alarm station in conformance with the following National Fire
Protective Association pamphlets:
a.
Central Station Signaling Systems, Pamphlet No. 71, 1977;
b.
Signaling Systems, Pamphlet No. 72-B, 1979;
c.
Remote Station Protective Signaling Systems, Pamphlet No. 72-C, 1975; and
d.
Proprietary Protective Signaling Systems, Pamphlet No. 72-D, 1979.
(b)
Class II central alarm stations. Each class II central alarm station shall be equipped and staffed adequately to handle
the number of subscribers which it services. The minimum requirements shall be:
(1)
The maintenance of an approved direct phone to the department.
(2)
Maintenance of one person on duty at all times whose only duty is to monitor the central
alarm station equipment;
(3)
Maintain on duty the number of operators, whose only duty is to monitor the central
alarm station equipment, established by the department based on the number of alarms
expected and the number of subscribers to the central alarm stations service;
(4)
Report of subscribers required. Submission to headquarters semiannually, on July first
and on December thirty-first a complete listing of its subscribers, containing such
information as may be requested by the department for verification of records used
for the transmission of alarms. Any changes in the subscriber list shall be brought
to the department's attention within twenty-four (24) hours and confirmed in writing
by the earliest first class mail.
(5)
All equipment which is used and monitored by the central alarm station shall be approved,
and the installation of the equipment shall be approved prior to it being put into
operation.
