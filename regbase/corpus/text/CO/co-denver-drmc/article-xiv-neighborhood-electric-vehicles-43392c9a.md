Persons who are licensed to drive a motor vehicle by the State of Colorado or who
are exempted from the state driver's license requirement by the laws of the State
of Colorado may operate a neighborhood electric vehicle or low-speed electric vehicle
upon the streets and highways within the city, provided however, that operation of
neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed electric vehicle shall conform to the
provisions of sections 54-661 and 54-662 .
(Ord. No. 629-98, § 7, 9-8-98; Ord. No. 686-09, § 13, 11-23-09)
This chapter 54 , all traffic laws of the State of Colorado, including but not limited to, laws concerning
financial responsibility, and all traffic laws and regulations of the United States
of America shall apply to persons operating neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed
electric vehicle.
(Ord. No. 629-98, § 7, 9-8-98; Ord. No. 686-09, § 14, 11-23-09)
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a neighborhood electric vehicle under
any of the following conditions:
(1)
Neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed electric vehicles shall not be operated
upon any street or highway that has a posted speed limit in excess of thirty (30)
miles per hour, provided however, that neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed
electric vehicles shall be allowed to cross, at intersections, streets and highways
that have a posted speed limit in excess of thirty (30) miles per hour.
(2)
Neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed electric vehicles shall not be operated
on controlled-access highways.
(3)
Neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed electric vehicles shall not be operated
at a speed in excess of twenty-five (25) miles per hour.
(4)
Neighborhood electric vehicles or low-speed electric vehicles shall not carry a greater
number of persons or carry a greater load than that specified by the manufacturer.
The driver and all passengers must be seated in seats substantially similar to those
placed in the vehicle by the manufacturer.
(Ord. No. 629-98, § 7, 9-8-98; Ord. No. 686-09, § 15, 11-23-09)
