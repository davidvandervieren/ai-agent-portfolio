It is the duty of the owner or occupant of every building in the city to number each
such building in the manner provided in this article.
(Code 1950, § 341.1)
The city engineer shall assign to every building its proper number.
(Code 1950, § 341.2)
Numbers heretofore assigned to lots or buildings, except as hereinafter provided,
shall remain the numbers of such lots or buildings respectively until otherwise changed
by ordinance.
(Code 1950, § 341.3-1)
In cases of mistake or conflict in street names or building numbers, or where some
special arrangement is necessary in consequence of irregularities in the streets or
avenues or lack of uniformity in the sizes of blocks, the city engineer shall direct
and make the proper adjustments in accordance with the spirit and intention of this
article.
(Code 1950, § 341.3-2)
In all that portion of the city lying east and south of the Platte River and north
of the line of Colfax Avenue, all buildings shall be numbered as follows:
(1)
One hundred (100) numbers shall be assigned to each block, beginning at First Street
in the west division of the city, calling it one hundred (100), running easterly and
northeasterly on each street until reaching a north and south street in each instance,
when new numbering shall be followed, one hundred (100) numbers to each block eastward,
commencing at Broadway as zero.
(2)
Beginning at Cheyenne Place as one hundred (100) and running northwesterly, all houses
shall be given one hundred (100) numbers to each block.
(Code 1950, § 341.4)
East of Broadway Street and north of Colfax Avenue, all buildings on the north and
south streets shall be numbered northerly one hundred (100) numbers to each block,
calling Colfax, fifteen hundred (1500).
(Code 1950, § 341.5)
(a)
In all that portion of the city south of Colfax Avenue, all buildings on the north
and south streets shall be numbered north and south from Ellsworth Avenue, as zero,
one hundred (100) numbers to each block; east and west from Broadway Street, buildings
shall be given one hundred (100) numbers to each block, calling Broadway zero, and
shall be designated by numbers, or west numbers, as they lie east or west of Broadway.
(b)
The numbers on Colfax Avenue shall be governed by the blocks on the south side of
the avenue, giving one hundred (100) numbers to each block east and west from Broadway,
designated as zero, and to be known respectively as numbers and west numbers.
(Code 1950, § 341.6)
(a)
In that portion of the city known as North Denver, in all of its territory laid off
in general conformity to the streets in the congressional grant, the houses shall
be given one hundred (100) numbers in each block from west to east, according to the
numbered streets, as hereinbefore provided, in the congressional grant of the east
division of the city and running northerly, shall be given one hundred (100) numbers
to each block, in continuation of the similar numbering in the congressional grant
south of the South Platte River.
(b)
In all the remaining portion of North Denver, all buildings on east and west streets
shall be given one hundred (100) numbers to each block calling Broadway zero, and
shall be designated by numbers, or west numbers as they lie east or west of Broadway;
and north, one hundred (100) numbers to each block, calling Colfax Avenue fifteen
hundred (1500).
(Code 1950, §§ 341.7, 341.8)
(a)
In all cases where a street shall have been named or numbered, renamed or renumbered,
in pursuance of any ordinance, it shall be the duty of the city engineer thereafter
to adjust and rename or renumber such street, as the same may be required from time
to time by action of the council.
(b)
In all cases where there is a mistake or conflict in names, or where some special
arrangement varying from the general terms of this article is necessary, the engineer
shall direct and make the proper adjustment of the same in harmony with the spirit
and intention of this article.
(Code 1950, § 341.9)
The city engineer shall prepare from time to time maps of the streets of the city
showing the present numbers of all buildings.
(Code 1950, § 341.10)
(a)
It shall be the duty of any owner or occupant of any premises, upon notice from the
city engineer, to cause the official number to be placed on any buildings so owned
or occupied.
(b)
Such numbering shall be done in the manner required within thirty (30) days after
service of the notice.
(Code 1950, § 341.11)
(a)
All numbers shall be of some metallic, glass, porcelain or other durable material,
or gilt lettering on the inside of a glass transom or door, and shall be distinctly
legible and at least three (3) inches in height.
(b)
All numerals shall be placed in a conspicuous position at the front of each building.
(Code 1950, § 341.12)
(a)
It is the duty of the owner or occupant of any building upon the streets of the city
upon which the system of numbering has been adopted and in which official numbers
have been provided for by assignment by the city engineer, to number the building
in accordance with the assignment.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for any such owner or occupant to retain or use or to permit
to be retained or used upon any such building any number other than the number designated
by assignment by the city engineer.
(Code 1950, § 341.13)
