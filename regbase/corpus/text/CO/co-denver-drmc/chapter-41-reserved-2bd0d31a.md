41-30—41-38, 41-46—41-50, in its entirety. Former chapter 41 pertained to Planning
and derived from the Code of 1950, §§ 131.3-1—131.3-4, 131.5-5, 644.2, 672.1; Ord.
No. 25-81, § 1, adopted Jan 26, 1981; Ord. No. 265-81, § 1, adopted May 26, 1981;
Ord. No. 72-82, § 1, adopted March 1, 1982; Ord. No. 220-83, § 1, adopted April 11,
1983; Ord. No. 19-84, § 1, adopted Jan. 16, 1984; Ord. No. 248-90, § 1, adopted April
30, 1990; Ord. No. 978-91, §§ 2—5, adopted Dec. 23, 1991; Ord. No. 500-92, § 1, adopted
July 27, 1992; Ord. No. 337-95, § 1, adopted May 8, 1995; Ord. No. 365-95, § 1, adopted
May 15, 1995; Ord. No. 436-97, § 2, adopted July 7, 1997; Ord. No. 548-98, § 1, Aug.
10, 1998; Ord. No. 200-99, § 1, adopted March 15, 1999; and Ord. No. 372-99, § 1,
adopted May 10, 1999.
