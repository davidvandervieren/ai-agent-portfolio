Editor's note— Ord. No. 86-94, § 1, adopted Feb. 7, 1994, repealed former Art. XII, §§ 54-672—54-675,
relative to mobile ice cream and food vendor regulations, and § 2 of the ordinance
enacted similar new provisions as Art. XII, §§ 54-672—54-676. Formerly, such provisions
derived from §§ 506.1-1(1), 506.1-2, 506.2 and 506.3 of the 1950 Code and Ord. No.
162-80, §§ 1, 2, adopted Apr. 14, 1980, and Ord. No. 651-80, § 2, adopted Dec. 8,
1980.
Cross reference— Mobile food units, pushcarts and food peddlers, § 23-69.
Words and phrases used in this article shall have the following meanings ascribed
to them:
(1)
Ice cream truck means any vehicle from which ice cream, frozen milk, frozen dairy or ice confection
products are sold or offered for sale.
(2)
Vehicle means every device in, upon or by which any person or property is or may be transported
or drawn upon the public streets, irrespective of the source from which the power
to propel the vehicle may come, and irrespective of the number of wheels of the vehicle.
(Ord. No. 86-94, § 2, 2-7-94; Ord. No. 1003-25, § 31, 7-28-25)
(a)
The provisions of this article shall not apply to the vending of food, food products,
or beverages by any person doing business under a permit issued pursuant to the provisions
of divisions 6.5 or 7 of article XIV of chapter 49 .
(b)
The provisions of this article shall not apply to the delivery of any products to
a dwelling as the result of a prior order made by the occupant of any dwelling, nor
shall it apply to the delivery vehicles of licensed businesses making deliveries upon
order to the occupants of dwellings within the city.
(Ord. No. 86-94, § 2, 2-7-94)
Any person employing or using the services of any other person who violates the provisions
of this article shall also be liable for the violation.
(Ord. No. 86-94, § 2, 2-7-94)
(a)
It shall be unlawful for any person vending food, food products or beverages from
any vehicle to:
(1)
Stop anywhere within twenty (20) feet of an intersection when making a sale or attempting
to make a sale;
(2)
Double park, or park in any manner contrary to any ordinance relating to parking when
attempting a sale or when making a sale;
(3)
Sell to any person who is standing in the street or a roadway;
(4)
Sell or attempt to sell at any time, from a motor vehicle located on a public street,
alley or other public right-of-way within the central business district, except when
such street, alley or right-of-way is closed to traffic pursuant to the provisions
of section 54-491 ; and for the purposes of this subsection, the central business district shall mean
that area of the city bounded by and including the following streets: commencing at
the intersection of Larimer Street and 20th Street; thence proceeding southwesterly
on Larimer Street to Speer Boulevard; thence southerly on Speer Boulevard to 13th
Street; thence southeasterly on 13th Street to Colfax Avenue; thence easterly on Colfax
Avenue to Lincoln Street; thence northerly on Lincoln Street to 20th Street; thence
northwesterly on 20th Street to the intersection of 20th Street and Larimer Street.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for any person to sell or attempt to sell any item from an ice
cream truck on any arterial street, collector street or laned highway as defined in section 54-1 of this Code.
(c)
It shall be an affirmative defense to charges brought under subsection 54-675 (b) that sales of ice cream, frozen milk, frozen dairy and ice confection products
provided less than ten (10) percent of the gross income from a vehicle on the date
charged.
(Ord. No. 86-94, § 2, 2-7-94)
The provisions of this article shall be in addition to applicable provisions of article
III of chapter 23 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 86-94, § 2, 2-7-94)
