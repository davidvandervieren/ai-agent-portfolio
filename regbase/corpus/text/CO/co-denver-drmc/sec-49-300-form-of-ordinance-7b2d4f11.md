(a)
The city shall have the power to cover or otherwise protect irrigating ditches or
any parts thereof which lie within any thickly settled residence portion of the city;
and to assess the cost thereof upon the property especially benefitted thereby.
(b)
All contracts therefor shall be awarded by the mayor, upon the recommendation of the
manager of transportation and infrastructure.
(c)
All such improvements shall be constructed by and under the direction of the manager
of transportation and infrastructure, in accordance with specifications prescribed
by the manager, provided, however, that the covering or otherwise protecting of any
irrigating ditch, or of any portion thereof, shall first be authorized by the council.
(Code 1950, § 376.1; Ord. No. 39-20, § 115, 2-3-20)
The initiation of proceedings for the covering or otherwise protecting of any irrigating
ditch or any portion thereof may be, except as provided in this article, by presentation
of a petition to the council, which petition shall:
(1)
Describe, in general terms, the irrigating ditch or portion thereof desired to be
covered or otherwise protected;
(2)
Specify the boundaries of the special improvement district to be created to bear the
expense of the improvement; and
(3)
Be signed by a majority, in area, of the owners in the proposed special improvement
district.
(Code 1950, § 376.2)
(a)
The signatures to any petition for the initiation of proceedings under this article
shall be acknowledged before a notary public in the same manner as conveyances of
real property.
(b)
Such petition may be in two (2) or more counterparts.
(Code 1950, § 376.3)
(a)
Upon the presentation of the petition under this article, the manager of transportation
and infrastructure shall cause the city engineer to make an estimate (if such estimate
has not been previously made) of the total cost of the covering or otherwise protecting
of the irrigation ditch, or any portion thereof, set out in the petition.
(b)
The manager of transportation and infrastructure shall determine the rate of interest
upon and the number of installments and the time in which the costs shall be payable,
including a reasonable percentage for the cost of collection and other incidentals
(and of interest to the time the first installment falls due); and shall prepare a
plan, with one (1) or more zones, for levying the cost of the improvement on the land
to be benefitted.
(Code 1950, § 376.4; Ord. No. 39-20, § 116, 2-3-20)
In case any of the funds for the covering or protecting of any ditch are to come in
part from any source other than an assessment upon the property in the special improvement
district, the amount of such funds to come from such other source shall be set out
by the manager of transportation and infrastructure in the plan to be prepared by
the manager under the provisions of section 49-294 , and such amount shall be deducted from the total amount to be assessed against the
property specially benefitted.
(Code 1950, § 376.5; Ord. No. 39-20, § 117, 2-3-20)
Whenever, in the judgment of the manager of transportation and infrastructure, the
protecting of any irrigation ditch, in whole or in part, can best be accomplished
by changing the location of such ditch, the manager may provide for such change of
location in the plan to be prepared by the manager under the provisions of section 49-294 .
(Code 1950, § 376.6; Ord. No. 39-20, § 118, 2-3-20)
(a)
Upon the completion of the matters set out in section 49-294 , the city clerk shall give notice by advertisement for ten (10) consecutive days,
exclusive of Sundays and legal holidays, in two (2) daily newspapers of general circulation,
published in the city, specifying the proposed boundaries of the local improvement
district, giving the data required to be ascertained in section 49-294 , and notifying all persons interested in the improvement or in any of the land in
the proposed district to file with the city clerk within twenty (20) days from the
first publication of the notice any objections thereto, including any objections to
the proposed boundaries or zoning of the local improvement district.
(b)
After the expiration of the twenty (20) days, the council shall proceed to consider
any objections that are filed and may, at its discretion, fix a time and place for
a public hearing at which any person who has filed objections may appear and be heard.
(Code 1950, § 376.7)
The council, after hearing all objections, shall, by ordinance, determine whether
or not to authorize the proposed improvement district and shall also determine the
boundaries and zoning of the local improvement district which is to bear the cost
of the improvement.
(Code 1950, § 376.8)
The city council expressly hereby reserves the right:
(1)
To proceed to the creation of a local improvement district for the purpose herein
specified without the necessity of a petition therefor in those cases where the council
finds that the danger to the safety of children is particularly great;
(2)
To permit interested persons to appear and speak in case of a public hearing as set
out in section 49-297 whether they have filed written objections within the time provided in section 49-297 , or not;
(3)
To change the boundaries of the proposed local improvement district by the exclusion
therefrom of any tract or tracts which the council shall find not to be specially
benefitted by the proposed improvement; or by the inclusion therein of other tracts,
not included within the proposed local improvement district, which the council finds
will be specially benefitted thereby;
(4)
To change the boundaries of the zones in accordance with the special benefits as found
by the council; and
(5)
To modify, in accordance with the findings of the council as to benefits, the relative
proportions of the cost of the improvement to be borne by and assessed against the
lands in the several zones of the district in case there are more than one (1) zone.
(Code 1950, § 376.9)
When the city council has finally determined the matters to be determined by it in
accordance with the prior sections of this article, the council by ordinance shall:
(1)
Describe in general terms the improvement to be made;
(2)
Define the exterior boundaries of the local improvement district to bear the cost
of the making of such improvement;
(3)
Establish the several zones in the local improvement district in case there is more
than one (1) zone;
(4)
Specify the several proportions of the cost which shall be assessed against the property
in the several zones, in case there is more than one (1) zone; and
(5)
Fix the maximum assessment as provided in this article.
(Code 1950, § 376.10)
(a)
The cost of improvement shall be borne by the property to be specially benefitted,
except as stated in this article.
(b)
In case there is only one (1) zone, the cost shall be divided pro rata on the entire
acreage in the district except that included in public streets, roads and highways.
(c)
If there is more than one (1) zone in the district, the division shall be in the same
manner, except that adjustment shall be made for the several rates fixed for the different
zones.
(d)
The ordinance shall specify the maximum rate of assessment, which shall be based upon
the engineer's estimate of total cost, including a reasonable percentage for the cost
of collection and other incidentals and of interest to the time the first installment
falls due.
(Code 1950, § 376.11)
No contract for any improvement shall be let for any amount exceeding the total estimate
of the city engineer as the cost of such improvement.
(Code 1950, § 376.12)
The provisions of the Charter shall be applicable to the improvements herein provided
for in the same manner and to the same extent as if repeated at length in this article.
(Code 1950, § 376.13)
The finding by ordinance that any petition for the initiation of a proceeding under
this article was subscribed by the required number of owners or that the improvement
was duly ordered after notice duly given, shall be conclusive of the facts so stated
in every court or other tribunal.
(Code 1950, § 376.14)
