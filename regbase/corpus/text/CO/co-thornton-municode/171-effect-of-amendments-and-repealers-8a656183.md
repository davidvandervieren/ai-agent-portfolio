Approval by the electors of any Charter Amendment which repeals any section or portion
of this Charter shall not be construed to be a limitation, denial, or suspension of
any power of the City of Thornton, or any power of its elected City Council otherwise
vested in or authorized to the City or the City Council by the constitution or the
statutes of the State of Colorado; except as expressly provided by new language, such
amendments shall be construed to the end that repealers or deletions eliminate obsolete,
unnecessary and limiting language in the original Charter Document which previously
served to restrict the powers of the City and the elected City Council.
