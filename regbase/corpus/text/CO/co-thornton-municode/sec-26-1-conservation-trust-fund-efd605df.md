(a)
A conservation trust fund is created pursuant to state law. The city has taken all
actions necessary to qualify for distribution of state moneys allocated to conservation
trust fund purposes.
(b)
Any moneys received from the state for conservation trust fund purposes shall be deposited
in the conservation trust fund and shall be expended, upon direction of the city council,
only in accordance with the provisions of applicable state law.
(Code 1975, §§ 5-122, 5-123; Ord. No. 705, 12-22-75; Ord. No. 2308, §§ 32, 33, 2-28-94)
