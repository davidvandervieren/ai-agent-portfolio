Editor's note— Ord. No. 3718, § 1, adopted Dec. 3, 2024 changed the title of Art. IV from "Tax Rebate
for Elderly Persons" to "Property Tax and Food Sales Tax Refund Program."
There is enacted a tax refund program to be known as the Property Tax and Food Sales
Tax Refund Program. It is the purpose of this program to refund to the qualified residents
of the city, who have limited incomes, the city's portion of the preceding year's
property tax and a defined portion of the food sales taxes which they have paid. Funding
is established by City Council through the annual budget appropriation process.
(Code 1975, § 9-1; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3718, § 1, 12-3-24)
The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Dwelling unit means any detached dwelling, multi-unit residential dwelling, attached dwelling,
or manufactured home within the city limits on which ad valorem property taxes are
levied and paid.
Household means one or more persons who live under one roof and who pay property tax or rent
on the home or dwelling unit or mobile home in which the members of the household
live. Any person who pays property taxes or rent on a home or dwelling unit shall
not be considered to be a part of any household other than such person's own.
Income means all amounts that go to or are received on behalf of the household during the
calendar year prior to application.
(Code 1975, § 9-2; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3436, § 1, 5-9-17; Ord. No. 3718,
§ 1, 12-3-24; Ord. No. 3745, § 15, 8-26-25)
Cross reference— Definitions generally, § 1-2 .
Any person who submits false, inaccurate or misleading information to the city in
order to qualify for a refund as provided in this article or any person who knowingly
applies for or accepts a refund contrary to the provisions of this article shall be
in violation of this article and for each such violation shall be punished as provided
in Section 1-8(b).
(Code 1975, § 9-8; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80)
The property tax portion of the program adopted in this article shall apply to: the
homeowner who has paid property taxes on a dwelling unit the homeowner occupies; a
renter occupying a dwelling unit. Every person desiring to qualify for a property
tax refund must submit to the city an annual written application, on a form provided
by the city. To qualify for a refund, a person must meet all of the following requirements:
(1)
The applicant must submit with the application proof of income acceptable to the city.
(2)
Relative to the calendar year immediately preceding the year in which the application
is submitted, the applicant must submit proof of meeting all of the following requirements,
showing the applicant:
a.
Was a resident of the city for the 12 months preceding the year in which the application
is submitted.
b.
Paid property taxes in the prior 12 months as substantiated by a tax statement on
a dwelling unit which the applicant owned and in which the applicant lived or evidence
of rent paid for the calendar year preceding the year in which the application is
submitted for the dwelling unit, multi-unit residential dwelling, or manufactured
home space.
(3)
Total income does not exceed 60% of the Colorado median income level as determined
annually by the Colorado Department of Health and Human Services (CDHS) for the year
in which the resident seeks assistance.
(Code 1975, § 9-3; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3436, § 2, 5-9-17; Ord. No. 3718,
§ 1, 12-3-24; Ord. No. 3745, § 16, 8-26-25)
(a)
If the applicant under this article lived in the city during the full calendar year
preceding the year in which the application is submitted and has met all requirements
of Section 26-354 , the city shall refund to the applicant an amount equal to the median property taxes
which will be levied on that type of residence by the city, utilizing the following
procedures:
(1)
The median assessed valuation per type of residence in the city shall be multiplied
by the mill levy of the city to ascertain the amount to be refunded. Both the median
assessed valuation and the mill levy are those figures used during the year preceding
the year in which the application is submitted to the city.
(b)
For purposes of this Section, types of residences include attached dwellings, multi-unit
residential dwellings, and manufactured homes.
(c)
If, during the full year preceding the year in which the application is submitted,
the applicant owned and lived in two or more homes or rented and lived in two or more
dwelling units or owned and lived in a home for a portion of the preceding year and
rented and lived in a dwelling unit for the remaining portion of the year, all within
the city, the city shall refund to the applicant a sum in accordance with subsections
(a), (b) and (c) of this section based on the number of months the applicant lived
in each home or dwelling unit.
(d)
If an applicant has lived in the City for less than a full year at the time of application
any refund will be limited and pro-rated for the number of months the eligible applicant
lived within the city.
(e)
No dwelling unit of any type may receive more than one property tax refund per year
regardless of the number of residents therein.
(Code 1975, § 9-4; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3718, § 1, 12-3-24; Ord. No. 3745,
§ 17, 8-26-25)
Every person desiring to qualify for a food tax refund must submit to the city a written
application, on forms to be provided by the city. To qualify for a refund, a person
must meet all of the requirements of Section 26-354 . Only one food tax refund will be authorized per household, per year based on the
number of persons in the household.
(Code 1975, § 9-5; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3718, § 1, 12-3-24)
The amount of food sales tax to be refunded shall be based on the per household member
maximum annual allotment for federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)
benefits for the year in which the resident seeks assistance. The city shall refund
an amount equal to the previous year's percentage of sales tax that would be assessed
and collected by the city for groceries times the maximum annual SNAP allotment for
the household.
(Code 1975, § 9-6; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80; Ord. No. 3718, § 1, 12-3-24)
It is the intention of the city council to make property and food sales tax refunds
to those who qualify. Refunds shall not be paid to anyone other than the applicant
personally.
(Code 1975, § 9-7; Ord. No. 976, 11-10-80)
