The purpose of this article is to provide for the administration and disposition of
unclaimed property which is in the possession of or under the control of the city.
(Code 1975, § 7A-1; Ord. No. 2102, 8-26-91; Ord. No. 2172, 6-22-92)
The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Depositor means an entity which has deposited funds in escrow with the city, including a corporation,
partnership, association, person or a duly authorized legal representative or successor
in interest of such.
Director means the city finance director.
Owner means a person, including but not limited to a corporation, partnership, association,
governmental entity other than the city, or a duly authorized legal representative
or successor in interest of such, who owns any unclaimed property held by the city.
Unclaimed property means:
(1)
Any intangible property, including any income or increment derived therefrom, less
any lawful charges, held by or under the control of the city, and which has not been
claimed by its owner or depositor for a period of more than one year after it became
payable or distributable. Also included in this definition of unclaimed property is
property held in a safe deposit box or any other safekeeping repository by the city
which remains unclaimed by the owner for more than one year after the lease or rental
period on the box or other repository has expired. Unclaimed, abandoned or seized
motor vehicles are excluded from this article, as such property is governed by this
Code. Municipal code bond forfeitures are also excluded, as such bonds are subject
to the provisions of the Colorado Municipal Court Rules.
(2)
In addition to property included in the definition of intangible property in C.R.S.
§ 38-13-102, intangible property shall also include but shall not be limited to: deposits
for water, electricity, gas or cable TV service; overpayments on water, electricity,
gas or cable TV service; street cut permit deposits; developer cost recovery agreements;
monies unclaimed by beneficiaries in a pension fund; unredeemed gift certificates
for recreation center services; uncashed payroll checks; bonds posted for reasons
other than for municipal courts; lost and found money turned into the city; and uncashed
court ordered restitution payments.
(3)
Any negotiable tender, money orders, checks or drafts which have been deposited in
escrow with city to ensure the depositor's compliance with city's ordinances, resolutions,
regulations, other applicable law or agreements with city or to ensure that the city
is reimbursed for any damages the city might sustain as a result of depositor's activities.
(4)
Any unclaimed property described in subsections (1), (2) or (3) of this definition
for which:
a.
Performance of the obligations which the unclaimed property is to secure has been
completed;
b.
Performance of the obligations which the unclaimed property is to secure has not been
completed and the time for such performance has expired pursuant to applicable law
or agreement; or
c.
The applicable law or agreement does not specify the time during which the performance
is to be completed, and the director has either determined that performance of the
secured obligations has not been commenced within a reasonable period of time following
the deposit of the unclaimed property, or that performance of the secured obligations
has not resumed within a reasonable period of time following the suspension of such
performance.
(Code 1975, § 7A-2; Ord. No. 2102, 8-26-91; Ord. No. 2172, 6-22-92; Ord. No. 2363,
§ 1, 1-9-95)
Cross reference— Definitions generally, § 1-2 .
(a)
Under this article, the director shall dispose of funds by sending a written notice
by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the last known address of any depositor
or owner who has unclaimed property, as defined in Section 26-27 , which is in the possession of the city and has been unclaimed for at least one year.
(b)
The last known address of the depositor or owner shall be the last address of the
depositor or owner as shown by the records of the city department with which the unclaimed
property is held.
(c)
The notice shall advise the depositor or owner of the type of unclaimed property,
the date on which the city came into the possession of the unclaimed property, and
the reason the unclaimed property is being held by the city. The notice shall also
state that if the depositor or owner fails to provide the director with a written
claim for the return of the unclaimed property within 30 days from the date of the
notice, the unclaimed property shall become the sole property of city, to be utilized
as it deems appropriate.
(d)
If the depositor or owner did not receive the notice by certified mail, return receipt
requested, notice shall also be published, pursuant to Section 2-1 , 30 consecutive days. The notice shall state that if the depositor or owner fails
to provide the director with a written claim for the return of the unclaimed property
within 30 days from the date of the last publication of the notice, the unclaimed
property shall become the sole property of city, to be utilized as it deems appropriate.
(e)
If the director receives no written claim within the 30-day claim period as provided
in subsections (c) and (d) of this section, the unclaimed property shall become the
sole property of city, and any claim of the depositor or owner to such unclaimed property
shall be deemed abandoned.
(f)
If the director receives a written claim within the 30-day claim period, the unclaimed
property shall be returned to such claimant, unless the claim fails to clearly establish
the claimant's right to such unclaimed property. The director shall be under no obligation
to investigate the validity of a claim but may, in the director's sole discretion,
request further supporting documentation from the claimant prior to the return of
the unclaimed property.
(g)
In the event that there is more than one claimant for the same unclaimed property,
the director may, in the director's sole discretion, resolve such claim by depositing
the disputed unclaimed property with the registry of the county district court in
an interpleader action.
(h)
In the event that all claims filed are denied, the unclaimed property shall become
the sole property of the city, to be utilized as it deems appropriate. If any legal
action is timely filed, the unclaimed property shall be returned pursuant to the order
of the court having jurisdiction over such claim.
(i)
The director is authorized and directed to establish and administer procedures for
the disposition of unclaimed property pursuant to the provisions of this section.
(Code 1975, § 7A-3; Ord. No. 2102, 8-26-91; Ord. No. 2172, 6-22-92; Ord. No. 2363,
§ 2, 1-9-95; Ord. No. 3392, § 13, 8-9-16; Ord. No. 3476, §§ 14, 7-10-18)
