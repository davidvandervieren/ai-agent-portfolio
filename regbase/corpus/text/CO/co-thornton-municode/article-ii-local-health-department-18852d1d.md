State Law reference— County and district health departments, C.R.S. § 25-1-501 et seq.
Pursuant to Colo. Rev. Stat. § 25-1-506, as amended, the Local Health Department exercises
general supervision over health matters within its jurisdiction, which extends to
and includes the city.
(Code 1975, § 5-116; Ord. No. 705, 12-22-75; Ord. No. 2308, § 31, 2-28-94; Ord. No.
3666, § 9, 9-26-23)
The Local Health Department may exercise its powers and duties within the city in
accordance with Colo. Rev. Stat. § 25-1-506 et seq, as amended.
(Code 1975, § 5-117; Ord. No. 705, 12-22-75; Ord. No. 3666, § 9, 9-26-23)
Editor's note— Ord. No. 3666, § 10, adopted Sept. 26, 2023, repealed § 30-28, which pertained to
the enforcement of state regulations and derived from Code 1975, § 5-118; and Ord.
No. 705, adopted Dec. 22, 1975.
