welfare of the City of Fort Lupton is to facilitate snow removal from curb to curb
on City streets, as efficiently and expeditiously as possible after a snow event.
The compilation of snow and/or ice removal policy and level of service known as "City
of Fort Lupton Snow/Ice Removal with Level of Service Policy and Procedure" as approved
and adopted by resolution.

to be assessed on each thereof, and the names of the owners thereof, and also designating
therein a time and place at which the City Council shall sit for the purpose of hearing
