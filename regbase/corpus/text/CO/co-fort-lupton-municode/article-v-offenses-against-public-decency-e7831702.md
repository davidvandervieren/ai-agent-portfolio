It is unlawful for any person to urinate or defecate in any public place where such
conduct is likely to be viewed by any other person. It shall not be an offense under
this Section where such voiding is made into a receptacle that has been provided for
that purpose, that stores or disposes of the waste in a sanitary manner and that is
enclosed from view of the general public.
(Ord. 946 §20, 2012)
