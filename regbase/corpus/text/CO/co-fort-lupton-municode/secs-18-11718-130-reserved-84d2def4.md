Pursuant to Title 31, Article 16, C.R.S., there is adopted the 2018 International
Plumbing Code, published by the International Code Council, 4051 West Flossmoor Road,
Country Club Hills, Illinois 60478-5795.
(Prior code 6-544-10; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 96-682 §1; Ord. 2007-884, Pt. 8; Ord.
2014-980 §14; Ord. No. 2021-1133 , § 6, 11-2-2021)
The City Clerk shall certify to the passage of this Article and make not less than
one (1) copy of the adopted code available for inspection by the public during regular
business hours.
(Prior code 6-544-22; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 96-682 §2; Ord. 2014-980 §15)
This Article shall apply to the jurisdiction of the City.
(Prior code 6-544-20; Ord. 96-682 §2)
This Article shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose
to conform with the International Plumbing Code adopted herein.
(Prior code 6-544-21; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 96-682 §2)
The following sections of the International Plumbing Code is hereby revised:
" 101.1 Title. Is amended by the addition of the term "City of Fort Lupton" where indicated."
" 305.4.1 Sewer depth. Is amended by filling in both areas where indicated to read "12 inches (305 mm)."
" 312.3 Drainage and vent air test. Is amended by the deletion of the first sentence."
" 903.1 Roof extension. Is amended by inserting the number "12" (152.4 mm) where indicated in the second
sentence."
(Ord. 2014-980 §16; Ord. No. 2021-1133 , § 7, 11-2-2021)
