(a)
The intent and purpose of this Article is to ensure that contractors doing business
within the City have the minimum qualifications and maintain adequate liability insurance
to perform construction by the issuance of a contractor's license as set forth herein.
(b)
For the purposes of this Article, the following terms shall have the following meanings:
Building Contractor means a person or company in the business of remodeling, fix-up type construction,
roofing, siding, concrete, excavations or other work requiring permits under the International
Building Code or regulated under other related codes and ordinances as adopted by
the City.
Construction means the work, including the erection, alteration, demolition, movement, repair,
or remodeling, of any building or structure, or portion thereof, requiring a building
permit pursuant to the Building Code and any work within the public ways or on any
public facility in the City.
Contractor means any person, firm, partnership, corporation, association, other organization,
or any combination thereof that performs construction work within the City unless
otherwise specified in this Article.
Contractor's License means the license issued to a contractor performing construction work within the
City.
Contractor's License Type means the specific license that pertains to the type of work that is performed by
the contractor. The type of license may include or exclude certain work that can be
performed by the contractor.
Electrical Contractor means a person or company involved in electrical work that requires permits under
the National Electrical Code or is regulated under other related codes and ordinances
as adopted by the City.
General Building Contractor means a person or company in the business of new construction. This contractor is
one who builds one (1) or more dwelling units or commercial or similar business-type
buildings per year.
Heating, Ventilating and Air Conditioning (HVAC) Contractor means a person or company involved in installing furnaces, heaters, ventilating equipment,
air conditioning or other work requiring permits under the International Mechanical
Code or regulated under other related codes and ordinances as adopted by the City.
Plumbing Contractor means a person or company involved in plumbing that requires permits under the International
Plumbing Code or is regulated under other related codes and ordinances as adopted
by the City.
(Ord. 2013-972; Ord. No. 2023-1162 , § 1, 12-18-2023)
(a)
It shall be unlawful for any person or entity to perform any work described herein
without first obtaining the required license.
(b)
Fees shall be charged one (1) year from the date of issuance and licenses shall be
required as follows:
(1)
Building Contractor "B-C." Any building contractor as defined in Section 6-111 above shall be required to hold a Building Contractor "B-C" license.
(2)
Electrical Contractor "E." Any electrical contractor as defined in Section 6-111 above shall be required to hold an Electrical Contractor "E" license. Prior to issuance
of this license, the electrical contractor shall be required to provide a copy of
a current master electrician's license as issued by the State. An individual who builds
only one (1) single-family residential dwelling with the intent of personal occupancy
or completes work as an owner/occupant of a single-family residential dwelling shall
be excluded from this licensing requirement.
(3)
General Building Contractor "A." Any general building contractor as defined in Section 6-111 above shall be required to hold a General Building Contractor "A" license. An individual
who builds only one (1) single-family residential dwelling with the intent of personal
occupancy shall be excluded from this licensing requirement.
(4)
Heating, Ventilating and Air Conditioning Contractor "Class-C." Any heating, ventilating
and air conditioning contractor as defined in Section 6-111 above shall be required to hold a Heating, Ventilating and Air Conditioning Contractor
"Class-C" license. An individual who builds only one (1) single-family residential
dwelling with the intent of personal occupancy or completes work as an owner/occupant
of a single-family residential dwelling shall be excluded from this licensing requirement.
(5)
Plumbing Contractor "P." Any plumbing contractor as defined in Section 6-111 above shall be required to hold a Plumbing Contractor "P" license. Prior to issuance
of this license, the plumbing contractor shall be required to provide a copy of a
current master plumber's license as issued by the State. An individual who builds
only one (1) single-family residential dwelling with the intent of personal occupancy
or completes work as an owner/occupant of a single-family residential dwelling shall
be excluded from this licensing requirement.
(c)
Prior to being issued a contractor's license, the applicant must provide proof of
competency in one (1) of the following manners:
(1)
The applicant shall provide documentation to the town that the individual who is performing
or overseeing the work has successfully passed the International Code Council (ICC)
exam, or
(2)
The applicant shall provide documentation that it has received a comparable contractor's
license from a municipality or other government agency that has the same or higher
application requirements.

All contractors shall be required to submit a certificate of general liability insurance
with coverage of not less than one million dollars ($1,000,000.00) for injury to or
death of one (1) person and one million dollars ($1,000,000.00) for injury to or death

(a)
All buildings to be moved within the City limits shall be inspected by the Building
Official prior to the move. Approval by the Building Official is required prior to
any permit being issued for the relocation and rehabilitation of the building within
the City limits. Any building approved by the Building Official will be required to
meet the same standards as new construction.
(b)
The following fees for moving of buildings shall be charged:
(1)
A seventy-five-dollar inspection fee shall be payable for each building inspected
for the purpose of moving the building to a location within the City limits, excluding
mobile homes and modular homes.
(2)
In the event the move originates outside the City, a mileage fee charge of thirty
cents ($0.30) per mile shall also be imposed. This fee shall be calculated based on
an estimate of round trip distance to and from the location of the building to be
moved.
(3)
All fees included in this Section shall be payable prior to inspection of the building.
(4)
These fees are in addition to normal building permit fees.

(a)
The contractor shall be responsible for all construction performed pursuant to a building
permit, which work will be completed without substantial departure from the drawings
and specifications filed and approved by the City, subject to approved changes. The
contractor shall observe the following standards:
(1)
The contractor shall obey all notices and orders issued by the Building Official.
(2)
The contractor shall observe generally accepted safety standards.
(3)
The contractor shall maintain liability insurance and workers' compensation insurance
as set forth in the contractor's application. Upon request, proof of insurance shall
be provided to the Building Official.
(4)
Upon request, the contractor shall identify all subcontractors performing construction
and contracting with the contractor.
(5)
The contractor shall maintain a current address and contact telephone number with
the Building Official.
(6)
The contractor shall not proceed with construction until after the issuance of a building
permit, and any other required permits, and shall obtain the required inspections
and authorizations to proceed with the work authorized under the permit(s).
(7)
If a contractor is released from or abandons construction, the contractor shall immediately
notify the Building Official in writing. No further work shall be done on a construction
project until the Building Official is notified in writing of the intended resumption
by an owner, or a different contractor entitled to obtain a building permit.

(a)
When the Building Official determines that a contractor has committed a violation
of this Article, the Building Code or the Code, the Building Official may order a
suspension or revocation of the contractor's license.
(b)
Notification of the suspension or revocation shall be in writing and shall be delivered
to the contractor by certified mail to the contractor's last known address, as contained
on the contractor's application. The notification shall state in reasonable detail
the essential facts and reasons for said action and shall advise the contractor of
the right to submit a written appeal to the Building Official within fifteen (15)
days, setting forth in detail the basis of the appeal.
(c)
Upon appeal, the Building Official shall have the power to affirm the suspension or
revocation and take any other disciplinary action when the Building Official determines
that the contractor has committed any of the following:
(1)
Knowing or deliberate disregard of this Article, the Building Code, or the Code.
(2)
Failure to comply with any lawful requirement of the Building Official.
(3)
Misrepresentation of a material fact in obtaining a building permit or a contractor's
license.
(4)
Employing subcontractors to perform construction for which a contractor's license
is required under this Article when such workers are neither employees nor exempt
as defined under this Article.
(5)
Requesting repeated inspections when such inspections reveal that the work performed
by the contractor fails to comply with the Building Code and such repeated noncompliance
occurs in a manner or to an extent that demonstrates that the contractor either is
negligent, not providing adequate supervision or not qualified to perform or supervise
the work.
(6)
When the Building Official suspends a contractor's license, the Building Official
shall state the period and conditions of the suspension.
(7)
When the Building Official revokes a contractor's license and the contractor does
not file an appeal, or when the Building Official revokes a contractor's license pursuant
to Subsection (c) above, the contractor shall not be granted another contractor's
license without approval of the Building Official. After revocation, the Building
Official, in deciding whether to approve a new contractor's license, shall determine
whether the contractor has demonstrated that any previous governmental disciplinary
action has resulted in the rehabilitation of the contractor to good and disciplined
character for lawful conduct as a contractor.
