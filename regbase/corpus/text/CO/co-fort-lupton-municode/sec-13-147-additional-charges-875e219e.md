For purposes of this Article, the following words shall have the meanings ascribed
hereafter:
Commercial and institutions means any nonresidential use of water.
Consumer means any person receiving water from the City waterworks.
Mains means the main pipes and connections forming a part of the City waterworks.
Service pipe means a branch pipe with its fittings and connections through which water is taken.
Street means any street, avenue, alley, lane or other thoroughfare.
Tap means an opening or connection in the mains through which water is taken.
Water plant investment fee means a fee charged for the privilege of connecting into the City's water system.

(a)
Any person annexing land to or developing within the City, or adding any new water
taps within the City limits connecting to the City's water system, who has not previously
made an adequate water dedication or cash-in-lieu-of-water fee payment or has had
that requirement waived, shall make a cash-in-lieu-of-water payment in an amount as
determined by the City or, as provided in Subsection (e) below, convey water rights
or shares of sufficient quantity and water quality as determined by the City, free
and clear of all liens and encumbrances. If water shares are to be tendered, they
must meet the following requirements:
(1)
Said water rights or shares will be of an amount to meet the annual water requirement
of all the development or annexation as measured by the number and size of water meters.
Raw water requirements per meter will be based on the schedule referenced in Subsection
(d) below.
(2)
In the event that the land to be annexed has no water rights or shares or has an amount
of water rights or shares that is not sufficient to meet the requirement, an additional
water dedication shall be made to the City to satisfy the requirements of this Section.
(3)
The City Administrator shall determine a cash-in-lieu-of-water fee on a quarterly
basis, or as needed, based upon the then-current market price for Colorado-Big Thompson
(CBT) water shares. This will be done by contacting water brokers, water consultants
and those with knowledge of current water prices. This cash-in-lieu-of-water fee will
be used as the basis for assessing water dedications required for new water taps.
(4)
The Utility Committee and the Planning Commission shall make recommendations to the
City Council in all annexation proceedings on matters appropriate to their jurisdictions,
specifically in the matters of the landowner's membership in the Northern Colorado
Water Conservancy District and how the membership relates to the foregoing water requirement
in this Section.
(5)
The cash-in-lieu-of-water fee will be paid in full or water rights will be conveyed
to the City prior to the issuance of a building permit.
(b)
A customer request for an increase in water meter size will be granted upon payment
of the difference in the cash-in-lieu-of-water fee for the meter size requested and
the meter size existing at the time of the request.
(c)
No refund shall be made to any customer for a decrease in size of water meter or abandonment
of water service. In addition, water dedications shall remain with a tap and will
not be decreased or transferred for any reason.
(d)
The water dedication requirements schedule shall be established, and amended from
time to time, by resolution of the Fort Lupton Utility Enterprise Board, to meet the
needs of the City.
(e)
The cash-in-lieu-of-water fee requirements may be suspended or modified, from time
to time, by resolution of the Fort Lupton Utility Enterprise Board, to meet the needs
of the City.

water service for any such lot prior to February 28, 2002, and not having a prior

Inasmuch as it is to the benefit of the entire community to conserve water, a property
owner who provides proof that they fixed a water leak on their property within three
(3) months of the start of high water usage may receive a credit adjustment on their
next utility bill. It is the intent of this policy that the City will share in the
cost of the excess water in order to encourage the property owner to fix any water
leaks in a timely fashion.
(a)
Customers are required to complete a form to request a review of their utility bill,
documenting when the leak occurred and other applicable information such as a plumbing
invoice, or an itemized receipt for materials and supplies purchased to repair the
leak. The burden of proof as to whether there was a leak and that the leak was repaired
is on the customer.
(b)
If, in the judgment of the City Administrator or his/her designee, the documentation
is complete and the repair was fixed within three (3) months of the start of the high
water usage, the City shall compute a credit adjustment for the next billing cycle.
The adjustment shall be based upon the previous year's history, or if there is no
history, the adjustment shall be calculated based on the average usage in the City
for comparable customer types with water usage. If the leak occurred during December—February,
which is utilized to calculate "summer" sewer rates, then the "summer" sewer rate
shall be recalculated. In no case shall a utility bill be adjusted in which high consumption
occurred for three (3) consecutive billing cycles.

(b)
Water service may be discontinued upon request by the owner, lessee or user of water.
The City may require an affidavit signed by the owner, lessee or user prior to shut-off.
The affidavit shall include the service address, reason for the shut-off request and
whether the property is owner occupied, tenant occupied or vacant. Water shall not
be discontinued if the reason for the requested shut-off is related to a landlord-tenant
dispute. If the shut-off is granted, there shall be a fee of fifteen dollars ($15.00)
to restore the water service.
(c)
Every time a water service employee takes a reading of the meter, other than the normal
monthly reading, there shall be a charge of fifteen dollars ($15.00).
(d)
If a customer requests a meter check, high consumption check, leak check or reports
a frozen meter yoke, the customer will not be charged for the first service call in
a calendar year. The customer will be charged fifteen dollars ($15.00), on the water
bill, each time thereafter.
(e)
An emergency turn-off due to a water leak in the service line will not be charged
a turn-off fee, but the customer will be charged a fifteen-dollar turn-on fee, on
the utility bill, if the City turns on the water.
(f)
Users of water from a designated City fire hydrant shall be assessed a twenty-five
dollar ($25.00) permit fee which shall be valid for a period of one (1) year. The
permit shall list the party taking the water, the main place of business address and
the purpose for taking the water and specific location of hydrant used. The user shall
also be required to pay for water taken at the current water rate. In the event any
quantity charges remain unpaid sixty (60) days after the completion of the project,
the user shall not be permitted to use Fort Lupton municipal water again until all
outstanding amounts have been paid, and all remedies available at law shall be pursued
to collect the unpaid water taken. All users of water from fire hydrants shall apply
for a hydrant meter from the City and supply their own backflow prevention equipment,
or may use a water meter provided by the City after payment of a one thousand five
hundred dollar ($1,500.00) deposit. Any costs for damage to the water meter will be
deducted from the deposit.
(g)
There shall be no charge for unmetered standby fire protection service to the Fort
Lupton Fire Protection District, but the Fire Department shall report the quantity
used to the Finance Department on a monthly basis.
(h)
Users of water for new construction shall be assessed a twenty-five-dollar fee, per
month, to cover costs of water used during the time of construction, excluding the
use of water for seeding or sodding a lawn. This policy authorizes use of water only
for the building for which the permit is issued. Twenty-five dollars ($25.00) shall
be charged and collected at the time a building permit is issued. The charge shall
be twenty-five dollars ($25.00) per month thereafter, with no proration of days, until
the water meter is installed and turned on. The remaining amounts due shall be paid
prior to the issuance of the certificate of occupancy. A water meter shall be installed
prior to outside seeding or sodding to measure and bill the owner for the actual water
usage.
