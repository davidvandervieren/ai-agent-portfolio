Editor's note— Ord. 2015-995 §§3, 4, adopted Dec. 7, 2015, repealed §§6-1—6-5 of Art. I and enacted new sections
as set out herein. The former §§6-1—6-5, pertained to entertainment licenses and derived
from §§5-69-1—5-69-6 of the prior code.
It shall be unlawful for any person to engage in the business of selling tangible
personal property at retail or the furnishing of services without first having obtained
a license therefor. Such license shall be granted and issued by the City Clerk and
shall be in force and effect until the 31st day of December of the year in which it
is issued, unless sooner revoked.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
It shall be the duty of each licensee on or before January 1st of each year during
which this Article remains in effect to obtain a renewal thereof if the license remain
in the business or liable to account for the tax herein provided, nothing herein contained
shall be construed to empower the City Clerk to refuse such renewal except revocation
for cause of the licensee's prior license.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
In case business is transacted at one (1) or more separate premises by one (1) person,
a separate license for each place of business shall be required.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
Any person engaged in the business of selling tangible personal property at retail
or furnishing of service in the City, without having secured a license therefore,
except as specifically provided herein, shall be guilty of a violation of this Article.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
Each license shall be numbered and shall show the name, residence, place and character
of business of the licensee and shall be posted in a conspicuous place in the place
of business for which it is issued.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
For each license issued under this Article, a fee to be set by resolution of the City
Council, shall be paid and shall accompany the application. A further fee to be set
by resolution to the City Council, shall be paid each year for which said license
is renewed. No license shall be transferable.
(Ord. 2015-995 §4)
It is unlawful for any person, corporation, partnership or other legal entity to engage
in the sales practice of selling or offering for sale or taking orders for any goods,
wares, merchandise, commodities, magazines (except newspapers) or services from or
upon private property outside of an enclosed structure by a person not the owner or
a lessee of such private property. This Section shall not apply to food products grown
or raised by the person so having them for sale and that are products of the State
as allowed by state statute. This Section shall not apply to local nonprofit organizations
and garage sales as permitted by Section 6-163 .
(Ord. 99-737)
It is unlawful for any person to engage in the sales practices described below before
obtaining a sales license from the City Clerk, to wit:
(1)
Selling or offering for sale or taking orders for any goods, wares, merchandise, commodities,
magazines (except newspapers) or services from or upon any street, alley, sidewalk,
park or property owned or controlled by the public or by the City; or
(2)
Selling or offering for sale or taking orders for any goods, merchandise, commodities,
magazines, toys or services, to residents, by any person going to a residential house
or houses, or residential building or within a residential apartment or condo building,
without the express invitation of an established customer at the residence of contact.
(3)
None of the foregoing sales practices are allowed between sunset and 8:00 a.m.
(4)
Vendors of meat and seafood products engaging in the foregoing sales practices must,
to obtain a license, and while engaging in the sales practices have a current Weld
County Health Department Certificate of Inspection for the product being sold.
(5)
As used herein the word person includes any individual, corporation, partnership or
other legal entity.
(Prior code 5-255-1; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 98-725; Ord. 2006-883)
Any person desiring to engage in any of the sales practices set forth in Section 6-6 shall make an application in writing to the City Clerk with the applicant's background
information contained therein, and before a license is issued, shall file with the
Clerk a bond in the sum of five hundred dollars ($500.00) running to the City for
the benefit of all citizens who may be contacted by the person to hold the license.
Each person to be engaging in the practices in Section 6-6 must provide applicable background information and must be named on the bond posted,
as it may be amended from time to time.
(Prior code 5-255-2; Ord. 98-725)
Each application shall contain the name and residence address of the applicant and/or
usual place of business, the nature of the product or services to be sold and references
to all other licenses held, denied or suspended from any other state, municipality
or county. Said application shall contain such further relevant information as may
required by the City Clerk, such as a description of vehicles to be used and proposed
hours of operation. Any missing or inaccurate information shall be cause for denial
or cancellation of the license.
(Prior code 5-255-3; Ord. 98-725)
Upon receiving an application, the City Clerk shall collect the application fee, including
fees for investigation, and refer the application to the Police Department for investigation
as is necessary to determine the truth of the facts set forth in the application and
to ascertain whether the applicant has any prior criminal convictions. The license
shall be denied to any person who has a history of abuses of sales practices or criminal
convictions related thereto.
(Prior code 5-255-4; Ord. 98-725)
Each person to be engaging in the sales practices in Section 6-6 shall be issued an identification badge by the City, which shall be worn at all times
that the person is engaging in said sales practices. Failure to display such badge
shall be prima facie evidence that the person has not been issued a license to engage
in the sales practices specified herein.
(Ord. 98-725)
Persons engaging in the sales practices in Section 6-6 and not having a license are committing criminal acts, as well as engaging in a public
nuisance. Law enforcement officers contacting such persons may arrest them and/or
issue them a municipal summons and complaint and/or seize merchandise, vehicles, order
books, samples or other objects used in the facilitation of these acts as evidence,
and abatement of the public nuisance.
(Ord. 98-725)
The City Clerk shall set the application fee herein to cover costs of the City, which
shall be not less than fifty dollars ($50.00) per application. The City Clerk shall
set the annual license fee herein which shall be not less than fifty dollars ($50.00)
per person for each license, or for each person operating under each license.
(Prior code 5-255-7; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 98-725)
Licenses may be renewed for subsequent one-year periods based upon continuing sales
practices in the public interests and qualifications of each person operating under
the license.
(Prior code 5-255-8; Ord. 98-725)
(a)
Sections 6-6 through 6-13 shall not apply to food products which are grown or raised by the person so having
them for sale and which are products of the State.
(b)
No sales license is required for local nonprofit organizations such as the Boy Scouts,
Girl Scouts, school districts, organized sports program, VFW, Moose Lodge and church
groups.
(c)
No license is required for persons having established routes or customers, where contact
is initiated by invitation or longstanding practice.
(Ord. 98-725)
No person, either as owner, licensee, manager, officer or agent or in any other capacity,
shall give, conduct, produce, present or offer, for gain or profit or for admission
to which the public is required to pay a fee, any of the following entertainments,
exhibitions or performances, to wit: All entertainments of theatrical, dramatic or
operatic character, lectures, readings, menageries, exhibitions of moving pictures,
circuses, merry-go-rounds, musical entertainments, concerts, dances, bazaars and all
other exhibitions, performances, entertainments or amusement devices not specifically
mentioned herein, at any place in the City, other than within a duly licensed theater,
opera house, hall or other enclosure or place, without a license issued for that purpose,
which said license shall be issued and procured in the manner hereinafter set out
in this Article.
(Ord. 2015-995 §5)
Any person desiring to produce, present, conduct or offer for gain or profit any of
the entertainments, exhibitions or performances mentioned in Section 6-15 , at any place within the City, other than within a duly licensed place as hereinafter
provided for, shall make application to the City Clerk in writing, setting out the
full name of the applicant, a description of the kind of entertainment he or she intends
to offer and the place at which it is desired to produce, present, conduct or offer
such entertainment, exhibition or performance. Upon the payment by such applicant
to the City Clerk of the license fee hereinafter specified, the City Clerk shall issue
to such applicant a license authorizing him or her to conduct, produce, present or
offer the kind of entertainment specified in such license, at the place designated
and for the period of time specified in such license.
(Ord. 2015-995 §5)
In any case where any person shall desire to produce, present, conduct or offer any
entertainment specified in Section 6-15 at any place other than a duly licensed place, such as is hereinafter provided for,
the amount of the license fee to be charged for the same shall be as follows: not
less than two dollars and fifty cents ($2.50) or more than twenty-five dollars ($25.00)
for each and every performance.
(Ord. 2015-995 §5)
Any person, the owner or lessee of any theater, opera house, auditorium, hall or other
place at or in which it is desired or intended to produce, offer, present or carry
on any of the entertainments mentioned in Section 6-15 , who desires to secure a license for such theater, opera house, auditorium, hall
or other place, shall be granted a license upon the following conditions: the applicant
shall make an application in writing to the City Clerk setting out his or her full
name and residence if an individual, and if a corporation, the full name and residence
of its principal officers, and also a description of the place for which a license
is desired. Thereupon the City Clerk shall issue or cause to be issued a license to
such applicant upon payment to the City of Fort Lupton of the fee of seventy-five
dollars ($75.00) per year.
(Ord. 2015-995 §5)
The Mayor shall have the right in his or her discretion to suspend the payment of
the license fee required by Section 6-18 of this Article when the object of any entertainment mentioned in Section 6-15 hereof is to raise money for religious, charitable or other like commendable purposes.

(a)
The following mobile retail food vending vehicles are recognized by this Section:
(1)
Mobile retail food establishment; retail food establishment that reports to and operates
from a commissary and is readily moveable, is a motorized wheeled vehicle or a towed
wheeled vehicle designed and equipped to serve food.
(2)
Pushcart: a non-self-propelled vehicle limited to serving commissary prepared or prepackaged
food a non-potentially hazardous food unless the equipment is commercially designed
and approved to handle food preparation and service.
(b)
Mobile retail food establishments and pushcarts shall be permitted in all non-residential
zoning districts subject to the following:
(1)
Operation in parks with permanent concession facilities is prohibited when park concessions
are in operations.
(2)
Exceptions. Zoning and separation requirements do not apply during special events.
(c)
The criteria for operation are as follows:
(1)
A Business License is required.
(2)
Licenses must be kept with the mobile retail food establishment or pushcart and presented
to City officials upon request.
(3)
Operations are responsible for maintaining trash receptacles and shall maintain all
areas used for food vending in a safe, clean and attractive condition.
(4)
Mobile retail food establishments and pushcarts must be removed from any site at the
end of each business day (unless otherwise approved).
(5)
Operator must obey all parking and traffic laws.
(6)
Mobile retail food establishments and pushcarts must not obstruct pedestrian or bicycle
access/passage and must not obstruct parking lot circulation or impede traffic flow.
(7)
Structures, canopies, tables or chairs are not permitted to be set up around the mobile
retail food establishment or pushcart.
(8)
Operators must dispose of all waste in accordance with Weld County Health Department
regulations.
