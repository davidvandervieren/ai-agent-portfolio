The skate park at Vincent Park, and any others subsequently built or maintained by
the City, shall be open to the public for use only under such terms as set forth herein,
or as later adopted by resolution of the City Council.
(Ord. 2001-791)
This skate park, as all City parks, is "use at your own risk." All persons using the
City skate park by their use agree to assume the risk of such use, understanding that
damages to property or personal injury or even death may occur from accidental or
intentional causes at this facility. No amount of design or installation care, nor
regulation or equipment requirements can or will eliminate such risks. While the incidence
of injuries at skate park facilities is low nationwide, and their use is not inherently
or uniquely dangerous, any physical activity combining movement with hard surfaces
(i.e. walking on a sidewalk) has such risks. This skate park will be offered for use
primarily without City supervision. Those having custody of children under eighteen
(18) years of age (parents, guardians, caretakers) have the duty to provide whatever
supervision, training or equipment deemed by them appropriate to minimize such risks.
(Ord. 2001-791)
It shall be unlawful for any person to fail to follow the regulations, set forth by
the City, attached to the ordinance codified herein as Exhibit "A," a copy of which
is on file at the City Clerk's office, or later adopted by the City Council by resolution
or posted at the skate park, including hours of use and required equipment. It shall
be unlawful for any person to disobey the orders for safe use of the park by any City
employee while on duty at the skate park, and such employee has the authority to temporarily
or permanently prohibit use to any person not following the regulations or posing
a danger to themselves or others.
(Ord. 2001-791)
