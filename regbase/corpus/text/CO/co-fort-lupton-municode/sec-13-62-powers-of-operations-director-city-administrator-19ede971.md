There is hereby created and established a water system and water treatment facilities
for the purpose of management, maintenance, care and operation of the water system
and water treatment facilities of the City.
(Prior code 12-510-1; Ord. 96-698 §2)
The Operations Director shall have the immediate control and management of all things
pertaining to the City waterworks system, and the Operations Director shall perform
all acts necessary for the prudent, efficient and economical management and protection
of said waterworks. The Fort Lupton Utility Enterprise Board shall have the power,
by written resolution, to prescribe such other and further rules and rates as it may
deem necessary. Technical services of a qualified engineer may be used in all cases
wherein the Operations Director does not have the requisite engineering knowledge
and experience to make decisions requiring such technical services. The City Administrator
is authorized to promulgate regulations, guidelines and procedures, consistent with
this Code adopted by the Fort Lupton Utility Enterprise Board, as are reasonably necessary
and appropriate to implement the rules and policies of the Board.
(Prior code 12-510-2; Ord. 96-698 §2; Ord. 99-739)
The Finance Director shall keep a correct account of all receipts and materials furnished
to water users and shall collect the same and deposit the proceeds so collected to
the credit of the Utility Enterprise Fund and in accordance with the policies of the
Fort Lupton Utility Enterprise Board.
(Prior code 12-510-3; Ord. 96-698 §2; Ord. 99-739)
Whenever the Operations Director deems it necessary, the Operations Director may inspect
the condition of all pipes, motors, meters and water fixtures, or the manner in which
the water is used. The Operations Director shall have the authority to make, or cause
to be made, any repairs or alterations to the water user's service as the Operations
Director deems reasonably necessary. The Operations Director shall be vigilant to
protect and remedy all abuses, whether from waste or other improper use of water.
(Prior code 12-510-5; Ord. 96-698 §2)
All water and water rights, water mains and lines, water meters, water treatment plant,
water storage, water pressure systems water wells and appurtenances thereto, machinery,
equipment and supplies used by the City to supply its water users with water shall
be known as the "Water System and Water Treatment Facilities"; provided however, that
the water service line from the curb stop to the structure or property served shall
be regarded as the property of the owner of said structure or property served.
(Prior code 12-510-6; Ord. 96-698 §2)
(a)
All fire hydrants shall be a part of the City water systems. Every hydrant shall be
tested at least annually.
(b)
No person shall use or operate any hydrant or other valves on the City water system
other than authorized City employees and members of the Fire Department for fire suppression
purposes and which is accessible to any public highway, alley or private way open
to or generally used by the public, unless such person first secures a permit for
use from the Public Works Director and the City Administrator.
(c)
Only wrenches specifically designed for operation of fire hydrants shall be used in
said operation, and all other tools are strictly forbidden.
(Prior code 12-510-7; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 2015-994 §52)
Users of water from a designated City fire hydrant shall be assessed a permit fee
which shall be valid for a period of one (1) year. The user shall also be required
to pay for water at the then-current rate. There shall be no charge for unmetered
standby fire protection service the Fort Lupton Fire Protection District. The Fort
Lupton Utility Enterprise Board reserves the right to cause the water to be shut off
in the water mains when the Operation Director deems the shut off to be necessary.
The Operations Director, for the purpose of repairing or making connections for extensions
to the water system or water treatment facilities or for the purpose of cleaning the
same, may shut off the water at any time; provided that before the water shall be
turned off from any school or public building, the Operations Director shall notify
the occupant or person in charge of such building; and in all cases of turn off for
such repairs or connections not determined to be an emergency by the Operations Director,
affecting industrial, commercial or residential buildings, the Operations Director
shall notify the occupant or person in charge of such buildings.
(Prior code 12-405-16; Ord. 96-698 §2; Ord. 99-739)
