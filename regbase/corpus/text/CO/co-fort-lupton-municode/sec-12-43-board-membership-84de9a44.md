The existing Water and Sewer Enterprises of the City, consolidated as one (1) "Utility
Enterprise," is hereby formally recognized as a government owned and operated business
pursuant to Article X, Section 20(2)(d) of the Colorado Constitution, Section 37-45.1-101,
et seq., C.R.S. and other applicable statutes and Chapter 13 of this Code and, by
and through its governing body, continues to be authorized to issue its own revenue
bonds. The Utility Enterprise of the City shall have no authority to levy or collect
any taxes, whether sales taxes, use taxes, ad valorem taxes or otherwise. The City
shall continue to maintain separate financial accounts for the receipt and disbursement
of funds related to the operation of this enterprise.
(Ord. 95-675; Ord. 98-733)
The existing Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise of the City is hereby formally
recognized as a government owned and operated business pursuant to Article 10, Section
20(2)(d) of the Colorado Constitution, Section 37-45.1-101, et seq., C.R.S., and Chapter
13 of this Code and, by and through its governing body, continues to be authorized
to issue its own revenue bonds. The Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise of the
City shall have no authority to levy or collect any taxes, whether sales taxes, use
taxes, ad valorem taxes or otherwise. The City shall continue to maintain separate
financial accounts for the receipt and disbursement of funds related to the operation
of this enterprise.
(Ord. 95-675)
There is hereby created a Board of Commissioners to manage the City's Utility Enterprise
and Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise, to be known as the Fort Lupton Utilities
Enterprise Board (the "Board"), which shall be the governing body of the Utility Enterprise
and the Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise.
(Ord. 95-675; Ord. 98-733)
The Board shall consist of the elected Mayor and members of City Council. The City
Administrator and Finance Director shall be ex officio, nonvoting members. The City
Clerk shall serve as secretary to the Board.
(Ord. 95-675)
Meetings of the Board shall be open, public meetings, subject to the requirements
of the Open Meetings Law, Section 24-6-401, et seq., C.R.S. Executive sessions may
be held as provided in the Open Meetings Law. Records of the Board are public records,
subject to the provisions of the Open Records Law, Section 24-72-201, et seq., C.R.S.
(Ord. 95-675)
The Board shall establish and prescribe the time and place of its meetings, provided
that the Board shall meet at least once per month. Meetings may coincide with City
Council meetings. The Secretary shall record the minutes of all meetings, including
the votes of all members on all questions, and shall separately maintain and preserve
the records of the Utility Enterprise and Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise.
(Ord. 95-675; Ord. 98-733)
The City Administrator shall provide staff support to the Board.
(Ord. 95-675)
The Board shall have and exercise the full power and authority of the City in connection
with the Utility Enterprise and Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise and the property
and rights connected therewith or incidental thereto, including all authority available
by law to water activity enterprises, and may utilize revenue from any lawful source,
subject to the following limitations:
(1)
The Board shall establish and may amend from time to time the rates and fees for service
by the enterprises. Different rates and fees may be established for different cases
of service. Rates must be sufficient, together with other revenues, to meet all expenditures,
including:
a.
All operations and maintenance.
b.
All debt service requirements and current capital expenditures;
c.
Reasonable and prudent reserves;
d.
Rates for service outside the City limits, if authorized by City Council, shall be
no less than one hundred fifty percent (150%) of rates for comparable service inside
the City limits;
(2)
Rates and fees shall be established or amended by resolution of the Board, following
the same procedures as are prescribed for an ordinance of the City Council, including
publication.
(3)
The Board shall establish separate annual budgets for these enterprises, and develop
long-range plans and budget projections with the City Administrator. The City Administrator
and City Council shall establish separate annual budgets for the Utility Enterprise
and Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprise and long-range plans and budget projections.
All actions of the Board shall be consistent with the budgets approved by City Council.
There are hereby created separate funds for the Utility Enterprise and the Storm Water
Drainage Facility Enterprise to account for their separate revenues and expenditures.
(4)
The management of the Utility and Storm Water Drainage Facility Enterprises, and the
authority over the personnel thereof shall be as set forth in Article III of Chapter 2 of this Code as amended by Ordinance 94-633.
(5)
The Board shall have no authority to levy or collect any taxes, whether sales taxes,
use taxes, ad valorem taxes or otherwise.
(Ord. 95-675; Ord. 98-733)
The existing Golf Course Enterprise of the City is hereby formally recognized as a
government owned and operated business as defined by Article X, Section 20(2)(d) of
the Colorado Constitution and other applicable statutes and by and through its governing
body continues to be authorized to issue its own revenue bonds. The principal function
of the Golf Course Enterprise will be the financing and operation of the City golf
course. The Golf Course Enterprise shall have no authority to levy or collect any
taxes, whether sales taxes, use taxes, ad valorem taxes or otherwise. The City shall
continue to maintain separate financial accounts for the receipt and disbursement
of funds related to the operation of this enterprise, and shall maintain separate
operating records for this enterprise.
(Ord. 95-679 §1)
The City Council is the governing body of the Golf Course Enterprise. Meetings of
the Council as the governing body of this enterprise shall be open, public meetings,
subject to the requirements of the Open Meetings Law, Section 24-6-401, et seq., C.R.S.
Executive sessions may be held as provided in the Open Meetings Law. Records of the
Board are public records, subject to the provisions of the Open Records Law, Section
24-72-201, et seq., C.R.S. Meetings may coincide with City Council meetings. The management
of the Golf Course Enterprise and the authority over the personnel thereof shall be
as set forth in Article III of Chapter 2 of this Code as amended by Ordinance 94-633, or may be contracted for by the City
Council in whole or in part. If, as contemplated, management of the golf course is
contracted for, in whole or in part, the City shall select the representative of the
City who shall, with consultation and approval of the Council, have authority to coordinate
the operation of the golf course with the representative of the management company.
The governing body shall approve the budget for the golf course and regulate the water
use and carry out the augmentation program, and approve the compensation owed to the
City by the Golf Course Enterprise for these and other matters, such as administrative
costs incurred by the City. The governing body may delegate to appointed officials
or executives of the City the responsibility and authority for signing documentation
needed in the financing and operation of the enterprise.
(Ord. 95-679 §1)
The Recreation Enterprise of the City is hereby formally recognized as a government-owned
and -operated business as defined by Article X, Section 20(2)(d) of the Colorado Constitution
and applicable statutes of the state and, through its governing body, is authorized
to issue its own revenue bonds. The Recreation Enterprise has been functioning as
such since January 1, 2004. The Recreation Enterprise may also be referred to as the
Recreation Department. The functions of the Recreation Enterprise shall include:
(1)
Administering, implementing and co-coordinating recreation, youth and senior programs
and other leisure activities, and
(2)
Managing, maintaining and operating City recreational facilities, including but not
limited to the Community Center and Recreation Center.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
There is hereby created a Board of Commissioners to manage the Recreation Enterprise,
to be known as the Recreation Board, which shall be the governing body of the Recreation
Enterprise.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
The Recreation Board shall consist of the Elected Mayor and members of the City Council,
unless the City Council, by resolution, appoints different persons to serve as Recreation
Board members. The City Administrator and Finance Director shall be ex officio, nonvoting,
member of the Recreation Board.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
Recreation Board meetings shall be held pursuant to Section 12-26 of this Code, or
as otherwise provided by City Council resolution.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
The Finance Director of the City shall be the Chief Financial Officer of the Recreation
Enterprise. The City shall maintain separate financial accounts for the receipt and
disbursement of funds related to the operation of the Recreation Enterprise.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
The City Administrator shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Recreation Enterprise,
and the Recreation Director of the City shall be the Chief Operating Officer of the
Recreation Enterprise, unless otherwise established by City Council resolution.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
The City may provide administrative support and staff services to the Recreation Enterprise.
The City may charge the Recreation Enterprise for any such support and services.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
The Recreation Enterprise shall have no authority to levy any taxes, whether sales
taxes, use taxes, ad valorem taxes or otherwise. The Recreation Enterprise receives
and will continue to receive operating funds from a voter-approved ad valorem tax,
initiated by the City, which are not subject to the revenue and expense provisions
of TABOR, as provided by that initiative.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
There is hereby created, within the Recreation Enterprise, the Fort Lupton Recreation
Foundation, in order to receive and disburse grants, contributions and other gifts
directed to it for the benefit of the Recreation Enterprise. Recreation Foundation
accounts shall be kept separate and apart from other Recreation Enterprise or City
funds. Recreation Foundation accounts may not be used for, borrowed from or pledged
to any other City funds. Recreation Foundation funds may be used to pay for services
for the Recreation Foundation provided by the City or outside vendors for Recreation
Foundation purposes (i.e. the cost of planting a tree donated to the Recreation Foundation).
Recreation Foundation grants, contributions or gifts must be used in the manner and
purpose under which they are given, if a use is so specified and if such use is approved
by the Recreation Board; or, if no specific use is specified, for the general benefit
of the Recreation Enterprise, and the people it serves. One of the authorized purposes
of the Recreation Foundation may be to support low-cost or no-cost admission and use
of facilities.
(Ord. 2004-820, Pt. 1)
