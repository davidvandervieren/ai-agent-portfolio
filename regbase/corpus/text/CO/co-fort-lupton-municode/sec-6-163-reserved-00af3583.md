Any person owning or operating a skating rink shall be required to obtain a license
from the City, upon payment of a license fee of seventy-five dollars ($75.00) prior
to conducting such business. If such license is renewed for more than one (1) year,
a bond accepted by the City Council shall be filed with the City Clerk in the penal
sum of five hundred dollars ($500.00), said bond to have two (2) sureties besides
the principals.
(Prior code 5-18-2; Ord. 645 §1, 1993)
