There is hereby created and established a free public library, in and for the City,
pursuant to state law.
(Ord. 2015-994 , §16)
(a)
The management of the library shall be vested in the Local Library Board of Trustees
which shall consist of not fewer than seven (7) trustees pursuant to C.R.S. Section
24-90-108(1). The Board of Trustees shall be responsible for the adoption and implementation
of operating policies and procedures to manage the library. The Board of Trustees
shall report to the City of Fort Lupton and Weld County School District RE-8, in an
advisory capacity. The Board of Trustees of the local library shall be set up as follows:
(1)
The Board shall be comprised not less than seven (7) voting members.
(2)
Each Board member's term shall not exceed four (4) years and no Board member may serve
more than two (2) terms.
(3)
No trustee shall be an employee of either the City of Fort Lupton or Weld County School
District RE-8.
(4)
There shall be one (1) ex-officio member comprised of either the Mayor or a City of
Fort Lupton City Councilmember.
(5)
There shall be one (1) ex-officio Weld County School District RE-8 Board member.
(6)
Three (3) trustees shall be appointed by the Weld County School District RE-8, School
Board.
(7)
Three (3) trustees shall be appointed by City of Fort Lupton City Council.
(8)
One (1) trustee shall be appointed by the six (6) voting members as described above.
(9)
The Weld County School District RE-8 shall have one (1) non-voting liaison to the
Board.
(10)
The City of Fort Lupton shall have one (1) non-voting liaison to the Board.
(11)
The Weld County School District RE-8 shall appoint a non-voting student member to
the Board.
(b)
Such trustees shall constitute a body corporate by the name and style of "the Board
of Trustees" of the City of Fort Lupton, of Weld County School District RE-8", and
in that name may receive by gift, grant or devise, real and personal property for
the use of the library and be party to all suites, proceedings and contracts the same
as municipal corporations in this state.
(Ord. 2015-994 , §16)
