For purposes of this Article, the following words shall have the meanings ascribed
hereafter:
Amusement center means a place of business where any person maintains mechanical amusement devices
as a sole business or in conjunction with some other business.
Amusement device means any device which upon insertion of a coin, slug, token, plate or disc, or payment
of a consideration, may be used by the public for use as a game, entertainment, amusement
or a test of skill, either mental or physical, whether or not registering a score.
It shall include such devices as marble machines, pinball machines, skill ball, mechanical
grab machines, pool tables and all games, operations or transactions similar thereto
under whatever they may be indicated.
Applicant means any person requesting a license pursuant to this Article.
Jukebox means any music-vending machine, contrivance or device which upon the insertion of
a coin, slug, token, plate or disc, or payment of any price, operates or may be operated
for the emission of songs, music or similar amusement.
(Prior code 5-489-1; Ord. 645 §1, 1993)
Any person displaying for public patronage or keeping for operation any jukebox or
mechanical amusement device shall be required to obtain a license from the City, upon
payment of a license fee prior to conducting business of an amusement center. Application
for such license shall be made upon a form to be supplied by the City Clerk for that
purpose. Schools are specifically exempted from the licensing requirements of this
Article.
(Prior code 5-489-2)
The application for such license shall contain the following information:
(1)
The applicant's name and address, age and date and place of birth;
(2)
Any convictions of the applicant;
(3)
The name and address of the amusement center;
(4)
Description of machines to be covered by the license; and
(5)
Number of machines to be covered by the license.
(Prior code 5-489-3)
The Chief of Police shall investigate the location wherein it is proposed to operate
such machines, ascertain if the applicant is a person of good moral character and
either approve or disapprove the application. In addition, the applicant must present
to the City Clerk approval of all wiring and connections to the machines as determined
by the State Electrical Inspector.
(Prior code 5-489-4)
Each applicant, before being granted a license, shall pay an annual license fee for
the privilege of operating or maintaining for the operation each jukebox or mechanical
amusement device of ten dollars ($10.00) per machine on the premises for any portion
of the period for which the license is issued pursuant to this Article.
(Prior code 5-489-5)
All licenses granted pursuant to this Article shall be for a term of one (1) year.
Said term shall commence on January 1st of each year and terminate December 31st of
the same year. Applications for a license occurring at any other time during the year
shall be treated the same as if they were made for one (1) calendar year and shall
terminate on the same date as all other licenses issued pursuant hereto. No proration
shall be permitted.
(Prior code 5-489-6)
The license or licenses provided for in this Article shall be posted permanently and
conspicuously at the location of the machine, in the premises wherein the device is
to be operated or maintained to be operated.
(Prior code 5-489-7)
The renewal of any of the licenses granted pursuant to this Article may be had by
payment of the licensing fee along with a statement that the information listed on
the original license application is still true and correct to the applicant's knowledge,
or a statement listing those items of information required for a license application
which have changed in the year since the license was granted or last renewed.
(Prior code 5-489-8)
No person under the age of nineteen (19) years shall serve in the capacity of manager
of any establishment licensed pursuant to the provisions of this Article.
(Prior code 5-489-9)
Nothing in this Article shall be construed to permit any unlawful gambling or wagering
within the City.
(Prior code 5-489-10)
The City Clerk may suspend or revoke any license granted pursuant to this Article
upon a finding of any of the following factors:
(1)
That any of the devices maintained upon the premises are being used for gambling purposes;
(2)
That repeated disturbances of public peace have been occurring within the licensed
establishment or upon any parking areas, sidewalks, access areas or grounds immediately
adjacent to the licensed premises involving patrons, employees or the holder of the
license of the establishment;
(3)
That the holder of the license or any employees thereof are illegally offering for
sale or illegally allowing to be consumed upon any parking areas, sidewalks, walkways,
access ways or grounds immediately adjacent to the licensed premises narcotics, dangerous
drugs or liquor;
(4)
That the holder of the license or any employees thereof are violating ordinances of
the City; or
(5)
That the conduct of the business constitutes a breach of the peace or is a menace
to the health, safety or general welfare of the public.
(Prior code 5-489-11)
