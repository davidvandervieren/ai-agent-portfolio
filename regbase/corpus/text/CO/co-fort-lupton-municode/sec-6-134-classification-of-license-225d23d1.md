For purposes of this Article, the following words shall have the meanings ascribed
hereafter:
(1)
Apparatus means any appliance, piece of equipment or any other project used in the
application of a massage; provided that apparatus shall not include furniture, towels,
linen, oils or showers used in conjunction with the application of a massage.
(2)
Apprentice means a person employed by a massage establishment and working under a
licensed massage technician or massage therapist for the purpose of learning the method
and practice of massage.
(3)
External baths means but is not limited to, water vapor, steam, oil, powder, sweat,
electric, salt, alcohol rubs or any other kind or character of massage.
(4)
Massage means pressure on, friction against, stroking, rubbing, kneading or other
manipulation of the body by any means with or without apparatus, including but not
limited to vibrators, infrared heat, sun lamps and external baths.
(5)
Massage establishment means an establishment having a place of business where any
person engages in, conducts or carries on, or permits to be engaged in, conducted
or carried on, any business of the manipulation of the body by means of massage as
herein defined.
(6)
Massage technician or massage therapist means any person who administers to another
person, for any form of consideration or hire, a massage, alcohol bath, electric or
magnetic procedure, manipulation of the body or other similar procedure.
(7)
Operator or massage establishment operator means any person owning or operating a
massage establishment. In the event of a corporate owner, the operator shall mean
the manager or the person in charge.
(8)
Recognized school means any institution of learning which has for its purpose the
teaching of theory, method, profession or work of massage, in which requires a resident
course of not less than five hundred (500) hours for graduation. If a school meets
state requirements, it shall be considered a recognized school for purposes of this
Code.

The following classes of persons and establishments are exempted from this Article:
(1)
Physicians, osteopaths, physical therapists, podiatrists, chiropractors and massage
therapists licensed in the State;
(2)
Registered nurses and licensed practical nurses who are licensed in the State, performing
such services in their usual nursing duties;
(3)
Beauticians and barbers duly licensed under the laws of the State in the course of
the practice of their usual and ordinary licensed vocation and profession, as defined
by the laws of this State;
(4)
Massage practices at the athletic department of any state accredited school, college,
university or seminary, or in connection with the conduct of professional athletics;
(5)
Hospitals, clinics, nursing and convalescent homes, and other similar institutions
dedicated to medical and nursing practices, licensed under the laws of the State,
where massage and baths may be given. Employees of the respective institutions shall
be exempt from this Article while in the normal course of their employment with such
institution;
(6)
Massage practiced at a recognized school as defined in Section 6-131 (8); and
(7)
A facility which is operated for the purpose of massage therapy to be performed by
a massage therapist is not a massage parlor or massage establishment and is therefore
also exempt from the requirements of this Article.

(a)
It shall be unlawful for any person to operate a massage establishment without first
having obtained a license to do so as hereinafter provided.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for any person to perform the services of a massage technician,
therapist or apprentice without first having obtained a license to do so as hereinafter
provided.
(c)
It shall be unlawful for an operator of a massage establishment to permit any person
in his or her massage establishment to act as a massage technician, therapist or apprentice
unless such person is duly licensed as provided in this Article.
(d)
Each licensee shall reapply for a license annually by application, and such license
shall issue without reexamination, provided that application for reissue is made prior
to expiration of the outstanding license. The license shall be valid for a period
of one (1) year from the date of issuance. Application for renewal of the license
may be submitted along with the appropriate annual fee as hereinafter provided not
less than forty-five (45) days prior to the date of expiration.

(a)
Unlimited. This license permits the performance of massage activities by any means
defined or permitted in this Article.
(b)
Apprentice.
(1)
This license permits the performance of massage upon any person only under the direct
supervision and direction of a licensed massage technician or massage therapist who
must be present in the establishment at all times while an apprentice is performing
massage in any form.
(2)
No apprentice license shall be issued to any person who has not completed a minimum
of one hundred (100) hours in massage theory from a recognized school.
(3)
The license issued under this Section shall be valid for a period not to exceed one
(1) year, and no extension or reissue shall be granted unless satisfactory evidence
shall be shown that the applicant has completed five hundred (500) hours of training
from a recognized school or has completed the course of instruction from such school.
Only one (1) extension or reissue shall be granted to any apprentice.

(a)
Operator. The owner of a massage establishment seeking to obtain a license shall submit
a written application to the City Clerk so that it may be determined that:
(1)
The applicant is twenty-one (21) years of age or older.
(2)
The applicant, including each member of the firm, partnership or association if the
applicant is a firm, partnership or association, and including the actual manager
of the business if the applicant is a corporation, is of good moral character. If
the applicant is a corporation, the date and the state of incorporation, the name
and address of the registered agent for the State, and names and addresses of officers
and directors of said corporation shall be stated.
(3)
The applicant, if he or she performs the duties of a massage technician or massage
therapist, or manages or supervises the therapists, is a licensed massage therapist.
(4)
The applicant, including each of the persons mentioned in Subsection (2) above, shall
submit two (2) portrait photographs and shall be fingerprinted by the Police Department,
where such parties are actively involved in the operation of the establishment.
(5)
The applicant, including each of the persons mentioned in Subsection (2) above, has
been examined by and has been issued a health certificate from a medical doctor stating
that said applicant has, within thirty (30) days immediately prior to application,
been examined and found to be free from any contagious or communicable diseases, where
such parties are actively participating in the operation of the establishment.
(6)
The applicant, including each of the persons mentioned in Subsection (2) above, has
not been previously convicted of any violation involving moral turpitude or any offense
against the decency of the community.
(b)
Massage therapist or apprentice. Any person seeking to obtain a license as a massage
technician, massage therapist or apprentice shall submit written application to the
City Clerk containing but not limited to the following information:
(1)
The full name and present address of the applicant, and all aliases by which the applicant
is or may be known;
(2)
The previous street and City addresses of the applicant for the two (2) years preceding
the application;
(3)
At least three (3) references from residents of the State stating that the applicant
is of good moral character;
(4)
Written proof that the applicant is twenty-one (21) years of age or older;
(5)
The applicant's height, weight, color of eyes and hair;
(6)
Two (2) portrait photographs at least two (2) inches by two (2) inches, one (1) of
which must be attached to the application;
(7)
Business, occupation or employment of the applicant for the three (3) years immediately
preceding the date of application;
(8)
The massage or similar business history of the applicant; whether such person in previously
operating in this or any other City or state under license has had such license revoked
or suspended, the reason therefor, and the business activity or occupation subsequent
to such action, suspension or revocation;
(9)
All convictions involving moral turpitude or any offense against the decency of the
community for the five (5) years preceding the application;
(10)
A certificate from a medical doctor stating that the applicant has, within thirty
(30) days immediately prior to the application, been examined and found to be free
of any contagious or communicable diseases;
(11)
The applicant must furnish a diploma or certificate of graduation from a recognized
school or other institution of learning wherein the method, profession and work of
massage technician is taught;
(12)
Such other identification and information necessary to discover the truth of the matters
hereinbefore specified as required to be set forth in the application;
(13)
The applicant shall be fingerprinted by the Police Department upon submission of the
application;
(14)
Classification of license desired, whether unlimited or apprentice.

(Prior code 5-421-9)
No massage establishment license shall be issued or renewed unless inspection by the
County Health Department and City Building Inspector indicates that the establishment
complies with the following minimum requirements:
(1)
A readable sign, in compliance with all provisions of Chapter 16 of this Code, shall be posted at the main entrance identifying the business as a
massage establishment.
(2)
Minimum lighting shall be provided in accordance with the Uniform Building Code, and
in addition, either direct or indirect lighting of not less than forty (40) foot-candles
shall be provided in each room or enclosure where services are performed on patrons,
and each minimum lighting shall be in use when services are being performed.
(3)
Minimum ventilation shall be provided in accordance with the Uniform Building Code.
(4)
Adequate equipment shall be provided for disinfecting and sterilizing instruments
used in administering or practicing any of the subjects or methods of treatment. Such
instruments shall be sterilized after each use.
(5)
Hot and cold running water shall be provided at all times.
(6)
Closed cabinets or other approved storage facilities shall be provided and used for
the storage of clean linens.
(7)
Adequate bathing, dressing, locker and toilet facilities shall be provided for patrons.
A minimum of one (1) tub or shower, one (1) dressing room, one (1) toilet and one
(1) washbasin, and facilities to provide a safe and secure place to keep valuables
and personal items, shall be provided in every massage establishment; provided, however,
that if male and female patrons are to be served simultaneously at said establishment,
a separate massage room or rooms, separate dressing facilities and separate toilet
facilities shall be provided for male and female patrons.
(8)
All walls, ceilings, floors, pools, showers, bathtubs, steamrooms and all other physical
facilities for the massage parlor must be in good repair and maintained in a clean
and sanitary condition. Wet and dry heat rooms, steam or vapor rooms or cabinets,
shower compartments and toilet rooms shall be thoroughly cleaned each day the business
is in operation. Bathtubs and massage tables shall be thoroughly cleaned and disinfected
after each use. Clean and sanitary linens shall be provided for each patron of the
massage establishment. The tables shall be provided with clean and sanitary linens,
paper towels or sheets for each patron.
(9)
A minimum of one (1) separate wash facility shall be provided in each massage establishment
for the use of employees of such establishment, which basin shall provide soap or
detergent and hot and cold running water at all times and shall be located within
or as close as practical to the area devoted to the performing of massage services.
In addition, there shall be provided at each washbasin sanitary towels or other approved
facilities placed in permanently installed dispensers.
(10)
The establishment shall be in compliance with all other applicable ordinances of the
City.

(a)
Any operator of any massage establishment or any massage technician or other employee
of such massage establishment and the licensee or any person or patron in or upon
the premises is hereby prohibited from:
(1)
Engaging in prostitution, masturbation, deviate sexual intercourse, oral copulation
or other act designed or intended to arouse or gratify the sexual desires of any other
person; or
(2)
Soliciting, directly or indirectly, any person to engage in prostitution, masturbation,
deviate sexual intercourse, oral copulation or other acts designed or intended to
arouse or gratify the sexual desires of any other person, whether such prostitution,
oral copulation, deviate sexual intercourse, masturbation or other acts are intended
to occur on or off the premises of the massage establishment; or
(3)
Engaging in the display of his or her genitals or pubic areas. For purpose of this
Subsection (3), the term display shall be defined to include the exposure of those
parts of the body described hereinabove when they are less than opaquely covered.
(b)
Any operator who knows or reasonably should know that any acts prohibited by this
Section have occurred or are occurring in or upon the premises of his or her establishment
shall be deemed guilty of committing such act and shall be held liable as a principal.
(c)
No operator, massage technician or other person in the employ of the massage establishment
shall violate any federal or state law or City ordinance or any provisions of this
Article in or upon the premises of any massage establishment. Any operator who knows
or reasonably should know that any violation of this Code or provisions of this Section
is occurring or does occur in or upon the premises of said establishment, and does
not immediately act to prevent the same and report the same to the proper authorities,
shall be deemed guilty of committing such act and shall be held liable as a principal.
(d)
Each operator of a massage establishment shall conduct his or her establishment in
a decent, orderly and respectable manner, shall not serve or permit the consumption
of spirituous, vinous or malt liquor or 3.2% beer on the premises, and shall not permit
within or upon the premises the loitering of habitual drunkards or intoxicated persons,
known prostitutes, lewd or indecent displays, profanity, rowdiness, undue noise or
other disturbance or activity offensive to the sense of the average citizen or to
the inhabitants of the neighborhood in which the establishment is located.

(a)
Any license issued pursuant to this Article shall be subject to revocation or suspension
whenever the holder thereof shall violate any of the provisions of this Article or
other applicable provisions of this Code relating to massage establishments.
(b)
Upon commencement of revocation and suspension proceedings, the City Council shall
set a time and place for the hearing of the matter.
(c)
The City Clerk shall give the licensee notice of the time, place and nature of the
hearing, the authority and jurisdiction under which it is to be held, and the violation
asserted. Such notice shall be served personally or by mailing by certified mail,
return receipt requested, to the last address furnished to the City by the licensee,
at least ten (10) days prior to the hearing.
(d)
The City Council may preside over the hearing or, in the alternative, may appoint
a hearing officer who may be a Councilmember, a municipal judge or other qualified
person, or a special committee of the City Council to preside over such hearing.
(e)
The hearing officer or the chairman of the special committee shall have the power
to administer oaths and issue subpoenas.
(f)
All evidence shall be recorded electronically. If the hearing is before a hearing
officer, he or she shall prepare a summary of the evidence and shall make recommendations
in writing to the City Council within ten (10) days after the close of the hearing.
A copy of his or her summary and recommendation shall be forwarded to the licensee
at the same time. At its next regularly scheduled meeting following receipt of the
summary and recommendations, the City Council shall consider the same. In its discretion,
the City Council may reject the hearing officer's recommendations, may refer the matter
back to him or her for further proceedings, may adopt the recommendations with or
without modification, or may order a new hearing, either before the entire City Council
or a committee or member thereof.
(g)
The Mayor may summarily suspend any license hereafter issued to any person pursuant
to the Article whenever the holder thereof shall violate any of the provisions of
this Article under which such license was issued or whenever, in the judgment of the
Mayor, the public welfare requires the same. Any person may appeal to the City Council
from the Mayor's suspension, and it may terminate such suspension. Appeal from the
Mayor's suspension of the license shall be to the City Council under the procedures
hereinbefore stated.
(h)
It shall be unlawful for any person to carry on or engage in the business as herein
described in this Article while any suspension or revocation is in effect.
