The drainage fee is hereby established for the purpose of raising funds for the construction,
enlargement and maintenance of storm water drainage systems to assist in solving drainage
problems caused or contributed to by development of property. The drainage fees shall
be computed on the basis of the area of the property being developed and shall be
applied to all newly developed property in the City limits.
(Ord. 538 §1, 1985)
The fee imposed shall be calculated on the gross lot area of the real property being
developed as determined by the Building Inspector. There shall be a minimum fee charge
as set forth in the fee schedule. Construction of churches, public clinics and hospitals,
schools and public structures shall be exempt from the drainage facilities fee.
(Ord. 538 §2, 1985)
(a)
The drainage fee or fees shall be paid at the time application is made for a building
permit. Under no circumstances shall the Building Official issue a certificate of
occupancy until the drainage facilities fee is paid in full.
(b)
If more than one (1) building is to be constructed on the property being developed,
as for example, in the case of a planned unit development or a planned residential
unit, the total fees shall be allocated among the several buildings in proportion
to the square footage of the buildings, and a pro rata portion of the drainage fees
shall be paid as each application for a building permit is made.
(Ord. 538 §3, 1985)
The fee schedule shall be set by resolution of the City Council and may be amended
from time to time.
(Ord. 538 §4, 1985; Ord. 2000-762, Part 1; Ord. 2001-778)
(a)
There is hereby created a storm water drainage fee to be assessed against residential
and nonresidential utility customers in order to provide revenue to improve, repair
and maintain the City's storm water drainage infrastructure.
(b)
All nonresidential utility customers will be assessed on the square footage of flat
area (nongreenbelt) of their property, and all residential customers will be assessed
a flat fee established from the average size of their property using an ERU (equivalent
residential unit).
(c)
The fee rate shall be set by resolution of the Utility Enterprise Board and may be
amended from time to time by resolution. The initial fee rate is set by Resolution
2007-005.
(Ord. 2007-886)
