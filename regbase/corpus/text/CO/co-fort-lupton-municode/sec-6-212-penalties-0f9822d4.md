There is hereby adopted by reference by the City Council, Part 172 of Title 49, Transportation,
Code of Federal Regulations, known as Hazardous Materials Tables and Hazardous Materials
Communications Regulations. This Part 172 is promulgated by the Department of Transportation
of the Executive Department and Agency of the Federal Government, National Archives
and Records Service, GAS, Washington, D.C. 20408. This Part 172 is a part of Title
49, Code of Federal Regulations and is revised as of October 1, 1981. This Part 172
lists and classifies those materials which the Department of Transportation has designated
as hazardous materials for purposes of transportation and prescribes the requirements
for shipping papers, package marking, labeling and transport vehicle placarding applicable
to the shipping and transportation of those hazardous materials. One (1) copy of the
Code has now been filed in the office of the City Clerk and the same is hereby adopted
and incorporated as fully as if set out at length, and from the date on which this
ordinance codified in this Article shall take effect, the provisions thereof shall
be controlling within the limits of the City.
(Prior code 10-508-1)
Any person doing business within the corporate City limits who uses, stores or manufactures
hazardous materials as defined in Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations must:
(1)
Apply for a permit for such activity with the office of the City Administrator. Permits
will be required of all such entities already doing business within the City limits
and said entities shall obtain permits within sixty (60) days from the effective date
of this Article or be in violation thereof.
(2)
Accompanying the permit application, such entities must specifically note the type,
quantity and description of the defined hazardous materials in storage, use or manufacture.
Any additions or deletions of hazardous materials must be immediately reported to
the office of the City Administrator. Such lists of hazardous materials will also
be delivered to the Chief of Police and to the Fire Chief before any permit will be
granted.
(3)
Fees for such permits will be determined by the City Administrator; they should be
of a sufficient amount to cover administrative costs.
(4)
After the appropriate application for a permit has been made and the fee paid, a hearing
will be scheduled before the City Council at one (1) of its regular meetings to grant
or deny the issuance of the permit. In determining whether to grant or deny the permit,
the City Council will consider the effect of the proposed use on the health, safety
and welfare of the citizens of the City. Restrictions and conditions may be made a
part of the issuance of the permit. Only in the most extreme cases will the financial
hardship of the applicant for a permit be considered in the decision of whether to
grant or deny the permit requested.
(Prior code 10-508-2)
(a)
No person who uses, stores or manufactures hazardous materials may do so within one
thousand (1,000) feet of any residential area, school, hospital or area zoned for
residential use. Uses in violation of this provision at the time of the enactment
of the ordinance codified in this Article shall be considered a nonconforming use
and a nonconforming use permit may be issued. Such a nonconforming use shall not be
expanded, increased or enlarged. The discontinuance of a non-conforming use for a
period of more than thirty (30) days shall terminate the nonconforming use, and any
future use shall be in compliance with the provisions of this Article.
(b)
Excepted from the prohibitions contained in this Article are gasoline service stations
where the storage of approved quantities of flammable liquids is a normal part of
the operation of such business. However, such flammable liquid storage and use is
subject to fire, zoning and building codes of the City, the Fire Protection District,
the County and the State.
(Prior code 10-508-3; Ord. 645 §1, 1993)
It shall be unlawful for any person to dump, dispose of or cause to be disposed of
any hazardous material product, waste or actual hazardous material within the City
limits or within a five-mile radius of the corporate City limits. Any person found
in violation of this Section shall be subject to the maximum penalty allowed by law
and said violator shall be liable to the City for full costs of cleanup and restoration
and any other actual or consequential damages arising from such violation.
(Prior code 10-508-4)
Any person that stores, uses or manufactures hazardous materials shall immediately
notify the Police Department and the Fire Protection District, in writing, of any
changes in the location, amount and type of hazardous materials from those designated
when a permit was originally issued. Any containers used for the storage of hazardous
materials must be clearly marked as to the type of hazardous material contained therein.
All hazardous materials must be stored in such manner as to not allow those materials
to affect or become contaminated by any other hazardous materials also stored on the
same premises. Hazardous materials must be stored in suitable containers that will
not allow the material contained therein to leak from the container onto surrounding
surfaces or into the atmosphere or to pollute any water well, water way, river, lake
or stream. All hazardous material must be stored in a container, enclosure or structure
that will prevent tampering or theft of such materials. To that end, all premises
being used for the storage of hazardous materials shall be securely locked or continuously
monitored by employees or agents of the person storing such materials.
(Prior code 10-508-5)
Any person who stores, manufactures or uses hazardous materials in the course of his
or her business shall be civilly, criminally and financially responsible for accidents,
incidents or use of said materials that result in personal loss of life or health
to persons, animals and livestock, as well as direct and consequential damages to
crops, buildings, structures, land, vegetation, wildlife and the environment, and
shall be financially responsible for the costs of restoration of property to its original
condition, the costs of emergency response to hazardous materials accidents or incidents
and any other costs associated with such accident, damage, incident, use or problem
associated with such storage, use or manufacturing of hazardous materials.
(Prior code 10-508-6)
The quantity and type of hazardous materials allowed for use, storage or manufacturing
within the City shall be clearly stated by those making permit applications, and the
suitability of such type and quantity shall be determined by the City Council, with
reference to the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of the City. The City
Council reserves the authority to prohibit or terminate such storage, use or manufacturing
if at any time such storage, use or manufacturing becomes a threat to the health and
safety of the citizens or of the property located within the City. Any person seeking
or receiving a permit under the provisions of this Article shall be presumed to agree
to the provisions herein and shall, at all times, have the burden of proof that any
use, storage or manufacture of hazardous materials is safe and out of detriment to
the public safety, health and welfare.
(Prior code 10-508-7)
(a)
The Police Department is hereby designated as the emergency response authority for
hazardous material incidents occurring within the corporate limits of the City. The
Chief of Police shall have the authority to coordinate and direct operations regarding
hazardous material incidents and has the authority to seek the assistance of the Fire
Protection District or other local, state or federal law enforcement or public safety
organization.
(b)
Should at any time the use, transportation, storage or manufacturing of a hazardous
material create an immediate threat to the health, safety or welfare of the people
or property located within the City, the Chief of Police may take or cause any action
to be taken which is necessary to abate the hazard existing, including but not limited
to:
(1)
Isolation of the hazard;
(2)
Evacuation of affected areas;
(3)
Dispersion or neutralization of a hazardous substance;
(4)
Confiscation of the hazardous substance; and
(5)
Specification of abatement procedures to any person responsible for the transportation,
use, storage or manufacture of the hazardous material.

(Prior code 10-508-9)
Flammable liquids, as defined by the Code of Regulations, Title 49, as adopted by
reference herein, may be transported within the City except that such flammable liquids
may not be transported for local delivery in vehicles consisting of more units than
a tractor and a semi-trailer combined, and provided that such transporting shall be
accomplished in accordance with the Federal Motor Vehicle Carrier Safety Regulations
as promulgated by the U.S. Department of Transportation. Such transportation shall
not deviate from the routes designated on the route map as maintained and revised
by the City under the authority of the Chief of Police. As used herein, the term local
delivery shall mean delivery of or loading or unloading of goods or any other service
rendered by a motor vehicle within the City served by the routes designated on the
route map referred to herein.
(Prior code 10-508-10)
When any street, alley, highway or other public way is restricted or prohibited to
truck usage for the transportation of hazardous materials, it shall be posted with
appropriate signs giving notice thereof; and it shall be unlawful for any person to
violate the provisions of such posted signs. However, this shall not be construed
as prohibiting the driver of any truck or truck tractor from traveling over such otherwise
restricted or prohibited sections of streets, highways or other public ways for reasonable
limited distances as may be necessary to actually make local deliveries or reach his
or her destinations which occur on local, restricted or prohibited streets, or near
those particular streets, highways or other public ways; provided that the shortest
reasonable route is used to travel from and to a route designated on the route map
referred to herein. It shall be unlawful for any person to operate any vehicle in
deviation from such routes except as provided herein.
(Prior code 10-508-11)
Any person convicted of a violation of this Article shall be punished in accordance
with the provisions of Section 1-72 of this Code. The fact that a corporation or other legal entity may be primarily
responsible for a violation does not insulate any other person, employee, corporate
officer or agent from civil or criminal liability if that person, employee or corporate
officer by his or her acts or failure to act commits a violation of this Article.
Every person who transports, uses, stores or manufactures hazardous materials has
the separate legal duty to comply with the provisions of this Article. Imposition
of the penalties described herein may be in addition to any other costs of financial
responsibility incurred by the operation of this Article.
