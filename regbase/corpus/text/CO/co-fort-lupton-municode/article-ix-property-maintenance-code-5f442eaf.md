Pursuant to Title 31, Article 16, C.R.S., there is hereby adopted the 2018 International
Property Maintenance Code, published by the International Code Council, 4051 West
Flossmoor Road, Country Club Hills, Illinois 60478-5795. This code contains requirements
for existing residential and commercial buildings.
(Prior code 6-544-2, 6-544-8; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 96-683 §7; Ord. 2001-777 §6;
Ord. 2007-884, Part 12; Ord. 2014-980 §24; Ord. No. 2021-1133 , § 13, 11-2-2021)
The City Clerk shall certify to the passage of this Article and make not less than
one (1) copy of the adopted code available for inspection by the public during regular
business hours.
(Prior code 6-544-22; Ord. 645 §1, 1993; Ord. 2014-980 §25)
This Article shall apply to the jurisdiction of the City.
(Prior code 6-544-20)
The following sections of the International Property Maintenance Code are hereby revised:
" 101.1 Title. Is amended by the addition of the term "City of Fort Lupton" where indicated."
" 102.3 Application of Other Codes. Is amended by the deletion of the section and is replaced with the following: "Repairs,
additions or alterations to a structure, or changes of occupancy, shall be done in
accordance with the procedures and provisions of the current adopted building and
zoning codes."
" 103.5 Fees. Is amended by the deletion of the section and is replaced with the following: "Costs
incurred in the performance of work shall be paid by the jurisdiction. The legal counsel
of the jurisdiction shall institute appropriate action against the owner of the premises
or owner's authorized agent where the unsafe structure is or was located for the recovery
of such costs."
" 111.2 Membership of board. Is amended by the deletion this section in its entirety and replaced with the following:
"The members of the Board of Appeals shall be comprised of the members of the Board
of Adjustment."
" 111.2.1 Alternate Members. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.2.2 Chairman. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.2.3 Disqualification of member. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.2.4 Secretary. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.2.5 Compensation of members. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.3 Notice of Meeting. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 111.4 Open Hearing. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 302.3 Sidewalks and Driveways. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 302.4 Weeds. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 302.8 Motor Vehicles. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 304.14 Insect Screens. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 308 Rubbish and Garbage. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 309 Pest Elimination. Is amended by the deletion of this section in its entirety."
" 604.2 Service. Is amended by replacing "NFPA 70" with "Electrical Code adopted by the State of Colorado."
(Ord. 2006-863; Ord. 2007-884, Pt. 14; Ord. 2014-980 §26; Ord. No. 2021-1133 , § 14, 11-2-2021)
