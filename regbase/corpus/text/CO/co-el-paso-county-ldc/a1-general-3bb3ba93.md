A.1.1.

  Purpose
The following documents are referenced within this Code, and are utilized for evaluation
of development applications, building permit authorization, and other actions as provided
for by this Code.
(Res. No. 16-233 , Exh. A, 6-28-2016)
A.1.2.

  Applicability
Inclusion within this Appendix does not constitute adoption of the referenced documents
and regulations, where not otherwise formally adopted by El Paso County, but acknowledges
the ability to utilize the information contained in those documents in the evaluation
of development applications, building permit authorization, and other actions as provided
for in this Code. The most current version of the referenced document is presumed
to be referenced, even if not explicitly identified. When new documents, regulations,
or ordinances are adopted by El Paso County in accordance with either the procedures
of this Code or procedures of the BoCC, reference in this Appendix is not required
for them to be applicable.
(Res. No. 16-233 , Exh. A, 6-28-2016)
A.1.3.

  Availability of Referenced Documents
A copy of each of these documents is available for review at the PCD or from the respective
adopting authority, and may be available in electronic version. The list includes
the source for each document so the reader may obtain a copy. The availability and
cost of documents is determined by the source agency.
(Res. No. 16-233 , Exh. A, 6-28-2016)
A.1.4.

  Inclusiveness of List and Amendments
The list is not all inclusive, and may be amended from time to time by the PCD Director
without specific action by the BoCC to amend this Code.
(Res. No. 16-233 , Exh. A, 6-28-2016)
A.1.5.

  Submittal Requirements, Forms, and Application Processing
Submittal requirements, forms, and application processing information for each process
identified in this Code are available from the PCD and published within the Procedures
Manual, and may be amended from time to time.
(Res. No. 16-233 , Exh. A, 6-28-2016)
