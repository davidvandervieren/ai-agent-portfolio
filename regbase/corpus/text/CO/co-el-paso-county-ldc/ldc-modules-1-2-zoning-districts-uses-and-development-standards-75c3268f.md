# LDC Modules 1 & 2: Zoning Districts, Uses, and Development Standards
We are always trying to enhance our website and make the information available to all. Accommodations or modifications will be available for people unable to access the complex content. To request reasonable accommodations or modifications, to report inaccessible technology or broken links and website issues please send the request/information to plnweb@elpasoco.com or call 719-520-6300.
The first two Modules of the El Paso County Land Development Code focus on zoning districts and uses and development standards . This consolidated draft reflects feedback received from the Working Group and County staff and includes a combination of new provisions and content from the existing Land Development Code that has been reorganized, revised, and reformatted. Footnotes throughout the draft identify source sections, significant changes, and drafting commentary. Chapters addressing general provisions, procedures, 1041 regulations, nonconformities, and enforcement will be completed as part of a future draft.
This document includes the following content:
Module 1: Zoning Districts & Uses (Last Updated July 2025)
Chapter 2: Zoning districts, Zoning Map rules, and general dimensional standards. Click here to determine the current zoning of your property.
Chapters 3–9: Agricultural, residential, mixed-use, employment, public, civic, open space, PUD, and overlay districts, including their purposes, allowed uses, dimensional standards, and district-specific requirements.
Chapter 10: Allowed uses and standards that apply to specific uses.
Chapter 21: Commercial mobile radio service facilities.
Chapter 22: Oil and gas operations.
Module 2: Development Standards (New)
Chapter 11: Environmental and hazard-related development standards.
Chapter 12: Transportation, drainage, utilities, water, and wastewater infrastructure standards.
Chapter 13: Parking, loading, bicycle parking, and site-access standards.
Chapter 14: Landscaping, buffering, screening, fence, and wall standards.
Chapter 15: Site-layout and building-design standards.
Chapter 16: Outdoor-lighting standards.
Chapter 17: Signs and attention-getting devices.
Chapter 18: Subdivision design, cluster development, and land-dedication standards.
Chapter 25: Measurement rules, rules of construction, and definitions.
Fire Protection and Wildfire Mitigation: I ncludes all content established through County Resolution 26-202 ( No additional revisions will be made to Appendix E content )
