There is established the Department of Information Technology, the director of which
shall be the Information Technology Director. The Information Technology Director
shall be appointed by and shall be under the supervision of the City Manager. In the
absence of an Information Technology Director, the City Manager may appoint an acting
information technology director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 3, 2025; Ord. 800-23 , § 7, 2023)
The Information Technology Director shall be responsible for the administration and
security of the computer and cyber network of the City and shall be responsible for
the procurement and security of all electronic devices used by the City. He or she
shall also be responsible for the care and custody of the property and equipment used
by the Information Technology Department. The Information Technology Director shall
have those duties and responsibilities as may be delegated by ordinance or by the
City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 3, 2025)
