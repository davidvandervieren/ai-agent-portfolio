There is established the Department of Economic Development, the director of which
shall be the Economic Development Director. The Economic Development Director shall
be appointed by and shall be under the supervision of the City Manager. In the absence
of an Economic Development Director, the City Manager may appoint an acting economic
development director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 4, 2025; Ord. 800-23 , § 8, 2023)
The Economic Development Director shall be responsible for business recruitment, retention,
and expansion activities to grow the local economy. He or she shall also be responsible
for the care and custody of the property and equipment used by the Economic Development
Department. The Economic Development Director shall have those duties and responsibilities
as may be delegated by ordinance or by the City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 4, 2025)
