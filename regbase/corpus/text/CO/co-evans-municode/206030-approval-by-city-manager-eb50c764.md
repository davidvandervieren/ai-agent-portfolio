No member of any board or commission of the City shall receive any compensation for
his or her service as a member of such board or commission.
(Ord. 589 84, 1984: prior code 2.20.010)
Any member of a board or commission of the City shall be reimbursed for any expenses
necessarily and actually incurred in the performance of his or her duties.
(Ord. 589 84, 1984: prior code 2.20.020)
All requests for reimbursement of expenses shall, prior to payment, be approved by
the City Manager.
(Ord. 589 84, 1984: prior code 2.20.030)
