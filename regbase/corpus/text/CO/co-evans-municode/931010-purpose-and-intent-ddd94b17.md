Section 1.3 of the Evans Home Rule Charter; Title 31, Art. 15 of the Colorado Revised Statutes;
and C.R.S. § 18-9-117 authorize the City Council to adopt such regulations as are
reasonably necessary for the administration, protection, and maintenance of City-owned
public property. The City Council hereby enacts this Chapter 9.31 to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public.
( Ord. 805-23 , § 1, 2023)
A.
The terms "camp" or "camping" as used in this Chapter shall mean to use or erect living accommodations, including
vehicles, whether temporary or permanent, on public property in the City of Evans,
including, but not limited to, the following circumstances and activities:
1.
Sleeping or making preparations to sleep, including the lying down of bedding for
the purpose of sleeping.
2.
Occupying a shelter out-of-doors. "Shelter" shall mean any cover or protection from
the elements other than clothing, such as a tent, shack, sleeping bag, or other structure
or material.
3.
Subject to the exception set forth in Section 9.31.030.B., below, occupying any operable
or inoperable "motor vehicle," as defined in C.R.S. § 44-20-102(16), or "recreational
vehicle," as defined in C.R.S. § 24-32-902(9), as may be amended.
4.
The presence or use of a campfire, camp stove, or other heating source or cooking
device.
5.
Keeping or storing personal property.
B.
The term "public property" as used in this Chapter shall mean and include any area or property owned by or dedicated
to the City, or other governmental entity, including but not limited to any park,
median, open space, sidewalk, waterway, stairway, parking lot, easement, or public
right-of-way to the extent such property is located within the City of Evans.
( Ord. 805-23 , § 1, 2023)
A.
Camping on public property, as defined in this Chapter, is prohibited.
B.
Notwithstanding the foregoing provision, a person may occupy a motor vehicle for a
period of no longer than forty-eight (48) hours on any public right-of-way or public
parking lot under circumstances constituting an emergency, including, but not limited
to, adverse weather conditions or vehicle inoperability. Periodic occupancy shall
not be a defense or exemption to the prohibitions set forth in this Chapter.
( Ord. 805-23 , § 1, 2023)
A violation of this Section is a misdemeanor infraction and shall be punishable under Chapter 1.16 of this Code.
( Ord. 805-23 , § 1, 2023)
