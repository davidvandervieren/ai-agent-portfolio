The City hereby incorporates the groundwater from all aquifers, including the Dawson,
Denver, Arapahoe, Laramie Fox Hills or Dakota Aquifers, underlying all property within
the boundaries of the City into the City water service plan.
(Ord. 621 85, 1985)
All property owners within the City shall be deemed to have consented to the withdrawal
of groundwater by the City, in accord with Section 37-90-137, C.R.S.
(Ord. 621 85, 1985)
