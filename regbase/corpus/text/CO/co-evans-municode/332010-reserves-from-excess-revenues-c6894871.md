Revenues from sources not excluded from fiscal year spending by Article X, Section
20 of the Colorado Constitution, which exceed fiscal year spending shall be reserved
to the extent necessary to maintain or maximize fiscal year spending limits.
(Ord. 897-93, 1993)
Such reserve increases shall be part of current fiscal year spending and shall be
a separate reserve from Emergency Reserves required by Article X, Section 20 of the
Colorado Constitution.
(Ord. 897-93, 1993)
The specific amount of the current fiscal year reserve increase shall be determined
as a part of year-end and audit procedures.
(Ord. 897-93, 1993)
