There is established the Community Development Department, the director of which shall
be the Community Development Director. The Community Development Director shall be
appointed by and shall be under the supervision of the City Manager. In the absence
of a Community Development Director, the City Manager may appoint an acting community
development director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 6, 2025)
The Community Development Director shall be responsible for administering and enforcing
zoning ordinances and other provisions of the Land Development Code, the comprehensive
plan, and the subdivision regulations. He or she shall also be responsible for the
care and custody of the property and equipment used by the Community Development Department.
The Community Development Director shall further have those duties and responsibilities
as may from time to time be given him or her by ordinance or by the City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 6, 2025)
