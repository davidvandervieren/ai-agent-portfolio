Regular City Council meetings shall begin at 7:00 p.m. on the first and third Tuesdays
of every month; provided, however, the City Council may by resolution change the time,
date and place of any particular regular meeting.
( Ord. 668-17 , 2017; Ord. 707 88, 1988; Ord. 273, 1973; Ord. 241, 1972)
A.
At each meeting the City Clerk shall call the roll, note those present and absent,
announce if a majority of the City Council is present and, if a majority if present,
the Mayor, or in his or her absence the Mayor Pro Tem, shall call the meeting to order
and proceed with the City's business.
B.
The City Council shall by resolution approve the order it desires for the agenda,
which shall be prepared at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance of the meeting.
C.
The publication of Bob's Rules of Order for Colorado Local Governments (Robert Widner, 2023 Edition) shall constitute the rules of order governing meetings
of the City Council. The City Council, by resolution, may amend or update Bob's Rules of Order or adopt alternative rules of order.
( Ord. 817-24 , § 1, 2024; Ord. 273, 1973)
The Mayor shall receive six hundred dollars ($600.00) per month, and each Council
Member shall receive four hundred dollars ($400.00) per month for his or her services.
( Ord. 827-24 , § 1, 2024; Ord. 243-03, 2003; Ord. 823-91, 1991; Ord. 612 85, 1985; Ord. 430 80,
1980; Ord. 273, 1973)
All petitions, memorials and remonstrances to the City Council shall be addressed:
"To the Honorable Mayor and City Council of the City of Evans, State of Colorado."
(Ord. 273, 1973)
The following shall be the only requirements necessary to adopt any code by reference
in the City:
A.
The title of the adopting ordinance shall specify the general name of the primary
code and every secondary code adopted.
B.
Not less than three (3) copies of the primary code and of each secondary code shall
be on file with the City Clerk from the date the proposed ordinance is passed on first
reading. Following adoption of such codes, copies shall be available at cost to the
City, plus one dollar ($1.00) as a handling charge.
C.
The adopting ordinance shall be adopted and published as other ordinances of the City,
except that the provisions of such codes need not be published or read at the Council
meeting. At least three (3) copies of the codes shall be available for inspection
or reading at the Council meeting when the adopting ordinance is voted upon.
D.
The adopting ordinance shall specify when it will be on the Council agenda for passage
the second time, and this shall be considered the public hearing concerning the code
or codes to be adopted.
E.
Amendments to such codes shall be made in the same manner as the adoption of the codes.
(Ord. 275, 1973)
