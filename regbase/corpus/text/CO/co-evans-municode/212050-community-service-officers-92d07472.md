There is hereby established the Police Department, the Director of which shall be
the Chief of Police.
(Ord. 273, 1973)
The City Manager shall commission all police officers to perform the functions of
the Police Department. All commissioned officers of the Police Department are authorized
to enforce the ordinances of the City and the statutes of the State.
(Ord. 273, 1973)
The duties and functions of the Chief of Police and Police Department shall be the
enforcement of City ordinances and resolutions, as directed by the City Council, and
the enforcement of state laws as directed by the statutes of the State. The Chief
of Police shall also be responsible for the care and custody of the property used
by the Police Department. He or She shall also serve as director of all public safety
activities other than those that are the responsibility of the Fire District and building
inspections.
( Ord. 678-18 , § 1, 2018; Ord. 273, 1973)
The Chief of Police is authorized to designate as special police officers those persons
appointed by the Fire Chief to serve as fire arson investigators. Such individuals
shall be considered to be Peace Officers, Level III pursuant to Title 16, C.R.S.,
as amended from time to time, for the enforcement of the Fire Code and related state
statutes. Any individual designated under this Section shall meet the requirements
set forth by the Peace Officers Standards and Training (P.O.S.T.) Board.
( Ord. 678-18 , § 2, 2018)
The Chief of Police is authorized to designate community service officers. Such individuals
shall have the authority to issue summonses and complaints for violations of this
Code.
( Ord. 678-18 , § 2, 2018)
