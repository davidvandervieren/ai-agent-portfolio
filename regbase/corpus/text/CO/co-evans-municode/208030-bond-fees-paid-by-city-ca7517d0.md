A.
There is established the Office of Finance, which shall be under the supervision of
the Chief Financial Officer who shall in turn be under the supervision of the City
Manager.
1.
The Chief Financial Officer shall be the treasurer of the City Council as provided
for by Section 4.8 of the Home Rule Charter and shall be appointed and removed by the City Manager with
the approval of the City Council.
B.
The Chief Financial Officer shall carry out those functions and duties outlined under
Section 4.8 of the Home Rule Charter for the treasurer. In addition to the duties outlined under
the Home Rule Charter for the Treasurer, the Chief Financial Officer shall assist
the City Manager in performing the following duties:
1.
Recommend an annual budget to the City Council and administer the budget as finally
adopted under policies formulated by the City Council and keep the City Council fully
advised at all times as to the financial conditions and needs of the City.
2.
Maintain a system of accounts of the City which shall conform to any uniform system
required by the City Council, and which shall conform to generally accepted principals
and procedures of governmental accounting. The Treasurer shall submit financial statements
to the City Council monthly, or more often as City Council directs.
3.
Prepare an annual financial report. Copies of such financial reports shall be made
available for public inspection at the office of the City Clerk.
4.
The Chief Financial Officer shall further have those duties and responsibilities as
may from time to time be given him or her by ordinance or by the City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 1, 2025; Ord. 761-90, 1990; Ord. 721 89, 1989; Ord. 651 86, 1986; Ord. 273, 1973)
All monies shall be disbursed or withdrawn from all City accounts or funds by checks
drawn and signed by the Chief Financial Officer and the City Manager. Both the Chief
Financial Officer and the City Manager may obtain a fidelity bond as approved by the
City Council. Until the City Manager has obtained the proper fidelity bond, the Mayor
shall countersign all checks.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 1, 2025; Ord. 761-90, 1990; Ord. 273, 1973)
The City shall pay the fee for obtaining fidelity bonds required by the City.
(Ord. 761-90, 1990; Ord. 273, 1973)
