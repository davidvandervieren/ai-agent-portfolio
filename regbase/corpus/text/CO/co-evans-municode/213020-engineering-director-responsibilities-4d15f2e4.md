There is established the Engineering Department, the director of which shall be the
Engineering Director. The Engineering Director shall be appointed by and shall be
under the supervision of the City Manager. In the absence of an Engineering Director,
the City Manager may appoint an acting engineering director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 5, 2025)
The Engineering Director shall be responsible for project management for capital improvement
projects, including the design and construction of public infrastructure, as well
as subdivision development plan reviews and utility permit reviews, and coordination
with the Public Works & Utilities Director on infrastructure maintenance and operations.
The Engineering Director shall develop and implement the capital improvement plan
of the City, addressing both growth-related infrastructure needs and deferred maintenance
of existing assets. He or she shall also be responsible for the care and custody of
the property and equipment used by the Engineering Department. The Engineering Director
shall further have those duties and responsibilities as may from time to time be given
him or her by ordinance or by the City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 5, 2025)
