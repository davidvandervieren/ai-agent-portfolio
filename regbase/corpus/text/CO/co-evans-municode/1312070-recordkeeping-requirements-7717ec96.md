The City Council shall have the authority to establish rules and regulations by resolution
for the use and management of the Evans Ditch. Permit holders shall agree to abide
by all such rules, or be denied water from the Evans Ditch.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
It is unlawful for any person or persons, corporation or association to use or cause
to be used any water from the Evans Ditch without making application to the City Manager
or his or her representative and having obtained his or her approval and a permit
from the City Treasurer.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
Any person or persons making an application for the use of Evans Ditch water shall
state the number of acres, blocks or lots to be watered or irrigated, or for whatever
purpose the water is to be used.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
Water from the Evans Ditch, when used for other than irrigation, such as commercial
and industrial uses, shall be approved by the City Council.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
The City Council shall, at a regular meeting prior to the first of April of each year,
establish the irrigation rates for the ensuing irrigation season. Rates for other
uses shall be established by contract.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
All moneys collected from the issuance of permits or from contracts for use of ditch
water shall be deposited with other water department funds.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
The City Treasurer shall make an annual report to the City Manager of all funds collected,
acreage or blocks and lots irrigated and other uses of ditch water.
(Ord. 274, 1973)
