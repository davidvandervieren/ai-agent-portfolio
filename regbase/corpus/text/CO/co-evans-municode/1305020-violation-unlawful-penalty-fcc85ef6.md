Unless otherwise specified by ordinance, or unless otherwise required or approved
by the Director of Public Works, the minimum depth of cover for all water lines from
the finished grade to the top of the pipe shall be:
A.
Four and one-half (4½) feet for pipelines twenty (20) inches and smaller;
B.
Four feet (4) for pipelines larger than twenty (20) inches.
(Ord. 616 85, 1985)
Any person who locates, constructs or lays a water pipeline in violation of Section 13.05.010 above is guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction, shall be fined not to exceed
two thousand six hundred fifty dollars ($2,650.00). Each day a violation continues
shall be a separate offense.
(Ord. No. 658-16 , 2016;
A.
Any person who constructs a pipeline in violation of Section 13.05.010 above which is located in a street, alley or other easement of the City shall be
liable to the City for all costs of repair caused by the freezing of water in the
pipeline, or caused by pressures created by ice in the pipeline.
B.
Any person who constructs a pipeline on private property in violation of Section 13.05.010 above shall be liable to the property owner for the costs of all repair caused by
the freezing of water in the pipeline, or caused by pressures created by ice in the
pipeline.
(Ord. 616 85, 1985)
This Chapter shall apply to all pipelines constructed or installed within the City
distribution system.
(Ord. 616 85, 1985)
