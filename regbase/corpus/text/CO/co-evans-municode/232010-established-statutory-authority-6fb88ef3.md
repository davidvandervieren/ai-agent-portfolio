The authority for the establishment and regulation of the cemetery is provided in
Section 31-25-701, et seq., C.R.S.
(Ord. 675-87)
A.
The operation and maintenance of the Evans Cemetery shall be under the general supervision
and control of the director of parks and recreation.
B.
The City Council may adopt, by resolution, rules and regulations for the cemetery
and may, by resolution, amend such rules.
(Ord. 1124-98, 1998; Ord. 675-87, 1987)
The City Clerk shall keep all records of burials, the sale of cemetery lots and prepare
deeds for signature of the City Clerk and Mayor.
(Ord. 1124-98, 1998; Ord. 675-87, 1987)
There shall be established a trust fund called the Cemetery Endowment Fund. Such trust
shall consist of a combination of the present Henry F. Behrens Cemetery Trust and
the Weld County Cemetery Trust. The City is further empowered to accept any additional
donations to enhance the corpus of the trust. The corpus (principal) of the trust
shall not be depleted, but the earnings from the trust may be used, buy only for the
following purposes:
A.
For the purchase of additional cemetery space for the enlargement of the cemetery
at the discretion of the City Council;
B.
For investments, and the interest therefrom shall be used for the general care of
the cemetery.
(Ord. 675-87, 1987)
All funds received from cemetery lot sales, grave openings and closings, donations,
interest from the cemetery endowment fund, and similar receipts shall be deposited
in the City's general fund.
(Ord. 675-87, 1987)
There is created and established a special fund, to be known as the "Evans Cemetery
Perpetual Care Fund." The City Council may, by resolution, provide for the collection
of a fee: (1) from the sale of cemetery lots, (2) for the interment of opening/closing
of a grave, and/or (3) by an annual assessment on cemetery lots. Such fees shall be
deposited in the perpetual care fund to be used only for the following purposes:
A.
For the care and maintenance of the cemetery grounds;
B.
For the capital improvements necessary for upkeep of the cemetery;
C.
For equipment necessary for maintenance of the cemetery.
(Ord. 675-87, 1987)
