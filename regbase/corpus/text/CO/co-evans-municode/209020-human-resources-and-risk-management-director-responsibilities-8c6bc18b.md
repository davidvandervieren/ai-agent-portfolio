There is established the Department of Human Resources and Risk Management, the director
of which shall be the Human Resources and Risk Management Director. The Human Resources
and Risk Management Director shall be appointed by and shall be under the supervision
of the City Manager. In the absence of a Human Resources and Risk Management Director,
the City Manager may appoint an acting human resources and risk management director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 2, 2025; Ord. 800-23 , § 6, 2023)
The Human Resources and Risk Management Director shall be responsible for the administration
of the adopted personnel policy of the City. He or she shall also be responsible for
the care and custody of the property and equipment used by the Human Resources and
Risk Management Department. The Human Resources and Risk Management Director shall
have those duties and responsibilities as may be delegated by ordinance or by the
City Manager.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 2, 2025)
