There is established the Public Works and Utilities Department, the director of which
shall be the Public Works and Utilities Director, also referred to as Utilities Director
elsewhere in this Code. The Public Works and Utilities Director shall be appointed
by and shall be under the supervision of the City Manager. In the absence of a Public
Works and Utilities Director, the City Manager may appoint an acting public works
and utilities director.
( Ord. 847-25 , § 8, 2025; Ord. 800-23 , § 1, 2023; Ord. 762-90: 615-85)
A.
The Public Works and Utilities Director shall be responsible for operation and maintenance
of the streets, stormwater drainage, water distribution, and wastewater collection
and treatment of the City. The Public Works and Utilities Director shall be responsible
for the maintenance of all City-owned or maintained buildings and grounds, including
the community complex, parks, and the cemetery. He or she shall also be responsible
for the care and custody of the property and equipment used by the Public Works and
Utilities Department. The Public Works and Utilities Director shall further have those
duties and responsibilities as may from time to time be given him or her by ordinance
or by the City Manager.
