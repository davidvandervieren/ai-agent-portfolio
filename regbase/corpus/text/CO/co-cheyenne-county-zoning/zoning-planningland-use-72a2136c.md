## Registration & Safety Requirements
The Planning and Zoning Board and Board of Commissioners strongly suggest wind companies register wind
farm
latitude/longitude sites on both the county website and the Colorado Agriculture Aviation
Association (CAAA) website. Aviation buoys are required on the upper 1/3 portion of the towers
for
conditional use applications
to be approved. Reflection tape is suggested.
