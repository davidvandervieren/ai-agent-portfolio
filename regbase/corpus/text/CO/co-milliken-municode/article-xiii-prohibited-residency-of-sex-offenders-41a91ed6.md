The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this Article, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning:
Licensed day care center means a child care center or a family child care home licensed by the State of Colorado.
Park means any "park" or ""open space" as such terms are defined in section 16-8-200 of this Code that contains any improvement such as a bench, an improved trail, or
playground equipment or any similar improvement. Open space that contains no improvements
other than periodic mowing or watering shall not be deemed a park for the purposes
of this Article.
Permanent residence means a place where a person abides, lodges or resides for five (5) or more consecutive
days.
Recreation center means a recreation, entertainment, or sports center or theater that is open to the
public for free or fee-based use, including any ball field, arcade, basketball court,
laser tag facility, bowling alley, or similar facility.
Restricted sex offender means an individual who is either a sexual predator or a specified sex offender or
both.
School means any public, private, parochial, charter or other school attended, at least
in part, by students under the age of eighteen (18), except for home schools.
School bus stop means any public or private school bus stop designated or approved by a school district.
Sexual predator means any person age eighteen (18) or older who is a "sexually violent predator"
as that term is defined in section 18-3-414.5, C.R.S., as amended or recodified from
time to time.
Specified sex offender means any person required to register under the Colorado Sex Offender Registration
Act, Article 22, Title 16, C.R.S., as amended from time to time, and:
(1)
Who has been convicted of a felony for an offense requiring registration where the
victim was under the age of eighteen (18); or
(2)
Who has multiple convictions for offenses requiring registration where at least one
(1) victim was under the age of eighteen (18); or
(3)
Whose offenses requiring registration involved multiple victims and at least one (1)
victim was under the age of eighteen (18).
Swimming pool means a water-filled structure used for the purpose of swimming or other water activities,
including splash parks and water slides, that is open to the public for free or fee-based
use.
Temporary residence means a place where a person abides, lodges or resides for a period of five (5) or
more days in an aggregate calendar year and which is not the person's permanent residence,
or a place where a person routinely abides, lodges or resides for a period of five
(5) or more consecutive or nonconsecutive days in any month and which is not the person's
permanent address.
( Ord. 825 , § 1, 5-14-2025)
(a)
It shall be unlawful for a restricted sex offender to establish a permanent or temporary
residence within one thousand (1,000) feet of any school, school bus stop, park, licensed
day care center, recreation center or swimming pool in the Town.
(b)
It shall be unlawful to let, lease, rent, license, or provide by permission without
any formal agreement any portion of any property, room, place, structure, trailer
or other vehicle to a restricted sex offender with the knowledge that it will be used
as a permanent or temporary residence in violation of this Article.
( Ord. 825 , § 1, 5-14-2025)
(a)
A restricted sex offender is not guilty of a violation of Section 10-13-20 above if:
(l)
The restricted sex offender had established the permanent or temporary residence prior
to the effective date of the ordinance enacting this Article; provided, however, that
this exception shall not apply if the restricted sex offender first became a restricted
sex offender under the definition set forth in section 10-13-10 after the effective date of the ordinance enacting this Article;
(2)
The restricted sex offender is under the age of eighteen (18) and resides with the
restricted sex offender's parent(s) or legal guardian(s);
(3)
The restricted sex offender is placed in the residence pursuant to a state-licensed
foster care program; or
(4)
The school, park, licensed day care center, recreation center or swimming pool was
opened, or the school bus stop was designated, after the restricted sex offender established
such person's permanent or temporary residence.
(b)
A person who lets or rents any portion of any property, room, place, structure, trailer
or other vehicle to a restricted sex offender with the knowledge that it will be used
as a permanent or temporary residence in violation of this Article is not guilty of
a violation of Section 10-13-20 above if:
(1)
The person let, leased, rented, licensed, or provided the property, room, place, structure,
trailer or other vehicle to the restricted sex offender prior to the effective date
of the ordinance enacting this Article;
(2)
The person lets or rents the property, room, place, structure, trailer or other vehicle
to a restricted sex offender pursuant to a state-licensed foster care program; or
(3)
The person let, leased, rented, licensed, or provided the property, room, place, structure,
trailer or other vehicle to the restricted sex offender prior to the opening of the
pertinent school, park, licensed day care center, recreation center or swimming pool,
or prior to the designation of the pertinent school bus stop.
( Ord. 825 , § 1, 5-14-2025)
For the purpose of determining separation distance, the measurement shall be made
by following the shortest possible straight line from the outer property line of the
property on which the school, park, licensed day care center, recreation center or
swimming pool is located to the nearest point on the outer property line of the property
on which the permanent or temporary residence is located. For a school bus stop, the
measurement shall be made by following the shortest possible straight line from the
edge of a circle with a radius of one hundred (100) feet with the center being the
center of the location identified by the school district to the nearest point on the
outer property line of the property on which the permanent or temporary residence
is located.
( Ord. 825 , § 1, 5-14-2025)
Violations of this Article are subject to any and all penalties as provided in Article
4 of Chapter 1 of this Code.
( Ord. 825 , § 1, 5-14-2025)
