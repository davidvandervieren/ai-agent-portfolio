Editor's note— Ord. 834 , §§ 27—30, adopted Dec. 22, 2025, set out provisions intended for use as §§ 18-15-10—18-15-30.
Inasmuch as there were already provisions so designated, said sections has been codified
herein as §§ 18-18-10—18-18-30, at the discretion of the editor.
Pursuant to Title 31, Article 16, Part 2, C.R.S., the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency
Code, 2025 Edition, as published and amended from time to time by the Colorado Wildfire
Resiliency Code Board in the Division of Fire Prevention & Control of the Colorado
Department of Public Safety, 1697 Cole Blvd, Unit 200, Lakewood, CO 80401, Chapters 1 through 5 inclusive and Appendixes A, B and C, is hereby adopted by reference as
the Town of Milliken Wildfire Resiliency Code to have the same force and effect as
if fully set forth herein.
( Ord. 834 , § 28, 12-22-2025)
At least one (1) copy of the Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code, 2025 Edition, certified
to be a true copy, is now on file and may be inspected by any interested person between
the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, holidays excepted. The
code as finally adopted shall be available for sale to the public through the office
of the Town Clerk at a moderate price.
( Ord. 834 , § 29, 12-22-2025)
The International Colorado Wildfire Resiliency Code, 2025 Edition, adopted herein
is hereby modified by the following additions, deletions, insertions and changes as
follows:
Section 101.1 (Title) is amended by the addition of the term "Town of Milliken" where
indicated.
Section 103 - Code Compliance Agency is amended to read as follows:
103.1 Responsible agency. The Community Development Director of the Town of Milliken is hereby named as the
code official for this code. The function of the code official shall be the implementation,
administration, and enforcement of the provisions of this code.
103.2 Referral. The code official shall refer submitted applications to the appropriate building
and/or fire code official for plan review, inspection, and enforcement.
( Ord. 834 , § 30, 12-22-2025)
