There is hereby created a police department for the town which shall consist of one
town marshal and as many police officer deputies as may from time to time be deemed
necessary for the safety and good order of the town.
(Ord. No. 123, § 7(7-1), 5-2-78)
The police department shall be operated and managed in accordance with such departmental
rules and regulations as may from time to time be adopted by the board.
(Ord. No. 123, § 7(7-2), 5-2-78)
Editor's note— Ord. No. 453, adopted Feb. 2, 2021, repealed § 11-3, which pertained to marshal; appointment;
duties generally and derived from Ord. No. 123, adopted May 2, 1978.
Before entering upon the duties of their office, the town marshal and each police
officer shall take and subscribe an oath that they will support the Constitution and
laws of the state, the Constitution of the United States and the ordinances of the
town, and that they will faithfully perform the duties of the office upon which they
are about to enter. They shall also furnish to the town a surety bond to be approved
by the board, conditioned upon the faithful performance of the duties of their office
in an amount to be determined by the board.
(Ord. No. 123, § 7(7-3), (7-5), 5-2-78)
The marshal, any police officer of the town or the sheriff of the county shall make
arrests on all cases arising under the provisions of any statutes or the ordinances
of the town.
(Ord. No. 123, § 6(6-7), 5-2-78)
State Law reference— Arrests, § 16-3-101 et seq., C.R.S. 1973.
All members of the police department shall:
(1)
Perform all duties required under this chapter of the town marshal.
(2)
Suppress all riots, disturbances and breaches of the peace, apprehend all disorderly
persons in the town and pursue and arrest any person fleeing from justice in any part
of the state.
(3)
Be the enforcement officers of the town and shall see that the provisions of this
Code and the laws of the state are complied with. They shall arrest without process
all persons engaged in the violation in their presence of any provision of this Code
or the law of the state. They shall also arrest without process when they have probable
cause to believe that (a) an offense was committed and (b) it was committed by the
person to be arrested. Upon any arrest they shall forthwith convey such offenders
before the proper officer to be dealt with according to law; provided, that they may
incarcerate any person whom they shall arrest at a late and unusual hour of the night
until the following morning, and provided further that in the special cases relating
to traffic offenses they may release an arrested person upon his written promise to
appear in court.
(4)
Report such offenses as may come to their knowledge to the proper town official or
they shall report the same to the municipal judge, securing a warrant for the arrest
of offenders when desirable.
(5)
Execute and return all writs and processes to them directed by the municipal judge
in any case arising under a town ordinances, and they may serve the same in any part
of the county under their commissions as deputy sheriffs.
(6)
Observe the condition of the streets, sidewalks and alleys of the town, and of any
obstruction, nuisance or impediments therein, and shall take necessary measures to
remove or abate the same.
(Ord. No. 123, § 7(7-3), (7-4), 5-2-78)
