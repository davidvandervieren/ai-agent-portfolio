(a)
All billing charges and fees included within the monthly bill also referred to as
the utility bill is subject to this section.
(b)
All utility bills shall have a due date. The due date shall be the 26th day of every
month, after the billed month of service, or the next business day if the due date
falls on a weekend or holiday. If the billed amount is not paid by the due date, a
base penalty of five percent of the account balance shall be added to the amount due.
Penalties shall be assessed to utility accounts with a total carry forward balance
in excess of $25.00.
(c)
In the event the account is unpaid 30 days after the due date of the monthly bill
and exceeds $25.00 for all such services, town staff shall issue a notice by mail
to the person obligated to receive the bill identifying the date by which payment
is due and the date billed services will be disconnected.
(d)
In the event the account remains unpaid ten calendar days after the postmark of the
notice, town staff shall dispatch a service call at the next available operational
day and attach a red tag delinquent notice physically on the property in a visible
location.
(e)
In the event the account remains unpaid five calendar days after the red tag notice
town staff shall dispatch a service call at the next available operational day to
disconnect water for accounts with water service and post a second red tag delinquent
notice.
(f)
If the account includes water service and water remains disconnected a full billing
month after a second red tag delinquent notice, town staff shall reduce all billed
rates to the seasonal rate if available.
(g)
Payment of the full account balance is required to restore services to the applicable
billing including a service call to reconnect water.
(h)
In the event an account remains delinquent and unpaid 89 calendar days prior to December
1 of each year, and a notice has been postmarked to the account holder by US Postal
Service no less than ten days before certification to the county treasurer, that account
will be certified to the county treasurer. A fee will be assessed and the certification
per C.R.S. § 31-20-105, shall be prepared.
(i)
Pursuant to the authority of C.R.S. § 31-20-105, the board of trustees hereby elect
to certify to the county treasurer for collection of any and all delinquent utility
service charges due and payable to the town, to be collected by the county treasurer
and paid over to the town in the same manner as ad valorem real property taxes are
authorized to be collected pursuant to C.R.S., title 31.
(j)
The board of trustees shall set fees for service call and certification to the county
treasurer by resolution.
(Ord. No. 465, 11-16-21)
