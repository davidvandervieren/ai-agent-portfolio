For the purposes of this chapter:
Ashes includes the solid waste products of coal, wood and other fuels used for heating
and cooking from all public and private establishments and residences.
Collector means a person, firm, partnership or corporation hired or franchised by the town
for the purpose of collecting refuse within the town pursuant to this chapter.
Garbage includes all putrescible waste except sewage and body waste, including vegetable
waste, animal offal and carcasses of dead animals and every accumulation of animal,
fruit or vegetable matter, that attends the preparation, use, cooking, dealing in
or storing of meats, fish, fowl, fruits and vegetables, but not including recognized
industrial by-products or waste products resulting from the construction or repair
of any buildings or resulting from any construction or building operation.
Hazardous material means any of the following:
Hazardous waste as defined by USEPA in 40 CFR 261, and state or local regulations,
these include, but are not limited to, lead acid or nickel cadmium batteries, solvents,
flammable liquids, or medical waste.
Substances identified in the Toxic Substances Act, (TSCA), and in the National Emission
Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants, (NESHAPS). These include, but are not limited
to, asbestos and poly chlorinated biphenils.
Medical waste as defined by 20 MNAC 9.1.
Ozone depleting chemicals identified in the Clean Air Act.
Radioactive materials regulated under the Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
Person means any natural person, firm, partnership, association, institution or corporation.
Refuse includes garbage, rubbish, ashes and all other putrescible and nonputrescible waste,
except sewage, and except hazardous materials as defined herein.
Rubbish includes all nonputrescible waste, except ashes, from all public and private establishments
and residences.
(Ord. No. 156, § 2, 11-2-82; Ord. No. 251, §§ 1, 2, 12-9-97)
The board of trustees of the town may, from time to time by motion or resolution,
prescribe regulations for the removal and disposition of refuse pursuant to this chapter,
and shall have the right to fix rates for the removal and disposition of refuse. The
board of trustees shall further have the right to contract with any collector, pursuant
to state law, for the removal of refuse within the town except for any commercial
property, industrial property or dwelling unit with eight or more separate residences.
Except as otherwise specified, the town shall collect, remove and dispose of all refuse
from residences within the town at least once a week and shall fix the rates to be
charged for such service.
(Ord. No. 156, § 3, 11-2-82)
(a)
It shall be unlawful for any person to burn, dump, collect or in any manner dispose
of refuse upon any street, alley, public place, stream or private property within
the town. Compost piles and collections of recyclable materials shall not be prohibited.
Paper drives, bottle drives or other approved fund-raising projects for nonprofit
organizations or clubs may be carried out in the town with the approval of the board
of trustees and under such rules and regulations as the board may prescribe.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for any person to burn waste paper, boxes, brush, leaves, grass,
tree clippings or other combustible material outdoors in the town, unless otherwise
authorized by this Code.
(c)
Every person in possession of any commercial property, industrial property or multiple-dwelling
unit with eight or more separate residences shall dispose of all refuse as often as
required for the maintaining of healthful and sanitary conditions at such establishment.
(d)
It shall be unlawful for any person to burn, dump, bury, discard, collect or in any
manner dispose of or attempt to dispose of any hazardous material in the town. It
shall be unlawful for any person to place any hazardous material in any container
to be collected or removed by any person for the purposes of disposal under the provisions
of any contract entered by the town with any contractor for waste disposal.
(Ord. No. 156, § 7, 11-2-82; Ord. No. 185, 4-1-86; Ord. No. 251, § 3, 12-9-97; Ord.
No. 357, 5-18-10; Ord. No. 427, § 1, 1-21-20)
It shall be unlawful for any person to collect refuse without having first secured
from the board of trustees a license to perform such service. Such license shall be
granted only to persons providing refuse collection service in compliance with rules
and regulations concerning the type of equipment and other conduct which the board
has the power to prescribe.
(Ord. No. 156, § 4, 11-2-82)

(a)
All persons in possession, in charge or in control of any dwelling for which refuse
collection is required under this chapter shall have their refuse collected by the
town, and a fee shall be charged for such service.
(b)
All persons required to have collection service shall be provided with a cart by the
town's contractor for waste disposal purposes, and the supplied cart shall be used
for the disposal of refuse as provided herein. The cart shall be placed out for collection
by the customer within four feet of the curb, alley or street at the point of collection
designated by the contractor for that customer, facing forward, at least three feet
from any other obstruction. The cart shall be placed for collection by the customer
no earlier than 12 hours before the scheduled day of collection and shall be removed
by the customer no later than 12 hours after collection. No other containers or disposable
bags shall be used by customers for the collection of refuse by the town's contractor.
(c)
All refuse shall be placed in carts supplied by the contractor for collection and
shall:
(1)
Be 96 gallons in size as provided by the contractor.
(2)
Not exceed 150 pounds in weight.
(3)
Have fitted covers which shall be in closed position for collection by the contractor.
(4)
Be kept clean by the customer and shall be kept in a good state of repair by the contractor,
free of any defects which could cause injury to any person.
(d)
All tree trimmings, hedge clippings and similar materials shall be cut to a length
not to exceed 12 inches beyond the top of the contractor supplied cart, and the lid
shall be in a semi-closed position for collection. Any material resulting from commercial
services or construction shall not be collected.
(e)
It shall be the duty of every owner or occupant of any property in the town to remove
or clean up any refuse which is spilled on public or private property from any trash
container which belongs to that person. Each day that refuse is allowed to remain
on any property shall be deemed to be a separate offense.
(Ord. No. 156, § 5, 11-2-82; Ord. No. 237, §§ 1—3, 3-5-96; Ord. No. 251, §§ 4—6, 12-9-97)
(a)
All bills for solid waste shall have a due date. The due date shall be the 26th day
of every month, after the billed month of service, or the next business day if the
due date falls on a weekend or holiday. If the billed amount is not paid by the due
date, a base penalty of five percent of the billed amount shall be added to the amount
due. Penalties shall be assessed to utility accounts with a total carry forward balance
for all combined utilities in excess of $25.00.
(b)
Disconnected water service. Any solid waste account in which the town water service has been disconnected for
a full billing cycle shall be billed at the seasonal rate. A seasonal rate is not
permitted with a sewer and solid waste only account.
(c)
The board of trustees may waive or reduce penalties by resolution for a specified
term.
(d)
A payment plan application shall be available to any customer seeking relief to defer
delinquent charges. At the town manager's discretion the application can be approved,
approved with conditions or denied.
(e)
All delinquent accounts shall comply with chapter 15 , article I, section 15-10 , delinquent accounts.
(Ord. No. 156, § 6, 11-2-82; Ord. No. 434, 4-14-20; Ord. No. 464, 11-16-21)
