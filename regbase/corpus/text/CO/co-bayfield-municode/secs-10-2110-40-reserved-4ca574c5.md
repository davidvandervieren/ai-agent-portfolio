Editor's note— Ord. No. 502, adopted July 16, 2024, amended chapter 10 in its entirety to read as
herein set out. Former chapter 10, §§ 10-1—10-84, pertained to planning and development,
and derived from Ord. No. 219, adopted Jan. 14, 1992 and Ord. No. 241, adopted Sept.
3, 1996.
All land use and development projects are required to comply with the Land Use Code
(LUC). It is important to keep a Land Use Code current and relevant through necessary
amendments as per the Land Use Code.
The Town of Bayfield will adopt Land Use Code amendments through an Ordinance approved
by the Board of Trustees. An official version of the Land Use Code will be available
for public inspection during office hours at Town Hall, 1199 Bayfield Parkway, and
on the Town's website.
(Ord. No. 502, 7-16-24)
Colorado Revised Statutes (C.R.S) 31-23-206 requires municipalities with populations
of 2,000 people or more to adopt a master plan, often referred to as a Comprehensive
Plan (Comp Plan). The Comp Plan is an advisory document to help guide future land
use decisions for Bayfield. Comp Plans should go through substantial updates or rewrites
every ten years. However, it is important to keep a Comp Plan current and relevant
through necessary amendments and ongoing implementation monitoring. Comp Plan amendments
shall meet the requirements of the Land Use Code.
The Town of Bayfield will adopt Comp Plans through Resolutions approved by both the
planning commission and board of trustees. An official version of the Comp Plan will
be available for public inspection during office hours at Town Hall, 1199 Bayfield
Parkway, and on the town's website.
(Ord. No. 502, 7-16-24)
