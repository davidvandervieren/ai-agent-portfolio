United Power shall not knowingly employ or contract with an undocumented worker or
illegal alien to perform work for United Power that is or may be related to this Franchise
Agreement or knowingly contract with a subcontractor who knowingly employs or contracts
with undocumented workers (illegal aliens) to perform work under this Franchise.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
