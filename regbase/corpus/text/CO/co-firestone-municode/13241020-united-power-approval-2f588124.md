United Power shall file with the Town Clerk its written approval of this Franchise
and of all of its terms and provisions at least ten days prior to public hearings
set for consideration of the ordinance by the Town Board. United Power shall file
with the Town Clerk its written ratification thereof within ten days after the approval
of this Franchise Agreement by the Town Board. The acceptance and ratification shall
in form and content be approved by the Town Attorney. If United Power shall fail to
timely file its written acceptance or ratification as herein provided, this Franchise
shall be and become null and void.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
