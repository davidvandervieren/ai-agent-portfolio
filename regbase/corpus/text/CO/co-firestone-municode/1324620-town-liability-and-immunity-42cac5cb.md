The Town shall be responsible for liability and damages associated with its own acts
and omissions in the manner and to the extent provided by applicable law. Nothing
in this Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of the notice requirements, defenses,
immunities and limitations the Town may have under the Colorado Governmental Immunity
Act (Section 24-10-101, et seq., C.R.S.) or of any other defenses, immunities or limitations
of liability available to the Town by law.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
