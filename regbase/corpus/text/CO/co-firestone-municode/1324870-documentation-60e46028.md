United Power shall, upon request, provide to the Town copies of documentation and
verification of immigration status and employment eligibility received by United Power
for itself or from subcontractors; and, if requested, copies of information received
from a subcontractor submitted to establish that the subcontractor has not knowingly
employed or contracted with an illegal alien.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
