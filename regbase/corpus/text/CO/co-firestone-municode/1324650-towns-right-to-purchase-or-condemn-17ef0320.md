The right of the Town to construct, purchase or condemn any public utility works or
ways, and the right of United Power in connection therewith, as provided by the Colorado
Constitution and statutes, are hereby expressly reserved. The Town shall have the
right during the term of this Franchise, and using the procedures set forth herein,
to purchase United Power facilities, land, rights-of-way and easements now owned or
to be owned by United Power located within the territorial boundaries of the Town.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
