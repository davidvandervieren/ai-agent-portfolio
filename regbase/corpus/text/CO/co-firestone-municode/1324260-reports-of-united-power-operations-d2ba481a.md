United Power shall submit reasonable financial and operating reports containing or
based on information available from United Power's books and records annually to the
Town and other reports the Town may from time to time request with respect to the
operations of United Power under this Franchise. Such reports shall be marked "CONFIDENTIAL"
by United Power and the Town shall keep the reports as confidential and not subject
to public inspection, except as otherwise required by law. Such form of reports may
be changed from time to time as mutually agreed between the Town and United Power.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
