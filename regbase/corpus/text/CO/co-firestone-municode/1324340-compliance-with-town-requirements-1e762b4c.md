Representatives of the Town and United Power shall meet annually to discuss annual
and long-term planning for capital improvement projects contemplated by each within
the Town. United Power shall include within its capital improvement projects the plans
of the Town relating to same. United Power and the Town shall exchange copies of their
reports or plans regarding annual and long-term planning for capital improvement projects
with descriptions of construction activities including, to the extent known, the timing
and method of construction.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
