The Town Board, upon giving ninety days' notice to United Power, may request that
the Town and United Power review the franchise fee rate and other material financial
aspects of this Franchise Agreement. Upon such a request by the Town, the parties
shall engage in good faith negotiations related to amending the franchise fee rate,
and/or other related provisions of this Franchise to allow the Town to receive a greater
franchise fee rate or other significant benefit in the financial aspects of the Agreement.
In no event shall the franchise fee be higher than the highest consideration paid
by United Power to any municipality in the State of Colorado.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
