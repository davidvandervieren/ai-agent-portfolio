The Town expressly reserves, and United Power expressly recognizes, the Town's right
and duty to adopt, from time to time, in addition to the provisions herein contained,
such provisions, ordinances and rules and regulations as may be deemed necessary by
the Town, in the exercise of its police power, to protect the health, safety and welfare
of its citizens and their properties.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
