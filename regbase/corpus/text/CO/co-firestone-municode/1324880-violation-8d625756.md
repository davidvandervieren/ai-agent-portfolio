If United Power violates a provision of Sections 13.24.820 — 13.24.870 above, such violation may constitute a material breach of this Franchise Agreement
and the Town, in its sole discretion, may terminate the Franchise for breach of contract.
If the Franchise is so terminated, United Power shall be liable for actual damages
to the Town.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
