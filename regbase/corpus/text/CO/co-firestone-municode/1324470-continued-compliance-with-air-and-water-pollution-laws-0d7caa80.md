United Power shall use its best efforts to take measures which will result in its
facilities and operations meeting the standards required by applicable Town, county,
state and federal air and water pollution laws, and laws regulating transportation
of hazardous materials. Upon the Town's request, United Power will provide the Town
with a status report of such measures.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
