13.24.090 - Payment of expenses incurred by Town in relation to ordinance.
At the Town's option, United Power shall pay in advance or reimburse the Town for
expenses incurred in publication of notices and ordinances related to this Franchise
Agreement.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
