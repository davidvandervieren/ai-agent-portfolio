If the supply of electricity to United Power's customers should be interrupted due
to a planned outage, except cases of emergency outage repair, United Power shall notify,
consistent with the provisions of Section 13.24.360 below, its customers as soon as practical in advance of the planned outage. United
Power agrees that it will in good faith try to contact all customers at least forty-eight
hours prior to a planned outage.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
