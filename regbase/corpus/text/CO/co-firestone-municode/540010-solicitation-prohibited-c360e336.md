Editor's note— Ord. No. 998 , § 2, adopted July 14, 2021, repealed chapter 5.40 and enacted a new chapter 5.40
as set out herein. The former chapter 5.40 pertained to transient merchants, solicitors
and peddlers and derived from Ord. 292 § 1(part), adopted 1992; and Ord. 610 § 4,
adopted 2006.
A.
Solicitation means any attempt to make personal contact with an occupant of a residence
at the residence, without prior specific invitation or appointment, for the primary
purpose of (i) attempting to sell, for present or future delivery, any goods, wares
or merchandise, or any services to be performed immediately or in the future; or (ii)
advertising a commercial event, activity, good or service that is offered at a location
away from the residence or at a future time.
B.
Solicitor means any person, whether as volunteer, owner, agent, consignee or employee,
who engages in solicitation.
C.
It is unlawful for a solicitor, while engaging in solicitation to enter upon the premises
of any residence in the town where there is conspicuously posted at or near the entry
of the premises or upon the entry of the premises, a notice bearing the words "No
Solicitation", "No Solicitors", "No Trespassing," or sign of similar import that communicates
the occupants' or owners' intent and desire not to be contacted by solicitors, unless
such solicitor is expressly invited onto the posted premises by the resident or occupant.
(Ord. No. 998 , § 2, 7-14-2021)
