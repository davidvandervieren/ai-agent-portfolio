When undertaking a project of undergrounding, the Town and United Power shall work
with other utilities or companies which have their lines overhead to attempt to have
all lines undergrounded as part of the same project. When other utilities or companies
such as cable television and telephone companies or other utilities with overhead
facilities embark upon a program of underground construction where United Power has
overhead facilities, United Power shall cooperate with these utilities and companies
and undertake to underground United Power facilities as part of the same project at
no cost to the Town. United Power shall not be required to pay for the cost of undergrounding
the facilities of other companies or the Town.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
