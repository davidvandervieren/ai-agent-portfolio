Editor's note— Ord. No. 1051 , § 1, adopted Oct. 9, 2024, repealed Ch. 5.24 §§ 5.24.010—5.24.080, which pertained
to concerts and derived from prior code § 4-24; Ord. 246; Ord. 251; Ord. 700.
Any person or organization desiring to operate any public address system or other
amplified sound system in a park, parkway, recreation area, or open shall first obtain
an External Special Event Application.
( Ord. No. 1051 , § 1, 10-9-2024)
