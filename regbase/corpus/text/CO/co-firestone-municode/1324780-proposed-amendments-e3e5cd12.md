At any time during the term of this Franchise, the Town or United Power may propose
amendments to this Franchise by giving thirty days' written notice to the other of
the proposed amendment(s) desired. However, nothing contained in this Section shall
be deemed to require either party to consent to any amendment proposed by the other
party.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
