Neither the Town nor United Power shall be excused from complying with any of the
terms and conditions of this Franchise by any failure of the other, or any of its
officers, employees or agents, upon any one or more occasions, to insist upon or to
seek compliance with any such terms and conditions. Further, neither the Town nor
United Power waives any rights under the laws, statutes and/or constitution of the
State of Colorado or of the United States except as otherwise specifically set forth
herein.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
