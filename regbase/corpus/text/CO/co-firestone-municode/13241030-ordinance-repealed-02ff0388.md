Ordinance No. 285, passed under date of February 22, 1991, and codified in Chapter 13.24 of the Firestone Municipal Code, is hereby repealed and of no further force or effect.
All other ordinances or portions thereof inconsistent or conflicting with this ordinance
or any portion hereof are repealed to the extent of such inconsistency or conflict.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
