United Power may install future improvements and technological advances to its equipment
and service within the Town, at United Power's discretion (but upon reasonable notice
to the Town), when such improvements and advances are technologically and economically
feasible, and safe and beneficial to the Town.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
