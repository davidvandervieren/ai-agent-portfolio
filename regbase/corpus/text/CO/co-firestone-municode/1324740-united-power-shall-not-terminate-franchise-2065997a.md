In no event, other than caused by the Town's material breach of this Agreement, does
United Power have the right to terminate this Franchise but may transfer its rights
hereunder after notice to Town and as otherwise set forth in Section 13.24.630 of this Chapter.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
