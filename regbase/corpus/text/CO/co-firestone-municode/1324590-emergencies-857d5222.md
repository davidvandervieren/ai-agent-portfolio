Upon request, United Power will cooperate with the Town in developing an emergency
management plan. In the case of any emergency or disaster, United Power shall, upon
verbal request of the Town, make available United Power facilities for temporary emergency
use during the emergency or the disaster period. Such use of United Power facilities
shall be of a limited duration and will only be allowed if the use does not interfere
with United Power's own use of such United Power facilities.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
