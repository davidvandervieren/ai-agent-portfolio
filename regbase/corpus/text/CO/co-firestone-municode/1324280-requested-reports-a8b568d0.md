Upon request by the Town, United Power shall provide the Town:
A.
A list of real property and leasehold interests in real property owned by United Power
within the municipal boundaries of the Town, for the purpose of calculating property
taxes.
B.
A map (and, to the extent available, a copy of such map and related data on computer
disk or compatible electronic format) indicating the location of United Power distribution
facilities within and contiguous to the Town.
C.
A report regarding the reliability of United Power facilities and electric service.
D.
A list of all Town electrical accounts and account numbers and items metered.
E.
A list of all street lights in the Town energized by United Power.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
