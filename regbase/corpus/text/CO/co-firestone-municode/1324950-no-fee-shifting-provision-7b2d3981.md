In any judicial or administrative action to enforce any of the terms or conditions
of this Franchise Agreement, each party shall be responsible for its own costs and
expenses incurred in such action, including reasonable attorney fees, unless the same
are otherwise awarded to a party pursuant to statute or Court rule.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
