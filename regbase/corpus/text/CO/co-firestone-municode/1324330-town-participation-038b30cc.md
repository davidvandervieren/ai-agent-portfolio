The Town shall have the right to approve major facility site plans within the Town.
Upon reasonable notice to United Power, the Town shall have the right to hold public
hearings related to United Power's facilities, site selection, undergrounding of overhead
lines, construction and service quality. United Power agrees to fully participate
in such public hearings as requested by the Town and to provide to the Town information
available to United Power that relates to the hearings described in this Section.
The foregoing shall not limit any rights of the Town under applicable law.
(Ord. 766 §1, 2011)
