"Accessory use" means a use that is naturally and normally incidental to, subordinate
to and devoted exclusively to the principal use of the premises and does not change
the basic character thereof, as determined by the principal use. Accessory uses may
include employee units, manager's unit and parking, but shall not include any use
that is prohibited or requires a special use permit in the underlying zone district,
such as outside storage.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02 (38))
