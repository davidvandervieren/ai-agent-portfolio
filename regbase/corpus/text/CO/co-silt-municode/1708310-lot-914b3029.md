"Lot" means the smallest unit of a subdivision individually numbered or designated
on the plat for purposes of description, recording, conveyance, development and taxation,
occupied or capable of being occupied by a building or group of buildings devoted
to a common use, together with the customary accessory buildings and open spaces belonging
to the same. "Lot" includes the words plot or parcel.
(Ord. 3-05 (part); Ord. 13-80 §§ 13.01 (part), 13.02(12))
