Editor's note— Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, adopted Apr. 27, 2015, repealed the former Ch. 5.12, §§ 5.12.010—5.12.450,
and enacted a new Ch. 5.12 as set out herein. The former chapter pertained to similar
subject matter. For prior history, see Code Comparative Table and Disposition List.
The town shall require that all individuals and/or companies who do work in the town,
except for that work done by an owner to one's own private residence, be properly
licensed as contractors.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
No person may engage in the following listed trades, jobs, or contractual services
(other than an owner on his private residence), as enumerated in this section, unless
he first secures a contractor license from the town and such contractor license is
approved by the town:
A.
Builder class AA—Unlimited contractor license (construction, alteration or repair
of any structure or type of occupancy, as regulated by the IBC).
B.
Builder class A—Commercial limited contractor license (construction, alteration, or
repair of any structure or type of construction or occupancy, with the exception of
type I-A and I-B fire-resistive construction, as regulated by the IBC).
C.
Builder class B—Light commercial limited contractor license (construction, alteration
or repair of any structure of type II and type V, as well as A-2, A-3, B, F-1, F-2,
I-1, M, R-3, R-4, S-1, S-2 and U occupancies as regulated by the IBC).
D.
Builder class C—Homebuilder (construction, alteration or repair of detached one and
two family dwellings, and multiple single-family dwellings (townhouses) not more than
three units and/or three stories in height with a separate means of egress, as well
as accessory structures as regulated by the IRC).
E.
Mechanical contractor license.
F.
Masonry contractor license.
G.
Concrete and form contractor license.
H.
Drywall contractor license.
I.
Framing contractor license.
J.
Roofing contractor license.
K.
Metal stud/structural steel contractor license.
L.
Excavation contractor license.
M.
House and building movers contractor license—No BEST card required.
N.
Demolition contractor license.
O.
Manufactured/modular/factory-built home installer contractor license—No BEST card
required.
P.
Exterior siding contractor license.
Q.
Electrician contractor license—No BEST card required.
R.
Plumber contractor license—No BEST card required.
S.
Specialty contractor license (painting, flooring, alarm/security system, landscaping,
irrigation, stucco, and the like)—No BEST card required.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
A.
All persons performing a trade, job or contractual service as listed in Section 5.12.020 within the Town of Silt shall first secure a BEST card;
B.
No BEST card shall last more than three years from the date of testing;
C.
Each contractor shall present such valid BEST card or ICC certification with his application
for contractor license, and shall timely submit new BEST card or ICC certification
if the one originally tendered expires during the term of the annual contractor license.
The town shall accept a valid BEST card that is issued by a jurisdiction with which
the town has an agreement providing for the acceptance of that jurisdiction's BEST
card. The validity of an ICC certification shall be verifiable with the ICC;
D.
The board shall set the BEST card testing fees annually, or more often as necessary,
by resolution;
E.
The town shall give a BEST test, upon an individual's request, any business day where
there is enough time for such individual to complete such test, in the town's sole
discretion;
F.
The town shall comprise the BEST test questions, and upon adoption of a newer building
code, the town shall modify the questions and answers on each BEST test to reflect
any code changes;
G.
An applicant for BEST test must obtain a minimum score of 70 percent on the BEST test
in order for the town to issue a BEST card;
H.
If an applicant scores below 70 percent, the applicant may retake another version
of the BEST test, in accordance with this section.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
A.
A complete application for a contractor license shall be on a form provided by the
town, and the town shall not process such application if incomplete.
B.
The town shall review applications for contractor license issued under this chapter;
the standards to be applied in the conduct of such review shall be the protection
of the public health, welfare and public safety of the citizens of the town.
C.
Contractors shall be qualified in terms of their skills, knowledge and practical experience
to contract for or to do the work for which they seek to be licensed.
D.
All licenses enumerated in this chapter shall be issued in accordance with the terms
and provisions of this chapter and other town ordinances and shall be subject to the
supervisory clauses contained in this chapter.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
An applicant for one or more of the trades, jobs or contractual services listed in
Section 5.12.020 shall submit to the town a fee set by the board annually (or more often as necessary)
by resolution, complete with a copy of his state certification, BEST card and/or ICC
certification, if applicable, as well as liability insurance and workmen's compensation
insurance, as required by this chapter, in order for the town to review such contractor
license application.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
An applicant for a contractor license per this chapter shall keep at all times during
the period of the license commercial liability insurance with the minimum limits of
$100,000.00 per individual and $500,000.00 for any one accident, property casualty
insurance with a minimum limit of not less than $100,000.00 for any one incident,
and workers' compensation insurance for all employees as required by law.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
An applicant for a contractor license shall submit to the town an insurance certificate(s),
signed by a qualified agent of an insurance company, stating that the policies required
in this chapter has been issued to the licensee with the minimum policy limits described
in this chapter including the effective date of such policy(ies) and the expiration
date of such policy(ies). Applicant shall provide updated insurance certificate(s)
to the town annually.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
In the event of a cancellation of a policy, the licensee shall be required to furnish
to the town a new certificate in full compliance with this chapter, within ten days.
If licensee does not furnish a new certificate in full compliance with this chapter,
the town will hereby revoke the license until licensee submits such certificate.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
An applicant for multiple contractor licenses within the town shall pay the entire
fee for the first license and shall pay half of the fee for each additional license
requested, according to the board approved annual resolution to set such fees.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
A contractor license issued under the provisions of this chapter shall terminate on
the last day of the fiscal year. The town may accept renewals for contractor licenses
beginning on December 1 and proceeding throughout the new year.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
The community development department may, upon evidence of a contractor's material
violation of the town's ordinances and building code(s), request that the planning
and zoning commission hold a hearing to review the evidence. The town shall notify
the contractor in person or shall notify him in writing (letter or email) within five
days of hearing. The town shall use the address on the latest contractor license application
for notification purposes.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
The planning and zoning commission shall have the power to temporarily or permanently
suspend the contractor's license if, after a proper hearing, the commission finds
that the evidence warrants such suspension. Such evidence must include acts prohibited
by any town ordinance, building code, or state law.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
A licensed contractor may apply for and be issued permits to do only such work as
he is entitled to do under his respective license.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
The town will deem null and void any application for a permit or license filed in
derogation of this title and title 15 .
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
A.
It is unlawful for any person to engage in the business of installing manufactured,
modular or factory-built homes, without first having secured a contractor license
and a State of Colorado installer license;
B.
No licensed manufactured, modular or factory-built home installer may prepare the
site or do plumbing, heating or electrical work, without first having secured those
respective licenses.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
The town shall allow a licensed contractor to employ individuals that do not have
their own BEST cards, so long as licensed contractor proves (if town requests) that
such individuals are covered under his workers' compensation insurance policy and
payroll and provided that licensed contractor is present for all required inspections.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
During the term of an active building permit, the licensed contractor shall be responsible
for such permit until the permit is concluded. However, notwithstanding the foregoing,
the licensed contractor may cease his relationship with the property owner and/or
building permit applicant while the permit is still active, and, in this case, the
licensed contractor shall immediately contact the community development department
to sign a document that he is no longer responsible for the permit.
(Ord. No. 2-2015, § 1, 4-27-2015)
The town may issue a citation for a violator of Chapter 5.12 (contractor licenses) in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 3, 11-28-2016)
