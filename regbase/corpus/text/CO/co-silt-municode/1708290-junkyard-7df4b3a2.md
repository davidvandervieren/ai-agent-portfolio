"Junkyard" means a lot, land or structure, or part thereof, used primarily for the
collecting, storage and sale of waste paper, rags, scrap metal or discarded material;
or for the collecting, dismantling, storage and/or salvaging of machinery or vehicles
not in operating condition, and for the sale of parts thereof.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(11))
