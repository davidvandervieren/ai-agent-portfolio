No building permits shall be issued for the construction, reconstruction or alteration
of any structure in the town unless such construction, reconstruction or alteration
conforms to the standards governing the use of safety glazing materials for hazardous
locations, as those terms are defined in article 2 of title 9, C.R.S. 1973.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.11.01 (part))
The board adopts, as standards for the use of safety glazing materials in hazardous
locations for the town, the provisions of article 2 of title 9, C.R.S. 1973, which
are incorporated in this chapter by this reference.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.11.01 (part))
The building inspector of the town shall inspect all places not inspected by the state
of Colorado Division of Labor under the provisions of article 2 of title 9, C.R.S.
1973, to determine whether such places are in compliance with the town's standards
for the use of safety glazing materials.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.11.01 (part))
