he intent of this chapter is to provide a guide for applicants to condominiumize existing
buildings or proposed buildings within the town into individual air space units, in
conformance with this title. An applicant for condominiumization may be subject to
other chapters within this title.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
The following terms are hereby defined. Whenever a term is not defined, the town administrator
or his designee shall define such term, according to generally acceptable practices,
the town attorney's legal definition or by reference to the American Planning Association.
1.
"Condominiumization" means the process by which an existing building is separated
legally into defined air space units, through the creation of a condominium map that
lists general common elements, limited common elements, and individual air space units.
2.
"Condominium declaration" or "declaration" means a document recorded in conformance
with this title and the Colorado Condominium Ownership Act or Colorado Common Interest
Ownership Act, or otherwise governing the subject condominium units or individual
air space units and their owners, which document describes the character, duration,
rights, obligations and/or limitations of condominium ownership.
3.
"Condominium map" means the plat(s) showing, among other things, the condominium units,
general common elements, limited common elements, and the boundaries of the project.
4.
"Condominium unit" means an individual air space unit together with the interest in
the common elements appurtenant to such unit, including but not limited to storage
area(s), individual parking space(s), porches, and decks.
5.
"General common elements" means the land or interest therein on which a building or
buildings including condominium units are located; the foundations, columns, girders,
beams, supports, main walls, trusses, roofs, halls, corridors, lobbies, staffs, stairways,
fire escapes, entrances, and exits of such building or buildings; the basements, yards,
gardens, parking areas, and storage areas; the premises for the lodging of custodians
or persons in charge of the property; installations of central services such as electric
service, natural gas service, water service, wastewater service, central air conditioning
or cooling, elevators, tanks, fire suppression, motors, fans, compressors, ducts and
in general all apparatus and installations existing for common uses; such provided
for in the declaration; and all other parts of the property necessary or convenient
for the condominium units' existence, maintenance and safety, or normally in common
use by residents of such building or buildings.
6.
"Individual air space unit" consists of any unenclosed room or rooms occupying all
or part of a floor or floors in such a building or buildings including condominium
units or one or more floors to be used for residential, commercial, professional,
or industrial purposes which has access to a public street either directly by or through
general common elements or limited common elements.
7.
"Limited common elements" means those common elements designated in the declaration
as reserved for use by fewer than all the owners of the individual air space or condominium
units.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
The creation of condominium units constitutes a subdivision which shall be subject
to subdivision approval procedures and public notification requirements pursuant to
this title.
B.
No person, whether owner or agent of an owner, shall sell or agree to sell individual
air space units, common elements, or limited common elements, nor shall any person
use a structure as a condominium unit(s), unless the board has approved such condominium
unit(s).
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant for condominiumization of an existing building or a proposed building
of six or more units shall comply with all subdivision regulations of this title,
including the procedural requirements and engineered design data and specifications
in Chapter 16.04 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant requesting condominiumization of an existing building with five or fewer
units may apply for minor subdivision which shall comply with all subdivision regulations
in Chapter 16.10 of this Code, including all general application submittal requirements in this title.
The town administrator or his designee shall determine at the pre-application conference
whether the application has a degree of complexity that the commission requires the
information provided for in Chapter 16.04 of this Code regarding a major subdivision. Nothing herein relieves the applicant
of compliance with Chapter 17.42 of the Code, regarding site plan review for multi-family structures. Further, the
commission and the board shall find the following in order for the application to
be considered a minor subdivision:
A.
All the proposed condominium units are located in a building existing at the date
the application is filed;
B.
Each condominium unit has adequate ingress and egress directly or through common or
limited common elements to public access;
C.
The board determines that the application is not subject to those requirements for
a major subdivision pursuant to Chapter 16.04 ;
D.
No part of the area depicted in the proposed condominium map has been previously subdivided
by a minor subdivision (or subdivision exemption prior to this codification) pursuant
to Chapter 16.10 ;
E.
Utility lines for water, wastewater, electric, natural gas, telephone and cable are
already installed within or adjacent to the area proposed for condominiumization;
F.
The board determines that the applicant has fragmented or divided a land use project
into phases to avoid the requirements of a major subdivision.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
Whether the condominium application is intended for five or fewer units (minor subdivision)
or six or more units (major subdivision), an applicant for condominiumization shall
create a condominium map that indicates the following:
1.
Title of the subdivision, clearly stating that the application is a condominium plat;
2.
Location of all condominium units, with each condominium unit clearly numbered;
3.
Location of general common elements;
4.
Location of limited common elements;
5.
Boundaries of the condominium project, depicted as one lot;
6.
Statement that the condominium application conforms to all procedural requirements
and engineered design data and specifications in Chapter 16.04 (major subdivision) of this Code, or conversely, that the commission agrees that
the requirements of Chapter 16.10 (minor subdivision) is adequate.
7.
Statement regarding the applicability of condominium declarations, with the town as
a third party beneficiary to the declarations;
B.
Condominium plat certificates as follows:
1.
Certificate of dedication and ownership, including all dedications, including utility,
access, and drainage easements, and including parkland, open space, trails and drainage
facilities, and the terms for which such easements and/or dedication shall occur;
2.
Surveyor's certificate from the engineer that prepared the condominium plat;
3.
Board of trustees certificate;
4.
Planning and zoning commission certificate;
5.
Title insurance company's or title attorney's certificate;
6.
Utility companies certificate; and
7.
Garfield County clerk and recorder's certificate.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
The town shall not issue a certificate of occupancy for a multi-family building until
applicant receives approval for the applicable subdivision application and until applicant
submits and town staff approves an improvements survey for that building, in conformance
with this Code.
B.
The town shall not issue a certificate of occupancy for a condominium building until
applicant receives approval for condominiumization, and until applicant submits and
the town staff approves an improvements survey and a condominium construction and
air space survey, in conformance with this Code.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
