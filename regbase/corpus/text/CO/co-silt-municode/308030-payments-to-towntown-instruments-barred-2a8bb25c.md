All town warrants or orders lawfully drawn upon the treasurer shall bear interest
at the rate of six percent yearly, from date of presentation thereof for payment to
the treasurer until there is money in the treasury for the payment thereof, and it
shall be the duty of the treasurer to endorse upon any such warrant or order which
may be presented to him for payment the date of such presentation and the rate of
interest the same will draw from that date, and to subscribe the same with his official
signature. (Gen. Ords. Art. XVII § 3, 1936)
All taxes, licenses, fines, penalties, forfeitures, debts, dues and obligations of
any sort whatever, due as of the effective date of the ordinance codified in part
in this chapter, which was adopted January 6, 1936, or that may thereafter become
due and payable to the town, shall be paid in lawful money of the United States. (Gen.
Ords. Art XVII. § 4, 1936)
A.
The town shall charge each maker of an insufficient fund check a fee as set by the
board of trustees by resolution annually, or more often as necessary. The fee is due
to the town even in the event that the check is subsequently paid by the financial
institution or the maker. Maker of insufficient fund check shall submit the fee within
ten days of the date of the town's notification to the maker by regular mail. Nothing
herein relieves the maker from paying the original debt owed.
B.
If the assessment fee is not paid within the ten-day period contemplated in (A), the
maker shall submit to the town another fee as set forth by the board of trustees by
resolution annually, or more often as necessary, which is in addition to all other
applicable fees in this code.
C.
In addition to the fees contemplated in (A) and (B) above, the maker of such insufficient
fund check shall also pay to the town any fees or charges imposed by a bank on the
town as a result of the insufficient fund check. The town shall notify the maker of
such fees either in writing or at town hall.
D.
If the maker of the insufficient fund check does not pay his debt to the town, the
town may either issue a lien against the maker's property or issue a citation in accordance
with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. 2-93 § 1)
(Ord. No. 2-2011, § 1, 4-25-2011; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
The town may issue a citation to a violator of Chapter 3,08 (warrants drawn and payments
received) in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. 2-93 § 2)
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 2, 11-28-2016)
No warrants, scrip or orders on the treasurer shall be received in payment for the
whole or any part whatever of any taxes, licenses, fines, penalties, forfeitures,
debts, dues and obligations due as of the effective date of the ordinance codified
in part in this chapter, which was adopted January 6, 1936, or that may thereafter
become due and payable to the town.
(Gen. Ords. Art. XVII § 5, 1936)
A.
Purpose. Certain sections of this code, other applicable laws and regulations, or
written agreements require applicants who seek licenses, permits, town approvals for
land use or other development, or other town action (collectively, "applicant"), to
pay all costs the town incurs in connection with such issuance, grant or approval
and to pay other established fees, charges and costs related to such action. The purpose
of this section is to establish penalties, including the imposition of interest, the
denial of future approvals, and reservation of the town's right to pursue all available
legal remedies, where an applicant or other person, entity or group, without limitation
responsible to pay such costs (hereinafter a "debtor"), fails to pay amounts owed
to the town (hereinafter a "debt") in a timely manner.
B.
Interest on Account. Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal of any debt owed
to the town from the later of the date which appears on the town's statement to the
debtor or the effective date of a writing executed by the town which evidences the
debt, and shall not exceed eighteen percent annually, or one and one-half percent
per month, compounded annually.
C.
Withholding or Denying Future Approvals. If an applicant fails to pay a debt, in addition
to all other penalties or remedies in this code and under applicable laws, the town
reserves the right to suspend review of the debtor's application, withhold approval
for any license, permit, resolution, agreement, ordinance or other matter requiring
town action for which the applicant or debtor has applied, and to postpone public
hearings, if scheduled or to be scheduled, on any matter the applicant or debtor has
presented to the town, until and unless such debt is paid in full, with interest and
other penalties due under this section and other applicable laws.
D.
Amounts Due Upon Delinquency. All debts shall be due and payable in full without demand
within thirty days after the town mails or otherwise transmits written notice of same
to the subject applicant(s) or debtor(s) (hereinafter a "statement"). Upon a finding
by the town treasurer that any town account is unpaid for more than thirty days ("delinquency"),
and ten calendar days after the town sends the debtor(s) written notice of same, all
payments due shall become payable in full immediately, with accrued interest, with
any additional penalties allowed under this section, and with costs of collection
incurred by the town, including but not necessarily limited to, any attorney fees
or out of pocket costs. If such debtor(s) pay(s) all amounts due within such ten day
period, the delinquency shall be deemed cured in the same manner as if such delinquency
had not occurred. Nevertheless, multiple delinquencies shall subject the applicant(s)
or debtor(s) to all available additional penalties and remedies under this section
and other applicable laws.
E.
Remedies Not Exclusive. The remedies provided in this section shall be cumulative
and not exclusive and shall be in addition to any other remedies provided by law.
Nothing in this section shall be construed a waiver of any civil remedies available
to the town.
F.
Optional Filing of Claim of Lien.
1.
The town may (but is not required to) direct the town clerk within sixty days after
such payment is due, to make out, sign, attest with the seal of the town, and file
for record in the office of the county clerk a claim of lien against property owned
by the applicant(s) or debtor(s) for the unpaid amount due to the town.
2.
When a claim of lien is so filed and any amount appertaining thereto is paid in full,
the town clerk shall release the lien against any specific property either by entering
and signing a receipt of payment upon the margin of the record thereof or by filing
for record in the office of the county clerk a separate release wherein payment of
the principal, interest and any penalty is recited.
G.
Indemnity. Any applicant or debtor who fails to pay a debt to the town shall defend,
indemnify and hold the town harmless from any and all claims or losses of any nature
whatsoever, including but not limited to attorney fees and costs, the town incurs
resulting from such debt.
H.
Severability. If any section, subsection, paragraph, clause, phrase or provision of
these regulations shall be adjudged invalid or held to be unconstitutional by a court
of competent jurisdiction, the validity of these regulations shall not be affected
in whole or in part, other than the provision adjudged to be invalid or unconstitutional.
(Ord. 16-00)
