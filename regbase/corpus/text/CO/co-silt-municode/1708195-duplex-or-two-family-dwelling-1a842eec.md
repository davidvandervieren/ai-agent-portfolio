"Duplex" means a two-dwelling detached principal building containing no more than
two (2) dwelling units sharing a common wall between both dwelling units or sharing
a common ceiling or floor, in whole or in part, connecting the two (2) dwelling units,
each of which is designed for and used as a dwelling unit exclusively by one (1) family.
Each unit may be further subdivided, creating two (2) separately conveyable single
family dwelling units, each with their appurtenant lots. A duplex is not a single-family
dwelling with an attached or detached additional dwelling unit, as defined by this
code.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
