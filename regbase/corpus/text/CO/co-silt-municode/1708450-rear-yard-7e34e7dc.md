"Rear yard" means a yard extending across the full width of the lot and measured between
the rear line of the lot and the rear building line of the main building. For those
lots that do not have an easily distinguishable rear yard, the town administrator
or his designee shall determine the location of the rear yard.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(44))
