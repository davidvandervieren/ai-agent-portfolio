"Public utility uses, facilities and services" means the following and similar uses,
facilities and services: above-grade or below-grade water storage tanks, substations,
transformers and related structures; service access roads, public works service facilities,
underground public utility transmission and distribution lines, and other minor underground
improvements associated therewith.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
