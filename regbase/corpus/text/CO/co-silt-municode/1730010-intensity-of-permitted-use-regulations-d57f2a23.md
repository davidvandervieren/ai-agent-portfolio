Chapter 17.30 - PUBLIC UTILITY ZONE DISTRICT
17.30.010 - Intensity of permitted use regulations.
Due to the unique nature of public utilities, it is recognized that the town has complete
discretion over the location, placement and maintenance of public utilities.
(Ord. 5-05 (part))
