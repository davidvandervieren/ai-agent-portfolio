"Sign" means an object or device or any part thereof visible from a street, sidewalk
or mall that is used to advertise, identify, display, direct or attract the attention
of a pedestrian or motorist to an object, place, person, institution, organization,
business, product, service, event or location by any means, including words, letters,
figures, designs, logos, fixtures, colors, illumination or projected images.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(24))
