The director of community development is authorized, and it shall be his duty, to
enforce and administer the provisions of this title.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 10.02)
In case any building is erected, constructed, reconstructed, altered, repaired, converted
or maintained, or any building or land is used, in violation of this title, the director
of community development or any other appropriate town authority, or any person who
would be damaged by such violation, in addition to other remedies, may institute an
action for injunction, or mandamus, or other appropriate action or proceeding to prevent
or remove such violation.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 10.06)
