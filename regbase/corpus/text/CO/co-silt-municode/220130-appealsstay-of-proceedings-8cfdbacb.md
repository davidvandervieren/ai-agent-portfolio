There is established a board of appeals and examiners, the members of which shall
be appointed by the board of trustees.
(Ord. 128 § l (part), 1978)
The board of appeals and examiners shall consist of five regular members and one ex
officio member. Each member shall serve for a three-year term and vacancies shall
be filled by the board of trustees for such terms or lesser terms, as necessary.
(Ord. 128 § 1 (part), 1978)
A.
The five regular members shall be either:
1.
A licensed architect; or
2.
A registered professional engineer; or
3.
A Class A or B general contractor, as defined in Chapter 5.12 .
B.
The board of trustees may appoint any other person to the board of appeals and examiners
who it determines is qualified by experience and training to pass upon matters pertaining
to building construction; provided, that there is unavailable for appointment any
qualified person within the categories listed in subsection A.
(Ord. 128 § 1(a), 1978)
The ex officio member of the board of appeals and examiners shall be the town building
inspector, who shall act as secretary of the board of appeals and examiners. The ex
officio member of the board of appeals and examiners shall be without power to vote
in each case.
(Ord. 128 § 1(b), 1978)
A quorum for the transaction of business of the board of appeals and examiners shall
consist of two of the regular members.
(Ord. 128 § 3(part), 1978)
The board of appeals and examiners shall adopt bylaws for the conduct of its business
not inconsistent with this chapter, and shall keep accurate minutes and records, and
make such rules of procedure as it deems necessary.
(Ord. 128 § 3 (part), 1978)
The board of appeals and examiners shall have the following powers:
A.
To hear appeals from persons licensed by the town as contractors and concerning their
use of construction materials or procedures found to be at variance with the town's
building code by the town building inspector;
B.
To hear appeals from persons not licensed by the town as contractors and concerning
their use of construction materials or procedures found to be at variance with the
town's building inspector;
C.
To adopt reasonable rules and regulations for conducting examinations and investigations
and to render all decisions in writing to the building official;
D.
To hear and determine all applications for contractor's licenses and suspension or
revocation of the same in conformity with town ordinances and board of appeals and
examiners procedures; and
E.
To perform such other duties as the board of trustees may, by the ordinance codified
at this chapter or any ordinance adopted herein, or any amendment thereto, from time
to time impose upon it.
(Ord. 128 § 2, 1978)
Any person aggrieved by any decision or order of the building inspector may appeal
such decision or order to the board of appeals and examiners.
(Ord. 128 § 4 (part), 1978)
Every appeal to the board of appeals and examiners must be perfected within thirty
days from the date of the decision or order appealed from. The board of appeals and
examiners may extend the time within which an appeal may be taken where grounds for
enlargement of time are shown. In cases where the decision or order appealed from
pertains to a structure or building which can be demonstrated by the building inspector
to be unsafe or dangerous, the building inspector may apply to the board of appeals
and examiners to limit the time for such appeal in order to avoid any hazard to life
or property.
(Ord. 128 § 4(a) (part), 1978.
An appeal is perfected by filing of notice of appeal with the secretary of the board
of appeals and examiners in the office of the building inspector upon a form provided
by the board of appeals and examiners. Such notice shall contain appropriate reference
to the decision or order appealed from, as well as the grounds of the appeal. A duplicate
copy of such notice of appeal shall be filed with the chairman of the board of appeals
and examiners.
(Ord. 128 § 4(a) (part), 1978)
At the time of perfecting a written appeal to the board of appeals and examiners,
the appellant shall be required to pay an appeal fee as set by the board of trustees
by resolution annually, or more often as necessary. At its sole discretion, the board
of appeals may return to the appellant such fee if the board of appeals rules substantially
in favor of the appellant.
(Ord. 3, Series of 1992 § 1 (part); Ord. 10 Series of 1983 § 1 (part); Ord. 128 §
4(b), 1978)
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
Whenever an appeal is taken to the board of appeals and examiners, the building inspector
shall transmit to the board of appeals and examiners all relevant records and data
upon which the appeal was taken.
(Ord. 128 § 4(c), 1978)
An appeal to the board of appeals and examiners shall stay all proceedings in connection
with the decision or order appealed from unless and until the building inspection
has certified to the board of appeals and examiners, after notice of appeal has been
perfected, that a stay would cause hazard to life and property. In such case, proceedings
pursuant to the decision or order of the building inspector shall not be stayed except
by order of the board of appeals and examiners or by a restraining order issued by
a court of record of competent jurisdiction. No such restraining order, whether temporary
or otherwise, shall be granted without notice, as prescribed in the Rules of Civil
Procedure, to the building inspector, and without due cause shown.
(Ord. 128 § 4(d), 1978)
Any work done contrary to the order of the building inspector after docketing of an
appeal under this chapter, pending determination of such appeal, shall be subject
to abatement should the order be affirmed and may not be considered by the board of
appeals and examiners as a mitigating or extenuating circumstance.
(Ord. 128 § 4(e), 1978)
The board of appeals and examiners shall fix a reasonable time for the hearing of
an appeal, giving notice in writing to the parties in interest, and shall reach its
decision within a reasonable time thereafter. Parties may appear before the board
of appeals and examiners in person, by agent or by attorney. The board of appeals
and examiners may require additional data and tests necessary for adequate consideration
of the appeal.
(Ord. 128 § 4(f), 1978)
The board of appeals and examiners shall have the power in all cases appealed to it
from decisions or orders of the building inspector, to reverse or affirm, or modify
in whole or in part, the decision or order appealed therefrom. No decision of the
board of appeals and examiners shall vary or be inconsistent with the terms, provisions
and requirements of this chapter. The board of appeals and examiners shall render
all decisions and findings in writing to the building inspector with a duplicate copy
to the appellant.
(Ord. 128 § 4 (g), 1978)
Within thirty days after the entry of any decision or order of the board of appeals
and examiners, any person who is aggrieved by such decision or order may seek to review
the same in a court of record of competent jurisdiction. Review shall not be extended
further than to determine whether the board of appeals and examiners has exceeded
its jurisdiction or abused its discretion.
(Ord. 128 § 4(h), 1978)
