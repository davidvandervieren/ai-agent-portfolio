A.
The intent of this chapter is to detail the various steps necessary in order to annex
a parcel into the Town of Silt, negotiate terms of an annexation and development agreement
to enumerate the exactions and public dedications, and subsequently zone the parcel.
The process by which the town approves a parcel for annexation into the town is ruled
by this Code, state statutes and the Town's Home Rule Charter. Because the annexation
of property is a matter of state law, the provisions of Colorado law will control
in addition to this chapter. In the event of a conflict between a statutory provision
and a provision of this Chapter 16.08 , the more specific shall control;
B.
As described in this chapter, the commission shall review and consider an applicant's
annexation petition and annexation application, together with the applicant's zoning
application and sketch plan application for the property, and provide recommendations
concerning the subject annexation, zoning and sketch plan to the board, prior to any
action by the board on an applicant's annexation petition and annexation application,
zoning application and sketch plan application;
C.
The applicant for annexation should consider retaining a planning consultant, attorney
or other professional(s) if the proposed annexation is complex. A property owner considering
an annexation petition and annexation application may wish to consult an attorney
familiar with municipal annexation law. While the town staff will cooperate with the
landowner and/or applicant for annexation, the town is not bound by any representations
made by staff regarding the status of or requirements of applicable laws, rules or
regulations;
D.
The annexation process may be a lengthy process, in order for the town and the applicant
and/or property owner to negotiate terms acceptable to both parties regarding the
annexation. Further, the town requires that the property owner and/or applicant proceed
through the subdivision and zoning process concurrently with the annexation process,
in order for the town to fully understand the scope of the request.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
An applicant for annexation shall schedule with the planning department a pre-application
conference in order to fully describe the applicant's intent regarding annexation,
zoning and subdivision of the subject parcel. At this meeting, the applicant and the
town shall discuss the following:
1.
One-sixth of the subject property's perimeter must be contiguous with the town limits,
as detailed in Colorado Revised Statutes;
2.
Application requirements for an annexation petition and accompanying application;
3.
Compliance of the proposal with the town's comprehensive plan;
4.
Water rights associated with the parcel to be annexed, or, if the property does not
have appurtenant water rights, the applicant's proposed payment of fee "in lieu" of
water rights dedication and/or purchase of a water contract(s);
5.
Proposed zoning of the parcel to be annexed;
6.
Proposed subdivision of the parcel to be annexed;
7.
Compliance with state statutes and local ordinances;
8.
Impact fees;
9.
Annexation impact report (if parcel is ten acres or larger), unless waived by Garfield
County;
10.
Public and private dedications;
11.
Environmental impacts; and
12.
Extension of public services to and within the parcel to be annexed.
B.
Following the pre-application conference and following the applicant's submittal of
the annexation petition and annexation application, the town administrator or his
designee shall schedule a staff meeting with those members of staff and consultants
that he deems necessary, in order to fully discuss the application and the proposed
annexation's impact on the town.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
Submittal requirements. An applicant for annexation shall provide such written information
on the land use application form and on forms provided by the town, plus any other
supplemental information needed to convey the information to the town, including the
following:
1.
Annexation petition in conformance with the state statutes;
2.
Annexation application, in conformance with this Code;
3.
Annexation impact report, in conformance with state statutes and this Code;
4.
Annexation plat, in conformance with state statutes and this Code;
5.
Annexation fee and deposit, in conformance with this Code;
6.
Zoning application in conformance with this Code; and
7.
Sketch plan application in conformance with this Code.
B.
Annexation application. An applicant for annexation shall submit an application with
the following information:
1.
Name and consent of applicant;
2.
Name of property owner, if not the applicant;
3.
Acreage, including existing lot and block information within Garfield County;
4.
Proposed land uses and proposed zoning;
5.
Vicinity map at a scale no less than one inch equals two thousand feet, with the subject
parcel outlined;
6.
Surrounding land uses and zoning;
7.
Impact statement for land use activities;
8.
Title commitment for the property;
9.
Deed for the property;
10.
Easements not shown in the title commitment or the public record;
11.
Statement regarding compliance with the town's comprehensive plan; and
12.
Special districts affecting or affected by annexed parcel.
C.
Water rights. An applicant shall comply with the town's water rights dedication Code
( Chapter 16.18 ) in its entirety and shall specifically provide an historical use affidavit concerning
the water rights proposed for dedication to the town pursuant to existing town ordinances.
The historical use affidavit shall contain the following information:
1.
The name(s) and address(es) of the owner(s) of the water rights proposed for dedication;
2.
A legal description of the land to be annexed and/or subdivided, re-subdivided and/or
provided with municipal water services;
3.
Existing and proposed use of property;
4.
The total number of acres to be annexed;
5.
The total number of acres presently being irrigated and/or proposed to remain in irrigation;
6.
A copy of all decrees concerning all water rights appurtenant to the property and
all water rights proposed for dedication;
7.
A copy of any court decree, judgment, order or decision that affects the title or
possession of such water rights;
8.
A copy of the documents by which owner received title to the water rights appurtenant
to the property and/or proposed for dedication;
9.
A copy of all diversion records for the last six years of the water rights proposed
for dedication;
10.
The owner's statement as to the historic use of the water rights appurtenant to the
property and/or proposed for dedication;
11.
Such additional information as the town determines is necessary or appropriate.
D.
Annexation impact report. For any annexation ten acres or larger, unless waived by
Garfield County, the applicant shall prepare an annexation impact report twenty-five
days prior to the scheduled board hearing to consider the resolution of substantial
compliance. The applicant for annexation shall file a copy of this report with the
Garfield County board of county commissioners twenty-five days prior to the scheduled
board hearing to consider the resolution of substantial compliance. The annexation
impact report shall include the following:
1.
A map of the area to be annexed showing its relation to the existing town boundaries,
the present streets, water infrastructure, wastewater infrastructure, irrigation infrastructure,
utility infrastructure, irrigation ditches, drainage structures (natural and manmade),
extension of streets, and proposed land use patterns in the area proposed for annexation;
2.
An introduction that addresses the requirements in C.R.S. 31-12-104 and 105;
3.
A copy of any pre-annexation agreement;
4.
A statement regarding extension of utilities, including town infrastructure;
5.
A statement identifying the existing governmental or quasi-governmental districts
within the area to be annexed;
6.
A statement regarding the effect of the annexation on the public school system, including
the estimated number of students generated and the capital construction required to
educate such students;
7.
A statement regarding the effect of the annexation on any Garfield County facility,
including but not limited to roads, bridges, and drainages; and
8.
Any other item that town staff may require.
E.
Annexation map/plat. An applicant for annexation shall submit an annexation map/plat
which includes the following:
1.
Name of the proposed annexation;
2.
Acreage of the proposed annexation;
3.
Name of the surveyor who prepared the annexation map/plat;
4.
Date of the survey;
5.
North arrow and graphic scale;
6.
Adjacent property owners, parcel numbers and address of record;
7.
Name of the applicant(s) filing the annexation map;
8.
Vicinity map at a scale of no more than one inch equals two thousand feet, with area
proposed for annexation outlined;
9.
Existing easements and special district boundaries;
10.
Contiguous boundary with the town limit;
11.
Calculation indicating that at least one-sixth the perimeter of the subject property
is contiguous with the town limit;
12.
Written legal description, as well as the survey points and basis of bearings; and
13.
Annexation certificates as indicated in Appendix A of this title as follows:
a.
Certificate of dedication and ownership;
b.
Surveyor's certificate from the surveyor who prepared the plat;
c.
Board of trustees certificate;
d.
Planning and zoning commission certificate;
e.
County surveyor's certificate;
f.
Title insurance company's or title attorney's certificate; and
g.
Garfield County Clerk and Recorder's Certificate.
F.
Copies. The applicant for annexation shall submit to the town eighteen copies of the
annexation plat, including supplemental material as required by this title, sized
eight and one-half inches by eleven inches for textual material and eleven inches
by seventeen inches for any maps and plat documents that will be included in the commission
packets. Additionally, four copies of all maps shall be submitted for staff review,
each measuring no less than twenty-four inches by thirty-six inches, plus four copies
of all other textual supplemental materials. The applicant for annexation, at his
sole cost, shall submit additional copies as needed and as requested by the planning
department for the board meeting(s). The above submittal requirements apply to annexation
plat revisions and re-submittals. Additionally, the applicant for annexation shall
submit to the town two electronic copies of the annexation plat and supplemental materials.

A.
As required by Section 16.08.010 , following the town's review of an applicant's petition for annexation and annexation
application, together with the applicant's zoning application and sketch plan application,
but in no event fewer than thirty days or more than sixty days from the date that
the town determines the application complete, the town shall schedule the application
before the commission for a recommendation to the board, unless another time period
is agreed to by the applicant;
B.
The applicant for annexation shall publicly notice the time, date and location of
the meeting per the guidelines set forth in this title; and
C.
No public hearing may occur if the public notification has not occurred per the provisions
of this title.

A.
The commission shall consider the annexation petition and the annexation application,
together with the applicant's zoning application and sketch plan application, at a
regularly scheduled meeting, following the applicant's public notification per this
title;
B.
The commission may recommend to the board approval, approval with conditions, or denial
of the application, or may continue the hearing to another date(s) in order to more
fully discuss the annexation petition and annexation application;
C.
If the commission continues the public hearing, the application shall be placed on
the next regularly scheduled commission agenda, provided that the applicant agrees
to such placement;
D.
If the commission approves, approves with conditions or denies the application, the
town shall provide a full description of the commission's action within the minutes
of the meeting, which shall then be forwarded to the board; and
E.
If the commission does not act on the application, either by recommending to the board
approval, or approval with conditions, or denial, within thirty days of the final
public hearing, the annexation petition and annexation application are deemed acceptable
to the commission. The town and the applicant may agree to a different time period
for commission review.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
Town staff shall prepare and the board shall consider the resolution of substantial
compliance regarding the annexation petition and annexation application at a regularly
scheduled meeting within sixty days following the commission action to approve, approve
with conditions or deny the application;
B.
The resolution of substantial compliance is the first step in the annexation process
before the board. The board shall determine whether the property is eligible for annexation,
per C.R.S. § 31-12-110:
C.
The board, upon approving the resolution of substantial compliance, sets a public
hearing no fewer than thirty days nor longer than sixty days from the date of approval
of the resolution of substantial compliance;
D.
The applicant for annexation shall publicly notice the time, date and location of
public hearing for the resolution of findings of fact and conclusions, per the following
guidelines:
1.
Not less than one-sixth of the perimeter of the area proposed to be annexed is contiguous
with the Town of Silt, Colorado;
2.
A community of interest exists between the town and the area proposed to be annexed
to the Town of Silt, Colorado;
3.
The area proposed to be annexed is urban or will be urbanized in the near future;
4.
The area proposed to be annexed is integrated or is capable of being integrated with
the Town of Silt, Colorado;
5.
In establishing the boundaries of the area proposed to be annexed, no real property
held in identical ownership, whether consisting of one tract or parcel of real estate
or two or more contiguous tracts or parcels of real estate or two or more contiguous
tracts or parcels of real estate, will be divided into separate parts or parcels without
the written consent of the landowners;
6.
In establishing the boundaries of the area proposed to be annexed, no real property
held in identical ownership, whether consisting of one tract or parcel of real estate
or two or more contiguous tracts or parcels of real estate, comprising twenty acres
or more (which together with the buildings and improvements situated thereon has a
valuation for assessment in excess of two hundred thousand dollars for ad valorem
tax purposes for the year next preceding the annexation) shall be included without
the written consent of the landowners;
7.
The property proposed for annexation is not presently part of any incorporated city
or town, nor have annexation proceedings commenced for the annexation of part of all
of such property to another municipality;
8.
The proposed annexation will not result in the detachment of real property from any
school district and the attachment of the same to another school district;
9.
The proposed annexation will not have the effect of extending the municipal boundary
more than three miles in any direction in one year;
E.
The applicant for annexation shall publish in a newspaper the resolution or the petition
once a week for four consecutive weeks in a manner prescribed by this title, prior
to the public hearing for the resolution of findings of fact and conclusions, per
the guidelines set forth in this title;
F.
The applicant for annexation shall also send a copy of the published notice, together
with a copy of the resolution of substantial compliance and annexation petition as
filed, and the annexation impact report, if not waived, by registered mail, to the
clerk of the board of county commissioners and to the county attorney, as well as
to any special district or school district having territory within the area to be
annexed, no fewer than twenty-five days prior to the date fixed for the public hearing
to consider the resolution of findings of fact;
G.
No public hearing may occur if the public notification has not occurred per the provisions
of this title.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
Town staff shall prepare a resolution of findings of fact and conclusions for the
board's consideration, following the applicant's public notification of the hearing
and following the planning and zoning commission's rendering of a recommendation regarding
the annexation, zoning and sketch plan for the property;
B.
The board shall hold a public hearing as required by C.R.S. § 31-12-109, as amended,
and, following such hearing, shall determine whether the area proposed for annexation
meets the criteria set out in the Annexation Act and whether the annexation petition
requires an election;
C.
The board shall approve the resolution as written, modify the resolution or deny the
resolution, or may continue the hearing to another date(s) in order to more fully
discuss the annexation petition and annexation application;
D.
If the board continues the public hearing, the application shall be placed on the
next regularly scheduled board agenda, if practicable, provided that the applicant
agrees to such placement;
E.
If the board approves or approves with conditions the resolution of findings of fact
and conclusions, and an election is not required, the town may prepare an annexation
ordinance for placement on the board's agenda at a time that is acceptable to the
board, in the board's sole discretion. The board may well decide to delay placement
of the annexation ordinance until such time as the board considers the zoning application
and subdivision application(s), up to and including the board's consideration of the
applicant's final plan and final plat for the property;
F.
If an election is required, the town must petition the District Court to hold the
election. The applicant for annexation shall provide public notice in the manner of
the resolution of findings of fact and conclusions and state statutes. If a majority
votes for annexation, the town may annex the area by ordinance upon the terms and
conditions, if any imposed. The applicant for annexation shall pay any and all fees
related to the annexation election;
G.
Upon consensus by the board that placement of the annexation ordinance on the board's
agenda is appropriate, the applicant for annexation shall publicly notice the time,
date and location of the first reading of the annexation ordinance per the guidelines
set forth in this title;
H.
At a minimum, an annexation ordinance requires two readings by the board prior to
approval, approval with conditions or denial; and
I.
The applicant for annexation need not notice the board meeting for the second reading
of the annexation ordinance, if the board continued the public hearing from the first
reading of the annexation ordinance.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
A.
If a plan, plat or other documents submitted by the applicant under the provisions
of these regulations, or submitted in conjunction with a petition to annex, are incomplete
or are modified so as to require additional town review, the applicant shall pay all
fees for the additional town review, upon further review of the submittal documents;
B.
If a plan, plat or other documents submitted by the applicant under the provisions
of these regulations, or submitted in conjunction with a petition to annex, are incomplete
or are modified so as to require additional town review, the town shall have up to
thirty days from the date of the last submittal item to the application which renders
the application complete;
C.
Neither the applicant's submission of an incomplete annexation petition and annexation
application, nor the applicant's abandonment or withdrawal of the annexation petition
and annexation application, nor the town's denial of such application shall relieve
the applicant from all those town fees and charges associated with the review of the
annexation petition and annexation application.

A.
Within thirty days of the board's approval of the annexation plat, with or without
conditions, the applicant shall submit the following to the town clerk for processing:
1.
Two fully executed Mylar copies of the annexation plat;
2.
Certified funds for any fees due to the town for recordation of the annexation plat

Quit claim deed or stock assignment for any water rights approved for dedication;
4.
Two fully executed copies of the annexation and development agreement, to be included

5.

Fees for recordation of all annexation documents; and

C.

D.
The applicant for annexation shall be responsible for all fees and charges incurred

and the actual costs of making corrections or additions to the master copy of the
official town maps or official town documents, as determined by the town, plus a fifteen

E.
If the applicant for annexation does not submit the recordation documents within thirty

may allow for a time extension for submittal of recordation documents, or conversely,
the board may disapprove the annexation.

A.
Construction impact fee. The town, through its consultants RPI Consulting, prepared
a Development Impact Analysis: Existing Conditions and 5-Year Outlook in 2007. This
analysis provided the basis for the fees set forth herein. For the privilege of annexing
property to the town and impacting the existing town facilities, staff and other resources,
each applicant for annexation shall be subject to a construction impact fee, unless
exempted herefrom by the board, pursuant to this section;
B.
"Development" defined. For purposes of this section, "development" means the act of
annexing land to the town for the purpose of constructing structures upon it or subdividing
it for the purpose of having structures placed upon such land;
C.
Amount of fee/escalation. The amount of the construction impact fee for land subject
to this section shall be based on findings by the board for the following units:
1.
Multi-family residential unit, up to a maximum of eight hundred square feet;
2.
Multi-family residential unit, up to a maximum of one thousand square feet;
3.
Multi-family residential unit, up to a maximum of one thousand two hundred square
feet;
4.
Single-family residential unit;
5.
Duplex unit (two family residential structure);
6.
One thousand square feet of commercial square footage, if such commercial use does
not proposed generated sales (or increase in sales tax from previous use), or jobs
from within the community, as determined by the board of trustees in its sole discretion,
following submittal by applicant of a fiscal impact analysis of such development;
D.
The fee set forth in this subsection shall be by a resolution adopted by the board;
E.
Partial fee exemption. Upon good grounds therefor, an applicant for annexation may
request that the board consider a partial waiver of construction impact fee by submitting
a written request to the town in the following manner:
1.
The applicant for annexation should present evidence in support of the request, including
but not limited to fiscal impact studies and/or professional opinions that compare
the town's revenue and expenses for such an annexation prior to and after annexation.
2.
An applicant for annexation shall pay all expenses incurred by the town and its consultants,
including but not limited to fees for professionals retained to review and analyze
such evidence;
3.
If the board analyzes the fiscal impact of certain development and determines that
the particular development will not increase the fiscal impact on town resources,
such development may not be subject to the entire construction impact fee.
4.
The board may waive a portion of the construction impact fee, or the board may approve
a schedule for payment of the construction impact fee as part of the annexation and
development agreement;
F.
Unless the board approves a different schedule as part of the ADA, an applicant for
annexation shall pay the entire construction impact fees prior to annexation plat
recordation;
G.
There shall be established a separate interest-bearing account in compliance with
C.R.S. 29-1-801 et seq. as amended, to account for the funds collected as such impact
fees and expended by the town for town resources impacted by development.

A.
An applicant for a pre-annexation agreement shall submit to the town an application
in a form acceptable to the town, and at a minimum, shall include the following:
1.
Statement regarding the reason(s) for request for pre-annexation agreement;
2.
Statement regarding the reason(s) that the property cannot be annexed;
3.
Statement as to the financial ramifications of the request, both for the town and
for the applicant; and
4.
Time period for the annexation to occur.
B.
The applicant for pre-annexation agreement shall publicly notice the time, date and
location of the public hearing per the guidelines set forth in this title;
C.
No public hearing may occur if the public notification has not occurred per the provisions
of this title;
D.
The commission shall consider the application for pre-annexation agreement at a regularly
scheduled meeting, and shall make a recommendation to the board for approval, approval
with conditions, or denial of the application;
E.
The commission meeting minutes shall be promptly forwarded to the board;
F.
The board shall consider the application for pre-annexation agreement, as well as
the commission's recommendation, at a regularly scheduled meeting, and shall, in its
sole discretion, approve, approve with conditions or deny the application;

If the board denies the pre-annexation application, the applicant may submit a new
application to the town in compliance with this section;
2.
If the board approves the application with conditions, the town may either require

a.
Applicant shall submit a revised application for future consideration by the board,
without submittal of an additional application fee, but the applicant shall be subject
to all costs incurred by the town for review of the application;
b.
Applicant shall submit a new application to the town in compliance with this section.
