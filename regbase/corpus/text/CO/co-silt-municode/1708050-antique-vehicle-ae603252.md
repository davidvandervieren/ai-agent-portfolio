"Antique vehicle" means a motor vehicle that is:
A.
At least twenty-five years old; and
B.
Specially registered and displays a special registration plate pursuant to Section
42-3-128, C.R.S. 1973.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02 (2))
