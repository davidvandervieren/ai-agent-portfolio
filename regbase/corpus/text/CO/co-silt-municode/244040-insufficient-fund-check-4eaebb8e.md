A.
For the purposes of this section, "alarm" means a signal from an alarm system requiring
the dispatch of a police officer, and "false alarm" means a signal from an alarm system
which is determined by the chief of police to have been made in error, as the result
of electrical or electronic malfunction, or deliberately made by the person in control
of said alarm system.
B.
Any person, firm, or corporation using a security alarm system shall pay to the town
a fee for response to a place of business or home by members of the town police department,
occasioned by a false alarm from the security system:
1.
First false alarm within a calendar year—a letter of warning;
2.
Second false alarm within a calendar year—a letter along with copy of ordinance codified
in this section;
3.
Third false alarm within a calendar year—fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution
annually, or more often as necessary;
4.
Fourth false alarm within a calendar year—fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution
annually, or more often as necessary, or the town may issue a citation in accordance
with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
C.
No false alarm shall be counted under the provisions of this section if the owner
or user of the alarm system has promptly notified the police department (via dispatch)
of the false alarm, or if the alarm is occasioned by any criminal activity, or if
the alarm occurs within thirty days after the installation of the system.

A.
The town shall charge each maker of an insufficient fund check a fee as set by the
board of trustees by resolution annually, or more often as necessary. The fee is due
to the town even in the event that the check is subsequently paid by the financial
institution or the maker. Maker of insufficient fund check shall submit the fee within
ten days of the date of the town's notification to the maker by regular mail. Nothing
herein relieves the maker from paying the original debt owed.
B.
If the assessment fee is not paid within the ten-day period contemplated in (A), the
maker shall submit to the town another fee as set forth by the board of trustees by
resolution annually, or more often as necessary, which is in addition to all other
applicable fees in this code.
C.
In addition to the fees contemplated in (A) and (B) above, the maker of such insufficient
fund check shall also pay to the town any fees or charges imposed by a bank on the
town as a result of the insufficient fund check. The town shall notify the maker of
such fees either in writing or at town hall.
D.
If the maker of the insufficient fund check does not pay his debt to the town, the
town may either issue a lien against the maker's property or issue a citation in accordance
with Chapter 1.12 of this code.

(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
A.
To secure a portion of the town's review expenses, upon an applicant's submittal of
an intergovernmental agreement (IGA) application, or an IGA amendment application,
the applicant shall submit to the town a fee and deposit as set by the board of trustees
by resolution annually, or more often as necessary, and shall be responsible for all
fees and charges incurred by the town in connection with such application, including,
but not limited to actual legal fees, planning fees, engineering fees, filing and
recording fees, and a 15 percent administrative fee based upon the total of all consultant
fees for the review of the application.
B.
No request for such IGA or amendment to an IGA shall be accepted unless the fee and
the deposit are paid in full.
C.
Application fee per this section includes six hours of review by the community development
department.
D.
Application for request of an IGA or an IGA amendment shall be on a form acceptable
to the town, and at a minimum, include the following:
1.
Statement detailing the purpose of the IGA;
2.
Statement regarding benefits both for the town and the applicant as a result of the
request;
3.
Statement regarding any negative effects both for the town and the applicant as a
result of the request;
4.
Statement regarding changes to other associated documents, necessary as a result of
the proposed change(s) to the IGA; and
5.
Statement as to the financial ramifications of the request, both for the town and
the applicant.
E.
The town, in its sole discretion, may elect to schedule a public hearing to consider
the request.
F.
The board, in its sole discretion, may approve, approve with conditions, or deny the
application;
1.
If application is denied, the applicant may submit a new application to the town in
compliance with this section;
2.
If the application is approved with conditions, the town may either require that applicant
submit a revised application for future consideration by the board without submittal
of a base fee, but subject to all costs incurred by the town for review of the application,
or alternatively, the town may require that the applicant submit a new application
to the town in compliance with this section.
