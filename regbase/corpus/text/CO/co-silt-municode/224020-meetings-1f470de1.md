The board of trustees shall constitute the board of health. The board of health may
elect any one of its members as chairman and the town clerk shall be ex officio clerk
of the board of health.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 1, 1936)
The board of health shall meet at such time and place as the chairman or any three
members designate by a verbal or written notice given each of the members at least
twenty-four hours in advance of each meeting, except in cases of emergencies.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 2, 1936)
Four members of the board of health shall constitute a quorum for the transaction
of any business which may properly come before it. Each board member shall be entitled
to cast one vote in any issue properly coming before the board of health.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 3, 1936)
The board of trustees hereby appoints the town administrator as the town's representative
in all matters pertaining to the administration of the board of health provisions.
The town, in its sole discretion, may seek assistance from another agency in the enforcement
of the provisions of this chapter.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 4, 1936)
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
The board of health shall exercise a general supervision over the health of the town,
with full power to:
A.
Take all necessary measures to promote the cleanliness and salubrity thereof;
B.
Abate nuisances of every description on public and private property;
C.
Prevent the introduction or spread within the town of malignant, contagious or infectious
diseases, by quarantine or other measures;
D.
Select and establish, when it may deem it necessary, such temporary or other hospital
as the condition of the public health or particular circumstances or emergencies may
in its judgment require; and
E.
Establish regulations for the government of such hospital and to remove thereto or
otherwise dispose of any person attacked by any such disease as aforesaid.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 5, 1936)
The town administrator, or his designee, in the exercise of due process of law, shall
have the power to enter into or upon any premises in the prosecution of this chapter,
at any necessary or appropriate hour, and cause the abatement of all nuisances therein
or thereon which is, in his judgment, deleterious to the public health, by serving
the owner, tenant, occupant or agent with a written or printed notice to abate the
same within such time as is prescribed in such notice. Upon the failure of any such
owner, tenant, occupant or agent to abate any nuisance after being so directed to
do, within the time so specified, such owner, tenant, occupant or agent is guilty
of a violation. The town may issue a citation for a violator of Chapter 2.24 in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. 18, Series of 1980 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VI § 6, 1936)
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
