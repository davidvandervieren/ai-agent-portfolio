Chapters:
For the purposes of this chapter, the following terms, phrases, words and their derivations
shall have the following meanings:
A.
"Brush" means a volunteer growth of bushes or shrubs growing out of place or planted
bushes or shrubs that have high or rank growth. "Brush" includes all trimmings, cuttings
or branches from grass, trees, bushes, shrubs or ornamental plants; and shall also
include all such plants that have become unsightly or obstacles to public passage
or safety.
B.
"Trash," "junk" and "rubbish" are used interchangeably and mean objectionable material
that includes, but is not limited to, every waste product that attends the preparation,
consumption, decay or dealing in food products; garbage; wood, leaves and dead vegetation;
used building materials; printed and paper matter; clothing; animal waste products;
ashes; refuse; household items; junk vehicles; vehicle parts or components; and any
other worthless or discarded material or object."Junk vehicle" means any vehicle that:
1. Is extensively damaged or unsightly; or 2. Is apparently inoperable. "Junk vehicle"
shall not include any vehicle that is a collector's item and has valid collector's
license plates.
C.
"Weeds" means any vegetation commonly referred to as a weed, or any unsightly, useless,
troublesome, or injurious herbaceous growing plant, or any vegetation that has been
designated a noxious weed by the town, including the most pervasive local weeds at
the publishing of the ordinance codified in this chapter, as follows:
1) Canada Thistle (Cirsium arvense);
2) Chicory (Chicorium intybus);
3) Common Burdock (Arctium minus);
4) Dalmatian Toadflax (Linaria genistifolia);
5) Diffuse Knapweed (Centaurea diffusa);
6) Hoary Cress (Cardaria draba);
7) Houndstongue (Cynoglossum officinale);
8) Jointed Goatgrass (Aegilops cylindrical);
9) Leafy spurge (Euphorbia esula);
10) Musk Thistle (Carduus nutans);
11) Oxeye Daisy (Chrysanthemum leucan-themum);
12) Plumeless Thistle (Carduus acanthoides);
13) Purple Loosestrife (Lythrum salicaria);
14) Russian Knapweed (Acroptilon repens);
15) Russian Olive (Elaeagnus angustifolia);
16) Salt Cedar (Tamarix ramosissima, Tamarix parviflora);
17) Scotch Thistle (Onopordum acanthium);
18) Spotted Knapweed (Dentaurea maculosa);
19) Yellow Starthistle (Centaurea solstitialis);
20) Yellow Toadflax (Linaria vulgaris).
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
All weeds, brush, trash, junk and rubbish not kept within a completely enclosed building
are declared to be a public nuisance. No person or parties shall create or continue
such nuisances or suffer such nuisances. Any person found to have violated this section
may be punished per chapters 1.12 and 8.04 of this code.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
All owners, tenants, occupants, developers and users of every lot and tract of land
within the town shall keep and maintain such lot and tract of land, together with
the alleys and/or rights-of-way behind and the sidewalk and/or right-of-way areas
in front and to the sides of such property considered right-of-way, free and clear
at all times of any and all weeds, brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds. All
noxious weeds shall be managed in a manner to prevent seed formation.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
If any such owner, tenant, occupant or user contemplated in Section 8.04.030 of this chapter fails to keep any of such property free and clear of such weeds,
brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds, then the town may give written notice
to any such owner, tenant, occupant or user to cause to be removed any such weeds,
brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds. Such notice shall be in writing and may
be either personally delivered to any such owner, tenant, occupant or user, or may
be delivered by posting the same, postage prepaid, with the United States mail, addressed
to any such owner, tenant, occupant or user at his/her last known mailing address.
Such notice shall be deemed delivered when so personally delivered or when deposited
in the United States mail.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
Such written notice issued by the town as is provided for in Section 8.04.040 of this chapter to the owner, occupant, tenant or user of any such lot or tract of
land shall provide that the removal of any and all such weeds, brush, trash, junk
and rubbish of all kinds shall occur by a date certain, to be specified in the notice,
which date shall be at least ten days from the date of delivery of such written notice.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
Such written notice as is provided for in this chapter shall be substantially the
following form:
You are hereby notified that you are in violation of Chapter 8.04 of the Silt Municipal Code by your failure to keep the following identified property,
to wit:
Free and clear of all weeds, brush, trash, junk, and rubbish of all kinds, and from
the alley and/or right-of-way behind and from the sidewalk and/or right-of-way areas
in the front and to sides of such property. You are hereby required to cause any and
all such weeds, brush, trash, junk, and rubbish of all kinds to be removed from such
lot and/or tract or land and the alley behind and the sidewalk areas in front of such
property no later than ___________, 20__, such removal to be accomplished at your
sole expense, without contribution from or claims against the Town of Silt.
If you fail to remove any and all such weeds, brush, trash, junk, and rubbish of all
kinds, then the Town of Silt may take either or both of the following actions:
1.
Issue you a citation, causing you to appear in municipal court where you may be fined
in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of the Silt Municipal Code;
2.
Cause the removal to be done by any means at the town's disposal and shall the whole
cost thereof, including 15% for inspection and other incidental costs in connection
therewith, upon such lot and/or tract of land. This assessment, when levied, shall
be and constitutes a lien against such lot or tract of land until paid by your and
shall have priority over all other liens except general taxes and prior special assessments.
In case you do not pay such assessment within fifteen days of the date of this notice,
the Town of Silt has the power and authority to certify with the Garfield County Treasurer
such assessment, with directions to the Garfield County Treasurer to collect the assessment,
together with a ten percent penalty for costs of collection, in the same manner as
other taxes are collected. The laws of the state of Colorado for assessment and collection
of general taxes, including the laws for the sale and redemption of property for taxes,
shall apply to the collection of such assessment.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 5, 11-28-2016)
Any manner of removal of all such weeds, brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds
is permissible so long as the same does not create a hazard, or health or safety problem,
to persons or property. The owner, tenant, occupant or user of any such lot and tract
of land shall employ a manner of removal which does not endanger life or property
or create a health or safety problem as a result of dust, fire or otherwise.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
If the person to whom the notice is given to remove such weeds, brush, trash, junk
or rubbish, fails to accomplish such removal by the deadline specified in the notice,
then the town may cause the same to be done by any means at its disposal; and the
town and those acting in its behalf or at its direction shall not be liable to anyone
for trespass, conversion or for any other civil or criminal liability, penalty or
claim when acting in good faith in accordance with the provisions of this section.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
In the event it becomes necessary for the town to itself cause to be removed all such
weeds, brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds, then the town shall assess the
whole costs thereof, including fifteen percent for inspection and other administrative
costs in connection therewith, upon the lots and tracts of land from which the weeds,
brush, trash, junk and rubbish of all kinds are removed. This assessment, when levied,
shall be a lien against each lot or tract of land until paid and shall have priority
over all other liens except general taxes and prior special assessments.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
The assessment contemplated in Section 8.04.090 of this chapter shall be levied by the town delivering, in the same manner provided
for the notice in Section 8.04.040 of this chapter to any owner, tenant, occupant or user, a notice of assessment and
lien, which shall describe the property against which the assessment has been levied,
the amount of the assessment, the fact that the assessment constitutes a lien against
the described property, the name of the owner, tenant, occupant or user involved,
and the date by which the assessment must be paid, which shall be within a period
of fifteen days, which is deemed to be a reasonable time, in order to avoid certification
of the assessment and lien to the county treasurer for collection proceedings.
(Ord. 14-01 § 1 (part))
In case the assessment contemplated in Section 8.04.090 of this chapter is not paid within the fifteen-day period provided for in Section 8.04.100 of this chapter, the town clerk shall certify such assessment to the county treasurer,
who shall proceed to collect the same, together with a fifteen percent penalty for
cost of collection, in the same manner as other taxes are collected. The laws of the
state for assessment and collection of general taxes, including the laws for the sale
and redemption of property for taxes, shall apply to the collection of such assessments.

(Ord. 6-06, § 1 (part))
Open burning shall mean any burning or fire that is not contained in an approved structure
specifically designed for safely permitting the burning of combustible materials.
An approved structure may be located outside of a dwelling if said structure is permanent
in nature (mounted or affixed to a foundation or concrete slab) or is a mobile fire
container that is raised above the ground and situated on a permanent, non-flammable
surface (such as a concrete or asphalt slab) and that also has a spark containing
cover.
