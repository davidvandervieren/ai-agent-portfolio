In pursuance of the authority conferred by section 29-2-101 et seq., C.R.S. 1973,
as amended, this chapter is enacted for the purpose of providing additional revenues
to the town, to assist in the acquisition, furnishing and improvement of needed services
and facilities within the town.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 1)
For the purpose of this chapter, the following words, terms and phrases are defined
as follows:
A.
"Motor and other vehicles on which registration is required" means any motor vehicle,
trailer, semitrailer vehicle, trailer coach, mobile home, motorcycle, motor-powered
(in part or in whole) vehicle, or other vehicle which is primarily designed to be
operated or drawn upon any highway. This definition does not apply to any such vehicles
owned and operated by any department of the federal government or state government
or any other agency or political subdivision thereof, firefighting vehicles, city
vehicles, farm tractors, farm trailers or other heavy, movable farm equipment primarily
used on farms and not on highways, and road machinery temporarily operated or located
upon a highway.
B.
"Person" means an individual, a corporation, a partnership, a joint venture, a firm,
an association, an estate, a trust, a receiver or any other group comprising more
than one person which is acting as a unit.
C.
"Retail sale" means all sales except wholesale sales.
D.
"Storage" or "storing" mean any keeping or retention of, or exercise of dominion or
control over, tangible personal property in the town.
E.
"Wholesale sale" means a sale by a wholesaler to a retail merchant, jobber, dealer
or other wholesaler for resale, and does not include a sale by a wholesaler to a user
or consumer not for resale, the latter-described sale being deemed a "retail sale."
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 2)
A municipal use tax is imposed upon the privilege of:
A.
Using or consuming in the town any construction and building materials purchased at
retail; and
B.
Storing, using or consuming in the town any motor and other vehicles on which registration
is required which are purchased at retail.
(Ord. 23, Series of 1993 § 2)
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of any tangible personal property the sale of which is subject to a retail sales tax
imposed by the town.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 4(1))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of any tangible personal property purchased for resale in the town, either in its
original form or as an ingredient of a manufactured or compounded product, in the
regular course of a business.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 4(2))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of tangible personal property brought into the town by a nonresident thereof for his
own storage, use or consumption while temporarily within the town; provided, that
this exemption shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption of tangible personal
property brought into the state of Colorado by a nonresident to be used in the conduct
of a business in the state of Colorado.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 4(3))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of tangible personal property by the United States Government or by the state of Colorado,
or by its institutions or political subdivisions in their governmental capacities
only, or by religious or charitable corporations in the conduct of their regular religious
or charitable functions.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 4 (4))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of tangible personal property by a person engaged in the business of manufacturing
or compounding for sale, profit or use any article, substance or commodity, which
tangible personal property enters into the processing of or becomes an ingredient
or component part of the product or service which is manufactured, compounded or furnished,
and the container, label or the furnished shipping case thereof.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 4(5))
A.
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of any article of tangible personal property the sale or use of which has already
been subject to a legally imposed sales or use tax of another statutory or home rule
town, city or city and county in an amount equal to, or in excess of, that imposed
by this chapter.
B.
A credit shall be granted against the use tax imposed by this chapter with respect
to a person's storage, use or consumption in the town of tangible personal property
purchased by such person in a previous statutory or home rule town, city or city and
county. The amount of the credit shall be equal to the tax paid by such person by
reason of the imposition of a sales or use tax of the previous statutory or home rule
town, city or city and county on such person's purchase or use of the property, although
the amount of the credit shall in no event exceed the amount of tax imposed by this
chapter.
C.
Upon request and following the town's building permit issuance for a specific project,
the town will provide a building permit applicant with the project-specific sales
tax exempt certificate, for use the duration of the building permit, for those materials
associated with the project. The town intends for the certificate to be used for the
applicant's project-specific goods purchases outside of town limits, and specifically
exempts the applicant from being charged another local jurisdiction's sales or use
taxes with respect to such purchases. The town does not warrant that the building
permit applicant is exempt from paying county and state use taxes. Building permit
applicants shall surrender the sale tax exempt certificate at the conclusion of the
project, or shall pay a fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution annually,
or more often as necessary.
(Ord. 38-00; Ord. 23-93 § 3)
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 2, 11-28-2016)
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of tangible personal property and household effects acquired outside the town and
brought into the town by a nonresident who may thereafter acquire residency in the
town.
(Ord. 13-79 § 4(7))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage or use of a motor
vehicle if the owner is or was, at the time of purchase, a nonresident of the town
and such owner purchased the vehicle outside of the town for use outside the town,
and actually so used it for a substantial and primary purpose for which it was acquired,
and such owner registered, titled and licensed the motor vehicle outside of the town.
(Ord. 13-79 § 4(8))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of any construction and building materials and motor and other vehicles on which registration
is required if a written contract for the purchase thereof was entered into prior
to the effective date of the ordinance codified at this chapter, which ordinance was
adopted September 10, 1979.
(Ord. 13-79 § 4(9))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage, use or consumption
of any construction and building materials required or made necessary in the performance
of any construction contract which was bid, let or entered into at any time prior
to the effective date of the ordinance codified in this chapter, which ordinance was
adopted September 10, 1979.
(Ord. 13-79 § 4(10))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall not apply to the storage of construction
and building materials in the town.
(Ord. 23-93 § 4)
A.
The collection of the use tax for construction and building materials imposed by this
chapter shall be administered by the town treasurer and shall be collected at the
time permits are issued for building and construction. The payment of the tax shall
be the responsibility of the person applying for the building permit.
B.
The rate of the use tax shall be three percent of the "sale value" of the construction
and building materials. Fifty percent of the valuation of the construction project
as stated on the building permit shall be deemed to be the "sale value" of the construction
and building materials used or consumed as a part thereof.
(Ord. 23-93 § 5)
As an alternative to the payment procedure set forth in Section 3.16.140 , the taxpayer may make arrangements with the treasurer to pay the tax monthly or
on a mutually agreeable basis, to be reported on returns to be filed with the town
treasurer. Under this alternative, a final project-cost report must be filed and approved
by the town treasurer before final building inspection is allowed and a certificate
of occupancy is issued. Unless the taxpayer pays the tax as set forth in Section 3.16.140 , failure to file the return required in this section is a violation of this chapter.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 5(2))
A.
Should any of the construction and building materials contemplated in Sections 3.16.140 and 3.16.150 be subject to any of the exemptions set forth in Sections 3.16.040 through 3.16.130 , the applicant for the building permit shall be entitled to a credit against the
use tax which otherwise would be due and payable or to a rebate of any use tax paid,
such credit or rebate to be in an amount not to exceed the use tax which otherwise
would be due and payable or which may have been already paid on the construction and
building materials.
B.
In order to be entitled to such a credit or rebate, the applicant for a building permit
shall maintain and preserve, and present to the town treasurer at the time of his
application for a building permit or thereafter, detailed purchase and receipt records,
which shall be subject to inspection and audit by the town treasurer or his authorized
agents and representatives. The town treasurer may require the applicant to submit
other proof of qualification for any exemption to which the applicant claims he is
entitled, such as an exemption certificate evidencing tax-exempt status.
(Ord. 13, Series, of 1979 § 5(3))
The use tax imposed on motor and other vehicles on which registration is required
and which are purchased at retail, shall be collected in amount equal to three percent
of the purchase price of such motor vehicle. As set forth in Section 39-26-208, C.R.S.
1973, as amended, no registration shall be made of any such motor vehicle and no certificate
of title shall be issued therefor by the Department of Revenue or any of its authorized
agents until the tax which is due upon the use, storage or consumption thereof pursuant
to this chapter is paid in full.
(Ord. 23, Series of 1993 § 6 (part))
A.
The use tax imposed by this chapter on such motor vehicles as are contemplated in
Section 3.16.170 shall be collected by the authorized agent of the Department of Revenue who is located
in Garfield County, Colorado pursuant to an agreement or agreements which shall be
entered into between the town and such authorized agent. The proceeds of the use tax
due to the town shall be paid to the town periodically in accordance with such agreement
or agreements. The town administrator is authorized to enter into and to execute on
behalf of the town any such agreement or agreements necessary or advisable for the
collection, administration and enforcement of this section and Section 3.16.170 , any such agreement or agreements to be in accordance with the provisions of Colorado
law including, specifically, Section 24-35-110, C.R.S. 1973, as amended.
B.
To facilitate collection of the use tax imposed on motor vehicles by this chapter,
the board of trustees shall certify to the Department of Revenue and to the Garfield
County clerk and recorder a true copy of the currently applicable tax ordinances of
the town, and shall likewise certify any subsequent changes therein.
(Ord. 23, Series of 1993 § 6 (part))
The use tax imposed by this chapter shall constitute a first and prior lien on the
tangible personal property subject to such use tax, subject only to prior liens as
referenced in Section 3.16.200 .
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 7 (part))
A.
If the use tax imposed by this chapter is not paid at the time it is due, the town
treasurer shall issue a notice to the person owing such tax, which notice shall set
forth the name of the delinquent taxpayer, the amount of the tax, a demand for payment,
the date of the accrual thereof, the date by which such tax and all collection costs
must be paid in order for the delinquent taxpayer to avoid further collection proceedings,
and that the town claims a first and prior lien therefor on the tangible personal
property of the taxpayer which is subject to such tax, except as to the adjudged priority
of any preexisting liens of a bona fide mortgagee, pledgee or judgment creditor whose
right may have attached prior to the filing, as provided in subsection B, of such
notice.
B.
Such notice shall be on forms prepared by the treasurer and, when a duplicate of the
notice is filed in the office of the clerk and recorder of any county in the state
of Colorado in which such taxpayer owns any real or personal property, shall create
a lien as aforesaid on such property in such county and shall constitute notice thereof
to the world.
C.
Such lien may be foreclosed against any such real or personal property using the same
method and procedures at the time provided by Colorado statutes for execution proceedings
against real or personal property to satisfy judgments.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 7 (part))
The town shall also, in addition to the remedy described in Section 3.16.200 , be entitled to avail itself of any and all other legally available remedies, including
all forms of legal or equitable relief, in order to cause the tax to be paid. In addition
to the amount of the tax, the town, in effecting any foreclosure or collection procedures
or remedies, shall also be entitled to recover interest on the delinquent amount at
the rate of 18 percent annually, plus all of its collection costs and attorney fees.
The town may issue a citation to a violation of Chapter 3.16 (use tax) in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 7 (part))
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 2, 11-28-2016)
The town treasurer, under the supervision and with the approval of the town administrator,
may adopt and promulgate reasonable procedural rules and regulations and may prepare,
adopt and use return, report, notice and other forms in order to carry out the provisions
of this chapter, such rules, regulations and forms to be at all times consistent with
Section 29-2-101 et seq., C.R.S. 1973, as amended, and to be consistent with and in
furtherance of all of the provisions of this chapter.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1979 § 9)
