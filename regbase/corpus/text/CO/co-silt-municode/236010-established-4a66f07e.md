There is created a police department for the Town of Silt, which shall consist of
one chief of police and as many policemen as may from time to time be deemed necessary
for the safety and good order of the town.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1986 (part))
The police chief of the police department shall have powers and duties as assigned
and shall perform all duties required of town marshals.
(Ord. 7-86 (part))
There shall be designated an account out of the town's general fund, known as the
"police department equipment" account. This designated account will receive proceeds
from a surcharge to be assessed as described in Section 2.08.232 of this code. This
police department account, and all money comprising the account, shall be used for
the purpose of police department equipment purchase.
(Ord. 24-92 § 2)
The board of trustees shall set vehicle identification Number (VIN) inspection fees
for residents and non-residents by resolution annually, or more often as necessary.
(Ord. 6-95 (part))
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
The board of trustees shall set fingerprinting fees for residents and non-residents
by resolution annually, or more often as necessary.
(Ord. 6-95 (part))
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
