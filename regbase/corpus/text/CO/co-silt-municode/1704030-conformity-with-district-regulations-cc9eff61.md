The ordinance codified at this title shall be known and may be cited as the "Zoning
Ordinance of the Town of Silt, Colorado."
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 2.01)
The provisions of this title shall apply within the town limits of the town as established
on the map entitled "Town of Silt Town Boundary and Zoning Map," as the same may be
amended after the effective date of the ordinance codified at this chapter, which
ordinance was adopted July 28, 1980.
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 5.01)
No building or land shall, after the effective date of the ordinance codified at this
chapter, which ordinance was adopted July 28, 1980, be used or occupied and no building
or structure or part thereof shall be erected, moved or structurally altered, except
in conformity with the regulations of this title or amendments thereto, for the district
in which it is located.
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 5.01)
No building shall hereafter be erected or altered so as to exceed the density regulations
of this title for the district in which it is located.
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 5.02)
It is the intent of district regulations in the chapters of this Title 17 of the Silt Municipal Code and the policy of this title that if any use or class
of use is not specifically allowed as a permitted use or prohibited use in a district
as set forth in these chapters, it is a special use in that district.
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 7.01)
In interpreting and applying the provisions of this title, each landowner shall be
held to the minimum requirements for the promotion of the health, safety, and general
welfare of the town. The regulations of this title are not intended to interfere with,
abrogate or annul other rules, regulations or ordinances of the town. However, if
the requirements of this title are at variance with the requirements of any other
lawfully adopted rules, covenants or agreements between parties, the more restrictive
or that imposing the higher standards shall govern.
(Ord. 5-05 (part): Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 14.01.00)
