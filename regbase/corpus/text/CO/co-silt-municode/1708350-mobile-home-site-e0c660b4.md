"Mobile home site" means a plot of ground within a mobile home park designated for
the accommodation and use of one (1) mobile home and containing all improvements and
utility connections required under this title and other applicable town codes.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(20))
