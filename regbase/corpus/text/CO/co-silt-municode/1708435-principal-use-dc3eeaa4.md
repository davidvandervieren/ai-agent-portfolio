"Principal use" means the main or primary purpose for which a structure or land is
designed, arranged or intended, or for which it may be occupied or maintained as provided
in the code. Each lot has one principal use as a permitted use.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(40))
