Any and all unpaid charges, assessments or taxes of every type and kind made or levied
by the town shall be delinquent thirty days after such charges, assessments or taxes
are due. When a different time for delinquency is specified for any given charge,
assessment or tax elsewhere in this code then that time shall prevail, however, all
other provisions of this chapter shall apply.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1985 (part), 1985)
Delinquent charges, assessments or taxes as defined in Section 3.24.010 of this chapter may be certified by the town clerk to the treasurer of the county
and be collected and paid over by the treasurer of the county in the same manner as
taxes, together with all such interest and penalties as authorized by Colorado Revised
Statutes and/or this code.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1985 (part), 1985)
