There is hereby established within the town a sales tax incentive program ("STIP").
The purpose of the sales tax incentive program is to encourage establishment and/or
substantial expansion of retail-sales-tax-generating businesses within the town, thereby
stimulating the economy of and within the town, providing employment for the residents
of the town and others, expanding the goods available for purchase and consumption
by residents of the town, and increasing the sales taxes collected by the town. These
increased sales tax collections will enable the town to provide expanded and improved
municipal services while at the same time providing public or public-related improvements
at no cost, or at deferred cost, to the town and its taxpayers and residents.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this chapter, shall have the
meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates
a different meaning.
A.
"Applicant" means the owner(s) of real property upon which a qualifying business or
coordinated group of adjacent qualifying businesses are operated or proposed or the
operator(s) of any qualifying business within the town.
B.
"Base amount" means that amount of sales taxes generated by the qualifying business
that is not subject to sharing by the town with the applicant, which amount shall
be determined by the board and set forth in the STIP Agreement. In the case of an
expanded or replacement business on property previously used for retail purposes,
the base amount shall never be lower than the amount of sales taxes collected by the
town at the property in question in the twelve months prior to the date the STIP Agreement
is executed plus a reasonable and agreed-upon percentage of the anticipated increase
in sales taxes (without expansion or participation in the STIP). In the case of a
newly established business, the base amount shall be determined by the town following
its consideration of the amount of sales taxes that could be generated from the new
business, or is being generated by another existing business in the town offering
the same or similar retail goods, without the participation by the applicant in the
STIP. The base amount may be represented as a percentage of sales taxes to be generated
by the qualifying business or a specified dollar amount.
C.
"Enhanced sales tax" means the amount of sales tax collected by the town and available
to the town for sharing with the applicant through the STIP Agreement over and above
the base amount.
D.
"Maximum net reimbursement amount" means the maximum amount set forth in the STIP
Agreement to be shared by the town with the applicant.
E.
"Operator" means the owner of any qualifying business.
F.
"Owner" means the record owner of the real property upon which one or more qualifying
businesses is operated or proposed.
G.
"Qualifying business" means a retail-sales-tax-generating business or coordinated
groups of adjacent retail sales tax generating businesses within the town that, as
proposed, newly existing or expanded following the effective date of this chapter,
will reasonably likely generate enhanced sales taxes of at least twelve thousand dollars
in the first year of operation following its opening or expansion.
H.
"STIP" means the sales tax incentive program created by this chapter.
I.
"STIP Agreement" means the signed agreement between the applicant and the town, which
implements the STIP for a specific qualifying business or property upon which a specific
qualifying business is operated or proposed.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
Participation in the STIP shall be based upon approval by the board exercising its
sole and exclusive discretion. Any owner or operator or coordinated group of owners
or operators may apply to the town for inclusion within the STIP.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
Approval by the board of a STIP Agreement entitles the applicant to share in enhanced
sales taxes derived from the applicant's property or business in an amount which shall
not in any event exceed fifty percent of the enhanced sales taxes, unless the town
determines another allocation to be in its best interest for businesses that are expected
to generate in excess of twenty-five thousand dollars per year in sales taxes. The
applicant may use such amount solely for public and/or public-related purposes such
as those specified herein and which are expressly approved by the board as a part
of the STIP Agreement. The time period in which the enhanced sales taxes may be shared
shall not commence until all public or public-related improvements contemplated by
the Agreement are completed, and shall be limited both to a specified time and to
a specified maximum reimbursement amount.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
A.
The uses eligible for the shared enhanced sales taxes shall be subject to approval
by the board through its approval of the STIP Agreement, and shall be strictly limited
to those that are public or public-related in nature. For the purposes of this chapter,
but not by way of limitation, public or public-related purposes means: (i) environmental
remediation improvements such as streets, sidewalks, curbs, gutters, pedestrian malls,
street lights, public drainage structures, demolition and site restoration for redevelopment,
landscaping, decorative structures, enhanced architectural features, public transportation
improvements, road and street improvements including traffic control signals, statuaries,
fountains, identification signs, traffic safety devices, bicycle paths, off-street
parking facilities, benches, public restrooms, information booths, public meeting
facilities, and necessary and appurtenant structures and improvements; (ii) installation,
relocation, extension or improvement of utility lines and facilities; and (iii) any
other improvements of a similar nature which are specifically approved by the board
upon a finding that said improvements are public or public-related improvements. Public
or public-related purposes shall not mean real property purchase costs, private service
utility line costs, costs of private drainage improvements and private building construction
costs (except for those building construction costs specifically attributable to enhancements
enumerated above that benefit the public as a whole). In the event the applicant is
reimbursed for the cost of such improvements from other public funds, cost recovery
mechanisms and/or grants, the amount eligible under the STIP program shall be decreased
by the amount of such reimbursement.
B.
In addition to the limitation set forth above, the STIP Agreement shall ensure that,
in accordance with Section 3.12.040 hereof, no less than one-third of all sales tax revenues collected from a qualifying
business shall be restricted to use for the repair, maintenance, installation and
improvement of streets, alleys, drainage, curbs and gutters within the town. Reimbursement
to an applicant for the cost of such improvements shall be permitted through the STIP
Agreement.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
Enhanced sales taxes shall be shared by the town with the applicant on a quarterly
basis pursuant to the STIP Agreement. If in any quarter there is no enhanced sales
tax, no funds shall be shared with the applicant for that quarter, and no increment
shall be shared until that deficit below the Base Amount, and any other cumulative
deficit, has been met, so that at the end of any twelve-month cycle, funds in excess
of those Enhanced Sales Taxes agreed to be shared shall not have been shared by any
applicant.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
It is an overriding consideration and determination of the town that existing sources
of town sales tax revenues shall not be used, impaired or otherwise affected by the
STIP. Therefore, it is hereby conclusively determined that only enhanced sales taxes
generated by the properties described in an application shall be subject to division
under this STIP. The town shall collect and hold all such enhanced sales taxes in
a manner that promotes an accounting system which accomplishes the overriding purpose
of this chapter.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
A.
Participation in the STIP shall be based upon approval by the town board exercising
its sole and exclusive discretion. The town board may permit such participation based
in part upon the following criteria, as a general guide.
1.
The amount of enhanced sales taxes that are reasonably to be anticipated to be derived
by the town by the qualifying business;
2.
The public benefits which are provided by the applicant through public works, public
improvements, additional employment for town residents, and the provision of additional
quality retail services, including the potential for location within the town of other
retail establishments as a result of the operation of the qualifying business;
3.
The amount of estimated expenditures which may be deferred by the town based upon
public improvements to be completed by the applicant;
4.
The conformance of the applicant's property or project with the comprehensive plan
and zoning ordinances of the town; and
5.
The agreement required by Section 3.14.018 having been reached, which agreement shall contain and conform to all requirements
of Section 3.14.018 .
B.
Approval shall be by motion adopted by a majority of the entire board. In the event
the agreement contemplated by Section 3.14.018 requires a multi-year fiscal commitment by the town, such agreement shall be subject
to voter approval pursuant to Article X, Section 20 of the Colorado Constitution.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
Each application for participation in the STIP shall be subject to approval by the
board solely on its own merits and in the sole and exclusive discretion of the board.
Approval of an application shall require that a STIP Agreement be executed by the
applicant and the town, which STIP Agreement shall at a minimum contain:
A.
A general description of the qualifying business to which the agreement applies, including
the approximate square footage of such business and the type(s) of retail goods offered
for sale. In the town's discretion, the agreement may also provide that sales tax
revenues from specific business categories shall be excluded from participation in
the STIP.
B.
A list of those public or public-related improvements that justify the applicant's
approval, the estimated amount that shall be spent on such improvements, and the amount
spent for such improvements that shall be subject to reimbursement through enhanced
sales taxes.
C.
The maximum net reimbursement amount, and the maximum time during which the STIP Agreement
shall continue, it being expressly understood that any such agreement shall expire
and be of no further force and effect upon the occurrence of the earlier to be reached
of the maximum time of the agreement (whether or not the maximum amount to be shared
has been reached) or the maximum net reimbursement amount to be shared (whether or
not the maximum time set forth has expired).
D.
A statement that the agreement is personal to the applicant, is not transferable without
the consent of the board, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, and does
not run with the land.
E.
That the STIP Agreement shall never constitute a multi-year fiscal obligation of the
town within any constitutional or statutory provision, or, in the event such agreement
does constitute a multi-year fiscal obligation of the town, that such agreement shall
be subject to voter approval.
F.
The base amount of sales taxes agreed upon by quarter, and a mechanism to ensure that
any reimbursement contemplated by the STIP Agreement complies with Section 3.14.015 above.
G.
That any enhanced sales taxes subject to sharing shall be escrowed in the event there
is a legal challenge either to this STIP or to the approval of any STIP Agreement.
H.
A statement that the obligations, benefits and/or provisions of the agreement may
not be assigned in whole or in any part without the express authorization of the board,
which authorization shall not be unreasonably withheld, and further that no third
party shall be entitled to rely upon or enforce any provision hereof.
I.
That the agreement shall provide that the successful applicant shall have no right,
claim, lien or priority in or to the town's sales tax revenue superior to or on parity
with the rights, claims or liens of the holders as any sales tax revenue bonds, notes,
certificates or debentures payable from or secured by the town outstanding as of the
effective date of the agreement provided for by this section; and that all rights
of the successful applicant are, and at all times shall be, subordinate and inferior
to the rights, claims and liens of the holders of any and all such sales tax revenue
bonds, notes, certificates or debentures, payable from or secured by any sales taxes
issued by the town.
J.
Any other provisions agreed upon by the parties and approved by the board.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
The board has enacted this STIP as a joint benefit to the public at large and to private
owners for the purposes of providing the town with increased sales tax revenues generated
upon and by properties improved as a result of this program. The board further finds
that the public benefits of this STIP include public improvements being completed
by private owners through no debt obligation being incurred on the part of the town,
and allowing the applicant an opportunity to improve properties which generate sales
activities, which improvements make those properties more competitive in the marketplace
and further provide to the applicant additional contingent sources of revenues for
upgrading such properties. The board specifically finds and determines that creation
of this STIP is consistent with the town's powers as a municipality and that exercise
of such powers in the manner set forth herein is in furtherance of the public health,
safety and welfare. Notwithstanding any provision hereof, the town shall never be
a joint venturer in any private entity or activity which participates in this STIP,
and the town shall never be liable or responsible for any debt or obligation of any
participant in this STIP.
(Ord. 2-04 § 1 (part))
