"Front yard setback" means an area extending the full width of the lot or parcel,
the depth of which is measured by the least horizontal distance between the front
lot line and that area defined as the building site or envelope, whereby no structure
may be built. A lot adjoining two streets has two (2) front yards as defined by this
title. Any yard adjoining a street shall be considered a front yard for purposes of
this chapter.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(43))
