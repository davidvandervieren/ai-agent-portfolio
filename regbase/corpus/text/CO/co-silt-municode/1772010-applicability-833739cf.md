The regulations set out in this chapter shall apply to all gasoline service or filling
stations.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05 (part))
All buildings at a gasoline service or filling station shall be located at least forty
feet from any street right-of-way line.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05(1))
Gasoline pumps and other appliances at a gasoline service or filling station shall
be located at least fifteen feet from any street right-of-way line.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05(2))
All service, storage or similar activities at a gasoline service or filling station
shall be conducted entirely on the premises.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05(3))
All major repair work, if any, at a gasoline service or filling station shall be conducted
within a completely enclosed building.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05(4))
Open storage of wrecked or inoperable cars, discarded tires, auto parts or similar
materials shall not be permitted at a gasoline service or filling station.
(Ord. 13, Series of 1980 § 8.05(5))
