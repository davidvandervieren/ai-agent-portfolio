"Development" means any of the following activities pursuant to this Land Use and
Development Code: (a) the subdivision of a parcel of land into two or more parcels,
divisions or ownerships; (b) the construction, reconstruction, conversion, expansion
or structural alteration, relocation or enlargement of any buildings, structures or
accessory structure; (c) any use, change in use or change in intensity of use of any
buildings, land or water; or (d) any clearing, grading, filling excavating or other
movement of land.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
