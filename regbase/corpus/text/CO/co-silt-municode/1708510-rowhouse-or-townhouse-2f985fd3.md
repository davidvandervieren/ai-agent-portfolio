"Rowhouse" or "townhouse" means a dwelling unit having at least one (1) common side
building wall with an adjacent similar dwelling unit and located upon land owned by
the owner of the dwelling unit, and for which a subdivision is attainable along the
common building wall in order to create separately conveyable units and the appurtenant
property above and below such unit, upon application to the town.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(23))
