The purpose of this chapter is to impose a sales tax on the sale of tangible personal
property at retail or the furnishing of services in the town.
(Ord. 103 § I, 1976: Ord. 48 § 1, 1969: Ord. 43 § 1, 1968)
For the purpose of this chapter and Chapter 5.08 , the definitions of words contained in this chapter and Chapter 5.08 shall be as defined in section 2 of article 5 of chapter 138, C.R.S. 1973, as amended,
and those definitions are incorporated into this chapter and Chapter 5.08 by this reference.
(Ord. 43 § 2, 1968)
There is imposed on the sale of tangible personal property at retail, or the furnishing
of services, a tax equal to three percent of the gross receipts of such sale or services.
The imposition of tax on individual sales shall be in accordance with schedules as
set forth in the rules and regulations promulgated by the state of Colorado Department
of Revenue, or by separate ordinance of the town.
(Ord. 1, Series of 1980 § 1: Ord. 103 § VIII, 1976: Ord. 94 § 1, 1975: Ord. 48 § VIII,
1969: Ord. 45, 1969: Ord. 43 § 5(51), 1968)
One-third of all sales tax revenues collected on and after January 1, 1981 shall be
restricted to use for the repair, maintenance, installation and improve-ment of streets,
alleys, drainage, curbs and gutters within the town, and for no other purpose or purposes.
(Ord. 1, Series of 1980 § 6)
The collection, administration and enforcement of this sales tax shall be performed
by the Director of Revenue of the state of Colorado in the same manner as the collection,
administration and enforcement of the state sales tax. The provisions of article 5
of chapter 138, C.R.S. 1973, as amended, and all rules and regulations promulgated
by the Director of Revenue, shall govern the collection, administration and enforcement
of the sales tax imposed by this chapter.
(Ord. 43 § 5 (5-2), 1968)
For the purpose of collection, administration and enforcement of this chapter by the
Director of Revenue for the state of Colorado, the provisions of section 138-5-14,
C.R.S. 1973, as amended, shall govern the collection, administration and enforcement
of the sales tax authorized under the ordinance codified in this chapter and Chapter 5.08 .
(Ord. 43 § 4(4-1), 1968)
The amount subject to tax under this chapter shall not include the state sales and
use tax imposed by article 5 of chapter 138, C.R.S. 1973, as amended.
(Ord. 43 § 4(4-2), 1968)
The gross receipts from sales shall include delivery charges when such charges are
subject to the state sales and use tax imposed by article 26 of title 39, C.R.S. 1973,
as amended, regardless of the place to which delivery is made.
(Ord. 1, Series of 1980 § 2: Ord. 43 § 4(4-4), 1968)
A.
State Taxable-items Provisions Applicable. Tangible personal property and services
taxable pursuant to this chapter shall be the same as the tangible personal property
and services taxable pursuant to section 39-26-104, C.R.S. 1973, and subject to the
same exemptions as those specified in section 39-26-114, C.R.S. 1973, except for the
exemption allowed by section 39-26-114(11), C.R.S. 1973, for purchases of machinery
or machine tools; the exemption of sales and purchases of electricity, coal, gas,
fuel oil or coke, as provided in section 39-26-114(1)(a)(XXI) C.R.S. 1973; and the
exemption for sales of food specified in section 39-26-114(1)(a) (XX), C.R.S. 1973.
B.
State Exemptions Not Applicable. Sales of food, as defined in section 39-26-102 (4.5),
C.R.S. 1973, exempted from the state sales tax pursuant to section 39-26-114(1) (a)
(XX), C.R.S. 1973; sales and purchases of electricity, coal, gas, fuel oil or coke,
exempted from the state sales tax pursuant to section 39-26-114(1)(a)(XXI), C.R.S.
1973; and purchases of machinery or machine tools, as provided in section 39-26-114(11),
C.R.S. 1973, are not exempted from but are specifically subject to the sales tax imposed
by the town.
C.
Exemption for Certain Sales to Non-residents. Furthermore, all sales of personal property
on which a specific ownership tax has been paid or is payable shall be exempt from
the sales tax imposed by the town when such sales meet both of the following conditions:
1.
The purchaser is a nonresident of, or has his principal place of business outside
of, the town; and
2.
Such personal property is registered or required to be registered outside the limits
of the town under the laws of the state of Colorado.
(Ord. 1, Series of 1980 § 3: Ord. 103 § VII, 1976: Ord. 48 § VII, 1969: Ord. 43 §
4(4-7), 1968)
Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the value of construction and
building materials on which a use tax has previously been collected by the town shall
be exempt from the sales tax imposed by the town if the materials are delivered by
the retailer or his agent to a site within the limits of the town.
(Ord. 1, Series of 1980 § 4: Ord. 43 § 4(4-8) , 1968)
The retail vendor or person furnishing the services shall be entitled to retain three
and one-third percent of all collections subject to the sales tax as a collection
fee.
(Ord. 103 § V, 1976: Ord. 48 § V, 1969: Ord. 43 § 4(4-5), 1968)
For the purpose of this chapter, all retail sales shall be considered consumed at
the place of business of the retailer unless the tangible personal property sold is
delivered by the retailer or his agent to a destination outside the limits of the
town or to a common carrier for delivery to a destination outside the limits of the
town.
(Ord. 43 § 4(4-3), 1968)
In the event a retailer or person furnishing services has no permanent place of business
in the town or more than one place of business, the place or places at which the retail
sales are consummated for the purpose of this sales tax shall be determined by the
provisions of article 26 of title 39, C.R.S. 1973, and by the rules and regulations
promulgated by the state of Colorado Department of Revenue.
(Ord. 103 § VI, 1976: Ord. 48 § VI, 1969: Ord. 43 § 4(4-6), 1968)
The board may, by amendment, alter or change the provisions of this chapter and Chapter 5.08 , except as to the rate of tax imposed by this chapter, subsequent to adoption by
a majority vote of the board. Such amendment, alteration or change need not be submitted
to the electors of the town for their approval.
(Ord. 43 § 6(6-1), 1968)
The town may issue a citation to a violator of Chapter 3.12 (sales tax) in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 2, 11-28-2016)
Every affected retailer or vendor shall be liable and responsible for the payment
of the fee assessed in C.R. S. § 25-17-505(1)(a) as provided in C.R.S. § 25-17-505(3)(d)(1)
and shall remit the fee on a quarterly basis with the first remittance to be due 20
days past the third quarter of 2023.
(Ord. No. 4-2023, § 1.2, 5-8-2023)
