"Compact parking space" means an area of permanent surface (asphalt, concrete, or
other material as determined by the town administrator or his designee), of not less
than eight feet by 18 feet. The town may approve up to 20 percent of the required
parking (for each use) as compact parking spaces, upon a site plan review for commercial
or multi-family structures only, and with the requirement that such spaces be marked
or designated as such.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 16-03 § 1 (part))
