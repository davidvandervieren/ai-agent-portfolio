"Bedroom" means any sleeping room within a dwelling unit that contains closet space
and has access to bathroom facilities, and further has all those features as determined
by the building code as adopted by the town. Dens, family rooms or recreational rooms
shall be considered bedrooms if determined to meet this definition.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
