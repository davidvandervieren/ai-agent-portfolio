The town shall process and review an application for subdivision and/or re-subdivision
of such land including multi-family units according to the provisions of Chapter 16.04 , and according to all applicable provisions of this Code.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant for a subdivision and/or re-subdivision of land creating multi-family
units on lots with zero building setbacks between adjacent units shall indicate on
the final plat the improvements required for those lots, including, but not limited
to the following:
A.
Each structure's building envelope.
B.
Proposed lot lines between multi-family units, or conversely, a plat note indicating
that the applicant will apply for subdivision or re-subdivision upon construction
of each of the multi-family units. Lot lines may be adjusted upon construction of
unit(s) and survey, if slightly different than proposed on subdivision plat, and a
subsequent plat shall be filed with the office by the owner(s) of said lot; notwithstanding
the foregoing, no plat shall be administratively approved that deviates substantially
from the original approved final plat, in the opinion of the town administrator or
his designee.
C.
Easements and/or proposed dedicated property for each multi-family unit, assuring
access and utility placement for each unit.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant proposing a multi-family subdivision and/or re-subdivision shall submit
a site plan in conformance with Section 17.42 and this chapter, in addition to any requirements pertaining to the subdivision or
re-subdivision of land. The town may review the applicant's multi-family site plan
concurrently with any subdivision application for the project.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant for a multi-family subdivision shall submit a site plan in accordance
with those requirements in Section 17.42 of the Code, as well as the following provisions:
1.
An appropriate engineering scale;
2.
Infrastructure construction details shown schematically, with scale of no more than
one inch equals one hundred feet;
3.
Entire area proposed for multi-family subdivision shown on one sheet if practicable,
with no map larger than twenty-four inches by thirty-six inches;
4.
North arrow, graphic scale and date;
5.
Boundary lines with bearings and distances, plus a property description of the tract
proposed for subdivision;
6.
Departing property lines;
7.
Street, block and lot layout;
8.
Existing and proposed easements for drainage, irrigation, access, utilities or other
facilities;
9.
Street names;
10.
Block and lot numbering;
11.
Existing contours, at five-foot intervals, unless a maximum ten-foot interval is approved
in rugged areas (topographic information shall be accurate within one-half of the
approved contour interval;
12.
Private and/or public open space to be dedicated to town or property owners association;
13.
Land use summary describing all proposed residential and non-residential land uses,
respective acreages and zoning of land uses, total acreage of subdivision, and number
of off-street parking spaces for each land use;
14.
Name(s) and address(es) of applicant(s) for the land use;
15.
Name(s) and address(es) of property owner, if not the applicant(s) for the land use;
and
16.
Vicinity map at a scale of one inch equals one-thousand feet, depicting the following.
a.
Tract lines and names of all abutting subdivision;
b.
Location of streets and highways adjacent to and within subdivision;
c.
Location of all adjacent and proposed utility systems;
d.
Natural drainage courses for streams flowing adjacent to and/or through the proposed
subdivision, with the limits of tributary areas shown where this is reasonable.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
The town may issue building permits for multi-family units, following the board's
approval of the site plan per Section 17.42 of the Code.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
The town shall review and approve all restrictive covenants, conditions and restrictions,
condominium declarations, association by-laws and articles of incorporation, prior
to the issuing of any certificate of occupancy for any multi-family building or part
thereof in a multi-family subdivision. Further, the town shall be a third-party beneficiary
to any and all such documents, with the ability but not the obligation to enforce
such documents, in the town's sole discretion.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant for a multi-family subdivision or re-subdivision shall apply the standards
established in this title for the design and construction of improvements such as
roads, sidewalks, curbs, gutters, bridges, water systems, sewer systems, utilities,
drainage systems or the like, even if the applicant proposes no public dedication
of such improvements.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
An applicant for a multi-family subdivision or re-subdivision shall submit a site
plan in which the individual lots and subdivision layout, the constructed improvements,
and the engineering investigation and design conforms to the provisions of this title
as well as applicable provisions of the town's zoning ordinances.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
