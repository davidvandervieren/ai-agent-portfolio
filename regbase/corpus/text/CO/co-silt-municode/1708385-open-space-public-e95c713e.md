"Open space (public)" means land retained in an open or natural state, except for
agricultural use or for the placement of landscape materials, including trees, shrubs
and grass, and structures limited to footpaths, bridges, irrigation, erosion protection,
underground utilities, or improved for park use. The designation of public open space
implies the right of access by the public.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
