Chapters:
Pursuant to the provisions of Sections 31-16-201 through 31-16-208 of the Colorado
Revised Statutes, there is adopted the Silt Municipal Code as published by Book Publishing
Company, Seattle, Washington.
(Ord. 7-83 § 1 )
All draft ordinances being proposed for adoption by the board of trustees shall be
read in full during a board meeting prior to the meeting in which the board adopts
such ordinance. Nothing in this Section 1.01.011 is intended to apply to the town's consideration or adoption of emergency measures.
(Ord. 26-96)
This code shall be known as the Silt Municipal Code and it shall be sufficient to
refer to said code as the "Silt Municipal Code" in any prosecution for the violation
of any provision thereof or in any proceeding at law or equity. It shall be sufficient
to designate any ordinance adding to, amending, correcting or repealing all or any
portion thereof as an addition to, amendment to, correction or repeal of the Silt
Municipal Code. Further reference may be had to the titles, chapters, sections and
subsections of the Silt Municipal Code and such references shall apply to that numbered
title, chapter, section or subsection as it appears in the code.
(Ord. 7-83 § 2)
The last ordinance included in the original code is Ordinance No. 32, Series of 1980,
passed December 15, 1980. The following ordinances of the town, passed subsequent
to Ordinance No. 32, Series of 1980, but prior to the adoption of this code, are adopted
and made a part of this code: Ordinance No. 1, Series of 1981 through Ordinance No.
28, Series of 1981; Ordinance No. 1, Series of 1982 through Ordinance No. 17, Series
of 1982; and ordinance No. 1, Series of 1983 through Ordinance No. 6, Series of 1983.
(Ord. 7-83 § 3 )
This code consists of all the regulatory and penal ordinances and certain of the administrative
ordinances of the town of Silt, Colorado, codified pursuant to the provisions of Sections
31-16-201 through 31-16-208 of the Colorado Revised Statutes.
(Ord. 7-83 § 4)
Whenever a reference is made to this code as the Silt Municipal Code or to any portion
thereof, or to any ordinance of the town of Silt, the reference shall apply to all
amendments, corrections and additions heretofore, now or hereafter made.
(Ord. 7-83 § 5)
The town clerk or town administrator is authorized to correct any minor typographical,
grammatical or clerical errors in the Municipal Code as long as such correction does
not alter in any way the intent or purpose of the code or any ordinance.
(Ord. 3-96)
Title, chapter and section headings contained herein shall not be deemed to govern,
limit, modify or in any manner affect the scope, meaning or intent of the provisions
of any title, chapter or section hereof.
(Ord. 7-83 § 6)
The provisions of this code shall not in any manner affect matters of record which
refer to, or are otherwise connected with, ordinances which are therein specifically
designated by number or otherwise and which are included within the code; but such
reference shall be construed to apply to the corresponding provisions contained within
the code.
(Ord. 7-83 § 7)
Neither the adoption of this code nor the repeal or amendment of any ordinance or
part or portion of any ordinance of the town shall in any manner affect the prosecution
for violations of ordinances, which violations were committed prior to the effective
date of the ordinance codified in this chapter, nor be construed as a waiver of any
license, fee, or penalty at said effective date due and unpaid under such ordinances,
nor be construed as affecting any of the provisions of such ordinances relating to
the collection of any such license, fee, or penalty, or the penal provisions applicable
to any violation thereof, nor to affect the validity of any bond or cash deposit in
lieu thereof required to be posted, filed or deposited pursuant to any ordinance;
and all rights and obligations thereunder appertaining shall continue in full force
and effect.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1983 § 8)
The following sections of the code adopted by this chapter are penalty sections, and
said penalty sections are hereinafter set forth in full and reenacted as required
by Section 31-16-204 of the Colorado Revised Statutes.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1983 § 9)
This code shall become effective on the date the ordinance adopting this code as the
Silt Municipal Code shall become effective.
(Ord. 7, Series of 1983 § 10)
The following words and phrases, whenever used in the ordinances of the town of Silt,
Colorado, shall be construed as defined in this section unless from the context a
different meaning is intended or unless a different meaning is specifically defined
and more particularly directed to the use of such words or phrases:
A.
"Board" means the board of trustees of the town of Silt, Colorado, consisting of one
mayor and six trustees.
B.
"City" and "town" each means the town of Silt, Colorado, or the area within the territorial
limits of the town of Silt, Colorado, and such territory outside of the town over
which the town has jurisdiction or control by virtue of any constitutional or statutory
provision.
C.
"County" means the county of Garfield, in the state of Colorado.
D.
"Law" denotes applicable federal law, the constitution and statutes of the state of
Colorado, the ordinances of the town, court cases interpreting any of the foregoing
and, when appropriate, any and all rules and regulations which may be promulgated
thereunder.
E.
"May" is permissive.
F.
"Month" means a calendar month.
G.
"Must" and "shall" are each mandatory.
H.
"Oath" includes an affirmation or declaration in all cases in which, by law, an affirmation
may be substituted for an oath, and in such cases the words "swear" and "sworn" shall
be equivalent to the words "affirm" and "affirmed."
I.
"Owner," applied to a building or land, includes any part owner, joint owner, tenant
in common, or joint tenant of the whole or a part of such building or land.
J.
"Person" includes a natural person, joint venture, joint stock company, mutual ditch
company, partnership, association, club, company, corporation, business, trust, organization,
or the manager, lessee, agent, servant, officer, director or employee of any of them.
K.
"Personal property" includes money, goods, chattels, things in action and evidences
of debt.
L.
"Preceding" and "following" mean next before and next after, respectively.
M.
"Property" includes real and personal property.
N.
"Real property" includes lands, tenements and hereditaments.
O.
"Sidewalk" means that portion of a street between the curbline and the adjacent property
line intended for the use of pedestrians.
P.
"State" means the state of Colorado.
Q.
"Street" includes all streets, highways, avenues, lanes, alleys, courts, places, squares,
curbs or other public ways in this town which have been or may hereafter be dedicated
and open to public use, or such other public property so designated in any law of
this state.
R.
"Tenant" and "occupant," applied to a building or land, include any person who occupies
the whole or a part of such building or land, whether alone or with others.
S.
"Written" includes printed, typewritten, mimeographed, photocopied, multigraphed or
otherwise reproduced in permanent, visible form.
T.
"Year" means a calendar year.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 1)
Use of the title of any officer, employee, department, board or commission means that
officer, employee, department, board or commission of the town.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 2)
All words and phrases shall be construed according to the common and approved usage
of the language, but technical words and phrases and such others as may have acquired
a peculiar and appropriate meaning in the law shall be construed and understood according
to such peculiar and appropriate meaning.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 3)
The following grammatical rules shall apply in the ordinances of the town, unless
it is apparent from the context that a different construction is intended:
A.
Gender. Each gender includes the masculine, feminine and neuter genders.
B.
Singular and Plural. The singular number includes the plural and the plural includes
the singular.
C.
Tenses. Words used in the present tense include the past and the future tenses and
vice versa, unless manifestly inapplicable.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 4)
When an act is required by an ordinance, the same being such that it may be done as
well by an agent as by the principal, such requirement shall be construed to include
all such acts performed by an authorized agent.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 5)
Whenever in the ordinances of the town, any act or omission is made unlawful, it shall
include causing, allowing, permitting, aiding, abetting, suffering or concealing the
fact of such act or omission.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 6)
Except when otherwise provided, the time within which an act is required to be done
shall be computed by excluding the first day and including the last day, unless the
last day is Sunday or a holiday, in which case it shall also be excluded.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 7)
The provisions of the ordinances of the town, and all proceedings under them are to
be construed with a view to effect their objects and to promote justice.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 8)
The repeal of an ordinance shall not repeal the repealing clause of an ordinance or
revive any ordinance which has been repealed thereby.
(Ord. 20, Series of 1980 § 9)
Whenever an ordinance or a part of an ordinance is repealed or modified by a subsequent
ordinance or part of an ordinance, the ordinance or part of ordinance thus repealed
or modified shall continue in force until the effective date of the ordinance repealing
or modifying the same unless therein otherwise expressly provided, but no suit proceeding,
right, fine or penalty instituted, created, given, secured or accrued under any ordinance
previous to its repeal shall in any wise be affected, released or discharged but may
be prosecuted, enforced and recovered as fully as if such ordinance had continued
in force unless it is therein otherwise expressly provided. (Gen. Ords. Art. XVIII
§ 1, 1936)
A seal, the impression of which is as follows: in the center the word "Seal" and around
the outer edge the words "The Town of Silt, Colorado," is the seal of the town. (Gen.
Ords. Art. II § 1, 1936)
No person shall violate any of the provisions of the ordinances of the town.
(Ord. 21-80 § 1)
Any person who violates any provision of the ordinances of the town may be punished
by the maximum fine allowed by law pursuant to C.R.S. § 13-10-113 and subsequent statutes
or by imprisonment not to exceed 364 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment,
except where a different punishment is otherwise prescribed.
(Ord. 21-99: Ord. 18-93 (part): Ord. 21-80 § 2)
(Ord. No. 3-2021, § 1, 4-12-2021)
Each such person is guilty of a separate offense for each and every day during any
portion of which any violation of any provision of the ordinances of the town is committed,
continued or permitted by any such person, and he shall be punished accordingly.
(Ord. 7-83 § 9; Ord. 21-80 § 3)
In accordance with the provisions of C.R.S. 31-10-102.7, the town of Silt hereby adopts
the Uniform Election Code of 1992 in lieu of the Colorado Municipal Election Code
of 1965 for the purpose of participating in any coordinated election in Garfield County
which is conducted by the county clerk and recorder.
(Ord. 4-96 § 1 ; Ord. 15-94 § 1)
The town clerk is hereby directed to work with the Garfield county clerk and recorder
and other designated election officials and to take all necessary actions to implement
Silt's portion of the coordinated election in accordance with Article 20 of Title
X of the Colorado Constitution, the Uniform Election Code of 1992, and all other appropriate
statutes.
(Ord. 4-96 § 2 ; Ord. 15-94 § 2)
Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit the town of Silt from conducting a regular
municipal election which is not a part of a coordinated election or a special municipal
election on a ballot question. Such elections shall be conducted in accordance with
the Colorado Municipal Election Code of 1965.
(Ord. 4-96 § 3)
No write-in vote for any municipal office in a town election shall be counted unless
an affidavit of intent has been filed with the town clerk by the person whose name
is written in prior to the deadline in accordance with the provisions of the Municipal
Election Code.
(Ord. No. 12-2015, § 1, 12-14-2015)

If the only matter before the voters in a municipal election is the election of persons
to office and if, at the close of business on the date before the election in accordance
with the provisions of the Municipal Election Code, there are not more candidates
than offices to be filled at such election, including write-in candidates filing affidavits
of intent pursuant to Section 1.16.040 of this chapter, the town clerk, if instructed by resolution of the board of trustees
either before or after such date, shall cancel the election and by resolution declare
the candidates elected. Upon such declaration the candidates shall be deemed elected.
Notice of such cancellation shall be published, if possible, in order to inform the
electors of the town, and notice of such cancellation shall be posted at each polling
place and in not less than one other public place.
