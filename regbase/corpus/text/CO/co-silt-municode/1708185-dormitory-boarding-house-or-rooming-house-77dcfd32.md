"Dormitory," "boarding house" or "rooming house" means any building or portion thereof
intended or designated specifically for sleeping facilities for an individual or a
group of four (4) or more unrelated individuals. A dormitory, boarding house or rooming
house shall have sanitation facilities either within or in close proximity to sleeping
areas, but shall not have a kitchen or a laundry room within the actual sleeping room.
Accessory uses may include laundry and kitchen facilities located outside designated
sleeping areas.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
