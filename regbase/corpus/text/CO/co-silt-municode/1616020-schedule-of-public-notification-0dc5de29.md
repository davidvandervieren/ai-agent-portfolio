An applicant for a land use application pursuant to this title shall complete the
following public notifications prior to scheduling of a public hearing before the
commission or the board:
A.
An applicant for a land use application shall, at his expense, send a notice to property
owners within two hundred feet of the subject location, such distance not to include
rights-of-way, rivers, tributaries, or public parcels, according to the schedule set
forth in Section 16.16.020 for number of days notice prior to the public hearing;
B.
An applicant for a land use application shall, at his expense, send a notice to a
newspaper of general circulation (as determined by town staff), according to the schedule
set forth in Section 16.16.020 for number of days notice prior to the public hearing;
C.
Town staff shall not place a land use application on a commission agenda or a board
agenda for consideration by the respective board, unless the applicant for a land
use application submits to the town clerk no fewer than six days prior to the proposed
public hearing:
1.
Original proof of publication from the newspaper of general circulation;
2.
Original certificate of mailing from the individual who physically mailed adjacent
property owner notifications in the U.S. mail; certificate of mailing shall be notarized
and completed to the town's satisfaction;
3.
Original postage receipts for adjacent property owner notifications, stamped and dated
by the post office with no fewer than the number of days notice required in Section 16.16.020 ;
D.
An applicant for a land use application shall deliver to the town clerk either prior
to the public hearing or at the public hearing to discuss the land use application,
return receipts, signed by adjacent property owners, or, conversely, those marked
"return to sender" due to delivery problems.
E.
In the town administrator's sole discretion, town staff may send public notices by
first class mail to two-hundred foot property owners prior to required public hearing,
based upon the applicant's list of two-hundred foot property owners provided in the
application. Town staff may require applicant to update any submitted two-hundred
foot property owner list, if, in town staff's sole discretion, the review period and/or
re-submittal period have potentially caused the list to become antiquated. The town
has no implied liability for any mistakes or omissions on the list or from the staff
mailing.
(Ord. No. 9-2011, § 1, 12-12-2011)
