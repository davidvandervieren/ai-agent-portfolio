The purpose of this article is to establish license and retail sale requirements for
cigarettes, tobacco and nicotine products and tobacco and nicotine paraphernalia retailers
to protect the health, safety, and welfare of individuals in the town, to encourage
responsible tobacco and nicotine product retailing, and to reduce illegal sales of
said products.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Title 44-7, C.R.S., and the Rules and Regulations of the Executive Director of the
Colorado Department of Revenue (collectively "Article 7 of Title 44"), as the state
licensing authority, with all subsequent supplements thereto, are adopted for purposes
of ensuring compliance as described in this chapter.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
The following definitions shall apply throughout this chapter:
A.
"Cigarette or nicotine product" means
1.
A product that contains nicotine or tobacco or is derived from tobacco and is intended
to be ingested or inhaled by or applied to the skin of an individual; or
2.
Any device that can be used to deliver tobacco or nicotine to the person inhaling
from the device, including an electronic cigarette, cigar, cigarillo, or pipe.
3.
Notwithstanding any provision of this paragraph cigarettes, tobacco products, or nicotine
products do not include a product that the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has
approved as a tobacco use termination product.
B.
"Compliance check" means the division or local authority engaging a person under twenty-one
years of age to enter a retail location to attempt the purchase of cigarettes, tobacco
products, or nicotine products for purposes of ensuring compliance with Article 7
of Title 44 and this Article.
C.
"Division" means the State of Colorado Department of Revenue's Enforcement Division,
except as provided otherwise.
D.
"ESD" means electronic nicotine delivery system.
E.
"Inspection" means a procedure conducted by the division or local authority to determine
whether a retailer is operating in compliance with the requirements of Article 7 of
Title 44 or this article as they relate to the retailer's sale of cigarettes, tobacco
products, or nicotine products.
F.
"Licensee" means the owner or holder of a tobacco product retailer license.
G.
"Licensing administrator" means the town clerk's office which is responsible for overseeing
and managing the license issuance, renewal, and collection of fees.
H.
"Local authority" means the governing body or the Licensing Administrator of the Town
of Silt.
I.
"Retailer" means the owner or operator of a business of any kind at a specific location
that sells cigarettes, tobacco products, or nicotine products to a user or consumer.
J.
"School" means a public, parochial, or nonpublic school that provides a basic academic
education in compliance with school attendance laws for students in grades one through
twelve. "Basic academic education" means the sequential program of instruction provided
by an independent or parochial school, and such program shall include, but not be
limited to, communication skills of reading, writing, and speaking, mathematics, history,
civics, literature, and science.
K.
"Sell" or "sale" means any of the following: To exchange, barter, or traffic in; to
solicit or receive an order for; to keep or expose for sale; to deliver for value
or in any way other than gratuitously; to peddle or to possess with intent to sell;
to traffic in for any consideration promised or obtained, directly or indirectly.
L.
"State license" means a license issued by the division in accordance with C.R.S. section
44-7-105.
M.
"Tobacco product" means
1.
Any product which contains, is made or derived from tobacco or used to deliver nicotine
or other substances intended for human consumption whether heated, chewed, absorbed,
dissolved, inhaled, snorted, sniffed, or ingested by other means, including, but not
limited to, cigarettes, cigars, chewing tobacco, pipe tobacco, snuff, bidis, snus,
mints, hand gels; or
2.
Electronic smoking devices used for tobacco.
3.
Has the same meaning as nicotine product notwithstanding any provision of subsections
1 and 2 to the contrary, "tobacco product" includes any component, part, accessory,
or associated tobacco paraphernalia of a tobacco product whether or not sold separately.
Tobacco products do not include any product that includes marijuana or any product
made from or derived from tobacco and approved by the Food and Drug Administration
(FDA) for use in connection with the termination of smoking.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
Tobacco products shall not be sold to any person under the minimum legal sales age
of twenty-one years of age.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
On or after October 31, 2025, it shall be unlawful for any person or establishment
to act as a retailer in the town unless a tobacco license has been obtained from the
local authority.
B.
No license shall be issued to authorize tobacco product retail sales anywhere other
than at a fixed location designated in the license application.
C.
No license shall be issued to an applicant under the minimum legal sales age.
D.
Every license is separate, distinct, and specific to the designated location.
E.
Licenses will not be issued to retailers located within five hundred feet of any school
as determined by the local authority. This restriction shall not apply to existing
retailers within five hundred feet of a school.
F.
The sale of tobacco products, cigarettes or nicotine products without a valid license
is a violation of this chapter.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
The following conditions shall apply to the licensee:
A.
Licensees shall stock and display all cigarettes, tobacco products or nicotine products
in a manner as to make all such products inaccessible to customers without the assistance
of a retail clerk. Self-serve displays and displays at the point of sale are prohibited.
B.
Restriction on Sales to Underage Persons. No Retailer shall sell or transfer cigarettes,
tobacco products or nicotine products to another person unless that recipient is at
least twenty-one years of age. It is an affirmative defense to prosecution that the
seller was presented with a valid, government-issued identification showing the person
to be over the minimum age.
C.
Sale of all cigarettes, tobacco products and nicotine products must take place on
the licensed premises.
D.
Advertising of any cigarettes, tobacco products or nicotine products shall not be
visible at the front entrance, or advertising of any products at the point of sale.
E.
License shall be displayed in a publicly visible location on the designated property.
F.
Minimum legal sales age requirement poster shall be displayed at the entrance or a
visible location and contain at minimum the following:
WARNING IT IS ILLEGAL FOR ANY PERSON UNDER TWENTY-ONE (21) YEARS OF AGE TO PURCHASE CIGARETTES,
NICOTINE PRODUCTS AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS AND, UPON CONVICTION, A FINE MAY BE IMPOSED
G.
No person who is younger than eighteen years of age shall, while employed at a retailer
location, sell, stock, retrieve, or otherwise handle cigarettes, tobacco products
or nicotine products.
H.
No retailer shall sell or transfer cigarettes, tobacco products or nicotine products
to another person who appears to be under the age of fifty years without first examining
the government-issued identification of the recipient to confirm that the recipient
is at least the minimum legal sale age.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
An application shall be filed with the licensing administrator or town clerk. It is
the responsibility of each applicant and/or licensee to be informed regarding all
laws applicable to tobacco retailing, including those laws affecting the issuance
of said license. No applicant and/or licensee may rely on the issuance of a license
as a determination by the town that the proprietor has complied with all applicable
tobacco retailing laws.
B.
All applications shall be submitted on a form supplied by the local authority, along
with the licensing fee.
C.
All applications shall be completed in full and must be accompanied by the payment
in full of all fees (as set by the board of trustees) and proof that the applicant
is in possession or has applied for a state license. An application not completed
in full and/or remitted without all fees will be deemed incomplete.
D.
A retailer shall inform the licensing administrator in writing of any change in the
information submitted on an application for a license within thirty business days
of the change.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Upon the receipt of a completed application for a license as required by this chapter,
the licensing administrator shall sign and issue a license within thirty days which
period may be extended by the licensing administrator for good cause unless substantial
evidence demonstrates that one or more of the following bases for denial exists:
A.
The information presented in the application is incomplete, inaccurate, or false;
B.
The applicant seeks authorization for a license at a location where it is prohibited;
C.
The applicant seeks authorization for a license and the applicant's current license
is suspended or revoked;
D.
The applicant is not qualified to hold the requested license under the provisions
of this chapter;
E.
The applicant and/or retail location is not in compliance with all town, state, or
federal laws;
F.
The applicant is indebted to, or obligated in any manner to the town of unpaid taxes,
liens, or other monies; or
G.
The payment of the licensing fee in the full amount chargeable for such license does
not accompany such license application.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
If the local authority denies the issuance of the license, the local authority or
its representative shall notify the applicant either in writing by regular mail postage
prepaid at the address shown in the application or through electric mail to the email
address provided on the application. The notice shall include the grounds for denial.
Notice is deemed to have been properly given upon mailing.
B.
An applicant has the right to appeal the local authority's denial of an application
to the board of trustees. Such an appeal shall be initiated by filing a written request
with the local authority within twenty days of the date of the notice of denial of
the issuance of a license.
C.
The applicant's failure to timely appeal the local authority's decision waives the
applicant's right to contest the denial of the license issuance.
D.
The appeal, including any right to further appeals, shall be conducted and controlled
by the provisions of Rule 106(a)(4) of the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure. The
standard of proof at such appeal shall be a preponderance of the evidence and the
burden of proof shall be upon the applicant. The licensee's failure to timely appeal
the decision is a waiver of the licensee's right to contest the suspension or revocation
of the license.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
All licenses issued under this code shall be for one year.
B.
A Licensee shall apply for the renewal of the license and submit the renewal application
and license fee no later than forty-five days before the expiration of the existing
term. If the applicant fails to apply for renewal at least forty-five days before
the expiration of the license, the applicant may be subject to a late filing fee set
by the board of trustees [per] S.M.C. [Section] 2.44.020 .
C.
A license that is not timely renewed shall expire at the end of its term. The failure
to timely obtain a renewal of the license requires submission of a new application.
There shall be no sale of any tobacco products after the license expiration date and
before the new license is issued.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
Licenses are non-transferable from one entity to another nor from one location to
another.
B.
If a licensee ceases to be a retailer at a retail location by reason of discontinuation,
sale, or transfer of the licensee's business, the licensee shall notify the local
authority in writing on or before the date on which it will take effect.
C.
When a license has been issued to a husband and wife, or general or limited partners,
the death of a spouse or partner shall not require the surviving spouse or partner
to obtain a new license for the remainder of the term of that license. All rights
and privileges granted under the original license shall continue in full force and
effect as to such survivors for the balance of the term of the license.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
The fee to issue or to renew a license shall be pursuant to the town's fee schedule,
a copy of which may be obtained from the town website and is available at town hall.
B.
The amount of fees charged by the town pursuant to this chapter shall be reviewed
and, if necessary, adjusted to reflect the direct and indirect costs incurred by the
town in connection with the adoption, administration, and enforcement of this chapter.
C.
The amount of the fees charged by the town pursuant to this chapter shall be set by
the board of trustees pursuant to S.M.C. Section 2.44.020 .
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
Tobacco festivals shall be prohibited within town limits. A tobacco festival is an
event where individuals pay a fee to gain entrance to an area where cigarettes, tobacco
products, and/or nicotine products are displayed, exhibited, and/or distributed for
consumption either on-site or off-site.
B.
No retailer will sell any cigarettes, tobacco products, and/or nicotine products where
an employee of the retailer, or a third-party delivery service, takes the cigarettes,
tobacco products, and/or nicotine products from the licensed property to the off-site
purchaser.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Any retailer that sells cigarettes, tobacco products, or nicotine products for consumers
to use at the retail location shall post signs in at least one conspicuous place and
at least four inches by six inches in size stating:
Smoking and Vaping allowed. Persons under twenty-one years of age may not enter.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
Compliance monitoring of this chapter shall be conducted by the Silt Police Department.
A minimum of two compliance checks shall be completed each calendar year. When a compliance
check is completed by the local authority, the local authority will notify the division
of any outcome, or action taken on the licensee as a result of the compliance check.
B.
An applicant or licensee shall permit representatives of the local authority, building
official, the director of the community development department, the police department,
the county health department, and the fire department to inspect the premises, for
the purpose of ensuring compliance with the law at any time it is occupied or open
for business.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
The local authority shall conduct the hearing. The hearing may be before the board
of trustees or before an officer designated by the board of trustees and may hear
all actions relating to the suspension or revocation of licenses under this chapter.
B.
In all such hearings, the town attorney shall act on behalf of and advise the local
authority. The local authority may appoint a special counsel to conduct an investigation
and prosecute the licensee on behalf of the town.
C.
The local authority shall set a time and place for a hearing on the matter, and the
town clerk shall give the licensee timely written notice of the date, time, and place
of the hearing. The notice shall describe generally the alleged violations and the
grounds showing probable cause for suspension or revocation of a license.
D.
Notice shall be mailed to the licensee by regular mail, postage prepaid at the address
shown on the license, or through electric mail to the email address provided by the
licensee, no later than fifteen days prior to the hearing date. Notice is deemed to
have been properly given upon mailing.
E.
If the local authority conducts a hearing, it shall render a decision that shall contain
such findings of facts and conclusions of law as are relevant and necessary to support
the decision under this code. If the hearing is held before a hearing officer, the
hearing officer shall make a recommendation in writing to the local authority within
thirty days after the close of the hearing. The written recommendation shall contain
findings of fact and conclusions of law based on the evidence presented at the hearing.
A copy of this summary and recommendation shall be transmitted to the licensee. At
the next regular board of trustees meeting following the submission of the hearing
officer's recommendation, the local authority shall consider the same. In its discretion,
the local authority may adopt the hearing officer's recommendation, adopt the hearing
officer's recommendation with modifications, or reject the hearing officer's recommendation.
F.
The local authority shall have the authority to suspend, revoke, or impose remedial
sanctions for violations.
G.
In determining whether a license should be suspended or revoked, and in determining
whether to impose conditions in the event of a suspension, the local authority and/or
hearing officer shall consider the following factors:
1.
The nature and circumstances of the violation;
2.
Corrective action, if any taken by the licensee;
3.
Prior violations, if any by the licensee;
4.
The likelihood of recurrence of the violation;
5.
Whether the violation was willful; and
6.
Previous sanctions, if any, imposed on the licensee.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
A.
The remedies provided by this chapter are cumulative and in addition to any other
remedies available at law or in equity. In addition to other remedies provided by
this chapter or by other law, any violation of this chapter may be remedied by a civil
action brought by the town attorney, including, but not limited to, nuisance abatement
proceedings and injunctive relief.
B.
Causing, permitting, aiding, abetting, or concealing a violation of any provision
of this chapter shall cause the offender to be subject to the penalties set forth
herein or in the Silt Municipal Code.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Every license issued under this chapter confers only a limited and conditional privilege
subject to the requirements, conditions, limitations, and qualifications of this chapter.
The license does not confer a property right of any kind. The license and privilege
created by the license may be further regulated, limited, or completely extinguished
at the discretion of the board of trustees or the electorate of the town, as provided
in this chapter, without any compensation to the licensee. Nothing contained in this
chapter grants to any licensee any vested right to continue operating under the provisions
of this chapter as they existed at the time the license was approved or issued, and
every license shall be subject to any ordinance or prohibition adopted after the license
was approved or issued.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
This chapter shall become effective as of October 1, 2025 and enforceable on and after
that date. Any retailer without a license after the effective date will be in violation
of this chapter. Current retailers with a valid state-issued license must apply for
a license within thirty days of the effective date of this chapter.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Whenever a local authority receives a complaint, the local authority will investigate
the allegations as deemed appropriate and will notify the state enforcement division
of the content of such complaint, as well as the outcome of any investigation, or
action taken on the licensee as a result of the complaint.
(Ord. No. 1-2025, § 1, 4-14-2025)
Violations of any provisions of this chapter shall result in penalties according to
the generally accepted and practiced state penalty guidelines provided below. Nothing
in the guidelines is meant to restrict the local authority from issuing a lesser penalty,
a higher penalty, or additional penalties as allowed by this code or state law, up
to and including suspension or revocation of a tobacco license or the imposition of
a fine in lieu of suspension as provided under the provisions of Section 44-7-106,
C.R.S. and/or Chapter 5.20 of this code. For the second incident in one year, any
days held in abeyance will be automatically imposed from the first incident in addition
to any other suspension imposed by the local or state licensing authorities. If there
are any additional provisions not listed, or if there are any changes newly adopted
by state guidelines, the local authority may accept such changes.
