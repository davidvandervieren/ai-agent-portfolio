A.
"Bulk Consumer" means any person, partnership, corporation, or any other legal entity,
public or private, their lessees and tenants, supplied with water for commercial uses
whereby domestic water is a main processing ingredient and/or service. The use shall
not constitute a consumptive use nor shall the use produce wastewater that has substantial
amounts of other compounds associated (i.e., oil, grease, food or harsh chemicals).
B.
"Dwelling unit" means a building or portion thereof providing complete and permanent
living facilities for one family, containing cooking, living, sleeping and sanitary
facilities and having a separate entrance.
C.
"Fixture unit" means the standard definition for fixture, as found in the United States
of America Standards Institute (USASI), National Plumbing Code, 1955.
D.
"Lead income recipient" means the person whose gross income under Section 61 of the
Internal Revenue Code, 1976, exceeds the gross income of any other person who resides
within the dwelling unit.
(Ord. 8-04 § 1 (part); Ord. 22-97 (part); Ord. 130-78 § 2: Ord. 76-73 § 1(B))
A.
Base Fee and Fee Schedule.
1.
There shall be assessed and charged to every dwelling unit, commercial establishment,
government entity, and any other in-town user of municipal water within the town for
which a metering device has been installed, is operative and is readily accessible,
unless specifically described in this chapter, a base rate as set forth by the board
annually (or as more often as necessary) by resolution, per EQR, for each wastewater
user's impact to the town wastewater system, in accordance with this code. Commercial
consumers include all commercial establishments and government non-residential consumers
not already provided for in this chapter. Such base fee shall include a base usage
of 6,000 gallons of water per EQR served per month as measured by the customer's water
meter.
2.
In addition, there shall be assessed a sewer fee as set forth by the board annually
(or as more often as necessary) for every 1,000 gallons of water provided, or portion
thereof over 2,000 gallons, up to 6,000 gallons per EQR per month.
3.
Consumers having purchased .6 EQR for the potable irrigation of less than 3,501 square
feet of irrigable area shall be allowed 15,000 gallons of water per month during the
irrigation season and thereafter shall be assessed a sewer fee as set by the board
annually (or as more often as necessary) by resolution, for every thousand gallons
of water provided or portion thereof, in excess of the 15,000 per EQR per month.
4.
Bulk consumers, as defined by this code, shall be allowed to provide proof to the
town that a portion of the domestic water used does not flow into the town's wastewater
collection system and that the type of water flowing into the system does not flow
into the town's wastewater collection system does not require the same level of treatment
as sewage. The town administrator or his/her designee, in his/her sole discretion,
may determine that, by reviewing the consumer's wastewater BOD (biochemical oxygen
demand) that the "bulk consumer" designation applies. Bulk consumers shall not include
grease producing facilities. Bulk consumers shall only use soaps approved by the town
administrator or his/her designee. If proof is provided to the town prior to billing,
bulk consumers shall be assessed a wastewater fee in an amount set by the board annually
(or more often as necessary) by resolution for every 1,000 gallons of water provided,
or portion thereof, in excess of 6,000 gallons per EQR per month.
5.
Fresh wastewater meeting town wastewater plant operator standards not exceeding 500
parts per million of chemical oxygen demand may be disposed of in a location as determined
by the town for a fee as set by the board annually (or more often as necessary) by
resolution, for each 1,000 gallons or portion thereof.
B.
The base fee and the dump fee shall be set by the board annually (or more often as
necessary) by resolution, but in the case that the board fails to set fees annually,
the last fee in effect is valid.
C.
Reserved Service (Stand-By)—Availability—Non-Active Meter—In-Town Sewer Service. If,
for any reason, town water or wastewater services are shut off, reserved or discontinued
to any property, the owner of such property may preserve the wastewater taps associated
with the property by requesting to be charged 50 percent of the base rate monthly,
as determined by the board annually (or more often as necessary) by resolution, in
addition to any other fees or charges for service shut-off contemplated elsewhere
in this chapter. Upon the town's determination that water use is occurring on the
property, the town shall deem the service active and apply full rates.
D.
Upon application for a building permit for a new structure, the town will deem the
newly purchased wastewater tap reserved for up to 180 days following issuance of the
building permit. After 180 days following issuance of the building permit, the town
will deem the service active and apply full rates.

A.
Reimbursement Amount. Upon application by a user to the town for approval of any request
that will result in the use of the First Street sanitary sewer improvement ("improvements"),
the town shall determine such user's pro rata share of such infrastructure costs based
on the product of the following:
1.
One hundred percent of the costs of the sanitary sewer infrastructure north of 1st
Street ("improvements"), to the extent such improvements or portion thereof will serve
the user;
2.
The end result of such user's EQR's to be served by the improvements (as determined
by Section 13.04.260 or successor provisions of the Silt Municipal Code), divided by the sanitary sewer
infrastructure capacity (total EQR's), multiplied by the total sanitary sewer infrastructure
costs; and
B.
Collection of Reimbursement Amount. The town shall require that user or land owner
pay their pro rata share of the costs of the improvements, as calculated above, plus
interest at the rate of five percent per annum, prior to the approval of any taps
to such user to connect onto any of the town's sanitary sewer infrastructure set forth
herein. Such payment shall be made to the town at building permit issuance.

A.
Reimbursement Amount. Upon a user's application to the town for connection to any
wastewater line that utilizes the Ferguson Crossing temporary LS or the Ferguson Crossing
Regional LS, the town shall determine such user's cost reimbursement obligation based
on the following formula: The town's cost multiplied by the quotient of the number
of EQRs required to serve the user's land use divided by the number of anticipated
EQRs that the regional LS will serve in the service area.
1.
The town's cost shall be:
a.
Prior to the construction of the Ferguson Crossing Regional LS, the town's estimate
of the costs of constructing the regional LS as identified on Exhibit C, as said cost
estimates may be administratively updated by the town from time to time.
b.
After the construction of the Ferguson Crossing Regional LS, the town's actual costs
of constructing the Regional LS, as determined by the town in its sole discretion,
plus interest accruing at the rate of five percent per annum from the date of construction
of the regional LS until the date of the user's application to the town.
2.
The EQRs required to serve the user's land use by the regional LS shall be determined
by Section 13.04.260 or successor provisions of the Silt Municipal Code;
3.
The number of anticipated EQRs that the regional LS will serve in the service area
is described on Exhibit C to the ordinance.
B.
Collection of Reimbursement Amount. The town shall require the user to pay the reimbursement
obligation, as calculated above prior to the approval of a user located in the service
area to connect onto any of the town's wastewater infrastructure set forth herein.
1.
The board of trustees shall set the amount of the reimbursement by resolution annually;
2.
User shall pay such reimbursement amount at the time of building permit issuance or
any connection to the town's wastewater infrastructure as set forth herein, whichever
occurs first.
C.
Termination Date. All rights and obligations related to the town's cost recoupment
shall expire and be of no further force or effect after 30 years from the effective
date of the ordinance from which this section was derived.
