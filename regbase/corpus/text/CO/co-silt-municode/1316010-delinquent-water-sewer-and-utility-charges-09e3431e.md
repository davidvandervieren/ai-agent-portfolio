A.
An account shall be deemed delinquent if it is not paid in fully by the due date.
Delinquent accounts are subject to a penalty assessment of 1.5 percent per month of
the unpaid monthly balance. Partial payments shall not result in an account being
deemed non-delinquent.
B.
Written notice of a proposed termination of water service for non-payment of water
charges or sewer charges shall be mailed to the last known address of the customer
as shown by the town's records thirty (30) days after the account becomes delinquent.
The notice shall advise that water services are subject to termination no later than
forty (40) days after the account becomes delinquent. Such notice shall also be mailed
to the owner of the property served at the address as shown by the records of the
county assessor.
C.
At least twenty-four (24) hours before actual termination and disconnection of water
(including raw water) and/or sewer services, a notice of such termination shall be
delivered to the occupants of the property served. If the occupants are not present
at the property, the notice shall be posted on the property.
D.
Prior to the date designated for termination of service, the customer or owner may
request a conference with the town administrator for the purpose of determining whether
termination of service is appropriate. If a conference is not timely requested, or
if payment of all charges is not received or other arrangements satisfactory to the
town administrator made for payment prior to five p.m. on the day prior to the notice
for termination, water (including raw water) and/or sewer services shall be terminated
and such services shall not be reinstated until the cause for termination has been
cured. If a conference is timely requested, termination of service shall be deferred
until the conclusion of the conference. The determination of the town administrator
shall be final and administrative remedies shall be considered exhausted.
E.
In addition to all other charges as set forth in this title, a charge to be determined
by resolution of the board of trustees, as may be amended from time to time, shall
be assessed upon termination of water and/or sewer services for non-payment.
(Ord. No. 7-2011, § 3, 8-8-2011)
A.
Written notice of a proposed termination of trash collection and/or other utilities
provided by or billed by the town for non-payment shall be mailed to the last known
address of the customer as shown by the town's records thirty (30) days after the
account becomes delinquent. Delinquency shall be deemed to occur when payment is not
made by the due date. Delinquent accounts are subject to a penalty assessment of 1.5
percent per month of the unpaid monthly balance. The notice shall advise that trash
service and/or other utilities are subject to termination no later than forty (40)
days after the account becomes delinquent. No further notice shall be required prior
to termination of trash and rubbish collection service and/or other utilities.
B.
In addition to all other charges as set forth in this title, a charge to be determined
by resolution of the board of trustees, as may be amended from time to time, shall
be assessed upon termination of trash and rubbish collection and/or other utilities
for non-payment.
(Ord. No. 7-2011, § 3, 8-8-2011)
A customer or property owner who has had services terminated for non-payment of water
(including raw water), sewer, trash and rubbish collection, or other utility charges
may have service reinstated during regular business hours by paying at town hall all
accrued arrearages, including all delinquent and termination charges, and a reinstatement
charge to be determined by resolution of the board of trustees, as may be amended
from time to time. Payments received after four p.m. will result in service being
restored the next business day.
(Ord. No. 7-2011, § 3, 8-8-2011)
A.
The owner of the premises served shall be responsible to the town for the payment
of all water (including raw water), sewer, trash and rubbish collection fees and other
utility charges arising out of the provision of such services to the owner's property.
Such liability shall be joint and several with that of the customer and shall not
relieve the customer from his/her obligation of payment for services when the customer
is different from the owner.
B.
All water (including raw water), sewer, trash and rubbish collection fees and other
utility charges prescribed by this title, together with interest, penalty and reasonable
costs of collection, including attorney's fees, shall constitute a perpetual and continuing
lien upon the property to which water or sewer services are provided. The penalty
and interest shall be assessed at a rate equivalent to that which is assessed by state
law upon delinquent general property taxes. Such lien shall have priority over all
other liens except general taxes and shall, upon the town's certification and filing
with Garfield County, be collected and paid to the town by the county treasurer in
the same manner as delinquent general property taxes.
C.
For individually owned properties within multi-family residential structures and/or
multi-unit commercial structures to which water is furnished by means other than by
metering the consumption of each individually owned unit, the lien for unpaid water
and sewer fees, service charges, interest, and costs imposed by this section attaches
upon each unit in an amount computed by dividing the total amount of the lien by the
number of units served through the master meter.
D.
The town may enforce the lien against the property, or the personal liability of the
owner and customer, or both.
(Ord. No. 7-2011, § 3, 8-8-2011)
