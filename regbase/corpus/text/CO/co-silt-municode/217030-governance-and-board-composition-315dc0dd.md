A.
This chapter is enacted pursuant to C.R.S. §§ 31-25-101 through 31-25-107 (Urban Renewal
Law) and the Town of Silt Municipal Charter.
B.
The purpose of the Silt Urban Renewal Authority is to eliminate blight, foster economic
development, and improve public health, safety, and welfare within designated urban
renewal areas in the Town of Silt.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
There is hereby created a public body corporate and politic known as the "Silt Urban
Renewal Authority (SURA)."
B.
The SURA shall be a separate legal entity capable of contracting, acquiring, and disposing
of property, and exercising powers necessary to carry out urban renewal projects.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
The SURA shall be governed by a board of commissioners composed of the following members:
1.
The seven members of the town board, term concurrent with their elected position;
2.
One representative appointed by the county within which the URA is located, term concurrent
with their elected position, or three years if appointed;
3.
One representative from each school district within which the URA is located, term
concurrent with their elected position, or three years if appointed;
4.
One representative of a Title 32 special district within which the URA is located,
term concurrent with their elected position, or three years if appointed;
5.
One at-large member appointed by the town board for a term of three years, if necessary
to maintain an odd number of members for voting purposes.
B.
The board shall elect officers annually, including a chair, vice chair, secretary,
and treasurer.
C.
Board members shall disclose conflicts of interest and abstain from voting where a
conflict exists.
D.
Meetings shall comply with Colorado Open Meetings Law (C.R.S. § 24-6-401 et seq.).
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
The SURA shall have the authority to:
A.
Conduct studies to identify blighted areas;
B.
Prepare and adopt urban renewal plans consistent with C.R.S. § 31-25-107 and HB 15-1348;
C.
Acquire and dispose of property in furtherance of urban renewal objectives;
D.
Execute contracts, borrow funds, and issue bonds or notes as permitted by law;
E.
Expend funds for removal of blight, infrastructure improvements, and economic development;
F.
Collaborate with public and private entities;
G.
Maintain records and report annually to the town board and the public.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
Plans must identify boundaries, goals, proposed projects, funding sources, and public
benefits.
B.
Before adoption or modification, the SURA shall negotiate with affected taxing entities
regarding tax increment financing (TIF) allocations.
C.
If negotiations fail, mediation must be pursued to reach an agreement.
D.
A public hearing must be held, with notice provided to affected taxing entities and
the public.
E.
Substantial modifications to plans require compliance with the same negotiation, notice,
and hearing procedures.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
The SURA shall conduct studies to document blighted conditions.
B.
Findings shall be filed with the town board and made publicly available.
C.
Blighted conditions may include physical deterioration, unsafe structures, inadequate
infrastructure, or economic obsolescence.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
The SURA may finance projects through grants, loans, TIF, bonds, or other lawful means.
B.
Any issuance of bonds or notes shall comply with applicable Colorado statutes and
town policies.
C.
Revenues generated from TIF shall be used solely for urban renewal purposes within
the designated area.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
The SURA shall maintain detailed financial and project records.
B.
Annual audits shall be conducted and submitted to the town board and made publicly
available.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
A.
The SURA shall operate in compliance with this chapter, C.R.S., and HB 15-1348.
B.
The town board may review and ensure SURA compliance with statutory authority and
procedural requirements.
( Ord. No. 13-25 , § 1, 11-24-2025)
