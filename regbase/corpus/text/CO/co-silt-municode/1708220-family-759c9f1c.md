"Family" means any individual, or two (2) or more persons related by blood or marriage
or between whom there is a legally recognizable relationship, or not more than two
(2) unrelated adults and their related children; or a group of not more than four
(4) unrelated adults occupying the same dwelling unit on a continuous basis for a
period of time greater than one (1) month.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(10))
