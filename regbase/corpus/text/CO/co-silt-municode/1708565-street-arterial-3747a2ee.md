"Street (arterial)" means a roadway which provides direct year-round connection to
another jurisdiction, or which links such roads, and is intended or used primarily
for free-flowing traffic movement. Traffic velocity is generally greatest on arterial
streets, due primarily to roadway design.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
