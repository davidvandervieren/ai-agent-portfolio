"Antenna reception or transmission device" means a structure or device for the reception
and/or transmission of aerial or satellite signals, including television signals,
AM or FM radio signals, telemetry signals, data communication signals using free air
space as a medium, whether for commercial or private use, but excluding any device
that is less than one (1) meter in diameter or diagonal measurement, and excluding
an antenna that is designed solely to receive television broadcast signals.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
