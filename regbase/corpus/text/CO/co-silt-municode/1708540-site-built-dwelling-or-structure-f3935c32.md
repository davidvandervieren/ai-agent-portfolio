"Site-built dwelling" means a dwelling that is fabricated primarily on the job site,
and may only include wall panels and trusses fabricated elsewhere, provided that the
wall panels and trusses are available for on-site inspection by the building inspector
prior to installation into dwelling. The design and construction of these homes must
be in conformance with the building code as adopted by the town.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
