"Lot of record" means a lot which is a part of a subdivision, a plat of which has
been recorded in the Garfield County, Colorado recorder's office, or a lot described
by other legal means, the description of which has been so recorded.
(Ord. 3-05 (part); Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(16))
