§§ 2.08.010—2.08.350, and enacted a new Ch. 2.08 as set out herein. The former Ch.

A municipal court in and for the town is created and established, pursuant to the
Home Rule Charter of the Town of Silt and is governed by the provisions of Article
10 of Title 13, Colorado Revised Statutes, as amended.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The municipal court shall have original jurisdiction of all cases arising under ordinance
provisions of the town. The municipal court shall have full power to punish violators
thereof by the imposition of such fines and penalties as are required by resolution
or court rule.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 3-2021, § 2, 4-12-2021)
The procedures of the municipal court shall be in accordance with the Municipal Court
Rules of Procedure, as promulgated by the Colorado Supreme Court. The presiding municipal
judge shall have the authority to issue local rules of procedure consistent with any
rules of procedure adopted by the Colorado Supreme Court.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
There shall be regular sessions of the municipal court for trial of cases. The municipal
judge may hold a special session of court at any time.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
A.
All sessions shall be open to the public. Where the nature of the case is such that
it would be in the best interest of justice to exclude persons not directly connected
with the proceeding, the municipal judge may order that the courtroom be cleared of
all persons not directly connected with the proceeding. The municipal judge may order
the general public to be excluded from any hearing, trial, or other proceeding involving
a child charged with any violation of the Town Municipal Code, or any other ordinance,
other than Title 10 "Vehicles and Traffic," on its own motion or the motion of any party, and if the
interests of the child so requires, and in such case, only such persons shall be admitted,
including persons whom the parents or guardian wish to be present, as having a direct
interest in the case or in the proceedings before the court.
B.
Any person failing or refusing to leave the courtroom after having been ordered by
the municipal judge to do so is in contempt of court, and upon conviction shall be
punished as provided in Section 2.08.130 .
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The court shall be presided over by a municipal judge appointed pursuant to the Town
Home Rule Charter. Additional judges may be appointed as may be needed to transact
the business of the court.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 5-2010, § 2, 6-14-2010)
The compensation of the judge shall be an annual salary in an amount set by the board
of trustees and shall be payable as other salaries to municipal employees.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
Before entering upon the duties of the office, a municipal judge shall make an oath
or affirmation that he or she will support the Constitution of the United States and
the Constitution of the state of Colorado, and the ordinances of the town, and will
faithfully perform the duties of the office.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The municipal court shall be a court of record, and the presiding municipal judge
shall provide for the keeping of a verbatim record of the proceedings and evidence
at trials by either a tape or digital recorder or stenographic means.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
Appeals from the municipal court shall be in accordance with the practice and procedure
provided by Sections 13-10-116 and 16-2-114 of the Colorado Revised Statutes.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
Except as is otherwise specifically provided in this chapter, the municipal court
of the town, its judges, clerk and employees are governed by all of the provisions
of Article 10 of Title 13, Colorado Revised Statutes, as amended, and the Municipal
Court Rules of Procedure promulgated by the Colorado Supreme Court.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
A.
The municipal judge shall have all judicial powers relating to the operation of the
municipal court, including the power to impose punishment for contempt of court.
B.
In addition to any other acts which may be deemed contempt of court, the municipal
judge shall have the authority to punish the following as contempt of court in accordance
with the provisions of Rule 107 of the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure:
1.
Failure to obey a subpoena properly served without adequate excuse.
2.
Failure to obey a summons for jury duty or to respond to inquiry from the court requesting
information necessary to choose a jury panel without adequate excuse.
3.
Failure of an attorney of record or party to appear before the court at a scheduled
appearance date without adequate excuse.
4.
Failure to obey a lawful order of the court without adequate excuse or other behavior
constituting contempt as provided in Rule 107 Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure.
C.
The court may impose punitive sanctions pursuant to Section 2.08.130 of the code.
D.
Nothing in this section shall prohibit the court from issuing a warrant for a person
who fails to appear. In all cases where a warrant is issued, a contempt citation may
be issued at the person's next appearance in court.
E.
This section shall not apply to failure of any person to appear for a traffic infraction.
Such failure to appear for a traffic infraction shall be governed by Chapter 10.05 of this code.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
Any person who is found to be in contempt pursuant to Section 2.08.120 shall, for each offense, be subject to a penalty not to exceed six months in jail
or a fine not to exceed the maximum fine in Chapter 1.12 of this code, or both such fine and imprisonment.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 3-2021, § 2, 4-12-2021)
The board shall establish the position of clerk of the municipal court; except, that
the municipal judge shall serve as ex officio clerk if the business of the court is
insufficient to warrant a separate full-time or part-time clerk.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
A.
It is unlawful for any person to violate his or her written promise to appear in municipal
court given to an officer upon arrest or issuance of a summons or notice for any violation
of the town's ordinances, other than noncriminal traffic infractions. Failure to appear
for violation of town ordinances, other than noncriminal traffic infractions, shall
be a separate ordinance violation. Any person who violates the provisions of this
section shall be punished by a fine in accordance with the then applicable penalty
assessment schedule of the town, as the same may be amended from time to time.
B.
No punishment shall be imposed upon a defendant who fails to appear or fails to satisfy
a judgment for any noncriminal traffic infraction, but judgment costs and interest
and other civil remedies may be assessed for failure to satisfy a judgment for noncriminal
traffic infractions, in accordance with the penalty assessment schedule of the town,
as the same may be amended from time to time; and the town shall report the judgment
to the State Motor Vehicle Division which may assess points against the defendant's
driving privileges and may deny an application or renewal of a defendant's driver's
license until the judgment and all other lawful costs are satisfied.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The clerk of the municipal court shall issue a subpoena for the appearance of any
witnesses in municipal court upon the request of either the municipality or the defendant.
The subpoena may be served upon any person within the jurisdiction of the court in
the manner prescribed by the rules of procedure applicable to municipal courts. Any
person subpoenaed to appear as a witness in municipal court shall be paid a witness
fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution annually, or more often as necessary.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
In sentencing or fining a violator, the municipal judge shall not exceed the sentence
or fine limitations established by ordinance or this code. The municipal judge may
suspend or defer the sentence or fine of any violator and place him or her on probation
for a period not to exceed one year.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The board of trustees finds that the consistent administration of justice, and enforcement
of the ordinances of the town will be promoted by the adoption of a penalty assessment
schedule establishing the fines to be imposed for certain stated offenses. The board
adopts the penalty assessment schedule adopted by resolution. Such penalty assessment
schedule may be amended from time to time, by the addition or deletion of offenses,
or by the change of the amounts of fines or costs by action of either the board of
trustees or the municipal court judge through adoption of court rules.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
In addition to any court costs or other costs imposed by this chapter, there shall
be assessed against each defendant, upon conviction, deferred judgment and sentence
or a finding of liability, a surcharge of ten percent of the amount assessed, which
surcharge shall be remitted to the town's general fund and designated for the purpose
of equipment purchases.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
A surcharge equal to 37 percent of the fine imposed for each violation of the town
code, including ordinance violations under the Model Traffic Code, in each municipal
court action resulting in a conviction, plea of guilty or no contest, or in a deferred
judgment and sentence, which municipal ordinance violation is charged pursuant to
town ordinances. All calculated surcharge amounts resulting in dollars and cents shall
be rounded to the nearest whole dollar. In the event a portion of the fine is suspended,
the surcharge levied shall be computed based upon the original fines regardless of
whether a portion of the fine has been suspended. The surcharges shall be paid to
the town by the defendant. The surcharge shall be in addition to any other fees, court
costs, and surcharges imposed by the court.
(Ord. No. 10-2017, § 1, 7-24-2017)
The municipal judge shall be empowered to assess costs against any defendant who is
found guilty or against whom judgment has been entered for violation of any ordinance
of the town, according to this code. A judge shall assess a defendant a fee when he
requests a trial by court or a trial by jury, or for other added administrative costs
that are created by the defendant's non-compliance with the requirements set by the
court. Such assessed costs are set by the board of trustees by resolution annually,
or more often as necessary.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
A.
In certain cases of alleged violations of the town's ordinances, at the discretion
of the town's prosecutor and police department, the entry into plea discussions with
the defendant may be appropriate considering the previous record of the defendant,
the defendant's employment, the defendant's attitude and the potential for rehabilitation,
and the facts and circumstances surrounding the charges filed against the defendant.
In such cases, the entry into a stipulation for a deferred judgment and sentence may
be an appropriate action.
B.
The board finds that certain unusual costs to the town are incurred when defendant's
desire to avail himself of the benefits of deferred judgments, including time expended
in plea discussions, preparation of additional court documentation, presentation thereof
to the court, administrative time, and monitoring the compliance and disposition thereof.
Accordingly, for each deferred judgment approved by the municipal court judge, the
costs imposed therefore shall be set by the board of trustees annually, or more often
as necessary.
C.
Nothing contained in this section shall be construed or applied to require any defendant
to enter into a deferred judgment. Each defendant accused of violating any ordinance
provision shall be entitled to trial as provided by law in the event that deferred
judgment shall not be freely and voluntarily agreed upon, and subsequently approved
by the municipal court.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
In sentencing a violator, the municipal judge is empowered to impose community or
useful public service, either in lieu of or in addition to any jail sentence, fine
or any other penalty permitted under the municipal code. In each instance in which
community or useful public service is imposed, the court shall require that the defendant
enter into the Silt Municipal Court Community Service Agreement and pay a fee as set
by the board of trustees by resolution annually, or more often as necessary. If the
defendant is unable or unwilling to enter into said agreement, the court shall utilize
sentencing alternatives other than community or useful public service.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
A.
In any action before municipal court in which the defendant thereto is entitled to
a jury trial by the constitution or the general laws of the state, and is charged
with an ordinance violation which may result in the imposition of imprisonment as
a possible penalty, shall be entitled to jury trial upon request. The jury shall consist
of three jurors unless a greater number, not to exceed six, is requested by the defendant.
B.
No defendant shall have a right to a trial by jury for any noncriminal traffic infraction.
C.
In the event that the defendant is charged with more than one traffic offense arising
out of the same incident and at least one of the charged offenses is a criminal traffic
infraction, as defined in Title 10 of this code, or it the defendant is charged with any other criminal violation arising
out of the same incident, the defendant shall have the right to demand and, if such
demand is in conformance with state law and this code, receive a trial by jury as
to all such offenses, which shall be consolidated for purposes of trial.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
For the purposes of this chapter, a defendant waives his or her right to a jury trial
or a court trial under Section 2.08.240 unless, within 20 days after arraignment or entry of a plea, he or she files with
the court a written jury demand, stating therein the number of jurors requested, and
at the same time tenders to the court a jury fee as set by the board of trustees by
resolution annually, or more often as necessary, unless the judge waives the fee due
to indigence of the defendant. If the action is dismissed or the defendant is acquitted
of the charge or if the defendant, having paid the jury fee, files with the court
at least 20 days before the scheduled trial date a written waiver of jury trial, the
town shall refund jury fee.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 5-2010, § 2, 6-14-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016,
§ 1, 11-28-2016)
At the time of arraignment for any criminal offense, the judge shall advise any defendant
not represented by counsel of the defendant's right to trial by jury, of the requirement
that the defendant, if he or she desires to invoke his or her right to trial by jury,
demand such trial by jury in writing within 20 days after arraignment or entry of
a plea, of the number of jurors allowed by law, and of the requirement that the defendant,
if he or she desires to invoke his or her right by jury, tender to the court within
20 days after arraignment or entry of a plea the jury fee as set by the board of trustees
by resolution annually, or more often as necessary, unless the judge waives the fee
due to indigence of the defendant.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 5-2010, § 2, 6-14-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016,
§ 1, 11-28-2016)
Juries shall be selected from a jury list as is provided for courts of record.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The town shall pay jurors a fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution annually,
or more often as necessary for each day of actual jury service. The town shall pay
each potential juror a fee as set by the board of trustees by resolution annually,
or more often as necessary, for each day of service on the jury panel alone.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010; Ord. No. 6-2016, § 1, 11-28-2016)
The municipal judge shall fully comply and abide with all conditions of employment
which may be imposed by the town administrator and also with all applicable Colorado
statutes, including the provisions of Article 10 of Title 13 , as amended.
(Ord. No. 1-2010, § 1, 2-8-2010)
The town administrator or his designee is authorized to use any lawful method of collecting
fines, fees, default judgments, personal recognizance bond forfeitures, civil penalties
or any other unpaid amounts due from any person assessed such sums by the municipal
court, including the reasonable cost of collection and interest at the statutory rate
for unpaid civil judgments. Reasonable costs of collection shall include, without
limitation, the fees and costs of the town attorney, private counsel, or of a collection
agency, but such fees and costs shall not exceed 18 percent of the amount collected
unless otherwise ordered by a court of law.
A.
Collections from the municipal court shall be approved and ordered by the judge at
such time that all other attempts to collect have failed.
(Ord. No. 7-2023, § 1, 11-27-2023)
