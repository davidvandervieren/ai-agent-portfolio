Editor's note— Ord. No. 3-2017, § 3, adopted Feb. 13, 2017, repealed former Ch. 2.12, §§ 2.12.010—2.12.070,
and enacted a new Ch. 2.12 as set out herein. The former chapter pertained to similar
subject matter. For prior history, see Code Comparative Table and Disposition List.
The board of trustees shall appoint a human relations director to undertake and fulfill
the duties provided under section 3-7 of the town charter, the town personnel policy
as may be adopted by the board of trustees, and such other duties as may be delegated
by the town administrator. The human relations director shall serve as either an at-will
employee or independent contractor and shall be subject to the supervision, control
and removal of the town administrator.
(Ord. No. 3-2017, § 3, 2-13-2017)
The board of trustees shall have the authority, by written resolution, to adopt and
enact personnel policies to promote the rules, regulations, and procedures applicable
to town officers and employees. The town's personnel policies shall cover any areas
which the board of trustees determines are appropriate, including, without limitation:
A.
Classification of employment subject to personnel rules and regulations;
B.
Personnel records;
C.
Recruitment and examination of personnel;
D.
Eligibility lists and appointments;
E.
Promotions;
F.
Reduction in force—Reinstatement;
G.
Removal, discipline, discharge, and appeal;
H.
Establishment and abolition of positions;
I.
Method of filling vacancies;
J.
Compensation;
K.
Holidays;
L.
Leaves;
M.
Dual employment and outside employment;
N.
Conflicts of interest;
O.
Resignation and retirement;
P.
Political activity;
Q.
Social media;
R.
Appeals.
(Ord. No. 3-2017, § 3, 2-13-2017)
