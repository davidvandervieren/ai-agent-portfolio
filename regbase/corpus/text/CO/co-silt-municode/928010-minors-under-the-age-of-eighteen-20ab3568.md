A.
Sale or Delivery to Minors. It is unlawful for any person to sell, serve, give away,
dispose of, exchange, deliver, or permit the sale, serving, giving or procuring of
any malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented malt beverage to or for any person
under the age of twenty-one years.
B.
Possession or Consumption by Minors. It is unlawful for any person under the age of
twenty-one years to possess or consume any malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented
malt beverage.
C.
Procuring by Minors. It is unlawful for any person under twenty-one years of age to
obtain or attempt to obtain malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented malt beverage
by misrepresentation of age or by any other method in any place where the same are
offered for sale.
D.
Public Consumption by Minors. It is unlawful for any person to have in his possession
malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented malt beverage in any store, in any
public place, including public streets, alleys, road, or highways, or upon property
owned by the state of Colorado or any subdivision thereof, or inside vehicles while
upon the public streets, alleys, roads, or highways, when such person is under twenty-one
years of age.
E.
Parent's Responsibility. It is unlawful for any parent or guardian knowingly or under
conditions an average parent or guardian should have knowledge of, to permit any person
under the legal drinking age of whom he or she may be a parent or guardian, to violate
Subsections C and D of this section.
F.
Services of Others Prohibited. It is unlawful for any person under the age of twenty-one
years to engage or otherwise use the services of any other person, whether for consideration
or not, to obtain malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented malt beverage for
such person under the age of twenty-one years.
G.
Unlawful Purchasing Prohibited. It is unlawful for any person, whether for consideration
or not, to knowingly obtain any malt, vinous or spirituous liquor, or fermented malt
beverage for any person under the age of twenty-one years.
H.
False Statement of Card Prohibited. It is unlawful for any person under the age of
twenty-one years to make false statements or to furnish, present or exhibit any fictitious,
false or altered registration card, identification card, or note or other document
in order to purchase or attempt to purchase any malt, vinous or spirituous liquor,
or fermented malt beverage.
I.
Affirmative Defenses. A defendant charged with any of the offenses described in subsections
A through H of this section is entitled to claim and prove the existence of affirmative
defenses as set forth in C.R.S. Section 18-13-122.
(Ord. 8, Series of 1993 § 2)
A.
Minors on Premises Prohibited. It is unlawful for any person under the legal drinking
age to enter, visit, frequent or be present in any establishment where malt, vinous
or spirituous liquors, or fermented malt beverages are sold for consumption on the
premises.
B.
Licensee Violations. It is unlawful for the licensee, proprietor, agent or employee
of any establishment having a license to sell malt, vinous or spirituous liquors,
or fermented malt beverages for consumption on the premises to permit or allow the
following:
1.
Persons under the legal drinking age to enter, frequent, visit or be present in any
such establishment;
2.
A licensee or proprietor of any establishment having a license to sell malt, vinous
or spirituous liquors, or fermented malt beverages for consumption on the premises
shall be legally accountable for the conduct of any agent or employee of said licensee
or proprietor who violates any provision of this section. The licensee or proprietor
shall be guilty of any offense created under this section if the offense is committed
by an agent or employee of such licensee or proprietor;
3.
It shall be an affirmative defense to any prosecution for violation of this section
if the licensee, proprietor, agent or employee of the licensed establishment can establish
by a preponderance of the evidence that he required the minor to exhibit a state of
Colorado operator's license, chauffeur's license, or identification card prior to
entry by such person into the establishment and such proof of age exhibited was fraudulent.
C.
Warning Display Required. Every licensee or proprietor of a place where malt, vinous
or spirituous liquors, or fermented malt beverages are sold, except for the holder
of a club license as defined in C.R.S. Section 12-47-112(l)(i), shall display at all
times in a prominent place near every entry way of the establishment a printed card
with a minimum height of fourteen inches and a width of eleven inches, with each letter
to be a minimum of one-half inch in height, which shall read as follows:
1.
For establishments selling malt, vinous or spirituous liquors, or fermented malt beverages
for consumption on the premises and not serving regular meals:
"WARNING
IT IS ILLEGAL FOR ANY PERSON UNDER TWENTY-ONE (21) YEARS OF AGE TO BE IN OR VISIT
THIS ESTABLISHMENT.
A FINE OR IMPRISONMENT MAY BE IMPOSED FOR THE VIOLATION OF THESE PROVISIONS PURSUANT
TO TOWN ORDINANCE."
2.
For establishments holding a hotel and restaurant license and selling malt, vinous
or spirituous liquors, or fermented malt beverages for consumption on the premises:
"WARNING
IT IS ILLEGAL FOR ANY PERSON UNDER TWENTY-ONE (21) YEARS OF AGE TO BE IN OR VISIT
THIS ESTABLISHMENT UNLESS HE IS ACTUALLY CONSUMING A MEAL SERVED BY THIS ESTABLISHMENT.
A FINE OR IMPRISONMENT MAY BE IMPOSED FOR THE VIOLATION OF THESE PROVISIONS PURSUANT
TO TOWN ORDINANCE."
D.
Exceptions. Nothing contained in subsections A and B of this section shall prohibit:
1.
Minors from entering the dining room portion of a licensed establishment for the consumption
of food, when the dining room portion of said establishment is separated from the
bar portion of the establishment by a complete physical barrier;
2.
Minors from entering or remaining in the restaurant portion of an establishment, holding
a hotel and restaurant liquor license or fermented malt beverage license for consumption
on the premises, for the limited purpose of consuming meals actually and regularly
served; provided, that a minor must leave the premises after consuming the meal;
3.
Minors from passing through the bar portion of an establishment for necessary ingress
and egress to and from restrooms;
4.
Owners or necessary maintenance employees from being in the establishment which they
own or where they work;
5.
Minors between the ages of eighteen and twenty-one years who are members of an entertainment
group paid or employed by the licensee from being present in the licensed establishment
during the period of time they are actually working or performing.

This chapter shall be known as the Silt Noise Control Ordinance, and its purpose is
to regulate and limit noise within the Town of Silt to ensure a peaceful, healthy
environment for residents, businesses, and visitors, in accordance with the authority
granted under C.R.S. Section 31-15-401 and C.R.S. Section 25-12-103.
( Ord. No. 7-2025 , § 1, 7-28-2025)
For the purpose of this chapter, the following definitions apply:
A.
Amplified sound means any sound projected or transmitted by artificial means including loudspeakers,
microphones, bullhorns, amplifiers, or similar devices intended to increase volume
or project sound beyond its natural level.
B.
Construction noise means any generated in connection with construction, demolition, excavation, alteration,
repair, or maintenance of buildings, structures, roadways, or utilities, including
the operation of power tools, heavy equipment, compressors, generators, and other
machinery.
C.
Decibel (db) means a unit for measuring the intensity of sound.
D.
Dba means the A-weighted sound level in decibels, adjusted to reflect the human ear's
response.
E.
Excavation equipment refers to the heavy machinery and tools used in construction, mining, and civil engineering
to dig, move, and remove earth, rock, or other materials from a site.
F.
Heavy machinery refers to large, powerful machines or equipment typically used in construction or
industrial work, including, but not limited to excavators, bulldozers, cranes, loaders,
dump trucks, compactors, forklifts, and graders.
G.
Industrial/utility zone means areas used for manufacturing, utilities, or other industrial operations.
H.
Commercial zone means areas designated for business or mixed-use development.
I.
Public space means parks, sidewalks, streets, and other areas owned or maintained by the Town.
J.
Receiving property line means the property boundary line of the property that is receiving the sound, typically
the boundary line of the nearest neighboring property where the sound is being measured.
K.
Residential zone means areas designated primarily for housing under the Silt zoning code.
L.
Sound level meter means a calibrated device used to measure sound intensity in decibels.
M.
Unreasonable noise means any sound that exceeds the maximum allowable levels.

A.
Permitted hours of construction noise. It shall be unlawful for any person to engage
in construction activities that generate any audible construction noise to the receiving
property line, including but not limited to construction noise, excavation equipment,
or heavy machinery, outside of the following hours:
1.
Monday through Friday: 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
2.
Saturday, Sunday and federal holidays: 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., except that the use
of excavation equipment and heavy machinery is prohibited.
3.
Prohibited hours: Any construction activity outside of the above time frame is prohibited
unless authorized by an exception, in the form of written permission from the town
administrator or designee.
4.
Exception: This provision shall not prohibit outdoor construction noise created by
travel upon public streets and highways, and emergency work by the town, its agents
and contractors or any public utility.
B.
Noise limits. Construction noise during permitted hours shall not exceed:
1.
Residential zones: 75 dBA measured at the property line;
2.
Non-residential zones: 85 dBA measured at the property line;
3.
Excavation equipment: 115 dBA measured at the property line;
4.
Impact Noises: Sounds that occur intermittently rather than continuously. In the B-I
Zone District, impact noises shall be permissible up to a level of ten dBA in excess
of the level stated in Section 9.16 from the hours of 7 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Saturday. Impact noise shall be
measured using the fast response of the sound level meter.
C.
Emergency work. Emergency construction work necessary for the protection of life,
health, or property (such as snow removal, utility repairs, or storm damage response),
is exempt from these restrictions.
D.
Exceptions/special requests. An exception may be issued for nighttime or Sunday construction
upon submission of a written application demonstrating necessity, including:
1.
Type of work;
2.
Equipment to be used;
3.
Justification for after-hours work;
4.
Proposed mitigation measures (e.g., noise barriers, mufflers).
E.
Enforcement. Violations of this section shall be subject to the same enforcement provisions
outlined in Section 9.16.080 , with penalties applied per incident.
