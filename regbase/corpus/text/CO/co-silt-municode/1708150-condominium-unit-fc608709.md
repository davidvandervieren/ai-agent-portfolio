"Condominium unit" means an individual air space unit together with the interest in
the common elements appurtenant to such unit, as defined and recognized by the Colorado
Common Interest Ownership Act. A condominium unit shall not include single-family
units with attached or detached additional dwelling units.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(5)(b))
