"Single-family dwelling unit" means a detached dwelling unit arranged, designed and
intended for occupancy of one (1) family upon one (1) lot, or a unit within a duplex
structure, as defined herein.
(Ord. 3-05 (part); Ord. 22-97 (part); Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(8))
