"Side yard setback" means an open, unoccupied space on the same lot with a building,
between the building and the side line of the lot, extending through from the front
building line to the rear yard. For those lots that do not have an easily distinguishable
side yard, the town administrator or his designee shall determine the location of
the side yard.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(45))
