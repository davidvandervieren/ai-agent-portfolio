"Variance" means a relaxation of the terms of this title where such variance will
not be contrary to the public interest and where, owing to conditions peculiar to
the property and not the result of the actions of the applicant, a literal enforcement
of this title would result in unnecessary hardships or practical difficulties, as
defined by this code and by state statutes.
(Ord. 3-05 (part): Ord. 13-80 § 13.02(41))
