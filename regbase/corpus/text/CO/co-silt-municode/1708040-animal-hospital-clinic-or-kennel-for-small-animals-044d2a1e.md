Animal hospital, clinic or kennel for small animals means a professional business
providing veterinary or accommodations for small domestic animals not exceeding one
hundred and fifty pounds. Such an establishment shall have no outside runs and does
not allow the breeding of animals.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
