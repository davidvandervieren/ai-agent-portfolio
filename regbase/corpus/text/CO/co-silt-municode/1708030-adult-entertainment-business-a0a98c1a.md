"Adult entertainment business" means adult arcade, adult bookstore, adult cabaret,
adult motel, adult motion picture theater, adult novelty store, model studio, peep
booth, or sexual encounter establishment as described in Chapter 17.74 . Each of these individual enterprises shall constitute a separate adult entertainment
business even if operated in conjunction with another adult entertainment business
at the same establishment.
(Ord. 3-05 (part))
