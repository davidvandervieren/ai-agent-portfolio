It is a nuisance and, as such, unlawful for any solicitor, peddler, hawker, itinerant
merchant or transient vendor of merchandise who does not have a license therefor from
the town to enter in and/or upon any private residence or private residence premises
within the town limits for the purpose of soliciting an order or orders for the sale
of goods, wares and merchandise, and/or for the purpose of disposing of and/or peddling
or hawking goods, wares or merchandise.
(Ord. 18-80 § II (part): Gen. Ords. Art. VIII § 2)
The town may issue a citation for a violator of Chapter 5.16 (transient merchants) in accordance with Chapter 1.12 of this code.
(Ord. No. 6-2016, § 3, 11-28-2016)
