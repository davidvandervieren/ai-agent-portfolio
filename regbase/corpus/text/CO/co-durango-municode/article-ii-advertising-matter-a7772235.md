It shall be unlawful for any person to post, paint, tack or otherwise attach any notice
or other advertising matter to any fence, wall or building or other property until
first obtaining the consent of the owner of such property. It shall be unlawful for
any person to post, paint, tack or otherwise attach any notices or advertising matter
to any telephone, utility or other such poles.
(Ord. No. 1992-28, § 1, 12-15-92)
