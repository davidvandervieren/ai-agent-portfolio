We, the undersigned members of the Rifle Charter Convention, elected under Article
XX of the Constitution of the State of Colorado, certify that the foregoing is the
proposed Charter as drafted and approved by the members of the Charter Convention
for submission to the people of Rifle at a special election to be held on the 16th
day of July, 1963.
Adopted in Convention this 23rd day of May, 1963, and signed in triplicate.
A. A. Swan, Secretary E. P. Brown, President Dan F. Cameron Edward M. Doak, Vice-President Vel L. Carleton Gaylord Henry Raymond J. Cole Wm. Atha Mason Lesley Estes F. M. McCluskey Jack H. Goodrich Carl C. McMurray Claude A. Graham Ted M. Peppiatt M. James Hall Richard Sarten Dallas V. Hamill John B. Scalzo H. H. Hawxhurst Charlie E. Wood Vern E. Shoup, Sr.
