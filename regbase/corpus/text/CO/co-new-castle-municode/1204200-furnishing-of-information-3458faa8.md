It is unlawful for any person, other than an officer or employee of the town in the
course of his or her employment, to undertake any construction or repair within, to
dig up, open or excavate, or cause to be dug up, opened or excavated, any street,
alley, sidewalk or other public right-of-way within the incorporated limits of the
town without first having secured a permit therefor from the town. Any person in violation
of this section shall be subject to a daily fine in the amount of fifty dollars ($50.00).
(Prior code § 10-05-010)
Application for a permit to construct, repair or excavate, required by this chapter,
shall be made upon a form provided by the town. The application shall recite specifically
the exact address or location, date, duration, and type of work to be performed, and
the purpose for which the privilege is requested. Work in a public right-of-way shall
not take place until the applicant has received a permit from the town. Prior to the
commencement of work under the permit, the applicant shall notify town hall of the
permitted work, one business day prior to the commencement.
(Prior code § 10-05-020)
No permit to construct, repair or excavate any street, alley, sidewalk or other public
right-of-way shall be issued unless the applicant for such permit has first paid to
the town a required fee in an amount set by the town's fee schedule. Any application
for excavation of asphalt or concrete must include a deposit as set by the town's
fee schedule. The deposit shall be refundable upon completion and inspection by the
town under Section 12.04.180 . Utility companies franchised in the town or registered with the public utilities
commission shall obtain the permit referenced by this chapter but will not be required
to pay the application fee and may substitute the deposit with a bond, letter of credit
or some other method of security approved by the town.
(Ord. 2007-11 § B(10))
The town may grant permits to undertake any construction or repair within, dig up,
open or excavate, or cause to be dug up, opened or excavated, any street, alley, sidewalk
or other public right-of-way in the town to the following persons:
A.
Any person possessing, by ordinance, resolution or contract of the town council, general
or special power to excavate in, or perform other work as aforesaid in or upon, the
streets, alleys, sidewalks or other public rights-of-way; or
B.
Any person filing an application which possesses a valid contractor's license issued
by the town and license classification pertaining to construction, repair, or excavation
work. Any permit issued under this chapter is, in its complete discretion revocable at will
by the town. Upon revocation, the permittee shall, at his or her own expense, remove
all improvements authorized by the permit.
(Prior code § 10-05-040)
The permit required shall be kept at the site of the construction, repair or excavation
while the work is in progress and shall be exhibited upon request to any police officer
or other authorized representative of the town. All work requiring a permit pursuant
to the provisions of this chapter shall be subject to inspection at all times by the
town engineer or other authorized town representative.
(Prior code § 10-05-050)
All permits issued under this chapter shall be issued according to the provisions
of this chapter and subject to such rules, directions and limitations as the town
prescribes in the approved permit regarding the time required for the work, the scope
of the work, and the manner in which the work is to be performed. All work done under
such permits shall conform to specifications identified in the town Public Works Manual
and any accepted project documents.
(Prior code § 10-05-060)
Permits shall be conditioned to ensure that all work performed under such permits
is in accordance with the rules and regulations of the town, providing for the proper
care and protection of the streets, alleys, sidewalks and other public places of the
town and persons and property thereupon. No work shall be performed in such manner
as to prohibit access by emergency vehicles to any building, structure or dwelling
unit abutting the street or public right-of-way.
(Prior code § 10-05-070)
The town shall keep a record of all applications made for construction, repair or
excavation permits and of any permits issued.
(Prior code § 10-05-080)
All permittees must possess a valid contractor's license and classification appropriate
for completion of the proposed work, and must demonstrate proof of adequate liability
insurance coverage for any work undertaken.
(Prior code § 10-05-090)
It is unlawful to obstruct more than the space of one block and one intersection at
the same time in any one street, or to keep the same blocked for more than three days.
In the event of unforeseen extenuating circumstances, an applicant may apply for an
extension of the three-day limitation. The town, after investigation of the extenuating
circumstances, may grant such extension provided that valid reasons and circumstances
exist and justify the extension. One lane of traffic in each direction of travel must
remain open at all times unless prior approval is granted by the town.
(Prior code § 10-05-100)
It is unlawful for any person to dig or cause to be dug any hole, drain, ditch or
any other excavation in any street, alley, sidewalk or other public place within the
town without providing sufficient warning lights during the nighttime, to be placed
with a suitable barricade or temporary fence around such hole, drain, ditch or other
excavation, in order to prevent persons, animals and vehicles from sustaining injury
or damage. During the daytime, the barricade shall be maintained, but warning lights
are not required.
(Prior code § 10-05-110)
Every construction repair or excavation shall maintain traffic control in conformance
with the Manual of Uniform Traffic Control Devices ("MUTCD"), in order to minimize
the disruption of the flow of traffic in the vicinity of the excavation. All construction,
repair or excavation work shall be properly barricaded with adequate warning lights
or other safety appliances necessary for protection of the public, at the sole expense
of the applicant. Failure to provide and maintain proper traffic control may result
in immediate suspension of a permit issued under this chapter.
(Prior code § 10-05-120)
Any cut in any pavement or surfacing shall be made in a neat and workmanlike manner,
with straight lines, square edges and square corners, and shall be made with a pavement
saw six inches back from each side of the excavation. All excess material taken from
the cut and excavation shall be trucked away and disposed of by the holder of the
permit at the time the cut and excavation are made.
(Prior code § 10-05-130)
Upon completion of any excavation work, each excavator shall immediately backfill
excavations. All excavated material shall be removed, and backfill shall be of select
material meeting the specifications established by the town Public Works Manual. The
plans and specifications set out herein may be modified by the town so that they may
be specifically adapted to the particular conditions of travel, load requirements,
terrain, subsoil and moisture where the backfill is to be effected.
(Prior code § 10-05-140)
All work performed shall be warranted by the applicant for a period of one year from
the date of town approval and acceptance. Any work performed which is not in strict
conformity with this chapter shall, within ten (10) days after notice to the owner
or person who performed the work, be made to conform to this chapter at the expense
of the applicant or responsible party. Any work performed shall be warranted by the
applicant for a period of one year from the date of town approval and acceptance.
In the event of settlement or subsidence of a particular repair or excavation or part
thereof within one year of completion, the permittee who performed the work shall
be responsible for all costs occasioned by such settlement or subsidence. Any damage
to pavement due to work performed pursuant to a permit issued under the provisions
of this chapter shall immediately be repaired to the satisfaction of the town. In
the event the permittee fails to undertake such repair and repairing within thirty
(30) days, the town may in its discretion undertake such repair and repairing and
the permittee shall be liable to the town for the actual costs of such work including
reasonable attorney fees and costs of collection.
(Prior code § 10-05-150)
After construction, repair or excavation in a street, alley, sidewalk, or other public
right-of-way, pavement and surfacing shall be replaced by the contractor within seventy-two
(72) hours, in accordance with specifications provided by the town. Excavators in
paved or hard-topped streets shall take care to separate surfacing material from the
soil removed from such excavation.
(Prior code § 10-05-160)
The permittee shall remove all excess excavated material, boulders, barricades, signs,
supplies, equipment, rubbish, and debris from the work site within seventy-two (72)
hours. The work site shall be left in a neat and workmanlike appearance.
(Prior code § 10-05-170)
Upon completion of permitted work under this chapter, the permittee shall notify the
town of such completion. The town shall inspect the work site no later than two days
following notification of completion. The inspection shall determine whether the permitted
work conforms to the provisions of this chapter, and the town shall promptly notify
the permittee of any nonconformity. The town may utilize any funds deposited under
Section 12.04.030 to remedy any nonconforming work. All remaining deposit funds shall be immediately
delivered to the permittee.
(Prior code § 10-05-180)
Any person who undertakes work pursuant to a permit issued under the provisions of
this chapter or performs work under contract with the town or by permission obtained
from the town council in accordance with provisions adopted by the town council shall
be answerable for any damage occasioned to persons, animals or property by reason
of carelessness or negligence connected with such work, and shall indemnify and hold
harmless the town, any departments, divisions, employees, and agents thereof from
any and all liability as a result of such person's work in, or use of, the street,
alley, sidewalk or other public right-of-way.
(Prior code § 10-05-190)
It is the duty of every person, on request, to furnish the town with information regarding
the location in any street, alley, sidewalk or other public right-of-way of the town
of any pipe or other structure installed, maintained or utilized by such person.
(Prior code § 10-05-200)
The owner or occupant of any lot, tract, or parcel of ground within the town having
a sidewalk along or adjacent to any boundary thereof shall cause all snow and ice
to be removed from the sidewalk within twenty-four (24) hours from the time of the
last accretion of such snow or ice upon the sidewalk.
(Prior code § 10-06-010)
It is unlawful for any owner or occupant of any lot, tract, or parcel of ground within
the town, or for any agent in charge of such property, to allow any snow or ice to
accumulate or remain upon such sidewalk for longer than twenty-four (24) hours from
the time of the last accretion of snow or ice.
(Prior code § 10-06-020)
Every person convicted of a violation of any provision stated or adopted in this chapter
shall be punished by up to one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) in fines and/or up to
one year in jail.

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions shall apply:
"Items advertised for sale" means any item of any nature whatsoever, including, but
not limited to, motor vehicles, motorcycles, trailers, boats, furniture, appliances
or equipment, advertised with a visible sign posted on or next to such item declaring
such item to be offered for sale, lease, or rent. For purposes of this chapter, "items
advertised for sale" shall not include a motor vehicle or motorcycle lawfully parked
on a public street or right-of-way that happens to be advertised for sale in a newspaper
or by other means when there is no visible sign posted on or next to such item, nor
shall the term apply to any item being offered for sale pursuant to a lawfully obtained
vendor's permit issued by the town if such permit authorizes vending activities within
a public street or right-of-way.
"Trailer" means any wheeled device used or designed to be towed behind a motor vehicle
(excluding "travel campers" as defined below) for the purpose of hauling recreational
equipment (including, but not limited to, boats, off-road vehicles, and other motorized
or non-motorized recreational equipment), construction equipment, vehicles, retail
goods, or other items.
"Travel camper" means living or sleeping quarters on a wheeled device designed solely
for the purpose of being used as a mobile dwelling unit, such as, but not limited
to, campers, motorhomes, camp trailers, or travel trailers. Vans, camper-vans, pickups
with toppers, or any other vehicles less than twenty-five (25) feet which serve primarily
as a commuter vehicle or means of producing income are excluded from this definition.
"Truck" means any self-propelled motor vehicle having more than two (2) axles or exceeding
twenty-five (25) feet in length.

It is unlawful to park or store any truck, trailer, travel camper, or item advertised
for sale for more than two (2) consecutive hours on any public street or right-of-way
within the town limits, subject to the exceptions set forth below. This prohibition
shall also apply to public municipal parking lots unless such parking lot is expressly
made exempt from the prohibition.

The following exceptions shall apply:
A.
Delivery Vehicles and Passenger Buses. Trailers and trucks, including delivery vehicles
and passenger buses, may be parked on town streets and rights-of-way for loading and
unloading purposes only but shall under no circumstances be left unattended for a
period of more than two (2) hours at a given time.
B.
Attached Trailers. Trailers attached to or being towed by otherwise lawfully parked
motor vehicles may be parked on town streets and rights-of-way for up to forty-eight
(48) hours during any 15-day consecutive period. As soon as the trailer is detached
within the town, this exception shall not again apply to the particular trailer for
at least one (1) month, even if the trailer is later reattached during the month in
which it was detached.
C.
Travel Campers. Travel campers may be parked on town streets and rights-of-way for
up to forty-eight (48) hours during any 15-day consecutive period and solely for the
purposes of loading, unloading, charging, or cleaning the travel camper.
D.
Town Maintenance and Construction Vehicles. This chapter shall not apply to town-owned
maintenance or construction vehicles.
(Ord. 99-23 § 4)
(Ord. No. TC 2021-2, § 2, 7-20-2021)
The town administrator or designated town staff shall have the authority and discretion
to issue a temporary permit exempting any truck or trailer from the restrictions of
this chapter. Travel campers and vehicles advertised for sale are not eligible for
the temporary permit provided for in this section. The permit shall be valid for a
period of up to seven (7) consecutive days. In exceptional circumstances, a temporary
permit renewal may be considered and issued at the sole discretion of the town administrator.
The permit shall specify the location where the truck, trailer, or vehicle may be
parked and shall not authorize parking except in such designated area. The permit
shall at all times be visibly displayed on or in the truck, trailer, or vehicle. The
initial permit shall be issued free of charge. The town administrator shall have the
authority to delegate his or her authority under this section as he or she deems appropriate.
(Ord. 99-23 § 5)

The first violation of this chapter shall carry a fine of up to twenty-five dollars
($25.00). The second violation by the same truck, trailer, or item advertised for
sale shall carry a fine of up to fifty dollars ($50.00). The third violation shall
carry a fine of up to one hundred dollars ($100.00), and any violation thereafter
shall carry a fine of up to one hundred fifty dollars ($150.00). Each day that the
violation continues shall be considered a separate violation.
Additionally, upon a third violation or more, or whenever a truck, trailer, or item
advertised for sale has been left on a public street or right-of-way of the town for
a period of more than seventy-two (72) consecutive hours, such truck, trailer, travel
camper, or item may be towed and impounded by or at the direction of the police department
or the town administrator. In the case of towing and impoundment of any motor vehicle
or trailer pursuant to this section, the town and/or the police department shall follow
the procedures set forth in Part 18, Article 4, Title 42, C.R.S., as now existing
or as hereafter amended, relating to abandoned motor vehicles and shall treat the
motor vehicle or trailer as an abandoned motor vehicle for the purposes of such procedures.
In the case of other items advertised for sale that are not motor vehicles or trailers
and are impounded pursuant to this section, the town and/or the police department
shall follow the procedures relating to the disposition of unclaimed property set
forth in Chapter 3.24 of the New Castle Municipal Code.

Except for general public use as outlined in this chapter or other town authorized
activities, all town parks and open space areas shall remain free of encroachments
and adverse impacts from private improvements, including, but not limited to, fences,
landscaping/plantings, furniture, pet enclosures, yard debris and discarded materials.
It is unlawful for any person to place private encroachments, alter landscapes, conduct
any commercial or industrial activity in or on any town open space, park or public
place, except when the town issues a permit authorizing such activity, as set forth
below.
As used in this section, "industrial activity" means the private use of a park or
open space area for the purpose of, or otherwise connected to, conducting a principal
industrial use such as manufacturing, fabrication, extraction activities, or construction
activities.
A.
Applications for permits authorizing private encroachment, landscape alteration, commercial
activity or any service, product or activity for which a fee is charged shall be made
to the town administrator and shall contain the following:
1.
An application on form(s) provided by the town containing a narrative description
of the purpose of the request, nature of the use, duration, potential number of participants,
impacts and the location requested;
2.
A fee to cover the cost of maintaining the property and administering the permit,
which shall be established by resolution of the town council or, in the absence of
such a resolution, by the town administrator;
3.
Proof of a business or vendor's license when such proof is required by the municipal
code;
4.
A site plan generally showing the area of use and any temporary facilities, including
a depiction of any required public safety and/or traffic control issues;
5.
A description of facilities required such as trash receptacles, temporary shelters,
toilets, barricades, traffic control, public safety facilities or impacts, and similar
items;
6.
Provisions for cleanup, restoration of the area to its original condition and repair
of any damage. The town administrator may require posting of a deposit to ensure that
cleanup is completed and/or damage is repaired in a timely fashion;
7.
If deemed necessary by the town administrator, provisions for adequate liability insurance
with an endorsement naming the town as a co-insured party in such amount as the town
administrator may reasonably specify depending on the type and size of the event;
8.
As applicable, the applicant shall execute an agreement indemnifying the town and
holding the town harmless against any and all damages to property or persons resulting
from the event or encroachment;
9.
As deemed necessary by the town administrator, execution of an agreement in a form
acceptable to the town attorney to pay costs incurred by the town associated with
the event, including, but not limited to, police services, public works services,
fire services, ambulance services, and other administrative costs associated with
approving and/or serving the event; and
10.
Any additional information that may be required by the town administrator.
The town administrator has the discretion to refer any application made pursuant to
this section to the town council for additional review and public input. Such additional
review may be either a public meeting or may be a public hearing. The applicant shall
bear the costs associated with any required public notice.
B.
Industrial activities are prohibited in town parks and open space, unless a permit
is issued by the town council. Anyone wishing to conduct an industrial activity within
a park or open space area shall submit an application to the town administrator, and
the application and review shall be subject to the same criteria and potential conditions
found in subsection A of this section. The application will then be referred to the
town council for review at the next available regular town council meeting, unless,
in the discretion of the town administrator, the application should be made subject
to the public hearing notice provision(s) of this code. In its review of the application,
the town council may impose such additional conditions and/or restrictions as the
town council deems appropriate in order to mitigate any and all adverse impacts of
the proposed use, including the posting of adequate security, in a form acceptable
to the town, to return the land to its previous condition. The applicant shall be
responsible for any and all costs associated with the processing of the application,
including any costs associated with required public notice.
C.
The town administrator shall have the authority to promulgate regulations and to issue
written permits reserving park lands and structures within park lands. When no permit
has been issued, the use of such areas shall follow generally the rules of first-come
first-served.
