The R-2 residential zone district shall be governed in conformity with the following
regulations.
(Prior code § 13-04-050 (part))
This R-2 district is established to provide moderate density for single-family neighborhoods
within the town.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(A))
It is the town's goal to conserve the character and heritage values of the town and
western Colorado atmosphere along with ample open space and neighborhood parks, and
to avoid congestion in the streets.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(B))

Minimum lot area shall be as follows:
A.
Single-family: seven thousand five hundred (7,500) square feet;
B.
Multifamily: four thousand (4,000) square feet per unit.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(E))
Minimum floor area shall be nine hundred (900) square feet per unit.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(F))
Minimum yard setback shall be as follows:
A.
Front yard: twenty (20) feet;
B.
Rear yard: ten (10) feet;
C.
Side yard: ten (10) feet;
D.
Between structures (accessories): ten (10) feet.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(G))
Maximum building height shall be thirty (30) feet.
(Prior code § 13-04-050(H))
