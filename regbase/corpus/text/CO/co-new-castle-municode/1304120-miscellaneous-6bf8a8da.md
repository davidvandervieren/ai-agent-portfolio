A.
The town is a statutory town authorized by Article 15, Title 31, C.R.S., as amended,
and Article 35, Title 31, C.R.S., to provide water and sewer activities, services,
and facilities within and without the municipal boundaries of the town;
B.
Such water and sewer activities include but are not limited to the acquisition, diversion,
storage, carriage, delivery, distribution, collection, treatment, use, reuse, augmentation,
exchange, or discharge of water and wastewater services and facilities related thereto
(hereinafter "water activities");
C.
The town has historically conducted and operated such water activities as a government-owned
business of the town, providing services in exchange for the payment of user fees
and charges;
D.
The town desires to establish and confirm a water activity enterprise in conformance
with the provisions of Article 45.1, Title 37, C.R.S. ("Act"), with the power to issue
its own revenue bonds for the financing of water and sewage facilities, in order to
exclude the enterprise from the provisions of Section 20, Article 10, of the Colorado
Constitution ("TABOR"); and
E.
The town's water activities will thereafter be operated and maintained as an enterprise
within the meaning of TABOR, and which enterprise shall be separate and distinct from
the governmental activities of the town.
(Prior code § 11-02-010)
There is established within the town, a water and sewer enterprise to be operated
and maintained as a government-owned business, so as to exclude its activities from
the application of TABOR, in accordance with the terms set forth herein.
(Prior code § 11-02-020)
The town water and sewer enterprise (hereinafter "enterprise") shall manage, operate,
use, maintain, and conduct all water, wastewater, and storm water activities, services,
and facilities of the town. The enterprise is authorized to use, operate, improve,
extend, enlarge, repair, replace, acquire, dispose of, encumber, contract with respect
to, and otherwise control and supervise all water activity facilities and property
of the town. The enterprise itself shall be wholly owned by the town. All facilities,
properties, and assets which are utilized by the enterprise or form part of the enterprise
shall be the property of the town, and upon the termination of the enterprise, shall
be returned to the town.
(Prior code § 11-02-030)
All water activities shall be conducted as a single enterprise. The enterprise may
conduct one or more water activities, including contracting with other governmental
entities, enterprises, or nongovernmental entities or persons. The town council may,
from time to time, establish other water activity enterprises, and restructure any
water activity enterprise as a separate enterprise.
(Prior code § 11-02-040)
The town council shall serve as the governing body of the enterprise, and the officers
of the town council and of the town shall serve as officers of the governing body
and the enterprise. The governing body shall conduct the business of the enterprise
in the same manner and follow the same procedures as the town council. All public
business of the enterprise shall be conducted only during regular or special meetings
of the governing body at which a quorum is present. The record of proceedings of the
governing body shall be considered as actions and business of the enterprise. All
business of the enterprise and actions of the governing body shall be governed by
and made subject to all requirements, privileges, immunities, protections, limitations,
and other provisions of law.
(Prior code § 11-02-050)
The governing body of the enterprise may, without limitation, exercise the town's
legal authority relating to water activities or otherwise available to any enterprise,
except as expressly provided herein. Such authority shall include all powers set forth
in the Act, and those powers set forth in this code which are consistent with the
authorities of an enterprise under the provisions of TABOR and necessary to operate
the enterprise, including but not limited to the following:
A.
To hold meetings concurrently with regular or special meetings of the town council;
B.
To adopt ordinances in the manner in which town ordinances may be adopted;
C.
To issue or reissue revenue bonds, notes, or other obligations payable from revenues
derived or to be derived from the provision of services in the same manner in which
town revenue bonds may be issued, without voter approval in advance;
D.
To pledge any revenues of the water and sewer systems and service to the payment of
such revenue bonds and to pay such revenue bonds therefrom;
E.
To set rates, fees, and charges for services provided by the enterprise;
F.
To enter into contracts relating to this water and sewer system service in the manner
in which town contracts may be entered into;
G.
To make representations, warranties, and covenants on behalf of the town, and to bind
the town to perform any obligation relating to the water and sewer system and service,
other than any multiple-fiscal year direct or indirect debt or other financial obligation
of the town, without adequate present cash reserves pledged irrevocably and held for
payments in all future years.
The powers and authority specifically conferred herein shall not modify, limit, or
restrict the powers conferred on the enterprise by any other law directly or indirectly
affecting the water and sewer activities of the enterprise, except as expressly provided
herein. The enterprise shall have no power that adversely affects the status of the
enterprise for purposes of the application of TABOR.
(Prior code § 11-02-060)
In no event shall the enterprise have the power to levy or assess any tax that is
subject to TABOR or to direct the town to exercise its taxing powers on behalf of
the enterprise.
(Prior code § 11-02-070)
The enterprise shall not accept or receive any revenue in grants (as defined in the
Act) from any state or local government, unless expressly authorized by the town council.
(Prior code § 11-02-080)
All contracts relating to water activities shall be approved by the governing body
of the enterprises and executed by enterprise officers. Any preexisting contract relating
to sanitation activities shall be considered as having been approved by the governing
body. All contracts relating to water activities shall be implemented and discharged
by the enterprise, unless otherwise provided by the town council.
(Prior code § 11-02-090)
All revenue for water activity services and facilities provided by the enterprise,
including rates, fees, tolls, charges, payments for services to the town, and all
other income of the enterprise shall be collected, used, and expended for water activity
purposes as determined by the governing body in accordance with and as set forth in
the fiscal budget of the enterprise adopted pursuant to law. Rates for water activity
services and facilities provided by the enterprise shall be established by the governing
body, approved by the town council, and collected and enforced in accordance with
state law.
(Prior code § 11-02-100)
An enterprise fund shall be established to separately account for all revenue and
expenditures of the enterprise. The enterprise shall prepare an annual budget and
perform an annual audit that may be included in the budget or audit of the town. All
budgets, reports, audits, and financial operations of the enterprise shall conform
to and be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles applicable
to governmental (enterprise) units and other requirements of state law.
(Prior code § 11-02-110)
Nothing set forth in this chapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the
governing body or the enterprise to utilize other policies or procedures for operating
or continuing the enterprise in conformance with the Act and TABOR, except as otherwise
expressly provided herein. It is the intent of the town council to establish the enterprise
in conformance with the provisions of the Act and TABOR according to its most reasonable
interpretations thereof. The application and effect of this chapter with respect to
the enterprise shall be both prospective and retrospective to the effective date of
TABOR, unless otherwise determined by the town council. If any term, section, or provision
of this chapter shall be determined to be invalid or in violation of the enterprise
qualification provisions of TABOR or the Act, the invalidity or disqualification of
such section shall not affect any of the remaining provisions of this chapter. This
chapter shall remain in effect, whether or not the enterprise currently qualifies
as an enterprise pursuant to TABOR, until modified or repealed by the town council.
(Prior code § 11-02-120)
