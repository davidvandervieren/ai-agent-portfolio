The R-3 multifamily residential zone district shall be governed in conformity with
the following regulations.
(Prior code § 13-04-060 (part))
This R-3 district is established to provide a variety of moderate and high density
residential neighborhoods to blend with single-family districts and commercial districts.
This R-3 district also is established as a transitional residential use when butting
next to a commercial district. Such transitional area shall be within three hundred
(300) feet of the commercial zone unless otherwise approved by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-060(A))
(To be established.)
(Prior code § 13-04-060(B))
Permitted uses shall be as follows:
A.
Single-family dwelling;
B.
Multifamily dwelling (maximum ten (10) units);
C.
Bed and breakfast lodge (maximum eight units);
D.
Public park and open space;
E.
Home occupation;
F.
Family child care home;
G.
Public utilities;
H.
Accessory uses;
I.
Group home.
(Ord. 2003-17 § 2; prior code § 13-04-060(C))
(Ord. No. TC 2022-1, § 2, 1-18-2022)
Conditional uses shall be as follows:
A.
Multifamily dwellings (exceeding ten (10) units);
B.
Bed and breakfast lodge (exceeding eight units);
C.
Church;
D.
School;
E.
Day nursery;
F.
Museum;
G.
Municipal facilities;
H.
Fire station;
I.
Medical clinic;
J.
Community center;
K.
Utility substation;
L.
Accessory dwelling units associated with a primary single-family dwelling.
(Ord. 2003-16 § 4; prior code § 13-04-060(D))
Transitional special review uses shall be as follows:
A.
Hospital, medical or dental clinic;
B.
Lodge, hotel or motel;
C.
Personal service establishments, as follows: barber or beauty shop, laundromat, shoe
or boot repair shop, studio, tailor shop;
D.
Bank;
E.
Mortuary;
F.
Travel agency.
All requirements of this section are the same as C-2 zone district.
(Prior code § 13-04-060(E))
Minimum lot area shall be as follows:
A.
Single-family: six thousand (6,000) square feet;
B.
Multifamily (up to four units): three thousand five hundred (3,500) square feet per
unit;
C.
Multifamily (five units or more): three thousand (3,000) square feet per unit.
(Prior code § 13-04-060(F))
Minimum floor area shall be as follows:
A.
Single-family: nine hundred (900) square feet;
B.
Multifamily: eight hundred (800) square feet;
C.
Multifamily with two or more stories per unit: add four hundred fifty (450) square
feet per unit.
(Prior code § 13-04-060(G))
