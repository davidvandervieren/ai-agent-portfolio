Except as expressly modified below, the property is subject to all R-1 and supplemental
zoning regulations in the New Castle Municipal Code.
A.
Permitted uses:
1.
Multifamily dwelling(s);
2.
Common elements, including open space and parking;
3.
Limited common space, including fenced back yards;
4.
Public utilities;
5.
Structures without a subsurface foundation, including but not limited to portable
storage sheds and playground sets, may be placed in setbacks.
B.
Minimum floor area: one thousand (1,000) square feet per unit.
C.
Minimum yard setbacks (from each multifamily building):
1.
Front yard: twenty (20) feet.
2.
Side yard: three feet.
3.
Rear yard: twenty (20) feet.
D.
Maximum building height: thirty (30) feet.
E.
Minimum off-street parking: three spaces per unit.
F.
Minimum open space within the lot: width of the unit by twenty (20) foot rear yard.
(Ord. 2003-15 § 5)
