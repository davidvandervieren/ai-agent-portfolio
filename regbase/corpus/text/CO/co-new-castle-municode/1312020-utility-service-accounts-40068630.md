Before any utility service shall be supplied to any person, application for such service
shall be made to the town on a form to be provided by the town. In addition to such
information as deemed necessary by the town administrator, the applicant shall be
required to provide his or her social security number on the application. The application
shall advise the applicant of the town's right to pursue collection of past due amounts
through a third party.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 2 (part): prior code § 11-12-010)
A.
Utility service accounts shall be in the name of the property owner, and monthly billings
shall be mailed to the property owner. All rates, charges, tolls, fees and assessments
for town utility service shall be the responsibility of the owner or owners of the
premises served. The town shall not be bound by any agreement between an owner and
occupant concerning utility service payments, irrespective of whether the town has
been notified of such agreement. However, if the property is leased or rented, the
name of a tenant may be added to the account, and monthly billings may be mailed to
the tenant in lieu of the property owner, if: (1) the owner consents, in writing,
to add the name of the tenant occupying the property to the account and to have all
billings mailed to the tenant; and (2) the owner agrees, in writing, that the owner
shall remain ultimately responsible for the payment of all utility service rates,
charges, tolls, fees, and assessments.
B.
The town shall assess an administrative charge for adding or changing the name on
a utility service account, in an amount established by town resolution.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 2 (part): prior code § 11-12-020)
A.
Definitions. Unless the context specifically indicates otherwise, the following terms,
as used in this section, shall have the meanings hereinafter designated.
"By-pass" or "by-passing" means any pipe, tube, faucet or other instrument, device
or contrivance by which water may be transmitted, diverted, taken, or used, connected
to any line used to supply water to the premises in such a manner as to transmit,
divert, take or use any such water without passing through an authorized meter for
measuring or determining the amount of such water.
"Tamper" or "tampering" means damaging, altering, adjusting or in any manner interfering
with or obstructing the action or operation of any meter provided for measuring or
determining the amount of water passing through such meter, or the remote reading
device attached thereto.
"Unauthorized metering" means removing, moving, installing, connecting, reconnecting
or disconnecting any meter or metering device for water service by a person other
than a town employee.
B.
Water Meter Requirement.
1.
All connections to the town water system shall be metered. Water meters may be purchased
by the consumer from the town, or the consumer may purchase such meters and necessary
appurtenances meeting town specifications from private suppliers. All water meters
shall be installed in accordance with design and engineering standards established
by the town and shall be subject to inspection by the town. The meter or a remote
readout connected thereto shall be installed and maintained in a location that is
convenient to the town's meter readers.
2.
A separate meter shall be required for each service connection and for each dwelling
unit. When requested by a consumer, and if in the opinion of the town council, services
cannot otherwise be supplied except by an excessive investment in meters, service
may be supplied through a master meter and water delivered to and paid for by one
consumer and furnished to tenants as a part of the rent without specific charge therefor.
For the purpose of this section, a separate unit shall be considered a separate living
unit in each dwelling place.
3.
Water meters so installed shall become and remain the sole property of the property
owner. It shall be the duty of the water consumer to maintain the water meter upon
his or her service line in good operating condition and to replace the water meter
if damaged due to any act or negligence of the water consumer, his or her tenants,
employees, or agents of any kind.
C.
Reading of Meters. Town staff shall read the water meters at such times as the town
council requires, make proper record of each meter reading, and file same with the
town clerk.
D.
Charges Based on Estimate. Where a meter fails to register, registers intermittently
or inaccurately, or partially registers for any period, the town may make an estimate
of the quantity of water used based upon the best information available to it and
charge the consumer in accordance with such estimate.
E.
Denial of Access to Meter. In the event the consumer denies access to the water meter
or remote reading device, the town, at its option, may charge the consumer for water
use as a nonmetered user in accordance with Section 13.16.020 ; may move or relocate the water meter or remote reading device at the expense of
the consumer; or may disconnect the customer from the water supply system. The fee
for reinstalling or reconnecting a water meter shall be set by the town's fee schedule.
F.
Meter Testing. Upon the written request of a water consumer and a deposit to cover
the cost of making the disconnection and performing the test, the town will disconnect
a meter and test the same. The amount of the deposit shall be established by town
resolution. The deposit will be returned if the meter is found to be inaccurate to
the extent of more than two percent; otherwise, the deposit will be retained to cover
the costs of the test. Further, if the test shows the meter to be inaccurate to the
extent of more than two percent, the town will make a charge or allow credit, as the
case may be, in proportion to the error, against the most recent billing for metered
water service but not otherwise.
G.
Unlawful Acts. It is unlawful:
1.
For any person to install a by-pass without the express written authorization of the
town administrator;
2.
For any consumer knowingly to receive water service by means of a by-pass which has
not been authorized in writing by the town administrator;
3.
For any person to tamper with a water meter or other water utility equipment;
4.
For any consumer knowingly to receive water service by means of tampering;
5.
For any person to engage in unauthorized metering;
6.
For any consumer knowingly to receive water service by means of unauthorized metering.
Proof of the existence of any by-pass, tampering or unauthorized metering shall be
deemed prima facie evidence that the consumer had knowledge of the by-pass, tampering
or unauthorized metering if it is proved that the consumer controlled access to the
water meter or other utility equipment where the by-pass, tampering or unauthorized
metering is proved to exist.
H.
Penalties for Violation. By-passing, tampering, or unauthorized metering shall be
grounds for immediate disconnection of service without notice to the consumer at such
premises, and service shall not be reconnected until any and all deficiencies in piping,
connections, meters and/or water facilities of the premises have been repaired, corrected
or otherwise altered to conform to the requirements of all applicable ordinances,
rules and regulations and until the requirements of this chapter are met. In order
for water service to be reconnected to premises where by-passing, tampering or unauthorized
metering has occurred, the consumer shall pay the following charges to the town prior
to reconnection:
1.
A service charge, the amount of which shall be established by town resolution, calculated
to compensate the town for all reasonable expenses incurred on account of the by-passing,
tampering or unauthorized metering including, but not limited to, costs of investigation,
disconnection, reconnection and service calls;
2.
The cost of repairing or replacing any damaged utility equipment; and
3.
The actual or estimated user charges not previously billed to the consumer as a result
of the by-passing, tampering, or unauthorized metering.

All trash and garbage shall be collected and removed by employees of the town or by

A.
If any rates, charges, tolls, fees, or assessments for town utility service remain
unpaid for thirty (30) days after their due date, or if there has been a violation
of any rule or regulation concerning utility service as set forth in this title, the
town shall give written notice of such delinquency or violation to the owner of the
premises served and notice that utility service will be terminated if the delinquent
charges are not paid in full or if the violation does not cease by the twenty-fifth
day of the month in which notice is sent. Furthermore, charges unpaid for five days
after their due date shall accrue an additional charge of one percent for each month,
or part thereof, that the initial charge remains unpaid. If an owner does not pay
all delinquent charges by the twenty-fifth day of the month as provided in the delinquency
notice, the town may terminate the utility service to the premises served. The town
shall have the right to collect from any customer delinquent on their account all
legal, court, and other costs necessary or incidental to collection of any delinquent
amount, including postage, reasonable attorneys' fees, filing fees, and recording
costs.
B.
The delinquency notice sent by the town shall be sent by regular mail to the owner's
billing address and shall set forth the amount of delinquent charges and/or the rule
or regulation concerning utility service that has been violated. The delinquency notice
shall state that the customer may avoid further delinquent and/or administrative charges
and possible termination of service by paying the past due amount in full by the twenty-fifth
of the month.
C.
If the customer does not pay all delinquent charges by the twenty-fifth (25 th ) day of the month as provided in the delinquency notice, the town shall send a notice
of termination. The notice of termination shall be delivered by one (1) of the following
methods chosen by the town in its discretion: (i) sent by regular mail to the owner's
billing address; (ii) sent by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the owner's
billing address; (iii) posting in a conspicuous place at or near the main entrance
of the premises served by such utility service and addressed to the customer listed
in the town's records, the occupant of the premises served, and/or the owner of the
premises served. The notice shall state the amount of the delinquent charges and the
date that termination of services is scheduled. The town shall assess an administrative
charge for processing and preparation of the notice of termination. This administrative
charge shall be established by town resolution. The notice of termination shall include
the following information:
1.
That the customer is entitled to a dispute conference with the town administrator
before termination of service by requesting such conference, in writing, within ten
(10) days of the date of the notice of termination, and that the customer is entitled
to a public hearing, if properly requested, following the dispute conference with
the town administrator;
2.
That the customer may avoid termination of service by satisfying one of the following
requirements within ten (10) days of the date of notice of termination: (1) paying
the past due utility service charges in full; (2) entering into a written agreement
with the town to pay the past due charges in installments; or (3) ceasing violation
of the rule or regulation concerning utility service;
3.
That in the event there is a medical certification delivered to the town within ten
(10) days of the date of the notice of termination, the certification indicating that
termination of service would be especially dangerous to the health and safety of the
customer or a permanent resident of the customer's household, as such medical emergency
is defined in Section 13.12.110 (B), there will not be discontinuance of service for sixty (60) days from the date
of medical certification with a possible thirty (30) day extension upon delivery of
the second medical certification;
4.
A list of major federal, state or local governmental agencies known to the town which
provide customer assistance or benefits relating to utility service.

A.
Upon the timely request pursuant to Section 13.12.080 (C)(1) of a recipient of a notice of termination, the town administrator shall meet
and consult with the recipient of the notice within seven days after receipt of the
recipient's request to do so. If the town administrator is unavailable, the town treasurer/finance
director shall then have all powers granted in this section to the town administrator.
The town administrator shall meet with the recipient and shall be empowered to settle
the dispute with regard to utility service charges and violations of rules and regulations
of a utility; however, any utility service charges which were not disputed by the
recipient pursuant to Section 13.12.080 (C)(1) shall not be raised at this time as such disputes shall be deemed to have been
waived by the recipient. The town administrator shall issue a decision on the dispute
at such conference and shall inform the recipient that the recipient has the right
to appeal the decision of the town administrator to the town water and sewer enterprise
within three business days of such conference.
B.
If a recipient files a written notice with the town clerk that the recipient wishes
to appeal the decision of the town administrator rendered pursuant to subsection A
of this section within three business days of such decision, then the town clerk shall
set a hearing on the appeal on the agenda of the next meeting of the board of directors
of the water and sewer enterprise ("board"), and notify the recipient of the date,
time and place of the hearing. The town and the appellant may present facts or data
relevant to the dispute with or without the swearing of an oath. The board shall consider
the data and evidence before it and at such hearing shall affirm, modify or reverse
the decision of the town administrator. The decision of the board is final. If the
decision of the board is to order discontinuance of utility service, then such discontinuation
shall occur unless on the day of the decision or the following day, the utility service
charges are paid or the violation of a rule or regulation is corrected.
C.
In addition to the towns right to shut off utility service, the town may enforce the
owner's payment obligations by any and all other lawfully available means, including
suits for collection and/or foreclosure of the town's lien on the owner's property.
In any event, the town shall be entitled to recover all costs incurred in the collection
of unpaid payments, including but not limited to reasonable attorneys' fees, recording
fees, filing fees, and court costs.

Utility service shall not be discontinued:
A.
Between twelve noon on Friday and eight a.m. on the following Monday or between twelve
noon on the day prior to and eight a.m. on the day following any federal holiday or
town holiday;
B.
During any period when termination of service would be especially dangerous to the
health or safety of any residential customer or permanent resident of the customer's
household and such customer has established that he or she was unable to pay for the
service as regularly billed by the town. "Termination of service that would be especially
dangerous to the health or safety of the residential customer or a permanent resident
of the customer's household" means that the termination of service would aggravate
an existing medical condition or create a medical emergency for the customer or a
permanent resident of the customer's household. Such shall be deemed to be the case
when a physician licensed by the state of Colorado makes a certification thereof in
writing and such certification is received by the town. In the event a medical certification
is delivered to or received by the town, a nondiscontinuance of service as herein
prescribed shall be effective for sixty (60) days from the date of the medical certification.
One thirty (30) day extension on nontermination of service pursuant to this section
may be effected by delivery to and receipt by the town of a second medical certification,
as defined above, prior to the expiration of the initial sixty (60) day nontermination.
A residential customer may invoke the provisions of this section no more than once
during any period of twelve (12) consecutive months;
C.
In the event the customer at any time proffers full payment of any utility bill by
cash or certified funds to the town administrator;
D.
If the customer enters into a written agreement with the town to pay the past due
charges in installments; or
E.
If violations of rules or regulations concerning utility service have ceased.

With the property owner's written consent, any premises served by the town water or
sewer system may be temporarily or permanently disconnected from town utility service.

premises from town utility service, in amounts established by town resolution. Before
such disconnection, the property owner shall submit an affidavit under penalty of
perjury certifying that the premises are vacant and unoccupied and that they shall

disconnection, the property owner shall agree, in writing, to forfeit the water tap
and to be responsible upon reconnection for full payment of a new water tap for the
premises at the rate applicable at the time of the request for reconnection. Premises
that are temporarily disconnected shall be eligible for the reduced utility charges
described in Section 13.16.050 (A). Upon written request of the property owner and payment to the town of any past
due rates, charges, fees and assessments, and upon payment to the town of the reconnection
charge pursuant to the town fee schedule, the town shall reconnect utility service
to the property.
