As used in this chapter:
"Disabled person" means a person whose physical or mental condition prevents him or
her from performing substantial gainful work, which condition is expected to last
for twelve (12) months or more, or a person receiving social security benefits as
a disabled person.
"Equivalent residential unit" ("EQR") means a number related to the volume of water
used by a single-family residential unit housing a statistical average of three and
one-half persons and having not more than two thousand five hundred (2,500) square
feet of irrigated lawn or garden. The volume of water used by all other uses is considered
to bear the same ratio to the water use of an average single-family residence as the
EQR value assigned to such uses in the table of EQR's as set forth in Section 13.20.060 bears to the EQR value assigned to the single-family residence in such table.
"Residential unit" means one or more rooms in a building or portion thereof used for
residential occupancy, occupied by one family, living independently of any other family.
"Senior citizen" means a person sixty-five (65) years of age or older.
(Ord. 2002-6 § 2 (part): prior code § 11-14-010)
A.
Monthly water service charges for users other than "special users" identified in subsection
B below shall be computed based on the water usage and rates set forth in the town's
schedule of fees.
B.
Monthly water service charges for the following "special users" only shall be computed
based on the water usage and rates set forth in the town's schedule of fees:
1.
Users within town limits in owner-occupied residences where the owner is a senior
citizen or in residences where the tenant is a senior citizen, provided that the senior
citizen pays the water bill and is a co-signer on the water account;
2.
Users outside town limits in owner-occupied residences where the owner is a senior
citizen or in residences where the tenant is a senior citizen, provided that the senior
citizen pays the water bill and is a co-signer on the water account;
3.
Schools; and
4.
Vacant property qualifying under Section 13.16.050 (A).
C.
Water Meter Required. The rates for monthly water service charges are based on metered
water usage. Accordingly, all users of town water service are required to have water
meters equipped with remote readouts installed and maintained in good working condition
and in locations that are convenient to the town's meter readers, as determined by
the town in its sole discretion. Any such users without water meters installed and
in good working condition shall be in violation of this subsection, and, in addition
to any other remedies the town may have, the town may assess such users a surcharge
fee in addition to the applicable minimum monthly charge as follows:

Period | Surcharge Fee
For first month (or part thereof) without water meter | $118.00 per EQR per month
For second consecutive month (or part thereof) without water meter | $236.00 per EQR per month
For third consecutive month (or part thereof) without water meter | $472.00 per EQR per month
For fourth consecutive month (or part thereof) and each subsequent month (or part
thereof) without water meter | $590.00 per EQR per month

The foregoing surcharge fees will increase by three percent annually on first day
of January of each calendar year, beginning in 2020.
Nothing in this subsection shall relieve any user of the requirement to have and maintain
an operational meter in accordance with Section 13.12.030 .
D.
Broken Meters. In the event that a water user's meter becomes broken or ceases to
function, such user shall be charged an estimated amount based upon the amount of
water used by such user during the same month of the prior year, computed at the rate(s)
set forth in subsections A and B above. If no such data is available, or if the meter
remains broken or nonfunctional for three or more consecutive months, such user shall
be subject to the monthly surcharge fee and minimum monthly charge provided for in
subsection C above. Nothing in this subsection shall relieve any user of the requirement
to have and maintain an operational meter in accordance with Section 13.12.030 .
E.
Water Theft. Any person using town water without proper authorization or without the
payment of a tap fee shall be liable to the town for the use of such water at the
rate of ten dollars ($10.00) per one thousand (1,000) gallons. This fee shall be in
addition to any civil or criminal penalties otherwise provided by law.
F.
Bulk Water Rates. The town administrator shall have the discretion to permit water
tankers or other such vessels to be filled with town water on an occasional, temporary
basis. Such users shall be charged at the rate identified in the town's schedule of
fees.
G.
Repair and Maintenance Charge. Each water service customer shall pay the town four
dollars and twenty cents ($4.20) per EQR per month in addition to charges assessed
pursuant to Section 13.16.020 (A) and (B). Funds collected as a result of this charge shall be used by the town
for water plant and distribution system maintenance and operational expenses. Any
funds collected and not spent on water plant and distribution system maintenance and
operational expenses within the year collected shall be reserved. The reserved funds
shall be used only for any of the following purposes: (1) water distribution system
maintenance and operational expenses; (2) meter reading systems and associated equipment;
or (3) water conservation initiatives of the town.
H.
Potable Water Leaks. Potable water lost due to leaks under Section 13.20.130 is the responsibility of the property owner and as such will be charged in accordance
with the rate schedules in Sections 13.16.020 (A) and (B). In the event of a leak under Section 13.20.130 , a property owner may request a reduction in the charge for potable water usage resulting
from a leak. Said request shall include documentation evidencing the leak and showing
that the leak has been repaired. The town administrator or his or her designee shall
review the request, all documentation provided therewith, the last twelve (12) months'
water utility billing for the affected property, and any other relevant information.
Upon reviewing said information, and at the discretion of the town administrator or
his or her designee, a credit not to exceed the total amount of the second-highest
monthly bill for water usage during the preceding twelve-month period may be applied
to the account for the property experiencing the leak; provided, however, that in
no event shall a credit be applied unless the leak has been repaired. A property owner
may only apply for a credit related to a repaired leak once every twelve (12) months.

A.
Definitions. As used in this section:
"Summer billing" means that billing for sewer usage which occurs in the months of
April through October, inclusive. New Castle Town Council finds that water usage in
the period of April through October does not accurately measure use of the town's
sewer system due to outside water usage.
"Winter billing" means that billing for sewer usage which occurs in the months of
November through March, inclusive. New Castle Town Council finds that water usage
in the period of November through March most accurately measures use of the town's
sewer system. Sewer rates are set based on winter water usage.
B.
Calculation of Sewer Billing Charges.
1.
Single-family and multi-family residences with landscaping irrigated by the town's
potable water system shall be subject to different summer billing and winter billing
for sewer service. For such sewer service customers, monthly winter billing shall
be calculated using actual monthly water usage during each of the months in the winter
billing period, and monthly summer billing shall be calculated in the following manner:
a.
Ongoing Accounts. The monthly summer billing charge for ongoing accounts shall be
based upon an average of monthly water usage during the preceding winter billing period.
The average shall be calculated annually by the town.
b.
Change-Over Accounts. For any existing accounts changed from one customer to another,
until the new customer establishes a water usage history, the previous usage history
attributable to the former customer shall be used to calculate the average charge
to the new customer for purposes of assessing summer billing charges.
c.
New Accounts. For new accounts established during the summer billing period for which
there is no account history during the preceding winter billing period, the town administrator
or his designee shall establish a monthly service charge for the first summer based
upon an analysis of other water accounts which are similar in kind to that of the
new customer.
2.
Accounts Without Water Service. If a sewer service customer does not receive water
service from the town, the town administrator or his designee shall establish a monthly
service charge based upon an analysis of other water accounts which are similar in
kind to that of the sewer service customer without town water service.
3.
For all other sewer service customers, sewer billing shall be calculated using actual
monthly water usage year-round.
4.
Commercial users, apartment buildings, condominiums and other like users who have
lawn and/or landscaping which is irrigated with potable water are encouraged to install
a second water meter (secondary meter) to measure such irrigation usage only. Such
users who have installed a secondary meter under this subsection will have their summer
billing charges based on the primary meter reading minus the secondary meter reading
on a month by month basis.
C.
Potable Water Leaks. Potable water lost due to leaks under Section 13.20.130 is the responsibility of the property owner and will be charged in accordance with
the rate schedule in Section 13.16.030 (F). If the lost potable water does not enter the sewer system, as determined by a
representative of the town's utilities department, the lost amount will not be used
in the calculation of sewer usage for the billing period in which the town was notified
of the leak. In this instance, account prior history will be used to determine the
sewer charge for the applicable billing period. At the discretion of the town administrator
or his designee, the charge for sewer usage that is deemed to be caused by a potable
water leak entering the sewer system may be reduced by no more than ten (10) percent
of the total due for sewer usage during the billing period. Billing periods identified
as potable water leaks will not be used in the averaging calculation undertaken in
Section 13.16.030 (B)(1), in which instance prior billing history will be used.
D.
Accounts with Missing Meter Readings. Except as provided in Section 13.16.030 (B)(2), for any accounts for which there were no water meter readings during one or
more of the months in the preceding winter billing period (for sewer customers with
different summer and winter billing) or during one or more months of the year (for
all other sewer customers), the water usage for the month(s) for which meter readings
are unavailable shall be deemed to be the water usage that took place during the same
month(s) of the previous year. If no water meter readings for such previous year's
months are available for the account, then the town administrator or his designee
shall establish a water usage amount based upon an analysis of other water accounts
which are similar in kind to that of the customer.
E.
If water meter readings are unavailable as a result of the customer's water meter
breaking or ceasing to function, and if the meter remains broken or nonfunctional
for three or more consecutive months, the town may assess such users a surcharge fee
as follows:

Period | Surcharge Fee
For first month (or part thereof) without water meter | $118.00 per EQR per month
For second consecutive month (or part thereof) without water meter | $236.00 per EQR per month
For third consecutive month (or part thereof) without water meter | $472.00 per EQR per month
For fourth consecutive month (or part thereof) and each subsequent month (or part
thereof) without water meter | $590.00 per EQR per month

The foregoing surcharge fees will increase by three percent per year beginning January
1, 2020.
This surcharge fee for sewer usage shall be in addition to any surcharge fee charged
to the customer for water usage pursuant to Section 13.16.020 (C).
Nothing in this subsection shall relieve any user of the requirement to have and maintain
an operational meter in accordance with Section 13.12.030 .

b.

c.
Vacant property qualifying under Section 13.16.050 (A).
2.
Repair and Maintenance Charge. Each sewer service customer shall pay the town four
dollars and fifteen cents ($4.15) per EQR per month in addition to sewer service charges
set forth in the town's schedule of fees. Funds collected as a result of this charge
shall be used by the town for sewer maintenance and operations expenses. Any funds
collected and not spent on sewer maintenance and operations expenses within the year
collected shall be reserved. The reserved funds shall be used only for any of the
following purposes: (1) sewer maintenance and operations expenses; (2) meter reading
systems and associated equipment; or (3) water conservation initiatives of the town.
3.
South Sewer Surcharge. In order to offset additional costs of operation, maintenance,
and repair of the lift station and sewer force main installed in connection with the
River Park PUD (hereinafter referred to as the "River Park Lift Station and Sewer
Force Main"), a monthly user fee surcharge in the amount of six dollars ($6.00) per
EQR shall be imposed on all sewer service customers utilizing the River Park Lift
Station and Sewer Force Main. The enterprise shall review the amount of the surcharge
provided for under this subsection on reasonable intervals and shall adjust it, if
necessary, from time to time to reflect the actual costs associated with operation,
maintenance, and repair of the River Park Lift Station and Sewer Force Main. This
south sewer surcharge is in addition to all other applicable sewer service charges
and surcharges.
4.
If a sewer service customer does not receive water service from the town, the town
administrator or his designee shall establish a monthly service charge based upon
an analysis of other water accounts which are similar in kind to that of the sewer
service customer without town water service.
5.
If water meter readings are unavailable as a result of the customer's water meter
breaking or ceasing to function, and if the meter remains broken or nonfunctional
for three or more consecutive months, such user shall be subject to the monthly surcharge
fee provided for in Section 13.16.030 (E). This surcharge fee for sewer usage shall be in addition to any surcharge fee
charged to the customer for water usage pursuant to Section 13.16.020 (C).

Monthly rates for trash collection and disposal service shall be set by the town's

A.
Each customer owning property with a water or sewer tap which serves a structure on
the property which is unoccupied, as for vacation or change of renters, may be permitted
to pay the reduced charges set forth in Sections 13.16.020 (B) and 13.16.030 (F) for "vacant property." Reduced charges may be granted only if application is made
with the town clerk prior to the premises being vacant. Following approval of the
application for reduced charges, town personnel shall shut off water service to the
property. Only town personnel shall turn water service back on, after the customer
has notified the town clerk that the property will once again be occupied. The property
owner shall pay all town fees and costs associated with the shut-off and turn-on of
water service provided herein.
B.
Each customer owning property with an approved water or sewer tap which does not serve
a structure on the property or is not used for irrigation of the property shall be
obligated to pay an administrative charge for each approved tap. The administrative
charge shall be established by town resolution. If such customer fails to pay such
charge, the customer shall be deemed to have abandoned the tap.
C.
Each customer owning property which is unoccupied, as for vacation or change of renters,
shall not be obligated to pay for trash removal service during the time the premises
are vacant, but only if application is made with the town clerk prior to the premises
being vacant. The customer shall notify the town clerk prior to the property once
again being occupied. After such notice, the town shall resume and the customer shall
be obligated to pay for trash removal service.
