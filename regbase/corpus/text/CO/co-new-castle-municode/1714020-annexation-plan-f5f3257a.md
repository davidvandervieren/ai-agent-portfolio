All annexations to the town shall follow the requirements of Article 12 of Title 31
of the Colorado Revised Statutes, as amended.
(Ord. No. 2009-10, § 2(E), 9-15-2009)
For purposes of Article 12, Title 31 generally, and Section 31-12-105(1)(e) specifically,
the "annexation plan" for the town shall be the comprehensive plan.
(Ord. No. 2009-10, § 2(E), 9-15-2009)
