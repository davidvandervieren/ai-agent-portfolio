It is adopted by reference the Uniform Code for the Abatement of Dangerous Buildings
(hereinafter U.C.A.D.B.), 1994 Edition, of the International Conference of Building
Officials, 5360 South Workman Mill Road, Whittier, CA 90601. The purpose of this code
is to provide a just, equitable and practical method to repair, vacate or demolish
buildings or structures that endanger the health and safety of residents in the town.
The subject matter of this code includes definitions of dangerous buildings, procedures
for abatement and appellate procedures.
(Prior code § 12-12-010)
