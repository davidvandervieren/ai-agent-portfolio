The performance zone district shall be governed in conformity with the following regulations.
(Prior code § 13-04-110 (part))
The purpose of the performance district is to provide an area for industry with minimal
adverse environmental effects.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(A))
Permitted uses shall be as follows:
A.
Underground utility line;
B.
Parks/open space;
C.
Accessory uses;
D.
Municipal facilities;
E.
Fire station;
F.
Police station.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(B))
Conditional uses shall be as follows:
A.
Domestic animals;
B.
Substation;
C.
PUD;
D.
School;
E.
Museum;
F.
Public institution;
G.
Retail and wholesale business;
H.
Office;
I.
Personal, social and professional service;
J.
Automobile service station;
K.
Vehicle sales lot;
L.
Limited industrial use;
M.
Wholesaling or manufacturing industry;
N.
Storage of oil, gasoline and petroleum products;
O.
Retail or service business;
P.
Outdoor storage;
Q.
Overhead utilities;
R.
Excavation and/or road construction facility;
S.
Day nursery or child care facility;
T.
Parking lot;
U.
Community center;
V.
Wholesaling;
W.
Church;
X.
Accessory uses.
Any conditional use permit granted under this section shall state with specificity
the accessory uses permitted along with the conditional use.
(Ord. 2005-7 § 3; prior code § 13-04-110(C))
Minimum lot area shall be two thousand five hundred (2,500) square feet.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(D))
None.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(E))
Minimum setbacks shall be as follows:
A.
Principal structure: fifteen (15) feet;
B.
Accessory structure: ten (10) feet;
C.
Conditional uses: fifteen (15) feet, unless otherwise specified by the town council;
D.
County Road 335 shall require a fifty (50) foot setback.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(F))
Maximum building height shall be forty (40) feet, allowing no more than three stories,
excluding the basement.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(G))
Lot coverage shall be no greater than eighty (80) percent (impervious), unless otherwise
specified by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(H))
A landscaping plan must be provided to and approved by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-110(I))
