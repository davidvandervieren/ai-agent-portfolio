As used in this chapter:
"Potable water" means water that is intended and fit for human consumption, free from
impurities in amounts sufficient to cause disease or harmful physiological effects.
The bacteriological, chemical and radiological quality shall conform with state of
Colorado drinking water regulations.
"Private raw water supplies" means water rights that are owned by a developer, homeowners
association, property owner, etc. for irrigation use on property owned by such person
or entity.
"Raw water" means water that has not been treated and is not fit for human consumption
and that is primarily intended for irrigation use.
"Raw water system" means all facilities used for collecting, pumping, storing, measuring,
or distributing raw water.
"Town raw water supplies" means water rights that are owned by the town and are designated
only for irrigation use within the town.
"User" means the property owner to whom raw or potable water and/or sewer service
is served, whether record owner, corporation, company, individual, etc.
"Water" refers to potable water except where specifically described as "raw water."
"Water main" means a raw or potable water line owned by the town and installed in
a public street, public property, or special easement, including all structures up
to and including the curb stop valve.
"Water service line" means the pipe or line connecting a user's structure to the town's
raw or potable water system, up to but not including the curb stop valve.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
The purpose of this chapter is to provide economic incentives for raw water irrigation
with the goals of reducing impacts on the town's water treatment plant and preserving
the availability of the town's legal and physical water supply by encouraging the
use of alternate private water supplies.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
A.
Application to Use Private Raw Water Supplies. Any person applying for new water service
from the town and submitting for approval a proposed raw water system using private
raw water supplies shall submit the preliminary drawings and plans for the system,
along with a report listing the irrigation water rights owned by such person and a
map showing any irrigation ditches, pipelines, or other irrigation facilities already
serving the land. The applicant shall have the burden of demonstrating to the satisfaction
of the town attorney and town engineer that he or she possesses water rights of sufficient
legal and physical availability reliably to serve the proposed raw water system. If
the town determines that the physical or legal water supply will not be consistently
reliable, the town shall have discretion to deny approval of a system as qualifying
under this chapter. The town may withhold approval of a proposal until compliance
with the provisions of this chapter has been satisfied.
B.
Application to Use Town Raw Water Supplies. Any person applying for new water service
from the town may request authorization to use town raw water supplies for irrigation
and to submit for approval of a proposed raw water system. The town staff, in its
sole and complete discretion, may approve or deny the request to use town raw water
supplies, which decision may be based on, but not limited to, such factors as the
physical availability of town raw water supplies to serve the proposed demand and
the ability to deliver such raw water to the proposed development. Any person requesting
approval of a proposed raw water system shall submit the preliminary drawings and
plans for the system for the review and approval of the town engineer. The town may
withhold approval of a proposal until compliance with the provisions of this chapter
has been satisfied.
C.
Ownership of Approved System. Upon review and approval of the raw water system by
the town engineer, the developer and its successors-in-interest shall continue to
own, operate, repair and replace the raw water system.
D.
Water Tap Fee Reduction for Installation of a Raw Water Irrigation System. In developments
served by a raw water system, by reason of the prohibition on use of potable water
for outside watering and irrigation, the building permit applicant shall be entitled
to a reduction of water tap fees in an amount equal to twenty-five (25) percent of
the water tap fees otherwise payable to the town for potable water service pursuant
to Section 13.20.060 of the town code. Nothing herein shall require the town to refund all or part of
any tap fees paid prior to the effective date of this chapter.
E.
Water Rights Dedication Credit for Installation of a Raw Water Irrigation System Using
Private Raw Water Supplies. Nothing herein obligates the town to accept cash in lieu
of water rights dedication. If the town accepts cash in lieu of water rights dedication
for developments served by a raw water system using private raw water supplies, then,
by reason of the prohibition on use of potable water for outside watering and irrigation,
the building permit applicant shall be entitled to a reduction of water rights dedication
fees in an amount equal to twenty-five (25) percent of the water rights dedication
fees otherwise payable to the town pursuant to Section 13.24.070 of the town code. Nothing herein shall require the town to refund all or part of
any water rights dedication fees paid prior to the effective date of this chapter.
(Ord. 2008-3 § 5; Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
A raw water system proposed under this chapter shall conform to standards issued by
the town engineer and appended to the Public Works Manual.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
Users of raw water systems served by town raw water supplies shall use only so much
water as is reasonable, without waste, to irrigate the area designated in the application
for service.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
All users in existing developments equipped with raw water irrigation systems shall
use the raw water system for irrigation and are prohibited from using the town's potable
water for such irrigation as is intended to be served by the raw water system.
A.
Existing Agreements. To the extent that existing agreements between the town, acting
through the enterprise, and existing developments including, but not limited to, Lakota
Canyon Ranch PUD, conflict with the terms and conditions of this chapter, including,
but not limited to, conflicts concerning water tap fee credits, water dedication fee
credits, and use and maintenance of the raw water system, the terms and conditions
set forth in the applicable agreements shall control and supersede over this chapter.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
The provisions of this chapter shall apply to Castle Valley Ranch PUD only to the
extent that this chapter does not conflict with the Second Amended Castle Valley Ranch
Annexation Agreement and Site Specific Development Agreement dated March 2, 2002 and
other applicable agreements between the town and the developer of Castle Valley Ranch
PUD.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
For all developments using raw water, the developer shall provide notification to
inform the public that raw water is being used for irrigation and is not safe for
drinking. The notification shall include posting of signs of sufficient size to be
clearly read in all application areas. All piping, valves and outlets shall be marked
to differentiate raw water from potable water use. Outlets for raw water shall be
posted or otherwise marked in a manner that will indicate clearly that the water is
unsafe and is not to be used for drinking.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
Prior to receiving the water tap fee reduction and/or water rights dedication credit
described in this chapter, users of raw water systems shall include the following
provisions in their restrictive covenants. If the users' properties are not already
subject to restrictive covenants, new covenants consisting of the following provisions
shall be created, accepted, and recorded by the users:
A.
Outside Use of Potable Water Prohibited. Any outside use of potable water is strictly
prohibited. This restriction on use applies when the raw water system is operational
and during periods, not to exceed three days, when the raw water system is shut down
for maintenance or repair purposes. If the raw water system is shut down for a period
exceeding three days, then users may commence outside use of potable water, subject
to payment for such use pursuant to the town's potable water rates; provided, however,
that any outside use of potable water must stop and shall be prohibited once the raw
water system becomes operational again; and provided further that the period of outside
use of potable water does not exceed thirty (30) days in duration.
B.
Covenant Modifications. The covenant provisions regarding raw water irrigation cannot
be revoked, amended or modified without the town's consent and approval.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
Unless otherwise specifically stated, the penalty for a violation of this chapter
shall be a fine in an amount up to two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00) for each day
the violation continues. In addition, the violator shall be liable for reimbursement
to the town of any and all actual costs and/or damages the town may incur as a result
of the violation, including, without limitation, engineering and attorney fees and
costs. Except as otherwise stated herein, in the event potable water is used for irrigation
through no fault of the town in a development where a raw water system has been installed
by a developer, and the building permit applicant has received a water tap fee reduction
and/or water rights dedication fee credit pursuant to this chapter, the building permit
applicant or his or her successors or assigns, shall take immediate steps to curtail
such uses. In the event of failure of the raw water system as determined by the town
engineer and/or deliberate use of potable water for irrigation, the offending user(s)
shall be charged in arrears for the twenty-five (25) percent water tap fee reduction
and/or water rights dedication credit provided to the user or building permit applicant.
Any such fines shall constitute a personal obligation of the owner of the lot or parcel
on which the violation occurred, which the town may enforce through an action at law.
In addition, the town may record, in the office of the clerk and recorder of Garfield
County, Colorado, a statement of lien setting forth the name of the owner, the legal
description of the lot or the parcel and the amount of the delinquent assessment,
which statement shall be signed and acknowledged by the secretary of the town and
shall be served upon the owner by ordinary mail, at the address of the lot, parcel,
or at such other address as the town may have in its records for the owner thereof.
Upon the expiration of not less than ten (10) days after mailing of such notice, the
town may proceed to foreclose the statement of lien in the same manner as provided
for the foreclosure of mortgages on real property. The town shall be entitled to recover
as a part of any action (whether a foreclosure action or a personal action) the full
amount of all delinquent fines, together with interest, late charges, costs and expenses
of suit, including without limitation its reasonable attorney fees incurred.
The town's rights in the event of a violation of the restrictions on outside water
usage provided herein are in addition to any remedy allowed by law or otherwise prescribed
in the town code. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the town may temporarily
or permanently disconnect service of town raw water supplies to the property, if applicable.
The town shall have the additional and further right to suspend potable water service
to any lot owner or user upon not less than ten (10) days advance written notice,
pending the correction of any violation or delinquency.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
Any excavation or repair work on raw water service lines within town-owned easements,
property, or rights-of-way shall be governed by and completed in accordance with the
reasonable engineering requirements and specifications of the town and is subject
to the observation, inspection, and approval of the town engineer. Any party proposing
to undertake excavation or repair work on a raw water service line within town-owned
easements, property, or rights-of-way shall notify the town of that intent at least
twenty-four (24) hours prior to the start of the work in order to give the town the
opportunity to observe. No excavation of or repair to raw water service lines within
town-owned easements, property, or rights-of-way shall be completed and covered until
the town is notified and given a reasonable opportunity to inspect the work.
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part))
Users of town raw water supplies to serve their raw water irrigation system shall
pay the town raw water service charges set forth in the town's schedule of fees. Said
service charges shall increase by three percent annually on the first day of January
of each calendar year beginning in 2020. Raw water service charges are based on a
flat fee rate per square feet of irrigated area.
The provisions of Town Code Chapter 13.12 (Utility Services Generally) shall apply to the town's raw water service, excepting
Sections 13.12.030 (water meters) and 13.12.040 (trash collection).
(Ord. 2007-E-1 § 2 (part); Ord. No. 2018 E-2, § 4, 12-4-2018, eff. 12-18-2018)
