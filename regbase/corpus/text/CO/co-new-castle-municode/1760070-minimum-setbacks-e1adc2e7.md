The highway business zone district shall be governed in conformity with the following
regulations.
(Prior code § 13-04-120 (part))
The purpose of the highway business district is to provide an area for highway-oriented
commercial development along major arterial and collector highways in such a manner
as to minimize interruption of traffic flow, safeguard pedestrian movement, and optimize
the aesthetic appearance to passing motorists.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(A))
Permitted uses shall be as follows:
A.
Underground utility lines;
B.
Parks/open space;
C.
Accessory uses;
D.
Offices;
E.
Police station.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(B))
Conditional uses shall be as follows:
A.
Church/religious institution;
B.
Substation;
C.
PUD;
D.
Farm animals;
E.
School;
F.
Museum;
G.
Public institution;
H.
Retail and wholesale business;
I.
Eating and drinking establishment;
J.
Bank;
K.
Personal, social and professional service;
L.
Automobile service station;
M.
Vehicle sales lot;
N.
Limited industrial use;
O.
Municipal facilities;
P.
Fire station;
Q.
Warehousing or manufacturing industry;
R.
Storage of oil, gasoline or petroleum products;
S.
Retail or service business;
T.
Outdoor storage;
U.
Overhead utilities;
V.
Excavation and/or road construction facility;
W.
Parking lot;
X.
Community service agency;
Y.
Wholesaling;
Z.
Hotel, motel, or lodge.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(C))
Minimum lot area shall be as follows:
A.
Permitted uses: one acre;
B.
Automotive service stations: thirteen thousand (13,000) square feet;
C.
Other conditional uses: none, unless otherwise specified by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(D))
Minimum floor area shall be as follows:
A.
Permitted uses: none;
B.
Conditional uses: none, unless otherwise specified by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(E))
Minimum setback from property lines shall be twenty (20) feet, except that buildings
shall be set back at least fifty (50) feet from the right-of-way of an adjacent highway.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(F))
Maximum building height shall be as follows:
A.
Permitted uses: thirty (30) feet, allowing no more than two stories;
B.
Conditional uses: none, unless otherwise specified by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(G))
At least ten (10) percent of the total land area shall be landscaped in accordance
with a landscaping plan approved by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(H))
Maximum lot coverage shall be fifty (50) percent.
(Prior code § 13-04-120(I))
