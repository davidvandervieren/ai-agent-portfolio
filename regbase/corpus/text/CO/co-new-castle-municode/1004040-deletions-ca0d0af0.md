trash, or recyclables.

shifting, leaking, or otherwise escaping from the vehicle;
(III) The load is required to be secured under and complies with 49 CFR parts 392

such that the contents will not escape from the vehicle. Such a load may include,

vehicle; or

blowing, dropping, sifting, leaking, or otherwise escaping from the vehicle.
