It is unlawful for any child under the age of eighteen (18) years to knowingly wander,
loiter, idle or play in or upon any public street, highway, road, alley or other public
ground, public place and public building, vacant lot or other unsupervised place subsequent
to the hour of eleven p.m. on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and
subsequent to the hour of twelve a.m. on Friday and Saturday, and prior to five a.m.,
except for lawful employment, an emergency errand or legitimate business directed
by his or her parent, guardian or other adult person having the care or custody of
the child, or unless such child is accompanied by the parent, guardian or other person
of the age of twenty-one (21) years, having permission of the parent or guardian to
have temporary custody and care of such child.
(Prior code § 8-26-010(A))
Each violation of the provisions of this chapter shall constitute a separate offense.
(Prior code § 8-26-010(B))
A.
It is unlawful for a parent, guardian, or other person having care or custody of any
child under the age of eighteen (18) years to intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly
allow or permit any such child to loiter, wander, idle or play in or upon a public
street, highway, road, alley, or other public ground, public place, public building,
vacant lot, or other unsupervised place in violation of the provisions of this chapter.
B.
The fact that the child is upon the street, highway, road, alley, or other public
ground, public place, public building, vacant lot, or other unsupervised place contrary
to the provisions of this chapter shall be prima facie evidence that the parent, guardian,
or other person having custody of the child, is guilty of violating this chapter.
(Prior code § 8-26-010(C))
It is unlawful for any person to knowingly permit any minor child or children to aid,
abet, or encourage in, or to approve, encourage, allow, permit, tolerate or consent
to the violation by any minor child or children of any provisions of this title or
any ordinances or code provisions of the town.
(Prior code § 8-26-010(D))
A.
Any child violating the provisions of this chapter shall be detained and arrangements
shall be made for the return of the child to his or her parent or guardian. In addition,
any child convicted of a violation of this chapter may be punished by a fine of not
less than five dollars ($5.00) nor more than one hundred dollars ($100.00) for each
separate offense.
B.
Any parent, guardian, or other adult person who violates Chapters 9.48 and 9.52 shall be fined not less than five dollars ($5.00) nor more than three hundred dollars
($300.00) for each offense.
(Prior code § 8-26-010(E))
