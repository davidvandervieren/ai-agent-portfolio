The commercial transitional (CT) zone district shall be governed in conformity with
the following regulations.
(Prior code § 13-04-140 (part))
The purpose of the CT district is to allow limited commercial uses with a moderate
impact that can provide a transition between residential areas and more intense commercial
development (see performance standards).
(Prior code § 13-04-140(A))
Uses permitted by right in CT districts shall be as follows:
A.
Residential on second story or above;
B.
Single-family residential;
C.
Home occupation;
D.
Domestic animals;
E.
Underground utility lines;
F.
Parks/open space;
G.
Accessory uses;
H.
Overhead utilities.
I.
Family child care home;
(Prior code § 13-04-140(B))
(Ord. No. TC 2022-1, § 2, 1-18-2022)
Conditional uses permitted in CT districts shall be as follows:
A.
Two-family residential;
B.
Multiple family residential;
C.
Churches/religious institutions;
D.
Substations;
E.
Room boarding;
F.
PUD;
G.
Schools;
H.
Museums;
I.
Public institutions;
J.
Retail and wholesale businesses;
K.
Eating and drinking establishments;
L.
Offices;
M.
Banks;
N.
Personal, social or professional services;
O.
Funeral homes;
P.
Limited industrial uses;
Q.
Municipal facilities;
R.
Fire station;
S.
Wholesaling or manufacturing industries;
T.
Businesses of a retail or service nature;
U.
Outdoor storage;
V.
Day nurseries or child care centers;
W.
Parking lots;
X.
Personal service outlets;
Y.
Pharmacies;
Z.
Nonprofit clubs;
AA.
Community centers;
BB.
Health care facilities;
CC.
Multiple dwelling units;
DD.
Police stations;
EE.
Community service agencies;
FF.
Hotels, motels and lodges.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(C))
Minimum floor area shall be as follows:
A.
Single-family residential uses: eight hundred (800) square feet;
B.
Other uses by right: no minimum requirements;
C.
Conditional uses: four hundred (400) square feet, unless otherwise specified by the
town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(D))
Minimum lot area shall be as follows:
A.
Two thousand five hundred (2,500) square feet for commercial uses;
B.
Five thousand (5,000) square feet for residential uses;
C.
Conditional uses: there shall be no minimum, unless otherwise specified by the town
council.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(E))
Minimum setback from property lines shall be as follows:
A.
Principal and accessory structures: ten (10) feet front and rear, eight feet side;
B.
Conditional uses: ten (10) feet, unless otherwise specified by the town council.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(F))
Maximum building height shall be thirty (30) feet, allowing no more than two stories,
excluding the basement.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(G))
Maximum residential density shall be one dwelling unit per three thousand seven hundred
fifty (3,750) square feet of land area for two-family units, and five thousand (5,000)
square feet of land area for single-family units.
(Prior code § 13-04-140(H))
