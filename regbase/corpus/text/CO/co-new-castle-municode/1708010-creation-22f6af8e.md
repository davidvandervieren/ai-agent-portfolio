Pursuant to the authority conferred by Article 23, Title 31, C.R.S., and the New Castle
Home Rule Charter, there is created a planning commission and a zoning commission
for the town. The members of the planning commission shall also serve and are appointed
as the zoning commission.
(Ord. 2003-4 § 2 (part): prior code § 13-18-010)
(Ord. No. TC 2021-6, § 2, 9-7-2021)
The planning and zoning commission of the town shall consist of seven (7) members.
The ex officio members shall include each member of the town council on a minimum
six (6) month rotating period, which period may be extended for up to six (6) additional
months with the approval of the town council. The town council shall appoint the remaining
six (6) members. The town council shall have the authority to appoint a second town
council member to the planning and zoning commission. The town council shall also
have authority to appoint up to two (2) alternate members who may serve when one (1)
or more of the regular members are unavailable due to absence or due to recusal or
disqualification from a particular matter; provided that no more than seven (7) members
shall participate at any one time. All members of the planning commission shall be
voting members, including the alternate member(s) when participating. If two (2) alternate
members have been appointed and are available but only one (1) is needed, the participating
alternate member shall be selected by coin flip.
(Ord. 2006-4 § 2)
(Ord. No. TC 2021-6, § 2, 9-7-2021)
All members of the planning and zoning commission shall be bona fide residents of
the town, and if any member ceases to reside in the town, his or her membership shall
immediately terminate. All members of the planning and zoning commission shall serve
as such without compensation.
(Ord. 2003-4 § 2 (part): prior code § 13-18-030)
(Ord. No. TC 2021-6, § 2, 9-7-2021)
The terms of the ex officio members shall be limited to their respective official
tenure. All regular members appointed after the date of the ordinance codified in
this chapter shall serve for terms of four years, except as otherwise provided in
Section 17.08.030 . It is the intent of this section that the terms of regular members are staggered
so that no more than three new regular members are appointed at any one given time.
If a vacancy should occur prior to the end of a term, the town council shall appoint
a replacement to serve until the end of the term in accordance with the provisions
in this chapter. Members of the planning and zoning commission may be removed by town
council only for cause. Non-ex officio members appointed prior to the effective date
of said ordinance shall continue to serve on the commission until the expiration of
the terms for which they were originally appointed.
(Ord. 2003-4 § 2 (part): prior code § 13-18-040)
The planning and zoning commission shall have all of the powers and perform each and
all of the duties specified under Article 23, Title 31, C.R.S., together with any
other duties or authority which may hereafter be conferred upon them by the laws of
the state of Colorado. The performance of such duties and exercise of such authority
are to be subject to each and all of the limitations expressed in such legislative
enactment or enactments.
(Ord. 2003-4 § 2 (part): prior code § 13-18-050)
