The town adopts this title relating to the regulation and control of animals in accordance
with the powers granted it in Section 31-15-401(m), C.R.S. 1973, as amended.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
For the purpose of prosecution for violations of this title, it shall not be necessary
in order to obtain a conviction to prove notice or knowledge on the part of the owner
or keeper of a dog or other animal in question if such dog or other animal was violating
any of the provisions of this title at the time and place charged, unless otherwise
provided in this title. It is the purpose and intent of this title to impose strict
liability upon the owner or keeper of any dog or other animal for the actions, conduct
and condition of such dog or other animal.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
Unless otherwise specified, violation of any of the sections in this title shall be,
upon conviction, punishable by up to one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) in fines and/or
up to one year in jail.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
A.
It is unlawful for any person to keep or harbor more than three dogs that are four
month of age or older on any premises. Only one litter of offspring may be kept on
the premises until the age of four months.
B.
It is unlawful for any person to keep or harbor more than five cats that are four
months of age or older on any residential premises. Only one litter of offspring may
be kept on the residential premises until the age of four months.
C.
It is unlawful for any person to keep or harbor a combined number of dogs and cats
exceeding five, no more than three of which may be dogs.
D.
Any pet owner within the town who owns in excess of the permitted number of dogs and
cats at the time of the effective date of the ordinance codified in this chapter may
continue to own, keep or harbor such pets in excess of the maximum number provided
such owner obtains the proper licenses. It is the owner's responsibility to provide
written documentation of the licensing of dogs. Any owner who has in excess of three
dogs, five cats or five dogs and cats combined and complies with this subsection shall
not replace any pet in excess of the permitted number if any of the pets dies or goes
missing.
E.
Exceptions to the maximum number of dogs and cats may be obtained by licensed kennels,
pet shops and town shelters.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
It is unlawful for any resident to keep, harbor, feed, or otherwise care for any stray
or homeless dog or cat without taking full responsibility for the animal. Full responsibility
means providing the animal with continued humane treatment which shall include proper
food, water, shelter, veterinarian care and rabies vaccination. Any person who keeps,
harbors, feeds or otherwise cares for a stray or homeless dog or cat must follow the
town's regulations in reference to licensing and vaccination.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
As used in this chapter:
"Cat" means any common domestic animal of the species, regardless of sex.
"Domestic animal" means any animal traditionally used and primarily kept for domestic
use including, but not limited to: cats, dogs, fowl, rabbits, reptiles, cattle, horses,
goats, pigs or hogs, and sheep.
"Fowl" means and includes chickens, ducks, geese, turkeys, guineas, pigeons and all
like fowl.
"Rabbit" means and includes all rabbits and hares.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
When any estray shall be found in the town, the New Castle police department or its
designee shall pick up such estray. Within five days thereafter, unless such estray
is first claimed by the owner, the New Castle police department shall notify by mail
the State Board of Stock Inspection Commissioners, and shall otherwise comply with
the provisions of Section 35-44-101, et seq., C.R.S. 1973. Such estrays shall be kept
and cared for as provided by Sections 6.16.010 et seq.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
In addition to any other duties which may be required from the New Castle police department
by the town council or by the town municipal code are the following:
A.
The New Castle police department or its designee may seize and impound any cat, fowl,
reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal which is in violation of any of the provisions
or requirements of this chapter.
B.
The New Castle police department may, whenever a violation of the provisions of this
chapter is committed by any person in its presence, or whenever it shall have probable
cause to believe that a violation of the provisions of this chapter has been committed
by any person, institute proceedings in the municipal court for the town for the prosecution
of such violation by issuing a summons and complaint or penalty assessment.
C.
The New Castle police department may, whenever a violation of this chapter is committed
by any person in its presence, or whenever it shall have probable cause to believe
that a violation of the provisions of this chapter has been committed by any person,
issue a warning notice requiring the correction or discontinuance of such violation
within a period of not more than seven days. Such notice may contain a statement that
the New Castle Municipal Code provides for issuance of a summons and complaint for
violations of this chapter and the possible penalties provided by this chapter.
D.
The New Castle police department or its designee may enter upon private property for
the purpose of seizing a cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal to be
impounded if the New Castle police department or its designee is in pursuit of a cat,
fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal which it has probable cause to believe
to be in violation of any of the provisions of this chapter and which has strayed
to or taken refuge on such private property in the course of such pursuit, or if such
entry onto private property is necessary to remove a danger of imminent bodily harm
to a human being, animal, dog or cat.
E.
The New Castle police department shall oversee the disposal of any dead cat, fowl,
reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal found in the town. If a dead cat has a rabies
tag attached, the New Castle police department shall make efforts to notify or cause
to be notified the owner thereof. The New Castle police department or its designee
shall remove and dispose of the rabies tag attached to such dead cat.
F.
The New Castle police department or its designee may seize and impound any injured
cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal found at large in the town when
the owner cannot be immediately located.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
A.
Every cat kept in the town shall be vaccinated every three years against rabies by
a veterinarian, or at such intervals recommended by a veterinarian.
B.
Any cat not previously vaccinated against rabies in the same calendar year shall be
vaccinated against rabies by a veterinarian within thirty (30) days after:
1.
The date on which such cat is brought into the town;
2.
The purchase or acquisition of the cat by the owner; or
3.
Such cat attains the age of six months.

execute and furnish to the owner of the animal, as evidence thereof, a certificate
containing the following information:

B.

C.

and

(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
A.

rabies as required by Section 6.08.040 .
B.
No person shall allow any cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal to run
at will within the town.
C.
It is unlawful for any person to own, keep or harbor any cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit
or other domestic animal which, without provocation, by frequent or habitual howling,
meowing, squawking, crowing, cackling or other audible sound shall cause an unreasonable
disturbance to any person. In addition to being unlawful, such harboring or keeping
of such a cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal making such frequent,
habitual or continued howling, meowing, squawking, crowing, cackling or other audible
sound is declared to be a public nuisance which may be abated pursuant to the provisions
of Chapter 8.22 of this code.
D.
No summons and complaint shall be issued unless there are two or more complaining
witnesses from separate households who shall have signed such complaint, nor shall
there be a conviction for violation of this section unless two or more complaining
witnesses from separate households shall have testified at trial. A code enforcement
officer or police officer of the town who has personally investigated the complaint
of a single complainant or multiple complainants from a single household, and who
has personally observed the problem behavior of the cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or
other domestic animal with regard to its frequent, habitual or continued howling,
meowing, squawking, crowing, cackling or other audible sound shall satisfy the requirement
for the second complaining witness and may sign the compliant and/or give testimony
to such person observation at trial.
E.
The owner of any cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal kept in the town
shall cause excreta to be removed from any pen, cage or yard in which such cat, domestic
animal, fowl, reptile or rabbit is kept to prevent contamination and to prevent diseases,
hazards and odors. The accumulation of excreta upon any premises in violation of the
provisions of this section shall be unlawful and shall be deemed a public nuisance.
F.
It is unlawful for any person to knowingly own, possess, keep or harbor within the
limits of the town any cows or other cattle, horses, goats, pigs or hogs, sheep, chickens,
geese, ducks, turkeys or other fowl, venomous snakes or any wild or undomesticated
animal, such that the keeping of which results in a noxious odor or which is dangerous
to the public health, safety or welfare of residents of the town.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
Any owner of a cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other domestic animal in the town who
knows or has probable cause to believe that such cat, fowl, reptile, rabbit or other
domestic animal has bitten another person shall report such fact to the New Castle
police department.
(Ord. 2004-18 § 2 (part))
A.
For horses, cows or other hard hoofed animals, there shall be at least one acre of
land upon which the animals are to be kept, exclusive of residence or business building
utilized by the public, with a limit of two hoofed species per acre.
B.
Fowls and Reptiles.
1.
All fowls and reptiles must be kept in an enclosed area. The number of fowls shall
not exceed one fowl for each nine square feet of enclosed area, exclusive of shelter
or coop. Shelter or coop shall contain an area of not less than two square feet for
each fowl. All enclosures and shelter must be kept clear of manure. Manure must be
stored in a flytight container and be removed from town at least once each week. This
provision does not apply to manure spread as fertilizer upon cultivated ground or
lawns unless the manure attracts flies or causes obnoxious odors.
2.
Drinking facilities must be provided with adequate overflow drainage to prevent saturation
of surrounding soil.
3.
Spillage and leftovers from fowl feedings must be disposed of to prevent rodent propagation
or creation of odors.
4.
The shelter or coop shall not be closer than twenty-five (25) feet to any dwelling
house or place of business or street.
5.
These requirements are not to be applied to the display of fowls or reptiles by entrants
in any show or fair authorized by the town.
6.
The premises upon which the fowl or reptile is kept shall be maintained in a sanitary
manner and shall comply with all sanitary conditions and regulations set forth in
this chapter. The premises shall at all hours reasonable be subject to inspection
by the New Castle police department or its designee. It is unlawful to refuse such
inspection.
C.
Rabbits.
1.
All rabbits must be kept in an enclosed area. There shall be no more than six rabbits
over the age of six months per five hundred (500) square feet of area.
2.
Drinking facilities must be provided with adequate overflow drainage to prevent saturation
of surrounding soil.
3.
Spillage and leftovers from rabbit feedings must be disposed of to prevent rodent
propagation or creation of odors.
4.
The shelter shall not be closer than twenty-five (25) feet to any neighboring dwelling
house or place of business or street.
5.
These requirements are not to be applied to the display of rabbits by entrants in
any show or fair authorized by the town.
D.
A permit must be obtained, but the fee will be waived for animals, fowl, reptiles
or rabbits being kept solely for the purpose of display. The premises upon which the
display animals, fowl, reptiles or rabbits are kept shall be maintained as set forth
in Section 6.08.120(A), (B) and (C).

A.
No person shall own, keep, possess or harbor any vicious dog within the town except
within a building or enclosure designed to be escape-proof or unless such dog is under
such person's control by the use of a leash adequate to accomplish physical restraint
of such dog. If a building or enclosure is designed to be escape-proof by means of
a fence, such fence must be securely attached to the ground in a way that such dog
cannot dig under the fence and tall enough to prevent such dog from jumping over the
fence. Such devices commonly known as invisible fences are not adequate to make a
building or enclosure escape-proof.
B.
For the purposes of this section, "vicious dog" is defined as a dog, which at any
place in the town including the fenced or enclosed premises of its owner:
1.
Inflicts a bodily injury upon or causes the death of a person or domestic animal;
or
2.
Has demonstrated tendencies that would cause a reasonable person to believe that the
dog may inflict injury upon or cause the death of any person or domestic animal.
C.

dog solely by reason of having inflicted injury upon or exhibited such tendencies
toward:
1.
A person who attacked such dog or who engaged in conduct reasonably calculated to
provoke such dog to injure such person or another person;
2.
Any person engaged in provoking or stopping a dog fight or any altercation between
such dog and another domestic animal;
3.
Any person engaged in unlawful entry into or upon the fenced or enclosed portion of
the premises upon which such dog is kept, or into or upon any automobile or other
vehicle parked or stored in, upon, or on the street adjacent to such premises;
4.
Any person engaged in capturing or attempting to capture such dog in the absence of
the owner; or
5.
Any person engaged in unlawful or unauthorized entry into any automobile or other
vehicle in which such dog is kept or confined.

It shall be the duty of the New Castle police department, or its designee, to impound
any such vicious dog that is in violation of this section. In the event such dog is
found to have inflicted a bodily injury upon or caused the death of a person or domestic
animal, such dog may be destroyed pursuant to court order under the procedures provided
in Section 6.12.090 . Any dog impounded pursuant to this section shall not be released until and unless:
(i) the owner has paid any fine imposed under this chapter; (ii) the owner has demonstrated
to the municipal court or the police department how he or she will comply with this
section in the future.

a dog is endangering the life or person of another, or inflicting death or injury
to another animal, such dog may be immediately destroyed.
