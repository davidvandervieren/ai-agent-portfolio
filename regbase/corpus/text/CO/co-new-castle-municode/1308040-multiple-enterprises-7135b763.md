A.
The town is a statutory town authorized by Article 15, Title 31, C.R.S., as amended,
to provide refuse collection and sanitation activities, services, and facilities within
and without the municipal boundaries of the town;
B.
Such sanitation activities include but are not limited to the collection and disposal
of refuse, and all services and facilities related thereto (hereinafter "sanitation
activities");
C.
The town has historically conducted and operated such sanitation activities as a government-owned
business of the town, providing services in exchange for the payment of user fees
and charges;
D.
The town desires to establish a sanitation enterprise (hereinafter "enterprise"),
with the power to issue its own revenue bonds for the financing of sanitation activities
and facilities, in order to exclude the enterprise from the provisions of Section
20, Article 10, of the Colorado Constitution ("TABOR"); and
E.
The town's sanitation activities will thereafter be operated and maintained as an
enterprise within the meaning of TABOR, and which enterprise shall be separate and
distinct from the governmental activities of the town.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-010)
There is established within the town a sanitation enterprise to be operated and maintained
as a government-owned business, so as to exclude its activities from the application
of TABOR, in accordance with the terms set forth herein.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-020)
The town sanitation enterprise (hereinafter "enterprise") shall manage, operate, and
maintain all sanitation activities, services, and facilities of the town. The enterprise
is authorized to use, operate, improve, extend, enlarge, repair, replace, acquire,
dispose of, encumber, contract with respect to, and otherwise control and supervise
all sanitation activities of the town. The enterprise itself shall be wholly owned
by the town. All facilities, properties, and assets which are utilized by the enterprise
or form part of the enterprise shall be the property of the town, and upon the termination
of the enterprise, shall be returned to the town.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-030)
All sanitation activities shall be conducted as a single enterprise. The enterprise
may conduct one or more sanitation activities, including contracting with other governmental
entities, enterprises, or nongovernmental entities or persons. The town council may,
from time to time, establish other sanitation activity enterprises, and restructure
any sanitation activity enterprise as a separate enterprise.
(Ord. 2005-20 § 2)
The town council shall serve as the governing body of the enterprise, and the officers
of the town council and of the town shall serve as officers of the governing body
and the enterprise. The governing body shall conduct the business of the enterprise
in the same manner and follow the same procedures as the town council. All public
business of the enterprise shall be conducted only during regular or special meetings
of the governing body at which a quorum is present. The record of proceedings of the
governing body may be incorporated into the minutes of the town council. No additional
oaths, bonds or other qualifications shall be required of the governing body. All
actions of the governing body shall be considered as the actions and business of the
enterprise undertaken by the town council acting as the governing body of the enterprise.
All business of the enterprise and actions of the governing body shall be governed
by and made subject to all requirements, privileges, immunities, protections, limitations,
and other provisions of law.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-050)
The governing body of the enterprise may, without limitation, exercise the town's
legal authority relating to sanitation activities or otherwise available to any enterprise,
except as expressly provided herein. Such authority shall include all powers set forth
in the Act, and those powers set forth in this code which are consistent with the
authorities of an enterprise under the provisions of TABOR and necessary to operate
the enterprise, including but not limited to the following:
A.
To hold meetings concurrently with regular or special meetings of the town council;
B.
To adopt ordinances in the manner in which town ordinances may be adopted;
C.
To issue or reissue revenue bonds, notes, or other obligations payable from revenues
derived or to be derived from the provision of services in the same manner in which
town revenue bonds may be issued, without voter approval in advance;
D.
To pledge any revenues of sanitation activities to the payment of such revenue bonds
and to pay such revenue bonds therefrom;
E.
To set rates, fees, and charges for services provided by the enterprise;
F.
To enter into contracts relating to sanitation activities in the manner in which town
contracts may be entered into;
G.
To make representations, warranties, and covenants on behalf of the town, and to bind
the town to perform any obligation relating to sanitation activities, other than any
multiple-fiscal year direct or indirect debt or other financial obligation of the
town, without adequate present cash reserves pledged irrevocably and held for payments
in all future years.
The powers and authority specifically conferred herein shall not modify, limit, or
restrict the powers conferred on the enterprise by any other law directly or indirectly
affecting the sanitation activities of the enterprise, except as expressly provided
in this chapter. The enterprise shall have no power that adversely affects the status
of the enterprise for purposes of the application of TABOR.
(Ord. 2005-20 § 3; Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-060)
In no event shall the enterprise have the power to levy or assess any tax that is
subject to TABOR or to direct the town to exercise its taxing powers on behalf of
the enterprise.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-070)
The enterprise shall not accept or receive any revenue in grants (as defined in the
Act) from any state or local government, unless expressly authorized by the town council.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-080)
All contracts relating to sanitation activity shall be approved by the governing body
of the enterprises and executed by enterprise officers. Any preexisting contract relating
to sanitation activities shall be considered as having been approved by the governing
body. All contracts relating to sanitation activities shall be implemented and discharged
by the enterprise, unless otherwise provided by the town council.
(Ord. 2005-20 § 4)
All revenue for sanitation activity services and facilities provided by the enterprise,
including rates, fees, tolls, charges, payments for services to the town, and all
other income of the enterprise shall be collected, used, and expended for sanitation
activity purposes as determined by the governing body in accordance with and as set
forth in the fiscal budget of the enterprise adopted pursuant to law. Rates for sanitation
activity services and facilities provided by the enterprise shall be established by
the governing body, approved by the town council, and collected and enforced in accordance
with state law.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-100)
An enterprise fund shall be established to separately account for all revenue and
expenditures of the enterprise. The enterprise shall prepare an annual budget and
perform an annual audit that may be included in the budget or audit of the town. All
budgets, reports, audits, and financial operations of the enterprise shall conform
to and be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles applicable
to governmental (enterprise) units and other requirements of state law.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-110)
Nothing set forth in this chapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the
governing body or the enterprise to utilize other policies or procedures for operating
or continuing the enterprise in conformance with the Act and TABOR, except as otherwise
expressly provided herein. It is the intent of the town council to establish the enterprise
in conformance with the provisions of the Act and TABOR according to its most reasonable
interpretations thereof. The application and effect of the chapter with respect to
the enterprise shall be both prospective and retrospective to the effective date of
TABOR, unless otherwise determined by the town council. If any term, section, or provision
of this chapter shall be determined to be invalid or in violation of the enterprise
qualification provisions of TABOR or the Act, the invalidity or disqualification of
such section shall not affect any of the remaining provisions of this chapter. This
chapter shall remain in effect, whether or not the enterprise currently qualifies
as an enterprise pursuant to TABOR, until modified or repealed by the town council.
(Ord. 2002-5 § 4 (part); prior code § 11-04-120)
