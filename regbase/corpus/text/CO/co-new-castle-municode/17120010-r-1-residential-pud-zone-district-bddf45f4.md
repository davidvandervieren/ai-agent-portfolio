The Creek Hollow planned unit development is subject to all regulations and requirements
defined in this code for the R-1 residential zone district, except as modified as
follows:
A.
Permitted uses: single-family dwellings in duplex buildings; other uses as provided
in the underlying R-1 residential zone district.
B.
Minimum lot area for duplex: two thousand two hundred ninety-five (2,295) square feet
per dwelling unit.
C.
Minimum yard setbacks:
1.
Front yard: twenty (20) feet from any PUD perimeter boundary lines;
2.
Rear yard: eight (8) feet;
3.
Side yard exterior: five (5) feet;
4.
Side yard interior: none.
(Prior code § 13-30-010)
