## Sec. 16-10-150. - Compliance with state environmental requirements.
The approval of an oil and gas special use permit shall not relieve the operators
from complying with all current applicable state and federal regulations and standards
concerning air quality, water quality and waste disposal.
(Ord. 653 §1, 2009)
