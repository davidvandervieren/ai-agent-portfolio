The governing body of the respective Water Activity Enterprise (the "Governing Body")
shall be the Board of Trustees, which body shall be subject to all of the applicable
laws, rules and regulations pertaining to the Board. Whenever the Board is in session
exercising its legal authority relating to any Water Activity Enterprise matter, the
Governing Body shall also be deemed to be in session. It shall not be necessary for
the Governing Body to meet separately from any meeting of the Board, nor shall it
be necessary for the Governing Body to specifically announce or acknowledge that actions
taken thereby are taken by the governing body of the Water Activity Enterprise. The
Governing Body is authorized to exercise the Town's legal authority relating to water
activities as provided in the Water Activity Law; provided however, that the Governing
Body may not levy a tax which is subject to Section 20(4) of the State Constitution.
(Ord. 326 §1, 1997)
