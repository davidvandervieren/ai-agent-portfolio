In the opinion of the Board of Trustees, the extension of the social security system
to employees and officers of the Town will be of great benefit not only to the employees
of said Town by providing that said employees and officers may participate in the
provision of the old-age and survivors insurance system, but also to the Town by the
efficiency of its government.
(Ord. 316 §1, 1997)
The Board of Trustees hereby authorizes the Town of Mead to participate in the Statewide
Defined Benefit Plan and the Statewide Death and Disability Plan administered by the
Fire and Police Pension Association of Colorado and directs the enrollment of the
members of the Mead Police Department in said statewide benefit plans as provided
by statute.
(Ord. 835 , §1, 2017)
