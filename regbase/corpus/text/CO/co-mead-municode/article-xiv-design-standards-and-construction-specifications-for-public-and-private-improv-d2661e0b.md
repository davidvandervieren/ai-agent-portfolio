The provisions of the ordinance codified herein shall be known and cited collectively
as the "Town of Mead Design Standards and Construction Specifications" or the "Standards
and Specifications." The Standards and Specifications set forth the minimum standards
for safety, health, and the general welfare of the Town by regulating the design,
construction, choice of materials used for, location, maintenance and use of all Public
and Private Improvements. These include, but are not limited to, sanitary sewer systems,
water supply systems, private utility service lines for water and sewer, public and
private storm drainage systems, public and private streets, landscape areas, open
space, parks and recreation facilities, traffic signals and devices, public and private
parking lots, and appurtenances thereto.
( Ord. 952 , §2, 2021)
Town Engineer means the Town Engineer for the Town of Mead or the Town Engineer's designee.
( Ord. 952 , §2, 2021)
(1)
The Town of Mead Design Standards and Construction Specifications for Public and Private
Improvements, 2025 Edition, as amended, with certain appendices as set forth therein,
and as published by the Town of Mead, 441 Third Street, Mead, CO 80542 (the "Standards
and Specifications") are hereby adopted by reference with the same force and effect
as though fully set forth in the Mead Municipal Code.
(2)
A copy of the Standards and Specifications is available on the Town's website and
at Town Hall, 441 Third Street, Mead, CO 80542.
( Ord. 952 , §2, 2021; Ord. No. 1073 , §2, 2025)
It is unlawful for any person to violate any provisions of the Standards and Specifications,
or any amendment thereof. Any person failing to comply with these Standards and Specifications
shall be subject to the fines and penalties as set forth in Section 1-4-20 of this Code. The Town may seek restitution for expenses of enforcement or damage
to public property. In addition, for any violation of these Standards and Specifications
or any condition which may constitute a threat to the public health, safety and welfare
or damage to property, the Town Engineer, on behalf of the Town, may elect to initiate
any one (1) or more of the following enforcement alternatives:
(1)
Place a hold on current or new permits or applications related to platting, construction,
expansion, or operation of a use until the violations are corrected, or until the
municipal court or other court of appropriate jurisdiction orders that the hold be
lifted;
(2)
Revoke permits, or refuse to issue or approve any land development permit, including
but not limited to, certificates of occupancy and right-of-way access, street cut,
overlot grading, and building permits;
(3)
Pursue an abatement action in accordance with the procedure set forth Section 18-1-30 of this Code, except that "Building Official" shall refer to the Town Engineer and
"Chapter" shall refer to Article 14 of Chapter 16 for purposes of this Section 16-14-40 ;
(4)
Issue a stop work order to stop any or all construction activities, which stop work
order shall be issued by written notice and shall state the conditions under which
construction activities are authorized to resume, except that where an emergency exists,
no written notice shall be required; or
(5)
File a summons and complaint in the Town of Mead Municipal Court.
( Ord. 952 , §2, 2021)
The Town Engineer shall have the authority to inspect, or cause to be inspected for
compliance with the Standards and Specifications, all Public and Private Improvements,
as defined in the Standards and Specifications, to determine compliance or noncompliance
with the provisions of the Standards and Specifications.
( Ord. 952 , §2, 2021)
