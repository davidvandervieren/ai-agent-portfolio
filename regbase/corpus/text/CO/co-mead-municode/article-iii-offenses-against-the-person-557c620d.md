A person commits the crime of menacing if, by any threat or physical action, he or
she knowingly places or attempts to place another person in fear of imminent serious
bodily injury.
(Ord. 282 §1, 1995)
A person who recklessly engages in conduct which creates a substantial risk of serious
bodily injury to another person commits reckless endangerment.
(Ord. 282 §1, 1995)
Any person who knowingly confines or detains another without the other's consent and
without proper legal authority commits false imprisonment. This Section shall not
apply to a peace officer acting in good faith within the scope of his or her duties.
(Ord. 282 §1, 1995)
A person commits the crime of assault if he or she intentionally, knowingly or recklessly
causes bodily injury to another; except that this provision shall not apply if such
bodily injury is inflicted by means of a deadly weapon.
(Ord. 282 §1, 1995)
