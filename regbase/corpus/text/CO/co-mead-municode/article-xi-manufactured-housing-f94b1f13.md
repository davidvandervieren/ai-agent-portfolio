This is a high density residential district on a parcel of land under single ownership
or control on which five (5) or more manufactured homes are occupied as residences.
In addition to the regulations and procedures contained herein for the zoning of a
manufactured home community district, the mobile home community so established shall
be subject to provisions of the "Mobile Home Park Act" contained in Section 38-12-200,
et seq., C.R.S.
(Ord. 653 §1, 2009)
As used in this Article, the following words shall be construed to have the meanings
defined below:
(1)
Manufactured home means dwellings constructed off the site, whether termed mobile
home, manufactured home, factory housing or modular housing and which have the following
characteristics:
a.
Is partially or entirely manufactured in a factory;
b.
Is not less than twenty-four (24) feet wide and thirty-six (36) feet in length;
c.
Is installed on an engineered permanent foundation;
d.
Has a pitched roof and brick, wood or cosmetically equivalent exterior siding;
e.
Is certified pursuant to the National Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety
Standards Act of 1974, as amended;
(2)
Manufactured home community means a manufactured home development with continuing
local general management and with special facilities for common use by the occupants,
including such items as common recreational buildings and areas, common open space,
community management office, service buildings and the like.
(3)
Manufactured home community plat or plat means the particular arrangement of streets,
lots, recreation areas and open space areas within the manufactured home community
district. The plat shall be recorded with the County Clerk and Recorder as a part
of the ordinance establishing a manufactured home community district.
(4)
Manufactured home lot or lot means the individual lots within the manufactured home
community plat which are designed for occupancy by a single manufactured home.
(Ord. 653 §1, 2009)
The following uses are permitted in a manufactured home community district:
(1)
Single-family dwellings located on individual lots in a manufactured home community.
(2)
Parks, playgrounds, recreational buildings, recreation areas and common open space
areas operated by the manufactured home community management for the benefit of the
manufactured home community residents.
(3)
Community management offices and service buildings.
(4)
Churches and church schools.
(5)
Hospitals.
(6)
Public utilities: distribution lines and substations only.
(7)
Reserved.
(8)
Accessory buildings and uses normally appurtenant to single-family residences.
(Ord. 653 §1, 2009; Ord. 936 , §24(Exh. A), 2020)
The following are minimum requirements for a manufactured home community. Aesthetic,
environmental or facility design may necessitate exceeding the minimum specified.
(1)
Minimum parcel size for a manufactured home community: twenty (20) acres.
(2)
Minimum lot area per dwelling.
a.
Per dwelling, up to a maximum forty percent (40%) of the homes in any plat: five thousand
two hundred (5,200) square feet.
b.
Per dwelling, up to a maximum sixty percent (60%) of the homes in any plat: five thousand
five hundred (5,500) square feet.
(3)
Minimum lot width: sixty (60.0) feet.
(4)
Minimum lot depth: eighty-six and six-tenths (86.6) feet.
(5)
Minimum front yard (setback).
a.
Principal buildings: twenty (20.0) feet.
b.
Accessory buildings: twenty (20.0) feet.
(6)
Minimum side yard (setback).
a.
Principal buildings: five (5) feet on each side.
b.
Side yard abutting a street: fifteen (15) feet.
c.
Accessory buildings: five (5) feet on each side.
(7)
Minimum rear yard (setback).
a.
Principal buildings: ten (10) feet.
b.
Accessory buildings: ten (10) feet.
(8)
Minimum floor area (not including basement, carport or garage), per dwelling: eight
hundred sixty-four (864) square feet.
(9)
Reserved.
(10)
Minimum off-street parking.
a.
Per dwelling, paved driveway required, spaces cannot be located in front or side yard
setback: two (2) spaces, a minimum of one (1) space to be fully enclosed.
b.
Visitor off-street parking where no on street parking is provided: one (1) paved space
for each five (5) units to be located in common lots.
c.
Recreational buildings, common open space: one (1) paved space for off-street parking
each two hundred (200) square feet of building area, or ten thousand (10,000) square
feet of common open space, whichever is greater.
(11)
Minimum usable open space on each lot, per dwelling: thirty percent (30%) of total
lot area.
(12)
Lot markers: the limits of each manufactured home lot shall be clearly marked on the
ground by permanent monuments set pursuant to Section 38-51-101, C.R.S.
(13)
Manufactured housing standards and building code requirements: all manufactured homes
placed within the Town shall be certified pursuant to the National Manufactured Housing
Construction and Safety Standards Act of 1974, (42 U.S.C. § 5401, et seq., as amended).
A building permit is required for initial on-site preparation and construction for
the manufactured home, and a certificate of occupancy must be issued prior to any
occupancy of the manufactured home. Building permits shall be required for remodeling,
additions, fences, accessory structures, etc., in conformance with other provisions
of this Chapter and the Uniform Building Code.
(14)
Siding material: Siding material for manufactured homes must be that which is generally
acceptable for a site-built housing. Any siding material may be used provided it has
the appearance of wood, masonry or horizontal metal siding. Reflection from horizontal
metal siding shall be no greater than that from siding coated with white gloss enamel.
(15)
Roofing material: roofing material for manufactured homes must be that which is generally
acceptable for a site-built housing. Any roofing material may be used provided it
has the appearance of a nonmetallic shingle, shake or tile roof. Roof shall also have
a minimum slope of twenty-five percent (25%) (3:12) and overhanging eaves.
(16)
Permanent foundation required: an engineered, permanent foundation system shall be
required for all manufactured homes placed in a manufactured home community. The developer
may provide one (1) or more "standard" designs which comply with FMHA and VA requirements
for a permanent foundation for the soil type(s) to be encountered within the manufactured
home community.
(17)
Skirting or foundation fascia required: skirting or foundation fascia shall extend
from the bottom of the home to ground level, shall be insulated to a minimum of R-11
and shall be brick, wood or cosmetically equivalent exterior siding. The ground surrounding
the manufactured home site shall be graded to assure drainage away from the site.
Skirting shall be installed and grading completed before a certificate of occupancy
is issued.
(18)
Orientation of manufactured home on the lot: the longest side of the manufactured
home shall be parallel to the street, with a main door on that side. Individual site
plans for the lot must be approved by the Town prior to the installation of the manufactured
home on the lot.
(19)
Accessory buildings, site-built additions to manufactured homes: accessory buildings
and site-built additions shall be aesthetically compatible with the home to which
they are associated. All additions must comply with the approved site plan for the
lot or an amended site plan for the lot.
(20)
Recreational buildings, recreational areas and common open space: there shall be provided
for the use and enjoyment of the residents of the mobile home community, recreational
buildings, recreational areas and common open space equal in area to ten percent (10%)
of the gross area of the mobile home community. This area shall be in addition to
any other areas for parks, schools or open space that may be required to be dedicated
to the Town for the benefit of the general population during annexation or subdivision
of property. The recreational buildings, recreational areas, common open space and
ancillary recreational/playground equipment, bikeways, etc., shall conform to the
site plan approved by the Town and shall be maintained in good repair by the owner
of the manufactured home community.
(21)
Minimum standards for streets, walkways and bikeways:
a.
All interior streets in mobile home parks shall be privately owned and maintained
by the property owners and shall be built to Town construction standards.
b.
Required Town streets on the perimeter of the development shall be built to Town construction
standards.
(22)
Utility design requirements.
a.
All public and private utilities shall be installed underground in accordance with
the Uniform Plumbing Code, the National Electrical Code and Town standards Manufactured
home communities shall have one (1) master meter for water service and individual
meters for electric service.
b.
Adequate provision shall be made for outdoor watering at each manufactured home site,
recreation building site, recreation area and common open space.
c.
No manufactured home shall be occupied until it is connected to public and private
utilities, such connections to be inspected by the Town prior to occupancy.
d.
Utilities shall be installed so that utility connections can be closed when not linked
to a mobile home, and shall be trapped so as to prevent any escape of odor or gas.
All water connections shall have a frost-free shutoff.
(23)
Traffic control.
a.
Pursuant to Section 42-4-1102, C.R.S., the Town elects to impose and enforce stop
sign regulations and speed limits posted in accordance with the Manual of Uniform
Traffic Control Devices upon all highways and streets which are privately maintained
in manufactured home communities. The owner of the manufactured home community shall
provide such signs as may be required by the Traffic Engineer, and agrees to erect
and maintain such signs within the mobile home park in conformity with the Uniform
Sign Code and the Model Traffic Code.
b.
The sign regulations and speed limits shall be determined by the Traffic Engineer,
but shall be consistent with the provisions of Sections 42-4-1101 through 42-4-1104,
C.R.S.
c.
There shall be posted at each entrance to any such mobile home park a sign giving
notice of such enforcement in the following language:
NOTICE Stop sign regulations and speed limits enforced by the Town of Mead.
d.
When all signs are in place, stop sign regulations and speed limits shall be enforced
and violations thereof punished in accordance with the provisions of the Model Traffic
Code then in effect.
(24)
Site design requirements.
a.
Principal vehicular access points. Principal vehicular access points shall be designed
to encourage smooth traffic flow, with controlled turning movements and minimum hazards
to vehicular or pedestrian traffic. In general, minor streets shall not be connected
with streets outside the district in such a way as to encourage the use of such minor
streets by substantial amounts of through traffic. No site within the manufactured
home community shall have direct vehicular access to a street bordering the development.
There shall be at least two (2) connections to the public street system.
b.
Pedestrian and bicycle access. Access for pedestrians and cyclists entering or leaving
or traveling through the manufactured home community shall be by safe and convenient
routes. Such ways need not be adjacent to, or limited to, the vicinity of vehicular
access points. Where there are crossings of such ways and vehicular routes at edges
of a manufactured home community, such crossings shall be safely located, marked and
controlled, and where such ways are exposed to substantial vehicular traffic at edges
of a manufactured home community, safeguards may be required to prevent crossings
except at designated points. Bicycle paths, if provided, shall be so related to the
pedestrian-way system that street crossings are combined.
c.
Perimeter screening.
1.
Where manufactured home communities adjoin public streets located along exterior boundaries,
a landscaped buffer at least twenty (20) feet in depth shall be provided adjacent
to such streets. Such yard may be used to satisfy open space requirements for the
manufactured home community. No direct vehicular access to individual lots shall be
permitted through such yards, and no group parking facilities or active recreation
areas shall be allowed therein.
2.
Where manufactured home communities are so located that one (1) or more boundaries
adjoin neighboring residential districts without an intervening street, alley or other
permanent open space at least twenty (20) feet in width, a buffer at least twenty
(20) feet in minimum dimension shall be provided.
3.
Along the perimeter of a manufactured home community, walls or vegetative screening
shall be provided where needed to protect residents from undesirable views, lighting,
noise or other off-site influences, or to separate occupants of adjoining residential
districts from the potentially inconsistent land use characteristics of manufactured
home community and residential districts. In particular, extensive off-street parking
areas and service areas for loading and unloading of other than passenger vehicles,
and for storage and collection of trash and garbage, shall be screened.
d.
Landscaping outdoor living area.
1.
Each manufactured home site shall be provided with one (1) tree meeting the size requirement
of the landscape guidelines.
2.
As part of the functional open space requirement, an outdoor living area shall be
provided on each site equal to at least ten percent (10%) of its area, provided that
in no case shall such area be less than three hundred (300) square feet or required
to be more than five hundred (500) square feet. The minimum horizontal dimension of
such area shall not be less than fifteen (15) feet.
3.
Such outdoor living area shall be properly drained, located for convenience and optimum
use, and walled, fenced or planted to provide reasonable privacy. Within such area,
a section suitably surfaced for garden furniture shall be provided, not less than
one hundred (100) feet in area or ten (10) feet in minimum horizontal dimension. This
section may be covered in whole or in part by a roof.
e.
Walkways and lighting. Paved walkways not less than four (4) feet wide shall be provided
from mobile home sites to service buildings and along all roadways. All walkways shall
be lighted at night with a minimum illumination of six-tenths (.6) foot-candles. Twenty-five-watt
lamps spaced at intervals of not more than one hundred (100) feet shall be deemed
adequate to meet this requirement.
f.
Solid waste collection and disposal.
1.
It shall be the responsibility of the owner of the manufactured home community to
establish and enforce rules and regulations governing solid waste storage and handling
that meet or exceed Town, state or federal regulations.
2.
The owner of the manufactured home community shall provide containers for the storage
of solid waste awaiting collection. Containers are to be sized to completely contain
all solid wastes that are generated on the premises. Such containers shall conform
to all applicable Town specifications or regulations, including size, sanitary conditions,
physical conditions and container closure.
3.
The owner of the manufactured housing community shall provide an adequate location
or locations for such containers which shall facilitate the collection of solid wastes
from the community. Such locations shall be accessible to collection vehicles and
to occupants of the community. The collection vehicle should be able to remain on
a community street and be able to stop directly adjacent to the container location.
4.
The collection points, including the containers located therein, shall be kept in
a neat and sanitary condition by the owner of the manufactured home community.
(25)
Manufactured home community permits.
a.
It is unlawful for any person to construct, maintain, operate or alter any manufactured
home community unless such person holds a valid manufactured home community permit
issued by the Town in the name of the owner described by the permit.
b.
Application for a manufactured home community permit shall be made in writing to the
Town and shall contain the following information:
1.
The name and address of the applicant;
2.
The name of the owner and operator of the manufactured home community;
3.
A copy of the site plan as approved by the Board of Trustees and recorded at the County
Clerk and Recorder's office.
c.
Manufactured home community permits shall be issued on an annual basis, and no such
permit shall be transferable to another manufactured home community or to any other
lot, tract or parcel of land.
d.
If the Town determines that the manufactured home community is in compliance with
all provisions of this Chapter and with other applicable regulations, the Town shall
issue a manufactured home community permit.
e.
The annual manufactured home community permit fee shall be as established by resolution
of the Board of Trustees.
f.
The person to whom a permit for a manufactured home community is issued shall at all
times operate the park in compliance with the provisions of this Chapter and regulations
issued thereunder, and shall provide adequate supervision to maintain the park, its
facilities and equipment in good repair and in a clean and sanitary condition at all
times. The permit issued by the Town shall be conspicuously posted in the office of
or on the premises of the manufactured home community at all times.
g.
The Board of Trustees may revoke any permit to maintain and operate a manufactured
home community.
h.
The Building Official is authorized and directed to determine the condition of a manufactured
home community and individual manufactured homes parked on private lots, in order
that such official may perform such official's duty of safeguarding the health and
safety of occupants of manufactured homes and manufactured home communities and of
the general public. Whenever the Building Official, or authorized representative,
has reasonable cause to believe that there exists within any manufactured home community
or manufactured home a condition which renders same dangerous to the health and safety
of the occupants of the manufactured home community, manufactured home or to the general
public, or such cause results from the refusal of the affected property owner or occupant
to permit a routine inspection of his or her premises to enforce any of the provisions
of this Chapter, an application for a warrant to search and inspect the premises by
the Building Official to the Municipal Court, pursuant to the provisions of Rule 241(b)(2),
Municipal Court Rules of Procedure, shall be made unless the application for warrant
is excused by such rule. In the event the Municipal Court is unavailable, application
may be made before any judge of the County District Court.
i.
Every manufactured home community owner shall maintain an office within the manufactured
home community in which a copy of the manufactured home community permit is posted,
copy of the certificate of occupancy for each lot is maintained and a register containing
a record of all manufactured homes and occupants using the manufactured home community
is maintained. Such register shall be available to any authorized person inspecting
the manufactured home community and shall be preserved for a period of three (3) years.
Such register shall contain:
1.
The names and addresses of all manufactured home occupants residing in the community;
2.
The make, model and license number of each motor vehicle and manufactured home;
3.
The state, territory or country issuing the manufactured home license;
4.
The dates of arrival and departure of each manufactured home.

(a)
Preapplication procedure.
(1)
Prior to filing of application for amendment to an existing district, the developer
shall submit to the Town Clerk a sketch plan and report as specified in this Chapter,
plus the following information:
a.
Evidence of unified control of the entire area proposed for development.
b.
A tentative schedule of development.
c.
Evidence of financial capability to complete the development as proposed.
d.
This procedure shall not require fee or formal application for amendment.
(2)
The Town staff shall study the material received to determine its general acceptability
and compliance with the Comprehensive Plan and the objectives of this Article.
(3)
Following such review the Town staff shall hold a conference with the applicant within
thirty (30) days of receipt of all materials to discuss desirable changes in the sketch
plan.
(b)
Consideration of preliminary development plan.
(1)
On reaching conclusions, informally as recommended in Paragraph (a)(2) above, regarding
the acceptability of the plan or development as presented in the sketch plan, the
developer may make formal application for amendment to establish a manufactured home
community district.
(2)
Application for amendment to establish a manufactured home community district shall
be filed with the Town, accompanied by a fee of seventy-five dollars ($75.00). Application
forms are available from the Town. The applicant shall be responsible for payment
of all actual costs incurred by the Town for giving public notices, review and processing
of application.
(3)
Material to be submitted with the application shall include the following:
a.
A preliminary development plan with such modification and additions as required, including
identification of building sites when used instead of lots, common open space not
dedicated for public use and other matters as appropriate to manufactured housing
development generally or to the specific manufactured home district. Specifically,
the preliminary development plan shall indicate sub-areas for phased development,
if any, major off-street parking and loading areas, location and use of structures
in relation to building site lines, open space areas in relation to the use for which
they are intended and other information as required to establish a clear pattern of
the relationship between structures, uses, circulation and open space.
b.
Indication as to order and timing of development and accompanying improvements.
c.
Proposals for improvement and continuing maintenance and management of any private
streets or ways or common open space not offered or accepted for dedication for general
public use.
d.
Drainage plan indicating how storm water runoff from existing and proposed developments
on upstream properties as well as for the project itself will be handled without adversely
impacting downstream properties.
e.
Other written and graphic materials that may be reasonably requested by the Planning
Commission.
(4)
The Town staff shall review the preliminary development plan, and consult with other
agencies as necessary to determine compliance with the requirements and standards
of this Article, and with the sketch development plan originally submitted by the
applicant.
(5)
The Planning Commission shall hold a public hearing on the proposal. The public hearing
shall be held not less than fifteen (15) days not more than forty five (45) days after
receipt of all application materials.
(6)
As soon as possible after such public hearing, the Planning Commission shall forward
its recommendation to the Board of Trustees indicating approval, approval with specific
conditions or disapproval, and its reasons therefore.
(7)
If, after a public hearing held not less than fifteen (15) days, nor more than forty-five
(45) days after receipt of the Planning Commission recommendation, the Board of Trustees
grants approval of the preliminary plan, such approval shall relate only to the preliminary
development plan, and shall include any conditions or modifications deemed necessary
to bring such plan into full accordance with the requirements and intent of this Article.
(8)
The cost of providing utilities or other facilities of a capacity greater than that
needed by the project itself as deemed necessary by the Board will be negotiated by
the Board and the developer.
(c)
Procedure following amendment.
(1)
Before any building permit may be issued in the manufactured home district, a final
development plan and report shall be submitted and approved by the Board of Trustees.
The final development plan and report shall be submitted to the Town at least fifteen
(15) days prior to a regularly scheduled Board of Trustees meeting and shall be considered
by the Board of Trustees within forty-five (45) days of receipt of all application
material. Such plan shall be in accordance with the preliminary development plan as
approved by the Board of Trustees with such modifications as attached by the Board
of Trustees in its preliminary development plan approval.
(2)
The final development plan and supplementary material shall include the following:
a.
A map or maps and reports with modifications and additions as appropriate to manufactured
housing developments, generally, and to the specific manufactured housing proposal.
b.
Detailed plans for individual buildings or groups of buildings including the following:
1.
Site plans (building envelopes) for the building sites, indicating relationship to
adjoining areas.
2.
Floor plans of buildings, indicating horizontal dimensions, uses of space and floor
areas.
3.
Elevations of the buildings involved, indicating height and first habitable floor
elevation.
4.
Site plans indicating the provision of adequate, paved, lighted, off-street parking
for the uses proposed.
c.
Land areas, floor areas and required open space areas as necessary to make a determination
as to compliance with the requirements for land use intensity.
d.
All agreements, covenants, contracts and deed restrictions in a form acceptable to
the Town. Specifically, there shall be included an improvements agreement which provides
the mechanism whereby the applicant, his or her successors, heirs and assigns, guarantee
to make the improvements required by the Town according to an acceptable time schedule.
The improvements agreement shall further specify the terms of insuring the quality,
performance and maintenance of said improvements. Said improvement shall include,
but not be limited to, streets, on and off-site drainage structures and improvements,
utilities, landscaping, traffic control devices, recreational buildings and recreational
areas, open spaces, laundries and the like.
e.
Dedication statements for any public land not designated on the plat.
f.
A landscape plan for the manufactured home district showing:
1.
All landscaping materials at mature sizes;
2.
The maintenance system for landscaping, including the location of all sprinkler lines,
valves, heads and electric control system;
3.
Any other landscaping features; and
4.
A complete listing of all landscaping materials, including scientific and common names,
quantity, size at planting, size at maturity and a symbol or label to identify materials
on the landscape plan.
g.
The location and dimensions of all existing and proposed traffic controls, trash disposal
areas and enclosures, electric transformers, landscaping materials shown at mature
sizes and the maintenance system for landscaping.
h.
Other written and graphic materials as may reasonably be required.
(3)
Submittals of the final development plan may be in phases. Each phase may constitute
only that portion of the approved preliminary development plan that the developer
proposes to develop at that time.
(4)
Approval of the final development plan shall be by ordinance and shall constitute
the amendment to the Official Zoning Map of the Town.
(5)
Following review and approval of the final development plan, the Town shall record
the approved manufactured home community development plan with the County Clerk and
Recorder. Building permits for the location of manufactured home units on individual
lots will be issued only after streets are paved, curb and gutter, sidewalks, sewer
lines, water lines, on and off-site storm drainage facilities and over-site grading
have been completed according to the time schedule in the improvements agreement,
and according to the requirements and standards of this Article and the subdivision
regulations. Certificates of occupancy shall be issued only after all terms of the
improvements agreement have been met.
(6)
The final development plan as approved by the Board of Trustees shall be binding and
shall not be changed during the construction of the manufactured home community district
except upon application to the Town under the following procedure:
a.
Minor changes in location, siting, bulk or size of structures or improvements may
be authorized by the Board of Trustees if required by circumstances not foreseen at
the time the final plan was approved.
b.
All other changes in use, arrangements of blocks or streets, or significant changes
in the provision of required open spaces must be made by the Board of Trustees under
the procedure provided by this Article for amendment of the zoning district as provided
in this Section.
