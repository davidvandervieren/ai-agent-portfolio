Editor's note— Section 3 of Ord. No. 75, 1987, adopted May 5, 1987, repealed Ch. 62 of the 1972 Code,
which had been included as Art. XII, §§ 15-341-15-344 of this Chapter. The provisions
regulated sheet metal workers engaged in heating and air conditioning trades.
