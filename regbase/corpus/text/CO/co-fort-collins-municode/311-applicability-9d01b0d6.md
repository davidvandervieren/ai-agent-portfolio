All development applications and building permit applications shall comply with the
applicable standards contained in divisions 3.1 through 3.11 with the following exceptions:
(A)
Single-family detached dwellings and extra occupancy rental houses on platted lots
that are subject only to building permit review.
(B)
Accessory buildings, structures and accessory uses associated with the single-family
dwellings and extra occupancy rental houses listed in (A) above.
Applications for the development noted in exceptions (A) and (B) above must comply
only with the standards contained in division 3.8 ; and with respect to extra occupancy rental houses, the additional standards contained
in Section 3.2.2 (k)(1)(j).
Existing Development. In addition to the foregoing, this Land Use Code shall continue
to apply to ongoing use of land in completed developments to the extent that the provisions
of this Land Use Code can be reasonably and logically interpreted as having such ongoing
application.
(Ord. No. 59, 2000 §11, 6/6/00; Ord. No. 204, 2001 §§1, 11, 12/18/01; Ord. No. 120,
2003 §1, 9/2/03; Ord. No. 198, 2004 §3, 12/21/04; Ord. 123, 2005 §3, 11/15/05; Ord.
104, 2006 §12, 7/18/06; Ord. No. 063, 2018 , §6, 6/5/18; Ord. No. 077, 2019 , §5, 7/16/19)
In the event of a conflict between a standard or requirement contained in Article 3 and Article 4 , the standard in Article 4 shall prevail.
