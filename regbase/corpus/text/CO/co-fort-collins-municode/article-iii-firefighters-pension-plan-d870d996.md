The maintenance of the firefighters' pension fund of the City and all duties, rights,
obligations and benefits relating to firefighters' pensions for individuals hired
before April 8, 1978, shall be in accordance with the terms and conditions of the
City of Fort Collins Firefighters' Pension Plan as adopted by the City Council by
resolution on January 21, 1986, and as amended from time to time.
(Code 1972, § 2-26)
The Board of Trustees of the Firefighters' Pension Plan shall be created and appointed
and have the powers and duties as provided in the City of Fort Collins Firefighters'
Pension Plan adopted by resolution of the City Council on January 21, 1986.
Cross reference— Boards and commissions, § 2-71 et seq.; Firefighters' Pension Fund Board of Trustees authorized to meet in closed
sessions for certain purposes only, § 2-71 .
The provisions of Section 31-30-1001 et seq., C.R.S., as may be amended from time
to time, relating to firefighters' pensions shall apply to all qualifying members
of the Poudre Fire Authority hired on or after April 8, 1978, and all duties, rights,
obligations and benefits relating to firefighters' pensions for such members shall
be in accordance with the statutory provisions.
(Code 1972, § 2-35)
Any person who knowingly makes any false statements or falsifies or permits to be
falsified any record of the City, the Poudre Fire Authority or the firefighters' pension
fund, in an attempt to defraud the firefighters' pension fund, shall be guilty of
a violation of this Section. No conviction or penalty resulting therefrom shall excuse
such person from the duty to make full restitution to the fund, and such duty may
be enforced by action at law or otherwise in any court of competent jurisdiction.
(Code 1972, § 2-27)

The City Council has adopted by resolution the Retirement Plan of the City of Fort
Collins which is on file in the City Clerk's office.
Cross reference— Board of commissions, § 2-71; employees' retirement pension plan fund, § 8-78.
The Retirement Plan provides for administration by a Retirement Committee, hereinafter
referred to in this Article as the "Committee."
(Ord. No. 92, 1992, § 29, 9-15-92)
(a)
The Committee shall consist of six (6) members. The Chief Financial Officer or his
or her designee within Financial Services shall serve as a standing member of the
Committee, and the remaining five (5) members shall be appointed by the City Council.
Of the appointed members, four (4) shall be either an employee who is covered by the
Retirement Plan, a terminated-vested member of the Plan or a retired member of the
Plan who is receiving a monthly retirement benefit. The fifth appointee shall either
be an employee who is covered by the Retirement Plan or an individual who is a taxpaying
elector of the City.
(b)
Each member shall serve without compensation for a term of four (4) years, except
that members may be appointed by the City Council for a shorter term in order to achieve
overlapping tenure. Appointments shall specify the term of office of each individual.
All members shall be subject to removal by the City Council. If a vacancy occurs on
the Committee, it shall be filled by the City Council for the remaining unexpired
portion of the term. No member shall serve more than two (2) consecutive terms. For
the purposes of this provision, a "term" shall include the balance of an unexpired
term served by a person appointed to fill a vacancy if such unexpired term exceeds
twenty-four (24) months.
(Res. 71-57, 6-24-71; Ord. No. 92, 1992, § 29, 9-15-92; Ord. No. 79, 1994, 6-7-94;
Ord. No. 47, 1999, § 1, 4-20-99; Ord. No. 93, 2002, § 1, 7-16-02; Ord. No. 030, 2012 , § 70, 4-17-12; Ord. No. 099, 2012 , § 1, 10-2-12)
The members of the Committee shall supervise the administration of the Retirement
Plan in accordance with the terms thereof and shall have all the powers provided in
the Retirement Plan.
(Res. 71-57. 6-24-71)
The Financial Officer shall take and file minutes in accordance with the requirements
of § 2-75 of the Code. On or before January 31 of each year, the Committee shall file a report
with the City Clerk setting forth the activities of the Committee for the previous
year. On or before November 30 of each year, the Committee shall file a work plan
with the City Clerk for the following year.
(Res. 71-57. 6-24-71; Ord. No. 79, 1988, § 22, 6-7-88; Ord. No. 92, 1992, § 29, 9-15-92;
