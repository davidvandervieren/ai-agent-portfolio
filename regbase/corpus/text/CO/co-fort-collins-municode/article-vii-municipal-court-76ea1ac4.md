(a)
Appointment of judges . There will be a Municipal Court vested with original jurisdiction of all causes
arising under the City's Charter and ordinances. The Council must appoint the judge
or judges of Municipal Court for two (2) year terms. Council will designate a Chief
Judge to carry out related duties as adopted by the Council by ordinance, and will
fix the compensation of the Municipal Judges.
(b)
Compensation . Such compensation may in no manner be contingent upon the amount of fees, fines
or costs imposed or collected.
(c)
Licensure . Each Municipal Judge must be licensed to practice law in the State of Colorado during
their tenure in office, but need not be so licensed prior to appointment.
(d)
Temporary judges . As Council determines necessary, the Council may designate one (1) or more reputable
and qualified attorneys to serve as temporary judge.
(e)
Removal . The Council may remove a Municipal Judge for cause.
(f)
Rules, costs and fees. Rules of procedure, costs and fees will be enacted by the Council upon recommendation
of the Chief Municipal Judge.
(Ord. No. 202, 1986, § 1, Parts V, X, 12-16-86, approved, election 3-3-87; Ord. No.
5, 1989, 1-17-89, approved, election 3-7-89; Ord. 13, 1997, § 1, 2-4-97, approved,
election 4-8-97; Ord. No. 004, 2017 , § 2, 1-17-17, approved, election, 4-4-17; Ord. No. 067, 2025 , § 1, 4-15-25, approved, election 11-4-25)
The Council will provide for enforcement of its ordinances. The maximum penalty for
a violation of the ordinances of the city will be set by the Council by ordinance.
(Ord. No. 202, 1986, § 1, Parts R, V, W, 12-16-86, approved, election 3-3-87; Ord. No. 067, 2025 , § 1, 4-15-25, approved, election 11-4-25)
