____________
Republished in 2024 by Order of the City Council
____________
This Code constitutes a republication of the general and permanent ordinances of the
City of Fruita, Colorado.
Source materials used in the preparation of the republication were the City Code,
as updated through September 6, 2023, and subsequent ordinances. The source of each
new section is included in the history note appearing in parentheses at the end thereof.
The absence of such a note indicates that the section is part of the 2024 Code. By
use of the comparative tables appearing in the back of this republication, the reader
can locate any section of the Code, as updated.
Special note regarding defined terms: Defined terms throughout the Code will be keycapped
and placed in italics. When a user sees a word keycapped and in italics, this will
signify that the word has a defined meaning within its respective title.
