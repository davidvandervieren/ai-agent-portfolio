A special revenue sharing trust fund is established to account for all moneys received
from distributions by the federal government under authority and by direction of the
State and Local Fiscal Assistance Act of 1972, including any and all earnings, or
prorated share of earnings, on said distributions.
(Ord. 288, S1, 1973)
