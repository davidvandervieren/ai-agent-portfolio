The funds received from the collection of charges and rentals authorized by this section
and Chapters 13.16 through 13.28 shall be deposited, paid out and applied only in the manner and form provided for
the issuance of sanitary sewer refunding and improvement revenue bonds for the City,
for the purpose of refunding and paying outstanding sanitary sewer revenue bonds of
the City, and for extending and improving the City's sanitary sewer system and treatment
plant such funds to be known and established as the "Sanitary Sewer Refunding and
Improvement Bond Fund" but nothing contained in this chapter or the Chapters cited
above shall be construed in any way to prevent the City Council from applying and
crediting to such a fund, available money derived from any other sources.
(Ord. 272 S20, 1972)
