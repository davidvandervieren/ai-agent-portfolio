State Law reference— Municipal Election Code, C. R. S. § 31-10-101 et seq.
The Council shall provide by ordinance for the manner of holding City elections and
such additional regulations in respect to elections not inconsistent with the provisions
of this Charter or the State Constitution as may be necessary to accomplish the intent
of this chapter [Charter].
The provisions of any and all state laws now or hereafter in force, except as herein
provided or as the Council may otherwise by ordinance provide, shall govern all municipal
elections.
State Law reference— Suffrage and elections, Art. VII, Colo. Const.
The elective officers of the city shall be seven (7) Councilors, all of whom shall
be nominated and elected from the city at large.
At each regular City election there shall be elected four (4) Councilors and such
additional number as may be required to fill vacancies pursuant to the provisions
of Section 5.6 . The three (3) receiving the highest number of votes shall each be elected for a
term of office of four (4) years, the one receiving the fourth highest number of votes
shall be elected for a term of office of two (2) years, and a number equal to the
number of vacancies being filled (if any) who shall receive the next highest number
of votes in order shall each be elected for a term of office of two (2) years. The
terms of office of Councilors shall commence at the next regular council meeting following
the certification of the election.
(Res. No. 2006-33, § 1(2A), 9-5-06)
Editor's note— Resolution No. 2006-33, adopted Sept. 5, 2006, amending § 3.2, was approved at referendum
on Nov. 7, 2006.
The City of Lafayette shall consist of one ward. The election precincts of the city
shall remain as they exist on the effective date of this Charter, until altered by
the Council as hereinafter provided. The Council shall by resolution from time to
time establish convenient election precincts in accordance with the Charter and statutes.
State Law reference— Establishment of precincts, C. R. S. § 31-10-502.
A regular city election shall be held on the first Tuesday in November in odd-numbered
years commencing in the year 1995. Special elections shall be held in accordance with
the provisions of this Charter.

Special City elections shall be held when called by resolution of the Council at least
thirty (30) days in advance of such election, or when required by this Charter or
statute. Any resolution calling a special election shall set forth the purpose of
such election.
The City Clerk shall prepare, in accordance with the election laws of the State of
Colorado, a list of candidates whose names are entitled to appear on the ballot, with
the name of the office to be filled, and shall publish the same, along with the text
of any ballot question lawfully to appear on the ballot.
(Ord. No. 1993-34, § 1, 8-17-93)
Editor's note— Ordinance No. 1993-34, adopted Aug. 17, 1993, amending § 3.6, was approved at referendum
on Nov. 2, 1993.
The polls shall be kept open on election day from 7:00 o'clock a.m. to 7:00 [o'clock]
p.m.
Any person who is qualified for the office to be filled may be nominated for an elective
office by petition. A petition for this purpose shall be signed by not less than twenty-five
(25) registered electors for each candidate to be elected. A registered elector may
sign petitions for as many persons as there are offices to be filled by election.
The signatures on the nominating petition need not all be subscribed on one (1) page,
but to each separate page of the petition there shall be attached a signed statement
of the circulator thereof, stating the number of signers on each page of the petition,
and that each signature thereon was made in his/her presence and is the genuine signature
of the person whose name it purports to be. Following each signature on the petition
of nomination shall be written the residence address of the signer. The nominating
petition shall be obtained from the City Clerk.
(Res. No. 2006-33, § 1(2A), 9-5-06)
Editor's note— Resolution No. 2006-33, adopted Sept. 5, 2006, amending § 3.8, was approved at referendum
on Nov. 7, 2006.
No nominating petition shall be accepted unless accompanied by a verified acceptance.
The City Clerk shall forthwith examine all petitions submitted. If a petition is found
to be signed by fewer persons than the number certified by the circulator, the signatures
shall be accepted unless void on other grounds. If a petition is found to be signed
by more persons than the number of signatures certified, the last signature in excess
of the number certified shall be disregarded. Within five (5) days after the filing
of the nominating petition the City Clerk shall notify the candidate and the person
who filed the petition whether or not it is found to be signed by the required number
of qualified electors. If a petition is found insufficient the City Clerk shall return
it immediately to the person who filed it, with a statement certifying wherein the
petition is found insufficient. Within the regular time for filing petitions, such
a petition may be amended and filed again as a new petition, in which case the time
of the first filing shall be disregarded in determining the validity of signatures
thereon, or a different petition may be filed for the same candidate. The petition
of each person nominated shall be preserved by the election authorities until the
expiration of the term of office for which the person has been nominated. All nominating
papers comprising a petition shall be filed as one (1) instrument with the City Clerk
in accordance with the election laws of the State of Colorado. The City Clerk shall
make a record of the exact time at which each petition is filed and shall take and
preserve the name and address of the person by whom it is filed. A person who has
been nominated may, not later than twenty (20) days before the day of election, withdraw
by filing with the City Clerk a request therefor in writing. No name so withdrawn
shall be placed upon the ballot, provided that if the election is conducted as part
of a coordinated election, the name of any person who has been nominated shall remain
on the ballot unless such person files a timely request for withdrawal in accordance
with the deadline therefor contained in the election laws of the State of Colorado.
(Ord. No. 1993-34, § 1, 8-17-93)
Editor's note— Ordinance No. 1993-34, adopted Aug. 17, 1993, amending § 3.9, was approved at referendum
on Nov. 2, 1993.
The election laws of the state of Colorado shall determine the form of ballots.

31-10-806; paper ballots, C.R.S. § 31-10-901 et seq.

The canvass of vote shall be conducted to conform to the State statutes.
State Law reference— Canvass of votes, § 31-10-1201 et seq., C.R.S. 1973.
Editor's note— Resolution No. 2006-33, adopted Sept. 5, 2006, and approved at referendum on Nov.

Judges and clerks of election and other election personnel shall receive such compensation
as may be set in advance of an election by the Council.
No informalities in conducting City elections shall invalidate the same, if they have
been conducted fairly and in substantial conformity with the requirements of this
Charter.
The registration of all voters shall conform to the State Statutes.
State Law reference— Qualifications and registration of electors, C. R. S. § 31-10-201 et seq.
Any incumbent of an elective office may be removed from office by the qualified electors
of the city after such person has held office for six (6) months.
A petition demanding the recall of the officer sought to be removed and signed by
qualified electors equal in number to at least twenty-five (25) per centum of the
total number of votes cast for the office that is being recalled, to conform with
Article XXI of the Constitution, shall be addressed to the Council and delivered to
the City Clerk.
Except as to requirements contained in this Charter, recall petitions and recall elections
shall conform to and be conducted in accordance with the municipal recall election
laws of the State of Colorado.
(Ord. No. 2019-25, § 1, 8-20-19; Ord. No. 2021-13 , § 1, 8-17-21)
