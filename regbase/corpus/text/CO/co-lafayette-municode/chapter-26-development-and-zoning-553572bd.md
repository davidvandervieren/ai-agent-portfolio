Editor's note— Ord. No. 1986-01, § 2, adopted Feb. 4, 1986, enacted provisions designated as Ch.
26, Development and Zoning, set forth herein. Due to the nature and scope of the material
and in order to conform to the style of the ordinance, the user will note that the
Section headings indicating general subject matter bear two-part numbers and are comparable
to article headings in other chapters of the Code, while sections thereunder containing
textual material bear a three-part number indicating chapter, Section (article), and
sec. (in sequential order).
