State Law reference— Power of city to establish an alternative pension plan or retirement system, C. R.
S. § 24-54-101 et seq.
Pursuant to C.R.S. § 24-54-101 et seq., as amended, there is hereby established the
"Lafayette Employees Retirement Association," for the purpose of establishing a retirement
plan or system for the city's elected or appointed officers and its employees, excluding
sworn ambulance and fire and commissioned police personnel. The Lafayette Employees
Retirement Association may be referred to in this article as the "association."
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 1, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
The association is being created for the purpose of establishing an alternative pension
plan or retirement system as permitted by C.R.S. § 24-54-101 et seq., as an alternative
to Lafayette's affiliation with the Public Employees' Retirement Association, established
by C.R.S. § 24-51-101 et seq.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 2, 5-19-92)
The membership of the association may include all elected or appointed officers of
the city, and the city's employees, as established by the retirement plan or plans
(hereafter "retirement plan") adopted by the board of retirement created hereby. The
retirement plan shall not apply to or include sworn ambulance and fire and commissioned
police personnel.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 3, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
Except for assignments for child support purposes as provided by Colorado statute,
and except for writs of garnishment which are the result of a judgment taken for arrearages
for child support or for child support debt, none of the monies, funds, annuities,
individual accounts, or other benefits adopted by the association shall be assignable
either in law or in equity or be subject to execution, levy, attachment, garnishment
or other legal process.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 4, 5-19-92)
There is hereby created a board of retirement in whom is vested the general administration,
management and responsibility for the proper operation of the association, and for
making effective and construing the provisions of this article.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 5, 5-19-92)
The board shall serve without pay and shall consist of five (5) members, as follows:
(1)
City administrator, mayor, and city treasurer;
(2)
Two (2) regular full-time employees, elected by said employees to serve overlapping
four-year terms, with the initial employee receiving the lesser number of votes to
serve a two-year term. An employee may be reelected to unlimited consecutive four-year
terms.
The board of retirement may select ex officio (nonvoting) individuals for membership
on the board.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 6, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
(a)
Chairman. The city treasurer shall act as chairman of the board.
(b)
Treasurer. The city treasurer shall be ex officio treasurer of the board and the administrative
officer of the association. The treasurer shall be bonded in an amount to be determined
by the board. The cost of the bond shall be paid from the retirement plan; provided,
however, if the bond of the treasurer required by the city is sufficient to meet the
board's requirements, no additional bond shall be required of the treasurer.
(c)
Secretary. The city clerk, or designee, shall act as secretary to the board.
(d)
Legal advisor. The city attorney shall act as legal advisor to the board.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 7, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
The board shall hold meetings regularly, at least annually, and shall designate the
time and place thereof. All meetings of the board shall be public, and notice thereof
shall be given in accordance with applicable state or ordinance requirements. Unless
the board shall otherwise determine at its first meeting in any calendar year, notice
of board meetings shall be posted on the main door to the city council chambers, located
in the Lafayette City Hall at 1290 S. Public Road, Lafayette, Colorado. Each member
of the board, other than ex officio, shall be entitled to one (1) vote in the meetings
of the board.
Three (3) members shall constitute a quorum at any meeting of the board, and a majority
vote of the board shall be determinative of any issue before the board. The board
shall adopt its own rules of procedure and shall keep a record of its proceedings.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 8, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
The board shall be vested with the following powers:
(1)
The board shall have full and complete control and management of any retirement plan
established by the board;
(2)
The board shall determine what type of retirement plan in which to participate and
shall select, on the basis of the most sound proposal, a plan based either on the
purchase of an insured group plan of retirement annuities funded through a group carrier,
or a noninsured trust retirement plan, or participation in the public employees' retirement
association;
(3)
The board shall hear and decide all applications for relief, pensions, annuities,
retirement, or other benefits pursuant to the plan or system adopted. A record of
such action and all other matters properly coming before said board shall be kept
and preserved;
(4)
The board may appoint such consultants, advisors, or other persons as it deems necessary
to assist it in performing its duties of control and management of the retirement
plan. The retirement plan shall be responsible for payment of any costs incurred thereby;
(5)
The board shall have any other powers necessary for it to carry out its functions
as established by this article, not inconsistent with or limited by state or federal
statute.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 9, 5-19-92)

The secretary of the board shall keep, or cause to be kept, such data as shall be
necessary to document actions of the board. The board shall render a report to the
city council at least annually.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 10, 5-19-92; Ord. No. 2007-22, § 1, 7-3-07)
Except as otherwise provided in this article, no board member or any officer or employee
of the association shall have any interest, direct or indirect, in the gains or profits
of any investments made by the board. No board member, nor any entity with which the
board member may have an interest, as owner, shareholder, employee, or otherwise,
may engage in any contractual relationship with the association. No board member or
other person connected with the board, directly or indirectly, for himself or as an
agent or partner of others, shall borrow any of the funds or deposits of the association,
or in any manner use the same, except to make such current and necessary payments
as are authorized by the board; nor shall any board member, or other person connected
with the board, become an endorser, surety or become, in any manner, an obligor for
monies loaned or borrowed of the retirement plan.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 11, 5-19-92)
In the event that any board member ceases to be qualified to serve on the board, the
board shall declare that member's office vacated. If any vacancy so occurs, the vacancy
shall be filled within ninety (90) days after the date of the vacancy for the unexpired
term, in the same manner as the office was previously filled.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 12, 5-19-92)
The board shall make all necessary rules and regulations for managing and discharging
its duties (not inconsistent with statute or with the provisions of this article),
for its own government and procedure in so doing, and for the preservation and protection
of any fund or annuity contract.
(Ord. No. 1992-10, § 13, 5-19-92)
