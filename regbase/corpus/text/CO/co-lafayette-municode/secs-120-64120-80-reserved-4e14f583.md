(a)
The schedule of water rates in this article shall be applied to water used or consumed
as determined by the water meter readings which are made by the city.
(b)
Water meters are to be read to the nearest one thousand (1,000) gallons; thus any
meter containing a portion of one thousand (1,000) gallons shall be rounded off to
the nearest one thousand (1,000) gallons.
(Ord. No. 621, § 5, 4-24-74; Ord. No. 911, § 2, 4-21-81)
Owners or agents in charge of business blocks or other buildings occupied by more
than one (1) tenant using water from the same service pipe shall be required to pay
the water rate for the whole of such buildings, unless a separate meter is installed
for each tenant.
(Ord. No. 621, § 11, 4-24-74; Ord. No. 911, § 2, 4-21-81)
(a)
Bills for water and water reclamation service charges shall be mailed regularly as
provided by policies and procedures established pursuant to section 120-2 of this chapter. Such bills shall state the amount of utility service charges attributable
to the customer's use of the utility system. Except as otherwise provided in section 120-7.1 , where the utility customer is not the owner of the property served, the bill shall
state the owner or property management company name after the tenant name. All final
utility bills shall be issued to the owner or property management company of any rental
property. The city may seek payment from any responsible party, but the owner of the
property is ultimately responsible for the payment of all charges.
(b)
In the event that a utility bill is not paid by the fourteenth day following the date
of mailing, the city treasurer may give the utility user a delinquent notice in writing
that the bill must be paid within seven (7) days of the date of the notice. There
shall be added to all utility bills that are thirty (30) or more days delinquent,
a delinquent payment fee in an amount authorized in chapter 4 of this Code. The owner or property management company of any rental property will
be notified by a duplicate delinquent notice. This action shall constitute proper
notification by the city to all parties responsible for payment of the utility bill
applicable to the property.
(c)
In case any person shall fail or refuse to pay all charges and fees as prescribed
herein, or fail or refuse to comply with the provisions of the water and water reclamation
utility ordinances of the City of Lafayette, the director of public works may shut
off the water to said premises, building, house, lot or dwelling unit, and water shall
not be turned on again until all charges and fees are paid, together with a shut-off
notice fee, shut-off fee, and turn-on fee, authorized in chapter 4 of this Code, and the cost of collecting said charges and fees, or until compliance
with the provisions of the water or water reclamation utility ordinances of the City
of Lafayette have been obtained. The charges and fees for out-of-city customers shall
be set by resolution of city council.
(d)
In cases where more than one (1) premises, lot, house, building or dwelling unit is
lawfully served by a common meter, pursuant to the exemptions listed in sections 120-7.1 and 120-7.3 , only one (1) statement or bill comprising all charges for water and water reclamation
services furnished shall be issued. If that bill is not paid when due, or if anyone
served through said common meter fails to comply with any of the water or water reclamation
ordinances of the City of Lafayette, the city may discontinue all water service through
said meter and to the premises receiving water through said meter, until such charges
and fees together with costs of collection, if any, are paid or the ordinances have
been complied with. No claim for damages because of such turnoff or discontinuance
of water service may be made against the water utility of the city by anyone receiving
water through a common meter, who has complied with the water or water reclamation
utility ordinances, or who has paid a proportionate or other share of the charges,
fees, and costs outstanding and remaining unpaid. As a condition precedent to the
furnishing of water through a common meter, any act or omission requiring the city
to discontinue water service through said common meter and to be premises receiving
water thereby shall be deemed to be a joint act of persons who are served through
said meter.
(e)
The owner of any given property shall be ultimately responsible for all utility services
charges and fees applicable to their property. The city shall have for security of
the collection of such utility charges and fees, together with the costs of collection,
if any, a lien upon the real property served, which lien shall become effective immediately
upon the supplying of utility service and shall not be discharged until the payment
is made of all the utility service charges, fees, and costs herein provided. In the
event a utility bill is more than sixty (60) days delinquent, the city may record
a notice of lien, executed by the city administrator or their designee, in the records
of the county clerk and recorder for the delinquent charges, fees, and costs, together
with the costs of recording. The city will transfer the unpaid balance of a final
utility bill to the active utility account on a given property after all other collection
procedures have been attempted.
(f)
If the lien as provided in this section is not discharged by payment, the city administrator
or their designee is authorized to collect the delinquent bill by causing such delinquent
charges to be certified to the treasurer of Boulder County to be collected and paid
over to the treasurer of the city in the same manner as taxes.
(g)
Alternately, upon the election of council, such proceedings may be instituted for
the enforcement and foreclosure of such lien in accordance with the provisions of
the statutes of the State of Colorado relating to mechanic's and material liens in
accordance with the authority contained in C.R.S. §§ 38-20-101 and 38-21-101.
(h)
In the event of such suit, the city shall also be entitled to judgment against the
owner or occupant of the premises served for the costs and expenses of such suit,
including attorneys' fees in addition to the amount delinquent and penalties.

(a)
Turn on and restore service . There shall be charged and added to bills for water service a fee in an amount authorized
in chapter 4 of this Code each time municipal water service is restored after being shut off.
This fee shall be charged at the time the water service is restored and shall be added
to the water bill as provided in section 120-61 . During the period that service is turned off, the municipal water user shall be
responsible for the payment of the minimum basic monthly charge for water service
pursuant to section 120-58 and the minimum basic monthly charge for sewer service pursuant to section 95-94 with the exception of water service provided by an irrigation service.
(b)
Meter reset fee . A fee in an amount authorized in chapter 4 of this Code shall be charged when any person requests an initial water meter setting
for a new water connection, and the property is not prepared for such connection at
the time the city attempts to set the meter. The fee shall be assessed to the person's
utility bill.
(c)
Shutoff fee and notice fee . Fees in an amount authorized in chapter 4 of this Code shall be charged for the physical placement of a final shutoff notice
on the property for which water service is to be disconnected and for shutting off
water service.
(d)
Stretcher bars/meter jumpers. For the purposes of this section a "stretcher bar" or "meter jumper" is defined as
an apparatus that allows a municipal water user to bypass the meter requirement and
utilize water free of charge. It shall be unlawful and a violation of this Code for
any person to install, permit the installation of, use, or otherwise benefit from
the use of a stretcher bar or meter jumper. In addition to any penalties that may
be assessed by the municipal court for violation hereof, any person violating this
section shall also pay to the city a civil penalty in the amount of one thousand dollars
($1,000.00) for each day that the stretcher bar or meter jumper remains in place.
