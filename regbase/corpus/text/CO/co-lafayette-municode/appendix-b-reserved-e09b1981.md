Editor's note— At the time of codification, App. B had been reserved for the subdivision regulations
ordinance which was saved from repeal by the ordinance adopting the code, and was
purportedly in process of revision. Ord. No. 1986-01, §§ 1, 2, adopted Feb. 4, 1986,
specifically repealed Ord. No. 497, adopted Apr. 20, 1971; and Ord. No. 584, adopted
Aug. 6, 1973, pertaining to subdivisions, and enacted a Development and Zoning Code,
designated as Ch. 26, to which the user's attention is directed for provisions concerning
subdivision matters.
