The purpose of this chapter is to provide for an orderly transition from the old government
of Lafayette to a new government under provisions of this Charter. Such chapter shall
constitute a part of this Charter only to the extent and for the time required to
accomplish that end.
Except as otherwise provided herein, after the effective date of this Charter, all
appointive officers and all employees of the city shall continue in that City office
or employment which corresponds to the town office or employment which they held prior
to the effective date of the Charter as though they had been appointed or employed
in the manner provided in this Charter, and they shall in all respects be subject
to the provisions of this Charter, except that any officer or employee who holds a
position which this Charter provides be held at the pleasure of the appointing officer
or body shall hold such position only at such pleasure regardless of the term for
which originally appointed.
The first Council to take office under provisions of this Charter shall be elected
at a special municipal election to be held on the 24th day of June, 1958. At this
election the voters shall be entitled to vote for not more than seven candidates for
Council. The three candidates who receive the three highest number of votes, shall
be declared elected for a term beginning on the first day of July, 1958, and ending
on Monday next following the date of the regular city election in 1961. The four candidates
for Council who receive the fourth, fifth, sixth and seventh highest number of votes
shall be declared elected for a term beginning on the first day of July, 1958, and
ending on the Monday next following the date of the regular city election in 1959.
After this initial special election the provisions contained in this Charter relative
to elections and terms of elective officers shall govern.
The period from the effective date of this Charter to July 1, 1958, shall be known
as the transitional period. During the transitional period the statutes governing
towns shall continue to remain in effect, except that for the purpose of nominating
and electing members of the Council or filling vacancies thereon, Chapter III of this
Charter shall be immediately operative.
Following the election and qualification of the first Council under this Charter,
the Council shall proceed with the selection of a Mayor, and all other offices and
employees shall proceed to effectuate the other provisions of this Charter so that
it shall be fully operative.
As the Board of Trustees of the Town of Lafayette, Colorado, prepared and adopted
a budget for the Town of Lafayette for the fiscal year commencing April 1, 1958, and
ending on March 31, 1959, the Council in adopting the budget for the fiscal year January
1, 1959, to December 31, 1959, shall take into consideration said adopted budget so
as to provide an orderly transition from the old fiscal year to the new fiscal year.
This Charter shall not affect local improvements not completed on the effective date
of this Charter. Neither shall the provisions of this Charter affect municipal bonds
outstanding on said effective date.
Until such time as the City Council shall provide by ordinance for the rules, procedure,
proceedings, costs and fees in the Municipal Court as required in Section 15.2 , the rules, procedure, proceedings, costs, and fees, as provided by the statutes
governing second class towns shall remain in effect.
This Charter shall not affect any suit pending in any Court or any document heretofore
executed in connection therewith. Nothing in this Charter shall invalidate any existing
contracts between the Town of Lafayette and individual corporations, or public agencies.
This Charter Convention recommends that this City Council and all future City Councils
are charged with the responsibility of maintaining perpetual care for the cemetery.
CERTIFICATE OF FINAL ADOPTION
We, the undersigned, present members of the Lafayette Charter Convention, duly elected
by the people of Lafayette, Colorado, at a special election held on December 31, 1957,
or duly appointed according to law, under authorization of Article XX, Constitution

do hereby certify that the foregoing is the proposed Charter as finally approved and
adopted by the members of the Convention on the 28th day of February, 1958, for submission
to the people of Lafayette at a special election to be held on April 1, 1958.
