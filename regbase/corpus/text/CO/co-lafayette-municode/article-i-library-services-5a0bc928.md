(1)
In accordance with C.R.S. 24-90-119, as amended, except as set forth in subsection
(2) of this section, the library shall not disclose any record or other information
which identifies a person as having requested or obtained specific materials or services
or as otherwise having used the library.
(2)
Records may be disclosed on the following instances:
(a)
When necessary for the reasonable operation of the library;
(b)
Upon written consent of the user;
(c)
Pursuant to subpoena, upon court order, or where otherwise required by law;
(d)
To a custodial parent or legal guardian who has access to a minor's library card or
its authorization number for the purpose of accessing by electronic means library
records of the minor.
(Ord. No. 2006-40, § 1, 9-19-06)
It shall be unlawful for any person to take any library property without complying
with the appropriate checkout procedures, or knowingly retain any library property
for thirty (30) days or more after notice in writing to return the same, or to damage
any library property. Any such violation shall not be punishable by imprisonment.
(Ord. No. 2006-40, § 1, 9-19-06)
