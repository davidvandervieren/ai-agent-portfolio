The intent of the agricultural district (AG) is to recognize lands which are primarily
rural in character, and where the agricultural uses predominately occur outside including,
but not limited to, the grazing, keeping, and use of livestock, the production, harvesting
and selling of agricultural or horticultural products, and accessory storage. Accessory
structures such as hoop houses or high tunnels to extend the growing season, or for
storage or maintenance of items that support agricultural use are allowed as part
of this use. Accessory structures must meet the requirements of Chapter 26 , "Building structure, accessory" in this zoning district.
