This article is to impose the oil and gas pollution tax approved by the voters in
November 2018 as provided in Resolution No. 2018-28. The voters approved a tax at
the rate of up to six dollars and ninety cents ($6.90) per barrel of oil and up to
eight-eight cents ($0.88) per thousand cubic feet of natural gas for oil or gas extracted
within the city limits. The city council has determined to set the rate of the tax
at the full amount approved by the voters for oil or gas extracted within the city
limits. This rate was determined based on the social, environmental, and economic
costs of fossil fuels.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
The following words as used in this article shall have the following meanings:
Gas means all natural gas and all other fluid hydrocarbons that are not oil, including
condensate.
Natural resource means all forms of natural gas and oil.
Oil means crude petroleum oil and all other hydrocarbons, regardless of gravity, that
are produced in liquid form by ordinary production methods, but does not include hydrocarbons
that were originally in a gaseous phase in the reservoir.
Owner means the person who has the right to drill on a tract or drilling unit, to drill
into and produce from a pool, and to appropriate the oil or gas produced therefrom
either for the person or for others, except that a person ceases to be an owner with
respect to a well when the well has been plugged in accordance with all rules and
regulations applicable to the plugging of wells, for so long as the well remains plugged.
"Owner" does not include a person who obtains a lease of the mineral rights for oil
and gas on a parcel of land if the person does not attempt to extract or produce oil
or gas within the city limits pursuant to such lease.
Person means any individual, firm, partnership, association, joint stock company, corporation,
or estate, or combination thereof.
Severance means the extraction or other removal of a natural resource from the soil or water
within city limits.
Severed means the point at which the natural resource has been separated from the soil or
water within city limits.
Severer means any person who actually removes the natural resources from the soil or water
within city limits.
Well pad means the area that is directly disturbed during the drilling and subsequent operation
of, or affected by production facilities directly associated with, any oil well or
gas well.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
(a)
Excise tax imposed. An excise tax is hereby levied and shall be paid and remitted on the privilege of
engaging in the severance of natural resources within the city limits at the rates
prescribed by this section:
(1)
Six dollars and ninety cents ($6.90) per barrel of oil severed from a well pad within
the city limits;
(2)
Eight-eight cents ($0.88) per thousand cubic feet of natural gas severed from a well
pad located within the city limits.
(b)
Obligation to remit tax.
(1)
A severer shall be liable and responsible to remit the excise tax imposed by this
article on all taxable severance transactions made by the severer or facilitated by
it for the owner of the well pad.
(2)
If the severer fails to remit the full amount due under this article within the time
prescribed, the city may recover any unpaid taxes, penalties, and interest from the
severer and, if the severer is not the owner, from the owner.
(c)
No retroactive application; applicability. The obligations to collect and remit the tax required by this article are applicable
commencing on the effective date of the ordinance from which this section was derived
and are applicable to the severance of natural resources from:
(1)
New oil and gas well pads that begin operating on or after the effective date of the
ordinance from which this section was derived; and
(2)
Existing oil and gas well pads to which a "substantial modification," as determined
under section 26-22.1-15 of the Code, is made on or after the effective date of the ordinance from which this
section was derived.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
(a)
No severer shall sever or sell a natural resource severed from a well pad located
within the city limits without first having obtained a license pursuant to and subject
to the provisions of division 2 of article III of this chapter 110 .
(b)
A severer shall be subject to the same recordkeeping requirements set forth in section 110-102 of this Code as any other person required to obtain a license under division 2 of
article III of this chapter 110 .
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
(a)
Each severer liable for the tax imposed by this article shall make and file returns
with the city finance director on the prescribed forms and at the prescribed times,
computing and reflecting therein the tax required by this article.
(b)
The returns shall be filed for every calendar quarter, as required by this section,
unless a different return period is prescribed for a taxpayer by the finance director.
(c)
If the accounting method regularly employed by the severer in the transaction of business,
or other conditions are such that reports of severance transactions made on a calendar
quarter basis will impose unnecessary hardship upon the severer, the finance director
may, upon request of the severer, accept reports at such intervals as will, in the
finance director's opinion, better suit the convenience of the severer and will not
jeopardize the collection of the tax.
(d)
A separate return shall be filed for each calendar quarter, or other period approved
by the finance director, or any part thereof, during which the severer holds a license
as provided by section 110-96 of the Code, or is required to hold the license, or during which the severer is required
to file a return. The return shall be filed on or before the fifteenth (15th) day
of the second (2nd) month following the end of each return period. The tax due is
payable along with the return. All such returns shall contain such information as
the city finance director may require to fairly administer the tax.
(e)
All returns shall be signed by the severer and shall contain the full and complete
information requested, and shall be made under penalty of perjury.
(f)
The finance director may, upon request of the severer, extend the time for making
returns and paying the taxes due under such reasonable rules and regulations as the
finance director may prescribe, but no such extension shall be for a greater period
than is provided in section 110-130 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
The burden of proving any exemption from remittance of the tax imposed by this article
and paying the same to the city or from making such returns is upon the person who
has the duty to remit or pay the tax imposed under such reasonable requirements of
proof as the city finance director may prescribe.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
(a)
Formulation and promulgation of rules and regulations. The administration of this article is hereby vested in the finance director, who
shall prescribe forms and reasonable rules and regulations in conformity with this
article for the making of returns, for the ascertainment, assessment and collection
of taxes imposed hereunder and for the proper administration and enforcement hereof.
(b)
Application of provisions of divisions 1 and 2 of article III of chapter 110 to this article VII. The finance director is empowered to enforce this article. The provisions of divisions
1 and 2 of article III of chapter 110 of this Code shall govern and apply to the administration, enforcement procedures,
collection and refund procedures, confidentiality, deficiency, delinquencies, penalties,
interest, assessments, liens, recovery, notice, and hearings under this article. To
the extent that any provisions of this article VII conflict with the terms and provisions
of divisions 1 or 2 of article III of chapter 110 of this Code, the provisions of this article VII shall prevail.
(c)
Severer and owner jointly liable. The severer and, if the owner is not also the severer, the owner shall be jointly
and severally liable for all taxes, penalty or interest imposed by this article and
for any assessment issued by the finance director pursuant to this article, and any
liens therefor may be enforced against the severer or the owner or both or all of
them in accordance with division 1 of article III of chapter 110 until such liens are fully satisfied. Nothing in this paragraph shall be construed
to limit the remedies available to the city under divisions 1 and 2 of article III
of chapter 110 to enforce this article.
(Ord. No. 2022-06 , § 1, 2-15-22)
