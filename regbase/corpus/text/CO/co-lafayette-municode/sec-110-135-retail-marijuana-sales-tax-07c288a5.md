The retail marijuana tax imposed pursuant to this division shall be administered and
enforced in accordance with the provisions of divisions 1, 2, and 3, of this article,
and in a manner consistent with the administration and enforcement of other city taxes,
including, without limitation, any penalties for failure to make any return or to
collect or pay any tax; except that, in the event of a conflict between the provisions
of this division and the provisions of divisions 1, 2, or 3 of this article, the provisions
of this division shall control.
(Ord. No. 2015-03, § 2, 1-20-15)
(a)
In addition to any other taxes imposed pursuant to this article there is imposed upon
all sales of retail marijuana and retail marijuana products a tax at the rate of five
(5) percent of the amount of the sale, to be computed in accordance with schedules
or forms prescribed by the city treasurer.
(b)
The maximum tax rate that may be imposed pursuant to this section is ten (10) percent.
The city council may, by ordinance:
(1)
Establish another tax rate to be imposed pursuant to this division that is equal to
or less than the maximum ten (10) percent tax rate provided in this section; or
(2)
After establishing a tax rate that is lower than ten (10) percent, increase the tax
rate to be imposed pursuant to this section; except that, in no event shall the city
increase the tax rate above ten (10) percent of the sale of retail marijuana or retail
marijuana products.
(c)
Nothing in this section shall be construed to impose a tax on the sale of marijuana
or marijuana products to any person by a medical marijuana center licensed by the
Colorado Department of Revenue to sell medical marijuana and medical marijuana-infused
products pursuant to the Colorado Medical Marijuana Code, article 43.3 of title 12,
C.R.S. To the extent any retail marijuana store exists at the same location and under
common ownership with a licensed medical marijuana center, the retailer shall strictly
segregate and account for sales of retail marijuana distinct from medical marijuana
in accordance with all applicable state and city laws and regulations governing collocation
of retail marijuana stores and medical marijuana centers.
(d)
The city treasurer may require retail marijuana stores to file tax returns and remit
payments due pursuant to this division electronically. The city treasurer shall promulgate
rules governing electronic payment and filing.
(Ord. No. 2015-03, § 2, 1-20-15)
Notwithstanding any limitations on revenue, spending, or appropriations contained
in Section 20 of Article X of the Colorado Constitution or any other provision of
law, any revenues generated by the retail marijuana sales tax imposed pursuant to
this division, as approved by the voters at the municipal election on November 4,
2014, may be collected and spent as a voter-approved revenue change and shall not
require further voter approval to modify the tax rate as provided for in this division,
or to collect and spend any revenue derived from a modified tax rate.
(Ord. No. 2015-03, § 2, 1-20-15)
