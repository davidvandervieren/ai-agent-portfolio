The department of recreation and facility management shall prepare and recommend to
the city council and the city administrator general plans for development of recreation
facilities and public recreation programs.
(Ord. No. 2006-22, § 26, 6-6-06)
To the extent that the rules and regulations in sections 80-50 through 80-80 are applicable to any parks and recreation facility under the authority
of the director of recreation and facility management, the director of recreation
and facility management shall be responsible to enforce them.
(Ord. No. 2006-22, § 26, 6-6-06)
Editor's note— Section 5 of Ord. No. 2011-33, adopted Sept. 20, 2011, recodified Ch. 50, Art. II,
as Ch. 81, Art. III, and renumbered § 50-10 as § 81-50. Section 6 of said ordinance
further provided for the addition of reserved §§ 81-37—81-49.
Editor's note— Ord. No. 2025-20 , § 14, adopted October 14, 2025, repealed § 81-50. Former § 81-50 pertained to the
senior advisory board and derived from Ord. No. 2006-40, § 1, adopted September 19,
2006; Ord. No. 2011-04, § 6, adopted February 1, 2011; Ord. No. 2011-33, § 5, adopted
September 20, 2011; Ord. No. 2024-04 , § 11, adopted June 18, 2024; and Ord. No. 2024-08 , § 8, adopted August 20, 2024.
