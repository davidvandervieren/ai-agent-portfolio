For the purposes of this chapter, the following definitions shall apply:
Accessory dwelling unit: A detached dwelling unit located on the same lot as the principal dwelling unit,
and regulated by Chapter 26 of this Code.
Apartment: A multiunit dwelling under a common ownership consisting of one (1) or more rental
units.
Building: "Building" as used herein for purposes of applying the schedule of charges means
an independent structure standing alone, excluding fences. To qualify as one (1) building,
all portions, additions or extensions shall be connected by an attachment that is
an enclosed part of the building and which is usable by the occupants thereof.
Combined uses: Developments with combined residential and other than residential uses in the same
structure or building.
Commercial: Any nonresidential use of water.
Condominium: Any multiunit dwelling consisting of separate estates in individual air space units,
each of which has an undivided interest in the common elements, if any.
Consumer: Any person, firm or corporation receiving water from the city waterworks.
Irrigation service: A metered water service within the city limits that supplies potable water and which
water is used solely for irrigation purposes and consequently does not contribute
effluent into the city's water reclamation system by supplying such water to a structure
capable of or currently housing water reclamation facilities.
Mains: The main pipes; and connections forming a part of the city waterworks.
Multiunit dwelling: A building used by two (2) or more families living independently of each other in
separate dwelling units. This definition does not include motels or hotels but does
include trailer courts with each trailer space considered as one (1) dwelling unit.
Multiunit residential structures: This term includes multiunit dwellings, townhouses, apartments and condominiums.
Other than residential: All utility customers not included under the classifications in this section as single-family
or multiunit dwellings shall be categorized as "other than residential," which includes
but is not limited to business, commercial and industrial connections, and recreational
facilities appurtenant to residential developments.
Service pipe: A branch pipe with its fittings and connections through which water is taken.
Single-family dwelling: A detached principal building other than a mobile home, designed for or used as a
dwelling exclusively by one (1) family.
Street: Any street, avenue, alley, land or other thoroughfare.
Townhouse: A multiunit dwelling in which the ownership of each dwelling unit consists of a separate
fee simple estate on an individually platted lot, together with an undivided fee simple
interest in the common elements, if any.
Water district: A duly formed and existing water district which is formed and existing pursuant to
applicable statutes of the State of Colorado.
Water service: A connection to the city waterworks and the ability to be served by the city waterworks.

by any injury to said service pipe.

payment of refunds by reason of pressure changes or stoppage of the flow of water
through the system. The protection of water-using devices and systems which requires

valves for such apparatus at the owner's expense. The city will make available the
following service to permitted premises:
(1)
Repair of leaks on user service pipes which have curb stops will be performed by the
city on that segment of the user's service pipe which lies between the water main
and the outlet side of the curb stop. The user will repair all leaks that emanate
from the threads on the outlet side of the curb stop and all other service pipe appurtenances
on the outlet side of the curb stop.
(2)
Repair of leaks on user service pipes that have outdoor meters and no curb stop will
be performed by the city on that segment of the service pipe which lies between the
water main through and including the valve at the inlet side of the meter and the
meter-attaching coupling. The user will repair all leaks emanating from the service
