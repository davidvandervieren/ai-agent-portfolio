Editor's note— Ord. No. 1987-12, § 1, adopted July 21, 1987, provided for the inclusion of provisions
designated as Art. IV, § 110-166, to read as herein set out. Formerly, Art. IV had
contained §§ 110-166—110-170, which established a property tax refund for elderly
taxpayers as derived from Ord. No. 552, §§ 1—4, adopted Feb. 6, 1972; and Ord. No.
936, § 1, adopted Dec. 14, 1981. Such provisions were repealed by Ord. No. 977, §
1, adopted April 19, 1983.
(a)
As used in this section:
(1)
Penalty assessment means a written notice of the city treasurer's determination that a violation of
C.R.S. Section 426-137(2) has occurred and assessment and demand for payment of the
civil penalty provided for in subsection (3) of section 110-166 .
(2)
Notice of deficiency means the notice issued by the city treasurer pursuant to section 110-69 of this chapter for failure, neglect or refusal to pay any sales or use tax or any
penalty or interest thereon.
(b)
It is unlawful to register a motor vehicle in violation of the provisions of C.R.S.
Section 42-6-137(2).
(c)
Any person who causes a motor vehicle to be registered in violation of the provisions
of C.R.S. Section 42-6-137(2) shall be assessed a civil penalty of five hundred dollars
($500.00) pursuant to the authority granted in C.R.S. Section 42-6-137(4). The procedure
for the assessment of such civil penalty shall be as follows:
(1)
When the city treasurer determines on such information as is available that a person
has caused a motor vehicle to be registered in violation of the provisions of C.R.S.
Section 42-6-137(2), the city treasurer shall provide to such person a penalty assessment.
If the city treasurer also has determined pursuant to section 110-69 that sales or use taxes are due to the city on the purchase of such motor vehicle,
such penalty assessment may be included in a notice of deficiency.
(2)
Such person shall pay such penalty assessment within the same time period provided
pursuant to section 110-69 for payment of any amount due pursuant to a notice of deficiency, unless such person
requests a hearing in the manner provided in subsection (3) of this paragraph (c).
(3)
If such person desires to protest such penalty assessment, such person shall request
in writing a hearing from the city treasurer pursuant to section 110-75 within the same period as provided pursuant to section 110-75 for requesting a hearing on a notice of deficiency. The request for hearing shall
set forth the facts which show that a violation of C.R.S. Section 42-6-137(2) did
not occur. The city treasurer shall issue a written decision affirming or withdrawing
such penalty assessment within the same time period and in the same manner as provided
pursuant to section 110-76 after a hearing on a notice of deficiency. If the decision affirms the penalty assessment,
such person shall pay such civil penalty within thirty (30) days of the date of the
mailing of the decision.
(4)
Such person may seek judicial review of the finance director's decision before the
municipal court in accordance with its judicial appeals procedure.
(5)
The city treasurer may enforce collection of such penalty assessment in the same manner
as provided pursuant to section 110-72 for the collection of unpaid sales or use taxes, penalties or interest.
(Ord. No. 1987-12, § 1, 7-21-87; Ord. No. 2004-21, § 12, 11-16-04)
