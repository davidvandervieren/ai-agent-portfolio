Editor's note— The amusements ordinances of the city are in the process of revision and will be included
in the Code in a supplement.
Cross reference— Alcoholic beverages, Ch. 10; licensing of amusement devices and coin-operated machines, § 55-21 et seq.; libraries,
Ch. 50; licenses and business regulations, Ch. 55; parks and recreation, Ch. 80.
Nothing in this Code or the ordinance adopting this Code shall affect any ordinance
relating to amusements or entertainment, and all such ordinances are hereby recognized
as continuing in full force and effect to the same extent as if set out at length
in this Code.
