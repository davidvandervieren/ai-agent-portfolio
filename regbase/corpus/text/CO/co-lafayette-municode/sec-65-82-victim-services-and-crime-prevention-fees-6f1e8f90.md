(a)
Review shall be before the municipal court if it is alleged that any municipal authority,
board, commission, city council (municipal body) or officer exercising quasi-judicial
functions has exceeded its jurisdiction or abused its discretion, and there is no
plain, speedy and adequate remedy otherwise provided by the charter or ordinance:
(1)
Review shall be limited to a determination of whether the municipal body or officer
has exceeded its jurisdiction or abused its discretion, based on the evidence in the
record before the municipal body or officer.
(2)
Review pursuant to this section shall be commenced by the filing of a verified complaint.
The complaint shall be accompanied by a motion and proposed order requiring certification
of the record of the hearing before the municipal body or officer. The court shall
order the municipal body or officer to file with the municipal court on a specified
date, the record or such portion or transcript thereof as is identified in the order,
together with a certificate of authenticity. The date for filing the record shall
be after the date upon which an answer to the complaint must be filed. To the extent
not otherwise specified herein, the complaint, answer, motions or other responsive
pleadings shall be filed and served in accordance with the municipal court rules of
procedure.
(3)
Within twenty (20) days after the date of receipt of an order requiring certification
of a record, the municipal body or officer may file with the municipal court a statement
designating portions of the record not in the order which it desires to place before
the court. The cost of preparing the record shall be advanced by the plaintiff, except
that the court may, pursuant to motion of the plaintiff, order the municipal body
or officer to advance payment for the costs of preparing such portion of the record
designated by the municipal body or officer as the court shall determine is not essential
to a complete understanding of the controversy. If the municipal body or officer fails
to comply with such order, the portions for which it has been ordered to advance payment
shall be omitted from the record. Any party may move to correct the record at any
time.
(4)
The proceedings before or decision of the body or officer may be stayed, pursuant
to municipal court rules of procedure.
(5)
Where claims other than claims under this rule are properly joined in the action,
the court shall determine the manner and timing of proceeding with respect to all
such claims.
(6)
The municipal body or officer required to certify a record shall give written notice
to all parties, simultaneously with filing, of the date of filing the record with
the municipal court. The plaintiff shall then file, and serve on all parties, an opening
brief within forty (40) days after the date on which the record was filed. The municipal
body or officer shall file and serve an answer brief within thirty (30) days after
service of the plaintiff's brief, and the plaintiff may file and serve a reply within
fifteen (15) days after service of the answer brief.
(7)
The court may accelerate or continue any action which, in the discretion of the court,
requires acceleration or continuance.
(8)
In the event the court determines that the governmental body or officer has failed
to make findings of fact or conclusions of law necessary for a review of its action,
the court may remand for the making of such findings of fact or conclusions of law.
(b)
