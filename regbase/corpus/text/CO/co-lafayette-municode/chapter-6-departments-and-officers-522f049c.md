(a)
Charter departments . The following departments are established in accordance with the City Charter:
(1)
A fire department as established by section 4.11 of the City Charter, which sets forth the department's functions and duties, and
which shall be under the supervision of the fire chief.
(2)
A judicial department as established by section 15.1 of the City Charter, which sets forth the department's functions and duties, and
the employees of which shall be under the supervision of the city administrator or
the city administrator's designee. The department shall be responsible for the judicial
resolution of all cases involving violation of the Code or City Charter.
(3)
A police department as established by section 4.12 of the City Charter, which section sets forth the department's functions and police
chief's duties, and which shall be under the supervision of the police chief.
(4)
In accordance with section 13.1 of the City Charter, each city owned or operated utility shall be administered as
a regular department of the city under the management and supervision of the city
council.
(b)
Administrative departments . In accordance with sections 4.6 and 4.16 of the City Charter, the city administrator is authorized to establish, combine,
reorganize, and abolish administrative departments of the city as the city administrator
deems necessary or advisable to carry out day-to-day operations of the city, and the
city administrator is authorized to prescribe the functions of each administrative
department and the duties, authorities, and responsibilities of the officers and personnel
of each administrative department. Notwithstanding the foregoing, and pursuant to section 4.6 and Chapter VIII (General Finance—Budget, Audit) of the City Charter:
(1)
The administrative departments and all expenditures for operations, personnel, and
purchases thereof are subject to budgeting and appropriation by the city council;
and
(2)
Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit, restrict, or abridge the authority
of city council in section 4.6 the City Charter to establish, combine, reorganize, or abolish by ordinance such
departments of the city as the city council deems necessary or advisable and to prescribe
by ordinance the functions of each department and the duties, authorities and responsibilities
of the officers of each department.
(Ord. No. 2006-22, § 1, 6-6-06; Ord. No. 2011-33, §§ 1, 2, 9-20-11; Ord. No. 2019-06,
§ 1, 2-19-19; Ord. No. 2025-05 , § 1, 4-1-25)
