For the purposes of this chapter, the following words and phrases are defined as follows:
Bulky item means refuse items that are too large or too voluminous to be placed in a residential
refuse container, which include, but are not limited to, furniture or appliances,
and small equipment, such as lawnmowers.
Collector means any person providing residential collection services for solid waste, and/or
recyclables and organics.
Commercial customer means any premises utilizing collection service where a commercial, industrial, or
institutional enterprise is carried on, including, without limitation, retail establishments,
restaurants, hospitals, schools, day care centers, office buildings, nursing homes,
clubs, churches, and public facilities. Customers, other than residential customers,
serviced using any type of collection container, including without limitation wheeled
carts, dumpsters, or roll-off bins, are considered commercial customers unless the
service is provided for an active construction or demolition project permitted by
the city building division. Residential customers at properties who use a dumpster
for solid waste collection are commercial customers.
Community event means any event for which a community event permit from the city is required under chapter 23 of the Code, as may be amended from time to time.
Director means the director of public works or the director's authorized designee.
Electronic device means a device that is marketed by a manufacturer for use by a consumer and that
is:
(1)
A computer, peripheral printer, facsimile machine, digital video disc player, video
cassette recorder or other electronic device specified by rule promulgated by the
State of Colorado Solid and Hazardous Waste Commission, or
(2)
A video display device or computer monitor, including a laptop, notebook, ultrabook,
or network computer, television, tablet or slate computer, electronic book or other
electronic device specified by rule promulgated by the State of Colorado Solid and
Hazardous Waste Commission that contains a cathode ray tube or flat panel screen with
a screen that is greater than four (4) inches measured diagonally.
Excluded waste means all commercial and industrial solid waste, refuse, construction debris, dead
animals, institutional solid waste, hazardous waste, offal waste, stable matter, vegetable
waste and special waste, highly flammable substances, liquid waste, certain pathological
and biological wastes, explosives, toxic materials, radioactive materials, material
that the disposal facility is not authorized to receive and/or dispose of, and other
materials deemed by state, federal or local law, or in the reasonable discretion of
the city, to be dangerous.
Food waste means all compostable pre and post-consumer food waste, such as whole and partial
pieces of produce, meats, bones, cheese, bread, cereals, coffee grounds, or egg shells,
and food-soiled paper, such as paper napkins, paper towels, paper plates, coffee filters,
paper take-out boxes, pizza boxes, or other paper products accepted by the city's
designated composting site. Food waste does not include dead animals, plastics, diapers,
kitty litter, liquid wastes, ashes, pet wastes, and other materials prohibited by
the city's designated composting site.
Group account means a residential customer account for solid waste collection services that provides
for collection of solid waste from multiple residential customers, regardless of the
method by which such services are contracted or arranged. An account for service arranged
by a single property owner for collection of solid waste from multiple locations owned
by the property owner shall not constitute a group account for the purposes of this
chapter.
Homeowner association means any covenant-controlled community containing eight (8) or more residential
property owners, which includes a lawfully constituted and operational board or other
similar entity which is empowered to enforce the community's recorded covenants.
Large dead animals means animals or portions thereof equal to or greater than ten (10) pounds in weight
that have expired from any cause, except those slaughtered or killed for human use.
Multi-family customer means a residential property consisting of eight (8) or more units that uses a collective,
common system for the collection of solid waste generated by the occupants.
Occupant means a person entitled to possession of the property or premises, whether or not
the owner.
Offal waste means waste animal (land or marine) matter from establishments such as butcher shops,
slaughterhouses, food processing and packing plants, rendering plants and fertilizer
plants.
Organics means food waste and yard waste separately or combined.
Owner means the owner of record, as shown by any records of the city, county, or state
or any other record available to the city, whether an individual, individuals, or
entity, any agent or representative of the record owner, and any person or persons
entitled to possession of the premises by lease, tenancy, or easement.
Property manager means any person who is an owner's representative, has charge of, or controls any
property of an owner, or is appointed to manage on-site property operations including
solid waste collection services for the property.
Recyclable materials means those materials, goods and items deemed as single-stream recyclables by the
city's designated recycling facility or those materials, goods and items deemed recyclable
by other licensed collectors operating within the city.
Recycling container means, for commercial and multi-family customers, a refuse container designated for
recyclable materials, and which is suitable for containing and setting out for collection
recyclable materials, and may include a wheeled cart, dumpster, or other metal or
other nonabsorbent container equipped with a tightly fitting metal or nonabsorbent
lid.
Refuse means all nonhazardous, solid waste, including rubbish generated at a residential
unit but does not include any excluded waste.
Residential customer means a person receiving solid waste collection services for a residential property
including those receiving service through a group account, but excludes multi-family
residences of eight (8) or more units.
Residential waste collection services means the collection and transportation of residential refuse, recyclables and organics
by the city's designated service provider from those participants in the city's residential
waste collection service program.
Rubbish means all waste wood, wood chips, shavings, sawdust, pasteboard, rags, straw, used
and discarded mattresses, used and discarded clothing, used and discarded shoes and
boots, combustible waste pulp and other products such as are used for packaging, or
wrapping, crockery and glass, ashes, cinders, floor sweepings, glass, mineral or metallic
substances, and any and all other waste materials not included in the definition of
excluded waste.
Refuse container means, for those persons subject to the city's residential waste collection service
program, a wheeled cart suitable for mechanical lifting with a lid for containing
and setting out refuse for collection in sizes of approximately thirty-two (32) gallons,
sixty-four (64) gallons and/or ninety-six (96) gallons. For all other persons "refuse
container" means a metal or other nonabsorbent container equipped with a tightly fitting
metal or nonabsorbent lid.
Single-stream recycling means the co-mingling of all forms of materials, goods and items that are approved
for recycling at the city's designated recycling facility.
Single-stream recycling container means, for those persons subject to the city's residential waste collection services
program, a ninety-six (96) gallon solid waste container designated for collection
of recyclables. For all other persons "single-stream recycling container" means a
metal or other non-absorbent container equipped with a tightly fitting metal or nonabsorbent
lid.
Solid waste means useless, unwanted or discarded materials with insufficient liquid content to
be free-flowing, that result from domestic, industrial, commercial, agricultural,
governmental and community operations, which require proper storage, collection, transportation
and disposal to prevent environmental pollution inimical to public health, safety
and welfare. Solid waste does not include sewage, earth or material used to fill land
in accordance with building codes, mining residues, slag, dissolved or suspended solids
in industrial waste water effluents which are not acceptable for disposal in any sanitary
sewage treatment system or any material included in the definition of excluded waste.
Special waste means nonhazardous solid waste that is subject to additional governmental regulations
or special handling requirements in its collection, transportation, processing or
disposal as a result of the characteristics of or processes which generate such waste.
Special waste includes, but is not limited to:
(1)
Waste iron from a commercial or industrial activity;
(2)
Waste generated by an industrial process or a pollution control process;
(3)
Waste which may contain free liquids;
(4)
Waste which may contain residue and debris from the cleanup of a spill of petroleum,
chemical or commercial products or wastes, or contaminated residuals;
(5)
Articles from the cleanup of a facility which generates, stores, treats, recycles
or disposes of chemical substances, commercial products or wastes;
(6)
Wastes which are nonhazardous as a result of proper treatment pursuant to Subtitle
C of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act of 1976 ("RCRA");
(7)
Asbestos containing or asbestos bearing material that has been properly secured under
existing federal, state, or local laws, rules and regulations;
(8)
Containers that once contained hazardous substances, chemicals, or insecticides so
long as such containers are "empty" as defined by RCRA;
(9)
Municipal or commercial solid waste that may have come into contact with any of the
wastes described herein this subsection;
(10)
Filter cake sludge wastes from waste water treatment processes;
(11)
Wastes containing any regulated polychlorinated biphenyls; ash, sludge, tires and
powders; and
(12)
Electronic devices.
Vegetable waste means putrescible solid waste resulting from the processing of plants for food by
commercial establishments such as canneries. This definition does not include food
waste or waste products resulting from the preparation and consumption of food in
places such as cafeterias and restaurants.
Yard waste means leaves, grass, and clippings of woody, as well as fleshy plants. Materials
larger than four (4) inches in diameter or four (4) feet in length are excluded. Bundles
of yard waste up to two (2) feet in diameter by four (4) feet in length, and no more
than fifty-five (55) pounds, shall be allowed, and must be secured by degradable string
or twine, not nylon or other synthetic materials. Un-flocked whole Christmas trees
cut to less than four (4) feet in height are acceptable.

The name and address of the applicant;

The principal location of the business;
(3)
A list of motor vehicles or fleets of vehicles owned and/or operated by the applicant

Upon a determination by the director of whether a license shall issue, the director

Applicability . The requirements of this section 100-15 shall apply to commercial customers, multi-family customers, and community events

(b)
Commercial customer requirements for recyclable materials collection .
(1)
The owner, property manager, or other person responsible for managing the contract
for solid waste collection of the commercial customer shall contract or arrange for
on-site collection service for recyclable materials with a collector licensed by the
city under section 100-13 , at a level that accommodates the total amount of recyclable materials generated
by the commercial customer, providing for collection at least every other week.
(2)
All commercial customers must separate recyclable materials from trash and other refuse
and wherever commercial customers provide trash containers to employees or customers,
they must also provide recycling containers for employees' and customers' use. Recycling
containers must be at least as conveniently located as trash containers and be of
adequate size and number to prevent recyclables from being mixed with trash. Recycling
containers utilized for collection must be approved by the commercial customer's collector
and shall be placed in a location or locations within reasonable and convenient proximity
to all buildings and other uses on site and be at least as convenient to occupants
as trash containers.
(3)
Commercial customers must provide conspicuous signage at or on each trash and recycling
container, clearly indicating the appropriate materials to be placed inside the container
in accordance with the collector's requirements, to ensure proper sorting and minimize
contamination.
(4)
When an owner or manager of a commercial customer provides janitorial services to
its tenants or occupants, the contract for janitorial services shall include recyclable
materials collection service that meets the requirements of this section.
(5)
At least once per year, commercial customers are encouraged to conduct training or
distribute materials that instructs all employees, tenants, and occupants how to use
the containers established for collection of recyclable materials pursuant to this
section. Commercial customers shall provide new employees, tenants, and occupants
with this information within thirty (30) days of when the employee begins work or
the tenant or occupant moves in and no later than the thirtieth day after a substantive
change in the recycling service or location of recycling containers established by
the commercial customer.
(6)
Owners and property managers of commercial customers must maintain and make available
upon request, to the director for inspection and copying during normal business hours,
any reports, contracts and/or invoices for collection and disposition of recyclable
materials for a period covering the most recent three (3) years.
(7)
The owner, property manager, or other person responsible for managing the contract
for recyclable materials collection of the commercial customer shall be jointly and
severally responsible to provide for collection service and containers for removal
of recyclable materials to the degree of service necessary to maintain the premises
in a clean and orderly condition. The owner, property manager, or other person responsible
for managing the contract for recyclable materials collection for the commercial customer
shall be jointly and severally liable for violations of responsibilities imposed upon
the commercial customer by this section.
(c)
Multi-family customer requirements for recyclable materials collection .
(1)
The owner or property manager of multi-family customers shall contract or arrange
for on-site collection service for recyclable materials with a collector licensed
by the city under section 100-13 , at a level that accommodates the total amount of recyclable materials generated
by the multi-family customer, providing for collection at least every other week.
(2)
Multi-family customers shall establish on-site collection areas for recyclable materials
that are convenient to occupants and tenants. The recycling containers shall be placed
in a location or locations within reasonable and convenient proximity to all buildings
and other uses on site and be at least as convenient to occupants and tenants as trash
containers and shall be of adequate size and number to prevent recyclables from being
mixed with trash.
(3)
When an owner or property manager provides janitorial services to its tenants, occupants,
or employees, the contract for janitorial services shall include recyclable materials
collection service that meets the requirements of this section.
(4)
Multi-family customers must provide conspicuous signage at or on each trash and recycling
container, clearly indicating the appropriate materials to be placed inside the container
in accordance with the collector's requirements, to ensure proper sorting and minimize
contamination.
(5)
At least once per year, the owner or property manager is encouraged to distribute
to all tenants, occupants, and employees information about how to use the on-site
system established for collection of recyclable materials pursuant to this section.
Owners and property managers shall provide new tenants, occupants, and employees with
this information within thirty (30) days of tenant or occupant move-in or employee
start date and no later than the thirtieth day after a substantive change in the recycling
service or location of recycling containers offered at the property.
(6)
Owners or property managers must maintain and make available upon request, to the
director for inspection and copying during normal business hours, any reports, contracts
and/or invoices for collection and disposition of recyclable materials for a period
covering the most recent three (3) years.
(7)
The owner, property manager, or other person responsible for managing the contract
for recyclable materials collection for the multi-family customer shall be jointly
and severally responsible to provide for collection service and containers for removal
of recyclable materials to the degree of service necessary to maintain the premises
in a clean and orderly condition. The owner, property manager, or other person responsible
for managing the contract for recyclable materials collection for the multi-family
customer shall be jointly and severally liable for violations of responsibilities
imposed upon the multi-family customer by this section.
(d)
Community event requirements for recyclable materials collection .
(1)
Pursuant to section 23-4 of the code, community events held on or after January 1, 2023, for which a community
event permit is required under section 23-3 of the code are required to comply with the city's environmental stewardship and
zero-waste event rules and regulations adopted by the city administrator in accordance
with section 23-10 of the code.
(2)
Any person to whom a community event permit has been granted shall be responsible
for compliance with this section and shall be liable for violations of responsibilities
imposed by this section or by chapter 23 .
(e)
Disposition of recyclable materials .
(1)
No person other than the person placing the recyclable materials for collection or
that person's designated collector shall take physical possession of any recyclable
materials separated from trash, set out in the vicinity of the curb or alleys, and
plainly marked for recyclable materials collection.
(2)
Each owner, property manager, commercial customer, or multifamily customer shall relinquish
recyclable materials to a collector only on the condition that the collector deliver
the recyclable materials only to a recyclables processing center as set forth in subparagraph
(f)(3)(iii) and (iv) below.
(f)
Collector requirements for commercial and multi-family customer recyclable materials
collection .
(1)
Each collector offering recyclable materials collection service to commercial and
multi-family customers must be licensed by the city under section 100-13 , and must charge such customers for the minimum recycling service described paragraph
(f)(2), which may be itemized separately on bills. Collectors shall not exclude the
cost of minimum recycling service unless such customer is granted an exemption in
accordance with subsection (i) below.
(2)
Collectors providing recyclables collection service to commercial or multi-family
customers through centralized collection areas shall provide containers for recyclable
materials. The volume of recyclable materials collection service for commercial and
multi-family customers shall be at least one-third (⅓) of the total collection volume
(including both solid waste and recyclables) based on the size of solid waste containers
and the service frequency provided to such customer ("minimum recycling service").
For example, if a customer is provided with pick-up of a 4-cubic-yard trash container
that is collected once per week, the collector shall also provide minimum recycling
service in an amount equal to not less than a 2-cubic-yard recycling container as
a part of such basic services (two (2) cubic yards is one-third (⅓) of the total service
volume [including both solid waste and recyclables] of six (6) cubic yards).
(3)
Each collector providing recyclable materials collection to commercial and multi-family
customers in the city shall be required to:
(i)
Obtain a waste hauler license from Boulder County (in addition to the required city
license required under section 100-13 of the code);
(ii)
Report Re-TRAC data to Boulder County;
(iii)
With respect to single stream and source-separated, clean fiber recyclable materials
collected from commercial and multi-family customers in the city, haul such materials
to the processing center owned by Boulder County or its successor in interest ("Boulder
County Recycling Center"); and
(iv)
With respect to all other recyclable materials not included in subparagraph (f)(3)(iii),
above, that are collected from commercial and multi-family customers in the city,
haul such materials to a recyclables processing center.
(g)
Annual survey to commercial and multi-family customers .
(1)
Between November 1 and December 31 of each calendar year, the city may survey all
commercial and multi-family customers, on a form supplied by the city, to collect
the following information:
(i)
Confirmation as to whether the customer has arranged or contracted for recycling collection
service;
(ii)
The name of the collector the customer utilizes for recycling collection service;
(iii)
Whether the customer shares recycling collection service with another customer or
customers and, if so, the name(s) of the other customer(s);
(iv)
The size of the container(s) utilized for collection of recyclable materials and the
frequency of collection; and
(v)
Whether the customer provides organics collection services.
(h)
Education .
(1)
Educational materials . At the city's sole discretion, the city will provide each commercial and multi-family
customer with educational materials, which may include by way of example:
(i)
Free, downloadable signage consistent with city and regional recycling guidelines,
in English and Spanish;
(ii)
Free, downloadable video and written educational materials, in English and Spanish,
with educational guidelines and information for customers, their employees, tenants,
and occupants on how to properly sort recyclable materials and the environmental and
economic benefits of proper recycling;
(iii)
Other educational materials and campaigns as determined by the city, in its sole discretion,
to meet the needs of city residents, businesses, and visitors.
(2)
Customer responsibilities . Each commercial and multi-family customer is responsible for the following:
(i)
Displaying city-provided or city-approved signage in a conspicuous location at, on,
or above trash and recycling containers;
(ii)
Distributing city-provided educational materials, either virtually or physically,
to employees, tenants, and occupants upon hiring or move-in, and at last once annually

(iii)
Distributing the customer's site-specific information about trash and recycling collection

(i)
Exemptions .
(1)
Applications for exemptions from complying with the requirements of this section must
be made by the owner of the multi-family or commercial property or the owner of the
business or other type of commercial customer. A commercial or multi-family customer
may apply for an exemption if they meet one (1) or more of the following criteria:
(i)
Producer responsibility variance term . A multi-family customer facing economic hardship may apply for an exemption to extend
the time for compliance with one (1) or more requirements of this section. Once funds
from Colorado's Producer Responsibility for Recycling program are available to multi-family
customers in 2026, then this variance term exemption will end.
(ii)
Shared service : A commercial customer qualifies for this exemption if the commercial customer shares
a recycling container(s) with other customers through a communal system for the collection
of solid waste.
(iii)
Space constraints : A commercial or multi-family customer may qualify for this exemption if the location
does not have space for recycling containers or the only available location for recycling
containers is not safely serviceable by the collector. To qualify for this exemption:
(A)
The applicant must explore all possible options to fully comply with this section,
such as reducing the size of the container for non-recyclable waste and exploring
options to adjust enclosure size; and
(B)
The applicant must provide a description and photograph of the area where the space
constraints exist and document efforts from item (A) above.
(iv)
Home-based business : A home-based business qualifies for an exemption from this section if the applicant
provides proof that the business owner's home receives recycling collection services.
(v)
Economic hardship : A commercial or multi-family customer may qualify for an exemption from one (1)
or more requirements of this section if the owner of the property or business can
demonstrate extreme economic hardship and that compliance with one (1) or more requirements
of this section would continue or worsen the economic hardship.
(2)
Upon receipt of a request for exemption, the director shall either approve the exemption
for good cause shown, or request additional information from the applicant that the
director, in their sole discretion, requires to fully consider the application for
exemption or disapprove the exemption. For purposes of this section, "good cause shown"
shall mean evidence presented by the applicant that, to the reasonable satisfaction
of the director, demonstrates that the applicant meets one (1) or more of the above
exemption criteria. The director shall issue a written decision on the application
within ten (10) working days and, if disapproved, shall state the reasons in writing.
(3)
If the director disapproves an application for exemption, the applicant may appeal
the director's decision to the city administrator by filing a written appeal with
the city clerk within ten (10) days of the date the director's decision is mailed,
by submitting a written request to the city administrator setting forth the reasons
why the appeal should be granted. The city administrator shall notify the licensee
of the date and time of the hearing. After such hearing, the city administrator shall
affirm, deny, or modify the findings of the director and provide the applicant written
notification of such order.
(4)
If an exemption is approved in accordance with the foregoing provisions, the applicant
shall not be required to subscribe to recycling collection services for the one-year
period following approval of the exemption. If the constraints on which the exemption
was based still exist at the end of the exemption period, the applicant may submit
a request for continued exemption, providing sufficient details regarding current
conditions to enable the director to find good cause to grant the request.
(j)
Enforcement .
(1)
Any person who violates any provisions of this section commits a civil infraction
and shall be subject to the general penalty provisions in section 1-10 of the code and/or the administrative penalty provisions in chapter 7 of the code, as may be amended.
