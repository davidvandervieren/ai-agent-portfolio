To carry out the purpose and provisions of this chapter and the city's comprehensive
plan, the City of Lafayette, Colorado, is divided into the following zone districts:
(a)
Single-family residential districts.
RE1 Rural estate residential
RE2 Rural residential
R0 Low density residential
R1 Medium density residential
(b)
Multiple-family residential districts.
R2 Single-family and two-family residential
OTR Old Town Residential
R3 Multifamily residential
R4 High density residential
RSR Senior and special residential
(c)
Commercial or business districts.
T1 Transitional business
B1 Community service business district
C1 Regional business district
(d)
Industrial districts.
M1 Industrial
(e)
Others.
DR Developing resource
P Public
AG Agricultural
(Ord. No. 1986-01, § 2, 2-4-86; Ord. No. 1990-4, § 1, 4-17-90; Ord. No. 1993-6, §
1, 3-2-93; Ord. No. 1994-1, § 1, 4-5-94; Ord. No. 1998-26, § 1, 8-18-98; Ord. No.
2002-31, § 2, 11-5-02; Ord. No. 2014-22, § 2, 1-6-15)
In addition to the basic zoning districts established by section 26-2-1 , there is established an overlay zone district designated as the old town neighborhood
overlay district. This district encompasses the entirety of the old town residential
(OTR) zone district and portions of adjacent R-1, R-2 and T-1 zoned areas, and is
established to maintain the existing character of the property within the overlay
district. Regulations are established for this overlay district that shall apply in
addition to, or as a modification of, the regulations established for any underlying
basic zoning district encompassed within the old town neighborhood overlay district.
(Ord. No. 2017-30, § 1, 9-5-17)
