The location and boundaries of the zone districts established by this chapter are
shown upon the "zone district map of the City of Lafayette", which is incorporated
into this chapter. The zoning map, together with all data shown thereon and all amendments
thereto, is by reference made a part of this chapter. The zone district map shall
be identified by the signature of the mayor, attested by the city clerk, and bear
the seal of the city and the date of adoption. Changes in the boundary of any zone
district shall be made only by amendment to this chapter and shall promptly be entered
on the zone district map. The zone district map shall be located in the office of
the city planning department and copies made available to the public at a moderate
price.
(Ord. No. 1986-01, § 2, 2-4-86; Ord. No. 1998-29, § 1, 8-18-98; Ord. No. 2011-33,
§ 13, 9-20-11; Ord. No. 2019-06, § 2, 2-19-19)
