The municipal corporation heretofore existing as the "Town of Lafayette" in Boulder
County of Colorado shall remain and continue a body politic and corporate and under
this Charter shall be known as the "City of Lafayette" with the same boundaries until
changed in a manner authorized by law.
State Law reference— Municipal Annexation Act of 1965, § 31-12-101 et seq., C.R.S. 1973.
