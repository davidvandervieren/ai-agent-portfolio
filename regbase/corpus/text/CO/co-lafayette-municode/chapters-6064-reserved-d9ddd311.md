Editor's note— At the time of codification, Ch. 60, § 60-1, provided that ordinances pertaining to
mobile homes and mobile home parks were saved from repeal upon adoption of the Code.
Ord. No. 1986-01, § 1, adopted Feb. 4, 1986, specifically repealed the following ordinances
which pertained to such subject matter:

Ord. No. | Date | Ord. No. | Date
483 | 9-1-70 | 580 | 7-17-73
525 | (unrecorded) | 634 | 9-3-74
526 | 1-4-72

Therefore, § 60-1 and the designation of Ch. 60 as Mobile Homes have been deleted.
The user's attention is directed to Ch. 26
Section 26-22, for provisions relative to such subject matter.
