(a)
The city council for the city is hereby authorized to:
(1)
Retain all revenue derived from its sales and use taxes and all revenue derived from
its development-related fees and charges (comprising its development fees, construction
permit fees, service expansion fees, public land dedication in lieu payments, parkland
acquisition and development fees, and storm drainage fees), in 1995 and in all subsequent
years; and
(2)
Spend all of such revenue and any investment earnings thereon as a voter-approved
revenue change and an exception to any spending limitations which might otherwise
apply.
(b)
Such revenue and investment earnings on said tax and fee revenues, which would, in
the absence of such revenue change, be subject to refund pursuant to Article X, Section
20 of the Colorado Constitution, shall be spent exclusively for library, police, and
fire protection services.
(c)
All retention and spending authorized hereunder shall not limit the retention and
spending of any other revenue of the city in any year.

(a)
Fund established. There is hereby created a special fund to be known as the "capital projects fund"
to which shall be allocated such revenues as the city council may, from to time, determine.
Monies in the capital projects fund may be appropriated to pay for capital construction
or projects, as defined herein. All unappropriated balances in said fund at the close
of any fiscal year shall remain there and shall not revert to the general fund. All
interest earned from investments of monies in said fund shall remain in said fund
and become a part thereof.
(b)
[Defined.] As used in this section, "capital construction or project" means:
(1)
Purchase of land regardless of the value thereof;
(2)
Purchase, construction or demolition of buildings or other physical facilities, including
utilities and streets or highways, or remodeling or renovation of existing buildings
or other physical facilities, including utilities and streets or highways, to make
physical changes, to meet standards required by applicable codes to correct other
conditions hazardous to health and safety of persons which are not covered by codes,
to effect conservation of energy resources, to effect cost savings for staffing, operations,
or maintenance of the facility, or to approve appearance;
(3)
Site improvement or development;
(4)
Purchase and installation of the fixed and moveable equipment necessary for the operation
of new, remodeled, or renovated buildings or other physical facilities, and for the
conduct of programs initially housed therein, upon completion of the new construction,
remodeling or renovation;
(5)
Purchase of the services of architects, engineers, or other consultants, to prepare
plans, program documents, life cycle cost studies, energy analysis, or other studies
associated with any capital construction project, and to supervise construction or
execution of such capital construction projects;
(6)
Any mechanical or scientific equipment, if the cost will exceed fifty thousand dollars
($50,000.00).
(c)
Use of funds. Except as provided herein, all monies deposited into the capital projects fund shall
be used only to pay for capital construction or projects. The foregoing notwithstanding,
city council may, by the adoption of an ordinance, authorize use of monies in the
capital projects fund for municipal purposes other than capital construction or projects.
(d)
Annual transfers to capital projects fund. Subject to annual appropriations by city council in the budgetary process, as of
July 1, 2012, and on July 1 of each year thereafter through July 1, 2032, the city
finance director shall transfer the sum of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000.00)
out of the general fund and into the capital projects fund. Scheduled transfers pursuant
to this subsection shall not preclude additional transfers by city council into the
capital projects fund.

The purpose of this article is to prescribe purchasing procedures that the city will
follow in contracting for constructing capital improvements, purchasing tangible property
and securing contract services. To the extent inconsistent with state law, city council
intends that these procedures supercede provisions of state law governing city purchasing
procedures.
(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07)
(a)
Bid list shall mean a list of vendors and prices or price agreements negotiated or obtained
by a public agency through a competitive bidding process.
(b)
City administrator shall mean the city administrator appointed by city council or his/her authorized
designee.
(c)
Capital improvement means a fixed public improvement including, without limitation, streets, alleys,
sidewalks, water or wastewater facilities, flood control facilities, traffic control
devices, street lighting, parks, public structures, and landscaping.
(d)
Professional services means services of a specialized nature provided by persons possessing specialized
educational qualifications, practical experience or professional certification, including,
without limitation, architects, engineers, legal counsel, planners, accountants, actuaries
and high tech equipment repair and maintenance.
(e)
Public agency shall mean the State of Colorado or any agency, department or political subdivision
thereof, or any association of any of the foregoing.
(f)
Purchased services means services other than professional services for the performance of specified
tasks, including, without limitation, janitorial, maintenance, repair, secretarial
or clerical services, as determined by the city administrator.
(g)
Tangible property means personal property and materials, including, without limitation, supplies, equipment,
art, printing, software that is not customized, and consumable supplies, but not including
insurance, real property leases, securities or water rights.
(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07; Ord. No. 2010-25,
§ 1, 10-5-10)
(a)
The city administrator shall call for formal competitive bids for the purchase of
tangible property and for capital improvement contracts that are anticipated to exceed
an amount of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00) unless:
(1)
The contract is awarded to a person or entity who has been awarded a contract by another
public agency through a competitive bid process within the last year and the unit
prices in the city contract do not exceed the unit prices in the public agency contract;
(2)
The contract is for goods to be re-sold by the city at retail; or
(3)
The city administrator determines that it is not practical and advantageous to call
for a competitive bid and city council is advised of the reasons therefore prior to
the award of the contract; or
(4)
The contract is awarded to a person or entity who is a responsible bidder whose bid
is the lowest available bid, or at nominal variance with the lowest available bid,
pursuant to a bid list maintained by a public agency.
(b)
The city administrator shall call for formal competitive bids for obtaining purchased
services in an amount of twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000.00) or more unless:
(1)
The contract is awarded to a person who has been awarded a contract by another public
entity through a competitive bid process within the last year and the unit prices
in the city contract do not exceed those in the public entity contract; or
(2)
The contract is for purchased services, which, as determined by the city administrator
are most practically or advantageously obtained from a sole source; or
(3)
The city administrator determines that it is not practical or advantageous to call
for a competitive bid and city council is advised of the reasons therefore prior to
the award of the contract.
(c)
All contracts for purchases of tangible property, for capital improvements, professional
services, or purchased services in excess of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00) must
be authorized by city council regardless of whether the project is competitively bid.
(d)
The city administrator may call for competitive bids for any product, capital construction
project or purchased services.
(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07; Ord. No. 2010-25,
§ 2, 10-5-10)
(a)
Each formal bid submitted to the city shall meet the following conditions:
(1)
Each bid is signed, enclosed in a sealed envelope, and timely filed as stated in the
advertisement for the bid.
(2)
No bidder submits more than one (1) bid.
(3)
Each bid is accompanied by a check or bid bond equal to five (5) percent of the bid,
to be forfeited to the city if a bid is accepted and the bidder fails to sign a contract
within fifteen (15) days of acceptance, unless the city administrator determines that
it is not practical or advantageous to require a bid bond.
(b)
The city administrator may require that no bid be withdrawn up to forty-five (45)
days after the date and time for opening bids, but a bid may be withdrawn up to twenty-four
(24) hours prior to the expiration of the deadline for submitting bids.
(c)
The city administrator may waive technical irregularities in the bid requirements
in this article or in the call for bids if the city administrator finds that such
waiver does not compromise the integrity of the bidding process.
(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07)
(a)
The city administrator shall publish a notice of call for bids at least once in a
newspaper of general circulation in the city containing:
(1)
A description of the project or work to be performed or the product to be purchased;
(2)
The location of where copies of plans, specifications and other documents may be examined;
(3)
The time and place where bids will be received and the time and place where bids will
be opened;
(4)
A statement that the city reserves the right to reject any or all bids and to waive
any minor informalities or irregularities therein;
(5)
Time and budget limitations, if applicable; and
(6)
A statement that the bid is prepared at the submitter's expense and becomes city record
and therefore public record.
(b)
Among other conditions in a call for bids, the city administrator may require standard
brands of tangible property, recycled and environmentally preferable products, and
a multiple-year relationship of up to five (5) years with the selected bidder. In
the event that a multiple-year relationship is anticipated, the call for bid shall
specify that continuation of the contract for years subsequent to the year of the
award of the contract will be subject to annual appropriations by city council.
(c)
Bids not submitted by the required deadline are ineligible for consideration and will
not be opened, but the city administrator may change the deadline at any time.
(d)
Bids shall be opened at the time and place provided for in the call for bids.

Bidders may inspect the bids after they are opened in accordance with the provisions

Confidential data, if identified as such, will be held in confidence upon request,

the data meet the requirements of the Colorado Open Records Act.

Nothing in this article shall preclude the city administrator from issuing a call

The city administrator may require an informal competitive bidding or quote procedure

(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07; Ord. No. 2010-25,
§ 3, 10-5-10)
(a)
Monetary authority for acceptance of bids and awards of contract for capital improvements,
tangible property, and purchased services shall be established by city council resolution.
(b)
The following criteria and procedures shall apply to capital improvement contracts
and tangible property purchases:
(1)
The city may reject any and all bids but otherwise shall accept the lowest bid satisfying
the minimum bid requirements and the responsibility criteria prescribed by paragraph
(3) of this subsection. For the purposes of this article, the definition of "lowest
bid" will include consideration of initial cost and, when applicable, life-cycle costs,
including without limitation, maintenance costs over the normal lifetime of the product
and energy efficiency and consumption of nonrenewable fuels.
(2)
The city shall determine if the bidder satisfies the minimum bid requirements and
the responsibility criteria prescribed by paragraph (3) of this subsection. If the
city determines that the lowest bidder does not meet the minimum bid requirement and
responsibility criteria, the city may reject the bid.
(3)
In determining to accept a bid, the city shall consider the following responsibility
criteria: the bidder's integrity, financial responsibility, skill, relevant technical
expertise, ability to complete the contract promptly and satisfactorily, whether the
bidder maintains a permanent place of business, whether the bidder has adequate plant,
equipment and support services to perform the contract, whether the bidder has previously
performed similar work satisfactorily, whether the bidder is likely to be engaged
in work that may impair the ability to finance the work covered by the bid or provide
equipment for its proper execution, whether the bidder proposes a reasonable approach
to achieve the objective sought, and whether there have been or are any claims raising
a substantial question about the bidder's ability to perform the contract.
(c)
Award of contracts for purchased services shall utilize the same criteria and procedures
as is applicable to capital improvement contracts and tangible property purchases,
as described in subsection (b) above, except that the lowest bidder need not be awarded
the contract if the authority awarding the contract determines, based upon documented
evidence or experience, that the quality of the services to be performed by a bidder
other than the lowest bidder justifies the increased cost to the city.
(d)
Local preference. The foregoing provisions of this section notwithstanding, for capital
improvements, tangible property purchases and purchased services, a local preference
in accordance with this subsection shall be afforded to any bidder that maintains
its principal place of business within the corporate boundaries of the City of Lafayette,
Colorado ("resident bidder") if such bid is not for supplies or services of inferior
quality to those offered by nonresident bidders. Such local preference shall be calculated
as follows:
(1)
If a bid of one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) or less is from a resident bidder, and
such bid is within twenty (20) percent of the amount bid by a nonresident bidder,
the bid of the resident bidder shall be considered as the lower of the two (2) bids.
(2)
If a bid of between one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) and fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00)
is from a resident bidder, and such bid is within ten (10) percent of the amount bid
by a nonresident bidder, the bid of the resident bidder shall be considered as the
lower of the two (2) bids.
(3)
If a bid from a resident bidder is greater than fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00),
the amount of such bid shall be reduced by five thousand dollars ($5,000.00), but
only for the purpose of comparison to the amount bid by nonresident bidders.
(4)
If a bid of twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000.00) or less is from a resident bidder
who maintains its principal place of business within the boundaries of the Lafayette
Urban Renewal Authority Area, and such bid is within twenty (20) percent of the amount
bid by a nonresident bidder, the bid of such resident bidder shall be considered as
the lower of the two (2) bids.
(5)
In the event that the bid of two (2) or more resident bidders are considered to be
lower than the apparent low bidder, by virtue of this subsection, then as between
those resident bidders, the bidder with the lower bid shall be considered as the lowest,
without regard to whether the bidder is located within the Lafayette Urban Renewal
Area.
If it is determined by the city authority awarding the bid that compliance with this
subsection may cause a denial of federal or state monies which would otherwise be
available or would otherwise be inconsistent with the requirements of federal or state
law, this subsection shall be suspended, but only to the extent necessary to prevent
denial of the federal or state monies or to eliminate the inconsistency with federal
or state requirements.
(Ord. No. 2005-41, § 1, 2-7-05; Ord. No. 2007-28, § 1, 7-17-07; Ord. No. 2008-18,
