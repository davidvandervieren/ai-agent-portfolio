The following words, terms and phrases used in this chapter shall have the following
meanings, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning:
Alarm system means any device designed or used for the detection of fire or other hazard to life
or property, which when activated causes an audible and/or visual signal or transmits
a signal or message to which fire department personnel are expected to respond or
that would imply that fire department personnel are needed at the premises. Alarm
system does not include individual smoke detectors.
Excessive false alarms means three (3) or more false alarms received by the fire department within forty-eight
(48) hours from the same premises.
False alarm means any signal originating from an alarm system to which the fire department responds
and which results from:
(1)
False activation, including reporting fire or other life or property emergency when
none have actually occurred; or
(2)
Alarm malfunction, including mechanical failure or electrical failure; or
(3)
Alarm triggered by the property owner's negligence, including overly sensitive settings.
Exempted from this definition are alarms activated by forces beyond the property owner's
control such as those caused by extreme weather or atmospheric conditions or failure
or malfunction of off-premises telephone lines.
Fire chief means the chief of the City of Lafayette Fire Department or the chief's designee.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
It shall be unlawful for any person to intentionally cause the transmission of a false
alarm to the fire department.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
(a)
A false alarm fee shall be imposed upon the property owner of the premises from which
excessive false alarms emanate.
(b)
The schedule and amount of the fee shall be set by resolution of city council.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
(a)
Whenever the fire chief determines a fee for a false alarm should be imposed pursuant
to this chapter, the fire chief shall, by first class mail, send a notice to the property
owner where the alarm system is located, setting forth the amount of the fee due and
the reason for its imposition.
(b)
It shall be unlawful for any person to fail to pay the false alarm fee within thirty
(30) days of the mailing of the notice provided for in subsection (a) of this section.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
Within ten (10) days of receipt of the notice for a false alarm fee, the property
owner may submit a written statement setting forth the reasons why such fee should
not be imposed. Within ten (10) days of the receipt of the written statement, the
fire chief shall make a decision based on the statement of the property owner and
all other information provided the fire chief. The property owner shall be notified
in writing of the chief's decision.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
If the property owner fails to pay when due any fee imposed by this chapter, the city
may, in addition to any other collection remedy, certify the charge as due and unpaid
and submit same to the Boulder County Treasurer to be levied against such person's
property and to be collected as are delinquent taxes, plus a fifteen (15) percent
administrative fee.
(Ord. No. 2005-16, § 1, 5-3-05)
