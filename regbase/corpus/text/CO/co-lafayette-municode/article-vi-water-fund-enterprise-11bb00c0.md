Editor's note— Ordinance No. 1993-53, adopted Jan. 4, 1994, did not specifically amend this Code;
hence, codification of §§ 1—4 of said ordinance as §§ 120-161—120-164 herein was at
the editor's discretion.
Pursuant to the authority of section 16.13 of the Charter of the city, there is hereby created and established the water fund
enterprise.
(Ord. No. 1993-53, § 1, 1-4-94)
The city council is hereby appointed as the board of the water fund enterprise. There
shall not be appointed any persons other than the city council to serve on the board
of the water fund enterprise nor shall city council delegate to any other person or
entity the powers reserved to the board of the water fund enterprise hereunder.
(Ord. No. 1993-53, § 2, 1-4-94)
The water fund enterprise, acting by and through the city council sitting as the board
of the water fund enterprise, may issue its own revenue bonds or other obligations
(including refunding securities) on behalf of the city, which revenue bonds or other
obligations shall be payable solely from the net revenue (including special assessments)
derived from the operation of the water fund enterprise. Such revenue bonds or other
obligations may be additionally secured by mortgages on or security interests in any
real or personal property of the city used in the operation of the water fund enterprise.
Such revenue bonds or other obligations shall be issued by ordinance of the board
of the water fund enterprise, adopted in the same manner and subject to referendum
to the same extent as ordinances of the council.
(Ord. No. 1993-53, § 3, 1-4-94)
The city council shall not authorize the board of the water fund enterprise to acquire,
construct or install or hold title to or dispose of any city-owned property used in
the operation of the water fund enterprise, to impose or adjust rates, fees, tolls
or charges for the use of any such property or for any service or commodity furnished
by the water fund enterprise, to levy special assessments or to exercise any power
reserved to the council or other city officials by the City Charter or otherwise (other
than the power to issue revenue bonds and other obligations).
(Ord. No. 1993-53, § 4, 1-4-94)
