Editor's note— Section 10 of Ord. No. 1997-6, adopted May 20, 1997, deleted in its entirety and reserved
for future use § 120-36. Former § 120-36 pertained to definitions and derived from
Ord. No. 841, § 3, adopted June 15, 1979. The user is directed to the definitions
in § 120-1.
No water service approval shall be granted, no water meter shall be installed and
no water service shall be made on any water main or other portion of the waterworks
of the city without first securing a permit or permits therefor. The application for
a water meter or water service permit shall be made in writing upon a form furnished
by the director of public works, and shall state clearly the water service and meter
size, address and name of the person applying for the water service and any other
information that the director of public works may reasonably require.
(Ord. No. 621, § 2, 4-24-74; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
Any applicant desiring to take and use water from the water utility of the city shall
pay to the city a water service fee pursuant to the schedule of water service fees
prescribed in section 120-39.1 . Applicants desiring to take and use water for a proposed structure within the city
limits shall pay such fee at the time of issuance of a building permit or at a time
pursuant to Article XIV of Chapter 30 of this Code. Applicants desiring to take and use water from the water utility of
the city for an existing structure within the city limits, or any structure outside
the city limits, whether existing or proposed, shall pay such fee prior to the issuance
of a water utility connection permit. This fee shall be in addition to any other charges
relating to water service elsewhere described in the ordinances of the city.
(Ord. No. 841, § 2, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 1990-17, § 2, 8-21-90; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2,
7-15-03)
Charter reference— Power of council to fix charges for public utility services, § 13.4 .
The city shall look only to the owner and the owner's successors in interest of each
property or building desiring water service for payment of the water service fee.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
Residential tap fees shall be based on meter size and in an amount authorized in chapter 4 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 860, § 1, 12-18-79; Ord. No. 895, § 1, 11-4-80;
Ord. No. 917, § 1, 7-21-81; Ord. No. 992, § 1, 9-20-83; Ord. No. 1985-28, § 1, 8-20-85;
Ord. No. 1985-37, § 1, 10-7-86; Ord. No. 1993-12, § 1, 4-20-93; Ord. No. 2000-44,
§ 24, 11-21-00; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03; Ord. No. 2005-10, § 50, 3-1-05; Ord. No. 2025-25 , § 75, 11-18-25)
(a)
All utility customers, other than residential, shall pay a water tap fee in an amount
authorized in chapter 4 of this Code. The size of the water meter, and therefore the amount of the water
tap fee, shall be based on the total maximum demand of the proposed use exclusive
of the demand for fire protection purposes. Maximum demand shall be determined on
a fixture-unit basis as described in the International Plumbing Code, as adopted by
the city in section 30-176 of the Code. The actual physical connection size and the
location of the water main shall be determined by the public works department.
(b)
Applicants shall provide all pertinent information necessary for determination of
the water service fee as required by the director of public works. The applicant shall
be required to provide the maximum design demand in gallons per minute for any landscape
irrigation system. The fees shall be paid upon application for a building permit,
or at any time prior to the purchase of the same, or at a time pursuant to Article
XIV of Chapter 30 of this Code. No building permit or water utility connection permit shall be released
until all public utility requirements have been satisfied, unless deferred as above
stated.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 860, § 2, 12-18-79; Ord. No. 895, § 2, 11-4-80;
Ord. No. 1986-37, § 2, 10-7-86; Ord. No. 1990-17, § 4, 8-21-90; Ord. No. 1993-12,
§ 2, 4-20-93; Ord. No. 2000-44, § 25, 11-21-00; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03; Ord.
No. 2005-10, § 51, 3-1-05; Ord. No. 2025-25 , § 76, 11-18-25)
Developments with combined uses shall be assessed a water service fee based on the
individual uses within the structure. The "other than residential" portion shall be
assessed in accordance with the "other than residential" fee schedule, and the residential
portion shall be assessed in accordance with the residential fee schedule.
(Ord. No. 1997-6, § 11, 5-20-97; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
Business, commercial and industrial developments designated to be sold and occupied
in a manner similar to residential condominiums shall be assessed a water service
fee pursuant to the "other than residential" schedule of fees. The industrial water
service fee shall be based on the total demand of the building or structure. Any increase
in total demand resulting from the installation of additional fixtures shall require
an additional water service fee.
(Ord. No. 1997-6, § 12, 5-20-97; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
The director of public works shall establish policies and procedures pursuant to section 120-2 to determine those utility customers who shall be required to obtain an irrigation
service for landscape irrigation. All utility customers who are required to obtain
an irrigation service pursuant to such policies and procedures, and all utility customers
who desire to install an irrigation service or utilize a water service for irrigation
purposes shall meet the requirements as specified by the director of public works
pursuant to section 120-2 , based on the schedule of fees for other than residential users.
(Ord. No. 1997-6, § 13, 5-20-97; Ord. No. 1999-50, § 5, 1-4-2000; Ord. No. 2003-20,
§ 2, 7-15-03)
If the existing water tap and water meter sizes are of sufficient size, based upon
fixture count and maximum gallon per minute flow rate, separate water service, water
meters and water rights fees are not required for secondary or accessory buildings
on private lots or for accessory dwelling units on private lots, on properties having
a main or principal building or use, provided that ownership of such separate secondary,
accessory buildings or accessory dwelling units and main or principal buildings is
by a single person or entity. If the addition of an accessory dwelling unit requires
an increase in water tap or water meter size, the owner must pay the difference for
and install the required water tap and water meter, and pay any applicable water rights
fees. If ownership of a secondary, accessory building or accessory dwelling unit and
a main or principal building is severed or is otherwise held by separate persons or
entities, both of the buildings shall be required to obtain a separate water service
and water meter, and to pay separate water rights fees. All costs for water service,
water meters and water rights fees shall be the responsibility of the new owner.
(Ord. No. 1997-6, § 14, 5-20-97; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03; Ord. No. 2015-23,
§ 1, 7-21-15; Ord. No. 2023-16 , § 5, 9-5-23; Ord. No. 2025-25 , § 77, 11-18-25)
Tap fees for connections outside the city limits shall be in an amount authorized
in chapter 4 of this Code.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03; Ord. No. 2005-10, § 52,
3-1-05; Ord. No. 2025-25 , § 78, 11-18-25)
Upon approval of the water service permit, the city shall install a water meter in
a meter pit provided by the applicant. The applicant shall bear the cost of the meter
and installation, yoke, corporation valve, pit and cap, and all costs of excavation
and plumbing from the water service to the buildings or uses served.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 1997-6, § 15, 5-20-97; Ord. No. 1999-50, § 6,
1-4-2000; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
If a water utility system user applies for and obtains permission to make changes
or additions to the property that would affect the demand on the water utility system,
a new water utility system permit shall be required. The water utility service fee
for such permit shall be computed on the basis of the new service as determined by
the schedule of fees authorized in chapter 4 of this Code. Credit shall be given for the current amount of the water service fee
required for the use of the water utility system prior to the changes or additions
to the property. Credit shall also be allowed for approved decreases in the amount
of the water utility system service. Under no circumstances shall the city be required
to pay cash refunds for either decreases in the amount of water utility system service
provided or for allowable credits in excess of the new water utility system service
fee. Credits allowed herein shall apply to the property served by the existing water
utility system service and shall be applied only to the water utility system service
fees owed to the city and not to other utility fees or charges.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 1989-42, § 1, 9-5-89; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2,
7-15-03; Ord. No. 2025-25 , § 79, 11-18-25)
(a)
In the event that a water service is issued by the city but such water service is
not put to use within one hundred eighty (180) days of issuance, the applicant shall
be responsible for and liable for payment of minimum monthly water utility service
charges as set forth in Article III of this chapter until such time as the water service
is put to actual use.
(b)
The city administrator shall have the authority to waive the requirements of paragraph
(a) for up to one hundred eighty (180) additional days in the event that the water
utility customer is able to show sufficient good cause for the waiver to be granted.
(Ord. No. 841, § 3, 6-15-79; Ord. No. 1986-45, § 1, 12-2-86; Ord. No. 1999-1, § 1,
1-19-99; Ord. No. 2003-20, § 2, 7-15-03)
