Chapter 17.12: ZONING DISTRICTS DESIGNATED - City of Riverton, WY
In order to carry out the provisions of this division, the city is divided into the following districts:
A.
Residential R-1, limited one-family;
B.
Residential R-2, limited multiple-family;
C.
Residential R-2A, gardening and agricultural;
D.
Residential R-4, multiple-family;
E.
Residential PUD, planned unit development;
F.
Office and institution, C-O district;
G.
Planned neighborhood shopping, C-P district;
H.
General commercial, C-1 district;
I.
Commercial highway, C-H district;
J.
Light industrial, I-1 district;
K.
Heavy industrial, I-2 district;
L.
Mobile home park, MHP district;
M.
Agricultural, AG district; and
N.
Airport, AP district.

The boundaries and zoning classifications of districts established are as shown on a map entitled, Zoning District Map of the city of Riverton, Wyoming, dated June 12, 2007, as amended from time to time, which map and all notations, references, data and other information shown thereon are by reference made a part of this chapter.
A.
District boundary lines are section lines; lot lines; the center lines of highways, streets, alleys, railroad rights-of-way or such lines extended; municipal corporate lines; natural boundary lines, such as streams; or other lines drawn to scale on the official zoning district map.
B.
The official zoning district map shall be kept up to date by the city, and copies of the map shall be available for public use.

Any owner seeking a zoning change shall pay to the city an administrative processing fee, amount of fee set by city council resolution, at the time of filing a request for zoning change.
