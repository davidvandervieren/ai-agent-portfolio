# Planning & Zoning
The Planning and Zoning Department deals with Land Use, Zoning, Subdividing, Residential & Commercial Building, Permitting and inspecting Septic systems, Rural addressing, and other related matters.
The Planning and Zoning Department is primarily responsible for the administration of various land use regulations, including zoning and the Comprehensive Plan. We are also responsible for enforcement of land use regulations, drafting and amending regulations and providing technical assistance to the Planning and Zoning Board and Sublette County Commissioners. We look forward to working with you on land use planning projects and assisting you with your land use permit applications.
