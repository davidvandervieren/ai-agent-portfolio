## 10.08.020 - Section 31-5-221—Turning left at intersection.
The driver of a vehicle intending to turn to the left shall yield the right-of-way
to any vehicle approaching from the opposite direction which is within the intersection
or so close thereto as to constitute an immediate hazard.
(Ord. No. 918, 8-13-2012)
