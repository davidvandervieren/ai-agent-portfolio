# Planning & GIS
The Planning Commission holds its monthly meeting on the third Wednesday of each month at 6:30 pm in the Board of County Commissioners meeting room in the Goshen County Courthouse. Normal agenda items will be addressed during meeting as well as potential work sessions to develop regulations on a number of topics.
Currently Goshen County does not have Zoning Regulations or Building Inspections.
Any person interested in serving on the Goshen County Planning Commission please contact the County Clerk.
