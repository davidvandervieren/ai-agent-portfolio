# Zoning
The purpose of the Natrona County Zoning Resolution is the protection of public health, safety, morals, and general welfare.
Zoning provides for:
The division of the unincorporated area of the County into zoning districts;
Regulation of location, erection, construction, reconstruction, enlargement, alteration and use of structures;
Regulation of the uses, condition of use, or occupancy of lands for agricultural, residential, commerce, industrial, recreational, and public purposes;
Promotion of the orderly development and performance standards which regulate physical development of lands within the county.
Please email the Planning Department or call 307-235-9447 for answers to questions relating to zoning.
