Easements
Easements on state lands give the grantee partial interest, but not ownership, in the land as authorized under
Chapter 3 of the Board of Land Commissioners' Rules and Regulations
.
Easements may be granted for:
ditches
overhead wires
pipelines
railroads
reservoirs
public roads and highways
roadways to private land or residences
snow fences
underground cables
open spaces
and any other appropriate uses
