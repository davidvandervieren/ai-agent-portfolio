Land and Lease Map Viewer
Explore our Land and Lease Map Viewer!
This application provides information regarding OSLI land assets and leases that are under the jurisdiction of the State Board of Land Commissioners.
Use the map viewer to:
Search surface plat book
Search subsurface plat book
Search/view state surface and subsurface ownership
Search/view surface and mineral leases on state land
Search/view state land public access
Search/view restrictions and closures on state land
Download certain data sets
Search tabular records from our land management systems

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I view State Land Public Access data in the Land and Lease Map Viewer?
Follow the steps below to view State Land Public Access data in the
Land and Lease Map Viewer
.
Open the
Land and Lease Map Viewer
and agree to the terms and conditions by clicking the checkbox and clicking OK.
Click on the Layers button on the toolbar then click on the State Land Public Access Layers button in the drop down.
The State Land Public Access Layers list will open. This list shows all layers related to State Land Public Access. These layers are turned off by default.
Check the boxes to the left of the layer name to turn the layers on. State Land Public Access, Restrictions & Closures, and Do Not Hunt - Cropland are now displayed on the map.
To find more information about a particular state land parcel or restriction/closure, zoom in and click to select a parcel or restriction/closure. A pop-up will open with more information regarding your selection.

How do I search the online plat books?
There are two ways to search the new plat books - using the
Plat Book Searches Tool
and using the
Attribute Table
.
Using the Plat Book Searches Tool:
Open the
Land and Lease Map Viewer
and agree to the terms and conditions by clicking the checkbox and clicking OK.
Click on the Plat Book Searches button on the toolbar to open the search window.
Click to select a search to perform.
Type the search criteria in the search boxes and press enter or click on Apply.
Search criteria format:
Sections:
1-36
Townships:
12N
-58N
(6th P.M.)
1
-9
North or South
(
WRM)
Ranges:
60
W-121W
(6th P.M.)
1
-6
East or West
(
WRM)
*If no results are displayed when searching by section township and range, the land may have been resurveyed. Try searching again and leaving the section box blank.
Search results will display in the window. Scroll down as necessary to view all search results.
Action Field Key:
E = Expired
T = Terminated
R = Relinquished
C = Canceled
O = Operating
Results can be exported to a CSV file or can be viewed in the attribute table by choosing those options from the results menu.
To perform a new search, click on the search tab at the top of the window.
To switch from one search to another, click the back arrow to return to the search selection screen.
Click here for a printable version of these instructions with screenshots.
Using the Attribute Table:
Open the
Land and Lease Map Viewer
and agree to the terms and conditions by clicking the checkbox and clicking OK.
Click on the Attribute Table button at the bottom of the map to open the attribute table.
To resize the attribute table, click and drag up on the line underneath the arrow.
Use either the rightward or downward facing arrow on the right-hand side of the attribute table tab bar to navigate to the Surface Online Plat Book tab or Subsurface Online Plat Book tab.
Click on the Option button on the attribute table toolbar to open the options menu.
From the option menu, click on Filter.
Once the Filter window opens, click Add Expression. Choose Township (String) from the first dropdown. Leave the second dropdown as is. In the textbox, type in the township you want to search for.
Repeat the step above to add filter criteria for range and section, if desired.
Click Add Expression and choose Range (String) from the first dropdown. Leave the second dropdown as is. In the textbox, type in the range you want to search for.
Click Add Expression and choose Section (String) from the first dropdown. Leave the second dropdown as is. In the textbox, type in the section you want to search for.
Click OK to perform the filter once you have you desired criteria set.
*If no results are displayed when searching by section township and range, the land may have been resurveyed. Try searching again with only township and range expressions.
Filter results will display in the attribute table. Scroll down as necessary to view all filter results.
Action Field Key:
E = Expired
T = Terminated
R = Relinquished
C = Canceled
O = Operating
Results can be exported to a CSV file by clicking on the Options menu and selecting Export all to CSV.
