### About
The Wyoming Cultural Records Office (WYCRO) collects and manages cultural resource information for the state of Wyoming. In accordance with Wyoming Statute (W.S. 36-4-106 (d) [i-vii]), both digital and papers documents are stored at the University of Wyoming. WYCRO provides this information to federal agencies, cultural resource management firms, researchers, and other parties through online GIS and non-spatial databases. To get access to these databases please visit WyoTrack .

Official file search requests are now submitted through the WyoTrack application. To request access to WyoTrack visit the WyoTrack page, complete a user application form and complete the sign up process detailed in the WyoTrack sign up guide. Instructions for submitting a file search are detailed in the WyoTrack user manual that can be found in the "Getting Started" section. In addtion to official file searches WYCRO can also provide GIS and tabular data clips. These data clips are often useful in the early planning process. To submit a GIS and tabular data request visit the WyoTrack page and complete the GIS data use agreement form. Fees associated with GIS and tabluar data request are detailed in the "Fees" section below.
Official File Searches:
$60.00 per section plus a transaction fee of 3.3% + $0.30
GIS and Tabular Data Requests:
Non-compliance based research= No fee*
Tabular data only= $15.00
1 to 99 sections= $30.00
100 to 999 sections= $100.00
>1000 sections= $250.00
*Non-compliance based research is considered any research conducted by a professional archaeologist, professor, or student that is not related to a Federal Project/Undertaking that would require the Section 106 Review Process (see 36 CFR Part 800 of the ACHP regulations).
Other Requests:
Billed at staff time, $30.00 per hour

As of May 6, 2019, the Wyoming Cultural Properties forms should be submitted through WyoTrack on-line. The on-line versions of the forms are the Wyoming State Historic Preservation Office's (WYSHPO) submission standard. Printed versions of the form completed in WyoTrack should be submitted along with the Class II or Class III report to the appropriate federal agency. Do not submit the old WYCPF in addition to the WyoTrack version. If you need access to WyoTrack, please contact the Wyoming Cultural Records Office at 307-766-5335 or click here to contact us .
WyoTrack Login
WyoTrack is a web application that provides access to the Wyoming Cultural Records Office GIS and non-spatial data. In addition this application allows tracking of projects through the section 106 process. To request access please complete the user application below. WyoTrack is managed by the Cultural Records office.
