# Planning and Zoning Commission
Planning and Zoning Commission meetings are typically held on the first Thursday of the month at 5:30 p.m. in the Sheridan County Courthouse, 2nd floor Commissioners’ Board Room, 224 S. Main St., Sheridan, Wyoming. Please check the Calendar for the next scheduled meeting.
Additional information for the agenda items can be obtained by calling 675-2420. Please send written comments to the Sheridan County Public Works Department, 224 S. Main Street, Suite 428, Sheridan, WY 82801. Enter the County Courthouse through doors located on the west and northwest side (Brooks Street) of the addition to the County Courthouse. The doors will lock after 7:00 p.m., people will be able to leave but not enter.
