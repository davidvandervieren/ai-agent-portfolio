EPA Region 8 solicits public comment on issuance of permit-related actions under the authority of the Safe Drinking Water Act and UIC Program regulations for a minimum of 30 calendar days on this website.

## Resources for Owners and Operators
Submitting a UIC Permit Application: Complete the Permit Application for the appropriate well class. The Class II Permit Application Completeness Review Checklist is intended to assist EPA staff’s review of the permit application during the Administrative Review process. This checklist can also serve as a useful guide as you prepare your Class II permit application and can be used for other well class applications . Attachment K lists additional federal laws that EPA must comply. The National Historic Preservation Act and Endangered Species Act requirements will apply to most permits issued by EPA Region 8. If the applicant is willing to initiate and complete the cultural resource and endangered or threatened species surveys, inclusion of these and/or recently conducted surveys can significantly expedite the permitting process. In addition to the permit application, Region 8 also requests that the applicant complete the attached Permit Applicant Spreadsheet (xlsx) (245 KB) to assist the EPA in technical review of the permit application.

The approved regulators for the UIC program in Region 8 are found in the table below.
In addition to the responsible agencies below, EPA Region 8 directly implements the UIC Program on all Indian Reservations in Colorado, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota, Utah, and Wyoming, with the exception of the Class II program on the Fort Peck Indian Reservation.
