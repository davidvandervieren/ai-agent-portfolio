# Find a Project on the BLM National NEPA Register
Welcome to the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) Register! You can easily review and comment on plans and project documents from your computer, tablet, or phone. The search results tables are customizable to display more data and views for upcoming and past events. Search for a project using a keyword, the NEPA #, project name or document name. Projects from Calendar Year 2015 forward are available on the map search feature. Older documents may be found with a text search. Can’t find the project you’re looking for? Please contact your local BLM office . If you’re interested in learning more about navigating the NEPA Register, take a moment to watch the video tutorials listed in the Help menu. For more information about the NEPA Register, head to BLM’s ePlanning homepage .
