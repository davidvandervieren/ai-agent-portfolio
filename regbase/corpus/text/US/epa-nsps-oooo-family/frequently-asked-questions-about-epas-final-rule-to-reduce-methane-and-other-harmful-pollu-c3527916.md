## Disclaimer
These frequently asked questions (FAQs) do not impose legally binding requirements on the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, states, Tribes, or the regulated community, nor do they confer legal rights or impose legal obligations upon any member of the public. Read the full disclaimer .
This page contains links to frequently asked questions about EPA's final New Source Performance Standards for new, modified and reconstructed source, and Emissions Guidelines for existing sources in the Oil and Natural Gas Sector. These rules are often referred to as " OOOOb " and " OOOOc ." The rule was published in the Federal Register on March 8, 2024.
