# Navajo Nation National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) Permits
EPA’s Pacific Southwest (Region 9) issues all National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permits on Navajo Nation tribal lands.
The tables below contain all final (i.e. active) and draft (i.e. proposed) NPDES permits issued by EPA’s Pacific Southwest (Region 9) within Navajo Nation lands. It includes permits for individual facilities, general permits, and stormwater permits. Click on the facility/permit name to view the permit and supporting documents.
For State-Issued Permits: Arizona Pollutant Discharge Elimination System See Also: Arizona Tribal Lands NPDES Permits
