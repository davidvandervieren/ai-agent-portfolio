CLEAN WATER ACT PERMITS
U.S. ARMY CORPS OF ENGINEERS
404 PERMIT-REGULATES THE DISCHARGE OF DREDGED OR FILL MATERIAL INTO WATERS OF THE 
UNITED STATES
401 PERMIT IS A LOCAL CONSULTATION WITH NAVAJO EPA WATER QUALITY
INITIAL CONSULTATION WITH THE U.S. ARMY CORPS OF ENGINEERS IS REQUIRED IN EITHER 404 
OR 401 PERMITING
404/401 PERMITTING COVERS CULVERTS, DREDGING, EMBANKMENTS
THE LARGER ARPA PROJECTS MAY NEED 404 OR 401 PERMITING

<!-- page: 9 -->
STORMWATER POLLUTION CONTROL 
PLAN
COMMONLY REFERRED TO AS SWWP PLAN
CONSTRUCTION DISTURANCE OVER 1-ACRE REQUIRES A SWWP PLAN
MUST BE SUBMITTED TO NAVAJO EPA FOR APPROVAL
APPLICANT MUST SUBMIT A NOTICE OF INTENT (NOI)
THE PURPOSE OF THIS IS TO PREVENT STORMWATER FROM LEAVING THE PROJECT SITE DURING 
CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES

NAVAJO NATION SOLID WASTE 
REGULATIONS
ALL OPEN DUMPING SHALL BE PROHIBITED
NO OPEN BURNING OF SOLID WASTE SHALL BE ALLOWED
SOLID WASTE SHALL BE COLLECTED AND TRANSPORTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH NN SOLID 
WASTE REGULATIONS
ANY TRANSPORTED SHALL USE VEHICLES WHICH HAVE COVERS OR ENCLOSURES TO PREVENT 
SOLID WASTE FROM BEING RELEASED
