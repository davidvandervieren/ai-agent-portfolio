# City of Farmington Zoning

<!-- heading-path: City of Farmington Zoning > What is Zoning? -->
## What is Zoning?
The City of Farmington has adopted Functional Zoning, which divides land into zoning districts with separate uses and regulations.
Zoning authority to the City of Farmington is provided in New Mexico Statute Chapter 3, Article 31.
Zoning has numerous purposes: Establishes health and safety benchmarks for the City. Protects private property rights. Helps keep property values stable. Supports rational and orderly development.
